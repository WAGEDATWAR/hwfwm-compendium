## 1. Summary

Three kinds of event reshape territory in the series. Astral spaces are pocket worlds attached to a physical world through apertures. Proto-astral spaces are short-lived, unstable versions of them that Earth suffers from. Transformation zones are sealed events that rewrite a place and the people in it. Monster surges are periodic increases in monster numbers across a world. All four are driven by the same source: raw magic leaking through from the astral.

## 2. How it works

**Astral spaces.** Farrah: "Astral spaces are pretty common, but normally they're sealed off and you need magic to track one down and break in. Being naturally open like this is rare, especially with so many apertures" (Book 1, Ch24 L38). Greenstone's river comes out of a large aperture (Book 1, Ch24 L38). An aperture connects a place on the physical world to the astral space; an astral space may have many apertures, and the mayor of one town notes theirs is "only one of many places with an aperture leading to that astral space" (Book 1, Ch31 L4). An astral space can be a rainforest island chain, with a sun that "just moving around on a circuit in the sky" and no night (Book 1, Ch104 L61). It can be far smaller than the world it is attached to: "the astral space is only a fraction of the size of the world it's attached to" (Book 1, Ch111 L24). Expeditions enter through the aperture, making camp, searching in teams and retreating through it for triage (Book 1, Ch104 L60-61; Book 1, Ch111 L28).

**Why they are attached.** Clive: "Astral spaces form attached to worlds, without which they immediately break down. Without a real world to anchor them, they cannot exist. But if an astral space is given the ability to sustain itself, even for just a brief period, the Builder can take it and anchor it to the world the Builder is creating from stolen parts" (Book 2, Ch21 L110). The Builder's process preserved the astral space at the cost of catastrophic destruction to the physical reality (Book 2, Ch21 L114).

**Surges.** "Every ten years there's a massive increase in the spawn rate of monsters. All across the world, all at the same time. Whole villages evacuate to fortified towns" (Book 1, Ch31 L71). The last one was eleven years earlier; "it's never exactly ten years. It's been as little as eight or as many as thirteen. The last few have all come pretty late" (Book 1, Ch31 L73-74). Most of the delta's population "comes into the city during monster surges" (Book 1, Ch35 L31). The Book 7 surge is "some five years overdue, during which time monster activity had gradually increased. Once the surge proper began, it was expected to be more brutal and last longer than any in history" (Book 7, Ch1 L56). During a surge adventurers earn contribution points instead of direct payment, and guilds use it to scout talent (Book 7, Ch38 L6).

**Proto-astral spaces (Earth).** A proto-astral space is "unstable dimensional pockets attached to the world" (Book 4, Ch37 L63). Their usual duration is "forty-three hours"; an anomalous one in Book 4 lasted "less than a second" and was the size and shape of a person, though its rated strength still registered above five (Book 4, Ch5 L127). The aperture to one "wasn't visible to the naked eye, although magical senses made it extremely easy to see. It was a more tenuous bridge across dimensional boundaries than a normal aperture and appeared... as if it might collapse at any moment" (Book 4, Ch40 L33). Entering one is announced by a System message: "The ambient magical saturation has increased. Your recovery rates will remain at normal levels without spirit coin consumption" (Book 4, Ch39 L55). Spirit coins cannot be formed in them: "Earth doesn't have the magic and coin formation takes months, so you can't do it in the proto-astral spaces" (Book 4, Ch37 L79).

**Transformation zones (Earth).** In the Russian city of Kostroma the whole city "was sealed off in a dome", actually a sphere that extended underground; "forty-three hours after the sphere moved into place, it vanished, revealing a city vastly changed" (Book 5, Ch44 L48). The changes remake the area "in the image of worlds used as patterns when the original Builder created this universe" (Book 5, Ch44 L52). Buildings look weathered with years of age (Book 5, Ch44 L51). On Earth many normal-ranked people were transformed into other races in accordance with the zone they were in, humans becoming "leonids, celestines and other races native to neither Earth nor Pallimustus" (Book 11, Ch1 L36).

**Zones as spaces to be claimed.** A transformation zone that Jason enters contains a genesis space, which his System names: "You have established a spirit domain. Expanding your spirit domain will define and stabilise unstable genesis space but trigger anomalous reactions from genesis space outside the spirit domain" (Book 6, Ch2 L15). Territories are claimed by defeating anomalies and then a final one: "You have overtaken a genesis space territory and purged all anomalous elements" (Book 6, Ch5 L32). See `Monsters.md` for the anomalies.

## 3. Variations and exceptions

- **Why zones happen on Earth.** "Transformation zones on Earth were the result of the World-Phoenix intervening in eons past, to prevent Earth from breaking under the influence of magic." Pallimustus has no such need, so the dimensional rupture there required "another hand to trigger the transformation zone" (Book 11, Ch1 L17).
- **Jason's three zones.** In the first he "had broken into a zone already in place" and it forcibly evolved an outworlder power into Spiritual Domain; in the second he could reshape it from the start and raised the base level of magic on Earth, so that "magic no longer manifested in proto-astral spaces before spilling into the world"; the third is on Pallimustus (Book 11, Ch1 L15-17).
- **The special zone.** The System describes the Book 11 zone: "This special transformation zone must be unified to restore stability, reintegrate it with physical reality and re-establish an astral space", unified by "claiming all non-central territories" (Book 11, Ch1 L38-39). Each territory is claimed by eliminating a final anomaly, and if several are present it "must be ceded to one individual through conflict or forfeit" (Book 11, Ch1 L40).
- **Nitra.** A transformation zone at Nitra in Slovakia forms as a "glimmering dome several kilometres across" that stays closed for days (Book 5, Ch68 L68). The Chinese forces under Li Li Mei claim a site around it (Book 6, Ch5 L48).
- **Low-magic zones.** In a low-magic area, "consuming a spirit coin of your rank or ten spirit coins of one rank lower will restore your recovery rates to normal for eight hours" (Book 12, Ch27 L19).

## 5. Common misconceptions in-world

- **Astral spaces are the same as other worlds.** They are small pockets attached to a world (Book 1, Ch111 L24).
- **Proto-astral spaces last forever.** They last about forty-three hours (Book 4, Ch5 L127).
- **A surge is exactly ten years.** It ranges from eight to thirteen (Book 1, Ch31 L74).

## 6. Changes over time

- **Level 2:** The first zones on Earth are 43 hours long and remake places in the pattern of other worlds (Book 5, Ch44 L48-52).
- **Level 3:** After Jason's second zone, "The base level of magic had risen; magic no longer manifested in proto-astral spaces before spilling into the world. He had begun a new age of magic, where monsters and essences manifested directly in the world" (Book 11, Ch1 L16).
