## 1. Summary

A **title** is how the System writes down a change to the soul: it appears as a window after a soul has been scarred or tested and usually changes the owner's aura. A **blessing** is a gift from a god or great astral being that evolves a racial gift. Titles come from what happens to a person, blessings from what a transcendent being chooses to give. The two meet in Jason's story because the same events scar his soul and attract the blessings of the World-Phoenix and the Reaper.

## 2. Titles

### What they are

- **The Interface's translation.** "When recovery from soul trauma affected Jason's abilities, his interface power used titles to translate the scars on his soul into game terms" (Book 4, Ch2 L26). Anyone who fully recovers from soul trauma finds themselves enhanced (Book 4, Ch2 L25).
- **How Jason explains it.** Years later he tells Delia, "It's a video game thing. Kind of like unlocking an achievement", then explains that "the soul is a blueprint for the body" and that scars on the body are a representation of defining moments (Book 13, Ch990 L61-65, L70).
- **They change the aura signature.** Each window says so: "Your aura signature has changed", with a note of what can be detected in it (for example Book 3, Ch14 L83). See Auras.md.

## 3. Blessings

### What a blessing does

- **A racial gift evolution.** A god or great astral being can give a follower a blessing that triggers an evolution of a racial gift (Book 1, Ch85 L47). Clive's blessing from the Celestial Book changed the human Special Attack Affinity to a spell affinity (Book 1, Ch85 L22).
- **Harmless and irrevocable.** The blessing "could not control the recipient outside of ordinary methods like gratitude and obligation" and, once granted, could be neither managed nor revoked by the giver, unlike an ability from a divine awakening stone (Book 4, Ch2 L44). Clive: "The blessings are harmless" (Book 2, Ch63 L47).
- **Balanced by the great astral beings.** They do not give favoured supporters blessings that contain as much magic as can be withstood, "to avoid escalation and to minimise mortal interference" (Book 4, Ch3 L46).
- **Not the same as star seeds.** Astral beings can bestow blessings like gods but cannot bestow essences and stones; instead they can send followers star seeds (Book 2, Ch21 L26). Blessing powers "do not offer control over their recipients" (Book 4, Ch3 L70).
- **Given to opponents too.** Some great astral beings give blessings to those who oppose their interests when ideologies mesh, or to enemies worthy of respect (Book 4, Ch2 L45; Book 2, Ch63 L47).

### Tokens

- **Astral blessing tokens.** "Something great astral beings give out to bestow blessings, as signs of approval", usable in an awakening ritual to arrange specific abilities, which only transcendent beings can arrange (Book 2, Ch63 L45-49). Clive's own arrived "one night while I was studying" as a patch of moonlight (Book 2, Ch63 L51).
- **Jason's tokens.** His Reaper token, produced by his quest system, is a surprise to Clive, who says only great astral beings can produce them (Book 2, Ch63 L53-55).

### Notable blessings

- **The World-Phoenix's blessing to Jason**: on rebirth he is told "You have received the blessing of the World-Phoenix"; if accepted his outworlder racial ability Astral Affinity evolves to Nirvanic Transfiguration (Book 4, Ch1 L33-35). It is "a product of a bargain struck between great astral beings" and works outside the normal balance (Book 4, Ch3 L48). Jason had already received a transfiguration through a Reaper token, with his use of the token while absorbing a stone serving as consent (Book 4, Ch2 L45).
- **Farrah's blessings**: after the bargain between the World-Phoenix, the Reaper and the Builder, the World-Phoenix agrees if "we bestow blessings to evolve her racial gifts. All her racial gifts" (Book 3, Ch69 L315-317). Magic Society investigators later find that every racial gift of an outworlder woman evolved through a blessing, each from a different great astral being, most commonly from the Reaper and the Celestial Book and rarely from the World-Phoenix (Book 7, Ch11 L73-75). The Builder "only blesses its own people" (Book 7, Ch11 L78). Farrah describes feeling "all these blessings ready to evolve my new outworlder ones" (Book 4, Ch58 L19).
- **Sophie's Boundary Breaker**: her celestine racial gift Astral Affinity evolves into Boundary Breaker in a fight (Book 3, Ch68 L13-16), where Clive calls it a likely "rank-jumping power".

### A god's blessing on a place

- The Healer gives Jory's clinic his blessing and declares it a sanctuary (Book 1, Ch95 L120, L128). Compare Church_of_the_Healer.md.

### Names given by enemies

- **The Rejector**: Builder cultists call Jason "the Rejector" (Book 3, Ch32 L105), a living demonstration that the Builder's power is not absolute (Book 3, Ch34 L7). Jason finds it "not a cool nickname" (Book 3, Ch32 L106).

## 4. Not in the text

The full list of titles a person can earn is not given; only Jason's and Delia's are shown. Blessings from the Legion and the All-Devouring Eye are described as not handed out (Book 7, Ch11 L75).
