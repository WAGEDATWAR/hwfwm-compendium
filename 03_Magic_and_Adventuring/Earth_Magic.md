## 1. Summary

Earth has the same magic as Pallimustus in a much thinner form. Through most of its history essences and monsters did not exist there at all; magic leaked in through short-lived proto-astral spaces, and a small number of secret societies used the powers that people could earn from them. When Jason returns in Book 4, Earth's magic is low and rising, with a planetary Grid of rituals monitoring and containing the leaks. The monster waves of Book 5 and the transformation zones that follow end that system. By Book 11 the rules have changed.

## 2. How it works

**The old magic.** Craig Vermillion: "Magic has always been a difficult and esoteric thing. Some five centuries ago, a new kind of magic appeared. People with no connection to the old ways could suddenly wield a variety of easy-to-use mystical powers. At that time, they were a limited threat. They were collected into various secret societies around the world, hoarding their knowledge" (Book 4, Ch11 L4).

**Tiers.** The shared numeric rank system runs from zero up: "Whether you call them tiers, categories, realms, or ranks, like you, the same numbers are recognised across the board" (Book 4, Ch11 L8). Tier zero is people without enough magic to "cross the first, transformative threshold" (Book 4, Ch11 L10). Blood servants can reach the first or second tier temporarily by ingesting vampire blood (Book 4, Ch11 L14). Vermillion says he and Jason are "tier two of five", that he has heard of tier-four Cabal figures who "entered forms of hibernation, hidden away for centuries", and that the fifth tier is "the limit of mortal power" (Book 4, Ch11 L19-22). In Network usage the ranks are called categories after spirit coin colours: category one is iron, two bronze, three silver (Book 4, Ch21 L33; Book 4, Ch31 L48). See `Ranks_and_Advancement.md`.

**The Grid.** The Grid is the Network's planet-wide monitor of dimensional incursions. "The grid infrastructure isn't like a normal formation array of permanent ritual circles. Each node is enormous and not made from a ritual circle at all. It's like the landscape is somehow operating as a series of interconnected rituals, creating a magical array from the natural features of the world itself. We're talking about nodes the size of cities, with elements made up of mountains, hills and rivers to form a magical array covering the entire planet" (Book 5, Ch5 L11). Its "express purpose is to identify and monitor dimensional spaces" (Book 4, Ch51 L35). It is "designed to be accessible to anyone with the requisite knowledge", so that once Jason knows about it he may tap into it himself (Book 4, Ch43 L33). It registers events by category: an incident in Book 4 "registered as being above category five" (Book 4, Ch5 L13). A local branch once hid the Saint-Étienne astral space by disabling the Grid in that area and "claiming that there was an infrastructure collapse" (Book 4, Ch51 L35).

**Where magic comes from.** Dawn explains: "The link between worlds ... It's been siphoning off magic from Pallimustus to this world for centuries. It was slow, at first, but the rate of transfer has been rapidly escalating over the last century and a half. Each proto-space that breaks down without the anchor creatures being destroyed deposits its magic into your world... after centuries, the magical density of your world has started to rise. This strengthens the link, which feeds the loop" (Book 4, Ch68 L59-61). The founders of the Grid and the Network "failed to prepare a response to proto-spaces appearing deep in the oceans", so the Network "slows the rate at which your world's magical density has risen" without stopping it (Book 4, Ch68 L63-65). See `Astral_Spaces_Zones_and_Surges.md` for the proto-spaces themselves.

**Supplies.** Earth has no spirit coin farms: "Earth doesn't have the magic and coin formation takes months, so you can't do it in the proto-astral spaces" (Book 4, Ch37 L79). Quintessence is called affinity gems: "We call them affinity gems. I'm pretty sure the Network is the main supplier" (Book 4, Ch23 L26). The Network gives its executive staff essences to reach category one and reserves cores for frontline staff (Book 4, Ch36 L77). Magical technology exists, built on leaning "on the magic as little as possible. Let the technology do the work and use magic to skip over the places where the tech would otherwise bottleneck" (Book 4, Ch46 L90).

**Characters on Earth.** Essence users on Earth are mostly category one to three in Book 4 (Book 4, Ch16 L108; Book 4, Ch18 L122). Vampires are the Cabal's classic power; the Engineers of Ascension are a radical group (see `05_Organisations/Earth_Powers.md`).

## 3. Variations and exceptions

- **Low-magic rule.** Entering the vicinity of a proto-astral aperture raises ambient magic enough that "your recovery rates will remain at normal levels without spirit coin consumption" (Book 4, Ch39 L55); outside such places Earth's magic is thin.
- **Core use as policy.** Network members are given cores according to role and rank rather than by ability (Book 4, Ch36 L77).
- **Training divergence.** The Network's own training is human-derived; Jason and Farrah introduce training "from the ground up" in Book 5 (Book 5, Ch1 L51-53).

## 5. Common misconceptions in-world

- **Earth has no magic.** It has a trickle, increasingly large (Book 4, Ch68 L59-61).
- **The Network can contain it.** Jason concludes that the Network's mission of containment "was completely stuffed from the start" because the oceans hide leaks, and Dawn agrees (Book 4, Ch68 L63-65).
- **Tier five is a myth.** Vermillion has "never seen proof" of it (Book 4, Ch11 L21).

## 6. Changes over time

- **Level 2:** In Book 5, grid failures and monster waves begin (Book 5, Ch14 L3; Book 5, Ch16 L78); the Network becomes publicly known as the Global Defense Network (Book 5, Ch17 L7). Zones such as Kostroma remake places (Book 5, Ch44 L48-52).
- **Level 3:** After Jason's second zone, "the base level of magic had risen; magic no longer manifested in proto-astral spaces before spilling into the world. He had begun a new age of magic, where monsters and essences manifested directly in the world" (Book 11, Ch1 L16). Many normal-rank people on Earth are transformed into other races (Book 11, Ch1 L36). By Book 13 the Asano clan's training and the Quilido Procedure give it influence over national essence-user programmes (Book 13, Ch1001 L8-9).
