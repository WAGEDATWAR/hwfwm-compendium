## 1. Summary

The appendices of Book 12 are the only complete statement of one character's essence abilities across all ranks. They are used here as a worked example of how abilities are built: each ability gains one new effect at each rank, and many effects add an affliction or a boon that other abilities then use. The author notes that the list "is written for brevity (believe it or not) rather than accuracy or comprehensiveness" (Book 12, Ch67 L203). This file records the facts and adds no numbers.

## 4. Gifts (Astral Nexus)

- **Prime Avatar**: a gestalt without a soul, "a mortal anchor for transcendent power"; its power is limited to Jason's mortal power (Book 12, Ch67 L195).
- **Numen**: spiritual domains, Akashic Speech, stripping remnant magic from killed magic entities, immunity to rank suppression and to detection, tracking and assessment magic, and negation of aura abilities by fully suppressing the user's aura (Book 12, Ch67 L196).
- **System Administrator**: full access to the System, with maps, voice and image chat, party and raid features and assessment; these can be granted to a party, and Jason can open another's interface with permission or after suppressing their aura (Book 12, Ch67 L197).
- **Relics of the King**: limited access to the astral throne, astral gate and soul forge, stabilising dimensional spaces and exceeding portal limits at a cost (Book 12, Ch67 L198).
- **Sacred Phoenix**: soul abilities refined for the avatar, ghost fire added to afflictions, and on lethal damage a transformation into a ghost fire phoenix that cannot be reused for one year, reduced by absorbing life force (Book 12, Ch67 L199).
- **Palanquin**: Jason's familiars become transport; the blood familiar reinforces a form and the doom familiar adds offence and defence (Book 12, Ch67 L200).
