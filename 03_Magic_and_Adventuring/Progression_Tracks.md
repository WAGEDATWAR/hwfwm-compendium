## 1. Summary

This file records, for Jason and a few teammates, the first chapter where each rank, essence and ability appears. Jason's lines come from the system messages that announce them. For Jason's full gold-rank power set, see Jason_Power_Set_Reference.md. "First shown" means the first line the compendium found, which may come a chapter after an unreported awakening.

## 2. Jason's four essences

| Order | Essence | First line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dark | "You have absorbed [Dark Essence]. You have absorbed 1 of 4 essences" (Book 1, Ch8 L32) |
| 2 | Blood | "You have absorbed [Blood Essence]. You have absorbed 2 of 4 essences" (Book 1, Ch21 L4) |
| 3 | Sin | "You have absorbed [Sin Essence]. You have absorbed 3 of 4 essences" (Book 1, Ch21 L47) |
| 4 | Doom | The confluence of his essences produces the Doom essence, which he may claim or reject; absorbed as 4 of 4 (Book 1, Ch21 L54-59) |

The Doom essence is a confluence essence, and it bonds to his Spirit attribute (Book 1, Ch21 L61).
