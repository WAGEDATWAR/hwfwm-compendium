## 1. Summary

An affliction is a condition that harms its target for a time; a boon is a condition that helps. Both are named in brackets in System messages, carry tags (poison, disease, curse, holy, unholy, magic and others) and can stack. Defence against afflictions is a game of resisting, cleansing and negating: a cleanse removes an affliction, resistance reduces the chance or strength of one landing, and some afflictions are built to defeat both. In this world a character who understands which tool beats which condition has a large tactical advantage, and Jason's power set is built around that interplay.

## 2. How it works

**Naming and tags.** Afflictions and boons appear in brackets with tags: "[Leech Toxin] (affliction, poison, blood, stacking)" (Book 1, Ch29 L15); "[Mark of Sin] (affliction, holy)" (Book 1, Ch87 L99); "[Resistant] (boon, holy)" (Book 12, Ch67 L303). A tag may be a type of harm (poison, disease, curse, wounding), a moral colour (holy or unholy) or the nature of the effect (magic, drain, stacking).

**Stacking.** Many conditions stack, each "instance" adding to the effect, as for Vulnerable: "Additional instances have a cumulative effect" (Book 1, Ch88 L132). Some stacks decay, like Penance, "Stacks drop off over time" (Book 12, Ch67 L281).

**Resistance.** Resistance is a chance and strength modifier. Jason's Sin Eater increases his own resistance to afflictions and gives him Resistant each time he resists or cleanses one (Book 1, Ch28 L93). His Hegemony aura raises allies' resistance and lowers enemies', and lowers it further for each instance of Sin (Book 1, Ch40 L41). In battle only a fraction land: "as many as one in three afflictions were taking hold" against a flesh monster in Book 2 (Book 2, Ch52 L51), and "almost half" against a lizard after resistance-lowering stacks (Book 3, Ch8 L76).

**Cleansing.** A cleanse removes afflictions. Jason's Feast of Absolution removes "curses, diseases, poisons and unholy afflictions", plus holy afflictions if the target is an ally, from a target other than himself, and it "circumvents all effects that prevent cleansing" (Book 1, Ch34 L40); a few chapters later, at Iron 1 (19%), the same spell removes holy and unholy afflictions from an ally or an enemy and "circumvents any resistance or immunity to cleanse effects" (Book 1, Ch39 L70-71). Cleansing triggers other abilities: "Cleansing afflictions has triggered [Sin Eater]" (Book 1, Ch34 L47). A cleansing aura, such as Cleansing Breeze, adds resistance to curses, diseases, magic afflictions, poisons and unholy afflictions and negates poisons in the air; it is a holy effect (Book 2, Ch9 L119).

**Cleanse conditions.** Many afflictions are written to resist cleansing: Mark of Sin "cannot be cleansed while target retains any instances of Sin or Legacy of Sin" (Book 1, Ch87 L99). A curse can protect another: a patient arrives with two curses, one adding poison to the other, and "the curse that kept making more of the other two couldn't be cleansed until the other curse was cleansed" (Book 1, Ch103 L130).

**Negating immunity.** Some afflictions remove an immunity: Weakness of the Flesh "negates immunities to disease and necrotic damage" and that "includes intrinsic immunities, such as from not having a biology or corporeal form" (Book 5, Ch31 L181). Blood From a Stone does the same for blood and poison, so "entities without blood can bleed" (Book 5, Ch31 L176), and Mortality does it for curses, even for the not alive (Book 5, Ch31 L179). Inevitable Demise, an aura Knowledge tried to give him, treated "any affliction immunities, including inherent immunities, as complete resistance" (Book 2, Ch13 L69).

**Healing interaction.** Bleeding "soaks up healing, negating its effect" and is removed only after absorbing enough; Leech Toxin reapplies it when negated, "leaving any healing stopped cold" (Book 1, Ch29 L15-16). Weight of Sin deals transcendent damage to anyone subjected to a holy boon, recovery or cleanse effect (Book 12, Ch67 L289); Sanction reduces healing (Book 12, Ch67 L339).

**Holy and unholy.** Effects carry a holy or unholy tag, and some effects cannot touch them. Hendren's ongoing cleansing powers "could not eliminate holy effects", and he had only one power that could (Book 3, Ch59 L38). Another ability grants resistance to curses, magic and unholy afflictions, and bars unholy boons (Book 3, Ch67 L93). Hegemony is "both a holy and unholy ability at the same time", which fascinates Jason (Book 1, Ch40 L42).

## 3. Damage types

- **Physical and elemental.** Normal cutting, piercing, fire and similar damage. Rank gives damage reduction against lower-rank sources (Book 1, Ch21 L67).
- **Necrotic.** The damage type of rot, drains and death magic. Sin, Creeping Death and many of Jason's afflictions raise or deal it (Book 12, Ch67 L215, L278).
- **Disruptive force and resonating force.** Two force types for special cases. Spell Breaker adds disruptive-force damage, "highly effective against magical defences and incorporeal entities" (Book 1, Ch56 L54); Stone Cutter adds resonating-force damage, "highly effective against physical defences" (Book 1, Ch56 L53). A defensive gift can reduce disruptive-force damage by a large amount (Book 2, Ch7 L162). An unarmed ability adds resonating-force damage and negates damage from actively intercepted attacks (Book 2, Ch8 L44). Gary's hammer for fighting constructs delivers "a resonating-force explosion" with each hit (Book 7, Ch31 L10).
- **Transcendent damage.** "Transcendent damage ignores all forms of physical and magical defence, damage reduction and resistances" (Book 1, Ch99 L169). Vincent is astonished at seeing an iron-rank ability deal it (Book 1, Ch99 L166). Jason's Castigate at iron already deals "slight transcendent damage" (Book 1, Ch87 L97), and execute effects like Verdict scale it with injury (Book 12, Ch67 L335).

## 5. Common misconceptions in-world

- **Healing beats damage.** Bleeding soaks up healing and Leech Toxin reapplies it (Book 1, Ch29 L16).
- **Cleansing is always available.** Afflictions like Mark of Sin and Persecution are written to block it (Book 1, Ch87 L99; Book 12, Ch67 L317).
- **Immunity is permanent.** Weakness of the Flesh, Blood From a Stone and Mortality cancel even intrinsic immunities (Book 5, Ch31 L176-181).
- **Rank is a wall.** Resistance and damage reduction from rank can be bypassed by transcendent damage (Book 1, Ch99 L169).

## 6. Changes over time

- **Level 1:** Early iron-rank afflictions are described plainly, like Vulnerable's "All resistances are reduced" (Book 1, Ch88 L132). Later versions gain extra clauses, such as the cleanse restriction and the intrinsic-immunity text (Book 5, Ch31 L176-181).
- **Level 2:** A speaker in Book 13 says Bussinger's Tribulation Lightning purges her own afflictions at the cost of harming her (Book 13, Ch980 L93).
- **Level 3:** By the end of Book 12 Jason's ability set is built around holy-tagged transcendent effects, such as Penance, Weight of Sin and Sanction (Book 12, Ch67 L281, L289, L339).
