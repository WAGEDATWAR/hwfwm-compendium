## 1. Summary

Clive is the team's battlemage and ritualist: Magic, Rune, Balance and the Karmic confluence (Book 2, Ch28 L56). His set is built on one big spell, retributive karma effects that punish attackers, ritual circles and mana support. Spells are his focus; he uses magical weapons like staves and wands and works with his familiar (Book 2, Ch28 L56).

## 3. Racial gifts

- **Mana Equilibrium**: balances ambient magic around him; he uses it before rituals (Book 1, Ch85 L17; Book 1, Ch103 L44).
- **Human Ambition to Thirst For Knowledge**: essence abilities advance more quickly and he can learn information through skill books (Book 2, Ch61 L144; Book 2, Ch62 L27-30).
- **A blessing from the Celestial Book** turned his Special Attack Affinity into a spell affinity (see Clive_Standish.md; Book 1, Ch85 L22-26).
- **Spirit Reaper** (magic): "special attack (melee, dimension, drain)" inflicting extra disruptive-force damage and draining mana (Book 2, Ch64 L75). The line introducing it names Humphrey, but the system message calls it a magic essence ability and Humphrey has no magic essence (Book 2, Ch64 L72-73); it is listed here for that reason, and Open_Questions does not track it.

## 5. How the set plays

He sets up before a fight and refreshes: the rune circle that triggers effects when attacked and the damage-reflecting mantle (Book 2, Ch30 L53), then uses Wrath of the Magister as his big attack (Book 3, Ch4 L33; Book 3, Ch10 L23). Neil's shields boost retribution damage, so Clive's karma powers work best behind them (Book 8, Ch7 L60). At silver the text says Instant Karma "had long been the power most difficult for him to raise, as he was simply bad at using it" (Book 8, Ch7 L48).
