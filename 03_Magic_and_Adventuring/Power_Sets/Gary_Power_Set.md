## 1. Summary

Gary is a leonid smith-warrior: hammer, fire, iron and the forge confluence (Book 9, Ch82 L22). His set is a conjured suit of armour, a heavy hammer and shield, a forge golem he summons from iron filings, and forge powers for repair and alloys. The Book 10 change replaces the set with divine essences and a demigod confluence.

## 2. Powers

- **Armour and weapons**: conjured full-body armour of dark steel plates held together with large bolts, engraved with runes (Book 1, Ch16 L28); he fights with a huge hammer and a shield (Book 1, Ch109 L14). He forges a hammer himself to fight constructs (Book 2, Ch76 L45).
- **Forge golem**: he pours iron filings into a circle, his hand bursts into flame, and a three-metre golem of ugly black iron rises with molten metal between the joints (Book 1, Ch17 L56-61). The text calls it a foundry golem when he names it (Book 1, Ch17 L61) and a forge golem later (Book 2, Ch28 L46; Book 3, Ch6 L35). It is a temporary summon, unlike a familiar (Book 2, Ch28 L46). Summoning powers need ritual understanding, as for Farrah (Book 1, Ch23 L37).
- **Forge powers**: he uses a forge essence power to repair a wagon wheel (Book 1, Ch31 L40), and creates "alloys heavier and stronger than steel" for the academy annex (Book 3, Ch26 L9).
- **Smithing**: he forges Jason's Dread Salvation (Book 4, Ch70 L44; see Hegemons_Will_and_Dominion.md for its later reforging).

- **Rescue work**: in the Book 9 surge he and other craftspeople shape stone and reinforce structures, and his golem's chest plate opens to carry survivors (Book 9, Ch82 L22-24).

## 3. The divine change (Book 10)

When Gary absorbs divine power belonging to the god of heroes, the System replaces his set (Book 10, Ch86 L60-75):

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Hammer essence | Divine Hammer (abilities reach Gold 0) |
| Fire essence | Divine Fire |
| Iron essence | Divine Iron |
| Forge confluence | Demigod |
| Craftsman's Gaze | All-Seeing Eye |
| Stoke the Forge | First Son of the Leonids |
| Inspector's Eye | Vessel of the Ancestors |
| Refinement Process | Divine Forge |
| Hand that Holds the Hammer | Hero |

The text also records an earlier list for him as "fire and iron essences" (Book 10, Ch67 L122) and "earth, iron and forge" (Book 10, Ch79 L63); the System text in Ch86 and the list in Book 9, Ch82 L22 are the fullest and are used here. See Essence_Catalogue.md and Hero.md for the god's side.
