## 1. Summary

Sophie is a skill-based speed fighter: a celestine whose Swift, Balance and Wind essences form the Mystic confluence. Her set stacks movement, perception and unarmed techniques around one idea, acting faster and more precisely than the enemy. Before Jason's help she had only the Swift essence and its single ability for more than half a dozen years (Book 2, Ch5 L37-41).

## 2. Essences and confluence

- **Choice**: Swift, Balance and Wind, producing the Mystic confluence, which Clive names when Rufus asks (Book 2, Ch5 L82-87). She absorbs Wind, Balance and Mystic in order (Book 2, Ch7 L118, L123, L152).
- **Stones**: five awakening stones are chosen for the team after the Reaper trials; Sophie takes a stone of the celestials on Constance's advice, and Clive hands her his karma stone (Book 2, Ch63 L25, L30-31). She also trades a death essence for two epic stones of the moment, and picks uncommon stones of preparation and of the surge for a skill-oriented set (Book 2, Ch65 L96-98).

## 5. How the set plays

Wind Blade and Wind Wave give her ranged and mobility options, Free Runner, Leaf on the Wind and Cloud Step carry her across walls, water and air, and the Mystic fist abilities turn her unarmed strikes into attacks that bypass physical and magical defences. Clive argued that stones of the moment suit a skill-based power set because their abilities "require precise timing but were incredibly impactful" (Book 2, Ch65 L97).
