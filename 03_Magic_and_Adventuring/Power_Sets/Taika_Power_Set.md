## 1. Summary

Taika is a bird-shifter and flyer: a garuda-confluence adventurer whose set is built on speed, momentum and a giant-bird form. By Book 9 his power set is in line with Humphrey's, sharing the might and wing essences, where Humphrey's confluence is dragon and Taika's is garuda (Book 9, Ch33 L15). Taika's first plan for the garuda confluence began with a bird essence (Book 4, Ch65 L22-27).

## 3. Late development

- By Book 13 he advances his Wing and Garuda essences by flying with migratory birds for days or weeks in bird form, and develops his Swift essence alongside Sophie (Book 13, Ch997 L38).

## 4. How the set plays

Taika builds Momentum with charges and speed, converts it with Unstoppable Strike and Speed and Power, and stays aloft in bird form. Amrita supports allies. The Book 9 chapter shows the whole set at Silver 0 right after the surge, when he is "on the very cusp of silver" in the character file's reading (Book 9, Ch45 L10).

## 5. Not in the text

The details of Unbowed, his Brother of the Dawn, Emerald Flesh, Golden Wings and Feaster of Serpents abilities beyond their names in King of the Sky are not shown in the lines read.
