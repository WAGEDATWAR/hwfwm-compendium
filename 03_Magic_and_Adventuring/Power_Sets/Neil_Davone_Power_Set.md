## 1. Summary

Neil is the team's healer and shield-caster: Shield, Growth and Renewal, producing the Prosperity confluence (Jason's notebook, Book 2, Ch28 L44). His set is built around timed, short-lived shields, buffs on allies and ritual healing, and Prosperity luck effects. Rufus is struck by the shielding: the shields "lasted only moments and would end after absorbing only a single attack", so they need good judgement and timing (Book 2, Ch30 L52).

## 5. How the set plays

He keeps buffs running (armour of renewal, which reduces damage taken and heals over time, and the size-growth power, Book 2, Ch30 L53) and times shield bubbles for teammates; Humphrey trusts Neil's shield so completely that he does not even block (Book 8, Ch7 L49). His shield's retribution boost makes Clive's effects stronger (Book 8, Ch7 L60). Hero's Moment is a team-wide trick with a price: when it wears off, the recipients suffer "debilitating effects as potent as the boosts" (Book 3, Ch59 L101).
