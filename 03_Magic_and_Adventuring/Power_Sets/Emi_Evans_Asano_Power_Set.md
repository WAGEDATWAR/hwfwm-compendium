## 1. Summary

Emi is a ritual specialist: a teenager who masters ritual magic before she has essences, then takes Vast, Myriad and Harmonic, whose Resonant confluence feeds a set of stacked ritual-circle shields and mana recycling. She is Jason's niece (see Emi_Evans_Asano.md).

## 2. Before essences

- **Ritual magic**: "greater command of ritual magic than most people on Earth" before she had essences (Book 11, Ch68 L817). Travis names her and Hiro as people with strong magical-theory knowledge, taught by Farrah (Book 7, Ch55 L35).
- **A specialty**: formation interactivity (Book 5, Ch54 L8, L13).

## 3. Essences and abilities

- **Essences**: Vast, Myriad and Harmonic, "two legendaries and an epic", which she expects to produce the Unity confluence; Rufus performs the rite and she absorbs all three at once (Book 11, Ch68 L926-929, L940). Her confluence is Resonant (Book 11, Ch68 L1568).
- **Combat rituals**: she is "the one person on Earth able to use them", with stacked ritual-circle shields whose synergy magnifies each shield (Book 11, Ch68 L1695-1698).
- **Consolidate Mana**: turns broken rituals into power for her next spell (Book 11, Ch68 L1702).
- **Cube devices**: nine cubes of dark metal from the outermost reaches of the astral space orbit her, each face divided into nine rune sections (Book 11, Ch68 L2343); they keep her and others aloft (Book 13, Ch950 L27). She builds cube constructs with Clive and Gary (Book 13, Ch953 L84).

## 4. Rank

Bronze when she faces a silver-rank manticore with her team (Book 11, Ch68 L1698), silver by the rooftop scene (Book 11, Ch68 L2345) and still in the upper reaches of silver in Book 13, because she favours magic research over combat (Book 13, Ch998 L4).

## 5. How the set plays

She wins fights through preparation: shields and rituals set before an enemy arrives, mana recycled through Consolidate Mana. She refuses monster cores (Book 11, Ch68 L909-913).
