## 1. Summary

Rufus is a swordsman-teleporter built on the sun and moon: a skilled fighter whose powers blend movement, teleportation, illusory after-images and attacks. In the Greenstone years he fights as a bronze-ranker with a golden and a silver sword; by Book 9 his essence is described as the eclipse confluence, and his combat shifts between sun, moon and eclipse states.

## 2. Powers (Books 1 and 2)

- **Conjured swords**: a golden sword and a silver sword that appear in his hand (Book 1, Ch108 L96-98; Book 1, Ch112 L44). The swords leave marks: those cut by the golden sword have a small orb of fire, "a miniature golden sun", floating around them and scorching them; those touched by the silver sword have a tiny moon that soaks up heat instead (Book 2, Ch76 L51).
- **Speed of light**: when he activates it "the world seemed to freeze in time around him", giving "only two seconds of accelerated time" (Book 1, Ch108 L99); the text calls it "one of his strongest trump cards" (Book 1, Ch109 L23).
- **Movement chain**: unlike Jason, he does not have one teleport he can use over and over; "he had a slew of powers that blended movement, teleportation, illusionary after-images and attacks" and chaining them takes skill and practice (Book 2, Ch76 L66).
- **Water-walking boots** are noted in his character file (Book 1, Ch16 L38).
- **Kingdom of Eclipse**: his incantation-driven power, whose chant ends "bring forth the kingdom of eclipse" (Book 2, Ch76 L71); it fires an eclipse orb that kills dozens (Book 2, Ch76; see Rufus_Remore.md).

## 3. At silver (Books 9 and 10)

- **The three states**: "Rufus had the eclipse confluence essence." The sun state is built around speed and offence, the moon state around elusiveness and stealth, and the eclipse state offers powerful but short-term buffs or powerful finishers; each state combines how he fights, moves and which powers he uses (Book 9, Ch20 L12-13).
- **Light of the Sun, Shadow of the Moon**: one of several abilities that change with his state; in the moon state it can make him intangible for a few seconds (Book 9, Ch20 L14).
- **Teleports**: two short-range teleports, Moonlit Step and Flash Step, help him navigate collapsing buildings (Book 9, Ch79 L21). He teleports "in a flash of silvery moonlight" (Book 10, Ch22 L68).
- **Eclipse zone**: his zone power, Eclipse, consumes the afflictions of the gold and silver flames he leaves on monsters (Book 9, Ch88 L53). His gold and silver swords leave gold and silver flames in his wake (Book 9, Ch89 L24).
- **Speed**: the sun essence gives him "flashes of brilliant speed", and Soramir says personal time acceleration is common, with Rufus getting his from the sun essence (Book 11, Ch68 L1304; Book 9, Ch20 L16).
- **Mobility**: silver-rank acrobatics and short-range teleports let him spend little time on the ground (Book 9, Ch89 L22).

## 4. How the set plays

He is a skirmisher who strikes, vanishes and re-appears; the moon state lets him dodge and the sun state lets him close the gap. In a duel Soramir notes he baited his opponent by burning one of his strongest abilities on a statement attack (Book 9, Ch20 L20).

## 5. Not in the text

No System window for Rufus's abilities is shown in the lines read; his essence list is the Sun essence (Book 9, Ch20 L16; Essence_Catalogue.md) with the eclipse confluence (Book 9, Ch20 L12), and the other essences are not stated here.
