## 1. Summary

Belinda is the team's trickster and utility caster: Magic, Trap and Adept, producing the Charlatan confluence (Book 2, Ch32 L67-72). Her set borrows other people's powers, shapes herself into other roles, sets traps and teleports allies. She is delighted by her own power: "I was looking through the abilities it's known to give and they sound fantastic" (Book 2, Ch32 L72, quoted in the character file).

## 5. How the set plays

Belinda is a force multiplier: she combines her tether and pit (the force tether drags the lizards into the pit while the pit damages them, Book 3, Ch11 L58-60), swaps cooldowns onto allies and borrows spells. She teaches Jason aura disguises, her power giving her an edge in learning them (Book 8, Ch29 L49-56, from the character file).
