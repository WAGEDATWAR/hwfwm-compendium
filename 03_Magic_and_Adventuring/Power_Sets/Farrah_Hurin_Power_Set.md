## 1. Summary

Farrah is a volcano-themed battlemage and ritualist: Earth, Fire and Potent, which form the Volcano confluence (Book 1, Ch19 L30). She fights in glossy obsidian armour with a sword that becomes a lava whip, burning orbs and lava spells, and her ritual magic underlies the team's summons and later the Grid repairs.

## 2. Powers

- **Obsidian armour**: heavy obsidian armour that glows with internal heat (Book 1, Ch109 L12); a full set of glossy obsidian shards swept into wing shapes with a red glow between the segments (Book 4, Ch61 L18).
- **Sword**: a huge obsidian sword whose blade is "a pillar of jagged segments" that can break up and whip around on a cord of glowing magma (Book 1, Ch108 L94). Unextended it is a jagged-edge greatsword; as a whip-sword the teeth are strung on a flexible cord of red-hot lava (Book 5, Ch60 L46).
- **Lava cannon**: a showstopping power that "devoured mana like pigs with a fresh corpse" (Book 4, Ch41 L31); she uses it to devastate a team of silver-rankers (Book 5, Ch50 L36).
- **Orbs**: three flaming orbs manifest over her head and shoot fiery beams (Book 4, Ch61 L34); at the Network fight they are "searing flame" orbs (Book 5, Ch50 L38).
- **Magma and obsidian terrain**: a glowing sigil on the ground produces a magma geyser (Book 4, Ch61 L33), a curtain of obsidian shards from a stomp (Book 4, Ch61 L34), an obsidian wall that explodes into shards (Book 5, Ch60 L37), and a spike that impales (Book 5, Ch60 L48).
- **Finisher**: she turns the obsidian fragments left in an enemy into lava (Book 5, Ch60 L68).
- **Magma elemental summon**: a pile of black and red magma with arms, summoned from a ritual circle like Gary's golem (Book 1, Ch17 L72).
- **Ritual magic**: she adjusts circles for ambient magic (Book 1, Ch23 L15-17) and teaches Emi, Hiro and others magical theory (Book 7, Ch55 L35).

## 3. Late development (Book 12)

- Farrah is offered the choice of becoming a Voice of the Will or a Defier of the Will; she accepts the Defier path and becomes immortal, still "a step or two from gold" (Book 12, Ch21 L43-46, per Farrah_Hurin.md).

## 4. How the set plays

She is a front-line brawler in heavy armour with a sweeping weapon. Jason notes she has a "peak bronze-rank speed attribute" and sweeping attacks that clear clusters but lacks mobility (Book 4, Ch70 L48). Against a gold-ranked vampire, her sword hits "nothing but air" because of the speed difference (Book 5, Ch60 L50).

## 5. Not in the text

No System window for Farrah's abilities is shown in the lines read, so the ability names beyond "lava cannon" and "Volcano" and their ranks are not stated.
