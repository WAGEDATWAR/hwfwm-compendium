## 1. Summary

Humphrey is the team's heavy hitter: "simple, reliable power" (Book 3, Ch43 L69). His dragon, wing and might essences give him a dragon-winged flyer with fire, a weapon-heavy attack set and racial gifts that scale with risk. His familiar is the mirage dragon Stash.

## 2. Essences and confluence

- **Dragon confluence**: reached through the might and wing essences (Book 9, Ch33 L15; Essence_Catalogue.md lists him with might, wing and dragon, Book 4, Ch65 L28).
- **Dragon abilities**: he awakens all five dragon essence abilities when he gets Spartoi, the party interface reporting it (Book 1, Ch88 L94-96).

## 4. Racial gifts

- **Hero's Drive** (from Human Ambition): essence abilities advance faster; higher-ranked enemies gain no extra resistance or damage reduction against his abilities; cooldowns are reduced when fighting higher-ranked or much more numerous enemies; mana and stamina gained over time (Book 1, Ch88 L25-30).
- **Attack of the Mirage Dragon** (from Special Attack Affinity): the evolution is reported for him in Book 2 (Book 2, Ch61 L147; Book 2, Ch62 L53). Neil asks "Why mirage dragon?" and Humphrey answers that Stash is a mirage dragon (Book 2, Ch62 L49-50).
- **Essence Gift evolutions**: Dragon Blood enhances Power and Spirit for moderate mana per second (Book 3, Ch43 L42-44); Wing Raider (Book 3, Ch43 L54); Unstoppable makes movement abilities impossible to negate or impair and imparts resonating-force and disruptive-force damage to obstructing objects (Book 3, Ch43 L70-72). The text shows it triggering against a Sphere of Incarceration (Book 3, Ch43 L66-68).
- **Hero's Sacrifice**: his evolved gift breaches his soul's limits: "Sacrifice your health to enhance the power of your special attacks", and the earlier effects of Attack of the Mirage Dragon are lost (Book 3, Ch68 L68-73).

## 5. Later (Book 9)

- **Razor-Wing Sword** (wing, silver 4, 11%): a conjured sword shaped like a wing; movement powers are enhanced while wielding it; feathers can be thrown as projectiles at bronze and animated to intercept physical projectiles at silver (Book 9, Ch59 L71).
- **Dragon Might** (dragon): an aura (recovery) (Book 9, Ch59 L82). Separately, his dragon essence racial gift lets him burn mana to increase his physical and magical strength (Book 2, Ch62 L47).

## 6. How the set plays

He opens with Dragon Wings and Dive Bomb or Flying Leap, stacks Unstoppable Force or Relentless Assault into a single hit and relies on teammates for shields: Neil's shielding and healing and Clive's retributive effects let him leave openings to attack (Book 2, Ch30 L54). Compare Taika's garuda set, built on the same might and wing essences (Taika_Power_Set.md).

## 7. Not in the text

The effects of Hero's Drive, Dragon Might and his gold-rank rewrites beyond the lines read are not shown here; the character file notes Spartoi at gold as a hundred dragon-tooth warriors (notes, Book 13).
