## 1. Summary

A confluence is the fourth essence a person receives. It is not picked, bought or found. It is produced by the three essences already absorbed, and it arrives the moment the third is used. In the main run of the series a given trio of essences always gives the same confluence, and the Magic Society keeps a list of the known trios. Confluences are rated and talked about as if they were essences in their own right, and many of the best-known power sets belong to a confluence.

## 2. How it works

**Arrival.** Farrah explains that "once you use your third essence, a fourth one manifests itself on the spot" and that confluences are "a result of the three essences you already have"; her own fire, earth and potent gave the volcano essence (Book 1, Ch19 L30). Confluences "only manifest after three essences are used", and cannot be found loose: "You can't find a volcano essence anywhere" (Book 1, Ch19 L31).

**The scene.** When Jason uses his third essence, three shimmering cubes rise from his torso and merge into one new cube; the System tells him the "confluence of your essences has produced the doom essence", which he "may claim or reject", and that a refused confluence "will not be available to you again" (Book 1, Ch21 L53-54). Rufus's practical advice is that refusing means one "can only replace it with a normal essence" and "I promise you that is not the way to go" (Book 1, Ch21 L57). Only the clergy refuse, because their gods give them divine versions of regular essences (Book 1, Ch21 L57).

**Looking them up.** The Magic Society's living document carries every essence and every combination it knows. A person can look an essence up to see what combinations are known and which are restricted (Book 1, Ch20 L97-104). Jason's dark, blood and sin is found there and produces doom, which is not restricted (Book 1, Ch20 L110). Clive can name a result from memory, as he does for swift, balance and wind (Book 2, Ch5 L83). The Society's common-combinations list is the basis for many choices, such as Ian's ministration confluence (Book 4, Ch71 L30). A larger list of "thousands of known essence combinations" is offered for sale to Jason by the Network's envoy (Book 4, Ch35 L123).

**Many routes, one result.** Some confluences are produced by many combinations. Mystic is common because "so many combinations produce it" and because "a lot of useful abilities come out of the mystic essence" (Book 2, Ch5 L87). Undeath is produced by "a wide variety of combinations, almost all of which involve the death essence" (Book 1, Ch20 L19). Garuda has at least two routes, might, swift and bird (Book 4, Ch65 L22), and might and wing, which Jason secures instead of the plan to give Taika renewal and the immortal confluence (Book 4, Ch64 L57; Book 4, Ch65 L28).

**Confluence abilities come late.** It is "quite unusual to awaken the confluence essence abilities first", and some advocates of a theory say people with a very strong sense of self do it (Book 2, Ch8 L45-49).

**What a confluence does to a person.** A confluence can carry a racial or personal gift with it. Kylie's vision confluence lets her awaken a perception power from each essence, "where other essence users only had the one" (Book 4, Ch42 L44). An aura power common to the force confluence is able to move things physically with an aura, which is unusual (Book 8, Ch60 L5).

## 3. Variations and exceptions

- **Adventurer's confluences.** Farrah calls chimera "an adventurer's confluence", unsuited to a civilian such as Kaito, who is given a combination of common essences: vehicle, wind and swift, forming the soaring confluence, a known non-combat flight combination (Book 4, Ch71 L4-5, L36).
- **Surprising results from the user.** Greg's magitech confluence did not look like the Network's high-tech magitech users; Jason and Ketevan remark that he ended "all steampunk Tesla" (Book 5, Ch7 L64-66). The same confluence can play very differently.
- **Rare essences, common results.** Sword, myriad and adept give master as readily as sword, swift and adept, in a different style: Lance, a spell-using elf, shares the confluence with Valdis, who relies on special attacks (Book 3, Ch1 L89-90).
- **A deliberate choice.** Jason and Farrah pick sets by the kind of power wanted. Erika is given feast, knife and dance, producing bounty, a food-magic confluence (Book 4, Ch69 L102, L106), and Yumi takes blood, flesh and bone, which give the avatar confluence and let her remake her body into an idealised younger one (Book 5, Ch31 L147). Jason's two sets for the former Engineers of Ascension are sun, blood and life (avatar, "about as perfect an anti-vampire set as you'll find") or gun, hand and adept (master) (Book 6, Ch26 L94).

## 5. Common misconceptions in-world

- **You can pick your confluence.** Only the three essences are chosen; the fourth follows from them. People do pick a set to aim at a confluence, with the Society list as guide (Book 2, Ch32 L67-72).
- **Refusing the confluence frees the slot for a better one.** Rufus: "I promise you that is not the way to go" (Book 1, Ch21 L57).
- **A confluence guarantees a role.** Clive taught Jason to shape a power set "by aiming for powers within a certain scope" rather than seeking specific powers (Book 4, Ch65 L29), and the same confluence plays differently by user (see section 3).

## 6. Changes over time

**Level 3. The fixed pairing ends.** Rufus is told that essence combinations have "only one potential confluence" and "before the Cosmic Throne was sundered, things were different. More flexible", that an essence combination could once "produce different confluences, from person to person" (Book 11, Ch68 L1574). After the sundering, "the Celestial Book locked off essence combinations" (Book 11, Ch68 L1580) and, if Jason repairs the throne, "all those restrictions will be undone"; the restrictions "are already starting to unravel" (Book 11, Ch68 L1580). The first reported case is Emi, whose vast, myriad and harmonic should produce unity and instead give resonant (Book 11, Ch68 L1559, L1568). A speaker in Book 12 reports that "the previously fixed essence combinations are starting to add variety to the confluence essences they produce" (Book 12, Ch10 L91), and that some people refuse the confluence, "especially now that the combinations are becoming less reliable" (Book 12, Ch10 L88).

**Level 3. Gary's Cup of Heroes.** When Gary becomes a demigod his essences are replaced by divine ones, and his forge confluence by a Demigod essence, with abilities replaced to match (Book 10, Ch86 L67-72). The effect on others is not shown.
