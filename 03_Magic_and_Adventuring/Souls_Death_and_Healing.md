## 1. Summary

A soul is the part of a person that essences and ranks change. It shapes the body, projects the aura, leaves marks when wounded and, on death, lingers briefly before drifting into the astral. Healing magic can reverse a death only in that grace period and only with powerful magic. Everyday healing is done by the Healer's church, by alchemists and by healing abilities, and it is in short supply compared to demand.

## 2. How it works

**Death and the grace period.** Clive: "When the soul dies, it only lingers with the body for a small time. Usually minutes, but an annihilated body might have the soul depart in seconds, while freezing to death might have it linger for an hour or even longer. It's why if a gold rank healer can repair the body in that grace period, the death can be turned back" (Book 2, Ch25 L53). After that, "an untethered soul is an astral object and drifts into the astral" (Book 2, Ch25 L55).

**Outworlders.** "An outworlder is someone whose soul has re-entered a physical reality, reflexively manifesting a body for itself... like a monster" (Book 2, Ch25 L57-58; Book 2, Ch25 L59). The Builder and others do not just resurrect their own minions because the great astral beings "are careful about encroaching upon one another's interests" (Book 2, Ch25 L51).

**The Reaper and the World-Phoenix.** Shade: the World-Phoenix has "the right to return mortals from death", used with restraint out of respect for the Reaper's authority, and the Reaper "only cares if you cheat being dead. Once your soul has gone to the astral, that is when it becomes his concern. Until then, it is the affair of any death god a world might have" (Book 4, Ch3 L34, L36). Jason's Nirvanic Transfiguration trades ordinary resurrection for a limited number of rebirths: "you would have only one chance to revive at each rank" (Book 4, Ch2 L50, L60). Its restriction is "not an artificially imposed restriction": a gestalt of body and soul, once it dies, stays spiritual (Book 4, Ch3 L40). The description says its restrictions "will change on reaching diamond rank" (Book 4, Ch2 L50).

**Souls and marks.** A soul "can't truly be harmed by external forces", but it can be marked (Book 4, Ch37 L14). Jason's interface "used titles to translate the scars on his soul into game terms" (Book 4, Ch2 L26); in Book 3 a woman who has been watching him says she can see the scars on his soul "more clearly than the ones on your body" (Book 3, Ch29 L46). A soul scar leaves a person depleted though physically well; Neil advises against rushing recovery with potions after four days asleep (Book 3, Ch14 L9). See `Auras.md` for the titles.

**Crests.** A soul crest "is a magical tattoo printed not on the body, but on the soul. That imprint would appear on the body in turn, in a form that resisted design. The form of the crest was a visible reflection of the bearer's true nature" (Book 2, Ch70 L36). The unique imprint on the aura stays the same even when the aura changes, making it "a guaranteed proof of identity" once recorded (Book 2, Ch70 L37-38). The Immortal Crest is an iron-rank consumable applied by a mystical tattooist, usable only on an iron-rank essence user (Book 2, Ch70 L33-35). The tattoo is purged as the wearer ranks up, so a new one is needed (Book 2, Ch70 L42). Humphrey has one in vivid blue (Book 2, Ch70 L44).

**Healing in practice.** "Most healing was done through the church of the Healer. From what he'd been told, their god supplied the essences and awakening stones that gave them their healing abilities. They could be sought out for a fee, but also sent people around the delta to heal people at more reasonable prices. It sounded good, but Jason had seen firsthand that there was always more demand for such services than supply" (Book 1, Ch35 L23). Both ritual magic and alchemy have ways to heal, but "the cost and expertise required placed both out of the reach for most people" (Book 1, Ch35 L22). The Healer personally tells a patient that an alchemist could not regrow a foot, and offers to station a healer at Jory's clinic (Book 1, Ch95 L69, L126).

**Healer powers.** Healing abilities exist for cleansing and recovery; see `Afflictions_Boons_and_Damage.md`. A healer who stops a person dying within the grace period is a "gold rank healer" in Clive's account (Book 2, Ch25 L53).

## 3. Variations and exceptions

- **Messengers.** Their life "is not just physical. It's tenacious": remnant life force remains after the body is annihilated and "resurrection magic doesn't work on messengers. Or anyone like them" (Book 9, Ch65 L22-23).
- **Diamond-rank effects.** "More potent diamond-rank resurrection effects" can return the soul after it has left the body, as long as it lingers in the world, and death gods may govern them (Book 4, Ch3 L38).
- **Healing the immune.** A person with an immunity may need special handling; see the afflictions that negate immunity in `Afflictions_Boons_and_Damage.md`.
- **Recovery after soul damage.** Jason's soul was "battered and beaten to the point that his entire torso was a landscape of ragged marks" and the damage gave him powerful capabilities (Book 4, Ch2 L25-26).

## 4. Examples from the books

- **Farrah's death** is the example Jason's team recalls when Clive explains the grace period (Book 2, Ch25 L54).
- **Humphrey's crest**, with its blue sigil (Book 2, Ch70 L44).
- **Jory's clinic**, an alchemy lab and healing station (Book 1, Ch35 L63).

## 5. Common misconceptions in-world

- **A good healer can undo any death.** Only within the grace period, and only against bodies that can be repaired (Book 2, Ch25 L53).
- **A soul can be harmed.** Soul scars are "marks" on a soul that "can't truly be harmed by external forces" (Book 4, Ch37 L14).
- **A crest can be chosen.** It cannot be (Book 2, Ch70 L39).

## 6. Changes over time

- **Level 2:** By Book 9, "Resurrection magic has been harder for a few years now. Even at gold rank, you have minutes at best, and only the most complex and difficult healing magic can do it", something "the gods of healing and death did to how magic works" (Book 9, Ch81 L61-63).
- **Level 3:** Jason's Sacred Phoenix gift lets him transform into a ghost fire phoenix on lethal damage, usable once a year (Book 12, Ch67 L199).
