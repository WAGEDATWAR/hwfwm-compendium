## 1. Summary

Most magic in the setting follows the essence-and-rank structure described elsewhere in this folder. A smaller body of magic does not: the gifts a person is born with or earns as a race, the outworlder abilities that give Jason a game-like interface, titles drawn from soul damage, time magic, and the powers of great astral beings and gods. This file collects them so a storyteller knows what exists and who has what. It does not attempt to explain the cosmology; see `02_Cosmology_and_Powers/`.

## 2. How it works

**Racial gifts.** Races have inherent abilities. Humans, for example, have an aptitude for special attacks (Book 4, Ch40 L72), "Human Ambition", "Special Attack Affinity" and "Essence Gift" (Book 2, Ch61 L144, L147; Book 2, Ch67 L113). Elves have "Life Affinity" and celestines "Mana Integrity" (Book 2, Ch61 L145-146). Gary notes that other races are less efficient: humans "have their essence abilities increase faster than other races" (Book 1, Ch15 L58). Racial gifts can evolve (see below).

**The outworlder gifts.** Jason's first status screen lists six racial abilities labelled Outworlder: Interface, Quest System, Inventory, Map, Astral Affinity and Mysterious Stranger (Book 1, Ch1 L37-43).
- **Interface:** the screens and System messages; "interpret reality through a recognisable medium" (Book 1, Ch87 L160).
- **Quest System:** quests with rewards, including a "racial gift transfiguration" (Book 2, Ch61 L130).
- **Inventory:** a grid of forty slots with a currency counter; items are plucked from and pushed into the screen (Book 1, Ch1 L82-89).
- **Map:** a self-updating map (Book 2, Ch61 L141).
- **Astral Affinity:** "Increased resistance to dimension effects and astral forces. Dimension abilities have increased effect and transcendent damage is increased" (Book 1, Ch1 L93).
- **Mysterious Stranger:** "Language adaptation. Essence, awakening stone and skill-book absorption. Immunity to identification and tracking effects" (Book 1, Ch1 L96-99).

**Evolutions of racial gifts.** A racial gift can transfigure when the owner completes a task or takes a blessing. In Book 2 Jason's Interface becomes Party Interface, with a contacts list and party features that members keep while they remain in the party (Book 1, Ch87 L157-162). Map becomes Tactical Map, which tracks nearby allies and enemies (Book 2, Ch61 L138-142). His teammates' gifts evolve in the same reward: Clive's Human Ambition to Thirst For Knowledge, Neil's Life Affinity to Life Guard, Sophie's Mana Integrity to Mana Wellspring, Humphrey's Special Attack Affinity to Attack of the Mirage Dragon (Book 2, Ch61 L144-147). Quest System later becomes Defiant, which loses the quest effects and gains the ability to "ignore the enhanced resistances derived from rank disparity" and rank-disparity aura suppression (Book 3, Ch21 L105-111). Mysterious Stranger evolves into Dark Rider in Book 2 (Book 2, Ch63 L83-85).

**Blessings.** A great astral being can bless a person: "You have received the blessing of the World-Phoenix. If you accept the blessing, your outworlder racial ability [Astral Affinity] will evolve to [Nirvanic Transfiguration]" (Book 4, Ch2 L39-40). Clive's explanation: such a blessing "could not control the recipient outside of ordinary methods like gratitude and obligation" and "once granted, could not be revoked like that from a divine awakening stone" (Book 4, Ch2 L44). A Reaper token used while absorbing a stone counted as consent to a transfiguration (Book 4, Ch2 L45).

**Titles.** Titles are given by the System for soul damage that changes the aura (see `Auras.md`).

**Time magic.** Time can be "sped up or slowed down" and one can "move forwards, vanishing and then reappearing at some point in the future. Affecting the past, however, is impossible. Even the Keeper of Moments, the great astral being that governs time, cannot do such a thing" (Book 6, Ch14 L80). The Builder sealed dead parts of his constructed worlds in time, "frozen and unchanging", where altered things revert to the state at the moment of sealing (Book 6, Ch14 L78). Danielle's time confluence has a limited scope at silver rank (Book 6, Ch14 L79).

## 3. Variations and exceptions

- **Time and speed.** Personal time-acceleration powers are very common among swift-essence users (Book 9, Ch20 L15). Rufus's sun essence "gave Rufus flashes of brilliant speed" (Book 11, Ch68 L1304).
- **Immunity to identification.** Jason's identification immunity means the Adventure Society badge does not track him (Book 1, Ch62 L55-59).
- **Soul-based power.** Spirit domains, Akashic Speech and the System itself are described in the Cosmology files; their statements are in Jason's gifts (Book 12, Ch67 L195-200).
- **Removal.** A divine stone's ability can be revoked; a great astral being's blessing cannot (Book 4, Ch2 L44).

## 4. Examples from the books

- **Humphrey's Hero's Drive.** The text credits Humphrey's Hero's Drive with letting him ignore the resistances of rank disparity (Book 3, Ch67 L95).
- **Sophie's Boundary Breaker.** Eliminates the damage reduction from rank disparity (Book 3, Ch69 L808).
- **Jason's interface** appears to other people in his party (Book 1, Ch87 L162).

## 5. Common misconceptions in-world

- **Racial gifts are rare.** Every race has them, although most never evolve.
- **A blessing is an obligation.** Clive describes it as no stronger than gratitude (Book 4, Ch2 L44).
- **Time travel into the past is possible.** Shade: "The past is inviolate" (Book 6, Ch14 L82).

## 6. Changes over time

- **Level 1:** The Reaper trials reward the team with racial transfigurations (Book 2, Ch61 L130-147).
- **Level 2:** Astral Affinity becomes Nirvanic Transfiguration in Book 4, with a limited number of rebirths (Book 4, Ch2 L46-49).
- **Level 3:** By Book 12, Jason's inherent gifts are the six Astral Nexus gifts and his Interface is the System Administrator gift (Book 12, Ch67 L162-167, L197).
