## 1. Summary

The books name far more essences than the wiki lists give with evidence. This file collects those the text actually shows, with who holds them and what the text says of their behaviour. Rarity statements come from the text; where the text gives none, none is invented. It is intended as a quick reference for building a character's power set, not as a mechanical list. The full set of essences the Magic Society knows is in its living document (Book 1, Ch20 L97).

The wiki's fuller lists of essences, confluences and combinations are in `Wiki_Reference_Lists.md` (wiki tier), with a cross-reference to the first book line for each entry found in the text. Where the wiki gives a rarity, it agrees with the text so far: sin is Legendary, renewal is Epic (the text's "second-highest rarity", Book 6, Ch13 L68) and rune and vast are among the highest.

## 2. How to read it

- An **essence** is named here by its concept. A **confluence** is a fourth essence produced from three others (see `Confluences.md`).
- "Seen with" lists a person or group for whom the text names the essence and gives a line.
- Rarity is only given where the text gives it. The text uses five stages for stones: common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary (Book 2, Ch9 L9; Book 2, Ch9 L17 puts four-star at the "second highest rarity"). Essences are discussed with the same words.

## 5. Variations and misconceptions

- **Names and concepts.** An essence name is a concept, not a promise of power: the bird essence might give "the power to talk to birds" rather than a combat power (Book 4, Ch65 L27).
- **Same essence, different user.** The vehicle essence gives drones to Kaito (Book 5, Ch7 L8) and a conjured limousine to Wesley Asano (Book 12, Ch59 L9) and a helicopter to another (Book 11, Ch68 L1526).
- **Wiki lists.** The fan wiki grades many essences by rarity; those grades are not used here when the text gives a different one. Where the text and the wiki conflict, the text is followed (for example renewal, above).

## 6. Later developments
