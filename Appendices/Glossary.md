# Glossary

Status: Draft (audited against the book text, chapter notes and wiki; entries without support in those tiers were reworded or removed)
Spoiler level: mixed (each entry is tagged)
Valid from: Book 1
Valid through: Book 13 (end)
Sources: book text, chapter notes, fan wiki
Related: ../01_World_Primer.md, ../00_Project_Structure.md

**Astral blessing token**: an item great astral beings give as a sign of approval; it triggers racial gift evolutions (Book 2, Ch63 L45-47; see `09_Items_and_Artefacts/Tokens_Stones_and_Relics.md`). **[2]**
**Fundamental Realm Authority Token**: transcendent-rank item that lets its wielder open gates to the fundamental realm; Jason looted it and Dawn says he must give it back (Book 8, Ch50 L87-91; Ch51 L68-70). **[2]**
**How to read this glossary.** Entries are alphabetical. Each ends with a spoiler tag: **[0]** public knowledge or Books 1-3 basics, **[1]** Books 1-3, **[2]** Books 4-9, **[3]** Books 10-13. Cross-references appear in *italics*. Names of people and places have their own documents (planned); this glossary covers terms and concepts, plus the briefest identification of recurring names so the reader can orient.

Essence and confluence names (Fire, Wind, Dragon, Volcano...) are not listed individually here; see `03_Magic_and_Adventuring/Essence_Catalogue.md`.

## A

**Action quota**: the minimum work every adventurer must complete during a monster surge, assigned at the jobs hall; guilds receive about sixty percent of the quotas. See *contribution board*. **[2]**

**Adept (essence)**: a common essence concerned with skill and proficiency; Belinda's *Instant Adept* ability grants temporary skill sets. **[1]**

**Adventure Society**: the worldwide organisation that posts monster notices and contracts, assesses adventurers, issues badges and stars, and enforces restrictions on dangerous essences. Run locally by directors and overseen by a *Continental Council*. **[0]**

**Adventurer**: a person who hunts monsters and takes contracts. In Book 12 Jason defines the role as putting yourself between the bad things and those who need protection (Book 12, Ch46 L54). **[0]**

**Affinity gem**: Earth's name for *quintessence*. **[2]**

**Affliction**: a lasting harmful condition inflicted by an ability. Afflictions often stack, can be resisted, cleansed or negated, and have names (Bleeding, Sin, Penance...). Jason is an *affliction specialist* (or, in Trenchant's assessment, an affliction skirmisher). **[0]**

**Akashic Speech**: the divine manner of speaking used by vessels of gods and astral beings; its meaning is understood by any speaker in their own language, "as if the soul were listening". Jason acquires it with his *Numen* gift. **[1/3]**

**Alchemy**: the craft of potions, ointments and reagents. Staples: recovery potion, healing unguent, crystal wash. **[0]**

**Anomaly**: a monster-like being in a *transformation zone*; weaker versions are born from the strongest presence or the claimant's memories. A *greater anomaly* guards a *territory*. **[2]**

**Apocalypse beast**: a creature of world-ending potential, usually artificial (the *sanguine horror*, the *world-taker worm*, the naga genesis egg). Some are weapons of messengers. **[1/2]**

**Arbour**: the forest city in Jason's *astral kingdom*, formerly a corrupted *birthing tree*, now a person and a nascent *Voice of the Will*. **[3]**

**Aristocratic Faction**: Yaresh's bloc of wealthy aristocrats and merchant barons who own most of the land and deliberately build power on money and influence rather than personal power. **[2]**

**Array**: a large-scale ritual formation, such as a defensive array or the Network's *Grid*. A *natural array* is a naturally occurring arrangement of elemental magic. **[1/2]**

**Astral**: the realm of pure magic between worlds. See also *astral space* and *deep astral*. **[0]**

**Astral gate**: one of the *Relics of the King*; governs a king's interaction with dimensional forces outside his domain. Jason's is a geyser of raw magic reached by a glass tunnel under a sea. **[2]**

**Astral king**: a being whose soul has become a miniature universe. Most were once messengers who transcended. Jason is an *astral nexus*. See also *original*. **[2/3]**

**Astral kingdom**: Jason's soul realm in its mature form, a pocket universe he rules. **[2]**

**Astral lantern**: Belinda's familiar Shimmer, which fires bolts and shields. **[1]**

**Astral nexus**: Jason's nature: an astral king who is also a nexus of great astral beings, gods and astral kings. **[3]**

**Astral space**: a pocket dimension attached to a world through an aperture (waterfall, cave, door). May contain monsters, treasure and ruins. A *proto-astral space* is a temporary or developing one. **[0]**

**Astral throne**: one of the *Relics of the King*; governs a king's internal functions. Jason's room for it holds a wicker hammock chair. **[2]**

**Attributes**: Power, Speed, Spirit and Recovery. **[0]**

**Aura**: the physical, intangible extension of a person's soul and will. Can be projected, retracted, suppressed, masked, blended. **[0]**

**Authority**: the fundamental power of transcendent beings: their flesh, blood and soul, and the currency by which they act. Exists in *set-state* (put to a purpose) or *potential-state* (raw). **[2/3]**

**Avatar**: (1) a god's or astral being's temporary body; (2) Jason's *prime avatar*; (3) *Avatar of Doom*, a familiar. **[1/3]**

**Awakening stone**: a consumable crystal that awakens an essence ability. Rated by rarity (stars). **[0]**

## B

**Badge (Adventure Society)**: a medallion that records kills and can locate the wearer's body; shows star rank. **[0]**

**Bag of Tricks**: Belinda's personal dimensional storage ability. **[1]**

**Binary**: a being whose body and soul are connected but discrete (the opposite of a gestalt). **[3]**

**Birthing tree**: a tree on which messengers grow. A corrupted one sits in the Cardinas natural array. **[2/3]**

**Blessing**: a gift from a god or astral being. Astral blessings (like tokens) can trigger *racial gift* evolution. **[1]**

**Blood root vine**: a plant-animal monster of Pallimustus (Book 2, Ch55 L3-4). **Blood oaks** are a vampire-side threat on Earth whose revelation is "good for Elizabeth's negotiating position" (Book 13, Ch961 L73), with limbs each ending in a body used as weapon, shield and potion (Book 13, Ch958 L68); that they are Elizabeth's weapons is Jason's guess, from notes. **[2/3]**

**Blood servant**: a human who drinks vampire blood for temporary power. **[2]**

**Boon**: a lasting beneficial condition. **[0]**

**Boundary, the**: the great astral being who sanctioned itself into the *World-Phoenix*. **[3]**

**Brand (messenger)**: a mark in a messenger's soul; Boris (Book 11, Ch25 L51) says a messenger's rank limits are a function of the brand. **[2]**

**Brightheart**: the Smoulder sub-species of Cardinas; obsidian skin with elemental glow. **[3]**

**Builder, the**: the great astral being of construction and world-forging. The *old Builder* created the twin universes and was sanctioned; the *new Builder* is a mortal messenger (Zithis Carrow Vayel) raised to the post. Its servants are the *Builder cult*. **[1/3]**

**Burning Violet**: an elite adventuring guild of the Remore family. **[1/2]**

**Butterflies of Makassar**: the name broadcasters gave to the public spectacle in Book 5 in which Jason's familiar Gordon spread the *Harbinger of Doom* affliction through a horde of undead at Makassar. Filmed from high in the air, the swarm of blue and orange eye-patterned butterflies looked beautiful; it left only black-stained bones. Jason was credited with it, and it made many people fear what he might do. **[2]**

## C

**Cabal**: Earth's old magical society, with vampire members (Book 4, Ch11 L4-8; wiki). **[2]**

**Category**: Earth's Network term for rank (0-5). **[2]**

**Celestial Book**: great astral being of magic's nature; venerated by Clive. **[1]**

**Celestine**: a race with metallic hair and eyes, born of two outworlders. **[0]**

**Chrysalis golem**: Neil's crystal summon. **[1]**

**Cloud flask**: Jason's bonded item containing cloud houses, ships and palaces. **[1]**

**Cloud palace / house / ship**: buildings and vehicles made of cloud substance. **[1]**

**Clockwork core**: a device that replaces a person's personality and abilities with obedience and raises their rank one step. Used by the Builder cult and Purity. **[1/2]**

**Clockwork king**: a gold-rank dragon-like leader native to the Builder's world; its body is the source of clockwork cores. **[1/2]**

**Colin**: Jason's blood familiar, a sanguine horror. **[1]**

**Confluence essence**: the special essence formed by three others. **[0]**

**Continental Council**: the body above Adventure Society branches. **[1]**

**Contribution board**: the Adventure Society's incentive ladder during a surge; work beyond the quota earns points on weekly and overall leaderboards with rewards at the end of each week and of the surge. **[2]**

**Core (monster core)**: crystallised magic from a monster; eaten to advance faster, at a long-term cost. **[0]**

**Cosmic Throne**: the embodied cosmic law that governed the great astral beings; sundered by them, restored by Jason in Book 11. See also *Sundered Throne*. **[3]**

**Council of Kings**: the ruling body of astral kings. **[3]**

**Crystal wash**: a cleaning liquid, always in short supply. **[0]**

## D

**Dark Rider**: Jason's evolved language-adaptation and tracking-immunity gift. **[1]**

**Deep astral**: pure magic where bodies cannot survive. **[1]**

**Defiant**: Jason's evolved quest system: ignores rank-disparity resistance. **[1]**

**Defier of the Will**: a bond choice offered to Farrah in Book 12; it lets the holder negate the influence of the Astral Nexus in various ways and confers immortality (Book 12, Ch21 L43-46). **[3]**

**Diamond**: the peak mortal rank. **[0]**

**Dimensional storage / bag**: pocket-dimension carrying devices. **[0]**

**Dimensional ulceration**: Dawn's term (Book 5, Ch68 L30) for a transformation zone forming in a space already coterminous with a proto-astral space: an open wound in the dimensional membrane. Its worst case is the collapse of the membrane and the annihilation of a world, possibly more. **[2]**

**Divine essence / stone**: those given by a god; revocable. **[0]**

**Domain (spirit domain)**: a territory claimed by Jason that mirrors his aura and rules. **[2]**

**Doom**: Jason's confluence (Dark + Blood + Sin), the affliction specialist. **[0]**

**Dread Salvation**: the sword Gary made for Jason; later Hegemon's Will. **[1]**

**Dumpling soup**: Clive's model of the cosmos (Book 1, Ch69 L85-96). **[1]**

## E

**EDJI**: the Earth Defence Joint Initiative (Book 13, Ch1020 L85-91). **[3]**

**Engineers of Ascension (EOA)**: an Earth faction; the Lyon branch's dimensional-space project head, Adrien Barbou, defected to it (Book 6, Ch29 L32; Book 6, Ch35 L73). **[2]**

**Echo array**: an artefact in Cardinas that extended the natural array. **[3]**

**Echo spirit**: Belinda's familiar Gemini. **[1]**

**Essence**: a crystal of pure magic that grants five abilities. **[0]**

**Eternal Moment**: Sophie's time-acceleration ability. **[1]**

**Execute**: a spell type that kills or deals transcendent damage based on injury (Jason's *Verdict*). **[1]**

**Eyes (Jason's)**: silver after Nirvanic Transfiguration; later nebula-like; masked by *blue coins*. **[2/3]**

## F

**Familiar**: a created creature bonded to an owner. **[0]**

**Fate sense**: an unconscious sense for destiny; Jason's Quest System was one. **[3]**

**Flames of Redemption**: the Order of Redeeming Light's rite; an extreme modification of lesser vampirism that leaves members with a lack of imagination. **[2]**

**Fundamental realm**: the layer under physical reality; node space is part of it. **[2]**

**Fundament Gate / Door**: the Builder's door to the *templates*, absorbed by Jason in Book 5. **[2]**

## G

**GAB**: great astral being. **[1]**

**Genesis Command**: a consumable gift from a god that creates or reassigns power (Life from the Healer; Source from Hero). **[3]**

**Genesis core / space / reclamation core**: components of a transformation zone. **[2]**

**Gestalt**: a being whose body and soul are fused. **[2]**

**Ghost fire**: white fire harmless to the living and ruinous to the undead. **[3]**

**Giant Slayer**: a title that makes weak-willed creatures fear Jason. **[2]**

**Global Defense Network (GDN)**: the public name of the Network after the monster waves began on Earth; later one of its fragments. **[2]**

**God**: a concept-embodying being outside physical reality. **[0]**

**Gold**: rank above silver. **[0]**

**Gordon**: Jason's Doom familiar. **[1]**

**Great astral being**: see *GAB*. **[1]**

**Grid**: Earth's detection network for dimensional incursions. **[2]**

## H

**Harbinger of Doom**: a stacking, unholy affliction applied through Gordon's orbs. It drains mana from the victim to conjure an incorporeal butterfly that seeks out nearby enemies, detonates with disruptive force when destroyed, and passes on every non-holy affliction of its host. It cannot be cleansed while any other non-holy affliction remains. See *Butterflies of Makassar*. **[2]**

**Hegemon / Hegemony**: Jason's aura ability (Sin essence) and public name. **[1/3]**

**Hegemon's Will / Dominion**: the reforged sword and scabbard. **[2]**

**Hierophant**: a former prime vessel who still serves, e.g. Dawn. **[3]**

**Hero (god)**: god of heroes, who gives the Cup of Heroes. **[3]**

**Heidel**: the local equivalent of a horse; a two-headed scaled draught animal. **[0]**

## I

**Immortal Crest**: soul crest tattoo for identification. **[1]**

**Incursion (dimensional)**: Earth's term for a proto-astral space. **[2]**

**Interface (Jason's)**: his early outworlder user-interface; became the System. **[0]**

**Interstice**: the city-universe at the cosmic centre. **[1]**

**Intrinsic-mandate magic**: high-order magic where the user's will is law within their domain. **[3]**

**Iron**: the first rank. **[0]**

**Isolation (duel) power**: a messenger ability that isolates two combatants. **[2]**

## K

**Keeper of Moments / Sands**: the great astral being of time. **[3]**

**Keeper of the Cycle**: Jason's title from the Death Pact. **[3]**

**Knowledge**: goddess of knowledge; mastermind of the Earth invasion plan. **[1/3]**

## L

**Lehenik**: Jason's soul-realm people, named from Basque for "first". **[3]**

**Lesser Miracle Potion**: iron-rank legendary potion that fully restores health, mana and stamina of normal and iron-rank people (Book 2, Ch44 L127-129). **[0]**
**Lesser spirit coin**: the lowest denomination. **[0]**

**Limit Break**: Farrah's ability to lift one ability above her rank. **[2]**

**Living document**: the Magic Society's marble-and-gold tablet, connected to a central record, listing every known essence and combination. **[0]**

**Lord of Magic**: Clive's aura. **[1]**

## M

**Magic Society**: the record-keeping, research and communications body. **[0]**

**Magical Research Association (MRA)**: the open-access research body Clive founds as a rival to the Magic Society. **[2/3]**

**Mana**: the resource spent on abilities; **stamina** the physical equivalent. **[0]**

**Messenger**: a winged servant race of astral kings. **[2]**

**Mirage chamber**: an illusion training facility. **[0]**

**Mirror King**: a diamond-ranker who rules the Kingdom of Mirrors. **[1]**

**Monster**: sentient magic without a soul. **[0]**

**Monster surge**: cyclical release of magic. **[0]**

**Moon's Edge**: Yaresh team of six gold-rank women raised by Allayeth and led by Miriam Vance (Book 10, Ch51 L43; Book 10, Ch59 L17); members include Ramona the ritualist and shield specialist, Valetta and the berserker Alice (Book 10, Ch63 L16; Book 10, Ch71 L39; Book 10, Ch81 L34). **[3]**

**Mr East**: seat on the Network's inner committee; Adrien Barbou becomes the second Mr East (Book 5, Ch8 L35). **[2]**

## N

**Natural array**: elemental magic arrangement under Yaresh. **[2]**

**Nature**: race, in the System's vocabulary. **[3]**

**Network**: Earth's coalition of essence users (Book 6, Ch1 L16-21; wiki). **[2]**

**Nirvanic Transfiguration**: Jason's World-Phoenix blessing: a body-soul gestalt that can be reborn once per rank. **[2]**

**Node space**: the fundamental-realm mechanism of the Earth-Pallimustus link. **[2]**

**Numen**: Jason's prime-avatar gift of divine aspects. **[3]**

## O

**Order of the Reaper**: ancient assassins and necromancer hunters, destroyed by the churches and now secretly surviving. **[1]**

**Order of Redeeming Light**: Purity's zealot order. **[2]**

**Original**: an astral king who was not a messenger. **[3]**

**Outworlder**: a person from another world. **[0]**

## P

**Palanquin**: Jason's gift that turns familiars into transport. **[3]**

**Party interface**: the System's party and voice-chat function. **[1]**

**Path of Shadows**: Jason's shadow-teleportation. **[1]**

**Penance / Penitence**: holy afflictions that deal transcendent damage. **[1]**

**Prime avatar / vessel**: a physical anchor for a transcendent being's power. **[3]**

**Projection Command: Presence**: a Dominion consumable controlling the presence of transcendent power. **[3]**

**Proto-astral space**: see *astral space*. **[2]**

**Pure servitor**: an essence user implanted with a purified clockwork core by the Order of Redeeming Light; magically controlled and unable to use essence abilities. **[2]**

**Purity**: a god whose title was occupied by a deceiver; his church caused much of the series' trouble. **[1/3]**

## Q

**Quest System**: Jason's early game-like quest list; became *Defiant*. **[0]**

**Quintessence**: elemental/essence crystal gems used in crafting (Book 1, Ch7 L68-69; wiki). **[0]**

## R

**Racial gift**: one of six inherent traits of a nature. **[0]**

**Rank**: iron through diamond; transcendent beyond. **[0]**

**Reaper, the**: great astral being of death's finality; Shade is one of its shadows (Book 11, Ch68 L2209-2214). **[1]**

**Reality core**: a battery of the universe; found in transformation zones. **[2]**

**Reality Hegemon**: Jason's title of dominion over reality within his domains. **[2]**

**Red Table**: blood cult with branches in remote areas, destroyed in Greenstone (Book 1, Ch18 L5; Book 3, Ch41 L66; Book 7, Ch55 L75). **[1]**

**Regalia of the Dark Hegemon**: the set of Jason's three soul-bound items, made by the Soul-Imprinting Triune (Book 6, Ch4 L26-38; see `09_Items_and_Artefacts/Soul_Bound_Items_and_Regalia.md`). **[1]**
**Relics of the King**: astral throne, astral gate and soul forge. **[2]**

**Restricted list**: the Adventure Society's list of essences and combinations judged dangerous to the public (Death, Corrupt, Sacrifice, Undeath, Succubus and others); holders cannot join the Society. **[0]**

**Resurrection**: restoring life; severely limited. **[1]**

**Rimaros**: the Storm Kingdom's capital city-of-islands. **[2]**

**Ring gate**: a giant stone ring, about a hundred metres across, that anchors a physical bridge across the astral between two spaces; one rises over the crater in the Reaper-trials boarding vessel in Book 3. **[1]**

**Ritual**: magic through circles and chants. **[0]**

**Rules of Intrusion**: cosmic law about entering worlds. **[2]**

**Sapphire Crown**: the Storm Kingdom's royal guild, whose non-royal members serve periodically in the royal guard and are examined quarterly. **[2]**

**Shade**: Jason's shadow familiar and butler. **[1]**

**Shining Scabbard, Team**: team led by Sigrid Freyn (Book 12, Ch15 L8). **[3]**

**Skill book**: a consumable that imparts a skill. **[0]**

**Soul crest**: see *Immortal Crest*. **[1]**

**Soul forge**: a structure for engineering souls. **[2/3]**

## U

**Umber**: a figure who "likes to play games" and told of trapping a servant of the Reaper's kind in an astral space for centuries (Book 8, Ch55 L85-89); that he is Shade's brother and plans against Jason in Book 13 is from notes. **[3]**

**Valash**: a transformed-human race. **[2]**

**Vessel of the Hegemon**: an orb turning vehicles into extensions of Jason's domain. **[2]**

**World-Phoenix**: great astral being of dimensional integrity; served by Dawn. **[1]**
