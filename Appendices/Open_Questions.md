## 1. People and peoples

1. **Why did the Ustei come south?** *(Narrowed in the build plan 1.5 pass.)* Known: they raid spirit coin convoys far from home and are nomads from a region to the north (Book 1, Ch83 L4; Book 1, Ch89 L50), and they came south about three years before the Earthlings (Book 12, Ch30 L9). The Adventure Society learned the cause: the desert astral space was failing, the waterfall shutting off in brief isolated instances near the city and much worse in the north, until the oases connected to it could no longer support all the nomad tribes (Book 1, Ch97 L19-21). The tribe was shipped back north in its sand barge (Book 1, Ch109 L8). Not known: the homeland's name, or what disrupted the northern astral space.
2. **Does Marek Nior Vargas lead the Unorthodoxy?** *(Narrowed in the build plan 1.5 pass.)* Known: Marek served Vesta Carmis Zell until recently (Book 10, Ch1 L53) and Jason calls his group "Marek's Unorthodoxy" with "victory or death" energy (Book 11, Ch2 L75; Book 11, Ch66 L95). It is Boris who explains the movement to messengers and who passes as a branded servant of Vesta Carmis Zell (Book 11, Ch25 L47-55), and Marek's people subordinate themselves to Boris as the Unorthodoxy's "messiah" (Book 11, Ch49 L71; Book 11, Ch67 L9). Not known: Marek's standing in the wider movement; the text shows him leading a band, not the whole.
3. **Who does the Unorthodoxy include among astral kings?** *(Narrowed in the build plan 1.5 pass.)* Known: the Council of Kings does not know that Boris is Unorthodoxy, nor how many kings are outside it (Book 11, Ch24 L86); Boris says the original astral kings were not messengers, that the Unorthodoxy has astral kings, and that there are "too many astral kings for anyone to keep track of", with the Unorthodoxy ones "slipping back into the general population" (Book 11, Ch25 L49-57). One is the king of Navise Den Rigal, a "rare" Unorthodoxy king (Book 12, Ch26 L19). Not known: how many, or who besides this one.

## 2. Powers and the cosmos

4. **Are messengers strictly capped at silver?** *(Resolved in the build plan 1.5 pass.)* No: the cap is a function of the brand. Before the brand there were no limits on rank, and the brand was instituted by messengers who became astral kings (Book 11, Ch25 L51); only at diamond rank can a messenger begin to resist the brand (Book 11, Ch25 L53). A silver-rank seal caps Tera Jun Casta (Book 9, Ch102 L55, L66-67).
5. **What is the debt of Zithis Carrow Vayel?** *(Narrowed in the build plan 1.5 pass.)* Known: while the Builder's world engineers prepare to move the planet, Jamis says "When this task is complete, the price is paid. Zithis Carrow Vayel will have fulfilled his debt to us" (Book 10, Ch15 L9), and Erigo says the messengers' ritualists provided "some low-level ritual magic" as a service for a reward (Book 10, Ch15 L21-22). So the debt is owed to the astral kings of the Council, for that magic, and ends with the move. Not known: what he owed beyond the magic.
6. **What is Hero's sanction?** *(Resolved in the build plan 2A.6 pass.)* Gwydion tells Gary that Hero has decided to sanction himself, as he has considered for longer than any of them has lived, in order to add "another miracle option. Something less powerful than the Cup of Heroes, with less of a price" by making the relics of dead demigods usable again for a short time (Book 11, Ch68 L707-709). A sanction changes an immortal in some way (Book 13, Ch1017 L11). Not known: when it happens or what else it changes in Hero.
7. **Did Death return the resurrection power Jason gave away?** *(Resolved in the 2026-10-22 pass.)* Yes: "I will return what was taken, Jason Asano, now that it cannot be used for its original purpose" (Book 12, Ch8 L10), and the reality material forms his prime avatar (Book 12, Ch8 L19).
8. **What do the lesser-named great astral beings govern?** *(Narrowed in the build plan 1.5 pass.)* Known: Legion administers the cosmic rules that govern life itself (Book 8, Ch55 L92); the Celestial Book locked off essence combinations after the sundering (Book 11, Ch68 L1580) and is venerated through a loose network tied to the Magic Society and the Church of Knowledge (Book 9, Ch65 L30); the Reaper is the god of finality of death (Book 2, Ch25 L67). The Celestial Book, the Seeker of Songs, the Whisper in Corners and Legion take part in the Book 11 gathering (Book 11, Ch65 L75; Book 11, Ch68 L159). Not known: the domains of the Whisper in Corners, the Word in the Silence and the Seeker of Songs.
9. **What does the Firmamental Bridge Anchor do?** *(Resolved in the build plan 1.5 pass.)* Dawn identifies it as something Jason can use to establish the bridge, and says using it consumes the authority in the item, so it is single-use and the great astral beings will not object to it (Book 8, Ch51 L66). Jason also calls it a "firmament bridge anchor" (Book 8, Ch51 L30).
10. **Why can Jason not keep the Fundamental Realm Authority Token?** *(Resolved in the build plan 1.5 pass.)* Dawn says it is a major demonstration of the Builder's failure and that great astral beings will not tolerate a mortal holding it, so he must give it back (Book 8, Ch51 L68-74). Jason trades it for concessions, including one opening of the way into the fundamental realm, and transfers the authority to the Builder (Book 8, Ch54 L54, L60-61, L99).
11. **How did Disguise take part in recruiting the Network's founder?** *(Resolved in the build plan 1.5 pass.)* Jason explains that Knowledge helped Disguise pose as Purity and, in return for help including the elimination of Purity's troublesome followers, "the fake Purity brought an outworlder from Earth into his church" (Book 13, Ch1017 L17-19). Not known: how the founder was chosen.

## 3. Places

12. **Is the Sky Link island Ascension's Gate?** Known: Jason and Farrah first arrived on an uninhabited island that is later a town with storm accumulators (Book 12, Ch56 L3-4), and the island renamed Ascension's Gate was seized by the Church of Knowledge's Holy Army (Book 13, Ch1023 L103). Resolved as far as the text goes: Jason names the Storm Kingdom island as the bridge's Pallimustus anchor (Book 12, Ch39 L47) and later says the Church has annexed it (Book 13, Ch1020 L95); the two names are never put in one sentence, but both describe that island.
13. **How strong is Vitesse's magic?** Known: it is a high-magic city whose manifestations are powerful and frequent (Book 12, Ch43 L47), the magic around it is strong enough for a cloud ship to fly (Book 12, Ch43 L46), and iron-rankers in high-magic areas are not allowed to hunt alone (Book 2, Ch53 L27). Vehicles that need a special power in weak-magic Greenstone run on ambient magic in high-magic zones (Book 7, Ch25 L3). Not known: a figure. **Narrowed 2026-10-24 (2F.5):** the books do give a rank band: typical monsters are silver, with as many golds as bronzes and the occasional diamond (Book 1, Ch30 L44), against Greenstone's mid-iron (Book 3, Ch34 L60). A numeric figure is still missing.
14. **Who governs the Old City?** *(Narrowed in the 2026-10-22 pass.)* Known: Adris Dorgan is mayor of Old City (Book 12, Ch29 L38) and the Duke of Greenstone is the city's ruler, over the town constables (Book 1, Ch36 L71). Not known: whether the mayor answers to the Duke; the compendium treats that as an inference.

## 4. Events and chronology

15. **How long did the Cardinas zone last?** *(Narrowed in the 2026-10-22 pass.)* Known: Nik is "six months old" at Book 11, Ch50 L15, so the zone lasted at least about six months from his creation; Gary is "almost a year" past the Cup of Heroes at Book 11, Ch68 L703, which caps it at roughly eleven months; "months" at Book 11, Ch60 L3. Not known: an exact length; the wiki's nine months fits inside the range.
16. **What set off the System's withdrawal from regions that keep indenture?** *(Narrowed in the 2026-10-22 pass.)* Known: the withdrawal is Jason's act as System Administrator (Book 12, Ch50 L28; the petitioners ask him to arbitrate "your withdrawal of the System", Book 12, Ch51 L13), and Cyrion's house guards are a form of indenture (Book 12, Ch48 L53). Not known: whether Cyrion in particular was the trigger.
17. **What part did Gerling play at Saint-Étienne?** *(Resolved in the 2026-10-22 pass.)* Gerling did not set the device: Mr North names Shako and Chen as the ones who placed it in the aperture (Book 6, Ch35 L78-80). Gerling entered separately, taking Adrien Barbou from the EOA headquarters and forcing him to open the dimensional space (Book 6, Ch30 L43; Book 6, Ch35 L68), and later fights beside Jason in the zone (Book 6, Ch44 L25-31).
18. **When is the Book 12 present?** *(Narrowed in the 2026-10-22 passes.)* Known: "almost twenty years" since the priests were freed (Book 12, Ch13 L36), Jason's "fifteen years or so" away (Book 12, Ch21 L61), magic known "less than fifteen years ago" (Book 12, Ch2 L107) and Book 13 in 2038 (Book 13, Ch951 L50) with sixteen years gone (Book 13, Ch972 L51). The Pallimustus chapters fit late 2037 to early 2038. The Earth arrival fits 2038 better: Lenora and Jason count "seventeen years" from the Earthlings' vanishing in autumn 2021 (Book 12, Ch67 L65), and Jota says Jason was born "forty-four years ago" (Book 12, Ch67 L113), which with a 1 April 1994 birth is April 2038 or later. The compendium uses 2037 to early 2038 for the tour and 2038 for the arrival. Not known: the exact month or the gap between the two.
19. **How long has the vampire war lasted?** *(Narrowed in the build plan 1.5 pass.)* Known: the first vampire war "ended without conclusion" when both sides were exhausted and the vampires got nuclear weapons (Book 13, Ch987 L63); the war was back "in full swing" (Book 13, Ch988 L26) and a character places the symposium "two years into the renewed vampire war" (Book 13, Ch995 L69). Jason says the operation will bring it to a final conclusion (Book 13, Ch1002 L64), and it ended in the months before the farewell (Book 13, Ch1023 L12). Simon McKean says it has been fought "off and on for decades" (Book 13, Ch1002 L69). Not known: the first war's length.
19a. **How old is Emi when Rufus arrives, against the sword line?** *(Resolved in the 2026-10-22 pass: not a contradiction.)* Emi is twelve in June 2019 (Book 4, Ch25 L60) and thirteen in Book 5 (Book 5, Ch19 L76), so she is fifteen when Rufus first reaches Earth about 2021-22 (Book 11, Ch68 L782). The "six years" of Gary's sword (Book 11, Ch68 L115) are counted from late 2017 to Jason's departure into the void in late 2023, a later moment; Emi is then about sixteen.

## 5. Organisations

20. **Where does the Order of the Reaper survive?** Known: a coalition of churches wiped out the original order centuries ago (Book 2, Ch25 L73; Book 13, Ch1017 L18); the new structure persists as a volunteer network of quiet allies, with the trials as a lure (Book 2, Ch60 L120-122), and Jason fears "the Order of the Reaper that still existed" (Book 2, Ch60 L185). Not known: its present strength or members beyond Shade.
21. **Is Adrien Barbou Mr East?** *(Resolved in the 2026-10-22 pass.)* Yes, as the second Mr East: the previous Mr East is "the late Mr East" (Book 5, Ch8 L28, L39) and Barbou is "now going by Mr East" (Book 5, Ch30 L43; Book 5, Ch32 L5).
22. **Where does the Adventure Society's continental council sit?** *(Narrowed in the build plan 1.5 pass; the text disagrees with itself.)* Known: Book 6 puts it in Vitesse, "rather than the capital city, Cyrion" (Book 6, Ch47 L604); Book 6 also calls Rakesh, on the north coast of the Africa-like continent, "the home of the Adventure Society's continental council" (Book 6, Ch47 L623); Book 7 calls Cyrion "home to the Adventure Society's continental council" (Book 7, Ch43 L58). Greenstone's inquiries come from "the Continental Council" (Book 2, Ch10 L51; Book 12, Ch36 L51). Rakesh sits on Greenstone's continent and Vitesse and Cyrion on Estercost's, so two councils (one per continent) are possible, but Vitesse against Cyrion is a direct conflict inside Estercost that the text never explains. Compendium rule: use "the continental council" without naming a city unless the story needs one.
23. **Who leads Moon's Edge?** *(Resolved in the 2026-10-22 pass.)* Miriam Vance leads it (Book 10, Ch59 L17; Book 11, Ch33 L20); Allayeth raised and supervises the team (Book 10, Ch51 L43; Book 12, Ch16 L8).
24. **Why does Sigrid call the Mirror King "Father"?** *(Resolved in the 2026-10-22 pass.)* She does not: she greets Hastor, the king's portal specialist, who is her father (Book 3, Ch5 L49-53).
