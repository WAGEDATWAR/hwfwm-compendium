Questions the books raise and leave unanswered, or answer only in the notes or wiki. Use them as freedoms and limits in a story: a question listed here is one the compendium should not answer on the books' behalf. Each entry gives what is known, then what is not. ## 1. People and peoples

1. **Why did the Ustei come south?** Known: they raid spirit coin convoys far from home and are nomads from a region to the north (Book 1, Ch83 L4; Book 1, Ch89 L50), and they came south about three years before the Earthlings (Book 12, Ch30 L9). Not known: what drove the tribe south, or its homeland's name.
2. **Does Marek Nior Vargas lead the Unorthodoxy?** Known: Marek served Vesta Carmis Zell until recently (Book 10, Ch1 L53) and Boris says he is Unorthodoxy and that his astral king does not know (Book 11, Ch24 L86). Not known: whether Marek leads it.
3. **Who does the Unorthodoxy include among astral kings?** Known: "how many astral kings are outside the Council of Kings" is unknown to the council (Book 11, Ch24 L86). Not known: how many, or who.

## 2. Powers and the cosmos

4. **Are messengers strictly capped at silver?** Known: a silver-rank seal caps Tera Jun Casta (Book 9, Ch102 L55, L66-67) and diamond-rank messengers can begin to resist the brand (Book 11, Ch25 L53). Not known: whether rank limits are absolute.
5. **What is the debt of Zithis Carrow Vayel?** Known: when a task is complete "the price is paid" and he will have "fulfilled his debt" (Book 10, Ch15 L9). Not known: the debt, or whom it is owed to.
6. **What is Hero's sanction?** Known: Gwydion tells Gary that Hero has decided to sanction himself and has long considered it (Book 11, Ch68 L707-709). Not known: what Hero becomes or when.
7. **Did Death return the resurrection power Jason gave away?** *(Resolved in the 2026-10-22 pass.)* Yes: "I will return what was taken, Jason Asano, now that it cannot be used for its original purpose" (Book 12, Ch8 L10), and the reality material forms his prime avatar (Book 12, Ch8 L19).
8. **What do the lesser-named great astral beings govern?** Known: the Celestial Book, the Seeker of Songs, the Whisper in Corners and Legion appear as names (Book 11, Ch65 L75; Book 11, Ch68 L159). Not known: the domains of the Whisper in Corners, the Word in the Silence and the Seeker of Songs.
9. **What does the Firmamental Bridge Anchor do?** Known: it is "transcendent rank, legendary" with unreadable effects and one use (Book 8, Ch50 L97-100). Not known: its effect.
10. **Why can Jason not keep the Fundamental Realm Authority Token?** Known: Dawn says "You can't have this" and that he must give it back (Book 8, Ch51 L68-70). Not known: whether, when or to whom he returns it.
11. **How did Disguise take part in recruiting the Network's founder?** Known: the fake Purity brought an outworlder from Earth into his church (Book 13, Ch1017 L19-26). Not known: how far Disguise controlled the recruitment.

## 3. Places

12. **Is the Sky Link island Ascension's Gate?** Known: Jason and Farrah first arrived on an uninhabited island that is later a town with storm accumulators (Book 12, Ch56 L3-4), and the island renamed Ascension's Gate was seized by the Church of Knowledge's Holy Army (Book 13, Ch1023 L103). Resolved as far as the text goes: Jason names the Storm Kingdom island as the bridge's Pallimustus anchor (Book 12, Ch39 L47) and later says the Church has annexed it (Book 13, Ch1020 L95); the two names are never put in one sentence, but both describe that island.
13. **How strong is Vitesse's magic?** Known: it is a high-magic city whose manifestations are powerful and frequent (Book 12, Ch43 L47), and iron-rankers in high-magic areas are not allowed to hunt alone (Book 2, Ch53 L27). Not known: a figure.
14. **Who governs the Old City?** *(Narrowed in the 2026-10-22 pass.)* Known: Adris Dorgan is mayor of Old City (Book 12, Ch29 L38) and the Duke of Greenstone is the city's ruler, over the town constables (Book 1, Ch36 L71). Not known: whether the mayor answers to the Duke; the compendium treats that as an inference.

## 4. Events and chronology

15. **How long did the Cardinas zone last?** *(Narrowed in the 2026-10-22 pass.)* Known: Nik is "six months old" at Book 11, Ch50 L15, so the zone lasted at least about six months from his creation; Gary is "almost a year" past the Cup of Heroes at Book 11, Ch68 L703, which caps it at roughly eleven months; "months" at Book 11, Ch60 L3. Not known: an exact length; the wiki's nine months fits inside the range.
16. **What set off the System's withdrawal from regions that keep indenture?** *(Narrowed in the 2026-10-22 pass.)* Known: the withdrawal is Jason's act as System Administrator (Book 12, Ch50 L28; the petitioners ask him to arbitrate "your withdrawal of the System", Book 12, Ch51 L13), and Cyrion's house guards are a form of indenture (Book 12, Ch48 L53). Not known: whether Cyrion in particular was the trigger.
17. **What part did Gerling play at Saint-Étienne?** *(Resolved in the 2026-10-22 pass.)* Gerling did not set the device: Mr North names Shako and Chen as the ones who placed it in the aperture (Book 6, Ch35 L78-80). Gerling entered separately, taking Adrien Barbou from the EOA headquarters and forcing him to open the dimensional space (Book 6, Ch30 L43; Book 6, Ch35 L68), and later fights beside Jason in the zone (Book 6, Ch44 L25-31).
18. **When is the Book 12 present?** *(Narrowed in the 2026-10-22 pass.)* Known: "almost twenty years" since the priests were freed (Book 12, Ch13 L36), Jason's "fifteen years or so" away (Book 12, Ch21 L61), magic known "less than fifteen years ago" (Book 12, Ch2 L107) and Book 13 in 2038 (Book 13, Ch951 L50) with sixteen years gone (Book 13, Ch972 L51). All the roundings fit if Book 12 is set in 2037, with Book 13 following within about a year; the compendium uses that. Not known: the exact month.
19. **How long has the vampire war lasted?** Known: "fought off and on for decades" (Book 13, Ch1002 L69) and back "in full swing" (Book 13, Ch988 L26). Not known: how long its latest phase lasted.
19a. **How old is Emi when Rufus arrives, against the sword line?** *(Resolved in the 2026-10-22 pass: not a contradiction.)* Emi is twelve in June 2019 (Book 4, Ch25 L60) and thirteen in Book 5 (Book 5, Ch19 L76), so she is fifteen when Rufus first reaches Earth about 2021-22 (Book 11, Ch68 L782). The "six years" of Gary's sword (Book 11, Ch68 L115) are counted from late 2017 to Jason's departure into the void in late 2023, a later moment; Emi is then about sixteen.

## 5. Organisations

20. **Where does the Order of the Reaper survive?** Known: it persists as a volunteer network of quiet allies, with the trials as a lure (Book 2, Ch60 L120-122). Not known: its present strength or members beyond Shade.
21. **Is Adrien Barbou Mr East?** *(Resolved in the 2026-10-22 pass.)* Yes, as the second Mr East: the previous Mr East is "the late Mr East" (Book 5, Ch8 L28, L39) and Barbou is "now going by Mr East" (Book 5, Ch30 L43; Book 5, Ch32 L5).
22. **Where does the Adventure Society's continental council sit?** Known: Vitesse, not Cyrion (Book 6, Ch47 L604); Cyrion's branch takes over from Vitesse in Book 7 (Book 7, Ch43 L58); Rakesh, on the north coast of the Africa-like continent, is "the home of the Adventure Society's continental council" (Book 6, Ch47 L623). Not known: whether these are separate councils for separate continents (Rakesh for Greenstone's continent, Vitesse for Estercost's), which the text never says.
23. **Who leads Moon's Edge?** *(Resolved in the 2026-10-22 pass.)* Miriam Vance leads it (Book 10, Ch59 L17; Book 11, Ch33 L20); Allayeth raised and supervises the team (Book 10, Ch51 L43; Book 12, Ch16 L8).
24. **Why does Sigrid call the Mirror King "Father"?** *(Resolved in the 2026-10-22 pass.)* She does not: she greets Hastor, the king's portal specialist, who is her father (Book 3, Ch5 L49-53).
