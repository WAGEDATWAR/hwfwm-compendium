These lists use the project's source hierarchy: Book Files > Chapter Summaries > Wiki. The wiki is the lowest tier, but its lists of essences, confluences, stones and monsters are the only place those items are gathered, so they are kept here with their tier marked. A cell in the "In the book text" column gives the first place the chapter files use the name in a phrase such as "<name> essence", "<name> confluence", "essence of <name>" or "stone of <name>"; a blank cell means no such phrase was found, not that the wiki is wrong. Rarity and ability data come from the wiki's essence pages and may be incomplete or out of date. The lists are regenerated by `src/tools/compose_wiki.py`.
