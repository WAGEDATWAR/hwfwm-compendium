## 1. What is hand-written and what is generated

- **Hand-written**: `Glossary.md`, `Name_Index.md`, `Open_Questions.md` and `Change_Log.md`. Update them whenever a document is added, a term appears or a question is settled.
- **Generated**: `Source_Register.md` and `Source_Map.md` are produced by `src/tools/build_register.py`; never edit them by hand. Rebuild after any document edit.
- **Working lists** live at the compendium root: `TODO_Verification.md` (unconfirmed claims) and `TODO_Build_Plan.md` (what is left to build).
