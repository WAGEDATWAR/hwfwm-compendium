## 1. Summary

The Sapphire Crown is the royal guild of Rimaros: a guild that works directly for the royal family, whose members rotate through the royal guard. It carries real power, and is held to the same surge quotas as any other guild.

## 2. Facts

- **Purpose**: in many places the role of guard had become ceremonial; "in Rimaros, the solution came in the form of a guild" (Book 7, Ch21 L11).
- **Royal support**: it is one of the most powerful guilds in Rimaros because of the support of the royal family, many of whom are among its most capable members; in return all non-royal members "were required to periodically serve within the royal guard" (Book 7, Ch21 L11).
- **Membership**: stringent, mostly from guild families; loyalty is paramount. All members are examined quarterly, rising to monthly or weekly while serving in the guard (Book 7, Ch21 L12).
- **Quotas**: the royal family does not shield it from the guilds' activity quotas. Trenchant Moore, a human gold-ranker in a guild of celestines, carries a good portion (Book 7, Ch21 L13, L15).
- **Liberty**: even its gold-rankers have "a rich amount of liberty", an exception to the usual monitoring of gold-rankers (Book 8, Ch17 L19).
- **Standing**: Liara is "a figure of accomplishment and respect in the Sapphire Crown", where power, not legacy, is the ultimate authority (Book 7, Ch21 L32).
- **Operations**: it joins Amouz family members in sweeping the Builder cult's hideouts (Book 9, Ch5 L3), watches over a familiar ritual (Book 9, Ch7 L4), and uses the duelling arena as a training hall (Book 9, Ch17 L8).
- **Royal guards**: a speaker recalls being one of the iron-rankers from the guild assigned as royal guards, with no camaraderie (Book 9, Ch40 L43); a character is asked whether she has considered switching to it (Book 9, Ch18 L98).

- **Duty and power**: Arabelle notes it has "more personal power than the royal family, but they've been instilled with ideals of duty" (Book 10, Ch14 L37).
- **At the outpost**: it has been present at the Storm Kingdom outpost since its early days, with a full contingent led by Trenchant Moore (Book 11, Ch60 L9).
