## 1. Summary

Burning Violet is an old, elite guild based in Vitesse, tied to the Remore family and the Remore Academy. Most of Jason's friends belong to it; Jason applies in Book 12, and the application becomes entangled with a power struggle over the Remore family's control.

## 2. Facts

- **Origin and links**: "an old guild, but after Rufus' grandfather became guild leader, it became more and more associated with the Remore Academy" (Book 6, Ch47 L125). Its membership is Rufus's family plus allies such as Gary and Farrah (Book 6, Ch47 L125).
- **Members**: most big-family graduates of the Academy do not join, so it is mostly graduates who do not come from power or money (Book 6, Ch47 L127). The Academy's scouting programme gives scholarships to people with potential (Book 6, Ch47 L129).
- **Symbol**: purple flames in the shape of a flower on a belt buckle (Book 7, Ch16 L3). Farrah introduces herself as "Burning Violet guild, although I don't have a guild pin on me" (Book 7, Ch7 L53).
- **Induction**: Miles Cotezee says Jason's team has been "inducted into the Burning Violet" (Book 7, Ch16 L9). By Book 11 all of Team Biscuit are members except Jason and Taika (Book 11, Ch68 L1067).

## 3. In Vitesse (Book 12)

- **Guild house**: a large but unassuming building directly across from the Remore Academy. It moved there when Roland Remore took over the guild long ago; he no longer manages it, but his presence loomed large (Book 12, Ch43 L64). It has four sets of double doors and a cavernous lobby (Book 12, Ch43 L66-67).
- **Standing**: Burning Violet members "are elite amongst elites" (Book 12, Ch44 L26). The guild is exclusive but big, with room for "head-breakers" as well as politicians and ritualists (Book 12, Ch44 L82).
- **Membership office**: Neiman Bassingthwaite screens Jason. The guild is "not prejudicial as to the background of our members", so its roster is eclectic (Book 12, Ch45 L6).
- **The contention**: two factions in the executive, one wanting the guild free of the Remore family's influence and one opposed (Book 12, Ch45 L12). Jason's membership "threatens to be the flashpoint that could set the whole guild ablaze" (Book 12, Ch45 L14).
- **Outcome**: Team Biscuit withdraws from the guild (Book 12, Ch45 L95).
