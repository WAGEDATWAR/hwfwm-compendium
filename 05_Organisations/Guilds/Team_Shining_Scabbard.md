## 1. Summary

Team Shining Scabbard is the adventuring team of Prince Valdis of the Mirror Kingdom, built around the healer Sigrid Freyn. The text calls it a royal-backed team, not a guild; the plan's "backers" are the Mirror King's family and the kingdom's resources. This file records what is stated.

## 2. Facts

- **Origin**: Jason says Valdis, "Prince of the Mirror Kingdom", is "the reason their team exists" (Book 3, Ch3 L78). Humphrey says its members "were being prepared for Valdis's team before any of them ever received an essence" (Book 3, Ch3 L80).
- **Structure**: "their team is built around their healer, Sigrid. She facilitates and directs strategy" (Book 3, Ch3 L76); the strategic core is Sigrid and the political core is Valdis (Book 3, Ch3 L78).
- **The match**: in the mirage chamber fight Jason's team defeats it by going for Sigrid while feinting at Valdis (Book 3, Ch3 L81; Book 3, Ch4 L43).

<!-- level:3 -->
- **Team Biscuit's friends**: Team Shining Scabbard came through the Reaper trials and trained with Jason's team at iron rank, and Sigrid is Valdis's wife (Book 12, Ch15 L8).
- **Royal backing**: the Mirror King's own portal specialist portals the team to Yaresh, and Valdis says "the kingdom's thick with princes and princesses", so the King will take a good adventurer over any of his children (Book 12, Ch15 L8-9). The team has royalty in the group, "less impressive with the Mirror Kingdom's surplus of princes and princesses" (Book 12, Ch16 L11).
- **Administration**: Valdis assembles the arena event in under two days, admired by the duke (Book 12, Ch16 L11).

## 3. Not in the text

The text names no guild patrons or corporate backers for Team Shining Scabbard beyond the Mirror Kingdom's royal family.
