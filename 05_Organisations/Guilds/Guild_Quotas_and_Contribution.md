## 1. Summary

During a monster surge the Adventure Society assigns every adventurer a minimum action quota, and runs a contribution board as an incentive on top. Guilds are given the bulk of the quotas and compete for the board's non-material rewards.

## 2. Facts

- **Action quotas**: after clearance an adventurer is "assigned an action quota" and must report regularly to the jobs hall for contracts (Book 7, Ch10 L124). "Action quotas are the minimum requirements every adventurer has to fulfil during the monster surge" (Book 7, Ch12 L15).
- **The contribution board**: contracts taken beyond the quota carry contribution point values, tallied on weekly and overall leaderboards, with rewards each week and major rewards at the end of the surge (Book 7, Ch12 L15). A large leaderboard in the jobs hall shows the top hundred contributors by action quota (Book 7, Ch19 L62).
- **Who pays for the rewards**: societies, associations and governments give out hefty rewards; in return the Adventure Society "assigns high contribution point values to the contracts that provide that protection" (Book 7, Ch12 L17).
- **What guilds want**: the big guilds monopolise the top of the board, but want "access to civic services" and organisational benefits from the Adventure Society, Magic Society and Alchemy Association, not material rewards (Book 7, Ch12 L19). Material rewards are the lower-rung prizes, which small guilds and independents can reach (Book 7, Ch12 L19-21).
- **The numbers**: Rufus says that in the city (Rimaros) about three in ten adventurers are in a guild and one in ten in a high-end one, yet "guilds are given sixty percent of the quotas to fill" (Book 7, Ch12 L23). Guilds have the highest quotas to beat before they reach the boards (Book 7, Ch12 L21).
- **Guild independence**: the guilds manage their own affairs, largely apart from the Adventure Society's structure. The Society handles their large quotas through liaison officers, "a test for the guilds to prove they deserved the privileges they enjoyed" (Book 7, Ch38 L7).
- **Not shielded**: the royal family does not shield the Sapphire Crown from the quotas (Book 7, Ch21 L15).
- **Rules**: normal rewards are suspended in a surge and handed out weekly on the leaderboard; some contracts do not count towards the quota or leaderboard (Book 7, Ch19 L73).
- **Escalation**: after losses minimum action quotas are raised and safety standards lowered (Book 7, Ch66 L29).

## 3. Not in the text

The quota size per rank and how guild quotas are shared among members are not stated.
