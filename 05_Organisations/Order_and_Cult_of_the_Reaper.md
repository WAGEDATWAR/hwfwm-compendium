## 1. Summary

Two different bodies carry the Reaper's name. The **Cult of the Reaper** is the old body of worshippers of the great astral being called the Reaper, who venerate "the finality of death" and work alongside the Church of Death. The **Order of the Reaper** is a faction that split from the cult to become assassins and political power-brokers, was "destroyed" by a coalition of churches, and has quietly returned. Cults_and_Orders.md holds the short entries; this file is the full account.

## 2. The old Order

- **What it was**: an ancient cult of assassins who venerated the Reaper. Emir says they were "scoured from this world, root and branch, by a coalition of churches" (Book 2, Ch25 L73).
- **Its place**: Shade says the Order served as royal assassins ("a knife in the dark can be the deliverance of nations"), was accepted by the nations, the Adventure Society, the Magic Society and most churches, and hunted the undead: "more necromancers fell to the Order than princes or kings". The church of the Unliving and another church formed "an unholy compact" to remove it (Book 2, Ch44 L72-78).
- **The last grandmaster**: when the churches found the training facilities beneath Sky Scar Lake, the last grandmaster sent the initiates into a sealed astral space and scattered the keys, so that someone worthy might one day reclaim what was left (Book 2, Ch44 L85-89). The churches, locked out, made a second bargain with the vorger, deep-astral entities that warp those they touch into flesh abominations (Book 2, Ch44 L99-109).
- **Legacy**: the Way of the Reaper martial art and its skill books, and relics found by Emir. Shade tells Jason the Order "does not maintain a roster of assassins at all" but a "volunteer network" of quiet allies (Book 2, Ch60 L120-123).

## 3. The cult and the split

<!-- level:2 -->
- **Skill books**: the Order's techniques are designed from the foundation to include skill-book use (Book 4, Ch36 L8).
- **The cult**: it "operates in that world, or it once did", but the Reaper does not speak to it directly, which would trespass on the local god of death (Book 4, Ch3 L30). Callum Morse says the cult venerates "the principles of the Reaper. The sanctity of death" and has long worked alongside the Church of Death. The Church does public-facing things and the cult can be its "hidden dagger" (Book 6, Ch47 L588-590; Book 9, Ch27 L87).
- **Cult versus order**: "The Order of the Reaper is an offshoot of the cult", a faction that wanted to become more active in the world and accrue political power (Book 6, Ch47 L590). The order "split from the cult, mouthing affinity to the Reaper while abandoning the principles", became self-serving assassins and then falsified its demise. The Sky Scar Lake remnant was a faction that wanted reconciliation with the cult and was "sacrificed for it" (Book 6, Ch47 L592).
- **Membership**: veneration must be authentic. The cult is hard to infiltrate because it is impossible to fake long-term (Book 9, Ch27 L57). Arabelle summarises the difference: the cult follows the Reaper's principles; the order is the offshoot (Book 9, Ch27 L22-23).
- **The Reaper's view**: a vessel-form of the Reaper tells Jason the order "paid only lip service to my principles while using my name in the pursuit of secular power" (Book 11, Ch68 L1319). The Reaper says the techniques of the Way were developed by his cult and are traditionally restricted to individuals raised within the cult and trained for decades (Book 11, Ch68 L1324).
- **Shade's contact**: before returning to Earth, the World-Phoenix's avatar contacted the cult's counterpart and persuaded him to let Shade, a shadow of the Reaper, act as a vessel (Book 5, Ch56 L69).

## 4. The order's return (Book 9)

<!-- level:2 -->
Arabelle tells Jason the story as Callum gave it to her (Book 9, Ch27).
- **The long plan**: the order is bringing "centuries of planning" to fruition around twenty-five years before (Book 9, Ch27 L24). It faked the demise of its entire order as part of a plan "more than half a millennium in the making" (Book 9, Ch27 L48).
- **Method**: it infiltrates other organisations, including low-level administrative staff of churches, where deep faith is not required (Book 9, Ch27 L26). Its aim is to return to being an open secret "playing tool to those in power while pulling the levers of power themselves"; the first time, it was "too crude and got crushed" (Book 9, Ch27 L50).
- **Strikes against threats**: it made critical strikes against the Church of Purity, the Cult of the Builder and other threats, having infiltrated them and let them grow dangerous first (Book 9, Ch27 L50-51). The cult suspects it also knew about the messengers (Book 9, Ch27 L54).
- **Melody Jain**: a highly capable member from "one of the old order families". She hid a husband and daughter, Sophie, and was discovered when Sophie was small (Book 9, Ch27 L24-26).
- **Marta Fries**: Melody's best friend in the order, who warned her and helped smuggle the husband and Sophie out (Book 9, Ch27 L30). Fries is under scrutiny after Melody's defection, and later helps trace Melody (Book 9, Ch27 L91).
- **Callum Morse**: a cult member, made Melody's handler when she came to the cult. They investigated the order together for years, but the cult never truly accepted her (Book 9, Ch27 L47, L63). He is in love with her (Book 9, Ch27 L65, L78).
- **The search**: Callum steered Emir and a diamond-rank historian friendly to the cult towards collecting Way of the Reaper skill books, hoping a stray user of the techniques would turn up Melody's husband (Book 9, Ch27 L65-73). That is how Sophie and Emir came to Marta Fries (Book 9, Ch27 L81).
- **The Purity infiltration**: the cult required Melody to infiltrate the Order of Redeeming Light as a momentous gesture before it would accept her, over Callum's objection (Book 9, Ch27 L83-85). She was "cleansed" and became an artificial zealot, and Callum lost track of her (Book 9, Ch27 L90-91).
- **Melody and Jason**: she fights Jason using the Way of the Reaper and says she was trained in it before he was born (Book 8, Ch34 L41). Her therapy and the Flames' damage are covered in Melody_Jain.md and Book 10, Ch36 L13-17.

## 5. Later books (spoilers)

<!-- level:3 -->
- **A joint force of churches**: Jason's account of events on Pallimustus says the original order was "eliminated by a joint force of churches" in an event engineered with Knowledge's help, which also killed priests who would have troubled the fake Purity (Book 13, Ch1017 L18).
- **Shade's brother**: Umber says he has "been building up their numbers" while preparing for Shade's escape (Book 13, Ch1024 L122-123). Jason says Umber created whatever Thadwick Mercer has become and has turned the order into more of the same (Book 13, Ch1024 L119).
- **A bigger conflict**: Shade tells Jason he believes the events of the treasure hunt were part of "a much larger and longer conflict" (Book 13, Ch981 L56).
- **Not known**: the order's current strength, and whether it is under Umber's control beyond what Ch1024 says. See Open_Questions 20.

## 6. Using this in a story

Use the cult for stern, principled men and women who will not betray the dead, and the order for patient infiltrators who may be anywhere. Someone claiming to follow "the Reaper's way" is probably cult; someone offering a favour and a polite knife is probably order. Remember that the order can be inside any church's administration.
