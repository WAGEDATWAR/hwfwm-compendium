## 1. Summary

Earth has three old magical powers and, from Book 5 on, a fourth. The **Network** uses essence magic, the **Cabal** is older and tied to vampires and "old magic", and the **Engineers of Ascension** (EOA) is a radical group with a secret agenda. The **Asano clan** is Jason's family turned into a faction. Governments and the United Nations become players when magic is made public.

## 2. The Network

The full account is in `The_Network.md`; the notes below are the short form.

- **Origins** (Craig Vermillion, Book 4, Ch11 L4): "some five centuries ago, a new kind of magic appeared." The founder, his familiar Mr North and the link between the worlds are told in `The_Network.md`, section 6, which is their home.
- **Ranks**: tiers or categories zero to five match the ranks on Pallimustus (Vermillion, Book 4, Ch11 L8, L19).
- **Going public** (Book 5, Ch17 L7): known to the public as the **Global Defense Network** (GDN) in the weeks after the monster waves began.
- **Fracture** (Book 11, Ch36 L7; Book 12, Ch3 L3-8): by Book 12 the Network has "fractured"; a nine-person tactical unit led by Nigel Thornton stays together through the upheavals (Book 12, Ch3 L3-8). At the Nitra dome in Book 6 the Network has split into four blocs: the US branches, the Chinese branches, the True Network (the former International Committee, which kept many of the government links) and the Global Defence Network, made up of rank and file and ex-EOA and Cabal members; the Cabal has split between those serving the vampires and those siding with humanity, and the EOA is splintering (Book 6, Ch1 L22-30). Marie Finnegan, the recently expelled director of the Zurich Network branch, lives in a chalet in Zermatt, Switzerland, and is allied with the vampire queen Elizabeth and the scientist Simon McKean (head of the Earth researchers in the old workgroup) in the Book 13 plot; a dimensional bag holds seven reality cores (Book 13, Ch975 L8). Book 13 also says her backers call using Elizabeth "the smart choice" even after miscalculations (Book 13, Ch975 L19-24).
- **Methods**: tactical teams answer incursions with "hammer-blow tactics" that Jason tells Nigel will not hold against stronger monsters (Book 4, Ch41 L91-97). Lyon's branch concealed the Dimensional Fortress at Saint-Étienne for seventy years by faking the Grid (Book 4, Ch51 L30-37).

## 3. The Cabal

See [The_Cabal.md](The_Cabal.md) for the full account.

Vermillion, the Cabal's front man in Sydney in Book 4 (Ch10 L116), says "the Cabal is everywhere". Vermillion describes the Cabal as the oldest of Earth's three dominant magical forces, tied to the "old magic" of vampires and things older than history, with "a tight grip on old money literally since the invention of money" (Book 4, Ch11 L101; the three dominant forces, Book 4, Ch10 L127); its tier-four members have entered hibernation (Book 4, Ch11 L21). Messengers hid among its leaders: "even many amongst the Cabal had considered the angelic founders of their collective a myth" (Book 13, Ch954 L3-6). By Book 11 Elizabeth has taken over Venice and rules it among the vampire lords, who resent her policy of treating humans better and her warnings (Book 11, Ch68 L2256-2261, L2266); in Book 6 she keeps the peace among the vampire lords Wassily and Klaus and questions Jason about the changes (Book 6, Ch39 L71-74); Anna describes a new leader among the vampires who has separated from the Cabal, and Jason answers "I've met Elizabeth" (Book 6, Ch47 L27 to L28). Later the Cabal is said to have unambiguously split from the vampire lords and those who followed them (Book 11, Ch68 L1496). Vermillion has been a vampire for seventy years, which has brought him to the second tier (Book 4, Ch11 L19; the seventy years is not yet located). In Book 11 the Cabal holds some of the UK and Scandinavia and displaces Network factions in areas such as Russia, Africa, Sulawesi and Papua (Book 11, Ch42 L59-65).

## 4. The Engineers of Ascension (EOA)

See [Engineers_of_Ascension.md](Engineers_of_Ascension.md) for the full account.

- **Structure** (Book 5, Ch8 L14-18): four Cardinals: Mr North, Mrs South, Mr East and Mrs West. A "former Mrs South" gave the Network information about the necromancer (Book 6, Ch25 L98-99), so she left the EOA. Adrien Barbou becomes Mr East: "the previous Mr East" is "the late Mr East" (Book 5, Ch8 L28, L39), and Barbou is "now going by Mr East" beside Mr North (Book 5, Ch30 L43), "the new Mr East" with a new Mrs South (Book 5, Ch32 L5). In Book 6 Jason demands Barbou from Mr North, who refuses to give him up.
- **Methods and plans**: North reports (Book 5, Ch3 L8-12) that disabling the Grid is "proving more difficult than anticipated" and that they have shut off only localised areas. In Book 13 Jason says Plan A was to wreck the Grid, forcing the World-Phoenix to step in; Plan B was an army of super-soldiers to fight Knowledge's forces, a project that the Asano clan took up and perfected (Book 13, Ch1017 L39-43).
- **Reach**: took Perth, then Melbourne, then moved on Sydney (Book 4, Ch9 L76). Its League of Heroes appears at Broken Hill.
- **Mr North** (Noreth) is the true leader, very old and not fully human; he was a rune spider and the Founder's familiar (Book 5, Ch40 L79-83; Book 13, Ch1017 L39-40).

## 5. The Asano clan

See [EDJI_and_Post_Reveal_Institutions.md](EDJI_and_Post_Reveal_Institutions.md) for the clan as an organisation and the institutions built around it.

Jason's family becomes a faction. Matriarch Yumi (his paternal grandmother) looks a third of her age through flesh-shaping essences (Book 11, Ch36 L8) and forbids members to leave the domain. The clan's administration is run by Ketevan Arziani. It lives inside Jason's Nitra and Saint-Étienne domains from Book 11 and takes on the EOA's super-soldier programme. A rival Japanese line, the "true Asano clan" under Noriko (her rank was not rechecked), holds Asano Village after the Australian government gives it to her in Book 12. Akari Asano and Mei are of that line (see Name Index).

## 6. Governments and the UN

Governments run the artefact site, create the Multinational Supernatural Response Taskforce, in which Lenora Coleman is the designated liaison to Jason (Book 13, Ch989 L48), and employ officials such as Holly Macrossan, who succeeds Annabeth Tilden as Under-Secretary-General of the UN Office of Supernatural Affairs (Book 13, Ch982 L30) and later takes an Australian government post as Liaison to the Multinational Supernatural Response Taskforce (Book 13, Ch994 L39). Annabeth Tilden is the UN contact earlier (Book 4, Ch5 L3).
