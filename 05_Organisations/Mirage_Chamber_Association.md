## 1. Summary

The Mirage Chamber Association is the body that owns and runs the mirage chamber complex in Jayapura, the biggest such facility the books show. A mirage chamber projects illusions that feel real to those inside; the Association turns that into a training service and a league sport, and it is powerful in its city for it. It is the only named body for mirage chambers; the Geller family's private chamber in Greenstone is not part of it.

## 2. What a mirage chamber is

- **The effect**: a chamber projects things from platforms along its walls (Book 1, Ch48 L7). Those inside feel everything as real, including the impact of blows, while their bodies lie unharmed in the control room (Book 1, Ch52 L5). Illusions can only affect, and be affected by, other illusions the chamber creates (Book 1, Ch48 L16).
- **Control**: a stone block under the control room window carries runes and diagrams; Rufus edits its monster imprints (Book 1, Ch48 L3, L13). Clive knows older stone-slab controls and has helped rebuild chambers in Boko and Hornis (Book 2, Ch17 L51, L60).
- **Limits**: low magical density restricts the Geller chamber to bronze-rank level for limited periods (Book 1, Ch37 L43). No chamber can simulate a whole world (Book 2, Ch20 L88).

## 3. The Jayapura complex

- **Scale**: the complex is larger and more sophisticated than the Gellers' private chamber. Higher magical density allows dimensional magic to replicate vast spaces and run several chambers at once (Book 2, Ch73 L3).
- **Business**: it hires out training space and is the city's premier entertainment venue, with essence users facing each other or illusory challenges before a paying audience (Book 2, Ch73 L4).
- **Standing**: the Association "had close ties with the Magic Society, Adventure Society and local government" and enjoys significant power and influence in Jayapura (Book 2, Ch73 L5).
- **Setting**: it is a flat circular building at the edge of the Mystic Quarter, spilling into the theatre district (Book 2, Ch73 L6).
- **Leagues**: two leagues a year in silver, bronze and iron divisions, leading to the grand championships; there is no gold division because the professionals stop using cores before gold (Book 2, Ch73 L10-12).
- **Management**: the place is run by former participants, now gold rank (Book 2, Ch73 L14).

## 4. Not in the text

The Association's membership, fees, rules beyond the leagues and presence in other cities are not stated. The Greenstone chamber belongs to the Geller family (Book 1, Ch37 L41-43).
