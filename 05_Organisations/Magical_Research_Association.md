## 1. Summary

The MRA is Clive Standish's rival to the Magic Society: an open-access research body that grew from nothing to prominence in a handful of years, drew patronage away from the older society, and was shielded by the Adventure Society, governments and churches. The Magic Society tries to crush it, and is later shown to have been behind vandalism of the Vitesse campus.

## 2. Facts

- **Founding and growth**: "In just a handful of years since its inception, the Magical Research Association had exploded into prominence" (Book 12, Ch1 L57). Clive has gone from silver-rank team member to the head of "a rival to the Magic Society" (Book 12, Ch1 L56).
- **Open access**: where the Magic Society "leveraged its research and secrets for political power", the MRA gave open access to records and research libraries. Many prominent academics stayed with the Society for personal gain; the MRA is "a bastion for young, bold and innovative researchers" (Book 12, Ch1 L57).
- **Funding and shield**: organisations fund the MRA knowing the results will be freely available. Government authorities, the Adventure Society and several churches, especially Knowledge, contribute, and also act as a shield against the Magic Society's attempts to crush it (Book 12, Ch1 L58).
- **Projects**: it is closely associated with the new sky communication network, and rumoured to be researching a transportation network, from years of study into the network Clive tracked earlier (Book 12, Ch1 L59). Satellites solved the problem of monsters attacking relay towers (Book 11, Ch68 L2035). Its portal network project is reported to Clive by Vice Chancellor Grantham (Book 12, Ch5 L99).
- **Leadership**: Clive is Archchancellor (Book 12, Ch1 L76); Lorelei Grantham is Vice Chancellor (Book 12, Ch56 L119). Staff address Clive as "Archchancellor" throughout (Book 12, Ch2 L52).
- **Security and spies**: the Security Director finds a spy for the Magic Society and Clive throws him through the ceiling (Book 12, Ch2 L56); staff are afraid of being thrown through the ceiling (Book 12, Ch2 L60-68).
- **Campuses**: the Yaresh campus stands across the road from the Alchemy Association's research centre (Book 12, Ch22 L3). Yaresh was one of the first branches, with an outdoor testing facility whose site Clive chose, between a river and a major road, so that the city's guards protect it while the Society is kept from undermining the association (Book 12, Ch23 L3-4). The Vitesse branch is a modest campus beside the older Magic Society's (Book 11, Ch68 L2034).
- **The Magic Society's hostility**: the courts were "quite punitive" after the Magic Society was proven to be behind most of the vandalism of the Vitesse campus (Book 11, Ch68 L2034). The Society has controlled long-distance communication through the water link for centuries and is working to replicate Travis's satellites (Book 11, Ch68 L2035-2038).
- **Fame**: Clive is famous as much for the Magic Society's hatred of him as for his organisation's success (Book 12, Ch16 L10).
