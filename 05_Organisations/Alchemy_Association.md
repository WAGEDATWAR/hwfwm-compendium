## 1. Summary

The Alchemy Association is the guild of Pallimustus' alchemists. It lends its name to the potions and salves sold in a city, sits just behind the Adventure Society and the Magic Society in a city's guild district, and wants anything that advances alchemy, from Neil's miracle potion recipe to research in Yaresh. The text never describes its ranks or leadership.

## 2. Facts

- **Branding**: a tin of salve Jason buys in Book 1 is labelled "Greenstone Alchemy Association" (Book 1, Ch7 L25).
- **Membership**: Jory is "a member of the Adventure Society and the Alchemist Association" (Book 1, Ch36 L28). Belinda says the same of another person, "in the Alchemy Association and the Adventure Society" (Book 1, Ch76 L100). Dual membership is ordinary.
- **Standing**: in Greenstone's guild district the Forge Society and the Alchemist Association are "clearly second to the Adventure and Magic Societies, but still occupied impressive chunks of real estate" (Book 1, Ch38 L52).
- **Assistants**: Jason is told he could get assistants from the Association (Book 1, Ch61 L56).
- **Interest in recipes**: the Association has been "hounding" Neil for the miracle potion recipe (Book 3, Ch16 L80).
<!-- level:2 -->
- **Benefits**: organisational benefits from the Adventure Society, Magic Society, Alchemy Association "and the like" are what the top guilds care about, not material rewards (Book 7, Ch12 L19).
- **Brokered supply**: a lord brokered the sale of a crate of potions through the Association, and stock runs short until people get their next batches from the vats (Book 7, Ch14 L74).
<!-- /level -->

<!-- level:3 -->
- **Yaresh**: the Association's main research centre stands across the road from the Magical Research Association's campus, in a district the urban planners chose for places prone to "unexpected explosions, inadvertent poison fog and accidental fire titan summoning" (Book 12, Ch22 L3).
- **Directors**: the Association has a director who attends Greenstone's great gatherings with those of the Magic Society and the Adventure Society (Book 12, Ch29 L9).
<!-- /level -->

## 3. Not in the text

Its leadership, rank structure, dues, relation to the Artifice Association and what its name guarantees on a product are not stated in the lines read. The Artifice Association is mentioned only as a source of kitchen equipment and as a workshop where Travis works (Book 1, Ch91 L58; Book 7, Ch60 L94) and the Irios family are "deeply involved" in it in Rimaros (Book 7, Ch37 L62).
