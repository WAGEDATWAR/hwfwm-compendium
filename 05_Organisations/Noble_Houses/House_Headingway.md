## 1. Summary

House Headingway is one of Estercost's pre-eminent families, with its title from Cyrion, the heart of civilisation. It is humbled in Book 12 when family members clash with Jason.

## 2. Facts

- **Standing**: Garret Headingway is "an upper-echelon member of House Headingway, one of Estercost's pre-eminent families" (Book 12, Ch50 L3). Percival Headingway is the patriarch and Duke, "holder of the family's highest title", which "came from Cyrion", unlike the claimed titles of rural city-state aristocrats; he serves under the throne but his power "dwarfed even the most prestigious of theirs" (Book 12, Ch53 L3).
- **Power**: "the true power of House Headingway came from their adventurers"; adventurer training had been mandatory in the family when Percival was young (Book 12, Ch53 L5, L7).
- **Ben**: Benjamin Headingway is the family's top gold-ranker (Book 12, Ch53 L8) and represents the noble houses of Cyrion when they ask to speak to Jason (Book 12, Ch52 L6).
- **Factions**: one faction, "the pure politicians", wants a proportional political reply (Book 12, Ch53 L10); Percival knows they are wrong (Book 12, Ch53 L11). Mariska Headingway manages business affairs (Book 12, Ch53 L12).
- **Pressure**: the Sky Link Company decides whether "continuing a relationship with House Headingway is appropriate" (Book 12, Ch53 L32).
- **The affair**: the Dominion church keeps the captured family members from genuine danger (Book 12, Ch53 L6).
