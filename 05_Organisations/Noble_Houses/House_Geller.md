## 1. Summary

The Gellers are an adventuring dynasty with branches across the world and a seat at an estate in the Greenstone delta. They hold only a small provincial title, train their young hard in Greenstone, and are the richest family in the city.

## 2. Facts

- **The estate**: the Geller family estate sits behind a thick high wall, so vast that monsters are as likely to be inside as out; household guards are iron-rank essence users (Book 1, Ch37 L3-4). The seat is "a sprawling estate in the delta" (Book 1, Ch97 L50), landscaped on secure ground with artful waterways and decades of defences (Book 2, Ch17 L23-24).
- **Standing**: "The Geller family stood at the peak of Greenstone society, and their local power was just a fragment of their world-spanning influence" (Book 1, Ch56 L6). The family "sprawled across continents" (Book 1, Ch66 L4), and branch families exist: one patriarch leads a branch family (Book 2, Ch10 L28).
- **Training**: Geller family tradition is to supply scions with enough awakening stones to get started, while the rest must be earned (Book 1, Ch64 L80). The family trains its young at "the south end of nowhere" so they can make their own mistakes (Book 1, Ch49 L60), with facilities including a mirage chamber and arena (Book 1, Ch66 L18, L27).
- **Why Greenstone**: other Greenstone families envy the influence the Gellers hold elsewhere and want outside connections (Book 2, Ch32 L39). Some families "simply wanted the next adventure", as the Gellers do (Book 2, Ch39 L7).
- **The academy annex**: the Remore Academy's Greenstone annex is a joint venture with the Gellers, with an internal Geller faction opposed to the agreement (Book 1, Ch88 L3, L9).

<!-- level:2 -->
- **No title but one**: the Gellers hold only the title linked to their small provincial city of origin, refusing all others (Book 6, Ch47 L605; Book 7, Ch57 L72). They are "technically aristocracy in some inconsequential city-state somewhere" and their prestige is as an adventuring dynasty (Book 8, Ch28 L45).
- **Everywhere**: "There are Gellers everywhere, Mr Asano. You'd have to ask the Church of Fertility about that" (Book 7, Ch45 L106). Branches have towers in Cyrion and estates in Rimaros (Book 7, Ch24 L4; Book 7, Ch43 L62).
- **The seat in Greenstone**: the family kept Greenstone as its seat even as its power spread, for the experience it gives the young (Book 7, Ch66 L56).
- **Training methodology**: "you don't understand just how deep Geller family training goes" (Book 9, Ch21 L18), and people scramble to be on a team with a Geller who went through the Greenstone programme (Book 9, Ch21 L20).
