## 1. Summary

The Ketterings are a Greenstone family tied to the Mercers who send their sons to the Mercer compound. They have people in Old City and supply adventurers, including Dustin and Hudson Kettering.

## 2. Facts

- **Mercer ties**: the Davone and Kettering families have "close ties to the Mercers" and send their boys to Thadwick (Book 2, Ch27 L41).
- **Adventurers**: Hudson Kettering is on Beth Cavendish's team (Book 2, Ch15 L44, L54). Dustin Kettering, a local, takes Jonah's defender role in Rick's team (Book 2, Ch29 L8).
- **Old City**: "The Ketterings have people in Old City"; Danielle coordinates with the Kettering family to mobilise them (Book 2, Ch59 L75-77).

<!-- level:2 -->
- **Dustin**: a friend of Neil's from Thadwick's team (Book 7, Ch59 L65). His family obliged him to work with the Mercers (Book 11, Ch6 L61).
