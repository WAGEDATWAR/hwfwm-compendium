## 1. Summary

House Amouz is a Rimaros noble house that controls or has a share in more than half of the mining operations in the Sea of Storms. Its heir is captured by the Builder cult in Book 8, and the family sends its people in numbers to the cleanup in Book 9.

## 2. Facts

- **Business**: House Amouz "controls or has an interest in more than half of the mining operations in the Sea of Storms, including the bulk of high-rank mineral acquisition" (Book 8, Ch18 L85).
- **Ties to the royals**: Liara's husband married out of House Amouz into the royal family, but remains a high-level manager in the family's business interests (Book 7, Ch17 L22).
- **Heir**: Gibson Amouz is "son of Lord Cassin Amouz and heir to the seat of House Amouz" (Book 8, Ch18 L76). He is captured by the cult, which wants the family's secrets (Book 8, Ch18 L89; Book 8, Ch20 L4).
- **Lord Cassin**: a gold-ranker with a goatee, who confronts Liara over his son's capture (Book 8, Ch20 L14-19). Liara suspects the son was targeted because of what he would be told on becoming gold rank and ready to assume his seat (Book 8, Ch20 L53).
- **Volunteers**: the Amouz family volunteers in numbers that surprise even Liara in the sweep of the hideouts (Book 9, Ch5 L3).

The family's title and its seat are not stated in the lines read.
