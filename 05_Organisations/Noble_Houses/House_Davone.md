## 1. Summary

The Davones are a Greenstone family with close ties to the Mercers who were, in effect, servants of that family for years. Neil Davone is their best-known member, and by Book 12 the family's standing has changed.

## 2. Facts

- **Mercer ties**: "the Davone and Kettering families, with their close ties to the Mercers" sent their boys to the Mercer compound, where Neil spent time as a boy (Book 2, Ch27 L41-42).

<!-- level:2 -->
- **Teaming**: the Davone and Mercer families "colluded to team him with Thadwick Mercer at a young age" (Book 9, Ch21 L5).
<!-- /level -->

<!-- level:3 -->
- **Servants to the Mercers**: Neil's family had been "stuck as what amounts to servants to the Mercers for years" but now the most powerful people in the city must show them respect (Book 12, Ch29 L15).
- **House Davone**: in Book 12 Neil escorts three of House Davone's young scions, his nieces and nephews (Book 12, Ch38 L9).
<!-- /level -->
