## 1. Summary

The royal family of Rimaros rules the sky-island city and its kingdom under a Storm King. It has its own guild (the Sapphire Crown), marries into the engineering and mining houses for stability, and carries sapphire hair and eyes.

## 2. Facts

- **The guild**: the royal family supports the Sapphire Crown, and many of its own are among its most capable members (Book 7, Ch21 L11). See Sapphire_Crown.md.
- **Marriage politics**: Zara, a princess, is matched with a member of House Irios, the pre-eminent producer of defensive engineering. The union would have created political stability before the surge (Book 7, Ch18 L66-72). Liara's husband was born into House Amouz (Book 7, Ch17 L22; Book 9, Ch5 L3).
- **Family traits**: Zareen has "the royal family's sapphire hair and eyes" (Book 8, Ch62 L36).
- **Liara**: a gold-rank member of the royal house, in charge of screening adventurers for Builder cultists (Book 7, Ch17 L3; see Liara_Rimaros.md).
- **Zara**: although she left the royal family for political reasons, "the Storm King did not stop caring for his daughter" (Book 11, Ch60 L9).

## 3. Not in the text

The royal line of succession, the king's name and the size of the family are not stated in the lines read.
