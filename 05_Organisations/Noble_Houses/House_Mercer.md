## 1. Summary

The Mercers are a very powerful Greenstone family, tied to the Duke through marriage, who run spirit coin farms and shape the city's other families through their patronage. Thadwick Mercer is their heir; Cassandra is his sister.

## 2. Facts

- **Power**: "Thadwick's Mercer family was very powerful in Greenstone", and more so when "the Duke of Greenstone's brother, Thadwick's father, married into it". The family ranks above many others, especially those without aristocratic title (Book 1, Ch77 L47).
- **Residence**: their main residence is a manor on the Island, five towers set in a ring connected by glass walkways, "a feat of magical engineering" (Book 1, Ch97 L50).
- **Numbers**: no family can match the Gellers' numbers, but the Mercers "still made an impressive showing" (Book 1, Ch98 L20).
- **Farms**: Humphrey says most of the spirit coin farms are operated by his family or the Mercers, under Magic Society regulation (Book 1, Ch80 L28). The Mercers do not offer part ownership as the Gellers do (Book 1, Ch80 L77).
- **Household**: the household staff are "raked over the coals" when Thadwick is provoked (Book 1, Ch82 L101).
- **Patronage**: "Thadwick had needed friends his own age and the Davone and Kettering families, with their close ties to the Mercers, had frequently sent their own boys over" (Book 2, Ch27 L41). Neil Davone grew up at the Mercer compound (Book 2, Ch27 L41-43).

<!-- level:3 -->
- **Obligation**: Neil and Dustin were forced to train and work with Thadwick through their families' obligation to the Mercers (Book 11, Ch6 L57, L61). The Mercer family gave Neil training, facilities and resources "that only the Gellers could match" (Book 11, Ch6 L58). The Mercer family is large and Thadwick was raised mainly by his father (Book 11, Ch6 L59).
