## 1. Summary

The Remores are a family of educators and adventurers in Vitesse who hold no title but run the Remore Academy, one of the most respected adventurer schools in the world. Roland Remore, a diamond-ranker, is arch-chancellor; Rufus is the heir.

## 2. Facts

- **The Academy**: the family "operates the Remore Academy, which is a big deal everywhere, not just Vitesse" (Book 1, Ch30 L58). It runs a Greenstone training annex built with the Gellers (Book 1, Ch88 L3; Book 3, Ch26 L9).
- **Roland**: "Although Roland Remore was rarely spotted on the Remore Academy campus, the presence of the diamond-rank arch-chancellor was always felt" (Book 3, Ch69 L587).
- **Rufus as heir**: Rufus "was the prince of Remore Academy, the talented heir-apparent to the power and prestige of his family legacy" (Book 3, Ch69 L589).
- **Graduates**: an academy graduate should have better control of her aura, so students have standing among adventurers (Book 2, Ch38 L36).

<!-- level:2 -->
- **No title**: the Remore family has no aristocratic title; the family's prestige comes from "generations of accomplishment", not their name (Book 6, Ch47 L605; Book 7, Ch57 L72). Like other prestige families they are offered titles and refuse (Book 7, Ch57 L72; Book 9, Ch14 L7).
- **Scholarships**: Rufus's grandfather set up a scouting programme for people with potential, with scholarships and dormitories (Book 6, Ch47 L129).
- **Students**: scions of international mercantile guilds, adventuring families, aristocrats and royalty, taught by methodology studied from across the globe (Book 9, Ch20 L26-27). The family nurtured Rufus's talent with carefully engineered opportunities (Book 9, Ch20 L57).
- **Guild influence**: some in the Burning Violet guild think the Academy has too much influence over guild affairs (Book 11, Ch68 L1069).
<!-- /level -->

<!-- level:3 -->
- **Gold-rankers**: the Academy is famous for recruiting adventurers near or at gold rank as faculty (Book 11, Ch68 L1835).
- **The control of the guild**: the Remore family's control of Burning Violet is contested by one faction in the executive (Book 12, Ch45 L12-14; see Burning_Violet.md).
- **Teaching**: Remore teaching is "built around the concept of individualised outcomes", with the most effort for those who show drive (Book 13, Ch971 L51).
- **Allies**: they are known allies of Jason: "The son of the Remores is on his team" (Book 11, Ch24 L50).
