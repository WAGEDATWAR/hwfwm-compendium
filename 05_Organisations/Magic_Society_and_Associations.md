## 1. Summary

The Magic Society records and regulates magic: it keeps the register of essences and their combinations, oversees spirit coin farms, runs communication networks and hoards research. The Alchemy Association and the Artifice Association are the guilds of the potion-makers and of the item-makers. In Book 11 onward a rival, the Magical Research Association (MRA), challenges the Magic Society with open access to knowledge.

## 2. The Magic Society

- **The living document** (Book 1, Ch20 L95-102): a marble-and-gold tablet, connected to a central record, that updates as the record changes and holds the full list of every essence and essence combination known to the Society. It is how Farrah and Rufus look up what a combination will produce.
- **Water link**: the Society's chambers allow conversation at a distance (Belinda says in Book 10 that the chambers have been closed to the public since the attack on Yaresh, Book 10, Ch35 L36). See `../03_Magic_and_Adventuring/Alchemy_Artifice_and_Magical_Materials.md` (communication).
- **Spirit coin farms**: registered farms exist (Clive, Book 1, Ch42 L58, L64: a spirit coin that did not come from "a registered spirit coin farm" implies an unregistered one that "needs to be found and shut down"). The Adventure Society treats anything touching the farms as three-star work.
- **Greenstone branch**: directed by Lucian Lamprey (corrupt; Book 1, Ch42 L25), who uses the Fortress as his office; his deputy director Pochard Finn is an elf and a local who runs the city campus and is "the de facto director of Greenstone's Magic Society" (Book 1, Ch42 L30). Pochard is Clive's boss. By Book 11 Pochard has become director: "It's Director now, Standish. I haven't been deputy director for several years" (Book 11, Ch68 L1214).
- **Rakesh campus** (Book 6, Ch47 L623): the largest Magic Society and Adventure Society strongholds on the continent share a campus at Rakesh on the north coast. Portals work only in designated zones. Rakesh is divided into castes, and the campus holds Clive against his will, restricting his movements while using Belinda, whom it dismisses as unimportant, as a go-between. Clive realises the corruption he found in Greenstone is not unique.
- **Behaviour** (Book 12, Ch1 L57): the Society leverages its research and secrets for political power; many prominent academics stay with it for personal gain.

## 3. The Alchemy and Artifice Associations

See [Alchemy_Association.md](Alchemy_Association.md), [Mirage_Chamber_Association.md](Mirage_Chamber_Association.md) and [Magical_Research_Association.md](Magical_Research_Association.md) for the separate accounts.

Both are guilds. The Alchemy Association has been hounding Neil for the miracle potion recipe (Book 3, Ch16 L80); Jason jokes about complaining to the Artifice Association about shoddy standards (Book 3, Ch17 L60). Their internal structure is unchecked. In Greenstone's guild district the Forge Society and the Alchemist Association are "clearly second to the Adventure and Magic Societies, but still occupied impressive chunks of real estate" (Book 1, Ch38 L52).

## 4. The Magical Research Association (Level 2-3)

- **Founding and role** (Book 12, Ch1 L55-57): Clive Standish builds a rival to the Magic Society. In a handful of years it explodes into prominence. Where the Magic Society hoards its knowledge for political gain, the MRA gives open access to records and research libraries. It is a bastion for young, bold researchers.
- **Funding and protection** (Book 12, Ch1 L58): government authorities, the Adventure Society and a variety of churches, especially the Church of Knowledge, contribute, and shield it from the Magic Society's attempts to crush it.
- **Projects** (Book 12, Ch1 L55-59): the new sky communication network and a rumoured transportation network (the "portal network project" in Grantham's report), the result of years of study into the network Clive had tracked earlier.
- **Leadership**: Clive is Archchancellor (Book 12, Ch1 L76). Lorelei Grantham is Vice Chancellor (Book 12, Ch56 L119): she first appears in Book 3 as a researcher assigned to work with the Adventure Society's Continental Council, and in Book 6 she is an administrator in the Magic Society's astral magic research wing in Rakesh, where Clive is held. She runs the MRA in Clive's absence and has a portal network project (Book 12, Ch5 L99). That she is sympathetic to Clive in Book 6 is from earlier notes and was not rechecked.
