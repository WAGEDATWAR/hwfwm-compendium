## 1. Summary

The Engineers of Ascension (EOA, also written EoA) is the youngest and weakest of Earth's three old powers: a union of outsider groups that tried to engineer a higher form of humanity and prepare for the day magic became public. Four "cardinals" ran it under Mr North, and its sabotage of the Grid ended the secrecy of magic and ended the organisation. Earth_Powers.md covers it briefly; this file is the full account.

## 2. Nature and aims

- **Outsiders joined together**: it is "largely made up of those who came to magic from outside the normal channels", formed by the strongest of the smaller groups "looking for strength in numbers", and its magic is "a hodgepodge, cobbled together from what they could beg, borrow, or steal" (Book 4, Ch11 L72).
- **Decentralised**: it operates in clusters, which makes it flexible but without cohesion, and "less likely to retaliate against the loss of individual members" (Book 4, Ch11 L64).
- **Engineering themselves**: it chooses its own path "into areas the hegemonic powers won't touch" and its name means they are trying to engineer themselves to a higher state (Book 4, Ch11 L83-84).
- **Preparing for the reveal**: its driving goal is to prepare for magic being revealed. It does not itself reveal magic, and plays along with secrecy while expecting the truth to come out (Book 4, Ch11 L86-88). It expects magic to become as valuable as money or political influence, and has been inducting influential private groups and individuals where the Network took the political ones (Book 4, Ch11 L97).
- **Products**: longevity treatments with few side effects, and more radical body modification (Book 4, Ch11 L99). Its "alchemically juiced-up thugs" are the foot soldiers (Book 4, Ch11 L144; Book 4, Ch12 L4).
- **Reach**: it seizes control of Perth and Melbourne and pushes into Sydney (Book 4, Ch9 L76). Hiro Asano's interests are bought out to get him out of its path (Book 4, Ch12 L41; Book 4, Ch13 L3).
- **Conflict with the Cabal**: its blood-servant research causes conflicts with Vermillion's organisation (Book 4, Ch11 L78). Compare The_Cabal.md.

## 3. The Four Cardinals

<!-- level:2 -->
- **Structure**: four Cardinals sit around a square table: Mr North, Mrs South, Mr East and Mrs West, each with subordinates (Book 5, Ch3 L5). Mr North is "the first among equals" (Book 5, Ch8 L53). Book 13 explains the names are the cardinal directions (Book 13, Ch959 L38).
- **Adrien Barbou**: he starts as one of Mrs West's flunkies (Book 5, Ch3 L9) and proposes helping Asano rather than hammering him (Book 5, Ch3 L51). He becomes the new Mr East after his predecessor dies, and shows the previous Mr East had stalled the plan to give Cabal factions time to prepare (Book 5, Ch8 L26-28). Mr North poaches him from Mrs West's circle (Book 5, Ch32 L8).
- **The grid plan**: the EOA's final stage is to shut down the Grid. The self-repair function makes a wide-scale blackout difficult (Book 5, Ch3 L8-12). Barbou warns that the Grid will be down for months, not two weeks, which is an apocalypse (Book 5, Ch8 L44-47). Mr North decides to go ahead within a week (Book 5, Ch8 L54).
- **Mrs South's refusal**: the first Mrs South, Audrey, says the EOA's original goal was "to democratise magic", that they became what they fought, and that she will not burn the world down (Book 5, Ch8 L62). Mrs West tells her she will not leave the room, and she answers "I'll die clean" (Book 5, Ch8 L65-67). A new Mrs South is installed (Book 5, Ch32 L5).
- **Mr North**: not human, a former rune spider and the Network founder's familiar (Book 5, Ch40 L79-86). He tells Jason his minions were disappointing "before Adrien Barbou came along" (Book 5, Ch40 L86). See Mr_North.md.
- **Barbou's later conduct**: Gerling grabs him from the US (Book 6, Ch30 L29). He tells Gerling that he is "a vessel subject to the prevailing winds" and would change sides, and that Mr North knew the founder well (Book 6, Ch30 L58-60).
- **Public campaign**: the EOA tries to swing public opinion enough to demand a seat at the table (Book 5, Ch17 L8-9). In the lead-up to the reveal it works to turn Jason into an urban legend, to pave the way for its superheroes (Book 13, Ch959 L96).
- **Network opponents**: Jack Gerling, a category-four Network essence user with the Onslaught essence, is sent to fight the vampires and later to fetch Barbou (Book 5, Ch47 L29-30; Book 6, Ch30 L29). Farrah Hurin, whom Barbou once held, is the key to the Network recovering the Grid (Book 5, Ch8 L50-51). See the Gerling and Farrah files.

## 4. After the sabotage

<!-- level:2 -->
- **Splintering**: by the Nitra dome in Book 6 the EOA is splintering as the other factions hunt it, and it is blamed for the monster waves; one faction survives under a leader whose origins Jason knows are older than most of its members realise. Its strongest members are modified humans who dose themselves with magical drugs and pose as superheroes (Book 6, Ch1 L23, L28). They drop the superhero gimmick and operate more like a superhuman paramilitary (Book 6, Ch1 L29). Former members who did not know what they had joined are absorbed by the Global Defence Network (Book 6, Ch1 L27).

## 5. Later books (spoilers)

<!-- level:3 -->
- **Noreth's failure**: Noreth was an unstable element whose isolationism was "disastrous". Boris refused to take part (Book 13, Ch954 L11). His drastic disabling of the Grid created the transformation-zone crisis and spiked Earth's magic (Book 13, Ch954 L12).
- **Plans A and B**: Plan A was to wreck the Grid so that the World-Phoenix and her agents would step in. Plan B was an army of super-soldiers to fight Knowledge's forces, but North's "shortcuts and fumbling, inhumane experiments led only to abominations and madness" until the Asano clan perfected it (Book 13, Ch1017 L40).
- **The program**: it had massive death rates and damaged alien implants, and the leader was "secretly being some kind of spider" (Book 13, Ch1001 L92). The superhuman products are a haunted lot, many dying (Book 13, Ch987 L35).
- **The end**: the sabotage "was ultimately the action that spelled the end of their organisation" (Book 13, Ch970 L3). By the later years, the EOA no longer exists (Book 13, Ch953 L23). Their research on the Grid is "a dead end", but Jason has the complete records (Book 13, Ch970 L16-18).
- **Audrey Blaine**: the cardinals murdered her for resisting; she came back as an energy vampire. She ran a joint magitech program with the Cabal and the Network and was the buffer between North's ambitions and his paranoia (Book 13, Ch987 L12, L24; Book 13, Ch959 L36-38).
- **North's vault**: he leaves a vault of his centuries of research to Jason, which becomes the basis for the clan's magitech and the EOA's research (Book 13, Ch987 L14; Book 11, Ch36 L14). North never fully trusted Boris, who supplied him with spirit coins (Book 13, Ch987 L28).
- **The legacy**: the superhero branding "stuck in the public imagination", and most major cities keep their own cadre of masked heroes (Book 13, Ch959 L92).

## 6. Using this in a story

Before the reveal the EOA is the aggressive newcomer with money, drugs and superheroes, and an internal fight over whether to burn the world. After the reveal its remnants are hunted or absorbed, and the damaged survivors of its experiments are a standing problem for the other factions.
