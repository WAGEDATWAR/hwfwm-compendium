## 1. Summary

The church of Purity is the church of cleansing: it supplies the healers who treat curses and taint, and it first developed the techniques for removing star seeds. In Book 3 its leadership vanishes when its involvement with the Builder cult comes to light, and the churches later learn it has been operating outside its dictates for some time.

## 2. Role and members

- **Healers and cleansers**: the church sends a healer, a girl from the Lasalle family, with Rufus's party (Book 1, Ch65 L130). Anisa Lasalle is a priestess of Purity (Book 1, Ch20 L80).
- **Contracts**: it makes noise about the completion bonus after Anisa leaves, but the contract was through the Adventure Society and was paid (Book 1, Ch41 L87).
- **Rituals**: the archbishop prepares a ritual circle of silver-rank spirit coins and orbs; such rituals "draw on the power of ambient magic but are infused with the glorious might of the divine. My god's will moves the magic and not the other way around" (Book 2, Ch19 L79-80). He is emotionless and cold at the death of Jonah Geller (Book 2, Ch19 L99).
- **Star seeds**: the church first developed the extraction techniques (Book 3, Ch22 L35) and the archbishop holds its records on them (Book 2, Ch21 L34, L127).
- **The archbishop**: the book calls him Nicolas Hendren (Book 2, Ch19 L73; Book 3, Ch31 L57) but also "Herston, the archbishop of Purity" in one scene (Book 2, Ch21 L21), and Book 3 spells it "Hedron" once (Book 3, Ch1 L106). The text is inconsistent; this compendium uses Nicolas Hendren.

## 3. The fall of the Greenstone leadership

- **Vanishing**: following the revelation of Purity's apparent involvement with the Builder cult, the archbishop vanishes along with other key members on "previously scheduled sabbaticals" (Book 3, Ch31 L57; Book 3, Ch1 L106). The Healer's new Chief Priest takes his seat at the war council (Book 3, Ch31 L57).
- **The Lasalle family**: Anisa and "almost the entire Lasalle family" vanish with the archbishop (Book 3, Ch1 L106; see Anisa_Lasalle.md). The vanished include Anisa, who joins the Builder's cult.

## 4. The ecumenical council and the false god

- **Council**: the churches convene an ecumenical council to judge the church's behaviour; it finds the church "had been operating well outside its dictates for some time" (Book 8, Ch48 L32). People from the church are detained under its authority (Book 3, Ch5 L67).
- **Replacement**: Carlos argues that Purity has been replaced by a deception god (Book 8, Ch48; see Purity_and_Disguise.md).

## 5. The Order of Redeeming Light

- The Order of Redeeming Light is a creation of Disguise masquerading as Purity; its "Flames of Redemption" are an extreme modification of lesser vampirism (Book 10, Ch36 L14). It takes in people from outside the faithful (Book 9, Ch27 L88-89). See Cults_and_Orders.md.
- Jason's account says Disguise took over Purity's position and had Knowledge's help in removing Purity's loyal priests, including through the destruction of the Order of the Reaper (Book 13, Ch1017 L17-18).
