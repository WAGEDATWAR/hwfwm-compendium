## 1. Summary

The church of Knowledge keeps the libraries, checks the assessments and has its acolytes at hand where information matters. From Book 7 it is also quietly building a fighting force across the world, which at the end of Book 9 stops hiding.

## 2. Role and structure

- **Assessors**: the Adventure Society's assessments are checked by a priest of the church of Knowledge: "Don't try to slip anything past them, because there isn't any point" (Book 1, Ch38 L68).
- **Temple**: in Greenstone the Temple of Knowledge is the plain, blocky building on the Divine Square (Book 1, Ch45 L55). The temple library is used by researchers (Book 2, Ch3 L22; Book 2, Ch25 L37) and the goddess "always welcomes seekers of truth" (Book 2, Ch21 L129).
- **Acolytes and dress**: its members wear recognisable regalia (Book 1, Ch101 L72-73; Book 6, Ch47 L780).
- **Answer room**: the Vitesse temple has an "answer room" where the goddess manifests (Book 6, Ch47 L614).
- **Rimaros**: the main Temple of Knowledge there stands on a sky island apart from the temple district and has a "symbiotic relationship" with the Magic Society branch, with a chamber for incoming portals (Book 7, Ch9 L3-4).
- **Authentication**: it is reluctant to hire out clergy to authenticate valuables (Book 7, Ch15 L40).
- **Education**: it "does what it can" for education, but meets pushback from nobility (Book 9, Ch29 L61).

## 3. The military build-up

- **Quiet muster**: "The Church of Knowledge has been building up fighting forces around the globe. Slowly but steadily, over the last fifteen years or so"; no one knows why, even its own people (Book 7, Ch56 L62-66).
- **Cover blown**: the force is mobilised on a single day, after which it no longer cared about being noticed (Book 9, Ch42 L69-72). The god War alone knew its scale (Book 9, Ch42 L71).
- **Reaching out**: a priest of Knowledge contacts Jason's team because of Jason's relationship with the goddess (Book 9, Ch42 L57-60).
- **Representatives**: Gabrielle Pellin, a young priestess, is assigned to Travis as a shadow and representative (Book 8, Ch6 L6; Book 9, Ch13 L85) and is a priestess of the Church of Knowledge (Book 10, Ch8 L9).

## 4. Later books

- **Funding**: it is one of the churches that funds and shields the Magical Research Association (Book 12, Ch1 L58).
- **Annexation**: it annexes the island that anchors the bridge on the Pallimustus side (Book 13, Ch1020 L95; Book 13, Ch1022 L25).
- **Plan**: in Jason's account Knowledge helped Disguise pose as Purity, and in return Disguise brought the Network founder into his church (Book 13, Ch1017 L17-19).
