## 1. Summary

The church of Dominion is the church of rulership and the exercise of authority. It is the outlier among churches in keeping low-ranking essence users under its thumb. The god is often seen; his priests rarely are.

## 2. Facts

- **Temple**: in Greenstone it is "the strange temple" on the Divine Square, called the temple of Dominion (Book 1, Ch45 L15).
- **Control of clergy**: other churches make little call on adventuring clergy; Dominion's is the most prominent outlier in keeping low-ranking essence users under its thumb (Book 3, Ch69 L859).

<!-- level:2 -->
- **Absent priests**: a character thought power and control were the church's remit, but has "never even met a priest of Dominion" (Book 9, Ch49 L28-29).
- **The god's description**: Dominion tells Jason he is "oppression-friendly", not an oppressor (Book 8, Ch56 L47-48).
<!-- /level -->

<!-- level:3 -->
- **A priest's account**: Brian, a priest of Dominion, says, "The irony of our church... is that those most favoured by our god will never be in it. We guide them, and sometimes we serve them." The church does not venerate power itself, "but the establishment of power over others. Rulership. The exercising of authority" (Book 12, Ch52 L72). He calls slavery "a petty tyranny" compared to what the god looks to (Book 12, Ch52 L72).
