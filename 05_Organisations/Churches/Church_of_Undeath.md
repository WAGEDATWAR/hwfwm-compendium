## 1. Summary

The church of Undeath serves the god of undeath and raises the unliving. It is stamped out wherever it is found, lies dormant for generations, and strikes only when given a chance. Its priests are unliving things led by Garth Larosse.

## 2. Facts

- **A past plague**: a zombie plague had "a Temple of Undeath at the heart of it all", with the local mayor as high priest (Book 7, Ch9 L99).

<!-- level:3 -->
- **Why it is stamped out**: those who fall to the undead become undead servants, allies and enemies alike, "why they are so thoroughly stamped out whenever and wherever they are found" (Book 11, Ch11 L48).
- **Dormancy**: "The Church of Undeath does not act frequently. We don't give them the chance" (Book 10, Ch78 L38). It is one of the few reliable allies of the Church of Destruction (Book 10, Ch78 L27).
- **Opportunity**: a place of magic gone wrong and a hidden location is "a veritable wish list" for it (Book 10, Ch80 L40).
- **Priests**: dozens of priests of Undeath, gold and silver rank, lead the army (Book 10, Ch80 L43). They use their god's power to feed on elemental magic and make more powerful undead (Book 10, Ch78 L70).
- **Leadership**: Garth Larosse is the High Priest, himself an unliving thing nine feet tall, in a dark green cloak (Book 10, Ch80 L42; Book 10, Ch92 L65). He leads the priests (Book 11, Ch8 L20).
- **Claiming**: the high priest "could not himself" hold claim over the unliving's unified territories, which an avatar then does (Book 11, Ch24 L5).
- **A holy war**: an undead army led by priests of Undeath is raised as a question of whether it counts as a holy war (Book 11, Ch9 L100).
- **Rexion**: the Undeath priesthood overran the domains of the gods in the old brightheart city (Book 12, Ch13 L7). Jason says building a temple of Undeath would be repugnant, as a drastic act (Book 12, Ch7 L52).
