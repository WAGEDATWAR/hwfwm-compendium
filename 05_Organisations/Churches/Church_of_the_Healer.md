## 1. Summary

The church of the Healer is the institution through which most healing on Pallimustus is done, with its priests drawing their abilities from the god's essences and awakening stones. It is strongest in isolated areas, and its Greenstone branch is the subject of the first great church scandal in the books.

## 2. Role and structure

- **Healing service**: "Most healing was done through the church of the Healer"; the god supplies the essences and awakening stones; its priests can be sought out for a fee and send people to other healers (Book 1, Ch35 L23).
- **Local reach**: it is "really important in isolated areas", though "more than one church leader has been known to go a bit power mad" (Book 1, Ch38 L34). Members are found in multiple towns and villages on the road to Greenstone (Book 1, Ch96 L16).
- **Hierarchy**: the Greenstone temple stands on the Divine Square (Book 1, Ch94 L3), led by a Chief Priest who arrives with bronze-rankers, iron-rankers and lesser clergy behind. The basic robe is simple brown, though the Greenstone group wore white-and-gold silks with brown embellishments (Book 1, Ch95 L8). High Priestess is the title at city level (Book 9, Ch74 L43).
- **Adventuring clergy**: like most faiths, the church makes little call on the time of adventuring clergy (Book 3, Ch69 L859). Neil Davone is a long-time churchman (Book 3, Ch69 L859).

## 3. The Greenstone scandal

- **Replacement**: the Healer replaces the excommunicated Greenstone clergy. A replacement priest, Donal Lange, arrives through a portal the Healer creates (Book 1, Ch102 L73). Priests then stop charging for services (Book 2, Ch16 L73).
- **The new Chief Priest**: after Purity's Greenstone leaders vanish, the new Chief Priest of the Healer takes the vanished archbishop's place at the war council (Book 3, Ch31 L57).
- **Sanctuary**: the Healer makes Jory's clinic sanctified ground, which stops others breaking in for the formula (Book 3, Ch16 L82).

## 4. Specialists and the wider church

- **Star-seed work**: "The church of the Healer has taken over from Purity in dealing with the star seeds"; the Healer provided the rituals (Book 3, Ch21 L73). Carlos Quilido, a gold-rank priest, is the specialist (Book 3, Ch22 L3, L35).
- **Mental health**: Arabelle Remore is a member specialising in mental health and trauma recovery (Book 8, Ch2 L49).

- **Mandate**: the church gives Jory a mandate and funding to spread his methods around the world (Book 5, Ch43 L94). It has its own skyships (Book 6, Ch47 L482).
- **Summons**: it can call away members such as Jory (Book 7, Ch16 L6).
- **Yaresh**: Hana Shavar is High Priestess of the Healer in Yaresh and takes personal charge of refugees (Book 9, Ch74 L43).
