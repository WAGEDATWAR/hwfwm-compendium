## 1. Summary

The church of Hero is the church of the god of heroes. The books show it through its priest Gwydion Remore, its temple in the Worship Ring in Outer Rexion, and its long collection of demigod relics.

## 2. Facts

- **Priests**: Gwydion Remore, Rufus's older brother, is a priest of Hero (Book 11, Ch60 L13, L24; Book 11, Ch67 L20).
- **Relics**: the church has collected the relics of dead demigods for millennia (Book 11, Ch68 L709).
- **The temple**: a sculpture of a leonid in bronze, silver and gold with dark iron stands in the middle of the boulevard before the temple of Hero in the Worship Ring of Outer Rexion (Book 12, Ch13 L3-4, L10-11).
