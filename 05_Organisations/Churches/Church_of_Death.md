## 1. Summary

The church of Death is the Pallimustus institution for the passage of the dead: it certifies who has died and returned, helps with registration, and has long watched against necromancy. It works alongside the Cult of the Reaper, which serves as its hidden dagger.

## 2. Role

- **Certification**: before Jason registers with the Magic Society, he is told to visit the Temple of Death for formal confirmation that he is who he claims, "returned from death" (Book 7, Ch7 L64). The paperwork helps with the Magic Society (Book 7, Ch11 L83) and a certification from the Church of Death serves as identification (Book 7, Ch10 L55).
- **The certificate**: Jason's certification says he has died four times, "so far" (Book 7, Ch17 L104).
- **The temple**: it looks like a memorial centre, with white stone, manicured gardens and clergy in white robes (Book 7, Ch9 L88, L102).
- **Verification of deaths**: it is needed to confirm that neither of two missing adventurers had made a miraculous last-minute escape (Book 9, Ch11 L44).

<!-- level:3 -->
- **Watching for undead**: "The Adventure Society and the Church of Death especially" do not give the Church of Undeath the chance to act, so it lies dormant for generations (Book 10, Ch78 L38).
<!-- /level -->

- **The cult**: "The cult often works with the Church of Death... as they are closely aligned. The church does more public-facing things for the cult, while the cult can be the church's hidden dagger" (Book 9, Ch27 L87; Book 6, Ch47 L590).
