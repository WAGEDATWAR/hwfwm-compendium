## 1. Summary

The Network is Earth's largest magical organisation: a federation of old secret societies, now national and city branches, that uses essence magic, controls most of the world's spirit coin supply, and maintains the Grid that finds and contains incursions. It went public as the Global Defense Network when the monster waves began, and later fractured into four blocs. Earth_Powers.md covers it beside the other factions; this file is the full account.

## 2. Structure

- **A federation, not a hierarchy**: "the Network isn't one large hierarchy. It's an organisation of old secret societies and the International Committee is more like a United Nations than an overlord. The branches are members, not subordinates" (Book 4, Ch35 L117).
- **The International Committee (IC)**: it holds local offices, such as the Canberra office (Book 4, Ch44 L82), and global executives; Asya represents it in Sydney (Book 4, Ch43 L3). Only the agreement of a majority of member branches can act punitively against a branch; the IC itself has no such authority (Book 4, Ch51 L8).
- **Branches**: the books name Sydney, Lyon, Fiji (as a joke about living on a beach, Book 4, Ch19 L56) and Berlin, where Cleary and Gerling work at the branch with its own grid monitoring station and Steering Committee (Book 5, Ch48 L18, L50, L57). The IC also has offices in Brussels, Shanghai, New York, Johannesburg and Cairo (Book 5, Ch8 L73).
- **Steering committees**: each branch has one, which directs the agenda and takes high-level decisions; the Sydney committee has eight members and Anna is promoted into it (Book 4, Ch19 L48; Book 4, Ch59 L11).
- **Divisions**: a branch has an Operations Director (Adrien Barbou in Lyon, Book 4, Ch43 L58), a Director of Tactical Operations (Koen in Sydney, Book 4, Ch40 L9, L32), and a Harvest Division headed by Eustace Brown (Book 4, Ch43 L3). The Research Division has been "divorced from specific branches and brought under the umbrella of the International Committee" so that breakthroughs are shared across the Network (Book 4, Ch46 L80).
- **Tactical teams**: the Sydney tactical presence is two platoons of three nine-person sections, with five category threes (Book 4, Ch40 L3-4). Nigel Thornton was a section leader there before leaving (Book 4, Ch40 L32).
- **Spirit coins**: the stronger branches in the US, China and Russia are the primary source of spirit coins and resist placing tactical forces under the IC (Book 4, Ch37 L78). The Sydney branch has none of its own and depends on the IC (Book 4, Ch59 L4).

## 3. Categories and cores

- **Ranks**: Network categories zero to five match the ranks on Pallimustus (Book 4, Ch11 L8, L19).
- **Who gets what**: all executive-level personnel are given essences to reach category one; cores are mostly saved for lower-ranked enforcement members on the front line, and only committee members like Keith go to category two with cores (Book 4, Ch36 L77).

## 4. The Grid

- **What it is**: the Grid exists to identify and monitor dimensional spaces, and every branch has access to it (Book 4, Ch51 L35).
<!-- level:3 -->
- **How simple it is**: Farrah says it is "so basic that it doesn't seem magical at all", based on principles "so sophisticated they work back around to being simple" (Book 13, Ch970 L7).
<!-- /level -->
- **A concealed sector**: in 1948 the Lyon branch's committee hid an incursion space that had become permanent near Saint-Étienne by disabling the Grid in that area, and the sector has been offline for seventy years (Book 4, Ch51 L30-37).
<!-- level:3 -->
- **Its failure**: the EOA's sabotage of the Grid let proto-astral spaces break down and eject monsters, the monster wave crisis (Book 13, Ch970 L3).
<!-- /level -->
<!-- level:3 -->
- **Research**: Farrah led a Network workgroup that fixed the sabotage; the people in it became the first outside Emi to be systematically trained in core magical fundamentals, but they are forbidden to pass on knowledge of the Grid (Book 13, Ch970 L5-13).

## 5. Going public and splitting

- **The public name**: the Network "had become known to the public as the Global Defense Network in the weeks since the monster waves began", presented as the government response through "supernatural task forces" (Book 5, Ch17 L7).
- **The EOA's campaign**: the EOA tried to swing public opinion so strongly that governments would give it a seat at the table (Book 5, Ch17 L8-9).
- **Anger at the IC**: branches resent resources being allocated to fighting alongside the Americans, and Berlin is furious at losing people to the IC's order (Book 5, Ch51 L60).
- **Four blocs**: by Book 6 the Network is split into the US branches and the old leadership faction (still calling themselves the Network), the Chinese branches, the True Network (the former IC, which keeps many government links and powerful members), and the Global Defence Network (rank and file with ex-EOA and Cabal members) (Book 6, Ch1 L15-16, L25-27). The US branches staged a secret coup of the US government (Book 6, Ch1 L25).
<!-- level:3 -->
- **Later**: the fragmentation was "well over a decade ago". The Chinese and US governments absorbed or were absorbed by their branches, and the largest faction was born from the leadership of the other branches (Book 13, Ch964 L9).

## 6. Origins and the founder

This section is the home of the Network's founding story; other documents point here.

- **Five centuries ago**: Craig Vermillion says "some five centuries ago, a new kind of magic appeared." People with no connection to the old ways could wield easy-to-use powers; they were collected into secret societies that hoarded their knowledge, and their strength seemed to be limited in growth (Book 4, Ch11 L4).
- **The founder's familiar**: Mr North says he "was a rune spider, originally" (Book 5, Ch40 L79) and that his essence user, the Network's founder, "was blinded by faith" (Book 5, Ch40 L84). See ../07_Characters/Mr_North.md.
<!-- level:3 -->
- **An outworlder predecessor**: Book 13 calls the founder Jason's "outworlder predecessor", whose schemes continue to play out unseen; Boris worked with the founder's familiar to betray the founder to the early magic societies that became the Network (Book 13, Ch954 L9).
- **Jason's account**: the founder, an outworlder brought into the fake Purity's church, returned to Earth with magic Knowledge had bargained from the messengers, and built the web of secret societies and the Grid as a pressure-release valve for the rising magic (Book 13, Ch1017 L19-23). His familiar Noreth, born on Pallimustus, handed him to the US secret societies at a time Jason guesses was around the War of Independence (Book 13, Ch1017 L24).
- **Noreth is Mr North**, leader of the Engineers of Ascension (Book 13, Ch1017 L39). The wiki calls him the original creator of the Grid and the Network's founder (wiki).

## 7. Using this in a story

Treat the Network as a federation of old institutions with rivalries and long memories. Local branches act on their own interest, the IC persuades more than commands, and a branch can hide something for seventy years. A story set before the split can lean on politics and spirit coins. A story set after it should check which bloc a character belongs to.
