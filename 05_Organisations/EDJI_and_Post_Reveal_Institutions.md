## 1. Summary

After magic goes public, Earth gains a layer of governmental and joint institutions on top of the old factions: national departments of Supernatural Affairs, a United Nations office, a multinational taskforce at Asano Village, a joint magitech weapons institute, and the Earth Defence Joint Initiative (EDJI) that Jason forces into being. The Asano clan acts as a faction in its own right and supplies the knowledge on which most of this depends.

## 2. National and UN bodies

- **Departments of Supernatural Affairs**: the US Network "ultimately morphed" into the US Department of Supernatural Affairs (Book 12, Ch40 L6). Other states have ministries of the same kind: the United Kingdom has a Ministry of Supernatural Affairs (Book 13, Ch950 L49), and the US has a Secretary of Supernatural Affairs (Book 13, Ch1021 L40). Australia has one too (Book 11, Ch68 L439).
- **The UN Office of Supernatural Affairs**: Annabeth (Anna) Tilden, the former Sydney Network branch director, is its Under-Secretary-General (Book 11, Ch68 L2068; Book 12, Ch40 L15). Holly Macrossan is her successor (Book 13, Ch982 L30). After losing the UN post she becomes Australia's Liaison to the Multinational Supernatural Response Taskforce (Book 13, Ch994 L39).
- **The summit site**: after the refugee handover negotiations, Asano Village and the surrounding area are made a special-interest territory, a neutral site for formal summits between governments and magical factions, managed by a multinational organisation under Lenora Coleman (Book 13, Ch960 L14). Coleman is a bureaucrat and a core-user; most of the world's gold-rankers belong to the United States and China (Book 12, Ch61 L22).
- **The taskforce**: the "yet-unnamed multinational taskforce" at Asano Village is run by Coleman (Book 13, Ch982 L30) and is also called the Multinational Supernatural Response Taskforce (Book 13, Ch994 L39). Its members attend by hologram (Book 13, Ch994 L40).

## 3. Jason's proposal and the Accords

- **The proposal**: Jason offers the participants unfiltered access to the magical knowledge of his people, a training centre and a share in the reverse engineering of the dimensional vessel he seized (Book 13, Ch994 L45). He admits it comes with "a level of control that some might call tyrannical" (Book 13, Ch994 L61).
- **The two principles**: everything developed from the information he provides must be open source, with no proprietary data, designs or patents, and published by an agreed deadline (Book 13, Ch994 L51). The second principle is that the use of what is developed is subject to terms he enforces, and "the final word is mine" (Book 13, Ch994 L59-61).
- **Third-world debt**: he states he will focus on nations where Cabal social programs have been advancing economies, and wants third-world debt forgiven, saying he will give the nations enough spirit coins to pay it off if not (Book 13, Ch994 L53).
- **The Saint-Étienne Summit Accords**: they set up exchange programs that "raised the bar for nations around the world" (Book 13, Ch1001 L8). Research must be shared "in a controlled manner", though not freely disseminated to all (Book 13, Ch1001 L44). The Accords' secrecy feeds conspiracy theories about a shadow government (Book 13, Ch995 L84), and their terms require all research to be shared "fully and in timely fashion" (Book 13, Ch995 L57).

## 4. The EDJI

- **Origin**: Jason pushed the Earth Defence Joint Initiative for a decade. China and Russia demanded more influence in the negotiations, and the United States nearly derailed it over the spelling of "defense"; the name was changed from Earth Joint Defence Initiative to Earth Defence Joint Initiative as a face-saving compromise, and troops pronounce it "edgy" (Book 13, Ch1001 L6-7).
- **Why nations joined**: the threat of military obsolescence. The Asano clan dominated the essence-user landscape, with a whole generation trained by Rufus Remore and its training regimens eclipsing the elite US ones (Book 13, Ch1001 L8).
- **The Quilido Procedure**: a slow, resource-intensive means of removing the taint of monster cores from an essence user, unlocking those stuck at silver rank. The clan makes it available only to nations that join the EDJI (Book 13, Ch1001 L9, L44). Intelligence agencies that tried to steal the secrets were stopped swiftly and permanently (Book 13, Ch1001 L45).
- **Forces**: augmented soldiers, not essence users, are the backbone of EDJI forces, with essence users as elite specialists (Book 13, Ch1001 L96). The exosuit is a magitech exoskeleton that only enhanced individuals can use (Book 13, Ch1001 L95). EDJI uniforms carry the markings of the national army (Book 13, Ch1001 L97). Its forces mobilise across Europe against the vampires, with flying ships of essence users and Cabal forces alongside (Book 13, Ch1006 L31).
- **People**: General Michael Mills is selected as spokesperson for the EDJI member states (Book 13, Ch1020 L91). Natalie Park, now Natalie Ladiv, left ASIS for the EDJI (Book 13, Ch1019 L64). Its formation multiplied Anna's workload (Book 13, Ch1015 L39).

## 5. The Joint Magitech Weapons Research Institute

- It is headed by Miriam Coyle with Travis Noble as lead researcher; in two years it "revolutionised" weapons used against the vampires (Book 13, Ch995 L14).
- Noble was born into a US Network family and trained as a magitech weapons specialist. He was recruited to the Asano clan, taken to the other universe for years of research, and returned with weapons "unrivalled in two worlds" (Book 13, Ch1001 L48). New lightning guns are expected in general deployment within forty days (Book 13, Ch995 L15-16).

## 6. The Asano clan as an organisation

- **Yumi Asano**: Jason's paternal grandmother, the clan matriarch, who looks a third of her age and forbids members to go that far out (Book 11, Ch36 L8). Her recruitment priority is researchers, drawn by the astral cities' technology (Book 11, Ch36 L13).
- **Ketevan Arziani**: the former Australian Network branch director, taken on by Yumi after the branch was broken up; she is the clan's administrator (Book 11, Ch36 L54; Book 11, Ch68 L557).
- **The rival clan**: Noriko is matriarch of the "true Asano clan" in Australia. She says the government sided with her clan in the schism of the major secret society and gave her the land of the "upstart clan" (Book 12, Ch63 L19, L26-30).
- **Standing**: the clan is the world's centre of essence training and magitech (Book 13, Ch1001 L8, L46). See Earth_Powers.md for its holdings and domains.

## 7. Using this in a story

After the reveal the world runs on committees and uniforms. Characters can meet an EDJI officer, a Supernatural Affairs official, or a clan administrator in the same afternoon. Remember that all of the joint bodies exist because of Jason's leverage, and that his control is the part the governments resent.
