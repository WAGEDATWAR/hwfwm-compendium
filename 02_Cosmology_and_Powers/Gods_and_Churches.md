## 1. Summary

Gods are transcendent beings outside physical reality, each focused on a single concept (Dominion, Healer, Knowledge, Death and more). They manifest avatars, speak with Akashic Speech, give followers essences and awakening stones that they can take back (Book 1, Ch21 L57; Book 3, Ch61 L11), and work miracles (Book 10, Ch82 L72). In Jason's words in Book 8, "gods are like regional managers of full-blown universes". Churches are their institutions.

## 2. What gods can and cannot do

The churches as institutions have their own files in `../05_Organisations/Churches/`: [Healer](../05_Organisations/Churches/Church_of_the_Healer.md), [Knowledge](../05_Organisations/Churches/Church_of_Knowledge.md), [Purity](../05_Organisations/Churches/Church_of_Purity.md), [Dominion](../05_Organisations/Churches/Church_of_Dominion.md), [Death](../05_Organisations/Churches/Church_of_Death.md), [Undeath](../05_Organisations/Churches/Church_of_Undeath.md) and [Hero](../05_Organisations/Churches/Church_of_Hero.md).

- **Sight**: gods cannot see into an astral space that is not part of their world. In Book 10 a goddess says she cannot see into the astral space Jason is holding prisoners in; in Book 8 a priest says his god cannot see inside a building hidden from the gods (Book 10, Ch31 L69-75; Book 8, Ch43 L48-53).
- **Domains**: gods cannot enter another's spirit domain ("higher rules for higher beings", Book 6, Ch24 L86); gods cannot reveal other gods' deceptions, being bound by rules of interplay (Book 8, Ch48 L22-26).
- **Miracles**: "if you want a miracle, you need one of my servants or a grand sacrifice" (a god to Jason, Book 10, Ch82 L72).
- **Death certification**: the Church of Death certifies deaths; in Book 7 an official is shown a certification that Jason has died four times "so far", and in Book 10 Jason shows Hana Shavar the card (Book 7, Ch17 L104; Book 10, Ch38 L73).
- **Essences and stones**: gods bestow divine versions of regular essences and divine awakening stones (Book 1, Ch21 L57; Book 2, Ch13 L60). They return to the god: freed cult captives with divine essences are weaker because the power "was not consumed by the process but returned to their god" (Book 3, Ch61 L11), and ex-clergy stripped of divinely gifted essences suffer a "debilitating imbalance in the body and soul" (Book 4, Ch11 L16). When the Healer casts out priests who exploited their healing, Dominion offers to take them into his service (Book 1, Ch95 L90-94).

## 4. Gods and churches in the story

Each of the following has its own file in `Gods/`: [Knowledge](Gods/Knowledge.md), [Death](Gods/Death.md), [the Healer](Gods/The_Healer.md), [Hero](Gods/Hero.md), [Purity and Disguise](Gods/Purity_and_Disguise.md), [Dominion](Gods/Dominion.md), [Undeath](Gods/Undeath.md), [Fertility](Gods/Fertility.md), [Hearth](Gods/Hearth.md), [Destruction](Gods/Destruction.md) and [Pain and Discord](Gods/Pain_and_Discord.md). The notes below are the short form.

### The Healer
God of medicine and healing. The Greenstone temple was corrupt and had to be corrected by divine intervention; Neil makes a stand in the temple in Book 1, where a priest tells those who stand against the church that they are no longer welcome (Book 1, Ch94 L3-7). After the revelations about Purity, "the church of the Healer has taken over from Purity in dealing with the star seeds" (Book 3, Ch21 L73); a Healer priest, Carlos, specialises in soul damage from star seeds (Book 8, Ch17 L45). Hana Shavar, an elf and High Priestess of the Healer, is in charge of operations that use Jason's cloud palace as a base after the messenger attack (Book 10, Ch3 L29; the city's priestess, Book 9, Ch74 L43); during the attack she avoided Jason's bunker and sent her priests into Emir Bahadir's (Book 9, Ch80 L6-13); Carlos Quilido is a Healer priest who joins the Cardinas expedition (Book 11, Ch67 L6).

### Dominion
The god of rulership. A believer says "Dominion issues the divine right to kings and nobles" (Book 1, Ch45 L18), but Dominion tells Jason "There is no divine right of kings. Mortals choose and I try to help them not make a complete mess of things" (Book 12, Ch13 L67). His church is the most prominent outlier in keeping low-ranking essence users under its thumb (Book 3, Ch69 L859), and he tells his new clergy "There is no service in my church... you will obey or suffer" (Book 1, Ch95 L94). He tells Gary "That is where monarchs come from. I love them most of all. I'll be keeping an eye on you, Gareth Xandier" (Book 1, Ch95 L104), and a character says in Book 9 that he likes "the ones who refuse to kneel" (Book 9, Ch41 L11).

### Purity and Disguise (Level 2/3)
Book 10 states that the Order of Redeeming Light was created by Disguise, a god masquerading as Purity: its Flames of Redemption are an extreme modification of lesser vampirism, and the order was founded only after Disguise took on Purity's mantle (Book 10, Ch39 L46-49, Arabelle and Carlos). Jason tells Melody Jain that her god is "an illusion. A disguise" (Book 10, Ch40 L16). In Book 8 Carlos Quilido tells Jason his theory that the gods may have sanctioned Purity long before, and that rules of interplay stopped them telling their clergy (Book 8, Ch48 L40-60). In Jason's Book 13 briefing, Purity, who embodies several contradictory meanings, was unstable about five hundred years ago and likely to sanction himself; Knowledge interfered; and a relic of Purity's divine power was left behind (Book 13, Ch1017 L14-24). Purity's most zealous worshippers carry many purgation tools (Book 7, Ch35 L39); the wiki's claim that the doctrine treats humans as the "pure" state is not used here.

### Knowledge (Level 3 for the plot)
In Book 1 Jason meets the goddess as a woman with colourful clothes and Mediterranean features, beautiful yet "detached and untouchable" (Book 1, Ch46 L16); she knows everything anyone in her world knows, including Jason, so she knows "Mario Kart shortcuts" (Book 1, Ch77 L10). In Book 13 Jason tells his allies that she has been preparing to invade Earth for half a millennium and used him as a key part of the plan; the briefing says she promised the messengers' leader a chance to invade Pallimustus and claim Purity's relic, and that around a third of the priests and priestesses of Knowledge are her spies (Book 13, Ch1017 L14-20). When Gabrielle Pellin refuses to believe it, a cube leaves her chest into Knowledge's hand, Gabrielle seizes, and Travis catches her (Book 13, Ch1023 L139). Jason answers Knowledge's claim that she will be "the one true God" of his world with "The next time we meet, Goddess, will be the day you die" (Book 13, Ch1024 L153).

### Death (Level 3)
The goddess of death appears to Jason in the Cardinas death chamber as "a stocky, middle-aged woman with a colourful dress" with the warmth of "a matronly tavern keeper" (Book 10, Ch82 L62). Her pact seals any ability to resurrect himself or another, persists through any change in his nature, and is enacted by the goddess guiding his soul to change itself, without her gaining access to it. In return he gains the title Keeper of the Cycle: abilities that apply afflictions also apply ghost fire, mana drain degrades the animating force of undead, and allies in his aura are affected by ghost fire while he suppresses undead (Book 10, Ch83 L75-78). In Book 12 Gordon suggests asking Death to return the resurrection power Jason gave away (Book 12, Ch6 L18-22), and Death does: "I will return what was taken, Jason Asano, now that it cannot be used for its original purpose" (Book 12, Ch8 L10), the reality material forming Jason's prime avatar (Book 12, Ch8 L19).

### Hero (Level 3)
The god of heroes is "the only core deity not to have subordinate gods" (Book 1, Ch48 L92). Those who make the ultimate sacrifice are offered the Cup of Heroes (Book 10, Ch89 L54; Gary drinks it, Book 10, Ch86 L59, and says "I'm only a demigod", Book 11, Ch1 L9). Almost a year later Gary is a haggard figure kept alive by Hero's power (Book 11, Ch68 L703-708). Gwydion tells Gary that Hero has decided to sanction himself, something he has considered for longer than anyone has been alive (Book 11, Ch68 L707-709). When Gary drinks it, his hammer, fire and iron essences become divine versions and his forge confluence is replaced by a Demigod essence (Book 10, Ch86 L67-70), not a "Hero" essence.

### Undeath (Level 3)
Hana Shavar says (Book 10, Ch78 L38) that the Church of Undeath lies dormant for generations and strikes when its god has "saved up his chances to intervene", bringing "dark miracles". Garth Larosse, a zemravore and priest of Undeath, leads the church's priests in Cardinas (Book 10, Ch88 L58-63; Book 11, Ch29 L3-5). Undeath is one of the gods with only hidden strongholds (Book 10, Ch85 L40).

## 5. Churches in practice

Churches provide healing, ritual rooms, scribes and, in some cases, armies. A church can be corrupt or reformed by divine intervention. The Church of Knowledge funds the Magical Research Association (Book 12, Ch1 L58).

## 6. Dangers and hooks

A fraudulent god, hidden temples, divine bargains with a price, a god's claim on a follower. Hooks: a temple raid, a divine gift with a catch, a priest who learns that their god's authority is a mask.
