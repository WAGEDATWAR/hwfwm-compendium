## 1. Summary

The goddess of Knowledge is the god Jason meets first and speaks to most. She is detached, polite, honest in form and a planner at heart, and she knows everything anyone in her world knows. Her church is scholarly and wealthy and funds research, and from Book 6 on it is shown mustering armies. By Book 13 the books reveal she has worked for centuries to invade Earth.

## 2. Facts

- **Appearance**: in the Divine Square a glorious light appears in front of a temple and the people fall to their knees (Book 1, Ch45 L31). In her temple she takes the form of a woman with colourful clothes and Mediterranean features, "beautiful, yet there was something detached and untouchable about her" (Book 1, Ch46 L16). She says "I am Knowledge. Everything that is, or ever was known in this world" (Book 1, Ch46 L22).
- **Omniscience, limited**: she knows what anyone in her world knows, including what Jason brought with him (Book 1, Ch46 L22). Jason says in a recording that deities are gender fluid, "heard from the goddess of knowledge herself" (Book 1, Ch77 L8), and jokes that she knows his Mario Kart shortcuts (Book 3, Ch57 L87).
- **The church**: acolytes of the church check Adventure Society assessments, "don't try to slip anything past them, because there isn't any point" (Book 1, Ch38 L68). Gabrielle Pellin introduces herself as an acolyte (Book 1, Ch101 L72). Danielle Geller will send her scholarly relatives to the temple's library, as "the goddess always welcomes seekers of truth" (Book 2, Ch21 L129).
- **Gifts to Jason**: a token the goddess claimed would send him home (Book 2, Ch63 L48) and books on dimensional transgression, which Clive annotates (Book 3, Ch3 L14).

## 3. Later developments, Books 4 to 9

<!-- level:2 -->
- **Answers**: Knowledge gives answers people need rather than those they want. In the Vitesse temple she tells Sophie, "Now is not the time to pursue this goal" (Book 6, Ch47 L614-616).
- **Temples**: the Rimaros temple is an important resource for magical researchers and has a symbiotic relationship with the Magic Society (Book 7, Ch9 L4).
- **Jason's survival**: Jason hopes it will not be a big deal that people knew he was alive because "Knowledge told people I was alive" (Book 7, Ch4 L58).
- **Armies**: Knowledge's church has been mustering forces around the world (Book 9, Ch42 L69), with a small holy army near Yaresh (Book 9, Ch36 L21; Book 9, Ch42 L80) that waits to strike the Yaresh force from behind (Book 9, Ch104 L76).

## 4. Later developments, Books 10 to 13 (spoilers)

<!-- level:3 -->
- **Teasing**: she appears to Jason as a bookish woman in brown robes beside an expedition crawler, and he is still piqued that she did not tell his friends he was alive. She answers, "I'm the goddess of knowledge, Jason, not of very confident guesses" (Book 10, Ch67 L19-23).
- **Funding**: the Church of Knowledge is one of the churches that funds the Magic Research Association (Book 12, Ch1 L58). Armies of nations and other gods lacked her forewarning (Book 12, Ch24 L3).
- **The plan**: in Jason's briefing Knowledge helped Disguise pose as Purity, and in return he brought the Network founder into his church. She promised the messengers' leader a chance to invade Pallimustus and claim Purity's relic, and spent centuries preparing Earth for invasion (Book 13, Ch1017 L15-29). Jason says he was "exactly what she needed" and that she tried to recruit him (Book 13, Ch1017 L32, L38).
- **Annexation**: the Church of Knowledge annexes the island that anchors the bridge on the Pallimustus side, seizing Storm Kingdom territory (Book 13, Ch1020 L95; Book 13, Ch1022 L25).
- **Gabrielle**: a priestess of Knowledge protests that she is not of Deception or Conquest (Book 13, Ch1023 L131), and a cube leaves her chest into the goddess's hand (Book 13, Ch1023 L139).
- **Confrontation**: Knowledge tells Jason the time has come "to fulfil your destiny... and open the gateway that I might claim your world" (Book 13, Ch1024 L133). She says he cannot sanction her, since that is for other gods (Book 13, Ch1024 L141), and he answers that he will kill her (Book 13, Ch1024 L146, L153).

## 5. Using this in a story

Knowledge is helpful in the way a very good librarian is helpful: she answers the question that was asked, and also the question behind it, and she never lies. Her motives are the issue. A scene with her should feel civil and slightly chilling, because she has seen how the scene will go. Her church is wealthy, careful and influential, and a priest of Knowledge is both a source of information and a possible agent.
