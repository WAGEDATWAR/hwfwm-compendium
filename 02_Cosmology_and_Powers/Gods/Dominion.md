## 1. Summary

Dominion is the god of rulership, the god "of deciding who gets to be king". He is young, handsome, sneering, charming and frank, he is the god most interested in Jason, and he turns up in person more often than any other. His church is the outlier among churches in keeping low-ranking essence users under its thumb. For all his menace he gives Jason surprisingly honest advice.

## 2. Facts

- **The temple and the creed**: a believer in the Divine Square calls it the temple of Dominion and says "Dominion issues the divine right to kings and nobles. It is he who determines who rules, and who serves" (Book 1, Ch45 L15-18).
- **First appearance**: at Jory's clinic he comes to take the Healer's cast-out clergy, "young and handsome, but with a look of disdain and faint cruelty", with an aura like a wave of fire (Book 1, Ch95 L87). He tells those who join: "There is no service in my church... under me, you will obey or suffer. Or both, as I choose, because you will not enter my service. You will belong to me" (Book 1, Ch95 L94).
- **Jason and Gary**: "I love that guy," Dominion says of Jason, because "the ones who won't kneel are always the most fun" (Book 1, Ch95 L101). He tests Gary with his gaze, laughs when Gary stands, and says "That is where monarchs come from. I love them most of all. I'll be keeping an eye on you, Gareth Xandier" (Book 1, Ch95 L103-104). He asked after Jason by name (Book 1, Ch97 L7).
- **Thanks in person**: at the priest camp he comes with other gods in purple and gold robes with a simple golden crown, and tells Jason "You never disappoint" (Book 2, Ch65 L75-76). Jason calls him a "work acquaintance", someone he would not take for after-work drinks (Book 2, Ch66 L104-106).
- **His church**: the church of Dominion is the most prominent outlier among churches in keeping low-ranking essence users under its thumb (Book 3, Ch69 L859).

## 4. Later developments, Books 10 to 13 (spoilers)

<!-- level:3 -->
- **The sin essence**: Jason's sin essence brought out the authoritarian tendencies that got Dominion's attention (Book 10, Ch66 L43). The goddess Liberty "likes to keep a wary eye on Dominion's favourites" (Book 10, Ch31 L61).
- **A voice in the meeting**: Dominion interrupts a call with the Yaresh authorities, and Jason calls his offer "half-arsed power-plays" (Book 10, Ch16 L76-87). A speaker notes that "the god of dominion—the god of deciding who gets to be king—showed up for a chat, and not for the first time" (Book 10, Ch18 L129).
- **Jason's view**: "Dominion is still kind of a dick, though" (Book 10, Ch83 L55), and the power becomes who he is, "not good for anyone. Except the god of dominion, maybe" (Book 11, Ch2 L81).
- **Advice**: after Jason handles a fanatic poorly, Dominion appears by the arm-shaped temple, saying "It's religion. Showmanship is part of the deal" (Book 12, Ch13 L47). When Jason asks for guidance, he suggests giant banners with his face (Book 12, Ch13 L56). He tells Jason "There is no divine right of kings. Mortals choose and I try to help them not make a complete mess of things" (Book 12, Ch13 L67). A gift from Dominion helps Jason contain his presence (Book 12, Ch17 L27).
- **Not what people think**: a character says Jason told her Dominion isn't what most people think, and that Dominion said Jason should let people find their own solutions to indenture (Book 12, Ch49 L82-84).
- **His priests**: Brian, a priest of Dominion, serves as Jason's ally in the Cyrion crisis, saying that his church does not venerate power but "the establishment of power over others. Rulership. The exercising of authority", and that slavery, a subordinate god's domain, is "a petty tyranny" (Book 12, Ch52 L50, L72).

## 5. Using this in a story

Dominion should be fun and dangerous in equal parts. He is charming, speaks his mind, never lies about what he wants, and enjoys defiance more than obedience. A scene with him should leave the character a little more informed and a little more worried. His church is the grim counterpart to his personality: strict, hierarchical and unforgiving.
