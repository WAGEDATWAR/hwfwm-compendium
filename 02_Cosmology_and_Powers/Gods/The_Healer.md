## 1. Summary

The god of medicine and healing, the Healer is kind, modest in manner and strict with those who exploit the sick. His church runs clinics, hospitals and research. In Book 1 he casts out the corrupt clergy of the Greenstone temple in person, and in later books his priests, among them Neil, Carlos and Arabelle, are central members of Jason's team.

## 2. Facts

- **Corruption in Greenstone**: Neil Davone makes a stand in the Greenstone Temple of the Healer, saying "This isn't about serving the Healer", and is told he is no longer welcome in the church (Book 1, Ch94 L3-7).
- **The god arrives**: the Healer appears at Jory's clinic, tells his clergy that merely not being shunned from his temples is "mercy enough", and refuses to restore their essences: "For that, I shall give you no salve. But you may turn to another" (Book 1, Ch95 L84-86). He says the power-seekers are of no use to him and hands them to Dominion, who takes those willing (Book 1, Ch95 L90-95).
- **A blessing**: he declares the clinic a sanctuary for healing, accepts that Jory's alchemy includes "togetherness jelly" ("I'm not going to begrudge people a little... togetherness"), and promises rituals of sanctification and a healer to staff it (Book 1, Ch95 L120-126).
- **Those who repent**: he answers a plea from a priest with "You blame others for your failings?", and gives a slight nod of assent to those who accept responsibility (Book 1, Ch95 L106-109).
- **The church in practice**: Neil reminds enemies he is "part of the church of the Healer" (Book 3, Ch21 L46), and Carlos introduces himself, "First and foremost... I am a priest of the Healer. Before anything else, I'm here to help you" (Book 3, Ch22 L73). The church opposes forms of punishment such as necromancy techniques (Book 3, Ch32 L101).

## 3. Later developments, Books 4 to 9

<!-- level:2 -->
- **Attackers' banner**: some of those who attacked the Order of the Reaper's fortress wore Healer robes, which Jason finds at odds with the picture of the church as intolerant, calling it "revisionist history" (Book 2, Ch58 L77-78).
- **Specialists**: Carlos specialises in soul damage from star seeds (Book 8, Ch17 L45). The church takes over from Purity in handling them (Book 3, Ch21 L73).
- **Research**: Carlos and three assistants from the Church of the Healer live and work in Jason's astral space (Book 9, Ch28 L44), and Carlos, Arabelle and Neil advise on the design of the hospital in Jason's palace (Book 9, Ch71 L38). Hana Shavar is High Priestess of the Church of the Healer in Yaresh (Book 9, Ch74 L114).
- **Compensation**: Neil says the Healer gave healing powers that can no longer resurrect the ability to heal "on a body-matrix level" to compensate for what Death took away (Book 8, Ch39 L146).

## 5. Using this in a story

The Healer is the gentle god whose gentleness has teeth. He does not argue and does not punish more than needed, but he does not give a second chance to those who treat the sick as a business. His priests are skilled and usually modest, and a team with one has a better chance of surviving a fight and a reason to listen to its healer's conscience.
