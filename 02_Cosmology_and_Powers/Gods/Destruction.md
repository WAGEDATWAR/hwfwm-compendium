## 1. Summary

Destruction is a god of the hidden kind, worshipped by those who want to destroy rather than rule. His church is small, secretive and well hidden, and its allies include the Church of Undeath. In Book 10 he takes an interest in the natural array under Yaresh, whose catastrophe or whose tree-turned-apocalypse-beast would serve him, and he speaks to Amos Pensinata through a prisoner priest.

## 2. Facts

- **The hidden kind**: Carlos explains that many gods fall into two camps: those with temples anywhere there are people, and "the gods whose priests dwell in the hidden places. Gods who you pray to not because of something you want, but something you want to avoid. Pain, Discord, Deceit" (Book 8, Ch48 L51). Destruction is not on his list, but the Book 10 priests work in the tunnels and the temples are hidden (Book 10, Ch66 L101); the wiki also counts Destruction among the hidden gods (wiki).
- **The priests under Yaresh**: captured priests are found with a messenger communication orb in the tunnels of the natural array (Book 10, Ch66 L84-86).
- **The voice of the god**: when Amos Pensinata presses a captured priest, "a new aura shrouded the prisoners, unmistakable in its divinity", and Destruction's voice rumbles "like an avalanche": "The souls of my priests are mine to toy with, not yours" (Book 10, Ch66 L90-93). Jason, as a nascent astral king, is unaffected by the god's will (Book 10, Ch66 L94-97).
- **His reply to a threat**: "You threaten the god of destruction with destruction? There is nothing you can do to me... Destroying my temples only fulfils their ultimate purpose... There are always those hungry for power to replace them... Those who hunger for power, not to dominate but to destroy" (Book 10, Ch66 L103). The priests explode (Book 10, Ch66 L104).
- **His aim**: Miriam says the god has taken an interest (Book 10, Ch73 L54). Jason suspects his goal is not the array blowing up but the tree: "he wants it to become some kind of apocalypse beast that devours every living thing on this planet" (Book 10, Ch73 L55). Emir adds that Destruction "wasn't too fazed by us taking out his priests" (Book 10, Ch78 L22).
- **Allies**: "The Church of Undeath is one of the few reliable allies the Church of Destruction has" (Book 10, Ch78 L27). Undeath's priest Jeff has a grandmother who is a high priestess of Destruction (Book 10, Ch88 L85).
- **The limits of the god**: Jason argues that if Destruction had the power to wipe them all out, he would have done so instead of talking (Book 10, Ch75 L25). Death will not say what Destruction had planned, since it is another god's affair (Book 10, Ch67 L34).
- **Outcome**: the god of Destruction was hoping for a seeping wound in reality from the transformation zone (Book 11, Ch41 L97). Jason tells the Undeath priests to step back and take stock "who won and who lost" (Book 11, Ch62 L18).

## 3. Using this in a story

Destruction is the god who does not need to win a fight: he needs his people to be hungry, and there will always be hungry people. His church is not a conquering army but a cult of saboteurs and fanatics, and a story that confronts it should expect to lose temples and win nothing permanent. He is best at pushing a larger catastrophe, as at Yaresh, rather than acting in person.
