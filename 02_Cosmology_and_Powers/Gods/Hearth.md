## 1. Summary

Hearth is a god of home and shelter. The books show only its church at work: its priests organise people who have lost their homes, alongside the church of Refuge. This file records what the text says.

## 2. Facts

- **The Healer's Hearth ritual**: Neil asks whether a procedure is "the Healer's Hearth ritual" (Book 9, Ch67 L33), suggesting a ritual named for Hearth is used by the Church of the Healer.
- **Organising refugees**: after Boko's destruction, Jason says "The priests of Hearth and of Refuge will be organising the people. Who wants to settle permanently, who wants to find a new home, and who wants to wait for the reconstruction of Boko" (Book 12, Ch39 L103).
- **In Jason's kingdom**: the clergy in his astral kingdom stay for his journey to Earth. The first contingent came from the churches of Hearth, Refuge and Dominion, and others joined as the work expanded, mostly for the logistics of a population in the tens of thousands (Book 13, Ch951 L33).

## 3. Not in the text

No scene shows the god, a temple, a named priest or its relationship with other gods. The wiki lists Hearth and Refuge as gods first mentioned at the Boko refugee scene (wiki).

## 4. Using this in a story

Hearth is the church for displaced people: a temple that finds rooms, food and a place to belong. Use it for refugee stories, new settlements and logistics, and give it a calm, practical priesthood.
