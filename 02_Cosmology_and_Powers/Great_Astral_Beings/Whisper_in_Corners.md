## 1. Summary

The Whisper in Corners is a great astral being Jason had never heard of before meeting Raythe and Velius. It joined the fight against the consequences of the sundering and appears in Book 11 as a veiled woman in lace whose voice comes from every direction.

## 2. Facts

- **Position on the sundering**: it is among those who "have come around and will fight" (Book 11, Ch65 L75).
- **Appearance**: "the woman in lace" (Book 11, Ch68 L159), laced and veiled, "not a great astral being Jason had heard of" before (Book 11, Ch68 L231).
- **Voice**: "Her mouth did not move, her voice instead a susurrus of whispers all around them" (Book 11, Ch68 L2160).
- **On forcing souls**: it warns the World-Phoenix that there are lines even great astral beings should not cross, and asks "Will you become like the Builder, World-Phoenix? Forcing your way into unwilling souls" (Book 11, Ch68 L2160). It later asks why she would entice others to stand against her (Book 11, Ch68 L2180).

No domain, vessel, church or cult is described. The name hints at secrets, but the books do not state its sphere.
