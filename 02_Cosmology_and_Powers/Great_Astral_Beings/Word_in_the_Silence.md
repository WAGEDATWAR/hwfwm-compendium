## 1. Summary

The Word in the Silence is a great astral being named only once. The text lists it among the beings that "stood apart" from the rest, even before the sundering.

## 2. Facts

- **Apart from the pacts**: Velius says of those who stood aside from the sundering, "They have ever stood apart from the rest, even before the sundering. The Keeper, the All-Devouring Eye, the Word in the Silence" (Book 11, Ch64 L49).

## 3. Not in the text

No domain, vessel, appearance, church or cult is described, and the being does not appear in the Book 11 battle or throne room.
