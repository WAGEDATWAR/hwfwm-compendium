## 1. Summary

Legion is the great astral being that administers the cosmic rules governing life itself. It is one of the beings that joined the pacts after the sundering, then came to think the sundering a mistake. In the Book 11 battle it appears in a mortal body as a deep-voiced man with antlers and a beard.

## 2. Facts

- **No blessings**: Legion and the All-Devouring Eye are not known to hand out blessings (Book 7, Ch11 L75).
- **Domain and realm**: Legion "administers the cosmic rules that govern life itself", and its realm abuts the All-Devouring Eye's region, a place "where life and annihilation are neighbours" (Book 8, Ch55 L92). A sanguine horror was summoned from a "nightmare realm where the Limitless Legion met the Final Domain" (Book 9, Ch62 L23).

<!-- level:3 -->
- **Position on the sundering**: Legion is among those who "have come around and will fight" (Book 11, Ch65 L75).
- **In a mortal body**: "the man with the antlers" (Book 11, Ch68 L159). It speaks in a "deep, rumbling voice" and objects to vessels that Jason provided: "There are rules" (Book 11, Ch68 L161). It looks impassive behind its beard (Book 11, Ch68 L178).
- **Its view of the plan**: it tells Jason that he could have given them far more advantages (Book 11, Ch68 L229), and says "We do not get 'used in turn'" (Book 11, Ch68 L238). Fighting beside him, it says "You are throwing away a chance we have waited eons for" (Book 11, Ch68 L261).
- **Against the World-Phoenix**: it says Jason agreed to help and that "we exploited that to come here", and explains that it is easier to make an immoral choice when you are not doing so alone (Book 11, Ch68 L2162, L2181).

## 3. Using this in a story

Legion is a being of law: it cares about the rules of life, expects them to hold, and prefers a disciplined plan to improvisation. It is a good voice for a great astral being who is more worried about consistency than about people.
