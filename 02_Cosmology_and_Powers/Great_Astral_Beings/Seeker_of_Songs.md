## 1. Summary

The Seeker of Songs is a great astral being whose domain the books never state. It was against the sundering from the start. It appears in Book 11 in a mortal vessel as a woman with blue eyes, and likes music from Jason's world.

## 2. Facts

- **Position on the sundering**: Raythe says the Celestial Book and the Seeker of Songs "were both against the sundering from the start" (Book 11, Ch65 L75).
- **Appearance**: "the woman with blue eyes" among the great astral beings in mortal bodies (Book 11, Ch68 L159).
- **Manner**: it identifies a phrase as "a song from Asano's world: 'Rumble in the Jungle' by Fugees, featuring A Tribe Called Quest, Busta Rhymes and John Forté" (Book 11, Ch68 L175). When the others complain at being used as a training tool it says "this man is hosting the largest gathering of great astral beings since the sundering, and he's using us as a training tool?" (Book 11, Ch68 L236).
- **Against the World-Phoenix**: it calls hijacking Jason's soul while his willpower is diminished "unconscionable" (Book 11, Ch68 L2164), and in the throne room, when the Reaper calls ceremonies pointless, says "I rather like them" (Book 11, Ch68 L2530-2531).

## 3. Not in the text

No domain, vessel, church or cult is described. The name suggests an interest in music, and the being is shown enjoying it, but the books do not state its sphere.
