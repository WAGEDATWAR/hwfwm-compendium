## 1. Summary

The All-Devouring Eye is the great astral being of annihilation. It does not communicate with the others, gives no blessings, and stands apart from the pacts. Its realm in the deep astral is where Gordon and the apocalypse beasts come from, and the great astral beings believe it created Gordon for the purpose of repairing the Cosmic Throne.

## 2. Facts

- **Among those interested**: it is one of the handful of great astral beings that take an interest in physical realities (Book 2, Ch21 L92).

## 3. Later developments, Books 7 to 9

<!-- level:2 -->
- **Its realm**: "The realm of the All-Devouring Eye" is where Gordon comes from, and Colin comes from the border territory with Legion's realm (Book 4, Ch32 L36).
- **No blessings**: Legion and the All-Devouring Eye "didn't hand out blessings" (Book 7, Ch11 L75).
- **Region**: it is "where I, and your friend Gordon, come from, and it abuts the realm of Legion, the great astral being whom administers the cosmic rules that govern life itself. Your sanguine horror comes from this place, where life and annihilation are neighbours" (Book 8, Ch55 L92).
<!-- /level -->

## 4. Later developments, Books 10 to 11 (spoilers)

<!-- level:3 -->
- **A silent being**: the Eye "is not one for communicating, even with other great astral beings", and the great astral beings believe it created Gordon for the throne repair (Book 11, Ch64 L91). "There is little point asking questions of the All-Devouring Eye" (Book 11, Ch64 L93).
- **Apart from the pacts**: it stands apart with the Keeper and the Word in the Silence (Book 11, Ch64 L49). Its stance is "least active of us all", a vessel says, and its last intervention is long ago (Book 11, Ch22 L38).
- **Avatars of doom**: they "are the exclusive domain of the All-Devouring Eye"; it has never employed its reality assassins for any task, instead loaning them to the Sundered Throne, for reasons nobody knows (Book 11, Ch34 L97).
- **The seed that grew**: Jason is "a seed that the World-Phoenix planted, but he has grown in ways only the All-Devouring Eye anticipated" (Book 11, Ch22 L29).
- **Its vessel**: an orb of leathery flesh, larger than a person, draped in a dark green cloak, whose front is an open mouth ringed with needle teeth and a single huge eye inside (Book 11, Ch68 L2526).
- **Its connection**: a speaker suggests Gordon's potential comes from "the connection of his kind to the Sundered Throne or the All-Devouring Eye", but will not elaborate (Book 10, Ch5 L94).
<!-- /level -->

## 5. Using this in a story

The Eye is the great silence behind Gordon. Keep it unreadable: no speeches, no demands, only effects. Anything it has caused looks like a coincidence until someone realises the pattern, and the beings who know it best are least willing to talk about it.
