## 1. Summary

The System is the interface of windows, ranks and messages that Jason carried alone from Book 1. When he restored the Cosmic Throne, an echo of him was imprinted on it, and his interface became available to everyone in the cosmos with essences or the potential to get them. He administers it through the System Administrator gift, with limits, and in Book 12 withdraws it from regions that keep slavery or indenture.

## 2. How it works

- **Before**: in Books 1 to 10 the interface is one of Jason's outworlder gifts, a private window, inventory and quest log; see `../03_Magic_and_Adventuring/Unusual_and_Unique_Magic.md`.
- **The announcement**: on every world with magic, every person who has essences or the potential for them sees a screen saying "Don't Panic. Everything is going to be fine. Welcome to the System", in whatever medium the species uses to communicate (Book 11, Ch68 L2535-2540).
- **Why**: "The throne could never be restored to what it was. Part of you was imprinted upon it. An echo of you, spread across the cosmos", and "You made your interface available to everyone with essences or the potential to get them" (Book 12, Ch1 L34-36).
- **What it does by default**: it lets people view their own information and nothing more, but species gift evolutions and essence abilities are expanding the base effects, as they did for Jason (Book 12, Ch1 L38).
- **How it arrives**: the System waits for a moment of safety before revealing itself to an individual (Book 12, Ch1 L44). Most deaths come from reactions to the System, not the System itself: religious furore, superstition, and mass killings to keep the new power from teaching oppressed people (Book 12, Ch1 L46).
- **Where it runs**: it imprints itself on devices such as mirage chamber projectors, and people love the integration (Book 12, Ch20 L21-22).
- **Terms**: the prime avatar's sheet uses "nature" instead of "race", and Shade suggests the term now has to cover the breadth of individuals the System serves (Book 12, Ch8 L100-102).

## 3. Variations and exceptions

- **The System Administrator gift**: it gives access to all aspects of the system, with extras such as maps, voice and image chat, party and raid groups and assessing creatures and objects, which Jason can grant to others (Book 12, Ch67 L197). It represents his relation to the System, akin to a great astral being's, with most control sealed until he reaches full transcendence (Book 12, Ch10 L14).
- **Limited control**: Jason says he has limited ability to manipulate it, around his avatar and in his domains, and cannot "use it like it's my personal toy"; there are rules and they apply to him (Book 12, Ch42 L92). In Book 13 he still lacks full control of it and a lot is autonomic (Book 13, Ch1020 L9).
- **Jason's perception**: the prime avatar quiets his perception of those connected to the System, "a cosmos worth of muted mumbles" that is sealed away so it does not destroy his mind (Book 12, Ch8 L70-71).
- **Not everywhere**: it is withdrawn from regions that keep slavery or indentured servitude (see below).
- **Disbelief**: Anna Tilden suggests the System might be some cosmic force reaching Earth that Jason is leveraging early access to, and Jason answers with a System Administrator window: "I'm being as open as I can, Anna" (Book 12, Ch42 L99-101).

## 4. Examples from the books

- **Quick recognition**: the System is "swiftly linked" to Jason by the organisations that had studied his abilities, and the Adventure Society sends Miles to find Clive (Book 12, Ch1 L54).
- **The withdrawal**: Jason sends letters to the powers holding Earth expatriates, saying the matter is not a negotiating position and that to have the power and do nothing is an abdication (Book 12, Ch50 L13-16). A System message says the Administrator will "shortly withdraw System access" in regions operating with slavery, indentured servitude or similar legal systems, with no System functions, alternate functionality for integrated abilities and diminished or failed rituals, for an indeterminate period (Book 12, Ch50 L28-29).
- **The reaction**: a mob gathers outside Jason's palace in Cyrion after the System goes dark (Book 12, Ch51 L3). The Queen of Estercost and others ask Vandrick to arbitrate "your withdrawal of the System" (Book 12, Ch51 L13).
- **The diamond-ranks' position**: Vandrick tells them that the diamond-rank community holds that the System "is yours to administer" (Book 12, Ch51 L59). Every new diamond-ranker gets one big world-changing action, and Jason's is using the System to push the abolition of slavery (Book 12, Ch51 L76-77). He is told to handle mortal affairs like a mortal and immortal affairs like an immortal (Book 12, Ch51 L82-84).
- **The cost**: a Cyrion house adventurer says Jason's ideals about slavery "mattered to no one until you took away the System" (Book 12, Ch52 L24), and warns him that he has made many enemies by taking it away (Book 12, Ch52 L30). A speaker for a trading house notes that the loss of the System was already an issue, though they can live without it (Book 12, Ch53 L34).

## 5. Common misconceptions in-world

- **"The System is a natural cosmic phenomenon."** Anna's fear that it is "some cosmic force" is answered by Jason's window (Book 12, Ch42 L99-101).
- **"Jason can pick who gets it."** The withdrawal covers whole regions at once, and access is reviewed region by region on his return (Book 12, Ch50 L29).

## 6. Changes over time

- **Book 11**: the System appears across the cosmos when the throne is restored (Book 11, Ch68 L2535-2540).
- **Book 12**: it is linked to Jason within days (Book 12, Ch1 L54) and withdrawn from regions with slavery (Book 12, Ch50 L28).
- **Book 13**: a System alert reads "[System Administrator] has identified a dimensional infraction and initiated an intervention action. The Hegemon has arisen. Beware his wrath," and everyone old enough for essences sees it (Book 13, Ch1014 L28-30; Book 13, Ch1020 L6-7). Jason says the system is part of him "like a spleen" (Book 13, Ch1020 L11), and four teams are linked by his interface in a raid (Book 13, Ch958 L51). Knowledge refers to him as "The System" in his coming role as a great astral being (Book 13, Ch1024 L141).

## 7. Using this in a story

Treat the System as part of Jason that everyone can use. Anything that appears in a window can be traced to his will or his autonomic reflexes, and he does not control it all. It is both a gift and a point of leverage: a power that can be withdrawn is a power that people fight over, and a character who sees it as impersonal is wrong.
