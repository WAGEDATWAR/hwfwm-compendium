## 1. Summary

The series leans on a handful of repeated phrases and running jokes. This file lists each with the chapters where it recurs, so a narrator can echo it without inventing a catchphrase. Only the short phrases themselves are quoted. The file supports Tone_and_Voice.md; it is level 3 because later uses reach Book 13.

## 2. Spoken incantations

- **"Feed me your sins"**: Jason's line when he casts his sin-feeding spell, used across the early books: first in the delta (Book 1, Ch34 L34), then again in Book 1 (Book 1, Ch39 L65; Book 1, Ch63 L6; Book 1, Ch69 L37; Book 1, Ch87 L32; Book 1, Ch95 L51), in Book 2 (Book 2, Ch8 L93), in Book 3 (Book 3, Ch28 L87; Book 3, Ch44 L57), and in Book 9 (Book 9, Ch8 L30; Book 9, Ch89 L47). Another adventurer in Book 2 asks "Did you just say 'feed me your sins?'" (Book 2, Ch54 L93).
- **Use**: it is a spoken trigger for his spells and is also a joke the others notice; keep it short and use it at the moment of casting.

## 3. Running gags

- **Biscuits**: the chapter "Jason Has the Good Biscuits" (Book 1, Ch78 L1) features Jason feeding biscuits to a shape-shifting familiar (Book 1, Ch78 L85-96). In Book 7 Stash demands biscuits from Clive and Sophie (Book 7, Ch6 L13-29), and Humphrey names the team Team Biscuit (Book 7, Ch56 L74). Marcus Xenoria wonders if "one of their familiars" picked the name (Book 10, Ch47 L78).
- **Handsome Doctor**: Jason mishears Hassan Dukhtar's name and keeps using the mistake despite corrections (Book 13, Ch1016 L23-31).
- **Pants and the lack of them**: Jason arrives with no pants and the first reward is a pair of simple linen ones (Book 1, Ch1 L19; Book 1, Ch3 L13-18). The gag recurs in chapter titles such as "A Better Pants Solution" (Book 4, Ch1) and "Of Course He Doesn't Have Pants" (Book 7, Ch1).
- **Clive's non-existent wife**: a stranger accuses Clive of sleeping with a wife he does not have (Book 2, Ch32 L106), and Clive reports it later (Book 2, Ch5 L123).
- **The sausage sizzle**: Jason runs a charity-style barbecue as a social move, in a joke about "a permit to host a charity sausage sizzle" (Book 9, Ch73 L44) and again when he feeds evacuees (Book 12, Ch36 L7-8).
- **Chuunibyou**: Jason says he is having "a chuunibyou moment" when he first wears his dark robe (Book 1, Ch61 L118-122), jokes that the ship has "well and truly set sail" (Book 1, Ch75 L62), and is later told he is more chuuni than ever (Book 5, Ch58 L20).
- **Captain Tremendous**: Jason mocks the cultists' nickname for him, proposing alternatives such as "Captain Tremendous" (Book 3, Ch32 L106-107).

## 4. Pop-culture references (Earth idiom in Pallimustus)

- **Star Wars**: his brother says he is pretty much "a lord of the Sith" in costume (Book 4, Ch25 L11-12); he calls Dawn's recorded face an Emperor Palpatine hologram (Book 9, Ch6 L17); he compares Garth's laugh to Skeletor (Book 10, Ch92 L42); he calls his own look "Sith" (Book 8, Ch25 L28), and the Knowledge priests look like Jedi to his Sith (Book 10, Ch9 L12).
- **Mario Kart**: Jason notes that Knowledge knows every Mario Kart shortcut anyone in her world knows (Book 1, Ch77 L10) and suggests the Builder and Knowledge play it (Book 3, Ch57 L87).
- **Other titles and nods**: "By the Power of Grayskull" (Book 1, Ch20 title) and "The Full Keanu" (Book 1, Ch50 title); Jason explains the Stanislavski system to Gabriel (Book 2, Ch33 L94).

## 5. In-world sayings

- **Greetings and address**: "G'day" and "mate" (Book 1, Ch38 L55), "Strewth" (Book 2, Ch44 L130). See Tone_and_Voice.md.
- **Personas**: the media name the cloaked figure the Starlight Rider and the healer the Starlight Angel (Book 4, Ch15 L12; Book 5, Ch11 L12).
- **Adventurers' proverbs**: iron-rankers "aren't allowed to hunt by ourselves" in areas of high magic density, Keane says (Book 2, Ch53 L27); see Tone_and_Voice.md for others.

## 6. Using these in play

- **Do not overuse them**: echo one motif per scene and keep it short.
- **Honour the level**: Handsome Doctor is a Book 13 gag; do not use it in Book 1 scenes.
- **Do not invent**: if a character is not shown using a phrase, do not give it to them.
