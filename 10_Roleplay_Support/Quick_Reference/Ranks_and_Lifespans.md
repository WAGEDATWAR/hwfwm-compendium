## 1. The ladder

The sequence is iron, bronze, silver, gold, diamond (Book 1, Ch22 L58). Normal people have fewer than four essences; four essences make iron rank (Book 1, Ch15 L100; Book 1, Ch21 L65-66). Each rank "has a suppressive effect on lower ranks" (Book 1, Ch19 L42).

## 2. Table

| Rank | Lifespan and body | Line |
|---|---|---|
| Normal | Fewer than four essences, no rank | Book 1, Ch15 L100 |
| Iron | Ageing is not noticeably slower; the body is purged of impurities and digestion changes | Book 1, Ch22 L58; Book 1, Ch22 L9; Book 2, Ch34 L73 |
| Bronze | Can live well past a hundred years | Book 1, Ch22 L58 |
| Silver | Can double that; a young riser looks young for decades; auras let the body levitate | Book 1, Ch22 L58; Book 4, Ch73 L92 |
| Gold | Lives for centuries and no longer needs to breathe | Book 1, Ch22 L58; Book 2, Ch34 L73 |
| Diamond | Rufus is not sure diamond rankers can die of old age; they can travel between physical realities | Book 1, Ch22 L58; Book 2, Ch25 L49 |

## 3. Other quick facts

- **Diamond is the highest rank** Rufus names (Book 1, Ch22 L50-51).
- **Diamonds in cities**: the Adventure Society keeps two or three in the largest cities "in case a diamond rank monster shows up" (Book 1, Ch22 L55).
- **Badges**: adventurers "need new badges at each rank", because the aura changes and tracking stones stop working (Book 3, Ch32 L88).
- **Progress to bronze** counts essences whose abilities are complete (Book 3, Ch14 L98).
