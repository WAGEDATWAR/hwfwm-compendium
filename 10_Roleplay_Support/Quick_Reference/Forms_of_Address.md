## 3. Names

- **Palindromic names** are assigned in adulthood and denote important members of a family (Book 7, Ch18 L42).
- **Mononyms**: Zato says his name is "my only name" (Book 3, Ch57 L121-122).
- **First names** are a step in a relationship (Book 5, Ch35 L71).
- **Jason's order**: "My surname is Asano and my personal name is Jason" (Book 9, Ch100 L23).
