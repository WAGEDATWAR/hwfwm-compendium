## 2. Shared facts

- Most Greenstone temples stand on the Divine Square (Book 1, Ch45 L15, L55; Book 1, Ch94 L3).
- Most churches make little call on adventuring clergy; Dominion is the outlier (Book 3, Ch69 L859).
- Gods give essences and awakening stones that return to them (Book 3, Ch61 L11).
