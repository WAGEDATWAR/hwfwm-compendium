## 1. Summary

For each of four places, a before-and-after table keyed to the major events. Use it to set a scene in an earlier or later period without carrying over the wrong state. Where the books say nothing about a period, the cell says so rather than guessing.

**Boko**: in Era 1 an old desert city of academics and artists with a Duke and an Adventure Society branch (Book 1, Ch109 L7-21). In Era 7 it is destroyed after Jason's assassination; most of its ninety thousand people are evacuated (Book 12, Ch32 L15-29; Book 12, Ch33 L3-4), the crater becomes a harbour (Book 12, Ch39 L37) and floating temples shielded by their gods remain (Book 12, Ch33 L3).

## 6. Using the tables
