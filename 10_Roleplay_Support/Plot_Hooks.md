# Plot Hooks by Era

Status: Draft (book text; facts carry a reference "Book N, ChX LY" to Source_Text; "notes" marks chapter-summary-only facts)
Spoiler level: 0 for the hooks themselves; each era marks later facts
Valid from: Book 1
Valid through: Book 13
Sources: Books 1-13 (chapter files)
Related: 00_Roleplay_Overview.md, Era_Selection.md, Spoiler_Management.md, NPC_Rosters_by_Era.md

## 1. Summary

Open situations in each era that a scene can start from. Each hook names a situation the books establish (with its line) and a question for the scene to explore; none of them states an outcome the books do not give.

## 2. Facts

**Era 1: Greenstone**
- **The Ustei in the desert**: nomads from the north are raiding spirit coin convoys far from home (Book 1, Ch83 L4; Book 1, Ch89 L50). *Why did they come south?*
- **An unregistered spirit coin farm**: a coin that did not come from a registered farm means an unregistered one "needs to be found and shut down" (Book 1, Ch42 L58, L64). *Who runs it?*
- **A corrupt branch**: the Magic Society's local director was banished to Greenstone for improprieties and uses the Fortress as his office (Book 1, Ch42 L25-30). *Who protects him?*
- **An indenture sale**: criminals' indentures are sold or auctioned to recoup the cost of their crimes (Book 1, Ch92 L32). *Who buys a particular indenture, and why?*
- **The Healer's temple**: a priest tells those who stand against the church that they are no longer welcome (Book 1, Ch94 L3-7).

**Era 2: Bronze and the Builder**
- **Star seeds**: a captured adventurer will not consent to examination, and his family must authorise it (Book 2, Ch10 L26-50). *Who else carries a seed?*
- **Purity under inquiry**: an ecumenical council investigates the church of Purity (Book 3, Ch1 L105).
- **A rain elemental over the Old City**: it appears in the city and bronze-rankers gear up to confront it (Book 3, Ch6 L18, L43).
- **Hunting Killian Laurent**: Jason's team spends days in the hunt for the man who possessed a star seed (Book 3, Ch24 L3).
- **The veldt contract**: an unconventional contract east of Greenstone beyond the desert astral space (Book 3, Ch27 L62).

**Era 3: Earth**
- **The EOA moves on Sydney**: they have "seized control in Perth and Melbourne, and now they're making no secret of their overtures into Sydney" (Book 4, Ch9 L76).
- **The Dimensional Fortress**: a sector of the grid has been offline for seventy years (Book 4, Ch51 L30-37).
- **Grid sabotage**: shutting down only localised areas, with a wider failure expected in two months (Book 5, Ch3 L8-19).
- **Reality cores**: one appears in each affected zone and the factions scramble for it (Book 5, Ch45 L9-11).
- **A domain nobody can enter safely**: gold-rankers explore Jason's Nitra domain until the hostile effects grow dangerous (Book 6, Ch23 L3).
- **The atomic device**: Jason, Farrah and Travis work on a semi-disassembled device on a cold winter day (Book 6, Ch31 L4).

**Era 4: Rimaros**
- **The outworlders**: a hundred and nineteen appear at once and must be placed (Book 7, Ch36 L86).
- **A political marriage that fell through**: Zara was matched with a member of House Irios for stability before the surge (Book 7, Ch18 L66-72).
- **Guild quotas**: guilds receive sixty percent of the surge quotas though few adventurers belong to them (Book 7, Ch12 L23).
- **Towns massacred under cover of monster attacks**: the Order of Redeeming Light disguises its conversions and killings (Book 8, Ch9 L40).
- **A kidnapped heir**: Gibson Amouz is spirited away from the island (Book 8, Ch20 L4).

**Era 5: Yaresh and the messengers**
- **A messenger camp**: Knowledge's army and the messengers set camp outside Yaresh while the city's government and Adventure Society worry (Book 9, Ch42 L80).
- **Servant races and masters**: messenger masters may treat servants as they please within limits (Book 9, Ch56 L5).
- **An alliance that will betray**: Emir calls the plan to ally with Jes Fin Kaal one in which she will betray them (Book 10, Ch10 L64).
- **The Aristocratic Faction**: landowners with no adventurers behind them undermine the Duke (Book 10, Ch14 L28-35).
- **A branded messenger**: Tera Jun Casta's brand was her astral king's tool for keeping her at silver (Book 10, Ch2 L19, L40).

**Era 6: Cardinas**
- **A buried city**: Cardinas lies under the mountains, ruled from the Citadel of Pillars, where fewer than ten thousand brighthearts survive (Book 10, Ch65 L3-6).
- **Undeath's patience**: the church lies dormant and then acts all at once (Book 10, Ch78 L38).
- **A soul forge**: messengers were sent down to create it from the natural array (Book 11, Ch1 L64).
- **A hero's price**: Gary drinks from the Cup of Heroes and the divine power will try to leave him (Book 10, Ch86 L59-61).

**Era 7: The return**
- **Gathering the Earthlings**: seventy-four are to return and about thirty cannot or will not (Book 12, Ch53 L57).
- **Indenture and the System**: the System will withdraw from regions that keep indenture (Book 12, Ch50 L28), and the Queen of Estercost asks Jason to arbitrate (Book 12, Ch51 L13).
- **A pirate admiral's ship on Earth**: Jota Withers is hosted by the Asano clan matriarch (Book 12, Ch57 L12; Ch63 L19).
- **A vampire war turning in humanity's favour**: it is "back in full swing" (Book 13, Ch988 L26) and Europe is largely liberated but vampires hold a few cities (Book 13, Ch1006 L59).
- **Finnegan, Elizabeth and McKean**: Finnegan's backers called using Elizabeth "the smart choice" (Book 13, Ch975 L19-24), and there has been no confirmed trace of McKean for over four years (Book 13, Ch998 L105).
- **Knowledge's long plan**: she has been preparing for around half a millennium (Book 13, Ch1017 L14).

## 3. Using this in a story

- **Use one hook per scene.** Pick the hook from the era you chose, make sure its spoiler level matches (`Spoiler_Management.md`), and let the books' own situation do the work.
- **Do not invent resolutions.** Where a hook asks a question, the books may or may not answer it; check the character and place files before improvising.
