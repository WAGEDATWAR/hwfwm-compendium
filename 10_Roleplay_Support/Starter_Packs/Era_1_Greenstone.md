## 1. Setting

- **Where and when**: the low-magic colony city of Greenstone and its delta, from late November on Jason's arrival (Book 4, Ch3 L112) through the Reaper contest of Book 2.
- **Scale**: iron rank; Jason's badge is iron with one star (Book 1, Ch62 L66-67). Field testing happens at the start of each month (Book 1, Ch38 L158).
- **The city**: Old City is where the money is made and the Island is where it goes (Book 1, Ch35 L19, L59); see ../../06_Places/Greenstone.md.
- **Turning points**: Farrah dies covering civilians (Book 1, Ch113 L41); Emir's contest and the City of Fallen Echoes free 419 time-displaced priests (Book 2, Ch63 L9).

## 2. Three starting situations

1. **The Ustei in the desert**: nomads from the north are raiding spirit coin convoys far from home (Book 1, Ch83 L4; Book 1, Ch89 L50). *Why did they come south?*
2. **An indenture sale**: criminals' indentures are sold or auctioned to recoup the cost of their crimes (Book 1, Ch92 L32). *Who buys one, and why?*
3. **A corrupt branch**: the Magic Society's local director was banished to Greenstone for improprieties and uses the Fortress as his office (Book 1, Ch42 L25-30). *Who protects him?*

## 3. Cast and voice cues

- **Jason**: deadpan about the absurd: "Question marks. That's enlightening" (Book 1, Ch1 L108).
- **Rufus**: courteous and formal; gestures with a sausage on a stick while explaining guild quotas (Book 7, Ch12 L22-23).
- **Farrah**: lectures on any subject (Book 1, Ch23 L15-17); alive only until Book 1's end.
- **Gary**: deep and growling (Book 1, Ch9 L121).
- **Humphrey**: precise and a little shy about romance (Book 1, Ch47 L88-89).
- **Clive**: a lecturer who jokes about wrestling an eel (Book 1, Ch68 L92-94).
- **Sophie**: wary of kindness (Book 2, Ch1 L77-80).
- **Danielle Geller**: blunt, quick on a weakness (Book 1, Ch47 L89).
- **Emir**: smooth and showy (Book 3, Ch5 L9).
- **Authority**: Elspeth Arella (Book 1, Ch65 L60), Vincent Trenslow (Book 1, Ch54 L36), Lucian Lamprey and Pochard Finn (Book 1, Ch42 L25-30). More in ../NPC_Rosters_by_Era.md.

## 4. Live dangers

A traditional blood cult in the hedge-maze estate (Book 1, Ch9 L50); the Ustei raids (Book 1, Ch83 L4); Old City's "Big Three" crime lords (Book 1, Ch50 L92); star seeds implanted through a hidden cult (Book 2, Ch21 L26-28).

## 5. What the characters know

- **Jason is an outworlder**: the team and Danielle know by Book 1 (Book 1, Ch21 L82-87; Book 1, Ch48 L101-105).
- **Party interface**: Jason's allies use his party chat (Book 2, Ch50 L8; Book 2, Ch65 L89).
- **Star seeds and a cult's aim to seal an astral space**: known to Greenstone's elite from Book 2 (Book 2, Ch21 L19-28, L123).
- **Not known**: who the Builder is, the Order's real state, Knowledge's plan, the System.

## 6. Narrator language that stays spoiler-safe

- Say "a hidden cult" before Book 2, Ch21; say "star seeds" only after.
- Say "the goddess of Knowledge" without hinting at a plan.
- Do not mention the System as a world event, Earth's magic or the vampire war.

## 7. Leave closed

Who the Builder cult's leaders are beyond what has been shown; the Order of the Reaper's actual survival; Jason's return to Earth.
