## 1. Setting

- **Where and when**: the Storm Kingdom, a city of islands in the Sea of Storms (Book 7, Ch5 L56); Jason arrives in a cyclone as the surge begins (Book 7, Ch1 L56).
- **Scale**: silver rank in the Storm Kingdom, where sky islands house the cream of society (Book 7, Ch5 L65).
- **Turning point**: the Astral Throne and Gate appear (Book 8, Ch49 L105).

## 2. Three starting situations

1. **The outworlders**: a hundred and nineteen appear at once and must be placed (Book 7, Ch36 L86). *Who takes them in?*
2. **A political marriage that fell through**: Zara was matched with a member of House Irios for stability before the surge (Book 7, Ch18 L66-72). *Who benefits from the scandal?*
3. **A kidnapped heir**: Gibson Amouz is spirited away from the island (Book 8, Ch20 L4). *Where was he taken?*

## 3. Cast and voice cues

- **Royals**: Soramir (Book 7, Ch18 L42) is dignified; Zara is courtly with a threat in the smile (Book 2, Ch69 L17-19); Liara is brisk and official (Book 7, Ch17 L3).
- **Locals**: Pelli is blunt (Book 7, Ch13 L51); Argy is laconic (Book 7, Ch13 L31); Trenchant Moore is reserved (Book 7, Ch21 L13).
- **Team Biscuit**: named in Book 7 (Book 7, Ch56 L74); Gabrielle Pellin is stern and accusing of Jason's methods (Book 7, Ch9 L7).
- **Opponents**: Melody Jain and the Order of Redeeming Light (Book 8, Ch18 L3-6).

## 4. Live dangers

Invasion platforms (Book 7, Ch8 L36); the One Day War (Book 7, Ch66 L27); guild quotas that give guilds sixty percent of the surge quotas (Book 7, Ch12 L23); towns massacred under cover of monster attacks (Book 8, Ch9 L40).

## 5. What the characters know

- **Jason is an outworlder**: Soramir tells him he sensed his arrival (Book 7, Ch18 L42).
- **The Builder's invasion**: known to the royals and the Adventure Society (Book 7, Ch11 L5).
- **Not known**: Knowledge's plan; that Jason is an astral king until Book 9 (Book 9, Ch62 L30-34).

## 6. Narrator language that stays spoiler-safe

- Say "the Builder's forces" and "invasion platforms"; avoid hinting at who the Builder is.
- Call Dawn "the World-Phoenix's servant" only where a character does.
- Do not use Book 9 terms such as astral king, messenger war or world-taker worm.
