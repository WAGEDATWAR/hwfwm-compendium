## 1. Setting

- **Where and when**: Australia, then Slovakia and France, from early June 2019 to a warm autumn day in 2021 (Book 4, Ch3 L112; Book 6, Ch47 L155).
- **Scale**: bronze at the start, silver by the Book 5 chapter "Silver" (Book 5, Ch31 L1).
- **The world**: the Engineers of Ascension hold Perth and Melbourne (Book 4, Ch9 L76); after the monster waves the Network is publicly the Global Defense Network (Book 5, Ch17 L7); transformation zones begin with Kostroma (Book 5, Ch44 L48-49).

## 2. Three starting situations

1. **The EOA moves on Sydney**: they have "seized control in Perth and Melbourne, and now they're making no secret of their overtures into Sydney" (Book 4, Ch9 L76). *Who in the family is in their path?*
2. **Reality cores**: one appears in each affected zone and the factions scramble for it (Book 5, Ch45 L9-11). *Who gets there first?*
3. **A domain nobody can enter safely**: gold-rankers explore Jason's Nitra domain until the hostile effects become dangerous (Book 6, Ch23 L3). *What do they want from it?*

## 3. Cast and voice cues

- **Jason**: dry understatement when grieving (Book 5, Ch20 L25).
- **Family**: Erika, Ian and Emi (Book 4, Ch3 L132), Hiro (Book 4, Ch1 L66-67), Ken and Cheryl (Book 4, Ch4 L105), Kaito and Amy (Book 5, Ch44 L3). Emi is sharp and political at thirteen (Book 5, Ch24 L3-5).
- **Network**: Anna Tilden (Book 5, Ch51 L48), Nigel Thornton (Book 4, Ch41 L91), Asya (Book 4, Ch36 L37); Mr North is amused and unhurried (Book 5, Ch41 L33).
- **Cabal**: Craig Vermillion (Book 4, Ch11 L101).
- **Pallimustus visitors**: Farrah is freed in Book 4 (Book 4, Ch51 L62); Dawn returns in a new avatar and is composed and dry (Book 5, Ch20 L21-24).

## 4. Live dangers

Proto-spaces opening in towns; ancient vampires fed on reality-core blood (Book 5, Ch55 L92); the EOA; the Network schism; Gerling (Book 6, Ch1 L3); Shako (Book 6, Ch17 L37).

## 5. What the characters know

- **Jason is an outworlder**: the Network learns it in Book 4 (Book 4, Ch18 L29; Book 4, Ch19 L69); the public learns of his personas (Book 5, Ch11 L12).
- **The magic link**: Dawn tells Jason of the siphoning and Purity's bargain (Book 4, Ch68 L59, L139).
- **Not known**: the Network founder's story, Knowledge's plan, the System.

## 6. Narrator language that stays spoiler-safe

- Call Jason "Mr Asano" to officials and "Jason" to family.
- Refer to "the other world" without the names Builder or Knowledge.
- Do not mention Jason's absence after Book 6 or the Earth vampire war's later phases.

## 7. Leave closed

The Network's founder; why the Cabal's leaders act as they do; the fate of the Earthlings after autumn 2021.
