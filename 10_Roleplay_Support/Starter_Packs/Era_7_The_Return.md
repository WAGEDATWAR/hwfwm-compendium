## 1. Setting

- **Where and when**: Pallimustus in late 2037 to early 2038, "almost twenty years" after the arrival (Book 12, Ch13 L36), then Earth in 2038 (Book 13, Ch951 L50).
- **The world**: the System has appeared and the world knows it is linked to Jason (Book 12, Ch1 L44-54); Boko is destroyed (Book 12, Ch33 L3); the vampire war is back in full swing (Book 13, Ch988 L26).

## 2. Three starting situations

1. **Indenture and the System**: the System will withdraw from regions that keep indenture (Book 12, Ch50 L28), and the Queen of Estercost asks Jason to arbitrate (Book 12, Ch51 L13). *Who benefits from keeping indenture?*
2. **A pirate admiral's ship on Earth**: Jota Withers is hosted by the Asano clan's matriarch Noriko (Book 12, Ch57 L12; Book 12, Ch63 L19). *Will the crew mutiny?*
3. **Finnegan, Elizabeth and McKean**: Finnegan's backers called using Elizabeth "the smart choice" (Book 13, Ch975 L19-24), and there has been no confirmed trace of McKean for over four years (Book 13, Ch998 L105). *Where is he?*

## 3. Cast and voice cues

- **Team Biscuit and friends**: Humphrey is crisp in command (Book 13, Ch958 L48); Sophie is silent about pain (Book 2, Ch45 L69-72); Clive loses the thread and apologises (Book 11, Ch68 L748); Danielle believes "diplomacy is war" (Book 12, Ch23 L79).
- **Earth 2038**: Anna and Holly Macrossan (Book 13, Ch982 L30), Natalie Park (Book 13, Ch961 L8), Lenora Coleman (Book 13, Ch989 L48), Nigel Thornton (Book 12, Ch3 L3), Emi and Vincent Baudrillard (Book 13, Ch971 L49), Nik (Book 13, Ch996 L3).
- **Visitors and rivals**: Jota Withers (Book 12, Ch57 L3), Raythe (Book 13, Ch991 L3), Jamis Fran Muskar (Book 13, Ch1000 L3), Boris Ketland openly leading the Cabal (Book 13, Ch1015 L49).
- **Antagonists**: Elizabeth and Simon McKean (Book 13, Ch979 L72; Book 13, Ch1008 L33).

## 4. Live dangers

Blood farms outside Jason's domain (Book 12, Ch5 L76); vampire-magitech abominations (Book 13, Ch1006 L60); pirates and their dimension ship (Book 12, Ch57 L3); the secret council of Earth's powers (Book 12, Ch59 L60-72); a holy war from Knowledge (Book 13, Ch1017 L44-47).

## 5. What the characters know

- **The System**: everyone has seen it; its link to Jason is guessed or known (Book 12, Ch1 L54; Book 12, Ch42 L99).
- **Knowledge's plan**: told to the Book 13 audience (Book 13, Ch1017 L13-47); not shown earlier. See ../Who_Knows_What_When.md.
- **Time**: Earthlings and friends have lived around fifteen years without Jason (Book 12, Ch21 L61).

## 6. Narrator language that stays spoiler-safe

- Keep the plan's name out of scenes before the lecture; say "something the goddess has been preparing".
- Use "the System" as a public phenomenon, not as Jason's creation, before Book 12, Ch10.
- Do not describe the bridge anchor outcome beyond the crowd gathering (Book 13, Ch1023 L3).

## 7. Leave closed

How the bridge works once built; the final fate of Knowledge's holy war; what each major character does after the Book 13 farewell.
