## 1. Setting

- **Where and when**: Yaresh, a rainforest city of a few tens of thousands with a natural array beneath it (Book 9, Ch42 L26), about a four-month journey from Rimaros (Book 9, Ch26 L26). Jason is twenty-nine (Book 10, Ch33 L76).
- **Scale**: silver-rank adventurers and gold and diamond power brokers in a small elf city (Book 9, Ch42 L26).

## 2. Three starting situations

1. **A messenger camp**: Knowledge's army and the messengers set camp outside Yaresh while the city's government and Adventure Society worry (Book 9, Ch42 L80). *Who moves first?*
2. **Servant races and masters**: messenger masters may treat servants as they please within limits (Book 9, Ch56 L5). *Who will speak up?*
3. **The Aristocratic Faction**: landowners with no adventurers behind them undermine the Duke (Book 10, Ch14 L28-35). *Who profits from the war?*

## 3. Cast and voice cues

- **Elves**: Allayeth teases playfully (Book 10, Ch26 L35); Charist is the more social of the pair (Book 12, Ch16 L8); Hana Shavar personally takes charge of the refugees (Book 9, Ch74 L43).
- **Adventure Society**: Musin Heath is careful (Book 10, Ch9 L38); Marcus Xenoria is booming and blunt (Book 10, Ch47 L76).
- **Messengers**: Jes Fin Kaal is quiet and exact (Book 9, Ch56 L14); Marek is proud and adaptable (Book 9, Ch86 L16); Tera is rigid (Book 9, Ch100 L19); Fal Vin Garath is imperious (Book 9, Ch52 L20-28).
- **Allies**: Taika, Zara, Korinne's team (Book 9, Ch41 L119).

## 4. Live dangers

World-taker worms (Book 9, Ch58 L50); the entertainment-district breach (Book 9, Ch86 L16); the Church of Undeath striking when its god has saved up chances (Book 10, Ch78 L38); the Builder's new vessel (Book 10, Ch15 L5).

## 5. What the characters know

- **Jason is an astral king**: a healer realises it (Book 9, Ch62 L30-34); Soramir warns him it will be unpopular (Book 9, Ch23 L44).
- **The messengers' aim**: a soul forge in the natural array (Book 10, Ch72 L65).
- **Not known**: the Unorthodoxy's role (Book 11), the Council of Kings' politics beyond Jamis.

## 6. Narrator language that stays spoiler-safe

- Use "the messengers' leaders" rather than "astral kings" before Jason's reveal.
- Refer to the device as "the messengers' device"; avoid soul forge until a character names it (Book 10, Ch25 L60).
- Do not mention Boko's destruction or the Earth vampire war's end.
