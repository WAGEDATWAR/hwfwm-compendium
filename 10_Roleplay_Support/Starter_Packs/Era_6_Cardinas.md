## 1. Setting

- **Where and when**: the underground city of Cardinas and its natural array (Book 10, Ch72 L31), then the third transformation zone Jason reshapes (Book 11, Ch1 L15). The zone lasts "months" (Book 11, Ch60 L3).
- **The city**: ruled from the Citadel of Pillars, where fewer than ten thousand brighthearts survive (Book 10, Ch65 L3-6).

## 2. Three starting situations

1. **A buried city**: the expedition descends into Cardinas, where Lorenn says the citadel chamber is the site of the echo array (Book 10, Ch65 L3-6; Book 10, Ch72 L58). *Who holds the citadel?*
2. **A soul forge**: messengers were sent down to create it from the natural array (Book 11, Ch1 L64). *Who benefits if the forge appears?*
3. **A hero's price**: Gary drinks from the Cup of Heroes and the divine power will try to leave him (Book 10, Ch86 L59-61). *What will he give up to keep it?*

## 3. Cast and voice cues

- **Brighthearts**: Lorenn leads, Marla commands (Book 10, Ch65 L9; Book 11, Ch62 L89); Marla is commanding and practical.
- **Allies**: Miriam Vance and Moon's Edge (Book 10, Ch59 L4, L17); Amos, Carlos and Hana Shavar (Book 10, Ch59 L5); Danielle and Gwydion (Book 11, Ch60 L13).
- **Boris**: measured and careful (Book 11, Ch68 L493-498).
- **Cosmic visitors**: Raythe is terse and final (Book 9, Ch97 L80); Velius is warm and wry (Book 11, Ch64 L51).
- **Opponents**: Garth Larosse, High Priest of Undeath (Book 10, Ch80 L42); Beaufort of the Builder cult (Book 10, Ch65 L30).

## 4. Live dangers

Undeath's patience (Book 10, Ch78 L38); the corrupted tree; territory claims that make enemies paranoid (Book 11, Ch17 L33); the divine power in Gary (Book 10, Ch86 L61).

## 5. What the characters know

- **The soul forge is the third part of an astral kingdom**: Dawn told Jason (Book 8, Ch65 L94).
- **Boris is Unorthodoxy**: revealed in Book 11 (Book 11, Ch24 L85).
- **Not known**: Knowledge's plan and the Network founder's story.

## 6. Narrator language that stays spoiler-safe

- Call the zone "the transformation zone" and its places "territories".
- Say "an old friend of the Cabal" for Boris until the reveal.
- Do not mention the System as a world event or Boko.

## 7. Leave closed

How long the zone lasted in the world's own count; what Gary's divine power finally does.
