## 1. Setting

- **Where and when**: Greenstone and the delta in a mild winter (Book 3, Ch1 L14), then the Builder's astral space from about month 7 until Jason's death at about month 15 to 16 (see ../../08_History_and_Timeline/Master_Timeline.md).
- **Scale**: Path of Shadows reaches bronze (Book 3, Ch29 L23); the astral space has twelve towers about eight kilometres apart (Book 3, Ch36 L36).
- **Turning point**: Jason dies covering the team's escape (Book 3, Ch69 L900).

## 2. Three starting situations

1. **Purity under inquiry**: an ecumenical council investigates the church of Purity (Book 3, Ch1 L105). *Who in the church knew?*
2. **A rain elemental over Old City**: it appears in the city, and bronze-rankers gear up to confront it (Book 3, Ch6 L18, L43). *Who takes the lead?*
3. **Hunting Killian Laurent**: Jason's team spends days looking for the man who possessed a star seed (Book 3, Ch24 L3). *Where did he run?*

## 3. Cast and voice cues

- **The team**: Jason, Humphrey, Sophie, Belinda, Clive, Neil (Book 3, Ch3 L70-82). Belinda is delighted by her own power (Book 2, Ch32 L72); Neil is tentative before he commits (Book 2, Ch27 L46).
- **Elders**: Danielle Geller, Thalia Mercer, Emir (voice cues in ../Tone_and_Voice.md).
- **Opponents**: Zato and Timos (Book 3, Ch7 L89; Book 2, Ch59 L87), Nicolas Hendren and Anisa Lasalle of Purity (Book 2, Ch19 L73; Book 2, Ch10 L92), Cole Silva and Killian Laurent (Book 3, Ch17 L14).
- **Cosmic visitors**: Dawn, Shako and Velius appear at the end (Book 3, Ch69 L233-238, L1036).

## 4. Live dangers

Star seeds; energy ghouls (Book 3, Ch62 L23); clockwork constructs and cores (Book 2, Ch25 L35-41); cult infiltrators among aristocrats; the astral space itself, called "a giant boarding vessel" (Book 3, Ch65 L79).

## 5. What the characters know

- **The cult exists and what it wants**: its aim is to reinforce an astral space and sever it from the world (Book 2, Ch21 L123).
- **The Rejector**: the cultists call Jason that (Book 3, Ch32 L105).
- **Not known**: Purity's real controller, Knowledge's plan, the Network, Earth.

## 6. Narrator language that stays spoiler-safe

- Refer to "the cult's lord" or "the Builder" only as the characters do.
- Keep Dawn's identity as the World-Phoenix's servant in the background unless the scene is the Book 3 epilogue.
- Do not hint that Jason will go back to Earth.

## 7. Leave closed

The outcome of the three-way negotiation between the Builder, World-Phoenix and Reaper beyond what the scene shows (Book 3, Ch69 L279); why Jason's soul survived death.
