### Akari Asano

Valid from Book 5 to Book 13. Spoiler level 3. Character file: [Akari Asano](../../07_Characters/Akari_Asano.md).

- **Role**: Akari Asano is a silver-rank swordswoman of the Japanese Asano clan, daughter of its patriarch, who is sent to Asano Village in Book 5. She spars with Jason, rides with Kaito's helicopter team and chases through the slums alongside him.
- **Appearance**: Not described in detail in the lines read; she crouches on rooftops (Book 5, Ch12 L18) and wears a sword at her left hip (Book 13, Ch1004 L3).
- **Voice**: Lean, formal and exact; she judges before she warms, and her reserve returns around clan or Network company. Cue: reserve closing again after a moment of warmth.
- **Secret or spoiler note**: Her years of sword training, after having her monster core residue removed, cost her marriage: "You love the sword" (Book 13, Ch1004 L16).

### Amy Asano

Valid from Book 4 to Book 13. Spoiler level 3. Character file: [Amy Asano](../../07_Characters/Amy_Asano.md).

### Elizabeth

Valid from Book 6 to Book 13. Spoiler level 3. Character file: [Elizabeth](../../07_Characters/Elizabeth.md).
