## 1. Summary

Seven playable eras, each with its calendar, setting, live dangers and the points where the world changes.

## 2. Facts

**Era 1: Greenstone (Books 1-2, about M0-M6, late 2017 to spring 2018)**
- **Calendar**: Jason arrives in late November (Book 4, Ch3 L112); Leust's lumber conspiracy falls in late summer moving into autumn (Book 1, Ch93 L5).
- **Scale**: iron rank; Jason's badge is iron with one star (Book 1, Ch62 L66-67). Field testing is held at the start of every month (Book 1, Ch38 L158).
- **Live dangers**: a traditional blood cult in the hedge-maze estate (Book 1, Ch9 L50); the Ustei raiding spirit coin convoys (Book 1, Ch83 L4); Old City's "Big Three" crime lords (Book 1, Ch50 L92).
- **Turning points**: Farrah dies covering civilians (Book 1, Ch113 L41); Emir's contest and the City of Fallen Echoes free 419 time-displaced priests (Book 2, Ch63 L9).
- **Spoiler level**: 0-1.

**Era 2: Bronze and the Builder (Book 3, about M6-M18)**
- **Calendar**: the book opens in a mild Greenstone winter (Book 3, Ch1 L14).
- **Scale**: Path of Shadows reaches bronze (Book 3, Ch29 L23); the Reaper-trial astral space has twelve towers about eight kilometres apart (Book 3, Ch36 L36).
- **Live dangers**: the church of Purity under an ecumenical inquiry (Book 3, Ch1 L105); star seeds, implanted through the cult (Book 2, Ch21 L26-28); energy ghouls (Book 3, Ch62 L23).
- **Turning point**: Jason dies covering the team's escape (Book 3, Ch69 L900). **Spoiler level**: 1.

**Era 3: Earth (Books 4-6, about June 2019 to autumn 2021)**
- **Calendar**: Jason wakes on Earth in early June, a year and a half after leaving (Book 4, Ch3 L112); Book 5 opens about nine months after that (Book 5, Ch3 L170); a cold winter day in Book 6 (Book 6, Ch31 L4) and a warm autumn farewell (Book 6, Ch47 L155).
- **Scale**: bronze at the start, silver by the Book 5 chapter "Silver" (Book 5, Ch31 L1).
- **Live dangers**: the EOA has seized Perth and Melbourne (Book 4, Ch9 L76); the Network is publicly known as the Global Defense Network after the monster waves begin (Book 5, Ch17 L7); transformation zones begin with Kostroma (Book 5, Ch44 L48-49); gold-rankers explore Jason's Nitra domain a month after the dome falls (Book 6, Ch23 L3).
- **Spoiler level**: 2.

**Era 4: Rimaros (Books 7-8, from the arrival to about the surge's end)**
- **Calendar**: Book 7 opens at the standing stones (Book 7, Ch1 L5, L16); the surge is "some five years overdue" (Book 7, Ch1 L56).
- **Scale**: silver rank in the Storm Kingdom, where sky islands house the cream of society (Book 7, Ch5 L65).
- **Live dangers**: a hundred and nineteen outworlders appear at once (Book 7, Ch36 L86); the One Day War, after which "It had been a month" (Book 7, Ch66 L27); the Order of Redeeming Light converting towns (Book 8, Ch9 L40).
- **Turning point**: the Astral Throne and Gate appear (Book 8, Ch49 L105). **Spoiler level**: 2.

**Era 5: Yaresh and the messengers (Books 9-10, Jason aged twenty-nine)**
- **Calendar**: Jason says he is twenty-nine (Book 10, Ch33 L76).
- **Scale**: silver-rank adventurers and gold and diamond power brokers in a small elf city (Book 9, Ch42 L26).
- **Live dangers**: a messenger camp and Knowledge's army outside the city (Book 9, Ch42 L80); world-taker worms (Book 9, Ch58 L50); the Aristocratic Faction undermining the Duke (Book 10, Ch14 L28-35); the Church of Undeath striking when its god has "saved up his chances to intervene" (Book 10, Ch78 L38).
- **Spoiler level**: 2-3.

**Era 6: The Cardinas zone (Books 10-11)**
- **Calendar**: the zone lasts "months" (Book 11, Ch60 L3); Gary is a haggard figure "almost a year later" (Book 11, Ch68 L703).
- **Setting**: the underground city of Cardinas and its natural array (Book 10, Ch72 L31); a third transformation zone that Jason reshapes (Book 11, Ch1 L16).
- **Spoiler level**: 3.

**Era 7: The return (Books 12-13, about twenty years after arrival; Earth 2038)**
- **Calendar**: "almost twenty years" since the Reaper's priests were freed (Book 12, Ch13 L36); Book 13 is set in 2038 (Book 13, Ch951 L50).
- **Live dangers**: Boko destroyed (Book 12, Ch33 L3); the System withdrawn from regions that keep indenture (Book 12, Ch50 L28); a pirate dimension ship led by Jota Withers (Book 12, Ch57 L3); a back-in-full-swing vampire war on Earth (Book 13, Ch988 L26); Knowledge's plan (Book 13, Ch1017 L14-24).
- **Spoiler level**: 3.

## 3. Using this in a story

- **Choosing**: for a first-time scene pick Era 1 or Era 3, where the rules of the world are still being taught. For a high-power story pick Era 7, where a single decision touches whole regions.
- **Staying in the era**: use the character file's "valid from" and "valid through" lines to decide who can appear, and the place files for what a location looks like at the time.
- **Mixing eras**: do not let a character use a power or know a secret from a later era unless the scene deliberately sits after that book.
