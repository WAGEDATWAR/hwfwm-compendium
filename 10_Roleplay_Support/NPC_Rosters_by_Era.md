## 3. Using this in a story

- **Check absence as well as presence.** Farrah is dead from the end of Book 1 until Book 4 (Book 1, Ch113 L41; Book 4, Ch51 L62); Kaito, Asya and Greg die in Book 5 (Book 5, Ch51 L40, L52).
- **Open the character file** for appearance, voice and secrets before you give a rostered NPC a line.
