## 1. Summary

For each of the seven eras, three lists: what is true, what people commonly believe, and what is being said to mislead (cover stories, lies and rumours). The separation helps a narrator let characters be wrong in a way the books support. The file is level 3 because the true lists reach Book 13; Spoiler_Management.md says which facts a scene may use.

## 2. How to read the lists

- **True**: a fact the books confirm, with the line.
- **Believed**: what a group is shown holding as true, which may be wrong.
- **Told or hidden**: a cover story, a lie or a rumour that someone puts in circulation, or a statement of how the story is being managed.
- A character may speak a "believed" or "told" item as their own view; use a "true" item only when that character is shown knowing it (see Who_Knows_What_When.md).

## 10. Using the lists

- **Pairs to dramatise**: the Order (Era 1), Purity's sabbaticals (Era 2), the gods and Purity (Eras 4-5), and Knowledge's preparations (Era 7) are belief-versus-fact pairs the books themselves supply.
- **Do not add lies the books do not show**; a new rumour should be labelled as invented in the scene notes.
- **Check the level**: an Era 7 "true" item is level 3; do not give it to an Era 1 character.
