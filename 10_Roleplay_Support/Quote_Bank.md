## 1. Summary

A short bank of voice lines for the speakers `Tone_and_Voice.md` describes. Each entry is a quotation of fewer than fifteen words, with its line, and says what it shows about the voice. It is not a list of memorable lines; it holds only what that file needs. Use the lines as cues for rhythm and attitude, not as dialogue to repeat. Speakers the voice notes describe without a short quotable line (Gary's growl, Neil's hesitation, Humphrey's precision) are left to their character files.

## 3. Using the bank

- **Keep to the cue**: take the rhythm and the attitude, not the words.
- **Match the era**: Raythe, Mr North's reveal and Nik's name belong to later books; see Spoiler_Management.md and the era packs before using them with a table that has not read that far.
- **Add no lines**: a new line for a character is invented and should be labelled so in the scene notes.
