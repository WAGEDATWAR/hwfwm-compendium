# Who Knows What, and When (by Group)

Status: Draft (book text; each cell names the earliest scene found; a dash means no scene found in the lines read, not that the group is ignorant)
Spoiler level: 3
Valid from: Book 1
Valid through: Book 13
Sources: Books 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 9, 10, 12, 13 (chapter files)
Related: Spoiler_Management.md, Era_Selection.md, ../07_Characters/Relationships/Knowledge_States.md, ../08_History_and_Timeline/Master_Timeline.md

## 1. Summary

This matrix follows the series' main secrets across groups of people, not individuals. For each secret it gives the earliest scene where each group is shown knowing it, in four book bands: A = Books 1-3, B = Books 4-6, C = Books 7-9, D = Books 10-13. The individual view for core characters is in ../07_Characters/Relationships/Knowledge_States.md; the secrets table with the first surfacing of each is in Spoiler_Management.md.

## 2. How to use it

- **Earliest safe point.** A group may speak with a secret only from the band shown or later.
- **A dash is not "never".** It means the compendium found no scene; check the chapter before allowing the knowledge.
- **Belief versus fact.** A cell records what the group is shown to know or suspect, not whether it is true.

- Add new rows only with a cited scene; keep dashes rather than guesses.
