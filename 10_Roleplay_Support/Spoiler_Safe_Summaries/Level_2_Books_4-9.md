## 1. Read this before a session

Everything here is known by the end of Book 9, on top of levels 0 and 1. It leaves out the new Builder's identity, the Unorthodoxy, the Throne's history, Knowledge's plan and Books 10-13.

## 2. Earth (Books 4-6)

- **Jason wakes on Earth** in early June, a year and a half after leaving (Book 4, Ch3 L112).
- **The EOA has seized Perth and Melbourne** (Book 4, Ch9 L76).
- **The Network goes public** as the Global Defense Network once monster waves begin (Book 5, Ch17 L7); transformation zones begin with Kostroma (Book 5, Ch44 L48-49).
- **Silver rank** is reached in Book 5 (Book 5, Ch31 L1).
- **The link between worlds** has siphoned magic from Pallimustus for centuries (Book 4, Ch68 L59).

## 3. Rimaros (Books 7-8)

- **The surge** is "some five years overdue" (Book 7, Ch1 L56).
- **One hundred and nineteen outworlders** appear at once (Book 7, Ch36 L86); the One Day War follows (Book 7, Ch66 L27).
- **Jason's death certificate** reads "died four times. So far." (Book 7, Ch17 L104).
- **The Order of Redeeming Light** converts towns (Book 8, Ch9 L40), and the gods may already have sanctioned Purity (Book 8, Ch48 L39-41).
- **The Astral Throne and Gate appear** (Book 8, Ch49 L105).

## 4. Yaresh (Book 9)

- **Jason is an astral king**, as a healer realises (Book 9, Ch62 L30-34).
- **Messengers camp outside Yaresh** (Book 9, Ch42 L80).

## 5. Do not state

Who the new Builder is, who created the Order of Redeeming Light, the messengers' brand, the Unorthodoxy, the Throne's sundering, who owns the System and Knowledge's plan. Beliefs and lies to use are in ../Secrets_and_Rumours.md sections 5 to 7.
