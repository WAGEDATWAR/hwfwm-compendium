## 1. Read this before a session

This is the full-knowledge page. It names the secrets the three earlier pages withhold. Read it only if every player has finished Book 13 or accepts the spoilers.

## 2. What Books 10-13 reveal

- **The new Builder** is the mortal messenger Zithis Carrow Vayel (Book 10, Ch26 L55).
- **Disguise created the Order of Redeeming Light** (Book 10, Ch36 L14-17).
- **The messengers' aim** is a soul forge in the natural array (Book 10, Ch72 L65); the birthing tree is the result of a failed attempt to create one (Book 10, Ch80 L29).
- **Boris is of the Unorthodoxy** (Book 11, Ch24 L85), and rank limits are a function of the messengers' brand (Book 11, Ch25 L51).
- **The Cosmic Throne's sundering** is told by Raythe and Velius (Book 11, Ch64 L24-27).
- **The System** is Jason's through the administrator gift (Book 12, Ch10 L14-17).
- **Earth's magic** is recent: "less than fifteen years ago, no one knew magic existed" (Book 12, Ch2 L107).
- **Knowledge** has prepared for around half a millennium, and the Order's destruction was arranged to help Disguise take Purity's place (Book 13, Ch1017 L14, L18).
- **Mr North** was a magic spider familiar, not a theorist (Book 13, Ch987 L16).
