## 1. Read this before a session

Everything here is known by the end of Book 3. It includes Farrah's death and Jason's death, so it is not for a table that has not read those books. It leaves out everything from Book 4 on.

## 2. Situation

- **Setting**: Greenstone, a low-magic delta city. Jason arrives in late November (Book 4, Ch3 L112), and Old City is where the money is made (Book 1, Ch35 L19, L59).
- **Rank**: iron, then bronze; Path of Shadows reaches bronze in Book 3 (Book 3, Ch29 L23).
- **Local threats**: a blood cult in the hedge-maze estate (Book 1, Ch9 L50); the Ustei raiding coin convoys (Book 1, Ch83 L4); Old City's "Big Three" (Book 1, Ch50 L92).

## 3. Events and secrets by the end of Book 3

- **Jason is an outworlder**, and his team and Danielle know (Book 1, Ch21 L82-87; Book 1, Ch48 L101-105).
- **Farrah dies** covering civilians (Book 1, Ch113 L41).
- **The Order of the Reaper**: Emir says the churches scoured it away (Book 2, Ch25 L73); Shade says it survives and the trials are a lure (Book 2, Ch44 L72-78). Treat Emir's account as belief.
- **Star seeds**: great astral beings send followers star seeds (Book 2, Ch21 L26-28).
- **Emir's contest** frees 419 time-displaced priests (Book 2, Ch63 L9).
- **Book 3 opens** under an ecumenical inquiry into the church of Purity (Book 3, Ch1 L105); the reaper-trial astral space has twelve towers (Book 3, Ch36 L36).
- **Jason dies** covering the team's escape (Book 3, Ch69 L900).

## 4. Do not state

The Builder's identity beyond the cult, anything on Earth, the System, an astral kingdom, or what Purity is. Beliefs and lies to use are in ../Secrets_and_Rumours.md sections 3 and 4.
