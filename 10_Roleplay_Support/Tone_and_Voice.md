## 1. Summary

The register the books use, how forms of address work, and a condensed voice note for the main speakers, each tied to a line.

## 2. Facts

**The series' register**
- **Deadpan humour inside the setting**: Jason answers an unreadable item description with "Question marks. That's enlightening. Do I have to pay a wizard to identify items?" (Book 1, Ch1 L108).
- **Modern references in a fantasy world**: he suggests the Builder and Knowledge "play Mario Kart" (Book 3, Ch57 L87) and likens a projected face to Emperor Palpatine (Book 9, Ch6 L17).
- **Australian idiom**: "G'day" and "mate" (Book 1, Ch38 L55), "Strewth" (Book 2, Ch44 L130) and "yonks" (Book 13, Ch1007 L22).
- **Bleeped profanity**: Nik's swearing is bleeped on the page: "[Bleep]ing rubes" (Book 11, Ch8 L102).
- **Humour does not cancel stakes**: Jason jokes about kneeling to the Storm King, and then answers a threat of execution flatly: "Die on my feet. I've done it before" (Book 7, Ch59 L81-83).

**Forms of address**
- Titles for officials: "Instructor Trenslow" and "Instructor Spalding" during an assessment (Book 1, Ch54 L36); "Priestess Pellin" (Book 7, Ch9 L18).
- Shade uses "Mr Asano" (Book 8, Ch50 L82) and "Master Nik" (Book 11, Ch36 L43).
- Messengers are addressed as lord whatever their sex (Book 9, Ch56 L13), and a Voice of the Will as "Voice Kaal" (Book 9, Ch56 L14).

**Condensed voice notes**
- **Jason**: sarcastic, deferential only when rank forces it, with a dry understatement when he is grieving ("It's not a celebratory time", Book 5, Ch20 L25).
- **Rufus**: courteous and formal, apologising for discourtesy to a priestess (Book 7, Ch9 L18), gesturing with a sausage on a stick while explaining guild quotas (Book 7, Ch12 L22-23).
- **Farrah**: lectures on any subject (Book 1, Ch23 L15-17) and is blunt when angry ("Barbou belongs to me", Book 6, Ch26 L9).
- **Gary**: deep and growling (Book 1, Ch9 L121), plain about death ("rattling around in a hole", Book 11, Ch57 L60).
- **Humphrey**: precise and a little shy about romance (Book 1, Ch47 L88-89), then crisp in command (Book 13, Ch958 L48).
- **Sophie**: wary of kindness (Book 2, Ch1 L77-80), silent about pain (Book 2, Ch45 L69-72).
- **Clive**: a lecturer who loses the thread and apologises (Book 11, Ch68 L748) and jokes about wrestling an eel (Book 1, Ch68 L92-94).
- **Neil**: tentative before he commits (Book 2, Ch27 L46).
- **Belinda**: delighted by her own power (Book 2, Ch32 L72) and teasing (Book 7, Ch6 L42).

## 3. Using this in a story

- **Match the voice to the speaker's rank and culture**: Pallimustus officials use titles; Earth characters use first names and slang.
- **Do not invent catchphrases**; use the quoted cues and the character file's own voice section.
- **Keep jokes short and in-world**, and let the plot beat arrive after them rather than undercut them.
