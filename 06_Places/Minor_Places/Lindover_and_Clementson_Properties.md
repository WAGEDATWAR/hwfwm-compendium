## 1. Summary

Two neighbouring lumber properties in the eastern delta, near Leust: Kyle Lindover's idle mill and the busy, ostentatious property of Eustace Clementson. In Book 1 a "fergax" sighting contract Jason takes turns out to be a land-grab staged by Thadwick Mercer, and the properties are where he finds the summoner behind it.

## 2. The Lindover property

- **Mill and house**: a still, empty lumber mill, past which Jason finds a "sizeable farmhouse" (Book 1, Ch93 L23). The mill is reached through a wooden archway bearing its name on a road through neat rows of trees (Book 1, Ch93 L22-23).
- **Owner**: Kyle Lindover is a leonid larger than Gary, "the most lumberjack-looking man Jason had ever seen" (Book 1, Ch93 L24). He shut the mill after repeated fergax sightings so no worker would be hurt; his wife and children are staying with her parents in Greenstone (Book 1, Ch93 L28-29).
- **Stakes**: his reserves are falling short, most workers have taken jobs elsewhere, and he fears a black mark on his record (Book 1, Ch93 L28-29).
- **Tracks**: bear tracks are almost identical to a fergax's, so other adventurers dismissed his reports (Book 1, Ch93 L81).

## 3. The Clementson property

- **Layout**: laid out like the Lindover property but in full operation, with magical saws audible from the mill and a farmhouse "both larger and more ostentatious" (Book 1, Ch93 L84).
- **Owner**: Eustace Clementson, who sweats and tries to divert Jason, then falls in behind him under his aura (Book 1, Ch93 L88-99).
- **Guest house**: a stone-and-wood building apart from the main residence, where the iron-rank adventurer Dean Tuckell lays low between visits (Book 1, Ch93 L99; Book 1, Ch94 L70).

## 4. History

Book 1: Jason takes the contract at the Adventure Society, stays at an inn in Leust, and tracks the monster's aura from Lindover's land to Clementson's property (Book 1, Ch93 L19-83). Dean says Thadwick Mercer set up the "land-grab deal": every couple of weeks Dean spooked Lindover with his summon and then hid in Clementson's guest house (Book 1, Ch94 L58-72). In the settlement Jason negotiates with Thalia Mercer, the Mercer family compensates Lindover for the months of stalled production and Clementson's takeover preparations are rolled back (Book 1, Ch97 L97-124).
