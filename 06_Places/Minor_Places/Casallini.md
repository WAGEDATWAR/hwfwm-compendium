## 1. Summary

Casallini is a small border city of Girlano, close to the coast where Girlano comes nearest to the Sea of Storms. It is where Sophie's Book 8 delivery contract leads, and where a gang is hired to ambush her on behalf of Purity loyalists (Book 8, Ch24 L25; Book 8, Ch25 L119).

## 2. Location and layout

- **Position**: the border city of Girlano, which lies inland and south of the Storm Kingdom (Book 8, Ch24 L22, L25). The region is the part of Girlano that comes closest to the coast, near enough that a tall hill gives a view of the Sea of Storms (Book 8, Ch24 L26).
- **Border station**: Storm Kingdom border guards run a station at the city gates (Book 8, Ch26 L43-45).
- **Streets**: generously spaced, with rooftops used by Jason and Sophie for a private talk (Book 8, Ch26 L39, L54).

## 3. Society and rule

- **Gangs and cartels**: the surrounding country is run by cartels producing heavily controlled or banned substances from rare plants and minerals (Book 8, Ch24 L22). The eight men who ambush Sophie are members of a small gang from the nearby city, one of many satellite groups to the local cartel, mostly strongmen who keep low-end officials in line (Book 8, Ch24 L21).
- **Hired muscle**: they are silver-rank in their forties, "raw garbage" with no training and reeking of cores, hired by Purity loyalists to bring Sophie in (Book 8, Ch25 L127-130).
- **Adventure Society presence**: the Society ignores borders and keeps monsters out of population centres; its neutrality is why Sophie heads this way, and Liara says "we have a large force in Casallini because it's a border city" (Book 8, Ch24 L24-25; Book 8, Ch25 L136).
- **Smuggler's paths**: semi-hidden paths through jungle-covered hills avoid border checkpoints, which have no patrols during the surge (Book 8, Ch24 L26-27).
- **Character**: "a lawless town full of crappy guys" in Sophie's words (Book 8, Ch26 L66).

## 4. History

Book 8: the gang is hired and the ambush fails (Book 8, Ch24 L21-30; Book 8, Ch25 L117-130). Jason and Liara judge the tip to be a trap, a "worm on a hook" from the Order of Redeeming Light, and Liara cancels Sophie's contract and sends Storm Kingdom civic forces to sweep the gang up (Book 8, Ch25 L133-138; Book 8, Ch26 L7). Sophie, meanwhile, scouts the city on her own and hands her prisoners to the border guards (Book 8, Ch26 L39-72).
