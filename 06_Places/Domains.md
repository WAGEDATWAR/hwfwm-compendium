## 1. Summary

Jason's domains are territories that his soul's power fills: the zones he claimed on Earth and Pallimustus, his soul realm and astral kingdom, and the places he has made permanent. This file gathers the rules the text gives for living in them and for being his enemy in them. For what each place looks like, see the individual place files. The file is level 3 because it includes Book 12-13 rules.

## 2. What a domain is

- **Made of set-state authority.** Jason's spirit domains are made of set-state authority, "the treehouse that I'm allowed to play in", and he thinks that if they broke down into potential-state authority it would kill him (Book 8, Ch57 L25, L66, L70; see ../02_Cosmology_and_Powers/Authority_and_Sanctioning.md).
- **A claimed zone becomes one.** In a transformation zone, claiming a territory puts his influence on it; once his influence is already established, others cannot contest the claim (Book 11, Ch1 L70). Claiming a territory also claims the inhabitants in stasis in it, who are imparted with the claimant's influence (Book 11, Ch1 L44).
- **A zone's domain covers the whole zone.** At the Saint-Étienne zone he extends his senses "throughout his domain, which covered the entirety of the transformation zone" (Book 6, Ch46 L104).
- **Growth.** He wants the domain to grow large enough that even his powers could be restored inside the zone (Book 6, Ch3 L28).

## 3. Perception

- **He senses everyone inside.** Jason can always sense people within his spirit domain, even from across the world (Book 6, Ch23 L9), and everything inside it (Book 6, Ch40 L30). His aura senses extend across the domain (Book 6, Ch10 L5).
- **Memory.** In his domains "he saw everything"; if he wanted to watch something that happened seven years ago in a corner of Rexion, the memory came to him (Book 12, Ch11 L113).
- **Known to others.** The Australian military assumes he knows everything that happens in his territory; the protocol is not to speak restricted information aloud on Asano ground (Book 13, Ch1001 L105). Farrah says his perception within his territory is "absolute" and "quite well known" (Book 13, Ch1008 L50).
- **Limits.** In Cardinas an avatar of Jason cannot go beyond the boundary of his domain, so he cannot rescue a blood farm kept outside it (Book 12, Ch5 L76).

## 4. Aura and enemies

- **Sin afflictions.** To act against Jason or his allies in his domain is a sin and is punished: the sin afflictions from his aura cannot be resisted (Book 8, Ch15 L47).
- **Aura inside a sealed zone.** Within a sealed zone he can use his aura within his domain, though without his aura power's effects (Book 6, Ch3 L28).

## 5. Gods and outsiders

- **Gods cannot spy.** Inside his spirit domain, even gods cannot spy on him; it is like territory from which they are excluded (Book 8, Ch52 L11).
- **Priests cut off.** Church of the Healer priests are cut off from their god's influence in the spirit domain (Book 9, Ch71 L35). Gabrielle of Knowledge likewise looks as if she bit a lemon when it cuts her off from her goddess (Book 10, Ch8 L83).
- **Portals.** The spirit domain prevents portal tracking within it, but external destinations remain subject to tracking effects (Book 9, Ch2 L107).
- **Ransacking.** Outsiders who entered an earlier domain tried to ransack it, then took footpath tiles and whole trees away to examine magically; Jason sensed them throughout (Book 6, Ch23 L9).

## 6. Divine ground and temples (Book 13)

- Jason says that his territories, the Asano clan lands, "function like the divine ground held by gods": mechanically they are temples of him, which matters to Knowledge's planned holy war (Book 13, Ch1017 L47).
- He forbids anyone to make Rexion a temple (Book 12, Ch13 L34).
- Clan members say Jason's presence gives them a feeling of protection and belonging, which is not the experience of an outsider (Book 13, Ch1002 L5).

## 7. Territories and zones

- **Seal.** Inside a zone his essence abilities are sealed while racial powers remain (see Astral_Spaces/Transformation_Zones.md).
- **Claiming.** A territory is claimed by clearing anomalies and its final boss; a single claimant must remain before a Stable Genesis Core is produced (Book 11, Ch8 L54).
- **Uses of claimed territory.** At least in a claimed territory Jason has access to his soul realm and its resources (Book 11, Ch2 L4). He can place outsiders, such as Builder cultists and brightheart survivors, in his spirit domain (Book 11, Ch60 L51).
- **Messengers.** Messengers in a claimed territory feel his aura as the incomplete but unmistakable feel of an astral king (Book 11, Ch42 L9).
