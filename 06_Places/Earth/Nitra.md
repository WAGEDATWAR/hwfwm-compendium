## 1. Summary

Nitra, in western Slovakia, is a city that became "a major centre for the Network after the organization had been pushed out of Bratislava by vampires" (Book 5, Ch68 L69). It is too small to host ancient vampires but large enough to warrant Network protection. At the end of Book 5 a transformation zone forms there; in Book 6 Jason claims it and it becomes his first Earth spirit domain.

## 2. The city before the zone

Blessed by its size, it weathered the monster waves well and became a major Network centre. Jack Gerling is found sitting at a café while the zone forms.

## 3. The transformation zone

A glimmering dome several kilometres across, "a giant rainbow under glass" (Book 5, Ch68 L68). Inside is a genesis space in which essence abilities, aura and perception are sealed (racial gifts remain). Jason claims territories (Arrival Pagoda, Soul Haven, Tranquil Shadow Woods, Steamforge Circuit and others; the renamings are at Book 6, Ch5 L41; Ch12 L41, L59; Ch16 L60), stabilises the zone into a permanent astral space, and earns the title Reality Hegemon (Book 6, Ch15 L45).

## 4. Nitra as Jason's domain

In Book 11 the clan lives here: seen from a zeppelin by Yumi, the city inside high walls looks like the work of "a mad steampunk elf", with dark rough-metal towers like industrial-age furnaces, walkways through parks, tramways through gardens, monorails, zeppelins docked at the towers, and at the centre a massive pagoda taller than any tower, topped by a blue and orange eye. Its steam engines are driven by a mix of fire and water quintessence, not coal, in a blend of Victorian and modern technology with magic that does not respect real physics (Book 11, Ch36 L12). Walls rust and monsters rise when Jason struggles; turrets blaze when he is winning. A sunless vampire quarter exists, where Craig Vermillion lives. In Book 11 Carlos Quilido's research centre is set up in Jason's soul realm during the Cardinas expedition, not in Nitra.

## 5. Notable people

Jack Gerling, the Network gold-rankers Chen, Guo and Tran, Yumi Asano and the clan, Craig Vermillion, Carlos Quilido.

## 6. Dangers and hooks

Zone anomalies, vampires, and the strain on the domain when Jason is away. Hooks: an expedition into the zone; a vampire quarter dispute; a domain-wide rust crisis.

## Later developments (spoilers)

In Book 12 Jason purges vampires of reality-core power here and liberates a blood farm.
