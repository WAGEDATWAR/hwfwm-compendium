## 1. Summary

Casselton Beach is Jason's hometown, a tourist beach town on the Mid-North Coast of New South Wales where he was born (Book 4, Ch1 L39). It is where he wakes in Book 4 after dying on Pallimustus, and it is the model for the head-shaped fortress he later builds in a transformation zone. Asano Village is the family compound on clifftop land nearby, built by the family after the magic arrives.

## 2. Location and layout

- **Casselton region**: the old West Regional Hospital (abandoned; Jason wakes naked in its fourth-floor maternity theatre, looking out over the semi-industrial part of West Casselton), the boardwalk, the Surf Club and the marina matter across Books 4-6.
- **Casselton Beach**: the town of Jason's childhood, with a long, quiet beach.
- **Asano Village**: eco-village buildings on clifftop land, a medical centre, a pub and spa, and a secret tunnel system (Book 5, Ch5 L83-89). Clifftop excavations served as a front for a network of tunnels linking the compound's nodes and carrying the magical infrastructure, with a two-way tramway; a separate, longer passage runs from the main residence out into the ocean and becomes a glass tunnel along the seafloor, with sea creatures drawn by subtle magic. Only Ken, Hiro, Emi and Farrah knew of the tunnels; the Network and the rest of the family did not. The long passage ends at Jason's cloud house, which sits at the bottom of the sea about two kilometres out, reached by a hidden tram (Book 5, Ch5 L106-113).
- **Family home**: the house where Jason's mother Cheryl and father Ken live, with a rebuilt gazebo and fence that Jason notices have changed.

## 3. Society and rule

An ordinary Australian coastal community until magic becomes public. The Asano family, led by Yumi, treats the village as the base of a clan. The Network and, later, the Australian government take an interest in it.

## 4. Magic level

Low. Earth has very little magic in Book 4: Jason uses spirit coins to normalise his recovery (Book 4, Ch3 L87). After the Grid fails, ambient magic rises (see `../../03_Magic_and_Adventuring/Earth_Magic.md`).

## 5. History

- Book 4: Jason wakes in the hospital; contacts uncle Hiro through the Stone Wall bar in Sydney's Kings Cross (Book 4, Ch4 L8); reveals magic to his niece Emi, then to the family.
- Books 5-6: the family evacuates to Asano Village when the Grid is sabotaged.
- Book 11: the clan lives in Jason's astral domains in Europe.
- Book 12: the Australian government seizes Asano Village and the stone-circle land and gives them to Noriko's Japanese-line "true Asano clan", allied to the Network (Noriko says the System link is "utter nonsense").
- Book 13 (2038): after negotiation the Australian government declares Asano Village and the surrounding area a special-interest territory, "similar to the standing stones", to be managed by a multinational organisation with Lenora Coleman in charge (Book 13, Ch960 L14); Jason says his family built it with their own money and toil, and Prime Minister Truffett is named as the one who suggested the seizure.

## 6. Notable people

Jason's parents Ken and Cheryl, siblings Kaito and Erika, Kaito's wife Amy, Erika's husband Ian, niece Emi, uncle Hiro, matriarch Yumi, administrator Ketevan Arziani, and Jason's school friends including Greg and Michael Aram.

## 7. Customs and daily life

Coastal Australian life: beach, surf club, pub. After the magic arrives the village adds training facilities and a medical centre, and secrecy matters less.

## 8. Dangers and opportunities

Incursions from nearby sites, vampire and EOA attacks, and legal disputes over land. Opportunities: the village's medical centre, training with Jason's methods, a safe place to settle.

## 9. Hooks

- A local incursion near the beach town.
- A land dispute between rival Asano clans.
- A returning outworlder finds home changed.

## Later developments (spoilers)

Kaito and Greg die in Book 5 at the hands of the American gold-ranker Gerling; Amy is mayor in the later books, preparing in Book 5 to commandeer accommodation across the tourist towns of Casselton Beach, Castle Reach and Casselton North. The Australian land seizure, Noriko's hosting of the pirate captain Jota, and the 2038 return form the Earth endgame.
