## 1. Summary

Saint-Étienne, France, is the site of the Dimensional Fortress, a permanent astral space found by the Network's Lyon branch in 1948. In Book 4 Jason frees Farrah from it; in Book 6 it becomes a second transformation zone, which Jason claims and later turns into a whimsical cloud city of refugees.

## 2. The Dimensional Fortress

- Discovered in 1948 when local Network operatives found "an incursion space dimensionally coterminous with an area near Saint-Étienne" with multiple apertures, as Paul Abreo of the Lyon branch Steering Committee explains (Book 4, Ch51 L28-30).
- Heightened magic; anomalous entities manifest directly; the Lyon branch used it as a monster-hunting reserve and spirit-coin farm and hid it from the wider Network for about seventy years by faking the Grid.
- It looks like dilapidated interconnected manors and crumbling castles in fog and drizzle.
- Adrien Barbou is the Lyon Operations Director, working out of the black site (Book 4, Ch43 L58); Paul Abreo confesses that many of the decisions behind it were "at least in part, mine" (Book 4, Ch51 L28). Barbou holes up in the fortress having sealed the apertures from the inside, and ritual specialists try to breach the seals (Book 4, Ch51 L60-68; Book 4, Ch52 L22-23). Jason passes the seals using Nirvanic Transfiguration.

## 3. The transformation zone and cloud city

In Book 6 Shako and Chen place a device in the aperture of the astral space (Book 6, Ch35 L80), which triggers a second transformation zone (Gerling and Mr North fight beside the vampires and Jason in the zone, Book 6, Ch44 L25-31; Book 6, Ch45 L3); a nightmare hag, "a diamond-rank entity that had little direct power but could manifest people's fears", is among the foes (Book 6, Ch46 L31). Jason claims it and stabilises it into his Saint-Étienne spirit domain; the allies he gathers there are the vampires, including Elizabeth, Mr North and Gerling (Book 6, Ch43 L71; Book 6, Ch45 L3), with the necromancer Todd "The Necromancer" Halverson a silver-ranker with heavy core taint (Book 6, Ch39 L25). Fates: Noreth, the EOA leader known as Mr North, is dead before Book 7 (Book 7, Ch48 L36); Gerling is killed more than once but keeps returning (Book 13, Ch1007 L62; Book 13, Ch1008 L31-33) and in Book 11 is Elizabeth's blood slave (Book 11, Ch68 L2267). By Book 12 it is a cloud city whose administration tower is "a looming edifice of renaissance architecture, the most overt divergence from the original design of the city" (Book 12, Ch41 L27); the clan's city has expanded green areas and waterways with streams running through. It houses refugees with green scales and wings.

## 4. Society

Refugee city for transformed non-humans; the Asano clan lives here; Erika and Ian host picnics. Nigel Thornton's families have joined. Gary Sharpton, a teenage inventor and the reincarnated Gary, lives nearby.

## 5. Dangers and hooks

Vampires, ghouls and blood servants (mad after Jason's Book 12 purge: a giant eye in the sky, Book 12, Ch4 L20, L41), hostile Network factions. Hooks: refugee integration; an inventor's saucer craft.
