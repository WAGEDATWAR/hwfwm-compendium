## 1. Summary

Dimensional Artefact Site One, called the artefact city, is "a small city" in outback Australia (Book 12, Ch61 L21), with "nine thousand or so workers" before an evacuation (Book 12, Ch64 L21), built around the standing stones where Jason and Farrah left Earth. It is run by a multinational coalition and is where the Earth end of the Earth-Pallimustus bridge sits.

## 2. Layout

A ring of monitoring and research stations surrounds half a kilometre of open concrete with concentric metal panels, each a weapon bunker with "retractable weapons, magically enhanced howitzers and rocket batteries" (Book 12, Ch61 L21). A coalition of nations "had spent the last fifteen years preparing it" as a defensive point (Book 12, Ch61 L18). Most of the workers are shop attendants, janitors, gardeners and others who keep the city operating (Book 12, Ch64 L21); command runs from bunkers beneath the city (Book 12, Ch64 L22-24). Deep underground, a chamber holds the standing stones set in a ritual diagram.

## 3. Society and rule

- **Director of Operations**: Lenora Coleman, Director of Operations for Dimensional Artefact Site One (Book 12, Ch61 L17); she was a graduate stuck for two years in a monitoring station when the site was "little more than some pre-fab buildings" (Book 12, Ch61 L16), and is a bureaucrat and a core-user (Book 12, Ch61 L22).
- **Deputy**: Barry, "what amounted to mayor of the artefact city" (Book 12, Ch61 L25); assistant Cassie (Book 12, Ch62 L45); Security Commander Higgins (Book 12, Ch62 L53). Sub-Commander Keene leads a security team under Director Lenora Coleman (Book 12, Ch67 L44-47).
- The original director was Gordon Truffett, who chose the site's name and is now the Australian Prime Minister (Book 12, Ch61 L17); he came to power "through fearmongering over magical gender transitions during rank-up" (Book 13, Ch950 L45).
- Gold-rankers on the rotating roster are mostly American and Chinese, which is why Lenora does not have the command her title suggests (Book 12, Ch61 L22). A bomber attack leaves the standing stones unharmed (Book 12, Ch61 L30).

## 4. The bridge

- **The Earth end** is at the standing stones in Australia. By Book 13 no traces remain of the small town that once stood there: only the ritual circle of standing stones, surrounded by weapons upgraded by Travis Noble. Lenora Coleman heads the yet-unnamed multinational taskforce.
- **The Pallimustus end** is an island in the Storm Kingdom that the Church of Knowledge's Holy Army has seized, renamed Ascension's Gate and converted into a military base with barracks and a sky port; at its centre stands a set of standing stones forming an enormous ritual circle. Zara notes the Church annexed only one small island but seized sovereign territory. The island is the one Jason and Farrah first reached years earlier, then uninhabited and ringed by the magical hurricane of the Sea of Storms, since bought and developed by the Sky Link Company (Book 12, Ch56 L3-4); Jason names it as the Pallimustus anchor point, an uninhabited island in the Storm Kingdom (Book 12, Ch39 L47) and in Book 13 says the Church of Knowledge has annexed it (Book 13, Ch1020 L95); it is renamed Ascension's Gate (Book 13, Ch1023 L103). The text never states the Sky Link name and the Ascension's Gate name for one island in a single sentence, but both describe the Storm Kingdom island the bridge is anchored on.
- **Timing**: Boris's reasoning in Book 13 says the Cosmic Throne detour gave fifteen extra years, the bridge is now set in place, and "within a decade at most, the bridge would become a true dimensional passage, no longer requiring sophisticated astral magic to cross" (Book 13, Ch954 L17). A threat to the bridge could undo Earth and possibly Pallimustus.

## 5. Dangers and hooks

Political: Lenora's power over the land seizure; military: a coalition fortress. Hooks: infiltration of the site; the bridge-calibration project.

## Open questions

- Exact layout and the site's relation to the earlier "proto-astral" stone circle.
- Whether the island renamed Ascension's Gate is the island where Jason and Farrah first arrived and that the Sky Link Company later developed: Book 12, Ch56 L3-4 describes that island (an uninhabited island in a magical hurricane, later a town with storm accumulators) and a chamber of standing stones beneath it (Ch56 L124); Book 13, Ch1023 L103 and Ch1024 L57 describe the island with standing stones as the counterpart anchor point. The books do not state outright that they are the same island.
