## 1. Summary

An incursion is a proto-astral space that opens on Earth and spills monsters into the surrounding area. Spaces are rated by category and last "forty-three hours, as a baseline" (Book 4, Ch37 L70). This file records the sites where the story takes place.

## 2. Dorrigo and South Australia (Book 4)

- **Dorrigo National Park** (Book 4, Ch39 L28):  the team meets silver-rank jungle trolls (Book 4, Ch41 L18, L32 confirms roaming jungle trolls, whose regeneration is shut down by fire; Nigel's team burns the bodies with bronze-rank everburn oil). Jason joins the Network team, kills anomalous dimensional entities (ADEs) and shows them adventurer tactics.
- **South Australia**: Jason and Farrah are flown from Sydney to join the Adelaide branch's tactical team (Book 4, Ch60 L18). Inside, flat scrubland holds a herd of grotesque animals: "three gigantoads, two yowies, and a yara-ma-yha-who", with barb-tailed mantas overhead (Book 4, Ch60 L59-65); the team's projection is that the space "might last as much as sixty hours" (Book 4, Ch62 L23).

## 3. Makassar (Book 5)

- **Setting**: Makassar, South Sulawesi, a city of one and a half million people (Book 5, Ch25 L82). A category-three proto-space starts dumping monsters into it; it later escalates to category four with a gold-rank anchor.
- **Dino-men**: silver-rank intelligent lizard-men lead dinosaur monsters; their king says "I, like you all, am less than two days old" (Book 5, Ch26 L17).
- **Outcome**: Jason enters, dies, and takes the form of a star phoenix (Book 5, Ch28 L86). The Makassar death toll "officially crossed a hundred and fifty thousand" with bodies in piles (Book 5, Ch30 L71), early estimates being one to two hundred thousand (Book 5, Ch31 L170), and a necromancer later animates Makassar's victims as a zombie horde (Mr North says (Book 5, Ch32 L11) it looks like an extension of long-discarded experiments from his own organisation's past; Jason says the necromancer who animated them got his start in North's house, and Book 6, Ch39 L25 names Todd "The Necromancer" Halverson).
- **Aftermath**: Sulawesi later declares independence.
- **The Butterflies of Makassar**: the name news broadcasts give to a public spectacle. Against the zombie horde Jason's familiar Gordon uses his affliction orbs to start the Harbinger of Doom affliction: it drains mana from each victim to conjure incorporeal blue-and-orange butterflies with the eye pattern on their wings, which seek out nearby enemies, detonate with disruptive force when destroyed, and carry every non-holy affliction of the host to the next victim. The swarm spreads from zombie to zombie until the sky is thick with butterflies. Filmed from high in the air it looks beautiful; once the wave passes only black-stained bones remain. Broadcasters credit Jason with it, and in Book 5 Amy cites it as a reason to fear what he might do to "regular people".

## 4. Broken Hill (Book 5)

- A New South Wales outback town whose population had grown from fewer than twenty thousand to almost thirty-five thousand as isolated outback residents were gathered there (Book 5, Ch17 L3); under Network protection with a support team and a nine-person tactical section.
- The EOA's League of Heroes infiltrates the town, cuts communications and a category-three proto-space opens. Jason estimates "somewhere between ten and fifteen thousand people had died" from the bodies and his portal count (Book 5, Ch18 L83), putting survivors at fewer than twenty thousand (Book 5, Ch18 L82).
- On camera Jason confronts the EOA media team and gives a speech. The Network's rebranding as the Global Defense Network follows.

## 5. Other sites

Sydney's Kings Cross (the Stone Wall bar), the Network's Sydney clinic where Jason secretly works as the "Starlight Angel", and the Starlight Rider episodes belong to Book 4: the media dub the figure the Starlight Rider after earlier stories of a starlight angel (Book 4, Ch15 L12). They are not full place files.

## 6. Dangers and opportunities

Monsters of category three or four, anomalies, political fallout from civilian deaths. Opportunities: loot, spirit coins and reputation.

## 7. Hooks

- A new incursion in a town with no Network team.
- A survivor of Broken Hill with a story the news missed.
