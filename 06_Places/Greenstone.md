## 1. Summary

Greenstone is a colony city at the mouth of a river delta in the low-magic far south of the Africa-like continent. It is the first city Jason knows on Pallimustus and the setting of most of Books 1-3. It was founded because its conditions suit low-end spirit coin farms: stable low magic, in a place that is also livable and close to the desert (Book 1, Ch80 L42). It is a place of green marble, walled harbours, crime lords with gentlemanly manners, and a Society full of ambitious, mid-ranked people who use monster cores rather than fight for their ranks.

## 2. Location and layout

- **Setting**: a vast river delta inside a western coastal region that is mostly lifeless desert; the delta is fertile ground between dune seas. Locals and adventurers wear loose, breathable clothes because of the heat.
- **The Old City**: "the original city of Greenstone, situated on the original harbour"; the Island was meant to be a massive breakwater (Book 1, Ch35 L15). It is built mostly from the same red and yellow stone as the desert, with a massive port along its whole shoreline, and "Old City is where the money is made, but the Island is where the money goes" (Book 1, Ch35 L19, L59; Book 1, Ch36 L98). The city authorities pay little attention to it so long as the elite's interests are secure, which makes its three biggest crime lords its de facto rulers (Book 1, Ch42 L22). Districts have names from old families, such as Cavendish, whose tavern the Townhouse is the largest building there (Book 1, Ch71 L3, L38).
- **The Island**: the affluent district built entirely of green marble (Book 1, Ch36 L68). Travel to it "was restricted without a valid reason for entry": trade and work permits admit a person to the trade lanes, and aristocrats, adventurers and residents come and go freely; a day's entry costs an iron-rank spirit coin (Book 1, Ch36 L71-72). Staying past sunset needs lodgings, which earn a temporary residence permit and a tracking stone (Book 1, Ch36 L89, L95; Book 1, Ch39 L34). Vehicles on the Island are magic-driven, not animal-drawn, and prices there are twice or three times Old City's (Book 1, Ch39 L32-33). The guild district in the north-west holds the Adventure Society campus (Book 1, Ch38 L3).
- **The Divine Square**: a huge crowded square on the Island, paved even in high-grade green stone, ringed by streets of temples that show "gods had hierarchies of their own"; booths sell religious paraphernalia, and even the flagstones are high-grade green stone (Book 1, Ch45 L4-5). The Temple of the Healer is one of the central temples on it (Book 1, Ch94 L3) and all the major temples front it (Book 2, Ch10 L92).
- **The Fortress**: a huge stone building in the grimy heart of Old City's warehouse district, "older even than the city walls", once the shelter for residents during a monster surge; now neutral ground where the Big Three divide responsibility and profit, and the best place in Old City to glimpse the Island elites (Book 1, Ch42 L21-23). Fights are held there in a steel cage, as Sophie's Nightingale bouts (Book 1, Ch50 L85-91).
- **Water and tunnels**: delta water runs through an elaborate tunnel network under the Old City and drains into an artificial strait between the Old City and the Island; Adventure Society contracts clear monsters such as stone-chewer rats from it. Transit uses loop terminals (Book 1, Ch45 L4); Jason's "magic trams?" is only his guess.
- **Surroundings**: green-marble quarry villages (Quarry Village Four, a lake village fed by a waterfall from an astral-space aperture), desert, mangrove and marsh. Boko, a desert academic city, lies to the north.

## 3. Society and rule

- **Colonial status**: the city began as a colony from the wider kingdom. The Adventure Society is a major force, but the isolation makes the local branch dependent on local powers; Rufus's remark to the village mayor is that "compromises must be made."
- **The Adventure Society branch** is directed by Elspeth Arella. Vincent Trenslow runs assessments and assigns contracts.
- **The Magic Society branch** is directed by Lucian Lamprey, a silver-ranker who watches Fortress fights from a viewing box (Book 1, Ch50 L103; Book 1, Ch52 L46). His deputy is Pochard Finn, whose son Cassowary serves as Lucian's dogsbody (Book 1, Ch49 L5; Book 1, Ch50 L104).
- **The Big Three of the underworld**: Clarissa Ventress (mansion in the canal district), Cole Silva (obsessed with Sophie) and Adris Dorgan (the "quiet one", Book 1, Ch103 L120; Elspeth Arella's father, Book 1, Ch104 L88; Book 12, Ch27 L44). They keep the underworld running and avoid gang war because it draws the attention of the Island authorities (Book 1, Ch42 L22, L29).
- **The great families**: the Gellers (an estate, with a mirage chamber), the Mercers (Thalia, Cassandra, Thadwick; the Mercer estate has a gate and guards), and the Remore family's academy annex.
- **History of the settlers**: most Greenstoners descend from Estercost immigrants a few centuries ago; Boko to the north is much older, with a native population (Book 12, Ch29 L59-60).
- **The Duke of Greenstone** rules the city from a walled residence in the Island's park district; only his household guard uses iron-rank essence users as basic troops. Adris Dorgan, once a crime lord, is mayor of Old City by Book 12 (Book 12, Ch29 L38); that the mayor answers to the Duke is unchecked.
- **Faiths**: temples in the Divine Square, including the Healer's. Purity's Anisa Lasalle begins the books in Rufus's party.

## 4. Magic level

Greenstone is a low-magic region where "iron-rank monsters are the norm, with a good smattering of bronze-rank" (Book 1, Ch19 L40; Book 1, Ch30 L43). This shapes the city: core use is nearly universal, because fighting for advancement is slow when monsters are few and weak; a bronze-rank mirage chamber can run only for short periods; there are only "forty or so silver rankers in the whole city", most semi-retired (Book 1, Ch61 L32), and the narration notes that Greenstone's other silver-rankers show more of their age than Emir, Danielle and Thalia (Book 1, Ch108 L35). Resting spirit coins and potions matter more than in higher-magic cities. The city exports green marble and lesser spirit coins.

## 5. History

- Founded as a colony and spirit coin farm. Almost nothing is known about the region's history before the original Greenstone Colony, as Clive observes (Book 1, Ch86 L25); the Gellers' ancestors came as the region was first being settled (Book 1, Ch37 L21).
- Book 1: Jason arrives, joins the Adventure Society, helps destroy a Blood Cult branch and fights alongside Rufus and Farrah.
- Books 2-3: Sophie's indenture, Emir's contest for the Scythe of the Reaper, Mercer family intrigue, the oasis tyrant, the Builder cult.
- Later: Greenstone remains a recurring home and reference point.

## 6. Notable people

Elspeth Arella (Adventure Society director), Vincent Trenslow (official), Lucian Lamprey (Magic Society director), Clarissa Ventress, Cole Silva, Adris Dorgan, Dink, Emir Bahadir and Constance, the Gellers (Danielle, Humphrey), the Mercers (Thalia, Cassandra, Thadwick; the Mercer estate has a gate and guards), Jory Tillman (alchemist), Clive Standish, Beth Cavendish (water healer), Hiram (aperture guard at Quarry Village Four), Arash (runic juice vendor), Jamar (bartender), the Berts (Bertinelli octuplets, clerks).

## 7. Customs and daily life

Aristocrats and the wealthy live on the Island; ordinary people in the Old City. Adventurers cluster in the Society campus and its taverns. Temples hold public services in the Divine Square. Fights at the Fortress are a major entertainment; recording crystals broadcast and preserve them. Heat makes loose dress universal. Local food and drink include runic juice from Arash's stall.

## 8. Dangers and opportunities

- **Dangers**: marsh hydras, tidal trolls, shabs and other delta monsters; desert bandits; astral-space expeditions; the underworld's sudden violence; Society politics.
- **Opportunities**: contracts from the Society, spirit coin farms, ancient complexes and ruins under the delta, trade in green marble.

## 9. Hooks

- A tour of the Fortress's neutral ground as a meeting place for the Big Three.
- A contract at a quarry village where an old aperture has started leaking monsters.
- An adventurer candidate's field assessment run by the Society.
- Magic Society politics around the Nightingale.
- (Level 1) Greenstone as the place Sophie's indenture ends, and the starting point for the Reaper contest.

## Later developments (spoilers)

Greenstone is repeatedly touched by the Builder cult through Books 3-8. By Book 12 the third crime family's leader has been legitimised as mayor of the Old City (the Duke rules the city; that the mayor answers to him is an inference), the Adventure Society is cleaner and, twenty years later, Elspeth Arella remains in the post she was forced into. Boko, the neighbouring academic city, is destroyed after Jason is assassinated there (Book 12, Ch32-33; Book 12, Ch33 L3); most of its ninety thousand people are evacuated first.
