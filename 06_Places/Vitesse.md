## 1. Summary

Vitesse, in the kingdom of Estercost, is the City of Flowers, "located in what Jason Asano would know as the French Riviera" (Book 6, Ch47 L603; Book 1, Ch30 L48). Its skyline is made of the **garden towers**: huge towers with flowering vines spilling down the exterior, "most had every third or fourth floor dedicated to gardens using water, light and plant magic", which are residences for the wealthy elite and headquarters of key organisations (Book 6, Ch47 L603; Book 7, Ch24 L3). It is a high-magic, wealthy city (the social programmes and labour laws described below are not confirmed in the lines read). It is the home of Remore Academy and of Burning Violet, and the place Rufus, Gary and Farrah come from.

## 2. Location and layout

- Estercost's southern coast, on the equivalent of the French Riviera.
- Garden towers, most with every third or fourth floor given over to gardens (Book 6, Ch47 L603). The Adventure Society branch is in one of them, with a meeting room opening onto a balcony (Book 7, Ch43 L18); the Adventure Society and Magic Society each hold an entire building, and the royal family keeps a tower as a palace (Book 6, Ch47 L604).
- Trees line every street, heavy with blossom.
- **Remore Academy** and the **Burning Violet** guild house stand opposite each other; the guild house moved there after Roland Remore took over the guild long ago (Book 12, Ch43 L64).
- A tower belonging to the Geller family stands in Vitesse: on a high balcony of one of the famous flowering towers Sophie leans into Humphrey (Book 11, Ch68 L1934-1935).

## 3. Society and rule

- Governed within the kingdom of Estercost. (Rakesh is also called the home of a continental council, Book 6, Ch47 L623; the two may be councils for two continents, see `Minor_Places/Rakesh.md` and Open_Questions 22.) The books disagree on where the Adventure Society's continental council sits: Book 6 says "the continental council for the Adventure Society sat in Vitesse, rather than the capital city, Cyrion" (Book 6, Ch47 L604), while Book 7 calls Cyrion "home to the Adventure Society's continental council" (Book 7, Ch43 L58).
- Remore family influence is pervasive: Roland Remore, a diamond-ranker who took over Burning Violet long ago (Book 12, Ch43 L64), is the Academy's arch-chancellor (Book 3, Ch69 L587) and Rufus's grandfather (Book 9, Ch18 L70). Burning Violet is the Remore family's elite guild.
- Strong labour protections and social programmes mean an unusually large comfortable middle class.

## 4. Magic level

The books give no numeric figure for Vitesse, but they give a rank band. Farrah says the area has a higher density of magic than Greenstone's region, so monsters are stronger on average (Book 1, Ch30 L43), and Gary says there "you get more silver rank monsters than anything. You see as many golds as you do bronze, and sometimes even diamond rank monsters" (Book 1, Ch30 L44). Compare Clive's rating of Greenstone as about mid-iron (Book 3, Ch34 L60). A surge precursor that shows as more silver-rank monsters in Greenstone shows as more gold-rank monsters in Vitesse (Book 3, Ch26 L13). Consequences: even bronze-rankers go out with a silver escort (Book 1, Ch30 L55), no adventurer below silver operates unsupervised (Book 12, Ch43 L68), monster manifestations are powerful and frequent across the whole region (Book 12, Ch43 L47), the Adventure Society detects them with a grid like Earth's (Book 12, Ch43 L48) and magical vehicles work for everyone on ambient magic (Book 7, Ch25 L3). Typical monster rank is therefore silver, with frequent gold and occasional diamond; this is a band, not a measured density figure.

## 5. History

Little is given directly. Vitesse is the origin of the Remore family's influence in the Adventure Society, and the base from which the Society mounts expeditions in northern Estercost. In Book 6 the Vitesse branch sends an expedition to attack a Purity church stronghold in northern Estercost; it is described as "more than they bargained for" and a "mess", with Jason involved.

## 6. Notable people

Roland Remore (diamond archchancellor; Rufus's grandfather), Arabelle and Gabriel Remore (Rufus's parents; Book 2, Ch33 L6), Neiman Bassingthwaite (the guild's chief membership officer, Book 12, Ch44 L112), the receptionist Monica (Book 12, Ch44 L13), plus the Gellers and the many students of Remore Academy.

## 7. Customs and daily life

Flowers pervade daily life. Dwellings and shops are built around vertical gardens. Academy students are a visible population; adventurers who wear Burning Violet's violet-flame flower pin are respected.

## 8. Dangers and opportunities

Political rather than monstrous: the factional struggle over Remore control of Burning Violet, rivalries between Academy graduates, and Purity and Builder-cult activity in the north. Opportunities include the Academy's teaching, the guild's contracts, and access to better training facilities.

## 9. Hooks

- A newcomer applies to Burning Violet and learns the factions behind the interview.
- A Remore Academy graduation and the contest over who gets sponsorship.
- A garden tower's flower magic goes out of control.

## Later developments (spoilers)

In Book 12 Jason applies to Burning Violet, but Roland Remore arranges the interview as a lesson and Team Biscuit withdraws their guild membership afterwards. Roland has his own conflict with an order he wants to keep Jason out of.
