## 1. Summary

For each place that changes, this file says what state it is in during each of the seven playable eras defined in Era_Selection.md. Use it to avoid placing a destroyed city on the map or a ruined one in its prime. A place missing from an era is simply not shown there in the lines read. The file is level 3 because it includes Book 12-13 outcomes.

## 2. The eras

1. Greenstone (Books 1-2). 2. Bronze and the Builder (Book 3). 3. Earth (Books 4-6). 4. Rimaros (Books 7-8). 5. Yaresh and the messengers (Books 9-10). 6. The Cardinas zone (Books 10-11). 7. The return (Books 12-13; Earth 2038).

## 3. Places that change

### Greenstone and its delta

- **Era 1-2**: the first city Jason knows; a colony and spirit-coin farm in a low-magic delta (see Greenstone.md). The Leust lumber conspiracy plays out in autumn (Book 1, Ch93 L5).
- **Era 7**: Jason is expected "in just a few days, to great fanfare" at the Adventure Society, twenty years after he was thrown down there (Book 12, Ch27 L45). A resident says Hornis is the furthest south he has been (Book 12, Ch38 L49).

### Boko

- **Era 1**: an old desert city of academics and artists with a Duke and an Adventure Society branch; Jason arrives by portal after the Ustei expedition (Book 1, Ch109 L7-21; Book 1, Ch113 L28).
- **Era 7**: the city is destroyed in Book 12 when the blast containment zone gives out after Jason's assassination; most of its ninety thousand people are evacuated (Book 12, Ch32 L15-29; Book 12, Ch33 L3-4). They shelter in Jason's astral kingdom (Book 12, Ch36 L3-5), the crater becomes a harbour (Book 12, Ch39 L37) and floating temples shielded by their gods remain (Book 12, Ch33 L3). The astral city of New Water is named so that it would not be named after Boko (Book 12, Ch39 L61).

### Cartise

- **Before Era 5**: a river city, the major hub of trade and travel in its area (Book 9, Ch29 L17-18).
- **Era 5**: destroyed just before the great surge when the Builder cult claimed a nearby astral space and a diamond-rank monster overwhelmed it; ruins "like London after the Blitz" and refugees overpopulating nearby towns (Book 9, Ch29 L17-19; Book 9, Ch30 L59). Jason's team clears the ruins (Book 9, Ch31 L11).

### Yaresh and Cardinas

- **Era 5**: a rainforest city of a few tens of thousands with a natural array beneath it (Book 9, Ch42 L26), besieged by messengers (Book 9, Ch42 L80). The Battle of Yaresh ends the siege.
- **Era 5-6**: the Cardinas expedition descends beneath it (Book 10, Ch72 L31). Cardinas, the brightheart city, is turned into a transformation zone in Book 11 (Book 11, Ch1 L16).
- **Era 7**: Yaresh remains a hub; the Valdis duel is staged in its mirage arena (Book 12, Ch16 L11; Book 12, Ch17 L55-73). Its survivors live in Rexion, a border town in the shaft walls managed by brighthearts (Book 12, Ch11 L46, L110).

### Rimaros and the Storm Kingdom

- **Era 4**: Jason arrives in a tropical cyclone as the surge begins (Book 7, Ch5-7; see Rimaros.md); a Builder fortress-city is destroyed above Livaros in the One Day War (Book 7, Ch65 L6). Palisaros is the town where Jason settles (Book 7, Ch13 L33).
- **Era 4 (Book 8)**: the Order of Redeeming Light converts towns and operates from a mountain stronghold (Book 8, Ch9 L40; see Rimaros.md).
- **Era 7**: the Storm Kingdom's ambassador Hils Jaramaris is warned against water or sky links (see Rimaros.md); Zara's brother Emiliano is Storm King (Book 12, Ch12 L17-20).

### Earth sites

- **Nitra**: Era 3, a Network centre until the Kostroma-style zone forms at the end of Book 5; Jason claims it in Book 6 (Book 5, Ch68 L68-69; Book 6, Ch5 L41). Era 6-7: the clan lives in a steampunk city around a pagoda (Book 11, Ch36 L9; see Nitra.md).
- **Saint-Étienne**: Era 3, the Dimensional Fortress found in 1948, hidden for about seventy years (Book 4, Ch51 L28-37); a second zone in Book 6 that Jason turns into a cloud city (Book 6, Ch35 L80; see Saint-Etienne.md). Era 7: a refugee city and clan centre where Erika and Ian live (Book 12, Ch41 L28).
- **Casselton and Asano Village**: Era 3, Jason's home and then the family compound, evacuated when the Grid is sabotaged; Era 6, the clan lives in Jason's astral domains in Europe (Book 11, Ch68); Era 7, seized by the Australian government in Book 12 and made a special-interest territory in Book 13 (Book 13, Ch960 L14; see Casselton_and_Asano_Village.md).

### Estercost

- **Era 3-7**: Vitesse is the City of Flowers on the Riviera (Book 6, Ch47 L603); Cyrion's analytical office tracks Jason in Book 6 and Jason studies Clive's bridge work there in Book 12 (see Cyrion.md). Beyond Cyrion's wall lie the toppled Builder golems from before the messenger invasion (Book 12, Ch48 L4-5).
- **Era 7**: the noble houses' abuse of indenture law triggers the System withdrawal (see Cyrion.md); a mob gathers outside Jason's palace after the System goes dark (Book 12, Ch51 L3).

### Other places

- **Rakesh**: Era 3, Clive is recruited and imprisoned on its campus (Book 6, Ch47 L656-662).
- **Kurdansk**: Era 3, Sophie's birthplace searched by the team (Book 6, Ch47 L386-390).
- **Casallini and Girlano**: Era 4, a border city where Sophie is ambushed (Book 8, Ch24-26; see Casallini.md).
- **Dragon Lands**: Era 7, seen from Jason's sky ship (Book 12, Ch26 L5-7).
- **Oleyu**: Era 3 only, a river border city (Book 6, Ch47 L838-840).
