## 1. How to use the Places files

Each place gets its own file following the project's place template: Summary, Location and layout, Society and rule, Magic level, History, Notable people, Customs and daily life, Dangers and opportunities, Hooks. This overview is the index and the map-in-words that ties them together. It does not replace the individual files.

The list of every place file, including the `Earth/`, `Astral_Spaces/` and `Minor_Places/` subfolders, is generated from the folder contents at the end of this file. The Great Southern Continent is named only once, as a stop on the team's route south in Book 9 (Book 9, Ch25 L4), and has no file.

## 2. The known world of Pallimustus

Pallimustus is a magic-rich world with two moons (Jason notices the second in the hedge maze on his first day) and a geography that the books repeatedly compare to Earth's. The comparisons are used by the characters and the narration to place things for the reader; they are not exact maps.

### 2.1 The nations and cities named in the books

- **Estercost**: a kingdom whose capital, **Cyrion**, is the largest city of the world. It contains **Vitesse**, the City of Flowers, which the books place where Jason would know the French Riviera. **Greenstone**, a delta city in the low-magic far south of the Africa-like continent, is a colony settled from Estercost a few centuries ago (Book 11, Ch68 L1343 calls Estercost the land from which Greenstone was colonised; Book 12, Ch29 L59 says most Greenstoners descend from Estercost immigrants). The Queen of Estercost appears in Book 12. Kazlahk is a city of sandstone, palm trees and money on Estercost's eastern coast, surrounded by desert and valuable for higher-magic spirit coin farms (Book 11, Ch68 L1380). Its notable institution is the Kazlahk University of Medicine (Book 11, Ch68 L1382).
- **The Storm Kingdom**: founded by Soramir Rimaros; a city of islands in the **Sea of Storms**. Its capital district is **Rimaros**; the largest city on its central island is named Jaitari in Book 9. It is ruled by a Storm King who must be gold-rank; the successor is chosen by the previous king after a contest, and the royal guard is provided by the Sapphire Crown guild.
- **Yaresh**: a rainforest city of a few tens of thousands with a natural array beneath it (Book 9, Ch42 L26).
- **Boko**: a desert academic city north of Greenstone, destroyed in Book 12 when the blast containment zone gave out after the System Administrator's assassination; most of its ninety thousand people were evacuated by Jason's aura before the blast, and the floating temples shielded by their gods remained (Book 12, Ch33 L3-9). The survivors were housed in **New Water**, an oasis city Jason named in his astral kingdom, where the Duke of Boko still holds management meetings (Book 12, Ch39 L61).
- **Zartos**: a subterranean city built around an underground river, largely populated by celestines (Book 7, Ch2 L75; Book 1, Ch16 L57); it is the home of the diamond-rank smith Virid Martine and his smithy, where Gary and Virid reforge Dread Salvation (Book 7, Ch2 L75).
- **Rakesh** on the north coast hosts a Magic Society campus that blocks portals outside designated zones (Book 6, Ch47 L656); **Hornis** is a port to the south, reached from Greenstone across the delta and the Veldt (Book 1, Ch102 L11). Book 6 places the continental council of the Africa-like continent at Rakesh, and a different passage places an Adventure Society continental council in Cyrion, so there appears to be one council per continent; Book 6 also puts the Adventure Society's continental council in Vitesse rather than Cyrion (Book 6, Ch47 L604), a conflict left open.
- **Where Greenstone sits**: Book 6 places it in the far south of the continent that Jason would call Africa, in a low-magic, sparsely populated south, while the north is far more populous. Rufus describes the western coastal region as mostly lifeless dunes around a fertile river delta.
- **Foreign lands** named at a distance: Kurdansk (in the People's Holy Federation of Dreisil; Sophie's birthplace), Pranay (the Sri Lanka equivalent; its port city Jayapura, Hester's birthplace, has a vast mirage chamber complex, Book 2, Ch69 L65-66, Ch73 L3; the island is larger and further south than Earth's, in the middle of the Indian Ocean equivalent; a portal stopover and one of the isolated city-states on the way to Estercost), Girlano (an inland nation south of the Storm Kingdom's coast, a kingdom in name but controlled by cartels; its border city is Casallini), Sadi Andali (a country whose slave markets are named in Book 12, Ch52 L35-41, where Jason's price is their destruction), Segurado (a small city-state in what would be Uruguay; messengers attack it in Book 12), Jorganis (a small island in the Pacific equivalent, almost entirely covered by a grimy city outside any society's or government's authority) and the Dragon Lands (the Antarctica equivalent, home of the draconians, named for subterranean apertures to a realm of fire that make volcanoes, hot springs and "dragon mouth" geysers; its farm exports pass through Greenstone's port).
- **Ruins and sites**: Cartise (a river city destroyed when the Builder cult claimed a nearby astral space, Book 9, Ch29 L17), Isart (a city on Jason's procession south that Emir asks him to reach, Book 9, Ch39 L12-20), Arcazitlan (a fortress town attacked by bone feasters, Book 7, Ch25 L77).

### 2.2 Magic in the landscape

Two figures matter for every place: **saturation** (how much magic a region produces; more frequent manifestations) and **density** (the rank of what manifests). Clive rates Greenstone's density as about mid-iron, "which is very low" (Book 3, Ch34 L60). The books give no numeric figure for Vitesse but place it a band higher: typical monsters are silver-rank, with frequent gold and occasional diamond (Book 1, Ch30 L43-44; see Vitesse.md). A place's rating shapes everything about it: who lives there, how quickly adventurers advance, what monsters are common, and which training tools operate. In Book 1 a family declines to lend a mirage chamber because the local magical density is too low to run it at more than bronze-rank for short periods.

Surges temporarily raise saturation. Natural arrays concentrate elemental magic and warp creatures. See `../03_Magic_and_Adventuring/Astral_Spaces_Zones_and_Surges.md` and `../03_Magic_and_Adventuring/Rituals_Arrays_and_Formations.md`.

### 2.3 Travel and communication

Travel between cities uses portals (limited by rank, range and previous visits), airboats and skimmers, trams in the cities, and the Adventure Society's networks of branches. Messages move by water link (Magic Society chambers) and the sky link communication network, which uses tablets. Both can be avoided if someone does not want a message traced; in Book 12 Hils Jaramaris is told to portal back to Rimaros in person rather than use either.

### 2.4 Earth

Earth appears from Book 4. Its principal sites are Casselton Beach (Jason's hometown, near Melbourne), the Asano family's village, the Network's installations, and the transformation zones at Nitra and Saint-Étienne. They are described in the Earth place files, when written.
