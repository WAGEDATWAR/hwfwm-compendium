## 1. Summary

The Storm Kingdom is a nation of islands in the Sea of Storms, founded by Soramir Rimaros, a diamond-ranker who was its first king. Its principal city, Rimaros, spans three islands and is protected by storm accumulators. Its monarch, the Storm King, must be gold-rank (and, per Book 7, Ch5 L67, royal heirs raised on cores are disqualified); the successor is chosen by the previous king after a contest. Its royal guard is supplied by the Sapphire Crown guild, whose members are examined quarterly, and more often in the guard (Book 7, Ch21 L12). It is the first place Jason lands when he returns to Pallimustus in Book 7, just as a monster surge begins.

## 2. Location and layout

- **Sea of Storms**: the Caribbean analogue (Book 7, Ch3 L44-47, with Jason's map at L45). The city of Rimaros comprises three islands; the largest concentrations of the kingdom's people live on a central landmass made up of what would be Cuba, Haiti and the Dominican Republic.
- **The three public islands** are Livaros, Arnote and Provo, in an east-west line (Book 7, Ch7 L70).
- **Livaros**: "the primary destination for adventurers... the adventurer island", with the Adventure Society campus and most services for essence users (Book 7, Ch7 L78; Book 7, Ch10 L5), and the skyport. Havi Estos owns property there and hires people to poke at Jason's aura (Book 7, Ch43 L47; Book 7, Ch62 L3, L43). A Builder fortress-city destroyed above it in Book 7.
- **Arnote**: the westernmost island, home to many wealthier citizens, with a relaxed lifestyle that is part of Rimaros's cultural identity and a minor branch of the royal family (Book 7, Ch7 L70-72); it is the least populous and least defended (Book 7, Ch64 L21). Palisaros is a little town on it, where Dawn stays and where Team Biscuit settles and of Jason's pagoda, built when authority infuses his Cloud Flask.
- **Provo**: the easternmost and most populous island, the general trade hub and home to most of the non-magical citizenry (Book 7, Ch40 L4).
- **The Royal Sky Island**: the palace.
- **Jaitari**: "the largest and most populous city" of the Storm Kingdom, on the largest island of the Sea of Storms (Book 9, Ch3 L81-82).
- **Defences**: windmill-like storm accumulators stand offshore, both shielding islands and powering their infrastructure (as on the island bought by the Sky Link Company). House Irios designs defences; Book 7 shows Irios family members controlling Livaros's defences, which fire back at attackers, behind a dome barrier. Irios designs and maintains the defences of Rimaros itself, the royal palace lake and many fortress towns.

## 3. Society and rule

- **The royal family**: Soramir Rimaros (founding king, a diamond-ranker with emerald hair who prefers disguise), Liara Rimaros (gold princess; husband Baseph), their children (Dara, the eldest, and Zara, the "Hurricane Princess").
- **The Sapphire Crown**: the royal guild; non-royals serve in the royal guard and take quarterly exams. Commander of the royal guard: Trenchant Moore, introduced as "Commander Trenchant Moore of the royal guard" and called "the siege sword" (Book 7, Ch52 L73; Book 8, Ch44 L50).
- **House Irios**: "the pre-eminent producer of defensive engineering" whose defences protect Rimaros and many fortress towns (Book 7, Ch18 L70); the family is "known as the shield of" the kingdom (Book 7, Ch37 L64). The text of its founder being Soramir's "brother" is not checked here.
- **The Magic Society** holds the second-largest sky island in Rimaros, after the palace (Book 7, Ch5 L67).
- **The Adventure Society** in Rimaros registers Jason and Farrah and questions the irregularities in his record.
- **Politics**: Jason's relationship with the throne is called the Storm Kingdom's most important political relationship by Soramir and Zila.

## 4. Magic level

High. Far above Greenstone's mid-iron. The Sea of Storms is saturated with storm and water magic; cyclones form as a matter of course, hence the accumulators. The Book 7 surge brings monsters from the deep.

## 5. History

- Founded by Soramir Rimaros.
- Book 7: Jason and Farrah arrive in a tropical cyclone as the surge begins; Zara's public claim that Jason proposed to her to escape an arranged match; the royal family "takes his measure"; bone feasters at Arcazitlan; Builder invasion platforms; the One Day War.
- Books 8-9: Jason's pagoda on Arnote; the Order of Redeeming Light and its mountain stronghold; the Storm Kingdom's allies and ambassadors.
- Book 12: the Storm Kingdom's ambassador Hils Jaramaris is warned not to use water or sky links to carry sensitive news.

## 6. Notable people

Soramir and Zila Rimaros, Liara and Baseph Rimaros, Dara and Zara Rimaros, Emiliano (the Storm King of Book 12, Zara's brother, Book 12, Ch12 L19; adopted cousin, Book 13, Ch1022 L20), Trenchant Moore, Jacinda Irios, Havi Estos, Pelli (the mayor of Palisaros, in the post since before Argy's family began growing fruit, Book 7, Ch13 L28-30), Hils Jaramaris.

## 7. Customs and daily life

Storm-watching, airships and sea-skimmers are everyday things; the islands rely on accumulators and magical defences. The Sapphire Crown's exams and royal ceremonies set the social calendar.

## 8. Dangers and opportunities

Cyclones, sea monsters (a kraken and a smoke dragon are among those named in the books), the surge's weekly monster quotas, Builder invasion platforms during the war, and the politics of an ambitious, powerful royal house.

## 9. Hooks

- A contract on Livaros that leads into the underworld of Havi Estos.
- An Irios engineer wants a favour.
- A cyclone that accumulators cannot manage.
- A royal contest to name a successor.

## Later developments (spoilers)

The Storm King who appears in Book 12 is Emiliano, Zara's brother and adoptive cousin. The kingdom becomes an ally of Jason's realm. The Builder's war and the surge are major events of this period.
