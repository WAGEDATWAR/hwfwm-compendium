## 1. Summary

Yaresh is a rainforest city with "only a few tens of thousands" of people, spread over "the footprint of a much larger centre" because its design deeply accommodates the natural environment (Book 9, Ch42 L26). Its walls are "a line of massive trees with walls of glossy black stone filling the gaps", with tunnels through the trunks (Book 9, Ch43 L33). It is the centre of the Battle of Yaresh in Book 9 and the gateway to the natural array beneath it.

## 2. Location and layout

- Rainforest setting; the outer walls are a line of enormous trees joined by black stone, with tunnels passing through the trunks to link wall-top sections.
- Black land skimmers queue at the gates for inspection (Book 9, Ch43 L33). Outside the central area most buildings have living magic trees as a core, shaped and supplemented with brickwork; the heart of the city has polished metal and glass towers (Book 9, Ch79 L5; Book 9, Ch81 L20).
- Towers stand at the heart of the city; the battle at the centre was "a dozen kilometres away" from the outskirts and the titanic figures could be seen looming over the towers (Book 9, Ch80 L3).
- **Beneath the city**: the natural array, the underground realm of Cardinas, ruled from the Citadel of Pillars (Book 10, Ch65 L3; Book 10, Ch72 L31) and, further below, an astral space occupied by the Builder cult.
- The Yaresh mirage-chamber arena hosts team exhibitions; the Duke of Yaresh watches from a ballroom-sized VIP box.

## 3. Society and rule

- Ruled by a duke, who attends the arena and sends representatives with Adventure and Magic Society convoys. The Aristocratic Faction, a bloc of wealthy aristocrats and merchant barons who own most of the land and provide most of the jobs, is the dominant political force in the region (Book 10, Ch14 L33-35); see `05_Organisations/Guilds_and_Houses.md`.
- Two diamond-rank adventurers live here: **Allayeth** and **Charist**, both elves.
- Hana Shavar is "the High Priestess of the Church of the Healer for the city of Yaresh" (Book 9, Ch74 L43, L114); she runs relief for refugees, and after the messenger attack much of Yaresh's population is homeless and housed at Jason's cloud palace (Book 9, Ch103 L84).
- Adventure Society presence is strong; Rufus stays in Yaresh during part of Book 9.

## 4. Magic level

High, shaped by the natural array's elemental magic. The array imbues elemental magic and makes messengers mutate with devolving intelligence; its changes "cause intelligence to rapidly and precipitously devolve" and cost the messengers most of their initial silver-rank force (Book 9, Ch104 L84).

## 5. History

- Before the series: the natural array and the Smoulder city of Cardinas lie beneath Yaresh.
- Book 8: Jason's team learns of Yaresh while dealing with Builder and messenger activity.
- Book 9: messengers besiege Yaresh; a naga genesis egg (an apocalypse beast) is triggered when Argrave Mericulato, an Adventure Society functionary whose aura mask hides his seething anger (Book 9, Ch77 L13), tosses an egg-like sphere at a guard (confirmed); the Battle of Yaresh ends the siege with the help of Jason's allies; Marek explains the natural array.
- Book 10: the Cardinas expedition descends, with Death Pact and Undeath events.
- Book 12: Yaresh remains a hub; the Valdis duel is staged in its mirage arena.

## 6. Notable people

Allayeth and Charist (diamond elves), the Duke of Yaresh, Marek Nior Vargas (defecting messenger), Tera Jun Casta and Kaal (messengers), Argrave Mericulato, Prince Valdis and Sigrid Freyn (visiting Mirror Kingdom team). Rufus is with Jason's group in Book 9: "Mostly doesn't," Rufus qualified (Book 9, Ch42 L34).

## 7. Customs and daily life

Residents live among the trees; buildings and wards are woven into the forest. The city is proud of its adventurers and of its mirage arena, whose exhibitions are commentated and sponsored.

## 8. Dangers and opportunities

Elemental monsters from the array, messenger incursions, and Builder-cult activity below. Opportunities: the arena, the diamond-ranked mentors, and the array itself, which attracts researchers.

## 9. Hooks

- An arena exhibition with a visiting team.
- A messenger defector asks for sanctuary.
- Digging teams of enslaved essence users need rescue.
- A researcher wants a safe passage down to Cardinas.

## Later developments (spoilers)

The Smoulder city is evacuated; its brightheart survivors, led by Lorenn, are sheltered in Jason's soul realm and later settle Rexion. The Battle of Yaresh makes the city a symbol of resistance to the messengers.
