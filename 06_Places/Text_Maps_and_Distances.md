## 1. Summary

A text map for each of four places: how the parts sit against one another, as the books describe them, and a table of the distances and travel times the text gives. Where the books give no distance, the table says so; do not invent one. The place files hold the detail; this file is for placing a scene quickly.

## 2. Greenstone

```
                 BOKO (older desert city, to the north; no distance stated)
                   |
   delta, desert, mangrove and marsh; quarry villages; Leust and other delta towns
                   |
   OLD CITY ---------- artificial strait ---------- THE ISLAND
   original harbour,                                 green marble, a bridge from Old City,
   sandy yellow stone,                               Divine Square (temples), the Duke's
   warehouse district (the Fortress),                residence in the park district,
   canal district (own wall)                         trade lanes with permits
```

- **Two halves**: Old City is "the original city of Greenstone, situated on the original harbour"; the Island was meant to be a massive breakwater (Book 1, Ch35 L15).
- **The bridge**: a green-marble bridge arcs over the water between the ports and the Island; Old City is built of sandy yellow stone (Book 1, Ch36 L68).
- **The Fortress**: older than the city walls, in the warehouse district (Book 1, Ch42 L21).
- **The canal district**: it has its own internal city wall and holds Clarissa Ventress's manor (Book 1, Ch53 L70).
- **Boko**: a desert academic city to the north of Greenstone (see Minor_Places/Boko.md).

## 3. Vitesse

```
   VITESSE, the City of Flowers on the Riviera
   skyline of garden towers (vines, roof gardens, a garden every third or fourth floor)
     |- the Geller tower (family and teams lodge by rank, staff on the bottom third)
     |- the Adventure Society branch, in a garden tower
     |- Remore Academy  <--- directly across --->  Burning Violet guild house
   CYRION, the capital of Estercost: closer than Vitesse to the Book 7 operation site
```

- **The towers**: huge towers with flowering vines, most with every third or fourth floor given to gardens; they are residences for the city's wealthy (Book 6, Ch47 L603).
- **The Geller tower**: Gellers and their teams lodge together, gold-rankers at the top and irons near the middle, with non-adventuring staff in the bottom third (Book 7, Ch24 L4).
- **The Adventure Society branch** sits in one of the iconic garden towers (Book 7, Ch43 L18).
- **The guild and the academy**: the Burning Violet guild house is "directly across from the Remore Academy", where it moved after Roland Remore took over the guild (Book 12, Ch43 L64).
- **Moving about**: a flying open-top carriage can dock halfway up a tower (Book 6, Ch47 L873).
- **Cyrion**: the capital is "closer than Vitesse" to the site of the Book 7 operation (Book 7, Ch43 L58).

## 4. Rimaros and the Storm Kingdom

```
   sky islands scattered above (the cream of society)
        palace island ........ above Livaros
        Magic Society island (the second largest, after the palace)

   ARNOTE ------------- LIVAROS ------------- PROVO
   west; wealthier       centre; the adventurer   east (by the line);
   citizens, a royal     island, the Adventure    most populous island,
   branch, quiet         Society campus, services the largest sky port
```

- **The line of islands**: the three public islands, Livaros, Arnote and Provo, lie "in a line running roughly east-west"; Arnote is the westernmost and Livaros is the central one (Book 7, Ch7 L70, L74).
- **Arnote** is home to wealthier citizens who lack a private island, and to a minor branch of the royal family (Book 7, Ch7 L70-72). Jason puts his cloud house there (Book 8, Ch39 L236).
- **Provo** is the most populous island and has the largest sky port (Book 8, Ch41 L57); that it lies east of Livaros follows from the line and is not stated.
- **Livaros** is the adventurer island, with the Adventure Society campus and most services for essence users (Book 7, Ch7 L78).
- **The sky islands**: they are for the cream of society; the Magic Society has the second-largest, right after the royal palace (Book 7, Ch5 L65-67). The palace floats above Livaros (Book 8, Ch39 L236).

## 5. Yaresh

```
   outer walls ........ barrier dome over the city
       outskirts
       entertainment district (messenger breach force, Book 9)
       Adventure Society campus (the old headquarters building is a secure residence)
       towers at the heart of the city
   camps of the gods' and messengers' forces: a few hours from the city
```

- **A small population on a large footprint**: a few tens of thousands of people, with a design that accommodates the rainforest, so the city covers the ground of a much larger centre (Book 9, Ch42 L26).
- **The entertainment district** is the area the breach force attacks (Book 9, Ch86 L16).
- **The Adventure Society**: the old headquarters building, with formidable defences, serves as a secure residence as the branch has grown to a campus (Book 9, Ch77 L3).
- **The barrier dome**: shockwaves of a fight some ten kilometres away still stir air under the dome (Book 9, Ch81 L18).

## 6. Using the maps

- **Pick the table row, not the diagram, for a distance**; the diagrams show only which part is beside which.
- **When a scene needs a distance the table lacks**, label the figure as invented in the scene notes.
- **Cross-check** the place files for people and customs, and Locations_by_Era.md for how each place changes between eras.

## 7. Open questions
