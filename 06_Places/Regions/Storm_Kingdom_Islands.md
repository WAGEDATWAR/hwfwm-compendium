## 1. Summary

The Storm Kingdom is a nation of islands in the Sea of Storms, the Caribbean analogue (Book 7, Ch3 L44-47), founded by Soramir Rimaros. Its best-known city, Rimaros, spans three public islands; its largest and most populous city is Jaitari (Book 9, Ch3 L81-82). Beyond the islands lies Girlano, the cartel country along its southern border.

## 2. The three public islands

The three public islands of Rimaros run east to west: Livaros, Arnote and Provo (Book 7, Ch7 L70).

- **Livaros**: "the adventurer island", with the Adventure Society campus, the skyport and most services for essence users (Book 7, Ch7 L78; Book 7, Ch10 L5).
- **Arnote**: the westernmost island, home to wealthier citizens and a minor branch of the royal family, the least populous and least defended (Book 7, Ch7 L70-72; Book 7, Ch64 L21). Palisaros is a laid-back town on it, where Jason's cloud house stands (Book 8, Ch52 L20).
- **Provo**: the easternmost, most populous island, the trade hub with most of the non-magical citizens (Book 7, Ch40 L4).
- **The Royal Sky Island**: the palace, above the islands.

## 3. Beyond the three islands

- **The central landmass**: the largest concentration of the kingdom's people lives on a central landmass made up of what would be Cuba, Haiti and the Dominican Republic (see ../Rimaros.md); the largest city there is Jaitari (Book 9, Ch3 L81-82).
- **Defences**: windmill-like storm accumulators stand offshore, shielding islands and powering infrastructure; House Irios designs and maintains Rimaros's defences (see ../Rimaros.md).
- **The coast and Girlano**: the Storm Kingdom controls the coast, and inland Girlano lies to its south, close enough that the Sea of Storms is visible from a tall hill (Book 8, Ch24 L22, L26). Casallini is Girlano's border city (Book 8, Ch24 L25).
- **Surges and forces**: the Storm Kingdom provides border-station guards and civic forces beyond the Adventure Society (Book 8, Ch26 L43-45; Book 8, Ch25 L136).

## 4. Rule

The Storm King must be gold-rank; the previous king picks a successor after a contest (see ../Rimaros.md). Soramir Rimaros, the founder, is still alive and deferred to even by the king (Book 9, Ch14 L4). Royal guard duties fall to the Sapphire Crown guild (see ../../05_Organisations/Guilds/Sapphire_Crown.md).
