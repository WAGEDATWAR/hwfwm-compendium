## 1. Summary

The continent that Jason would call Africa holds Greenstone, where Books 1-3 are set. In the text it is separate from Eurasia (see the World Primer), is far more populous in the north than the south, and has its continental Adventure Society and Magic Society strongholds at Rakesh on the north coast (Book 6, Ch47 L623). This file sets out the places the compendium already files here, from south to north.

## 2. The far south: Greenstone and its delta

- **Greenstone** is a colony city at the mouth of a river delta in the low-magic far south, in a western coastal region that is mostly lifeless desert around a fertile delta (see ../Greenstone.md). It was settled from Estercost a few centuries ago (Book 11, Ch68 L1343).
- **Magic level**: Clive rates Greenstone's density at about mid-iron, "which is very low" (Book 3, Ch34 L60). The cities of the south are low-magic and sparsely populated, while the north is far more populous (Book 6, Ch47 L623 in the Greenstone file).
- **The delta and the veldt**: the overland route from Greenstone runs through the delta, east into the veldt and then south to Hornis (Book 1, Ch102 L11; Book 3, Ch24 L17).
- **The lumber region**: on the south side of the river in the eastern delta, farthest from the city, is Leust with the Lindover and Clementson lumber properties (Book 1, Ch93 L3, L22; see ../Minor_Places/Leust.md and ../Minor_Places/Lindover_and_Clementson_Properties.md).

## 3. Ports and cities of the south and coast

- **Hornis** is a port "south and around the coast" from Greenstone, Greenstone's nearest rival port, used for smuggling and escape routes (Book 3, Ch24 L17; Book 2, Ch14 L16).
- **Boko** is an old desert city of academics and artists to the north of Greenstone, about an hour's recovery by portal from it for Hester (Book 1, Ch109 L9; Book 12, Ch29 L60). It was a coastal city but never a port (Book 12, Ch39 L37). It is destroyed in Book 12 (Book 12, Ch33 L3-9).
- **The Ustei**: a desert people whose fashion is less calm than Boko's (Book 1, Ch113 L21).

## 4. The north: Rakesh and the border country

- **Rakesh** is a populous, caste-divided city on the north coast, with a desert gulch and a hidden Builder-cult portal a few dozen kilometres south (Book 6, Ch47 L623, L651). The Gramid Passage links it with Vitesse (Book 6, Ch47 L703).
- **Oleyu** is a pleasant, prosperous border city of moderate size on river trade, where the Rakesh Magic Society branch's area of influence ends (Book 6, Ch47 L838-840).
- **Zartos** is a celestine-populated underground city around an underground river; its continent is not stated (Book 7, Ch2 L75).
- **Sadi Andali** is "a little dirtball country", almost lawless, in or near an eastern desert (Book 12, Ch50 L125-128). Its continent is not stated in the lines read.

## 5. Councils and authority

- The continental council of the Adventure Society sits at Rakesh (Book 6, Ch47 L623), while Cyrion and Vitesse are also described as seats of a council; the text reads most simply as one council per continent (see ../Minor_Places/Rakesh.md).

## 6. The Great Southern Continent

The only mention is the team's route plan in Book 9: move south down the continent, cross to the Great Southern Continent, cross north again to Hornis, then on to Greenstone, Vitesse and Cyrion (Book 9, Ch25 L4). Nothing describes it.
