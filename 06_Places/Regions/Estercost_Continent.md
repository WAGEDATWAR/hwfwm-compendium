## 1. Summary

Estercost is the kingdom whose capital Cyrion is the largest city in the world and whose Vitesse sits on the equivalent of the French Riviera (Book 6, Ch47 L603). The text places it on the Europe-like continent and calls it the home of Greenstone's founders. It is the setting of most of Books 6 and 11 and the base of Remore Academy and the Burning Violet guild.

## 2. The kingdom

- **Crown**: a queen in Book 12, her name not found (Book 12, Ch51 L13).
- **Capital**: Cyrion, ringed by concentric walls and farmland of "some of the most magical and valuable growing land on the planet" (Book 12, Ch48 L3). It looks "like Tokyo by way of the Emerald City of Oz" from the air (Book 12, Ch48 L22). It is the seat of the Adventure Society's continental council in Book 7 (see ../Cyrion.md).
- **Vitesse**: the City of Flowers on Estercost's southern coast, famous for garden towers with every third or fourth floor given to gardens (Book 6, Ch47 L603). Remore Academy and the Burning Violet guild house stand opposite each other (Book 12, Ch43 L64).
- **Kazlahk**: a wealthy coastal city on Estercost's eastern border, surrounded by desert and spirit coin farms, famous for scalding heat (Book 11, Ch68 L1380-1381).
- **Remains of an old war**: toppled Builder world-engineer golems lie beyond Cyrion's outer wall from before the messenger invasion (Book 12, Ch48 L4-5).

## 3. Links to other regions

- **Greenstone** was settled from Estercost (Book 11, Ch68 L1343; see Africa_like_Continent.md).
- **Rakesh** is reached from Vitesse across the Gramid Passage (Book 6, Ch47 L703).
- **Kurdansk** is in the People's Holy Federation of Dreisil, Sophie's birthplace; the files do not say it is on this continent (Book 6, Ch47 L390).

## 4. Magic and culture

The books give no density figure for Vitesse; the wiki says it is much greater than Greenstone's (wiki; see ../Vitesse.md). For customs and dress see ../../04_Peoples_and_Cultures/Estercost_Culture.md and Vitesse_Culture.md.
