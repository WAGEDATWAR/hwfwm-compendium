## 1. Summary

Jason's personal space grows over the series: a dimensional inventory that only he can physically enter (Book 4, Ch2 L70: "others cannot be invited or forcibly intrude") becomes the Spirit Vault, which those who trust him completely can enter (Book 4, Ch57 L59), then a spirit domain, a soul realm that travels along the world link, and finally an astral kingdom once he possesses an astral throne and gate. Places inside it are described below.

## 2. Evolution

Inventory (Book 4, Ch2 L70: only Jason may enter); the Spirit Vault, the enhanced inventory (Book 4, Ch30 L45), which only people who implicitly trust him can enter and which he may seal against anyone (Book 4, Ch57 L59); Erika, Emi, Ken and Hiro could enter, as later did Farrah, Asya and Ian (Book 5, Ch44 L44-45); the gate to his storage space admits "those you allow" (Book 6, Ch13 L79); a spirit domain (Book 6, Ch2 L15); the vault as the extradimensional realm within his soul (Book 7, Ch1 L18); an astral kingdom once the Astral Throne and Gate appear (Book 8, Ch49 L105); consolidation of the Cardinas zone with the Soul Forge and the forest city Arbour (Book 11, Ch1 L54, L64 on the soul forge; Arbour introduces itself, Book 11, Ch68 L2474).

## 3. The vault

Seen first as a floating obsidian gazebo of black-and-white marbled obsidian, circular with four archways and open sides, floating in a rain-filled void (Book 4, Ch30 L49). The garden form is confirmed in the text as a largely hanging garden of flower-wreathed bamboo trellises over flagstone paths, which does not take more space as it grows (a function of rank). The same passage describes a blood section of red flowers and blood-red flagstone like walking through an artery, a sin section of black, red, white and gold beds, a dark section in an underground cave with luminescent fungus and silver grass, and a doom section of trees and bushes, with a creek that runs through all of them to a waterfall and an underground fairy grotto (Book 4, Ch57 L3-10). In Book 8 Clive tours the same garden estate, where the essence sections intermingle, a subterranean network of fungus-lit tunnels runs under it, the sin garden is a manicured plot of black and white flowers and the doom areas are wild jungle (Book 8, Ch2 L57-63). Jason's cloud house, in Book 8, becomes a pagoda of smoky crystal flecked with gold, silver and blue light, the same design as the pagodas at the heart of his Earth domains; in the soul space the pagoda is larger than those and stands at the edge of a large public square. Inside, others cannot use abilities; Jason and his familiars can affect them. Emi and Farrah are the first to enter.

## 4. The domains

- **Slovakia (Nitra)**: the territory renamed the Arrival Pagoda, whose portals connect to places in the astral space and which any non-hostile may use by default (Book 6, Ch5 L41; Book 6, Ch19 L62). The arrival building is rebuilt as an octagonal pagoda (Book 6, Ch5 L53); the portal area holds ten portal arches in dark crystal (Book 6, Ch19 L21); the central city, about eighteen kilometres across, has a semi-industrial, steampunk feel, with a ring of about a hundred and sixty kilometres around it and roamable land about seven hundred kilometres in each direction (Book 6, Ch23 L48, L56). Hostile intruders suffer ability-ignoring afflictions; those who venerate Jason have afflictions converted to Integrity.
- **France (Saint-Étienne)**: a cloud city. See the Earth files.

## 5. The astral kingdom

After the Astral Throne and Astral Gate appear (Book 8, Ch49 L105), Jason is an astral king. The kingdom is an island "roughly thirty kilometres by fifteen, with widely differing elevations", with "a small mountain, sandy beaches and what looked to be an area sunk below sea level" (Book 8, Ch65 L30). The island has a lagoon at the opposite end from the mountain with a small town along the shore, a larger city in the middle (without population) and a pagoda ten storeys tall instead of the usual five (Book 8, Ch65 L29-31). Features include a sea-cave geyser of raw magic and a glass tunnel under the sea (the Astral Gate), and the Soul Forge later.

## 6. The soul realm tour (Book 9-11)

By essence: a rose garden (blood), underground mushroom caverns (dark), a rope-bridge gorge with mana creeks, a farmhouse where Tera perches, a vampire-manor estate, the flesh pit, a subterranean blood-and-flesh space tied to the Colin summoning stone (Book 10, Ch33 L109-111), and the Colin greenhouse. Mana flows in creeks. He can stabilise any physical reality he is inside.

## 7. Arbour, New Water and Rexion (spoilers)

- **Arbour**: an avatar introduces itself as "Arbour" in Book 11; the notes describe it as a tree city in Jason's astral kingdom with redwood tree mansions and light rail, with its own soul and avatar, formerly the corrupted World Tree or birthing tree (notes, Book 13); in Book 13 Jason and Emi stand on a platform in a tree mansion spanning several towering redwoods in Arbour (Book 13, Ch960 L3).
- **New Water**: the oasis city in his astral kingdom, named so that it would not be named after Boko (Book 12, Ch39 L61); the Duke of Boko is holding a management meeting in its administrative centre when Jason fetches him, and addresses him as "Lord Asano" (Book 12, Ch39 L61-65).
- **Rexion**: a city for Cardinas's brightheart survivors, led by Lorenn (confirmed as their leader); Book 12 describes a border town dug into the walls of the shaft, managed by the brighthearts with a mostly transient population (Book 12, Ch11 L46, L110), where Jason forbids anyone to make Rexion a temple (Book 12, Ch13 L34). The array-powered citadel is unchecked.

## 8. Dangers and hooks

Jason's moods shape the landscape; intrusions from the great astral beings; the Flesh Pit. Hooks: a guest who trusts him enough to enter the vault; a refugee settlement dispute; a visit to the Colin greenhouse.
