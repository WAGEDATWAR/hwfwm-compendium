## 1. Summary

An artificial astral space that turns out to be a giant Builder boarding vessel. The Order of the Reaper repurposed its delivery system into a portal system powered by Jason-like power. The team enters it around the middle of Book 3.

## 2. Layout

- Twelve portal towers about eight kilometres apart, each holding a dormant world engineer golem, and a central tower. Jason calls them "a dozen diamond-ranked super-golems" that must not be allowed to wake.
- A giant stone ring gate about a hundred metres across floats horizontally above the crater and rises toward a sky anomaly. Shade identifies it as the anchor point of a physical bridge across the astral between this space and Jason's world.

## 3. Events

Clive plans to carry an inversion device up the central tower to burn out the world engineers and power a portal home. The cult's leader Zato says their people planted a beacon inside the astral space, which let them target it and begin establishing an astral bridge; once the portal opens, everyone goes through (confirmed). The Builder's forces include converted clergy with clockwork cores, spyder drones and an energy ghoul.

## 4. Dangers and hooks

World engineers waking, cultists, and the vessel's drift. Hooks: an expedition to salvage the portal system; a cult remnant that escaped.
