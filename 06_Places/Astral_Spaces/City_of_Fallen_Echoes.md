## 1. Summary

An ancient, circular, overgrown city in an astral space with a sea around it, entered through the drowned fortress at Sky Scar Lake. It is the site of Emir Bahadir's contest for the Scythe of the Reaper, a trial gauntlet that tests wisdom, courage, capability, intellect and resolve.

## 2. Layout

- Twelve gate towers; each person who enters arrives at a random tower.
- A circular city with streets and ruins, overgrown by the astral space.
- Monsters: siege golems, margolls, a sloth demon, grizzards, vorger swarms, flesh abominations and a blood root vine. A sloth demon is a gold-rank monster with a soporific power that cripples speed (Book 2, Ch45 L7); the space's overall density is not stated in the passages I checked.

## 3. The trials

Shade administers five trials in order, with a hidden sixth at the end (Book 2, Ch44 L39-41):
1. **The first trial** (Book 2, Ch44 L47): a choice between wisdom and courage that "cannot be failed"; it decides which of the two virtues is tested at the third trial. Choosing wisdom gives a Medallion of Escape (silver-rank, uncommon; projects the aura to evacuate, works only in the city) and a Lesser Miracle Potion (Book 2, Ch44 L119-128).
2. **The second trial, capability**: the City of Fallen Echoes itself, the longest of the five; the objective is to reach the centre (Book 2 Ch44 L41-45; completed Ch57 L29). Reward: a random magic item.
3. **The third trial** (Book 2, Ch57 L68): those who chose courage first now face the test of wisdom; those who chose wisdom face courage, each alone behind a chosen door, where each meets a nightmare hag (diamond-rank astral entities that cannot harm directly; Jason faces an evil future self, Humphrey his family, notes). Failing wisdom means removal; failing courage means death.
4. **The fourth trial, intellect** (Book 2, Ch58 L9-24): a time-limited test of calm under pressure; failure means death. Jason receives a Summoner's Die: Form (an iron-rank growth item of legendary rarity) after the constellation puzzle.
5. **The fifth trial, resolve** (Book 2, Ch58 L70-74): the invigilator asks Jason to execute the Order's captive enemies; he refuses ("Not a chance") and the reward is an Immortal Crest (Book 2, Ch58 L100-102). Reward: a random magic item.
6. **The hidden trial, insight** (Book 2 Ch60 L54-56): the "ability to see beyond appearances"; reward unknown at the outset.

Participants may withdraw before each later trial through a gate (Book 2 Ch44 L57-59; Ch57 L70).

Nineteen adventurers pass all the trials. About four hundred and nineteen time-displaced priests and holy warriors are freed.

## 4. Dangers and hooks

Time displacement, hags that read the mind, and the contest's politics. Hooks: a freed priest with centuries-old knowledge; a trial reward whose use is unclear.
