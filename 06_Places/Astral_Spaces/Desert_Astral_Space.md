## 1. Summary

A water-and-islands astral space reached from the desert by a submerged aperture. A large Greenstone expedition goes in at the climax of Book 1 to find what is disrupting it. They are ambushed by a construct army.

## 2. Layout

- Entered through a submerged aperture; Danielle Geller surfaces in "a turquoise lagoon, under a clear cerulean sky", mostly bounded by rocky rises with scrubby trees (Book 1, Ch100 L8).
- Islands, searched one by one by the expedition. On the third island the party finds "a five-sided column, about as tall as a person and covered in magical engravings" (Book 1, Ch107 L3); several are later packed away (Book 1, Ch113 L45). The Magic Society's researchers recognise them as part of the disruption.
- The expedition is supported from the desert: Emir's cloud palace, and his portaler Hester, who can open portals only to places she has previously visited (as is normal for long-range teleports). Boko is where the Adventure Society returns the freed Ustei slaves and women and children who wanted out of the tribe (Book 1, Ch109 L8).

## 3. The ambush

A construct army of "wooden puppets, awkward but numerous", stone golems two or three times human size, clockwork creatures such as a giant steel-cog tiger, and a huge steel spider carrying a robed figure (Book 1, Ch108 L90, L97; Book 1, Ch107 L76), led by sandy-robed bronze and silver masters and silver-rank abominations (metal grafted into flesh, extra metal arms), attacks the expedition. Farrah Hurin is killed; Henry Geller dies; Jonah and Thadwick and three others are found injured; Rufus loses an arm. Two captives take their own lives by exploding crystals.

## 4. Aftermath

The incident ties the Builder cult to the region and sets up Book 2.

## 5. Hooks

- Return to the islands to retrieve the remaining columns.
- A survivor of the ambush wants answers.
