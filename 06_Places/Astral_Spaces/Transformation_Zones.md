## 1. Summary

A transformation zone is a sealed dome or sphere in which a place is rewritten; the Kostroma sphere extends underground and completely encapsulates the city (Book 5, Ch44 L48). Inside is a genesis space: a landscape of anomalies, territories and cores. The wiki's phrase for zones, "scabs over dimensional ulcers", was not found in the book text; the later Book 13 briefing speaks of removing reality cores making "scabs on wounds in the universe" only in earlier notes. In Book 5 Dawn names the danger "dimensional ulceration" and says the worst case is that the dimensional membrane rapidly collapses and the world is annihilated, tearing a chunk out of reality; it is more likely contained to the planet or at least the solar system. She adds that "in the best case it will establish a second source of magic that will start feeding into this world" (Book 5, Ch68 L34-38).

## 2. Mechanics of the world

- **The seal**: "forty-three hours after the sphere moved into place, it vanished" at Kostroma, the first zone (Book 5, Ch44 L48).
- **Sealing**: inside a zone Jason's essence abilities are sealed (Book 6, Ch3 L5) while his racial powers remain (Book 6, Ch1 L83); a normal transformation stays sealed about two days (Book 6, Ch3 L39); the first zone message is Book 6, Ch2 L15. That aura and perception are sealed is not found.
- **Anomalies**: shape-shifting monsters (silver or gold) born from the strongest presence and memories in the zone.
- **Cores**: defeated anomalies yield Stable Genesis Cores (used to expand a spirit domain), Unstable Genesis Cores (dangerous; they transformed Guo and Tran), orbs that unseal an essence ability, and the Genesis Reclamation Core.
- **Territories**: each at least about a hundred kilometres across in the Cardinas zone ("a hundred kilometres across at a minimum"; the estimate puts the whole zone "somewhere between a continent and a planet" in size, Book 11, Ch17 L10); claiming one requires clearing anomalies and the final boss. Defenders have 117 minutes after a challenge (Book 11, Ch17 L27), and claiming several territories causes anger and paranoia (Book 11, Ch17 L33).
- **Stabilisation**: claiming territories shows a "severity reduction" like a loading bar; at one hundred percent the zone becomes a permanent astral space, the dome vanishes, and a reality core remains. If the borders are not resolved the zone collapses inward.

## 3. Known zones

- **Nitra (Slovakia)**: Book 5 end to Book 6; becomes Jason's first Earth domain. Territories include Arrival Pagoda, Soul Haven and Tranquil Shadow Woods.
- **Saint-Étienne (France)**: Book 6; a second zone triggered inside the old Dimensional Fortress.
- **Cardinas (Pallimustus)**: Book 11; around the natural array and its corrupted tree. A mountain fortress shaped like Jason's head contains a replica of Casselton Beach. The zone's length is "months" (the territory is under two weeks old at Book 11, Ch18 L4; Nik is six months old at Book 11, Ch50 L15); the Tree territory is "roughly eight kilometres across" and "close to perfectly round" (Book 11, Ch51 L3).
- **Kostroma (Russia)**: the first transformation event; the whole city is sealed in a sphere and Jason, Farrah and Dawn enter after it drops.

## 4. Dangers and hooks

Zones shape their enemies from the minds of those inside. Hooks: a zone opens under a settled city; a cult exploits the Genesis Reclamation Core; refugees from a collapsed territory.
