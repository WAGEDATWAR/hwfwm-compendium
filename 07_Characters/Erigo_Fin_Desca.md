## 1. Summary

The Builder's prime vessel, a former messenger of the Unorthodoxy heavily modified by the Builder.

## 2. Facts

- The Builder's new prime vessel after Shako was taken by the Sundered Throne (Book 10, Ch15 L5): originally a messenger, now with silver metal wings, alabaster skin, amber glass eyes and no hair, in a toga of interlaced metal (Book 10, Ch15 L5; Book 11, Ch68 L2528).
- He corrects Jamis: "His name is the Builder... He is not one of our kind anymore" (Book 10, Ch15 L10), and says he has moved on from the Unorthodoxy (Book 10, Ch15 L15-20).
- He warns that the planet goes nowhere unless civility is kept and goes to supervise the proceedings (Book 10, Ch15 L25-26).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Cold and exact; he has no patience for being addressed wrongly.
