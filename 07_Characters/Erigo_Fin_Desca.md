## 1. Summary

Erigo Fin Desca is the Builder's new prime vessel, a former messenger of the Unorthodoxy heavily modified by the Builder after Shako was taken by the Sundered Throne.

## 2. Appearance

- Silver metal wings, alabaster skin, amber glass eyes and no hair, in a toga of interlaced metal (Book 10, Ch15 L5).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Insistent on names**: "His name is the Builder... He is not one of our kind anymore" (Book 10, Ch15 L10).
- **Corrects titles**: he corrects Jamis's usage of the Builder's name (Book 10, Ch15 L10).
- **Supervisory**: he warns that the planet goes nowhere unless civility is kept (Book 10, Ch15 L25-26).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Prime vessel of the Builder; the avatars gather around him on the dimensional ship (Book 10, Ch15 L5).

## 5. Background

- Originally a messenger of the Unorthodoxy, he says he has moved on from it (Book 10, Ch15 L15-20).
- Became prime vessel after the Sundered Throne took Shako (Book 10, Ch15 L5).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 10**: the observation-room scene with the astral king avatars (Book 10, Ch15 L5-26).

## 7. Relationships

- **Jamis Fran Muskar**: an astral king whose avatar he corrects (Book 10, Ch15 L6-10).
- **The Builder**: the god he serves (Book 10, Ch15 L10).
