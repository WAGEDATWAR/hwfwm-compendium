## 1. Summary

A heavy-armoured Geller family fighter on Rick's team who is captured by the Builder cult and dies when the star seed is extracted.

## 2. Facts

- He challenges Jason in front of Danielle: "I'm going to show this little no-name weed what it means to fight a Geller" (Book 1, Ch66 L72-78).
- On Rick's team he is the bulwark, slowed by heavy armour and a shield (Book 1, Ch67 L14-15); he shouts to draw out monsters (Book 1, Ch67 L66).
- After the desert astral-space expedition Danielle seeks legal permission from his family to have him examined for a star seed, as he refuses (Book 2, Ch10 L26-50).
- Humphrey says Jonah had quit Rick's team and was found with a new team in Hornis (Book 2, Ch14 L116).
- Years later he is remembered as captured, implanted with a star seed by the Builder cult and dead in the extraction; Dustin Kettering took his place on the team (Book 9, Ch1 L39; Book 12, Ch16 L83).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Brash and loyal; the Geller who learned too late what the family name could not protect him from.
