## 1. Summary

Jonah Geller is a heavy-armoured Geller family fighter on Rick's team who is captured by the Builder cult and dies when the star seed is extracted. Brash and loyal, he learns too late what the family name cannot protect him from.

## 2. Appearance

Heavy armour and a shield slow him on Rick's team (Book 1, Ch67 L14-15).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Brash**: "I'm going to show this little no-name weed what it means to fight a Geller" (Book 1, Ch66 L72-78).
- **Loud in battle**: he shouts to draw out monsters (Book 1, Ch67 L66).
- **Resistant to help**: Danielle must seek legal permission from his family to have him examined for a star seed, as he refuses (Book 2, Ch10 L26-50).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- The team's bulwark in heavy armour and shield (Book 1, Ch67 L14-15).

## 5. Background

- Humphrey says Jonah had quit Rick's team and was found with a new team in Hornis (Book 2, Ch14 L116).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: challenges Jason (Book 1, Ch66 L72-78); fights on Rick's team (Book 1, Ch67).
- **Book 2**: star seed examination fight; his gruesome demise (Book 2, Ch10 L26-50; Book 2, Ch21 L19).
- **Book 9-12**: remembered as captured, implanted with a star seed by the Builder cult and dead in the extraction; Dustin Kettering took his place (Book 9, Ch1 L39; Book 12, Ch16 L83).

## 7. Relationships

- **Rick**: team leader (Book 1, Ch67 L14).
- **Danielle Geller**: family elder who sought his examination (Book 2, Ch10 L26-50).
- **Dustin Kettering**: replacement on the team (Book 12, Ch16 L83).
