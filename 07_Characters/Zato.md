## 1. Summary

Zato is the silver-rank leader of the Builder cult's local forces in Book 3, a metal-bodied shapeshifter who recruits Thadwick, drives the cult's survivors to the Vane Estate and is the final opponent of Jason's team. He is competent, unfazed and sure of victory.

## 2. Appearance

- A silvery metal body that flows "like quicksilver", reshaping itself to produce combat abilities (Book 3, Ch67 L69).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **On top of things**: Jason says "He does seem pretty on top of things... I actually kind of like him" and invites him to join team "Hopelessly Outmatched" (Book 3, Ch57 L119).
- **Convinced of victory**: "Zato knew that his victory was imminent. He knew that the adventurers were no match for his power" (Book 3, Ch68 L60).
- **Quiet in command**: he stands with his back to the door, eyes lingering on Timos's neck marks, saying nothing (Book 3, Ch41 L73).
- **Recruiter**: "I see your true potential" (Book 3, Ch46 L60).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Silver rank, with a metal body of shifting abilities (Book 3, Ch67 L69).
- Immovable in the final fight: no damage seems to impair him, even Humphrey's strikes (Book 3, Ch68 L46).
- Trump card: after baiting out Sophie's absorb power, his body explodes into a mass of shrapnel (Book 3, Ch67 L100).

## 5. Background

- "The last silver-ranker left in the Builder cult's local forces"; tasked with leading the evacuation when the island stronghold was invaded, he escaped with a good number of people, though none of the other silver-rankers survived (Book 3, Ch7 L88-89).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 3**: leads the evacuation (Book 3, Ch7 L88-89); stands before the inert archway (Book 3, Ch41 L73); recruits Thadwick (Book 3, Ch46 L60); asks what to do with bronze-rankers the cult cannot convert with clockwork cores (Book 3, Ch60 L37); falls before Jason and Sophie flee to the gate (Book 3, Ch66 L45); the climax with Humphrey and Sophie (Book 3, Ch67 L69-100; Book 3, Ch68 L46-61).

## 7. Relationships

- **Thadwick Mercer**: his recruit (Book 3, Ch46 L60).
- **Humphrey and Sophie**: opponents in the climax (Book 3, Ch68 L58-60).
- **Timos**: his follower, whose neck he notices (Book 3, Ch41 L73).
