## 1. Summary

The last silver-ranker of the Builder cult's local forces, who leads its survivors to the Vane Estate.

## 2. Facts

- "The last silver-ranker left in the Builder cult's local forces", he leads the evacuation of their island stronghold and escapes with many of their people; none of his fellow silver-rankers survive (Book 3, Ch7 L88-89).
- Recruits Thadwick: "I see your true potential" (Book 3, Ch46 L60).
- Asks what to do with the bronze-rankers when the cult cannot convert them with clockwork cores (Book 3, Ch60 L37).
- Humphrey faces him in the climax, throwing his dragon sword (Book 3, Ch68 L59-61).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Zealous and desperate; he talks of the Builder's glory while his people die.
