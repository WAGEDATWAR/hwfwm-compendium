## 1. Summary

Carlos Quilido is a gold-rank priest of the Healer and the world's leading specialist in soul damage, star seeds and aura conditions. He helps Jason recover from the Builder's attack, leads a healing team, studies Purity's Flames of Redemption on Melody Jain, and later develops the Quilido Procedure that removes monster-core taint.

## 2. Appearance

- A broad-shouldered, swarthy man with the polished perfection of gold rank, who wears casual clothes rather than Healer robes (Book 3, Ch22 L6).
- "A humble man in simple clothes, though the world will not let a gold-ranker be too humble" (Book 8, Ch41 L58).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Priest first**: "First and foremost, though I am a priest of the Healer. Before anything else, I'm here to help you, not for you to help me" (Book 3, Ch22 L73).
- **Uneasy at society events**: Arabelle plays guide to "Carlos, a priest of the Healer who was not comfortable at fancy social events" (Book 9, Ch14 L7).
- **Dogged**: he once harangued Jason to be an experimental subject when Jason had barely woken from a coma, and Jason threw him across the room with his aura; Carlos backing down since is called "considerable progress" (Book 10, Ch39 L41).
- **Protective of patients**: in a lab coat and a glare he looks "like a doctor shooing people away from his harried patient" (Book 13, Ch979 L93).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- **Specialty**: "Finding and dealing with soul influences is his specialty; he's at the top of his field. From star seeds to vampirism to plain soul trauma, he's the best there is" (Book 9, Ch27 L10).
- **Star seeds**: the Healer's church took over extracting star seeds from Purity: "we, the church of the Healer, have taken over that task" (Book 3, Ch22 L35); he warns that extraction can be lethal without continuous healing (Book 3, Ch22 L11).
- **Rituals**: gold-rank soul-projection ritual lamps and circles (Book 3, Ch23 L36, L63).
- **Soul realm research**: he conducts his research inside Jason's soul realm because there "pain, damage, even death itself could be suspended" (Book 12, Ch9 L4); they work seven hours of ritual magic and pseudo-surgery (Book 12, Ch9 L19).
- **The Quilido Procedure**: a slow process that removes the taint of monster cores from an essence user (Book 13, Ch1001 L9; see EDJI_and_Post_Reveal_Institutions.md).

## 5. Background

- He once met Amos Pensinata "in a lunatic's dungeon" (Book 9, Ch28 L50) and has worked with Arabelle many times (Book 8, Ch41 L64).
- The leader and healer of Team Work Saw (Book 8, Ch12 L61).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 3**: arrives in Greenstone portalled and examines Jason; helps with soul projection and the adventurer group therapy (Book 3, Ch22 L3; Ch23; Ch24 L43).
- **Book 8-9**: leads his team; he helps Jason after the coma; goes with Arabelle on the long journey (Book 8, Ch61 L3; Book 9, Ch28 L47).
- **Book 10**: develops the calming technique for Melody with Jason and Amos (Book 10, Ch36 L16), explains how the Flames differ from lesser vampirism (Book 10, Ch39 L49), and is one of the gold-rank participants in Cardinas (Book 10, Ch59 L5).
- **Book 12-13**: soul-realm research (Book 12, Ch9) and the Earth healing work with Neil and Ian (Book 13, Ch988 L4; Book 13, Ch953 L84).

## 7. Relationships

- **Arabelle**: long-time colleague and the other half of the therapy partnership (Book 3, Ch24 L43).
- **Jason**: patient, research subject and eventually collaborator; he can still be thrown across the room (Book 10, Ch39 L41).
- **Melody Jain**: his long-term case (Book 10, Ch36 L15-17).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

His work on the Flames of Redemption leads to the Book 10 conclusion that they are an extreme modification of lesser vampirism (Book 10, Ch36 L14).

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Calm, practical and curious, with a priest's bedside manner and a scientist's stubbornness. Cue: "First and foremost, though I am a priest of the Healer."
