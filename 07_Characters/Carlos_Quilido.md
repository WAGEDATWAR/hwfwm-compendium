## 1. Summary

A priest of the Healer who helps Jason restore his soul and later develops the Quilido Procedure.

## 2. Facts

- "The Healer Priest, Carlos Quilido, had helped Jason restore his damaged soul after it was besieged by the Builder's star seed" (Book 8, Ch29 L44).
- A humble man in simple clothes, though the world will not let a gold-ranker be too humble (Book 8, Ch41 L58).
- Joins the Cardinas expedition with a new research team (Book 11, Ch67 L6; Ch68 L367).
- The Quilido Procedure removes the taint of monster cores (Book 13, Ch1001 L9).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Humble, scholarly and cautious about consequences.
