## 1. Summary

Yumi Asano is Jason's paternal grandmother and the matriarch of the Asano clan. She came to magic late in life and her flesh-shaping powers restored her youth, so she looks no older than Jason. Authoritative, affectionate and unimpressed by his status, she pushes him to formalise the clan.

## 2. Appearance

- Her flesh-warping powers restored her youth, giving her a mid-twenties appearance (Book 11, Ch68 L565); in Book 13 she looks to be in her early thirties (Book 13, Ch1002 L31).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Stern**: Jason greets her "G'day, Nanna... Sorry, Grandmother" and earns a glare (Book 4, Ch74 L40).
- **Deliberate**: she quietly takes in Jason's explanation, sipping tea, before responding (Book 5, Ch12 L84).
- **Scolding**: she scolds Jason "in a very matronly tone" (Book 13, Ch1002 L31).
- **Wary**: she wonders whether any of the boy she knew is still inside Jason (Book 12, Ch2 L102).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Flesh-shaping powers (Book 11, Ch36 L8).
- Forbids members going far outside the domain (Book 11, Ch36 L8-12).

## 5. Background

- Jason's paternal grandmother (Book 11, Ch68 L565).
- Book 12 also calls a woman named Noriko "matriarch of the Asano clan" (Book 12, Ch63 L19); the two descriptions are not reconciled (open question).
