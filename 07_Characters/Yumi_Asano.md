## 1. Summary

Jason's paternal grandmother and the matriarch of the Asano clan.

## 2. Facts

- Present in Book 4, raising her eyebrows at Jason (Book 4, Ch74 L43).
- Pushes Jason to formalise the clan (Book 6, Ch47 L58).
- Forbids members from going far outside the domain (Book 11, Ch36 L8-12).
- Magic leaves her looking no older than Jason (Book 12, Ch2 L92).
- In Book 13 she sits as matriarch beside General Mills and scolds Jason in "a very matronly tone for someone who looked to be in her early thirties" (Book 13, Ch1002 L23, L31).
- Book 12 also calls a woman named Noriko "matriarch of the Asano clan" (Book 12, Ch63 L19); the two descriptions are not reconciled (open question).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Authoritative, affectionate and unimpressed by his status.
