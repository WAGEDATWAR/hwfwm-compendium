## 1. Summary

Killian Laurent is Cole Silva's right-hand man and the leader of Silva's bronze-rankers. He works with the Builder cult, uses a star seed, flees Greenstone with Silva's wealth and is later captured and chained by Jason's team.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in the lines read.

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Unctuous and without limits**: he ushers in guests (Book 3, Ch16 L43, L70; Book 9, Ch4 L11).
- **Merciless**: he argues that a man who can defy the star seed's power cannot be left alive (Book 3, Ch19 L78).
- **Exploitative**: in Book 8 he is described as dangerous for how he wormed into corners, exploiting and betraying those he works with (Book 8, Ch17 L8, L28).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Not given a rank in the lines read; Laront says his real name isn't Laurent, he "chose that to annoy me" (Book 8, Ch17 L8).
- He uses a star seed for the cult (Book 3, Ch18 L130).

## 5. Background

- "Of the bronze rankers under Silva, Killian was the unquestionable leader" and he runs the secret site (Book 3, Ch17 L13-26).
- He had worked behind the scenes to plunder the Silva family while Jason distracted Silva (Book 9, Ch2 L85).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 2**: Silva calls for him (Book 2, Ch69 L12).
- **Book 3**: waits in Silva's lounge (Book 3, Ch16 L16-43); Jason's team hunts him for days (Book 3, Ch24 L3); he leaves the city on a ship loaded with Silva's holdings (Book 3, Ch25 L4).
- **Book 9**: Havi plans to deliver him but he captures Havi first; Jason's team takes and chains him in a ritual array (Book 9, Ch4 L11-39).
- **Book 10**: Jason can only guess at his fate (Book 10, Ch58 L106).

## 7. Relationships

- **Cole Silva**: employer he betrays (Book 9, Ch2 L85).
- **Jason**: enemy (Book 9, Ch4 L11-39).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

Open question: where he is held after Book 9 and whether he escapes; Jason only guesses he is "in a Magic Society basement somewhere" (Book 10, Ch58 L106).
