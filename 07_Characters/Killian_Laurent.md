## 1. Summary

Cole Silva's right-hand man, the leader of Silva's bronze-rankers, who works with the Builder cult, uses a star seed and flees Greenstone with Silva's wealth.

## 2. Facts

- Silva calls for him in Book 2 (Book 2, Ch69 L12); he waits in Silva's clandestine lounge and ushers in guests (Book 3, Ch16 L16-43).
- "Of the bronze rankers under Silva, Killian was the unquestionable leader" (Book 3, Ch17 L14) and runs the secret site (Book 3, Ch17 L13-26).
- He uses a star seed for the cult (Book 3, Ch18 L130) and argues that a man who can defy that power cannot be left alive (Book 3, Ch19 L78).
- Jason's team spends days hunting him (Book 3, Ch24 L3); he has left the city on a ship loaded with a good part of Silva's holdings (Book 3, Ch25 L4).
- In Book 8 he is described as silver rank but dangerous for how he wormed into corners, exploiting and betraying those he works with (Book 8, Ch17 L8, L28).
- In Book 9 he had worked behind the scenes to plunder the Silva family while Jason distracted Silva (Book 9, Ch2 L85); Havi plans to deliver him on a plate, but Killian captures Havi first (Book 9, Ch4 L11-39). Jason's team then takes Killian and chains him in a ritual array; Jason tells him "I hope we never see each other again" (Book 9, Ch5 L4-99). In Book 10 Jason guesses he is in a Magic Society basement, parcelling out information to stay alive (Book 10, Ch58 L106).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Unctuous and without limits (Book 3, Ch16 L70; Book 9, Ch4 L11).
