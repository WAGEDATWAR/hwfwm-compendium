## 1. Summary

Rick Geller is a Geller cousin and team leader, a big swordsman who is the main rival of Jason's team in Greenstone. He loses his teammate Jonah Geller to a star seed, takes Dustin Kettering on, and becomes a friend and ally of Jason in Rimaros and the later wars.

## 2. Appearance

- "Like his sister, his skin was dark brown, his hair light brown. His build was more like Humphrey, tall and broad-shouldered" (Book 1, Ch66 L58).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Earnest and wide-eyed**: the 'standard Rick' expression is wide-eyed, as Jason thinks of it (Book 9, Ch1 L111).
- **Respectful of rank**: he realises he is speaking to a gold-rank princess and his head dips as if yanked by a string (Book 9, Ch1 L59).
- **Loyal to his team**: he does not like having his team split (Book 9, Ch1 L83), and watches over Jonah in the rescue (Book 2, Ch17 L10).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- A huge sword wielded "like a baton", with an essence ability that "temporarily ramped up his speed and power" (Book 1, Ch67 L99).
- Rank: iron-rank in Book 1 (Book 1, Ch66 L17), a spear-wielder by Book 10 whose team smashes messengers with Stash's help (Book 10, Ch70 L38).
- Hunt style: put an enemy in front of him and nothing at iron rank survives (Book 1, Ch66 L17).

## 5. Background

- Brother of Phoebe Geller; his team includes Jonah, Henry, the Adeah twins and later Dustin Kettering (Book 1, Ch110 L51; Book 2, Ch29 L8; Book 9, Ch104 L54).
- His girlfriend is Hannah Adeah, who slaps the back of his head in Book 10 (Book 10, Ch13 L26).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1-3**: the mirage-arena match with Jason (Book 1, Ch66-67), the survivors of the expedition (Book 1, Ch110 L51), Jonah's death (Book 2, Ch17 L10), the team contest (Book 2, Ch29 L69).
- **Book 7**: he and his team arrive in Rimaros, taken to the throne room by Vesper (Book 7, Ch59 L65-72) and present a recording (Book 7, Ch59 L122).
- **Book 9-11**: he meets Jason in Rimaros (Book 9, Ch1 L59-111), flies over Yaresh after the battle (Book 10, Ch1 L57), fights in Cardinas (Book 10, Ch70 L38) and brings groups to Jason's territory with Sophie (Book 11, Ch21 L104).

## 7. Relationships

- **Dustin**: Rick's team replaced Jonah's role with Dustin, who "was a friend of Neil's" (Book 7, Ch59 L65).
- **Humphrey**: a close match in style (Book 2, Ch30 L51).
- **Jonah**: the teammate he fails to save (Book 2, Ch17 L10).
- **Phoebe**: his sister, on his team (Book 9, Ch104 L54).
