## 1. Summary

A Geller adventurer who demands a rematch with Jason's team and leads his own team of Gellers and elves.

## 2. Facts

- Dark-brown skin and light-brown hair, with a build like Humphrey's, "tall and broad-shouldered"; he leads four others (Book 1, Ch66 L58-62).
- His demand for a rematch is "the drive to improve himself that was common to the Geller clan" (Book 1, Ch66 L61).
- Three of his team of five are Gellers; the other two are twin elf sisters (Book 1, Ch66 L62; Book 9, Ch104 L54).
- He has a wife, Hannah (Book 12, Ch15 L6).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Driven but not proud; a good-natured rival.
