## 1. Summary

Allayeth is one of Yaresh's two diamond-rank elves, the more reclusive of the pair. She is Charist's rival and sometime partner, trains the all-female team Moon's Edge, and is the most powerful person in the room at Jason's Yaresh meetings. She comes to respect Jason's restraint.

## 2. Appearance

- Diamond-rank beauty that she turns aside with her aura; soft green eyes, light-brown skin "of a fawn's fur" and wavy wood-brown hair, "like a dryad of myth" (Book 10, Ch7 L23).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Playful but exact**: she tries to put Jason at ease "with some playful teasing" (Book 10, Ch26 L35).
- **Learns to read power**: she realises that pushing Jason as hard as Charist advocated "could have greater repercussions" (Book 10, Ch6 L17).
- **Unrepentant**: she turns on the dead envoys without guilt (Book 10, Ch45 L82).
- **Understanding of burden**: she nods at Jason's account of power and responsibility (Book 10, Ch25 L122).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Diamond rank, the most powerful at the meeting, in the front row (Book 10, Ch9 L25).
- She moves in a blur to stand before Jason (Book 10, Ch45 L82).
- She works with a silver priestess when they find an incongruity in the ruins (Book 10, Ch31 L49).

## 5. Background

- Resident of Yaresh; "the more reclusive Lady Allayeth" (Book 12, Ch16 L8).
- She raised Team Moon's Edge, the all-female team led by Miriam Vance (Book 12, Ch16 L8; Book 10, Ch59 L4, L17).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 9**: finds Schaat's corpse with Charist and sees a star phoenix (Book 9, Ch98 L4-5).
- **Book 10**: Allayeth and Charist discuss his approach inside a privacy screen (Book 10, Ch23 L7); she explains Gormanston Bynes to Jason (Book 10, Ch14 L3); in the siege she leads Moon's Edge among the gold-rankers (Book 10, Ch59 L4).
- **Book 12**: at a VIP gathering (Book 12, Ch16 L8).

## 7. Relationships

- **Charist**: fellow diamond and rival; he looks like a child caught doing something stupid when they talk (Book 10, Ch23 L7).
- **Jason**: she learns to respect his concessions (Book 10, Ch6 L17).
- **Team Moon's Edge**: trained by her (Book 10, Ch59 L4).

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Cool, exact and quick to smile when she has the upper hand; wary of anything that unsettles her sense of scale. Cue: playful teasing that stops when Jason's aura bears down.
