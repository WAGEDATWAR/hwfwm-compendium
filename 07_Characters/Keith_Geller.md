## 1. Summary

Keith Geller is Danielle Geller's husband and Humphrey and Henrietta's father. The text shows him mostly in groups; he is the quiet, reliable counterpoint to Danielle. (He is not the Network's Keith, who appears with Anna in Book 4.)
