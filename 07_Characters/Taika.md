## 1. Summary

A huge man in Hiro's employ who becomes a core fighter of the Asano clan and a garuda shape-changer.

## 2. Facts

- Introduces himself as "Taika, by the way. Like the director, but I don't make films" (Book 4, Ch4 L27).
- By Book 9 his power set is in line with Humphrey's; they share the might and wing essences, giving Taika a garuda confluence where Humphrey's is dragon (Book 9, Ch33 L15). By the end of the surge he is "on the very cusp of silver, but had not quite made it" (Book 9, Ch45 L10; Book 9, Ch71 L43).
- In Book 12 his bird form carries a flying-saucer vehicle with Anna, Rufus and Gary Sharpton aboard (Book 12, Ch62 L4-9).
- In Book 11 he travels to Earth with Rufus and Boris (Book 11, Ch68 L305).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Large, good-humoured and loyal; a complaint about his mother's opinions on French food is his signature (Book 6, Ch47 L155).
