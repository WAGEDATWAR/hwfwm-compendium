## 1. Summary

Taika is a huge Māori man who works for Hiro Asano, befriends Jason on his return to Earth and becomes one of the Asano clan's core fighters. He rockets up the ranks, takes the garuda confluence and a golden bird form, and travels to Earth with Rufus and Boris in Book 11.

## 2. Appearance

- "The mountainous Māori", comparable in size to a leonid or a draconian (Book 4, Ch17 L42), with tattoos that stand out against his skin when it turns emerald to resist damage (Book 9, Ch93 L27).
- A "massive frame" that does not buckle a reinforced chair when he drops into it (Book 11, Ch52 L29).
- In bird form he is a giant golden bird (Book 10, Ch21 L34).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Easy and cheerful**: "Taika, by the way. Like the director, but I don't make films" (Book 4, Ch4 L27); "the numbers for letters thing is a bit naff, though, right? It's not 2004" (Book 4, Ch16 L44).
- **Companionable**: he sits behind a plastic drum kit while Jason and Emi play a rhythm game (Book 4, Ch44 L3). He tells Jason "I don't normally like watching people's holiday videos, but they were pretty sweet" (Book 7, Ch37 L25).
- **Sharp-eyed**: his "narrowed eyes lingered on Boris" before he got to work (Book 11, Ch68 L580).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Rank: he races up through the proto-spaces and monster waves, "especially with being human as an accelerating factor" (Book 9, Ch33 L14). At the end of the surge his aura is "so close to breaking through to silver that it was like an egg starting to hatch" (Book 9, Ch71 L43, per the Power Sets file).
- A mobility brawler: Farrah teaches him to fight "like Taika", adapting to his new abilities, and his performance helps convince the Network to grant him a place (Book 4, Ch65 L30).
- Garuda confluence and bird form: see Taika_Power_Set.md (Book 9, Ch93; Book 10, Ch21).

## 5. Background

- He is "Taika Williams" (Book 7, Ch44 L61), employed by Hiro in Sydney (Book 4, Ch5 L40) and knows the ropes of the Asano family business.
- When offered the renewal essence and immortal confluence Jason picked out, he chose a different combination after discussion (Book 4, Ch65 L22).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 4**: he phones to say a man claiming to be Hiro's nephew is looking for him (Book 4, Ch4 L17) and drives Jason to a code-locked building, telling him the code so he can come and go (Book 4, Ch5 L40).
- **Book 7**: he and Travis stay with the Gellers in dormitories for young trainees, preferential treatment for being Jason's friends (Book 7, Ch44 L61).
- **Book 9**: his rank-up (Book 9, Ch92 L3), his fight with the messenger Fal (Book 9, Ch93) and his meeting with Haliastur (Book 9, Ch78 L11).
- **Book 10-11**: he flies after Jason and Farrah as a golden bird, meditates atop Onslow's shell and goes to Earth with Rufus and Boris (Book 10, Ch21 L34; Book 10, Ch22 L67; Book 11, Ch68 L305).
- **Book 12**: he and Boris's group go to meet Shiro and the bodyguards (Book 12, Ch59 L35), and his bird form carries a saucer (Book 12, Ch62 L4-9).

## 7. Relationships

- **Hiro Asano**: his boss, who rides in silence with him in Book 4 (Book 4, Ch13 L80).
- **Rufus**: both could benefit from each other's knowledge (Book 9, Ch6 L41); they travel together (Book 11, Ch68 L618).
- **Jason**: "boss" (Book 4, Ch16 L44).
- **Emi**: sits behind a drum kit as she and Jason play a rhythm game (Book 4, Ch44 L3).

None in the lines read.
