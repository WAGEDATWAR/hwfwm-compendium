## 1. Summary

Beaufort is the gold-rank elf who leads the Cult of the Builder's forces in Cardinas, an uneasy ally of the brighthearts and then of Jason's expedition. He gave up his essence powers for a star seed and a war form of metal, and his loyalty to the Builder is tested when Jason removes the star seeds in Book 11. He shares his name with Thadwick Mercer's father (Beaufort Mercer) but is not him (Book 10, Ch72 L41).

## 2. Facts

- **Who he is**: "the head of the Cult of the Builder's forces and their uneasy ally"; the gold-ranker "had given up his essence powers and was some manner of semi-artificial" being (Book 10, Ch65 L30).
- **War form**: his body contains too much metal for his elven frame; when not in war form most of it sits in a dimensional space created by his star seed. He would stay in his fifteen-foot metal skeleton war form if diplomacy allowed (Book 10, Ch69 L3-4). He has two extra arms, longer than his legs, wielding magically enhanced weapons swapped through his internal portal (Book 10, Ch69 L5).
- **His view of himself**: his true form is "so much more perfect than his old elf body, which now served as little more than a disguise" (Book 10, Ch69 L5).
- **Alliance**: "Come now, Lorenn. The choice may be hard, but the calculation is easy"; he urges the brighthearts to abandon the cult's uneasy alliance if they must (Book 10, Ch65 L33). They keep it (Book 10, Ch65 L34).
- **Jason**: he calls Jason "the Defier", "perhaps, the greatest enemy of my Lord Builder on this planet" (Book 10, Ch65 L39).
- **The cult's story**: it invaded Cardinas with no interest in the natural array, fought its way into the local astral space and tried to sever it from the universe for the Builder; the astral space was too unique and they were trapped underground (Book 10, Ch73 L4, L10, L18).
- **In battle**: a trident shot from his arm, trailing a chain, injects a messenger with volatile liquid metal (Book 10, Ch69 L21). He and the cult's gold-rankers burrow into the enemy while silvers are spent where replaceable (Book 10, Ch69 L6).
- **Meeting Jason**: Jason says "I'm going to sneak through the enemy, talk with this Beaufort bloke" (Book 10, Ch68 L93); Beaufort answers "If even our Lord finds dealing with him an issue, what chance does some god playing pretend have?" (Book 10, Ch69 L42).
- **Control of the cultists**: Jason judges that "this Beaufort character seems to have them on a tight leash" (Book 10, Ch70 L6). In the brightheart realm he leads the expedition through the empty heart of the chamber (Book 10, Ch71 L48-60).
- **Allied council**: the cult's leader in the alliance (Book 11, Ch17 L83; Book 11, Ch51 L45), who insists a decision should be "a group decision and not one for you alone" (Book 11, Ch23 L78).
- **Fighting on**: he and Boris are the non-adventurer gold-rankers trapped with the adventurers; "The head Builder cultist was the one that dug their way out", his body "a massive frame of narrow metal and sharp blades, like a man made of scaffolding" (Book 11, Ch54 L5-9).
- **After the extraction**: when Jason removes the star seeds, Beaufort loses his composure ("You did this to us, Asano") while Jason says "I still did this for you" (Book 11, Ch66 L5-17), and Jason presses him about what the cultists want now (Book 11, Ch66 L24-31).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Stern, proud and practical: he treats alliances as arithmetic, resents being pitied and dislikes Jason precisely because Jason keeps being right.
