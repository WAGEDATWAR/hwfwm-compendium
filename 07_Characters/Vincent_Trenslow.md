## 1. Summary

An Adventure Society official with a magnificent moustache who runs Jason's field assessment.

## 2. Facts

- Rufus notes his professional manner (Book 1, Ch44 L23); his moustache "extended past either side of his head" (Book 1, Ch54 L29).
- Hands Jason his badge and explains how stars work: expect to drop a star each time you advance a tier (Book 1, Ch62 L61-93).
- Drinks with Rufus on the houseboat after Farrah's death (Book 3, Ch1 L115).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Strict and fair; he does not flatter.
