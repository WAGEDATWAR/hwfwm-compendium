## 1. Summary

Vincent Trenslow is an Adventure Society official in Greenstone with a magnificent moustache, who runs Jason's field assessment and speaks to him with strict professionalism. By Book 12 he is a drooping senior official preparing for a shipload of gold-rankers.

## 2. Appearance

- A moustache that "extended past either side of his head" (Book 1, Ch54 L29); in Book 12 "everything about Vincent Trenslow was drooping except for his immaculate moustache" (Book 12, Ch28 L3).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Professional**: Rufus notes his manner "had been nothing but professional" (Book 1, Ch44 L23).
- **Barking**: "Mercer, if I see you try to use an ability on a fellow candidate again, you will fail on the spot" (Book 1, Ch54 L30).
- **Disdainful of favouritism**: he shows "a hint of disdain around the eyes" at Guy's division of the group (Book 1, Ch54 L41).
- **Unhappy official**: he explains an administrative error "unhappily" (Book 1, Ch44 L24).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- He uses a silver-grey recording crystal during assessments (Book 1, Ch54 L50-52).
- He identifies thirteen ratlings that would run to their burrows (Book 1, Ch54 L59).

## 5. Background

- Rufus worked closely with him during the last field assessment and called him "exactly what I expect from a Society official" (Book 1, Ch54 L32-33).
- He extends the director's apologies and says she is "new to the role" (Book 1, Ch44 L28).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: explains the assessment procedure (Book 1, Ch44 L24-28; Book 1, Ch54 L35-60); hands Jason his badge and explains stars (Book 1, Ch62 L61-93).
- **Book 2-3**: drinks with Rufus on the houseboat (Book 3, Ch1 L115); at the marina group (Book 3, Ch22 L3); on the terrace with Rufus (Book 2, Ch28 L13).
- **Book 12**: managing requests from nobility before the gold-rankers arrive (Book 12, Ch28 L3).

## 7. Relationships

- **Rufus**: worked together on the assessment (Book 1, Ch54 L32).
- **Elspeth Arella**: his director (Book 1, Ch44 L28).

Nothing hidden in the lines read.
