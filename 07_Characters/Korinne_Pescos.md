## 1. Summary

Korinne Pescos leads a Rimaros guild-elite team, later called Storm Shredder, that fights beside Jason in the surge, travels with him in Book 9 and joins the Cardinas campaign. In Book 11 she survives the Undeath siege dazed and morose after losing a friend.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in detail in the lines read; in Book 11 she has "dead eyes" until she finds Rosa (Book 11, Ch18 L29).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Efficient**: she relays orders swiftly, her efficiency "only enhanced by Jason's communication power" (Book 7, Ch48 L16).
- **Loyal**: "he's our asshole, and we don't turn on our own assholes" (Book 7, Ch50 L51).
- **Peacemaker**: "Let it go, Kalif... You don't want to make an enemy of the next Amos Pensinata" (Book 7, Ch47 L59).
- **Guarded then curious**: she visits Jason's cabin guarded about his methods and takes a slice of baked goods thoughtfully (Book 9, Ch25 L31, L89).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Leader of a silver-rank guild team of six; they were star performers, clearing enemies faster than the other teams, with two high-damage members and the rest built around them (Book 9, Ch34 L74).
- Her team "operated alone through most of the surge" (Book 9, Ch25 L31-63).
- She feels Jason's aura as "the cold anger of an icy hell" (Book 9, Ch39 L87).

## 5. Background

- Leader of Orin and Kalif's team, who coordinated the underwater complex rescue (Book 9, Ch24 L49-52).
- Her team eschewed the yacht for their own vehicle during travel (Book 9, Ch29 L14).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 7**: the mixed-force expedition (Book 7, Ch47 L54-59; Book 7, Ch48 L16; Book 7, Ch50 L51).
- **Book 8**: one of Shade's four intact bodies stays with her at the mining facility (Book 8, Ch42 L45).
- **Book 9**: asks Jason to train the team (Book 9, Ch25); travels in convoy (Book 9, Ch29 L14); Zara joins the team vehicle (Book 9, Ch41 L119).
- **Book 10**: "Storm Shredder's leader" at the breach (Book 10, Ch90 L51); in Jason's group meeting (Book 10, Ch9 L13).
- **Book 11**: stalked by a priest, finds Jetta dead and meets Rosa (Book 11, Ch6 L13-20; Book 11, Ch18 L29).

## 7. Relationships

- **Rosa, Orin, Kalif, Zara**: team members (Book 11, Ch32 L74; Book 7, Ch47 L59).
- **Jetta**: a friend, dead as a puppet (Book 11, Ch6 L19).
- **Jason**: she guesses he will lecture on Rimaros-style versus Vitesse-style adventuring (Book 9, Ch25 L58).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

In Book 11 she scrambles between rocks in a desert canyon while a priest taunts her (Book 11, Ch6 L13-16).

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Brisk, proud of her team's training; she softens only when someone earns it. Cue: "He's our asshole."
