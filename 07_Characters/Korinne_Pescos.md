## 1. Summary

A guild-elite team leader who first meets Jason in the Rimaros surge and later asks him to train her team.

## 2. Facts

- Leader of the guild team in the mixed-force expedition: "The leader of the guild team, Korinne, moved to them" (Book 7, Ch47 L54). She tells her angry team member Kalif he does not want to make an enemy of "the next Amos Pensinata" (Book 7, Ch47 L59).
- She relays orders efficiently once her training kicks in, with no sign of the unruliness she had shown earlier (Book 7, Ch48 L16).
- She defends Jason to others: "he's our asshole, and we don't turn on our own assholes" (Book 7, Ch50 L51).
- Full name Korinne Pescos, leader of Orin and Kalif's team, who had coordinated the underwater complex rescue (Book 9, Ch24 L49-52).
- In Book 9 she visits Jason's cabin and is guarded about his methods; her team are silver-rank guild elites who "operated alone through most of the surge" (Book 9, Ch25 L31-63).
- She guesses Jason will talk about Rimaros-style versus Vitesse-style adventuring, specialisation versus generalisation (Book 9, Ch25 L58).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Brisk and proud of her team's training; she softens only when someone earns it.
