## 1. Summary

Kaito's wife and Jason's childhood best friend from next door.

## 2. Facts

- The girl next door and Jason's best friend as far back as he remembers; she had a crush on Kaito from an early age (Book 4, Ch26 L47).
- Her parents retired to Tasmania and sold her the house next door (Book 4, Ch3 L133).
- Her daughters with Kaito are Hana and Jace; in 2038 Hana is twenty-three and Jace nineteen (Book 13, Ch950 L29).
- Amy is the village's "de facto mayor" (Book 5, Ch44 L3) and acts as mayor in the Book 5 evacuation (Book 5, Ch11 L82); the notes add that she is mayor of the Casselton Regional Council and Jason's former girlfriend (notes, Book 4).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Steady and perceptive; she watches Jason's expression before she speaks.
