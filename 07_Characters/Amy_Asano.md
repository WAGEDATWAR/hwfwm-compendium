## 1. Summary

Amy Asano is Kaito's wife and Jason's childhood best friend from next door. She and Jason had a fraught romance that ended badly; in Book 4 she meets the changed Jason, in Book 5 she acts as village mayor, and in Book 13 she is a protective widow with two daughters.

## 2. Appearance

- Wavy brown hair and fair skin, "pretty, but only by Earth standards" compared with the other world's beauties (Book 4, Ch26 L46).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Steady and perceptive**: her face is stricken as she catalogues the changes from the boy she remembered (Book 4, Ch27 L74).
- **Protective**: she stands ahead of her daughters at the Book 13 meeting (Book 13, Ch950 L28).
- **Wary of Jason emotionally, not physically**: she knows he is an emotional threat to her children (Book 13, Ch950 L28).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Amy has seen essences before; Book 4 marks her first time holding one (Book 4, Ch72 L25).
- She is the village's "de facto mayor" (Book 5, Ch44 L3) and acts as mayor in the Book 5 evacuation (Book 5, Ch11 L82).

## 5. Background

- The girl next door and Jason's best friend; she had a crush on Kaito from early on (Book 4, Ch26 L47).
- Her parents retired to Tasmania and sold her the house next door (Book 4, Ch3 L133).
- The notes add that she is mayor of the Casselton Regional Council (notes, Book 4).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 4**: the family party (Book 4, Ch26 L45-48); reunion (Book 4, Ch27 L74); marina arrival (Book 4, Ch55 L58); essence holding (Book 4, Ch72 L25).
- **Book 5**: village leader (Book 5, Ch11 L82; Book 5, Ch44 L3).
- **Book 13**: floats up with Emi and her daughters (Book 13, Ch950 L27-30).

## 7. Relationships

- **Kaito**: husband; widowed in Book 5 (Book 13, Ch950 L27).
- **Hana and Jace**: daughters (Book 13, Ch950 L29).
- **Jason**: history "like London fog" (Book 13, Ch950 L30).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

Her daughters grow up with "a dead father" and a mysterious absent uncle (Book 13, Ch950 L30).
