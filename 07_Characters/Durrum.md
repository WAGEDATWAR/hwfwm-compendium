## 1. Summary

Durrum is a massive earth-aspect brightheart, one of two leaders who claim territory in Jason's unified battlefield. Conservative and plain-spoken, he leads his followers from the front, chooses to trust Neil's plan, and ends up the leader of the brightheart territories after Lorus is ruled out.

## 2. Facts

- **Leader from the front**: his messengers "had inherited the toughness of the massive brightheart Durrum, who led them from the front"; Lorus pushes aggressively while Durrum is "more conservative" (Book 11, Ch10 L8).
- **Origins of his territory**: he and Lorus arrived after claiming other territories and looking to expand; their standoff lasted so long that the Undeath priests and the Builder cult forced the issue (Book 11, Ch10 L9).
- **Anti-cult but practical**: he "hated the Builder cult as much as every other brightheart, yet had been willing to abide by their alliance" and tries to reconcile the cult and the brighthearts (Book 11, Ch10 L10-13).
- **In battle**: his earth powers leave him unobstructed by mud, strong enough to hurl enemy messengers and undead aside as he fights his way to Lorus (Book 11, Ch10 L14).
- **Confronting Lorus**: "LORUS! Are you betraying us?" and "Every second we spend arguing or fighting the cultists takes us closer to defeat. You're killing us" (Book 11, Ch10 L16-20). He warns Lorus not to give a lightning-wielding Neil a reason to make him the demonstration (Book 11, Ch10 L46).
- **Wall and talks**: his protective wall, with Dustin's, keeps the cultists from attacking the brighthearts on sight so that all sides agree to talk (Book 11, Ch11 L6).
- **Calling out Lorus**: "Because you hid yours behind mine and let them do the dying" (Book 11, Ch11 L35); he tells Lorus to let "the aboveworlder finish" (Book 11, Ch11 L62).
- **Terms**: if Neil can bring lightning control into each new territory he will agree to Neil's leadership (Book 11, Ch11 L44), and he asks him to share how the lightning is controlled (Book 11, Ch11 L57).
- **Trust**: "I am willing to trust in our people. In the strength we have, not that of the monsters we've shackled" (Book 11, Ch11 L81); he accepts Lorus's territory (Book 11, Ch11 L86). The cult says it will follow Durrum (Book 11, Ch11 L79).
- **Relief**: after he claims the territory he first feels power, then relief when it is gone (Book 11, Ch12 L6).
- **The rabbit**: he looms over Nik and challenges his claim to know how things are done where he comes from (Book 11, Ch12 L74-84). He says "Asano took what was left of my people and gave them a haven... I would never do anything to offend him" (Book 11, Ch12 L106).
- **Ambush**: he and another earth brightheart, Kurik, hide Neil, Dustin and elite brighthearts underground to spring a trap on the priests (Book 11, Ch18 L52).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Heavy, slow to anger and slower to forgive; he says what he sees and keeps his word. His rumbling voice, "a sound like gravel being crushed", is the signature (Book 11, Ch12 L65).
