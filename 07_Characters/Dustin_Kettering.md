## 1. Summary

An ice-armoured defender from the Kettering family who starts as one of Thadwick Mercer's offsiders, joins Rick's team in Book 2 and later fights beside Neil in Cardinas.

## 2. Facts

- One of Thadwick Mercer's two offsiders with Neil: he protects the group with his powers while Neil heals them (Book 1, Ch110 L17).
- The Davone and Kettering families have close ties to the Mercers, which is how the boys came to Thadwick's side (Book 2, Ch27 L41). He and Neil are relieved to be free, having been known as Thadwick's flunkeys (Book 2, Ch27 L57).
- A local who fills Jonah Geller's defender role on Rick's team (Book 2, Ch29 L8): "a classic defender, not very mobile but very hard to go around or through" (Book 2, Ch29 L9), clad in armour forged entirely of ice (Book 2, Ch29 L59, L69).
- His cousin Hudson Kettering is his counterpart on Beth Cavendish's team (Book 2, Ch29 L9; Book 2, Ch15 L44).
- In Book 7 he is a friend of Neil's from their time on Thadwick's team (Book 7, Ch59 L65); by Book 9 he is the only non-local on Rick's team, picked up after Jonah's death (Book 9, Ch104 L54).
- In Book 11 he is dropped into the same territory as Neil, gives the party a frontline and healer pairing and helps hold a protective wall between brighthearts and Builder cultists (Book 11, Ch6 L64; Book 11, Ch11 L6, L43-49).

## 3. Timeline of involvement

- Book 1-2: Thadwick's team, then Rick's (Book 1, Ch110 L17; Book 2, Ch29 L8).
- Book 7: arrives with Rick's group in Rimaros (Book 7, Ch59 L65).
- Book 10-11: fights messengers on Rick's team, where Stash smashes him and Rick together (Book 10, Ch70 L38); in Book 11 he and Neil claim a territory as a defender-healer pair (Book 11, Ch8 L23).

## 4. Roleplay notes

Dry and steady, a shield for others; he points out hard truths about the weight of numbers (Book 11, Ch11 L49).
