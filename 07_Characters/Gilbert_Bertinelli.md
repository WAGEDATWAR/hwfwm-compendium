## 1. Summary

A Greenstone tailor who sells Jason his combat robe and later suits.

## 2. Facts

- Runs "Gilbert's Resilient Attire For the Discerning Gentleman" and invites customers to call him Bert; one of octuplets named after Berts, including the "handsome" Bertrand (Book 1, Ch61 L76-87).
- Sells Jason the trap-weaver-silk and leather combat attire, haggled by Gary and priced in bronze coins, which is unusual for iron-rank gear (Book 1, Ch61 L92-114).
- Makes Jason a tuxedo (Book 1, Ch65 L6) and refers him to his brother for specialised desert boots (Book 1, Ch76 L3-5).
- In Book 3 he has reached iron rank late, having kept all his cloth essence abilities at bronze (Book 3, Ch2 L59-62), and shows Jason a winter wardrobe "largely in the Vitesse style" (Book 3, Ch2 L64).
- Jason's combat robe is "custom made for him by Gilbert Bertinelli" (Book 5, Ch30 L58); Gilbert supplied his armour (Book 2, Ch44 L8).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Courtly, theatrical and sharp about quality; he does not hide his rivalry with his brothers.
