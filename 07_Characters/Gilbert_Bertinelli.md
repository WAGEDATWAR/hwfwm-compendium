## 1. Summary

Gilbert "Bert" Bertinelli is a Greenstone tailor who sells Jason his combat robe and later his suits. Courtly, theatrical and sharp about quality, he does not hide his rivalry with his brothers.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in the lines read.

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Theatrical**: he runs "Gilbert's Resilient Attire For the Discerning Gentleman" and invites customers to call him Bert (Book 1, Ch61 L76-87).
- **Competitive with his brothers**: one of octuplets named after Berts, including the "handsome" Bertrand (Book 1, Ch61 L76-87).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Cloth essence abilities at bronze; he reached iron rank late (Book 3, Ch2 L59-62).
- He makes the trap-weaver-silk and leather combat attire haggled by Gary and priced in bronze coins, unusual for iron-rank gear (Book 1, Ch61 L92-114).

## 5. Background

- Jason's combat robe is "custom made for him by Gilbert Bertinelli" (Book 5, Ch30 L58); Gilbert supplied his armour (Book 2, Ch44 L8).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: tuxedo (Book 1, Ch65 L6); refers Jason to his brother for desert boots (Book 1, Ch76 L3-5).
- **Book 3**: shows a winter wardrobe "largely in the Vitesse style" (Book 3, Ch2 L64).

## 7. Relationships

- **Jason**: client (Book 1, Ch61 L92).
- **Gary**: haggles for Jason (Book 1, Ch61 L92-114).
- **His brothers**: he is "loath" to refer Jason to his brother (Book 1, Ch76 L3-5).
