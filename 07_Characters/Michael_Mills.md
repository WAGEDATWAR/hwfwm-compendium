## 1. Summary

General Michael Mills of the United States Army is the operational commander of the EDJI's largest military action to date, the war against the vampires in Book 13. A career soldier who rebuilt infantry doctrine for the magical age, he is trusted to handle Jason, and is chosen as spokesperson for the EDJI member states.

## 2. Facts

- **Past posting**: he was stationed at Saint-Étienne "two decades and several ranks ago", before the Asano Clan took over (Book 13, Ch1001 L3).
- **Seen the horrors**: he has seen "with his own eyes the power of the vampire lords and the land of horrors they had made" and believes the stories of Asano reclaiming his territory (Book 13, Ch1001 L4).
- **The EDJI**: he had never thought it would happen and was there for the negotiations when China and Russia demanded more influence; the US almost derailed it over the spelling of "defense" (Book 13, Ch1001 L6). He does not approve of the troops calling it "edgy" (Book 13, Ch1001 L7).
- **Doctrine**: he commanded the Infantry Reimplementation Program, redefining infantry doctrine as augmented soldiers with magitech weapons returned infantry to the forefront (Book 13, Ch1001 L91-94).
- **Command**: with his doctrine knowledge and experience fighting vampires, he is placed "in operational command of the EDJI's largest military action to date" (Book 13, Ch1001 L96).
- **Observations of the base**: he is met by a saluting EDJI lieutenant in a US Army-marked uniform (Book 13, Ch1001 L97); the tarmac holds augmented infantry and Cabal contingents (Book 13, Ch1001 L98), and he finds a "wild amount of diversity" in Asano Clan territory (Book 13, Ch1001 L99).
- **Hopes and fears**: he hopes the unity will outlast the common enemy, and worries what awaits when the threat is dealt with (Book 13, Ch1001 L101).
- **Politics**: he has no Network origins and has navigated Network and anti-Network factions his whole career (Book 13, Ch1001 L119).
- **Meeting Jason**: he has met Jason several times, mostly in passing at political events (Book 13, Ch1001 L91). In a meeting with Yumi he says "It was always my intention to pit you against McKean" (Book 13, Ch1002 L42).
- **Candour**: "Mr Asano, it's been many years since you first came back to Earth. Yes, a lot of people made less than ideal choices in those days" (Book 13, Ch1002 L49). He asks why Jason has spent a decade forging humanity's military into a single sword rather than going home (Book 13, Ch1002 L55), and why he has not told anyone about the specific threat (Book 13, Ch1002 L65).
- **Spokesperson**: he is chosen as the spokesperson for the EDJI member states, "known as someone who could handle Jason without triggering some meeting-ending debacle" (Book 13, Ch1020 L91), and enters in an informal EDJI service dress uniform with a briefcase (Book 13, Ch1020 L85).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Courteous, dry and watchful: a soldier who prefers a plan to a speech, and honest enough to name his worries to the man who frightens him.
