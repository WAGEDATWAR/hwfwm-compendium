## 1. Summary

Hess Jor Nasala is a silver-rank messenger, dark-skinned and silver-haired, who serves as Jes Fin Kaal's chief assistant and mouthpiece. Deferential and careful, he relays the messengers' objections and counsels caution.

## 2. Appearance

- "Dark-skinned and silver-haired" (Book 9, Ch66 L3).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Cautious**: "We cannot base our activities on doubts and assumptions" (Book 9, Ch56 L101).
- **Discreet**: he does not respond, knowing better than to talk for the sake of it (Book 9, Ch66 L41).
- **Honest about danger**: he admits he would not fight Jason (Book 10, Ch16 L26-53).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts
