## 1. Summary

A silver-rank messenger, dark-skinned and silver-haired, who serves as Jes Fin Kaal's chief assistant and mouthpiece.

## 2. Facts

- Kaal thanks him in front of Fal Vin Garath, and he sits beside them (Book 9, Ch56 L40, L75).
- He is described as "dark-skinned and silver-haired", subordinate to Kaal, who is gold rank and a Voice of the Will (Book 9, Ch66 L3).
- He advises caution ("We cannot base our activities on doubts and assumptions", Book 9, Ch56 L101) and names the Unorthodoxy when Kaal raises a problem (Book 9, Ch66 L39).
- In Book 10 he is Kaal's chief assistant and mouthpiece and relays the messengers' objections; he admits he would not fight Jason (Book 10, Ch16 L26-53).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Deferential and careful. He does not respond, knowing better than to talk for the sake of it (Book 9, Ch66 L41).
