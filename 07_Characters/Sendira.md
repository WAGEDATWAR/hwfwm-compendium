## 1. Summary

Sendira is Melody Jain's second in command in the Order of Redeeming Light's local enclave: a silver-rank zealot and the person Melody trusts most. She commands the Purity forces on the island in Book 8, carries the aura beacon and the clockwork kings, and holds the order's discipline together while suspecting others.

## 2. Facts

- **Rank in the order**: Melody leads the enclave and Sendira, her second, reports on what the scouts have found (Book 7, Ch58 L38). The overall leadership is the same silver rank as the individual cell leaders, so there is "a constant tension" (Book 8, Ch9 L17).
- **Reliability**: she is "ever-reliable"; like another cell leader, Kelleigh, she is not ambitious, which places her as Melody's second (Book 8, Ch33 L16).
- **Doctrine**: she tells the converted, "Only Purity is worthy of our devotion, not his servants... We are all unworthy, seeking to cleanse the taint that lingers within us" (Book 8, Ch9 L21), and "Distraction and frivolity taint pure dedication of purpose" (Book 8, Ch9 L14).
- **Command on the island**: she is "the commander of the Purity forces on the island", separating from most of her group to take a smaller team underground (Book 8, Ch13 L3).
- **Control of subordinates**: she wants Fila and Ramona "under her thumb instead of causing trouble for Melody's plans" (Book 8, Ch13 L6). She snaps at Ramona that "What Melody wants with the target is no concern of yours. Your place is to obey, not criticise in ignorance" (Book 8, Ch13 L54).
- **The beacon**: when the grab team seizes Melody's daughter, "Sendira couldn't activate the beacon fast enough" (Book 8, Ch13 L51). She launches a flare that scatters motes of light (Book 8, Ch14 L59), and the beacon's pervasive aura drenches the island until it is cut off, at which she shows "an expression of uncertainty" (Book 8, Ch14 L70-72).
- **Clockwork kings**: when the Builder cult delivered the first clockwork king, it came with a control device; she explains that the order purified both (Book 8, Ch14 L67). She orders the kings through a portal after taking out a silver pyramid (Book 8, Ch16 L85-92).
- **Doubt among her people**: Jaime says "Sendira is using us as decoys" (Book 8, Ch16 L29).
- **Assessment**: "The likelihood of their having realised our objective is high" (Book 8, Ch16 L82).
- **At the leaders' meeting**: she stands next to Melody as hostile cell leaders glare (Book 8, Ch17 L55); angry at a remark, she is silenced by Melody's gesture (Book 8, Ch18 L8). Melody asks whether she has been telling people they were going after Melody's daughter (Book 8, Ch18 L80).
- **Search**: she crouches with the others to feel faint vibrations through the floor (Book 8, Ch33 L22).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Rigid, loyal and humourless: she speaks in doctrine, obeys Melody absolutely and treats questions as faults. When her plan fails she falls quiet rather than doubting.
