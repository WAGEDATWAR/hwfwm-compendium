## 1. Summary

Gwydion Remore is Rufus's older brother and a priest of Hero. He is the family's softer edge: easygoing, card-playing and quietly devout.

## 2. Appearance

- Skin of dark chocolate and "a thornbush of curly hair" (Book 11, Ch60 L13).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Easygoing**: he brings Danielle a fresh drink at her table (Book 11, Ch60 L13).
- **Curious**: "I'm looking forward to meeting this Asano" (Book 11, Ch60 L30).
- **Family man**: he worries about his family underground during the Cardinas expedition (Book 11, Ch60 L22).
- **Sheepish before his mother**: Arabelle watches him with raised eyebrows (Book 11, Ch67 L60).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts
