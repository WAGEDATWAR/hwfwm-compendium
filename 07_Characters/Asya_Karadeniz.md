## 1. Summary

A school friend of Jason's and Network administrator who crews Kaito's helicopter and is killed in Book 5.

## 2. Facts

- She says she went to school with Jason Asano and is "just an administrator" who keeps the people doing the work "happy and funded" (Book 4, Ch36 L37-41).
- She is on Kaito's three-person helicopter crew with Greg (Book 5, Ch7 L12), operating the weapons when a black winged lizard comes into her sights (Book 5, Ch7 L21-23).
- Her mother Rabia represents the non-Asano families on the Asano Village committee (Book 5, Ch13 L74-75).
- She enters Jason's spirit vault "as her relationship with Jason deepened" (Book 5, Ch44 L45).
- Anna says of the Berlin deaths: "The bastards killed Asya" (Book 5, Ch51 L52); Akari says Kaito, Asya and Greg died fighting for their world (Book 5, Ch51 L40).
- Her parents and siblings, the Karadeniz family, confront Jason; her mother slaps him (Book 5, Ch56 L24-26).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Sharp, funny and fiercely loyal; her death is a wound the others carry into Book 5.
