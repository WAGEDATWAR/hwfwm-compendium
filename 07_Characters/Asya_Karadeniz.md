## 1. Summary

Asya Karadeniz is a school friend of Jason's from the Mid North Coast who works in Network magitech administration. She joins Jason's side in Book 4, crews Kaito's helicopter with Greg, enters Jason's spirit vault as their relationship deepens, and dies in the Berlin attack in Book 5. Her family stays in the Asano clan afterwards.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in the lines read.

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Dry about herself**: "I was always fancy" (Book 4, Ch36 L37).
- **Modest about her job**: "I'm just an administrator there... My job is to keep the people doing the actual work happy and funded" (Book 4, Ch36 L39).
- **Deliberate in contact**: she asked for the Sydney slot because she went to school with Jason (Book 4, Ch36 L41).
- **Shaken by the gap between boy and man**: the recording of Jason's power unnerved her, set against "the boy she had known" with his "sexy, impish grin" (Book 4, Ch43 L24-25).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- An executive-level Network administrator given an essence to reach category one, without monster cores (Book 4, Ch36 L77).
- She operates the helicopter's weapons when a winged lizard enters her sights (Book 5, Ch7 L21-23).

## 5. Background

- Went to school with Jason and Greg (Book 4, Ch36 L41; Book 4, Ch66 L59).
- Her mother Rabia sits on the Asano Village committee for the non-Asano families and helps introduce the village to magic (Book 5, Ch13 L75).
- Her cousin is Lina Karadeniz, who is also Emi's friend (Book 11, Ch68 L828).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 4**: she arrives with Anna and speaks with Jason's circle (Book 4, Ch36 L37-41); follows Hector at the cloud lodge (Book 4, Ch52 L83); visits the Network's local headquarters with Jason, Farrah and Jason's sister (Book 4, Ch59 L7).
- **Book 5**: on the plane and in the helicopter crew with Greg (Book 5, Ch7 L12; Book 5, Ch25 L54); sits beside Jason on the motorboat (Book 5, Ch39 L110); the core of the new combat team with Greg and Kaito (Book 5, Ch44 L17); dies in Berlin (Book 5, Ch51 L40-52).
- **After**: her family confronts Jason and her mother slaps him (Book 5, Ch56 L24-26); the Karadeniz and Greg's families are brought into the Asano clan (Book 11, Ch68 L632).

## 7. Relationships

- **Jason**: old school friend; she enters his spirit vault "as her relationship with Jason deepened" (Book 5, Ch44 L45).
- **Greg and Kaito**: teammates (Book 5, Ch44 L17).
- **Anna**: "The bastards killed Asya" (Book 5, Ch51 L52).
- **Rabia and the Karadeniz family**: mother and relatives (Book 5, Ch13 L75; Book 5, Ch56 L24-26).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

Her death in Berlin (Book 5, Ch51 L40-52) is a loss the Karadeniz family blames on Jason and is later part of the clan tension around Emi and Lina (Book 11, Ch68 L831).

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Brisk, wry and unafraid of Jason's reputation; modest about her desk job. Cue: "I'm just an administrator there."
