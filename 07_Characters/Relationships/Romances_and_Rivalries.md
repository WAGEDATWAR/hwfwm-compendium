## 1. Summary

This file lists romantic ties and standing rivalries between named characters, in the order a reader meets them. Romances are given as pairs with the book they begin, change or end; rivalries are given with what each side wants. It is level 3 because it includes outcomes up to Book 13.

## 2. Romances and marriages

### Jason

- **Amy**: the girl next door and his best friend; she "had a crush on Kaito from an early age" and later returned Jason's feelings, but the fallout when she and Kaito got together ended it (Book 4, Ch26 L47-50). In Book 13 the history "hung in the air like London fog" (Book 13, Ch950 L30).
- **Cassandra Mercer**: they kiss in Book 1 (Book 1, Ch98 L35). Her family ends it because they decided he "wasn't going to amount to anything" (Book 11, Ch68 L1104). In Book 12 she finds him "different, yet the same", his features "sharper and less boyish" (Book 12, Ch46 L20).
- **Asya Karadeniz**: she enters his spirit vault "as her relationship with Jason deepened" (Book 5, Ch44 L45); she dies in Book 5 (Book 5, Ch51 L40).
- **Zara Rimaros**: the two are walking the promenade deck hand in hand in Book 13 (Book 13, Ch956 L3) and she kisses him later (Book 13, Ch1004 L44-52).

### Others

- **Kaito and Amy**: married, with two daughters; Kaito dies in Book 5 (Book 4, Ch3 L133; Book 13, Ch950 L29).
- **Erika and Ian Evans**: married, with a daughter, Emi (Book 4, Ch3 L132).
- **Gabriel and Arabelle Remore**: married, with two sons (Book 3, Ch1 L73).
- **Danielle and Keith Geller**: married, with two children (Book 2, Ch62 L24).
- **Thalia and Beaufort Mercer**: married (Book 1, Ch77 L47).
- **Liara Rimaros and Baseph Amouz**: married for political reasons in youth, living largely separate lives but staying friends (Book 7, Ch17 L19-20).
- **Humphrey and Gabrielle Pellin**: they were a couple; he ended it (Book 2, Ch28 L25-30). Their break-up is partly blamed on her friction with Jason (Book 10, Ch8 L10). Gabrielle later marries **Travis Noble** (Book 13, Ch1016 L74).
- **Rick Geller and Hannah Adeah**: girlfriend, fiancée, then wife (Book 10, Ch1 L77-80; Book 12, Ch15 L6).
- **Valdis Volaire and Sigrid Freyn**: husband and wife (Book 12, Ch15 L8).
- **Emir Bahadir and Constance**: employer and chief of staff for years, then married (Book 6, Ch47 L494, L539-542; Book 12, Ch51 L49).
- **Belinda Callahan and Estella Warnock**: married by Book 13 (Book 13, Ch1011 L23).
- **Akari Asano and Itsuki Tiwari**: married; he leaves her in Book 13 so she can focus on the sword, saying "Your life doesn't have room for me anymore" (Book 13, Ch1004 L5-23).
- **Emi Evans-Asano and Vincent Baudrillard**: lovers in 2038; Jason recognises her as a grown woman who does not need his protection (Book 13, Ch971 L49).
- **Rufus Remore and Hassan Dukhtar**: Rufus introduces him as someone he is dating (Book 13, Ch1016 L23-27).
- **Lorelei Grantham and Clive Standish**: partners in Book 13 (Book 13, Ch992 L122-124).

## 3. Rivalries and standing hostilities

- **Jason and Thadwick Mercer**: Thadwick "was always fixated on me" (Book 9, Ch26 L59), partly after Jason dated Thadwick's sister (Book 4, Ch68 L177).
- **Jason's team and Team Shining Scabbard**: a friendly rivalry through the mock battles; the team is built around Sigrid with Valdis as its political core (Book 3, Ch3 L76-81).
- **Padma's team leader and Valdis**: both sword specialists with almost identical essence combinations but very different swordsmen (Book 3, Ch1 L89).
- **Rick's team and Beth's team**: they fight in the Fallen Echoes contest, where Beth is struck down by the thorn fountain (Book 2, Ch29 L74; Book 2, Ch30 L80).
- **Cole Silva and Clarissa Ventress**: Clarissa uses Sophie to provoke the rash Silva (Book 1, Ch50 L92-96).
- **Elspeth Arella and Jason**: wary opposites from Book 1, easing into polite acquaintance by Book 12 (Book 1, Ch106 L82; Book 12, Ch29 L32).
- **Kalif and Jason**: Kalif provokes Jason on first meeting and comes to work with him (Book 9, Ch24 L50-52).
- **Allayeth and Charist**: partners with different approaches to Jason (Book 10, Ch6 L17; Book 10, Ch23 L7).
- **Gilbert Bertinelli and his brothers**: he is loath to refer Jason to one of them (Book 1, Ch76 L3-5).
- **Gabrielle and Jason**: mutual friction (Book 10, Ch8 L10).
