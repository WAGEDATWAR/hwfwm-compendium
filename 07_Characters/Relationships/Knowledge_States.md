## 1. Summary

This file tracks four things a roleplayer needs to keep straight for the core characters: whether the character knows that Jason is an outworlder from Earth, how much they know about the party interface and the later System, what they know about the Earth-Pallimustus link and Earth's magic, and what they know of Knowledge's plan. For each, it names the earliest scene where the text shows the character knowing, and the band of books that scene falls in. It is the character-by-character companion to the secrets table in Spoiler_Management.md.

## 2. Method and limits

- **First evidence, not full proof.** An entry says "Book N" when a cited line shows the character with the knowledge. The character may have known earlier in a scene the compendium has not read; a blank means no scene was found.
- **Bands.** A = Books 1-3; B = Books 4-6; C = Books 7-9; D = Books 10-13. A character shown as knowing in band A is assumed to know in every later band unless the text says otherwise.
- **Belief versus fact.** The table records what the character is shown to know or be told, not whether it is true.
- **Level 3.** The file is level 3 throughout because it names Book 13 reveals.

## 4. Topic 2: The party interface and the System

The party interface is an outworlder ability Jason evolves in Book 1 (Book 1, Ch87 L157-163); it gives party members chat and voice chat (Book 1, Ch89 L76). No first-use scene was found for Rufus, Farrah, Gary or Taika. The later, world-wide System is a separate matter and is covered below the table.

The System:

- **Appearance in the world.** At the end of Book 11, the System message "Welcome to the System" is shown (Book 11, Ch68 L2540). By Book 12, the world's people know the interface calling itself the System appeared and the Adventure Society linked it to Jason (Book 12, Ch1 L44-54).
- **Who knew Jason held it.** Anna Tilden suspects the System might be some cosmic force rather than Jason (Book 12, Ch42 L99); Jason is shown receiving the System administrator gift (Book 12, Ch10 L14-17).
- **Hazy memories.** The Book 12 characters say things were hazy when the System first appeared (Book 12, Ch2 L110).

## 7. Using the table

- **Rule of thumb.** Until a scene shows a character being told, do not let them speak with the knowledge. Use the band column as the earliest safe point.
- **Jason's own state.** Jason's own understanding of the plan sharpens across Books 8-13: by Book 8 he can summarise the Builder and outworlder story (Book 8, Ch57 L53-59), and by Book 13 he names Knowledge's role (Book 13, Ch1017 L31-32).
- **Open points.** First-telling scenes for Sophie, Belinda, Neil, Gary and the team's grasp of the Earth link are not found in the lines read. Add them here if a later pass finds them.
