## 1. Summary

This file gathers who is related to whom in the six family lines roleplay needs most: the Asano family (with the Evans line through Erika), the Remores, the Gellers, the Mercers and their circle, and the Rimaros royal house. It adds nothing that the character files do not already cite; its job is to put the links in one place. The file is level 3 throughout because it names deaths and Book 13 outcomes.

## 2. How to read the trees

- A line like "A → B" means A is a parent of B as the cited chapter says.
- "Spouse" and "partner" are given only where the text says so.
- Where the text is silent or two sources disagree, the item is listed under "Open points" at the end of that section.

## 3. The Asano family (Jason's line)

### Grandparents and parents

- **Yumi Asano**, Jason's paternal grandmother, whose flesh-warping powers restored her youth; she looks no older than her mid-twenties (Book 4, Ch74 L40; Book 11, Ch68 L565). In Book 13 she sits as matriarch of the clan (Book 13, Ch1002 L23).
- Jason's **other grandmother**, his mother's mother, is seen "chugging a beer" beside Yumi (Book 4, Ch74 L43) and had a miraculous recovery that sets his mother's thoughts racing (Book 4, Ch35 L52).
- **Ken Asano**, Jason's father, was born in Japan and raised in Australia from a very young age (Book 1, Ch1 L88). His brothers are the elder **Shiro**, "the favourite", and **Hiro**, "the disappointment"; Ken "laid low in the middle" (Book 4, Ch7 L10).
- **Cheryl Asano**, Jason's mother, comes from a farming and mining family and was the one fascinated with Japan (Book 1, Ch1 L88). Her brother is **Robert** (Book 4, Ch31 L3).

### Jason and his siblings

- **Kaito** is Jason's older brother by a year (Book 2, Ch11 L65); **Erika** is the older sister (Book 5, Ch56 L7). Jason is the youngest of the three.
- Kaito married **Amy**, the girl next door, who is Jason's childhood best friend (Book 4, Ch26 L47; Book 4, Ch3 L133). Their daughters are **Hana** (twenty-three in 2038) and **Jace** (nineteen) (Book 13, Ch950 L29). Kaito dies in Book 5 (Book 5, Ch52 L20).
- Erika married **Ian Evans**, a doctor (Book 4, Ch3 L123); their daughter is **Emi** (Book 4, Ch3 L132), twelve in Book 4 (Book 4, Ch25 L60).

### The extended line

- Emi's cousins are Hana and Jace, and her great-grandmother Yumi is "Nana Yumi" to her (Book 4, Ch56 L46; Book 11, Ch68 L836-837).
- **Nik**, the rabbit Jason brought into being when his soul realm became a transformation zone, calls Jason "Dad" (Book 11, Ch61 L16; Book 12, Ch39 L15).
- Families brought into the clan through Jason's circle include those of Greg and Asya (Book 11, Ch68 L632) and Chloe Baudrillard's (Book 11, Ch68 L633).

### The old Japanese clan

- The Japanese Asano clan is a separate line. Its patriarch is a silver-rank man named Shiro, father of **Akari**; Jason's own eldest paternal uncle is also named Shiro, a different man (Book 5, Ch34 L70). Its strongest member is **Noriko**, the patriarch's mother (Book 5, Ch36 L3). Jason tells her "My Asano family and your Asano clan henceforth have no connections" (Book 5, Ch37 L92).
- Akari married **Itsuki Tiwari** of the Tiwari clan (Book 13, Ch1004 L4-5); his father is Koya, youngest of three elders (Book 5, Ch39 L3).

### Open points

- How the Japanese patriarch's line connects to Ken and Hiro's family is not stated in the lines read.
- Book 12 calls Noriko "matriarch of the Asano clan" while Yumi is also called matriarch (Book 12, Ch63 L19); the two descriptions are not reconciled.

## 4. The Remore family

- **Roland Remore**, diamond-rank sword master and archchancellor, is Gabriel's father ("It's Dad", Book 12, Ch45 L56) and Rufus's grandfather (Book 3, Ch20 L25).
- **Gabriel** and **Arabelle** are husband and wife (Book 3, Ch1 L73); Roland is Arabelle's father-in-law (Book 10, Ch14 L50).
- Their sons are **Gwydion**, the older, a priest of Hero (Book 11, Ch67 L20), and **Rufus** (Book 2, Ch33 L6). Arabelle once said "I think it's time for another child, husband. A daughter, this time" (Book 3, Ch1 L73).

## 5. The Geller family

- **Danielle Geller** and **Keith Geller** are husband and wife; their children are **Henrietta**, the older sister, and **Humphrey** (Book 2, Ch62 L24).
- **Rick** and **Phoebe** are brother and sister of a distant branch; Phoebe is a distant cousin of Humphrey (Book 1, Ch66 L3-4; Book 1, Ch110 L51).
- **Jonah Geller** was Rick's cousin, whom he failed to save (Book 2, Ch17 L10). **Henry Geller** is also named on Rick's team (Book 1, Ch110 L51).
- **Dustin Kettering** replaced Jonah on Rick's team (Book 12, Ch16 L83) and is cousin to **Hudson Kettering** (Book 2, Ch15 L44).
- Rick married **Hannah Adeah** (Book 12, Ch15 L6).

## 6. The Mercers and their circle

- **Beaufort Mercer** is the Duke of Greenstone's brother and **Thalia Mercer** is his wife, a silver-rank adventurer (Book 1, Ch77 L47; Book 2, Ch59 L4).
- Their children are **Cassandra** and **Thadwick**; Cassandra is the older (Book 2, Ch27 L43). Thalia "looked more like her daughter's sister than her parent" (Book 1, Ch65 L120).
- **Neil Davone** grew up in the Mercer orbit because the Davone and Kettering families had close ties to the Mercers (Book 2, Ch27 L41).

## 7. The Rimaros royal house

- **Soramir Rimaros** is the founder; even the Storm King defers to him (Book 9, Ch14 L4).
- The **Storm King**'s firstborn daughter is **Zara**, the Hurricane Princess (Book 7, Ch5 L68-69). Her brother **Emiliano** becomes Storm King (Book 12, Ch12 L17-20); the notes for Book 13 call him her adopted cousin-brother (notes, Book 13). Zara later adopts into her mother's House Nareen (Book 9, Ch40 L18).
- **Vesper Rimaros** is Zara's aunt (Book 2, Ch69 L18) and is sixty-seven (Book 7, Ch15 L110-113).
- **Liara Rimaros** comes from "one of the more remote branches of the royal family" (Book 7, Ch17 L14). She married **Baseph**, born into House Amouz (Book 7, Ch17 L19-20; Book 9, Ch5 L3). Their children are **Dara**, **Zareen** and **Joseph** (Book 8, Ch62 L28-39).
- Liara calls **Cassin Amouz** "Uncle Cassin" when angry (Book 8, Ch20 L21).
- **Pelli** is "Aunt Pelli" to Vesper (Book 7, Ch15 L67); the text does not say she is a blood relative.
