## 1. Summary

This file lists the named adventuring teams and who was on them, by era. Roster changes (deaths, departures) are noted where the text gives them. It is level 3 because rosters run to Book 13.

## 2. Early years (Books 1-3, Greenstone and the Reaper contest)

### Jason's team (Team Biscuit)

- The name was picked by a familiar; Marcus Xenoria wonders aloud whether it was (Book 10, Ch47 L78).
- Core roster in Book 3: Jason, Humphrey, Sophie, Belinda, Clive and Neil, planning against Valdis's team (Book 3, Ch3 L70-82).
- Gary and Farrah are Rufus's old team, as Gabriel puts it (Book 10, Ch57 L92). Farrah dies in Book 2: Rufus remembers it during the island raid (Book 2, Ch76 L8).
- Zara joins in Book 9 and is named Team Biscuit by Book 12 (Book 12, Ch0 L14; Book 12, Ch49 L7).
- By Book 11 all of Team Biscuit are Burning Violet members except Jason and Taika (Book 11, Ch68 L1067); the team withdraws in Book 12 (Book 12, Ch45 L95).

### Other teams

- **Rick's team**: Rick, Phoebe, the Adeah twins Hannah and Claire, Jonah (until his death), Henry, and later Dustin Kettering (Book 1, Ch110 L51; Book 2, Ch29 L8; Book 9, Ch104 L54).
- **Beth's team**: Beth Cavendish, Hudson Kettering, Niko, Emily and her cousin Mose (Book 1, Ch89 L53-85).
- **Team Shining Scabbard**: built around Sigrid Freyn with Prince Valdis as its political core (Book 3, Ch3 L76-81).
- **Padma's team**: Rufus's juniors from Remore Academy (Book 3, Ch1 L87).
- **Emir's old team**: Emir, Gabriel and Arabelle Remore and Callum Morse (Book 2, Ch76 L3; Book 3, Ch24 L35).

## 3. Middle years (Books 4-9, Earth and Rimaros)

- **The Network defectors' combat team**: Asya, Greg and Kaito form the core, with Itsuki and Taika added (Book 5, Ch44 L17). Asya, Greg and Kaito die in Berlin (Book 5, Ch51 L40).
- **Storm Shredder**: led by Korinne Pescos with Orin, Kalif and Rosa, six members in all (Book 9, Ch24 L49-52; Book 9, Ch39 L80); Zara joins their vehicle (Book 9, Ch41 L119). Their name is given by Kalif (Book 9, Ch26 L69).
- **The surge expedition**: guild elites with independents and two princesses under Jeni Kavaloa (Book 7, Ch47 L33-60).

## 4. Late years (Books 10-13)

- **Moon's Edge**: six gold-rank women raised by Allayeth, led by Miriam Vance (Book 10, Ch51 L43; Book 10, Ch59 L4, L17).
- **Emir's old team reformed**: Emir, Gabriel and Arabelle with Constance replacing Callum Morse, who was not trusted for the mission (Book 10, Ch59 L4).
- **Cardinas expedition gold-rankers**: Amos Pensinata, Carlos Quilido and Hana Shavar join the teams above (Book 10, Ch59 L5).
- **Storm Shredder after the zone**: loses two members; Orin, Rosa and Korinne survive (Book 11, Ch32 L73-74; Book 11, Ch68 L378).
- **Nigel Thornton's unit**: a nine-person mercenary unit that began as a Network tactical team, now gold and silver-rank core users (Book 12, Ch3 L3-5).
- **Team Shining Scabbard in 2038**: led by Sigrid Freyn, with Valdis her husband and the Mirror King's backing (Book 12, Ch15 L8-10).
- **Neil's training**: Neil trains with Sigrid and faces her in a healer match (Book 12, Ch20 L9).

## 5. Open points

- Where Gary and Farrah stand in the Team Biscuit roster in Books 6-9 is covered in their character files.
