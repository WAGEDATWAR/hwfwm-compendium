## 1. Summary

Hudson Kettering is the huge earth-essence front-liner of Beth Cavendish's team and cousin of Dustin Kettering. He is a stone-shielding bruiser who meets Jason on the sand barge assault, fights Humphrey in a Geller-versus-Cavendish contest, and joins the Reaper trial expedition.

## 2. Facts

- **Size**: "One of the members of Beth's team was a huge human named Hudson. He looked like Humphrey with twenty percent bonus person, to the point of being almost as large as Gary" (Book 1, Ch89 L54).
- **First meeting**: he tells Jason "You don't seem evil" (Book 1, Ch89 L60).
- **Team role**: Beth puts him "front and centre" in the assault on the sand barge (Book 1, Ch89 L77), and a large flat block of sandstone rises under the team to carry them forward like a raft (Book 1, Ch89 L89).
- **In the fight**: a bronze-rank captain's charge shatters his stone shield and sends him staggering (Book 1, Ch90 L11); he ploughs up the ramp "crashing into the people inside and making space for the others" (Book 1, Ch90 L16). Beth tells him "No slacking" (Book 1, Ch90 L49).
- **Family**: he is cousin to Dustin, "the only one I can think of who could stack up to Jonah" among defenders, and cannot be pried from Beth's team (Book 2, Ch15 L44). Dustin sits beside him with their teams around them (Book 2, Ch28 L13).
- **Humphrey's offer**: Jason brings Humphrey to talk to Hudson about his cousin joining a Geller team; Hudson says a Geller name is good "but so was Mercer", and the family wants real adventurers for Dustin after Thadwick (Book 2, Ch15 L56-59).
- **Memorial**: his team attends the memorial because "Her standing strong is the reason my team all got out alive" (Book 2, Ch15 L63).
- **Powers**: his body takes on the colour of the desert stone, flesh transmuting into living rock (Book 2, Ch29 L48), and he conjures a huge stone shield at the front of a stone-raft run (Book 2, Ch29 L65). Two wall abilities spend him: "That's both my wall abilities. I won't have them again for a while" (Book 2, Ch29 L54).
- **Elemental pair**: he shields his team with earth powers while Dustin is clad in ice armour (Book 2, Ch29 L59); Humphrey notes that Rick's team has Kettering where "we have Hudson" (Book 2, Ch29 L18).
- **Down**: Humphrey smashes him after a switch-teleport with Beth leaves him prone (Book 2, Ch30 L4), and Hudson is soon "scrambling on the ground and conjuring shield after shield for Humphrey to smash through" (Book 2, Ch30 L15).
- **Reaper trial**: in the astral city he stays quiet while Beth plans; his earth powers are the team's most prominent (Book 2, Ch54 L85). He receives a vine-looped wrist band that adds effects to his earth conjuration (Book 2, Ch56 L41). He appears in the party contact list (Book 2, Ch54 L18).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Large, quiet and steady: he lets Beth talk, gives his team a wall to stand behind and says little. He is protective of his cousin.
