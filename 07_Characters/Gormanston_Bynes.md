## 1. Summary

Gormanston Bynes is a gold-rank elf, leader of Yaresh's Aristocratic Faction and head of its civic administration, who is outmanoeuvred by Marcus Xenoria and Jason. Proud, controlled and shrewd, he conceals contempt behind careful politeness.

## 2. Appearance

- A broad, powerful frame unusual among elves, with not a trace of monster core in his aura (Book 10, Ch16 L62-63).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Proud and controlled**: the faction's chief standard bearer and new lead representative of the Yaresh civic administration (Book 10, Ch16 L61).
- **Foresighted**: he fears the elite's neglect of the foundations of their power and wanted a different crisis than the invasion he got (Book 10, Ch47 L60-63).
- **Ruthless**: Allayeth says he hates weakness, squeezes value from enemies and would use or ally with Jason (Book 10, Ch18 L133, L137).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Gold-ranker (Book 10, Ch16 L62).
