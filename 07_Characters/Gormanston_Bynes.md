## 1. Summary

A gold-rank elf, leader of Yaresh's Aristocratic Faction and head of its civic administration, who is outmanoeuvred by Marcus Xenoria and Jason.

## 2. Facts

- Allayeth describes him as one of the most prominent members of a powerful political faction in Yaresh; his son Calcifer is the man Jason sent running (Book 10, Ch14 L3).
- He has "not a trace of monster core" in his aura, is a gold-ranker with a broad, powerful frame unusual among elves (Book 10, Ch16 L62-63).
- He is the faction's chief standard bearer and new lead representative of the Yaresh civic administration (Book 10, Ch16 L61).
- He fears the elite's neglect of the foundations of their power and wanted a different crisis than the invasion he got (Book 10, Ch47 L60-63).
- His niece Juniper, twenty-two, reached silver during the surge (Book 10, Ch48 L4).
- He stands up to Marcus Xenoria in the Adventure Society hall (Book 10, Ch48 L9-10); the aristocrats' power is left scattered (Book 10, Ch58 L58).
- Allayeth says he hates weakness, is efficient at squeezing value from enemies and would use or ally with Jason (Book 10, Ch18 L133, L137).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Proud, controlled and shrewd; he conceals contempt behind careful politeness.
