## 1. Summary

Greg is an old school friend of Jason's who joins the Network defectors on Kaito's helicopter crew and is killed in Book 5. His family is taken into Asano Village afterward. He is nerdy, self-deprecating and braver than he believed.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in the lines read; he drives a 2017 Ford Taurus (Book 4, Ch66 L59).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Self-deprecating**: he was "never much more than an adequate lawyer" and had been pushed out of his father's practice (Book 5, Ch58 L14).
- **Enjoys small comforts**: he holds a mug of hot chocolate on a chalet balcony (Book 5, Ch47 L37).
- **Teased by Asya**: "If all your friends are cool, what does that say about you?" (Book 4, Ch66 L66).
- **Sensitive to portals**: he fights portal sickness on Erika's floor (Book 4, Ch74 L140).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Bronze rank by Book 5; his body "could easily endure the cold" (Book 5, Ch47 L37).
- He crews Kaito's helicopter with Asya (Book 5, Ch7 L12).

## 5. Background

- School friend of Jason and Asya who roped Jason and Amy into hours-long board games (Book 4, Ch7 L42; Book 4, Ch66 L59).
- He says he had lived more in six months of magic than in six years before (Book 5, Ch58 L14).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 4**: meets Jason with Asya and Craig (Book 4, Ch66 L59-66); portal trip (Book 4, Ch74 L140).
- **Book 5**: part of the combat team core with Asya and Kaito (Book 5, Ch44 L17); dies in Berlin (Book 5, Ch51 L40); Jason speaks with his spectre in the cloud palace hall (Book 5, Ch58 L3-14); Jason bows his head before the family (Book 5, Ch56 L26).
- **Book 11**: his family remains in the clan (Book 11, Ch68 L632).

## 7. Relationships

- **Asya**: friend and fellow crew member (Book 4, Ch66 L66).
- **Jason**: old friend (Book 5, Ch58 L3).
- **Kaito**: teammate (Book 5, Ch44 L17).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

Jason's talk with his spectre follows his death (Book 5, Ch58 L3-14).
