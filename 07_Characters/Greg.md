## 1. Summary

An old school friend of Jason's who joins Kaito's crew and is killed in Book 5.

## 2. Facts

- Jason remembers an old school friend Greg who roped him and Amy into long board games (Book 4, Ch7 L42).
- On Kaito's helicopter crew with Asya (Book 5, Ch7 L12; Ch14 L42).
- Says he was "never much more than an adequate lawyer" and had been pushed out of his father's practice; he had lived more in six months of magic than in six years before (Book 5, Ch58 L14).
- His family is among those taken in at Asano Village (Book 5, Ch13 L74); Jason later bows his head before Greg's family (Book 5, Ch56 L26).
- Akari says Kaito, Asya and Greg were fighting for their world (Book 5, Ch51 L40).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Nerdy, self-deprecating and braver than he believed.
