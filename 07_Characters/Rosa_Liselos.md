## 1. Summary

Rosa Liselos is the scout of Korinne's Team Storm Shredder, with the best perception on the team. Practical and comfort-loving, she backs Kalif in wanting rest, spots Jason's skulking admirer, and guides her wounded team back through enemy lines in Book 11. By Book 12 she has retired and works for the Adventure Society.

## 2. Facts

- **Introduced as a rival**: a Rimaros adventurer addresses her as "Rosa Liselos, isn't it? What do you say, Liselos? Can you do better than Asano's shadows?" (Book 7, Ch47 L73).
- **Role**: "Rosa Liselos, the scout from Korinne's team" (Book 9, Ch26 L110); "the team scout, she had the best perception amongst them" (Book 9, Ch53 L118).
- **Comfort**: she says she "can definitely live with it" if working for Jason means cloud beds and giant dinner spreads (Book 9, Ch26 L110), and joins Kalif in telling Korinne the monster surge is over: "This is a can for storing food, not adventurers" (Book 9, Ch28 L106-110; Book 9, Ch29 L8).
- **Insight**: she names the difference "between adventuring approaches in Rimaros and Vitesse" (Book 9, Ch34 L86).
- **Gossip**: she bets Jason "doesn't go for those stuffy society dances" (Book 9, Ch42 L52).
- **Candour about Zara**: she tells a princess to get over a man who is not keen on her (Book 9, Ch53 L114) and senses him "skulking around" (Book 9, Ch53 L118).
- **Surprise**: she asks "How did you get so close? You didn't use to be this good" of someone who has improved (Book 9, Ch54 L8).
- **Book 11**: the team scout guides the wounded team back to friendly lines "through the enemy throng" to the healers (Book 11, Ch32 L74).
- **Book 12**: "Rosa retired. She works for the Adventure Society now" (Book 12, Ch12 L61).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Sensible and a little sardonic: she notices things before anyone, wants a bed and a hot meal, and would rather say what she thinks than keep a mask.
