## 1. Summary

Nicolas Hendren is the archbishop of the Church of Purity, a silver-rank barrier-and-force specialist who flees the inquiry and joins the Builder cult's camp before Jason's team kills him. Stiff, proud and rule-bound, he wants Purity's dignity even as it is dragged into the cult's plans.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in the lines read.

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Disgruntled and proud**: he is summoned to the cult camp and refuses to taint the church for "short-term power" (Book 3, Ch54 L21; Book 3, Ch56 L57).
- **Moral authority absent god**: his god's voice is silent, making him the highest moral authority in the realm he stands in (Book 3, Ch56 L71).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Silver-rank barrier and force specialist: his barrier shields his group from a falling building (Book 3, Ch58 L3); a force wave power (Book 3, Ch59 L103); "always the true danger" (Book 3, Ch58 L47).

## 5. Background

- Archbishop of the Church of Purity, introduced at a Greenstone meeting (Book 2, Ch19 L73). One Book 3 line spells the name Hedron (Book 3, Ch1 L106).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 2**: enters the ritual room with Danielle Geller, Thalia Mercer, Elspeth Arella and Emir Bahadir (Book 2, Ch19 L73).
- **Book 3**: vanishes on a "previously scheduled sabbatical" (Book 3, Ch31 L57); joins the camp (Book 3, Ch54-56); the battle (Book 3, Ch58-59); killed, with a large part of Purity's funds in his storage (Book 3, Ch60 L12); the Builder says losing Hendren's power is a blow though he was a reluctant ally (Book 3, Ch60 L32).
