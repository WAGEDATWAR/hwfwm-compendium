## 1. Summary

A tall celestine diamond-ranker, the prime vessel of the Reaper.

## 2. Facts

- "A tall celestine, with dark skin and a bushy mound of curly, silver hair that matched his eyes" (Book 3, Ch69 L238); Shako greets him warmly in the dome chamber (Book 3, Ch69 L233-237).
- He says he made the mistake of getting his descendants out of trouble again and again until he washed his hands of them (Book 3, Ch69 L245).
- When he and Shako change, the Reaper speaks through Velius and the voice becomes cold (Book 3, Ch69 L250-251).
- In Book 11 he says the Builder is done with Pallimustus (Book 11, Ch22 L11); Raythe and Velius lead the descent of peak diamond-rankers into the shaft (Book 11, Ch61 L8).
- Lord Velius is "the prime vessel of my progenitor", the Reaper (Book 11, Ch64 L9).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Warm and wry in person; chilling when the Reaper speaks through him.
