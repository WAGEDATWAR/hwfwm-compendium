## 1. Summary

Velius is a tall celestine diamond-ranker, the prime vessel of the Reaper. Warm and wry in person, chilling when the Reaper speaks through him.

## 2. Appearance

- "A tall celestine, with dark skin and a bushy mound of curly, silver hair that matched his eyes" (Book 3, Ch69 L238).
- When the Reaper speaks, his eyes become black orbs and his voice turns cold and bleak (Book 11, Ch64 L51).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Warm**: Shako greets him warmly in the dome chamber (Book 3, Ch69 L233-237) and he waves Shako into a lounge chair (Book 3, Ch69 L242).
- **Rueful about descendants**: he says he made the mistake of getting his descendants out of trouble again and again until he washed his hands of them (Book 3, Ch69 L245).
- **Earnest in work**: Rufus finds him "earnest and diligent" in a role normally handled by the church of Fertility (Book 11, Ch68 L778).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Prime vessel of the Reaper (Book 11, Ch64 L9).
- With Raythe he leads the descent of peak diamond-rankers into the shaft (Book 11, Ch61 L8).

## 5. Background

- Someone who has known Velius a very long time calls him "an old friend" (Book 6, Ch35 L50).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 3**: the dome chamber (Book 3, Ch69 L233-251).
- **Book 11**: says the Builder is done with Pallimustus (Book 11, Ch22 L11); the descent (Book 11, Ch61 L8); the Reaper speaks through him (Book 11, Ch64 L51); asks for a meeting in the clan maternity ward (Book 11, Ch68 L947).

## 7. Relationships

- **Shako**: friendly rival (Book 3, Ch69 L233-237).
- **Raythe**: fellow peak diamond-ranker (Book 11, Ch61 L8).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

When he and Shako change, the Reaper speaks through Velius and the voice becomes cold (Book 3, Ch69 L250-251).
