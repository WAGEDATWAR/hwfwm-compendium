## 1. Summary

Rufus Remore is a bald, dark-skinned swordsman from Vitesse, grandson of the diamond-ranker Roland Remore. He is one of the three adventurers Jason meets in the Vane cellar, runs Jason's field assessment, and remains his friend for the rest of the series.

## 2. Appearance

Dark skin, a bald head and "stupidly handsome"; when Jason first sees him, grimy from his ordeal, he looks like an action hero heading into act three (Book 1, Ch11 L49). His voice is "like dark chocolate" (Book 1, Ch11 L51). The notes describe him as tall (notes, Book 1; no height is stated in the lines checked).

## 3. Personality and voice

- He is calm under pressure: when Anisa demands that Jason be burned as tainted, he answers "I did [know]" and refuses her any say (Book 1, Ch30 L88-93).
- He feels responsible as the leader of his group: Farrah tells Jason "Rufus is in charge, so he blames himself" for their capture, and says the pressure on him from his family is enormous (Book 1, Ch30 L57-60).
- After Farrah's death he is driven by obsession, which strains his relationships (Book 3, Ch33 L11-12).
- The family joke, repeated by Arabelle and Rufus in Book 10, is "my family runs a school" (Book 10, Ch21 L19-21).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- **Swordsmanship**: an elegant scimitar of golden light with red-gold inscriptions that he conjures in his hand (Book 1, Ch16 L31-36); silver and golden swords (Book 1, Ch16 L31-36).
- **Other powers**: boots that let him walk on water (Book 1, Ch16 L38) and a "speed of light" power that makes the world "seem to freeze in time" for "two seconds of accelerated time" (Book 1, Ch108 L99; Book 1, Ch109 L23).
- **Kingdom of Eclipse**: a sun-and-moon power invoked by incantation ("Darkness and light, sun and moon; be mine to awaken and move at my command...", Book 2, Ch76 L71).
- **Rank**: bronze at the start of the series (Book 1, Ch15 L92: "We're bronze-rank adventurers"), and in Book 11 he is the most powerful combatant on Earth below gold rank (Book 11, Ch68 L1514); the chapters read do not state that he reaches gold.
- **Teaching**: he teaches Jason footwork, balance and combat (notes, Book 1) and later teaches the Asano clan (Book 13, Ch1001 L8).

## 5. Background

Son of Gabriel and Arabelle Remore (Book 2, Ch33 L6); grandson of Roland Remore (Humphrey calls Roland "the grandfather who means so much to his friend", Book 12, Ch45 L97); his older brother Gwydion is a priest of Hero (notes, Book 11); Rufus's uncle is clergy of Hero (Book 1, Ch48 L92-96). Rufus was "really late fighting his first monster" but did it "by nineteen" (Book 11, Ch68 L918).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: captured with Gary and Farrah by the Vane blood cult; frees them with Jason; runs Jason's field assessment; present when Farrah dies.
- **Books 2-3**: uses his Kingdom of Eclipse power in the strike on the Builder cult camp (Book 2, Ch76 L71).
- **Book 7**: reunites with Jason in Rimaros.
- **Book 11**: travels to Earth with Taika and Boris, arriving at the standing stones and meeting Yumi Asano (Book 11, Ch68 L470-473; Yumi is from the notes).
- **Book 13**: by 2038 he teaches the Asano clan; he introduces Hassan Dukhtar, whom he is dating (Book 13, Ch1016 L23-27).

## 7. Relationships

Jason (closest friend), Gary and Farrah (childhood friends), Roland, Arabelle, Gabriel and Gwydion (family), Humphrey Geller, Taika Williams, Hassan Dukhtar. The books only imply a relationship with Vincent Trenslow, so it is not recorded here (project decision).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

His journey to Earth and role teaching the clan (Book 11 onward) are level 3.

## 9. Roleplay notes
