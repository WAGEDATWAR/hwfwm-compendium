## 1. Summary

Zareen Rimaros is Liara and Baseph's middle child: a three-star adventurer and political enthusiast with the royal family's sapphire hair and eyes. She serves as the royal family's liaison to Jason in Rimaros, is proposed as a political match, and in Book 13 is a lady in waiting to Princess Zara on the embassy to Earth. Her sister Dara and brother Joseph are covered in Baseph_and_the_Rimaros_Children.md.

## 2. Facts

- **Family**: "Dara was the eldest and, like the middle child, Zareen, had followed their mother into adventuring"; the youngest, Joseph, is an administrator (Book 8, Ch62 L32).
- **Appearance**: "physically smaller" than her sister, not inheriting her mother's height, but with "the iconic sapphire hair and eyes of the royal family" (Book 8, Ch62 L36).
- **Politics, not combat**: she is "much more interested in politics than her mother" (Book 8, Ch62 L36), and was close to Vesper, "picking up her relative's taste for the politics that Liara disdained but could never seem to escape" (Book 9, Ch11 L53).
- **Interest in Jason**: "I'm more interested in the man himself... Is it true that his aura is as strong as a gold-ranker's?" and Liara scolds her "Don't even think about it" (Book 8, Ch62 L54-59). Jason later says he is "not looking for the entanglements a political marriage would bring" (Book 8, Ch63 L106).
- **A political match**: Zara notes whether Jason chooses "Zareen, myself, someone else or no one at all" is up to him (Book 9, Ch12 L108); Dara and Baseph exchange looks about Zareen's absence from dinner (Book 9, Ch11 L51). Rufus guesses she would be the royal choice, since her sister is "pure adventurer" without political sensibilities (Book 9, Ch12 L89).
- **Liaison**: she is the royal family liaison to Jason's team (Book 9, Ch13 L45). Liara scolds her for making "a bold move instead of talking to your mother about it because you knew she would be against it" (Book 9, Ch14 L61).
- **At the duel and ball**: she sits with Jason and Liara at the confrontation with Hector de Varco (Book 9, Ch16 L3), asks whether he is "remotely torturing a group of gold-rankers" (Book 9, Ch18 L15), and asks "Did you plan for the king to step in?" (Book 9, Ch15 L103).
- **Intelligence on the duel**: she explains that most of a rival guild fought the city attack on Livaros, with its special members on monster watch on Provo (Book 9, Ch15 L16).
- **Book 12**: she is assigned to the Rimaros diplomatic delegation to Earth (Book 12, Ch56 L117).
- **Book 13**: "technically a princess, but far enough down the hierarchy that she was formally referred to as 'lady'"; she is something like a lady in waiting to Princess Zara and "had a history with Jason... mostly a social connection, through her mother" (Book 13, Ch953 L50-51). She teases Zara by swooning at her pacing (Book 13, Ch953 L56).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Sharp, strategic and amused: she sizes people up for leverage, enjoys politics and teases her cousins. She says what she wants and tests how far the answer will bend.
