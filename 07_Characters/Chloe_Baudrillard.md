## 1. Summary

Chloe Baudrillard is a French epidemiologist with Doctors Without Borders whom Jason befriends in Book 5 while healing patients in a quarantine tent. She is later sheltered by the Asano clan with her family and works on magical healing.

## 2. Appearance

Not described beyond her hazmat suit in the quarantine tent (Book 5, Ch2 L44).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Wry**: "My biggest enemy beat me out for the good parking space at the hospital where I used to work" (Book 5, Ch8 L12).
- **Awed but steady**: she watches Jason calm the ward "in disbelief" (Book 5, Ch2 L51-53).
- **Friendly**: Jason calls her "a good egg" (Book 5, Ch2 L85).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- A medical scientist, not an essence user in the lines read; by Book 13 she works with healers on magical healing (Book 13, Ch953 L84).

## 5. Background

- French; a Doctors Without Borders epidemiologist (Book 5, Ch2 L9; Book 5, Ch3 L146).
- In Book 11 she and her family are displaced by the vampire occupation and taken in by the clan (Book 11, Ch68 L633).
