## 1. Summary

A French epidemiologist with Doctors Without Borders whom Jason befriends in Book 5 and who later shelters with her family.

## 2. Facts

- Jason asks Dr Chloe Baudrillard what he should call her; she is French (Book 5, Ch2 L9; Book 5, Ch3 L130).
- Jason calls her "a good egg" (Book 5, Ch2 L85) and introduces her to his family as of Doctors Without Borders (Book 5, Ch3 L146).
- In Book 11 she and her family are displaced by the vampire occupation of Europe (Book 11, Ch68 L633).
- In Book 13 she works on magical healing with Jason's brother-in-law (Book 13, Ch953 L84).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Practical, clear-headed and kind; she meets magic as a scientist.
