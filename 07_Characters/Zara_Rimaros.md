## 1. Summary

Zara is a celestine princess of the Storm Kingdom, the "Hurricane Princess". Jason meets her in a tent during the contest for the Scythe of the Reaper, when she is nineteen and he is iron-rank. Years later the scandal of her false engagement claim, her loss of the succession, and her adoption into her mother's house bring her to Team Biscuit and, in 2038, into a romance with Jason.

## 2. Appearance

Celestine with caramel skin, sapphire eyes and waterfall-blue hair (Book 2, Ch69 L16). After her adoption into her mother's House Nareen she has milky teal hair and eyes (Book 9, Ch40 L7-13); by Book 12 she is still "turning your hair and eyes copper" (Book 12, Ch12 L10).

## 3. Personality and voice

At first she is regal ("royal highness", Book 2, Ch65 L47) and wary: in Book 2 Jason teleports into her tent and offers gem-berry and milk-nut squares (Book 2, Ch65 L55). She invents a fiancé (Book 9, Ch40 L55) and tells Korinne "I'm looking for a place to belong" (Book 9, Ch40 L32). In Book 13 she tells Jason he is a terrible leader but "a good lord" and advises him to keep a consistent persona (Book 13, Ch969 L56-63).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- **Powers**: wind and water, big storms (Book 12, Ch0 L14 says she has "been blowing things up with big storms"); recovery, speed, astral and holy affinity (notes, Book 2).
- **Rank**: gold by Book 12: she stays in a suite fit for gold-rank adventurers (Book 12, Ch12 L3) and calls up a storm of gold-rank force (Book 13, Ch965 L86); her rank when she first meets Jason is not stated in the chapters read.

## 5. Background

Firstborn daughter of the Storm King, called the Hurricane Princess (Book 7, Ch5 L68-69); her brother Emiliano becomes Storm King, and the notes for Book 13 call him her "adopted cousin-brother", an adopted baby shielded from the parents' treason; Zara herself is adopted into her mother's House Nareen (Book 12, Ch12 L17-20). Vesper Rimaros is Zara's aunt (Book 2, Ch69 L18); Vesper says she is sixty-seven and calls Pelli "Aunt Pelli", who remembers her as a wilful girl on the island (Book 7, Ch15 L110-113), so the notes' "descendant of Pelli" is corrected to niece (or at least a younger relative); she hopes to reach gold during the surge (Book 7, Ch15 L114-115), which places her below gold, and she is silver rank ("Vesper's silver-rank vision", Book 7, Ch15 L74; "Vesper was only silver rank to Liara's gold", Book 7, Ch21 L32); Liara Rimaros is a gold-ranker of the royal house, and Zareen Rimaros, Zara's lady in waiting and cousin, is Liara's daughter (Book 8, Ch62 L32-36).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 2**: meets Jason in a tent at the Scythe contest; the Hurricane Princess at the farewell feast.
- **Book 7**: Zara had been matched with a member of House Irios, a political marriage the royals wanted for stability before the surge (Book 7, Ch18 L66-72).
- **Book 9**: she is a former princess of the Storm Kingdom by Book 9 (Book 9, Ch40 L21); she says "I'm Zara Nareen now" (Book 9, Ch40 L18) and that she adopted into her mother's house, whose colouration she wears (Book 9, Ch40 L13), to signal her withdrawal from the contest for the crown (Book 9, Ch40 L72).
- **Book 12**: meets Jason again after fifteen years (Book 12, Ch12 L20); has joined Team Biscuit (Book 12, Ch0 L14; Book 12, Ch49 L7).
- **Book 13**: she is left with Emi and Erika for an hour, and Jason's father and brother-in-law join them (Book 13, Ch956 L40); Jason tells Ken how they met twenty years ago (Book 13, Ch956 L41); she walks the promenade deck hand in hand with Jason (Book 13, Ch956 L3) and kisses him later in a scene about Allayeth and Rufus (Book 13, Ch1004 L44-52).

## 7. Relationships

Jason, Vesper and Liara Rimaros, Emiliano, Team Biscuit, Zareen Rimaros.
