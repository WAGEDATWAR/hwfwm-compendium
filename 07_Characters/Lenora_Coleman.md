## 1. Summary

Lenora Coleman is the bureaucrat who runs the dimensional artefact site in Australia. She was recruited from university, spends her working life in a monitoring station, and by Book 12 directs a bunker operation against pirates arriving via Jason's domain.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in the lines read; sweat drips off her in the monitoring room in the middle of the night (Book 11, Ch68 L413).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Disillusioned**: "her excitement had been well and truly killed off" (Book 12, Ch61 L16).
- **Composed**: she pinches the bridge of her nose, breathes out and schools her expression (Book 12, Ch62 L54).
- **Dry**: Anna says she will "play this objectively" (Book 12, Ch62 L28).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- A core user and not a fighter (Book 12, Ch61 L22).
- She directs operations from the command-and-control room, the bunker's most restricted zone (Book 12, Ch64 L24).

## 5. Background

- Excited to join the program out of university, then a year in the monitoring room drained that (Book 11, Ch68 L413).
- Worked there for "almost her entire adult life" (Book 12, Ch61 L16).
- Her deputy Barry is mayor of the artefact city (Book 12, Ch61 L25).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 11**: monitoring room (Book 11, Ch68 L413).
- **Book 12**: Jason's arrival and the pirate battle (Book 12, Ch61 L16; Book 12, Ch66 L18-34).
- **Book 13**: occupies the former mayoral residence (Book 13, Ch961 L11).

## 7. Relationships

- **Barry**: her deputy (Book 12, Ch62 L80).
- **Anna**: counterpart (Book 12, Ch62 L28).
