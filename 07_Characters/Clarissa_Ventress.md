## 1. Summary

Clarissa Ventress is one of the crime lords of Old City and holds Sophie's indenture. She uses Sophie to provoke the rash Cole Silva and learns what Lucian Lamprey wants in order to control him.

## 2. Appearance

- Dark cascading hair and a walk "so sultry she was almost swerving"; she looks only a few years older than the two women with her (Book 1, Ch43 L29).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Calculating**: "The key to controlling a person was finding what they wanted" (Book 1, Ch52 L57).
- **Amused**: she is "happily imagining the look on Silva's face" as she watches Sophie's match (Book 1, Ch50 L93).
- **Irritated by independence**: she is less happy with Sophie's friend Belinda (Book 1, Ch50 L93).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Rank is not stated in the lines read; she travels with a huge leonid bodyguard (Book 1, Ch43 L29).

## 5. Background

- Lives in a sprawling manor in Old City's canal district, which has its own internal city wall (Book 1, Ch53 L70).
- Her arrangement with Sophie: Sophie would help her provoke Silva into the kind of rash action his father would have prevented (Book 1, Ch50 L96).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: enters the chamber with Sophie and Belinda (Book 1, Ch43 L29); watches Sophie's match (Book 1, Ch50 L92-96); learns Lucian's wants (Book 1, Ch52 L57); summons Sophie and Belinda to her manor (Book 1, Ch53 L70).

## 7. Relationships

- **Sophie**: indentured servant (Book 1, Ch50 L96).
- **Cole Silva**: rival, newest of the Big Three (Book 1, Ch50 L92).
- **Lucian Lamprey**: someone she plans to control (Book 1, Ch52 L57).

Nothing hidden in the lines read.
