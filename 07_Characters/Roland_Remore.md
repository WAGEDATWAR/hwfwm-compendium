## 1. Summary

Rufus's grandfather, a diamond-rank sword master and the archchancellor of Remore Academy in Vitesse.

## 2. Facts

- "The famous, diamond-rank sword master, Roland Remore"; Rufus often repeats advice from him (Book 3, Ch20 L25).
- Rarely seen on the Remore Academy campus, "the presence of the diamond-rank arch-chancellor was always felt" (Book 3, Ch69 L587).
- "Looked like a well-preserved forty, a tenth of the reality": tall, strong and handsome, with a round bush of dark curly hair (Book 3, Ch69 L588).
- Humphrey calls him "the grandfather who means so much to his friend" (Book 12, Ch45 L97).
- Burning Violet became more associated with the Academy after he became guild leader (Book 6, Ch47 L125).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Measured, aphoristic and rarely seen; scenes with him should feel weighty.
