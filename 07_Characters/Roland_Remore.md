## 1. Summary

Roland Remore is Rufus's grandfather, a diamond-rank sword master, archchancellor of Remore Academy in Vitesse and leader of Burning Violet. He rarely appears but his presence is felt, and he arrives with the diamond-rankers at the end of Book 13.

## 2. Appearance

- "A well-preserved forty, a tenth of the reality": tall, strong and handsome, with a round bush of dark curly hair (Book 3, Ch69 L588).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Weighty and remote**: his presence on the Academy campus is always felt and draws all eyes (Book 3, Ch69 L587).
- **Proud of the guild**: Gabriel says "He's always treated the guild like it's his own little fiefdom" (Book 12, Ch45 L56).
- **Famous**: Rufus often repeats his advice (Book 3, Ch20 L25).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Diamond-rank sword master (Book 3, Ch20 L25); his aura surges out in Book 13 (Book 13, Ch1024 L35).
- He personally brought a boy from the countryside to the academy, rumoured (Book 3, Ch69 L588).

## 5. Background

- Archchancellor of Remore Academy; the guild Burning Violet became more associated with the Academy after he became guild leader (Book 6, Ch47 L125).
- Rufus's grandfather; Gabriel's father (Book 12, Ch45 L56).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 3**: introduced via Rufus's advice and the Academy (Book 3, Ch20 L25; Book 3, Ch69 L587-588).
- **Book 7**: Farrah has met him (Book 7, Ch46 L78).
- **Book 9**: Jason asks about him (Book 9, Ch18 L70).
- **Book 12**: Humphrey says Roland was looking forward to meeting Jason (Book 12, Ch45 L97).
- **Book 13**: emerges from the dimensional vessel and meets Jason's aura (Book 13, Ch1024 L14, L35).

## 7. Relationships

- **Rufus**: grandson, who repeats his advice (Book 3, Ch20 L25).
- **Gabriel**: his son (Book 12, Ch45 L56).
- **Humphrey**: friend of Rufus who knows his reputation (Book 12, Ch45 L97).
