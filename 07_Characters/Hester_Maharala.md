## 1. Summary

Emir's portal user and head of logistics, born in Jayapura in Pranay.

## 2. Facts

- Emir directs "a woman named Hester" to open a portal for the Boko rescue; she traces a circle in the air and the portal shimmers (Book 1, Ch109 L6). She can only open a portal to places she has visited, and the time before her next use depends on range (Book 1, Ch109 L7-9).
- She is the Asiatic-looking person Jason sees first in this world, closer to South Asian than his own Japanese features (Book 2, Ch34 L55).
- "Hester was one of Emir's most important staff members", in charge of logistics and coordination between his operations (Book 2, Ch69 L64).
- Born in Jayapura, a port city of Pranay (Book 2, Ch69 L65-66); she has a clifftop house with grotto rooms and a swim-access bar (Book 2, Ch69 L69-72).
- A shopkeeper calls her "Hester Maharala... The lady with the house on the hill" (Book 2, Ch70 L70).
- Jason compares her portal range to his own shadow portals (Book 3, Ch8 L25); she portals Clive back to the city (Book 3, Ch30 L21) and Emir plans to ask her to portal Callum back (Book 3, Ch69 L186).
- In Book 6 Gary imagines Hester arriving to portal him to a memorial service (Book 6, Ch47 L172).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Warm, travelled and practical; the one who gets people where they need to be and keeps Emir's operations running.
