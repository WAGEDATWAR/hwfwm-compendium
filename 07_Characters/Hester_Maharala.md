## 1. Summary

Hester Maharala is Emir's portal user and head of logistics, born in Jayapura in Pranay. Warm, travelled and practical, she gets people where they need to be and keeps Emir's operations running.

## 2. Appearance

- The Asiatic-looking person Jason sees first in this world, closer to South Asian than his own Japanese features (Book 2, Ch34 L55).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Warm and practical**: Jason compares her portal range to his own shadow portals (Book 3, Ch8 L25).
- **Respected**: "Hester was one of Emir's most important staff members" (Book 2, Ch69 L64).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Portal user: she traces a circle in the air to open a portal (Book 1, Ch109 L6); she can only open portals to places she has visited and the time before her next use depends on range (Book 1, Ch109 L7-9).
- She portals Clive back to the city (Book 3, Ch30 L21).

## 5. Background

- Born in Jayapura, a port city of Pranay (Book 2, Ch69 L65-66).
- Has a clifftop house with grotto rooms and a swim-access bar (Book 2, Ch69 L69-72).
- A shopkeeper calls her "Hester Maharala... The lady with the house on the hill" (Book 2, Ch70 L70).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: opens the portal for the Boko rescue at Emir's direction (Book 1, Ch109 L6).
- **Book 2**: head of logistics (Book 2, Ch69 L64).
- **Book 3**: Emir plans to ask her to portal Callum back (Book 3, Ch69 L186).
- **Book 6**: Gary imagines Hester arriving to portal him to a memorial service (Book 6, Ch47 L172).

## 7. Relationships

- **Emir**: employer (Book 2, Ch69 L64).
