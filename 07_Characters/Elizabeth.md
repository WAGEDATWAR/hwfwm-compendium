## 1. Summary

The gold-rank leader of the vampires who take Europe.

## 2. Facts

- "Their leader was known only as Elizabeth. She was a gold-ranker, and extremely tired of people asking if she was Elizabeth Bathory" (Book 11, Ch68 L1488).
- Combat with the vampires "began in earnest shortly after" Jason left; a Cabal member living among humans tells Jason "I think you made an impression on the vampire leader, Elizabeth" (Book 11, Ch42 L38).
- In the Saint-Etienne zone Mr North and Elizabeth "teamed up to use their unique ritual magic variations to set up defensive rituals"; her five ritual circles send out red tentacles that squeeze the vorger (Book 6, Ch45 L39-40).
- Gerling is later her blood slave (Book 11, Ch68 L2267).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Amused, patient and predatory.
