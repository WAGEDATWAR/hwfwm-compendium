## 1. Summary

Elizabeth is the gold-rank leader of the vampires who take Europe, a blood-ritual specialist who establishes a détente with the world and later trades with Jason's side. She is tired of being asked whether she is Elizabeth Báthory.

## 2. Appearance

- Pale skin, red lips, delicate features and midnight hair; her face lacks an essence user's polish, but her slight smile and smouldering eyes hold "the seductive promise of sultry intelligence" (Book 6, Ch41 L81).
- She moves in a ball gown "like she was floating" (Book 6, Ch41 L81).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Dangerous when asked her name**: those around her stopped asking if she was Báthory once she started "immediately eating anyone who did" (Book 11, Ch68 L1488).
- **Pragmatic**: her edicts about treating humans better were not compassion; they bought sustainability (Book 11, Ch68 L2261).
- **Primus inter pares**: Jason says "You clearly have primacy amongst them" about the other vampires (Book 6, Ch41 L82).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- A gold-ranker and "a master of luring groups into traps set out using blood rituals" (Book 6, Ch44 L21).
- She and Mr North pair their ritual magics for defence; her five ritual circles send out red tentacles that extend and snake round corners to squeeze the vorger (Book 6, Ch45 L39-40).
- Humanity tried to kill her with a nuclear device flooding Rome with magical sunlight, modified from a design Travis made for Jason (Book 11, Ch68 L1490).

## 5. Background

- Under her, the vampires establish a détente: every major city still hides vampires, and bombings in Europe are answered by assassinations in Beijing, Washington, Moscow and London (Book 11, Ch68 L1489).
- She rests in a vault in Venice that was once a bank (Book 11, Ch68 L2251).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 6**: led out to Jason by Shade and settled with the coalition (Book 6, Ch41 L81-82); fights the vorger (Book 6, Ch44 L21; Book 6, Ch45 L39-40).
- **Book 11**: leader of the European vampires and target of the sunlight device (Book 11, Ch68 L1488-1490).
- **Book 13**: with Gerling and her blood orb trailing "like pets", steps through a portal, followed by Remy (Book 13, Ch973 L82); at a meeting table opposite Neil, Carlos and Ian (Book 13, Ch988 L4).

## 7. Relationships

- **Gerling**: her blood slave (Book 11, Ch68 L2267).
- **Mr North**: ally in ritual defence (Book 6, Ch45 L39).
- **Jason**: he asks her to settle the other vampires, acknowledging her primacy (Book 6, Ch41 L82).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

Her treatment of humans is strategic rather than compassionate (Book 11, Ch68 L2261).

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Amused, patient and predatory, with an aristocrat's poise. Cue: refusing to be asked about Báthory.
