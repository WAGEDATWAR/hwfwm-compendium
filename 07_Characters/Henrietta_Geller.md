## 1. Summary

Henrietta Geller is Humphrey's older sister, a bronze-rank summoning specialist who takes her sibling role seriously. She trains the team, helps defend Greenstone in the Book 3 surge, and by Book 12 is an adventurer on Cassandra's team.

## 2. Appearance

- Statuesque, like her brother, with strong, handsome features and short practical hair; the siblings both favour their burly father over their slender mother (Book 2, Ch75 L70).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Protective and critical**: she makes clear Humphrey's teammates are yet to meet her approval (Book 2, Ch62 L24) and appoints herself to look after him and his team (Book 3, Ch5 L73).
- **Coaching**: "It's not enough to be a distraction for your damage dealer" (Book 3, Ch9 L27).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Summoning specialist, "something of a one-woman team"; one summon is an angelic messenger with healing powers (Book 7, Ch36 L8; Book 6, Ch37 L93).
- Bronze rank for almost two years in Book 2 (Book 2, Ch75 L68); the senior adventurer at twenty-one in Book 3 (Book 3, Ch7 L28).

## 5. Background

- Daughter of Danielle and Keith; Humphrey's older sister (Book 2, Ch62 L24).
- Returned to Greenstone with her father ahead of the monster surge (Book 2, Ch75 L68).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 2**: Humphrey says "Henri has agreed to help us train" (Book 2, Ch75 L69).
- **Book 3**: gears up against the giant elemental (Book 3, Ch6 L43); briefs the ranking adventurer (Book 3, Ch7 L3).
- **Book 6-7**: Jason notes her summoning (Book 6, Ch37 L93; Book 7, Ch36 L8).
- **Book 12**: on Cassandra's team in the jobs hall (Book 12, Ch46 L3).

## 7. Relationships

- **Humphrey**: younger brother (Book 3, Ch9 L27).
- **Cassandra**: teammate (Book 12, Ch46 L3).
