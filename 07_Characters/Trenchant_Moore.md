## 1. Summary

Trenchant Moore is a gold-rank human sword specialist of the Sapphire Crown royal guard who escorts Jason's group in Rimaros. Watchful and reserved, he reads places and people for danger.

## 2. Appearance

- A human in a guild of mostly celestines: pale-skinned and dark-haired, lean and angular with icy blue eyes (Book 7, Ch21 L13-14).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Reserved**: he drops to one knee before the royals (Book 7, Ch46 L74).
- **Cautious about politics**: he warns that Vesper's world is political (Book 7, Ch58 L53).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Gold rank sword specialist who has served as a guard on and off for decades (Book 7, Ch21 L13-18).
- He boards ships checking society contracts (Book 7, Ch22 L93).

## 5. Background

- The royal family does not shield the Sapphire Crown from surge quotas (Book 7, Ch21 L15-18).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 7**: summoned to a low-key palace meeting where Vesper Rimaros lets him keep his sword (Book 7, Ch21 L21-25); escorts Jason's group to the cloud house (Book 7, Ch53 L102); Jason tells him to hear his debrief (Book 7, Ch35 L72); stands with Liara when Zara confronts them (Book 7, Ch46 L81).

## 7. Relationships

- **Vesper Rimaros**: the royal who gives him leave to keep his sword (Book 7, Ch21 L21-25).
