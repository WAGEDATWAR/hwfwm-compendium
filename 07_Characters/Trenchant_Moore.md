## 1. Summary

A gold-rank human sword specialist of the Sapphire Crown royal guard who escorts Jason's group in Rimaros.

## 2. Facts

- A human in a guild of mostly celestines, pale-skinned and dark-haired, lean and angular with icy blue eyes, gold rank (Book 7, Ch21 L13-14).
- A sword specialist who has served as a guard on and off for decades; the royal family does not shield the Sapphire Crown from surge quotas (Book 7, Ch21 L15-18).
- Summoned to a low-key palace meeting where Vesper Rimaros lets him keep his sword (Book 7, Ch21 L21-25).
- He boards ships checking society contracts (Book 7, Ch22 L93), escorts Jason's group to the cloud house (Book 7, Ch53 L102) and warns that Vesper's world is political (Book 7, Ch58 L53).
- Jason tells Trenchant to hear his debrief (Book 7, Ch35 L72); Trenchant stands with Liara when Zara confronts them (Book 7, Ch46 L81).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Watchful and reserved; he reads places and people for danger.
