## 1. Summary

Tera Jun Casta is a branded messenger who challenges Jason to a duel in Yaresh, is knocked out when Jason strips her seal and later works through the destruction of her sense of self. She ends as a member of the freed messenger community living with the Asano clan.

## 2. Appearance

She and Marek wear simple leather armour of magical synthetic instead of the diaphanous clothing messengers favour (Book 10, Ch2 L18).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Zealous**: "I am Tera Jun Casta... I will be the one to kill you" (Book 9, Ch100 L19).
- **Rigid and devout**: a "zealous follower of the messenger orthodoxy before her forced liberation" (Book 11, Ch67 L12).
- **Duellist by temperament**: she chooses weapons that instil the most fear (Book 9, Ch100 L69).
- **Guarded**: she listens to Jason's story "without comment or expression, but her aura was a calamity of emotions" (Book 13, Ch973 L85).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- She fights comfortably with her abilities suppressed, in an orthodox style (Book 9, Ch100 L49).
- Her seal keeps her at silver rank; Jason removes it by working inside her soul (Book 9, Ch102 L54-67).
- A messenger unable to advance beyond silver is a loose addition to the raid forces (Book 10, Ch1 L17).

## 5. Background

- Her soul is branded and she was raised on lies and exploited her entire life (Book 10, Ch42 L7).
- Her old friend from "their days of enslavement" is Jali Corrik Fen (Book 13, Ch973 L85).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 9**: duel with Jason in a ruined tavern and dormitory (Book 9, Ch100 L49-72; Book 9, Ch101 L12-23); stripped of her seal (Book 9, Ch102 L54-67).
- **Book 10**: Marek sits with her (Book 10, Ch2 L20); the astral king punishes the emotions she held back (Book 10, Ch52 L67).
- **Book 11-13**: Jali's only friend (Book 11, Ch68 L2480); at the kitchen table with Jason, Boris and Jali (Book 13, Ch973 L85).

## 7. Relationships

- **Jali Corrik Fen**: old friend (Book 11, Ch68 L2480).
- **Marek Nior Vargas**: her commander (Book 10, Ch1 L17).
- **Jason**: freed her against her will (Book 11, Ch67 L12).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

Inflicting soul torture on her was the only way Jason could win the death match she forced on him (Book 11, Ch67 L12).

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Rigid, devout and furious at being freed; stoic on the surface. Cue: "I will be the one to kill you."
