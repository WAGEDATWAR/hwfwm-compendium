## 1. Summary

A branded messenger who challenges Jason to a duel and later has her seal removed.

## 2. Facts

- "I am Tera Jun Casta... I will be the one to kill you" (Book 9, Ch100 L19); fights comfortably with her abilities suppressed, in a practised, orthodox style (Book 9, Ch100 L49).
- Her seal keeps her at silver rank; Jason removes it by working inside her soul (Book 9, Ch102 L54-67).
- Her soul is branded and she was raised on lies (Book 10, Ch42 L7).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Rigid, devout and furious at being freed.
