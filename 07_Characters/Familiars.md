## 1. Summary

A familiar is a purely magical creature from the deep astral given a body by a summoning (Clive, Book 1, Ch69 L85-107). Jason has three, each very different, and Humphrey has one.

## 2. Shade

- **Who**: a shadowy figure who appears in Book 2 as the entity that governs the trials of the City of Fallen Echoes (Book 2, Ch44 L37). He was the summoned familiar of the Order of the Reaper's last grandmaster, an outworlder with the dark essence, before he became Jason's (Book 2, Ch44 L93). Book 11 identifies him as one of the Reaper's shadows (Book 11, Ch68 L2198); the wiki calls him the Reaper's shadow (wiki).
- **Powers**: Jason's gold-rank sheet lists Shadow of the Hegemon as three bodies at iron, seven at bronze (with minor physical force and dimensional storage), thirty-one at silver and two hundred and eleven at gold (Book 12, Ch67 L229-232); the notes' detail of incorporeal bodies hiding in other people's shadows and draining mana by touch is from notes, Book 2. On reaching silver any shadow body can teleport to any other (Book 5, Ch16 L103-108), and the bodies can form vehicles (Book 5, Ch16 L103-108).
- **Incantation**: Jason's summoning chant begins "I call to the realm beyond cold and darkness, where death has no meaning, for life has no place. Let mine be the dark beyond darkness..." (Book 2, Ch72 L15).
- **Evolution (Level 3)**: "Shadow of the Reaper [Shade] has sanctioned himself" and becomes a Shadow of the Hegemon (Book 11, Ch68 L2209-2214). His brother Umber is the enemy who plants a ritual to trap Jason's consciousness (Book 13, Ch1024 L127-130).

## 3. Colin

- **Who**: an apocalypse leech swarm. Jason names him in Book 1: "I've decided to call him Colin. And his friends. Team Colin." (Book 1, Ch30 L85). He is summoned using an awakening stone looted from a sanguine horror (Book 10, Ch33 L109, on the Flesh Pit in his soul realm: "I looted the awakening stone that let me summon Colin") and lives in Jason's blood: Jason pricks his finger and lets him "melt back into his bloodstream" (Book 1, Ch30 L89).
- **Evolutions**: a blood clone, four red-eyed copies of Jason, by Book 13 (Book 13, Ch958 L25; the Book 5 date is from notes); in Book 9 Colin can awaken as a Voice of the Will after extended hibernation in the astral kingdom (Book 9, Ch69 L55-59).
- **Origin**: Colin comes from the border region between the realms of the All-Devouring Eye and Legion, Legion being the great astral being who administers the cosmic rules of life (Book 8, Ch55 L92-93).
- **Manner**: eager to eat: "I'm definitely not going to eat that. Next time, you should fight the god of blood or flesh. I'll tuck right in there" (Book 11, Ch33 L50).

## 4. Gordon

- **Who**: Jason's third familiar, named by him in Book 2 (Book 2, Ch75 L75-79). A disembodied dark cloak with a nebula in the shape of an eye in the chest, with blue and orange orbs "drifting around" (Book 3, Ch4 L28-32).
- **Powers**: two floating orbs fire sustained beams: an orange beam of resonating-force damage that penetrates armour and a blue beam of disruptive-force damage that works against magical protection and incorporeal enemies (Book 2, Ch75 L81-84). His orbs also deliver the Harbinger of Doom affliction (Book 5, Ch31 L187) and create shield orbs (Book 5, Ch31 L187). In Book 9 he draws a massive magical orrery (Book 9, Ch94 L24-26).
- **Origin**: Gordon comes from the realm of the All-Devouring Eye, as the messenger Carmen says in Book 8 (Book 8, Ch55 L92-93; wiki).

## 5. Stash

- **Who**: Humphrey Geller's familiar, whose full name is Velitraxistaasch; "He likes 'Stash,' though" (Book 1, Ch38 L58). A mirage dragon (Book 2, Ch20 L29) who takes small forms: a mouse in Humphrey's pocket, a bird on Jason's shoulder and a puppy (Book 1, Ch78 L84-85; Book 2, Ch62 L50).
- **Team Biscuit**: in Book 1 Jason feeds Stash biscuits (the chapter is titled "Jason Has the Good Biscuits", Book 1, Ch78). The team's name was picked by a familiar: "Did they let one of their familiars pick the name?" "Actually, yes, they did" (Book 10, Ch47 L78); the wiki says Stash submitted it to the Adventure Society while the team discussed alternatives (wiki).
- **Special attack**: Humphrey's racial ability evolves into Attack of the Mirage Dragon (Book 2, Ch61 L147).

## 6. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Shade is butler-formal (Book 8, Ch50 L82; Book 11, Ch36 L43); Stash barks like a dog while a lizard (Book 1, Ch78 L84). See `../10_Roleplay_Support/Tone_and_Voice.md`.

- Shade: a formal, shadowy presence; Jason talks with him as an equal.
- Colin: hungry, direct.
- Gordon: a silent nebula-eyed cloak whose orbs speak for him.
- Stash: a small, shapeshifting dragon who likes biscuits.
