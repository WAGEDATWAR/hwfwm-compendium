## 1. Summary

Cressida Vane is the matriarch of the blood-cult family that holds Jason and his friends in Book 1. Beautiful, haughty and weary of her family's ambitions, she insists they are "a nice, traditional blood cult family" and her body is last seen on the chamber floor after the confrontation.

## 2. Appearance

- A young-looking woman in her early thirties with olive skin and dark hair, "no older than her son Landemere" (Book 1, Ch11 L65). Standing next to the ordinary-looking high priest Darryl, she is "the beautiful Cressida Vane" (Book 1, Ch14 L17).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Haughty**: "I am Lady Cressida Vane" (Book 1, Ch11 L80); Jason mocks her high-priestess name (Book 1, Ch11 L81-83).
- **Traditional**: "This is my manor, my family, and we do things the traditional way" (Book 1, Ch9 L54).
- **Dismissive of her husband**: "I don't care what my stupid husband thinks; he married into the family" (Book 1, Ch9 L54).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Leader of the blood cult family's rituals (Book 1, Ch9 L47-54).
- Complained that astral beings were "the death" of her son (Book 1, Ch9 L47-50).

## 5. Background

- Mother of Landemere Vane (Book 1, Ch11 L65); she asks what Landemere was trying to summon (Book 1, Ch9 L47).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: the basement scene (Book 1, Ch9 L47-54; Book 1, Ch11 L65-83); her body lands on the chamber floor, not the pool (Book 1, Ch13 L55); Jason reflects on her (Book 1, Ch14 L17).
- **Book 2**: Jason asks Clive what she said about her son (Book 2, Ch37 L78).

## 7. Relationships

- **Landemere**: son (Book 1, Ch11 L65).
- **Darryl**: the high priest beside her (Book 1, Ch14 L17).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

Her body ends up on the stone floor at the edge of the chamber rather than in the pool (Book 1, Ch13 L55).
