## 1. Summary

Marek Nior Vargas is a gold-rank messenger commander who leads the breach force over Yaresh's entertainment district in Book 9. Held captive inside Jason's astral kingdom, he slowly questions the indoctrination every messenger undergoes and becomes the leader of the free messengers.

## 2. Appearance

Nine-foot messenger with wings (Book 9, Ch87 L31); he and Tera wear synthetic leather armour rather than the diaphanous clothes messengers favour (Book 10, Ch2 L18).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Arrogant, then honest**: confident in his superiority over "servant races" (Book 9, Ch87 L34), but he learns that "when pushed, even the least of sapient species would push back" (Book 9, Ch86 L17).
- **Respects craft**: he appreciates "a power used well over one that was mindlessly strong" (Book 9, Ch86 L22) and wants to face Humphrey in battle (Book 9, Ch89 L3).
- **Disaffected from the Voice**: he hates Jes Fin Kaal and avoids politics (Book 9, Ch96 L32).
- **Quietly longing for freedom**: freedom from astral kings "was what he had always hoped for and never believed possible" (Book 10, Ch2 L20).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Gold-rank commander (Book 9, Ch96 L32); he blocks a projectile with his wing without effort (Book 9, Ch87 L31).
- He spent far longer than Tera's lifetime working free of the conditioning every messenger goes through (Book 10, Ch2 L20).

## 5. Background

- Messengers' soul spaces are blank concrete utilitarian landscapes; Marek compares them with Jali's vibrant realm (Book 10, Ch55 L12).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 9**: leads the breach force (Book 9, Ch86 L16); worries as Humphrey plunges into the monster torrent (Book 9, Ch87 L35); Schaat sees him as a potential rival (Book 9, Ch96 L32); recognises Jason as Asano (Book 9, Ch97 L51); sits with Payan Nior Roel and pauses at Tera's fate (Book 9, Ch103 L56; Book 9, Ch104 L3).
- **Book 10**: waits in Jason's kingdom near the cocoon dome (Book 10, Ch1 L49); fears the leonid prisoner (Book 10, Ch1 L50); Jason says "Your kind are easy to hate" (Book 10, Ch27 L82); leads the messenger prisoners (Book 10, Ch42 L4).
- **Book 11**: involved in the Undeath siege (Book 11 mentions 46).

## 7. Relationships

- **Tera Jun Casta**: young messenger whose identity rests on the indoctrination; Marek sits beside her with concern (Book 10, Ch2 L20-21).
- **Jason**: his captor; Marek senses Jason looks "for reasons not to kill them" (Book 10, Ch4 L9).
- **Jes Fin Kaal**: he hates the Voice (Book 9, Ch96 L32).
- **Humphrey**: a respected enemy (Book 9, Ch89 L3).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

He notes that knowing messengers can survive without an astral king will be a revelation (Book 9, Ch103 L114).

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Proud, dry and dutiful on the surface; adaptable, honest once his certainties break. Cue: "Freedom from astral kings was what he had always hoped for."
