## 1. Summary

A messenger who leads the breach force over Yaresh's entertainment district and later a free messenger.

## 2. Facts

- Leads the breach force at Yaresh (Book 9, Ch86 L16); absently blocks a gold adventurer's projectile with his wing (Book 9, Ch87 L31).
- Held in Jason's astral realm, he talks with Payan Nior Roel (Book 9, Ch104 L3) and says that knowing messengers can survive without an astral king will be a revelation (Book 9, Ch103 L114).
- Leads Jason's messenger prisoners and plans their uprising (Book 10, Ch42 L4).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Proud, adaptable and honest once his certainties break.
