## 1. Summary

Liara Rimaros's husband and their three silver-rank children.

## 2. Facts

- Baseph is Liara's husband, silver rank, met leaving her townhouse (Book 7, Ch17 L20). In Book 8 he hammers a pipe to draw raiders (Book 8, Ch33 L64), fears what lies below because it is people who died because of what he did (Book 8, Ch37 L77) and is saved by Jason (Book 8, Ch62 L51). He holds Liara's hand under the table (Book 8, Ch63 L3).
- Dara is the eldest, a one-star adventurer happy to hunt monsters, with Liara's height and Baseph's dark copper hair and eyes, a muscular frontline combatant (Book 8, Ch62 L39).
- Zareen is the middle child, physically smaller, with the royal family's sapphire hair and eyes, a politics enthusiast close to Vesper and a three-star adventurer (Book 8, Ch62 L36).
- Joseph is the youngest and only son, an administrative official with the Amouz family business interests like his father (Book 8, Ch62 L32); he wants to meet the man who saved his father (Book 8, Ch62 L51).
- Dara's later remark about "the one from up north" with the elf twins refers to Rick (Book 9, Ch11 L60).

## 3. Roleplay notes

A warm family dinner: Zareen sharp and strategic, Dara blunt and cheerful, Joseph mild.
