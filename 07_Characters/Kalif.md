## 1. Summary

Kalif is a member of Team Storm Shredder who has "bad blood" with Jason from their first meeting, and who ends up on his side in the war against the undead and messengers. He is blunt, sulky and loyal to his team leader Korinne.

## 2. Facts

- **Hot-headed**: Korinne tells her "still-angry team member" to let go of a quarrel, "You don't want to make an enemy of the next Amos Pensinata" (Book 7, Ch47 L59).
- **History with Jason**: he first met Jason "at a bad time", at the height of Jason's volatility (Book 9, Ch24 L50). They worked together on the underwater rescue under Korinne, after which Kalif "wanted nothing more to do with Asano" and was relieved he would leave Rimaros (Book 9, Ch24 L52-55). He then learned their team was going with him.
- **Agreeing to go**: "Of course we go. That doesn't mean we go blindly, and you know that Asano and I have bad blood" (Book 9, Ch24 L58). He eventually shakes Jason's hand (Book 9, Ch24 L64).
- **Team Storm Shredder**: he names the team to Jason (Book 9, Ch26 L69) and thinks being watched by Jason's guard dog is "unsettling. Makes it hard to relax" (Book 9, Ch26 L109).
- **Driving**: left behind to drive the team's new vehicle, he is "starting to get a handle on it" and has hit few other craft (Book 9, Ch29 L15).
- **Vitesse approach**: he sums up Humphrey's idea as "the Vitesse approach, but scaled up" (Book 9, Ch34 L93).
- **Complaints**: he grumbles that his team will work for two weeks while Jason is "knocking boots with the cute pink-haired woman" (Book 9, Ch42 L45).
- **Opinions on afflictions**: he says of area specialists, "No iron-rank monster is tough enough to make afflictions worthwhile" (Book 9, Ch53 L108).
- **Cardinas**: in the siege of Cardinas he asks "There's ten of us. How many undead are out there?" and "Shouldn't we have brought more people?" (Book 10, Ch90 L5-9). In Book 11 he commands silver-rankers with the cult and brighthearts (Book 11, Ch4 L21).
- **Wanting to rest**: after the surge he and others argue they should "loosen up and enjoy what we've earned" (Book 9, Ch28 L103-106).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Prickly, practical and honest about his dislikes; he follows orders without enjoying them and warms up slowly.
