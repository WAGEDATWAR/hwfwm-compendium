## 1. Summary

A swordmaster prince of the Kingdom of Mirrors whom Clive meets in the City of Fallen Echoes; he is cheerful, reckless and a rival and friend to Jason's team.

## 2. Facts

- Introduces himself as "Valdis" and praises Clive's reputation (Book 2, Ch48 L77); the system names him Valdis Volaire (Book 2, Ch51 L56).
- A prince of the small but influential Kingdom of Mirrors, whose borders have not changed in eight centuries (Book 2, Ch51 L8); his father, the Mirror King, is eight hundred years old and the kingdom is "thick with princes and princesses" (Book 2, Ch55 L63; Book 12, Ch15 L9).
- A reckless enthusiast who leads his three teammates up a tower in search of treasure (Book 2, Ch51 L9-10); he has a light-step power, a slow-fall power and a wall run (Book 2, Ch51 L17, L28, L32).
- He chooses the path of courage in the trial and is the first to step forward (Book 2, Ch51 L42; Book 2, Ch57 L63).
- A sword master, mobile striker and the stronger team's leader (Book 3, Ch4 L3, L7); his team is a highly efficient cohort trained since before they had essences (Book 3, Ch3 L80).
- His father collects him from Jason's ship, to Valdis's embarrassment (Book 3, Ch5 L42-50); he portals in for Jason's memorial (Book 3, Ch69 L895).
- By Book 12 he is Sigrid's husband (Book 12, Ch15 L8), assembles an arena event in under two days (Book 12, Ch16 L11), and fights Jason in a mirage chamber using Mind's Eye to sense the space around him (Book 12, Ch17 L55-73).

## 3. Timeline of involvement

- Book 2: Clive's team-mate in the City of Fallen Echoes; party and trials (Book 2, Ch48-61).
- Book 3: friendly rival team; mock battles (Book 3, Ch4; Book 5, Ch64 L24).
- Book 12: Mirror Kingdom arena duel with Jason (Book 12, Ch15-17).

## 4. Roleplay notes

Boisterous, flirtatious and fond of a good story ("the virile verve of ten men", Book 2, Ch61 L74); he loves duelling famous warriors for the push, not the win (Book 12, Ch17 L56).
