## 1. Summary

Boris Ket Lundi, who goes by Boris Ketland on Earth, is a messenger from Earth who is secretly one of the Unorthodoxy. He appears in Book 10, helps Jason's alliance in Cardinas, takes Rufus and Taika to Earth in Book 11, and is a leader of the Cabal in Book 13.

## 2. Background and nature

- A messenger with no indoctrination, from Earth, who says "I'm from Earth, Asano" and that he has been on Earth "for a lot longer than you" (Book 11, Ch31 L69, L87); the television detail is from notes, Book 10. Unorthodoxy messengers hid on Earth since before hominids; Earth was chosen because of the Builder sanction scandal; the Council of Kings avoids it (Book 13, Ch954 L7-12).
- He is Unorthodoxy secretly (Book 11, Ch24 L69, L85), serving under Vesta Carmis Zell, who hired him for his elemental powers; what she knows of astral kings outside the Council is from notes, Book 11.
- The Cabal's "angelic founders" were messengers (Book 13, Ch954 L4), and a database lists Boris Ketland as a Cabal executive (Book 11, Ch68 L481); that he is a founder is from notes, Book 10. In Book 13 he contemplates events from the Cabal's main headquarters in what was the Democratic Republic of the Congo (Book 13, Ch954 L20).
- He worked with the founder's familiar to betray the Network's founder to the early societies (Book 13, Ch954 L9).
- Messengers can hide their wings and shrink their size (Book 13, Ch1000 L3-7); Boris has long been comfortable with human proportions and once spent several millennia as a celestine research assistant (Book 13, Ch954 L5-6).

## 3. Powers

Elemental powers (notes, Book 11); he can take two people with him when he returns to Earth (Book 11, Ch68 L305); he draws ritual diagrams with Clive and Belinda to carry Rufus and Taika by egg-shaped conjured vessels (Book 11, Ch68 L305).

## 4. Timeline

- **Book 10**: Gabriel and Arabelle Remore meet him at the death-chamber gate in Cardinas, where he gives his real name as Boris Ket Lundi (Book 10, Ch79 L44-54).
- **Book 11**: confronts Mahk Den Kahla; reveals himself as Unorthodoxy; takes Rufus and Taika to Earth.
- **Book 13**: leads Unorthodoxy messengers and the Cabal; tells that within a decade the bridge becomes a true passage.
