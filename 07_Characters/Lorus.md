## 1. Summary

Lorus is a fire-aspect brightheart who claims a territory in the unified battlefield and wants all the power. Ambitious, impatient and proud, he refuses to hand over his territory, is out-argued by Neil and Durrum, and loses his followers' support.

## 2. Facts

- **Fire aspect**: "Lorus, a fire-aspect brightheart whose messengers were likewise possessed of flame powers", each shrouded in steam as they evaporated the rain (Book 11, Ch10 L7).
- **Aggressive**: he pushes aggressively "in spite of casualties" while Durrum is more conservative (Book 11, Ch10 L8), and he has no intention of stopping his offensive against the cult (Book 11, Ch10 L12).
- **Betrayal charge**: Durrum shouts "Are you betraying us?" and "You're killing us" (Book 11, Ch10 L16-20).
- **Defiance of Neil**: the brighthearts around him yell that they would never let the undead go (Book 11, Ch10 L49), and Lorus grandstands once Neil has left the cage (Book 11, Ch11 L7).
- **His case**: "I will not hand over my power on their say-so. Those cultist abominations invaded our city. They were the beginning of the end for our entire people!" (Book 11, Ch11 L31).
- **Power grab**: "Then give the territories to me. Once everyone actually obeys, I will make us stronger as we go... Our messengers are stronger than theirs" (Book 11, Ch11 L53).
- **Neil's read**: Neil gambles that Lorus is "a volatile mix of ambition, impatience and pride, with an unearned sense of entitlement" (Book 11, Ch11 L67), and says his actions show he likes "having command of all these messengers" (Book 11, Ch11 L64).
- **Losing support**: Lorus looks around and finds even his followers will not meet his eyes, "their faces showing disgust, disdain or disappointment" (Book 11, Ch11 L71-73).
- **Last appeal**: "Durrum. Don't let this outsider lead you to disaster. Without enough messengers..." (Book 11, Ch11 L80). Durrum answers that he has never been content with what he has (Book 11, Ch11 L81).
- **Outcome**: Durrum accepts Lorus's territory (Book 11, Ch11 L86).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Loud and sure of himself, with grievances that are real and a need to be seen as the strong one. He fights bravely and bargains badly.
