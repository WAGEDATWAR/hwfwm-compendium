## 1. Summary

Craig Vermillion is a vampire of seventy years' standing and the Cabal's front man in Sydney. He explains the magical world to Jason, becomes his friend and political counsel, and rises to gold rank by feeding on risen vampire lords in the war against them. He plays up the mysterious vampire act only for the "normals".

## 2. Appearance

- A vampire who drives a 1967 Maserati Ghibli (Book 4, Ch66 L64).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Casual behind the mystique**: "Craig is fine when I'm not on the job. I have to play it sinister and mysterious around the normals" (Book 4, Ch10 L63).
- **Helpful**: Jason thanks him for the information (Book 4, Ch11 L124).
- **Wry**: "I say roll with it... You built this place for melodrama, right?" (Book 12, Ch58 L97).
- **Troubled**: he worries about the sane vampires' future (Book 12, Ch58 L103).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Vampire for seventy years (Book 4, Ch11 L4); every aura in the room trembles at the name Vermillion (Book 4, Ch7 L49-50).
- By Book 12 he has accelerated to gold rank by feeding on several risen vampire lords, though he lacked the power to defeat a lord himself (Book 12, Ch58 L89-90).
- Jason developed his blend-into-crowds technique by studying Craig's aura (Book 10, Ch7 L5).
