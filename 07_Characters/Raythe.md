## 1. Summary

A prime vessel of the Keeper of Moments who steps in on Jason's behalf.

## 2. Facts

- In Yaresh she answers a challenge: "I am Raythe, and I have told you as much of my intentions as you need to know. Leave or die" (Book 9, Ch97 L80).
- She asks whether the Reaper did not tell Velius why they have come (Book 11, Ch22 L12); Raythe and Velius are the peak diamond-rankers from beyond Pallimustus (Book 11, Ch61 L8).
- "Lady Raythe is, indeed, the prime vessel of the Keeper of Moments" (Book 11, Ch64 L9); she tells Jason that only the nameless will stand with the World-Phoenix against him (Book 11, Ch65 L55).
- After the great beings leave, Raythe alone remains; the time there is synchronised with Jason's universe while the castle stays connected (Book 12, Ch1 L5-11); she tells Jason he will see her again (Book 12, Ch2 L9).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Calm, precise and amused; she says little more than the situation requires.
