## 1. Summary

Raythe is the prime vessel of the Keeper of Moments, a peak diamond-ranker from beyond Pallimustus who steps in on Jason's behalf in Yaresh and stays after the great beings leave.

## 2. Appearance

- An elf with dark brown skin and light brown hair flecked with green (Book 13, Ch991 L3).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Direct**: "I am Raythe, and I have told you as much of my intentions as you need to know. Leave or die" (Book 9, Ch97 L80).
- **Calm and amused**: "You shall see me again when it is done. Not too soon, as you have earned your rest" (Book 12, Ch2 L9).
- **Reluctant when needed**: she tells Jason with a straight face that the World-Phoenix will crawl into his soul, with reluctance in her expression (Book 11, Ch65 L97).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Prime vessel of the Keeper of Moments (Book 11, Ch64 L9); with Velius she leads the descent of peak diamond-rankers into the shaft (Book 11, Ch61 L8).
- Boris says the Keeper of Moments polices time manipulation harshly (Book 11, Ch63 L98).
- She tells Jason only the nameless will stand with the World-Phoenix against him (Book 11, Ch65 L55).

## 5. Background

- After the great beings leave, she alone remains; time there is synchronised with Jason's universe (Book 12, Ch1 L5-11).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 9**: in Yaresh she tells a challenger to "Leave or die" (Book 9, Ch97 L80).
- **Book 11**: asks if the Reaper did not tell Velius why they came (Book 11, Ch22 L12); the descent (Book 11, Ch61 L8-11); revealed as the Keeper's vessel (Book 11, Ch64 L9).
- **Book 12**: remains with Jason (Book 12, Ch1 L5-11; Book 12, Ch2 L9).
- **Book 13**: walks beside Jason through the empty tree city of Arbour (Book 13, Ch991 L3-42).

## 7. Relationships

- **Velius**: fellow peak diamond-ranker (Book 11, Ch61 L8).
- **Jason**: ally and counsel (Book 11, Ch65 L55).

Nothing hidden in the lines read.
