## 1. Summary

The expelled director of the Zurich Network branch who ran a secret programme working with Elizabeth and Simon McKean.

## 2. Facts

- Introduced sipping hot chocolate in a glass-walled chalet, answering a call as "Finnegan" (Book 13, Ch964 L3-6); she runs video conferencing with feeds from Pakistan and a grid system (Book 13, Ch964 L36).
- She is the recently expelled director of the Zurich Network branch, fleeing with Elizabeth (Book 13, Ch973 L49).
- The leadership council kept the vampire programme contained at her insistence; her contacts with Elizabeth and McKean were critical (Book 13, Ch975 L24). Her backers called using Elizabeth the smart choice (Book 13, Ch975 L22).
- She worked directly with Elizabeth, "intimately" according to Remy (Book 13, Ch979 L72).
- Jason takes her to talk with her and says he will hand her over to the Network afterwards (Book 13, Ch984 L34; Book 13, Ch985 L32-102).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Controlling and secretive; she thinks she is running a tight operation (Book 13, Ch979 L64).
