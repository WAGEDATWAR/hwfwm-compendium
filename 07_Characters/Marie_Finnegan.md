## 1. Summary

Marie Finnegan is the recently expelled director of the Zurich Network branch who ran a secret programme working with Elizabeth and Simon McKean. Controlling and secretive, she thinks she is running a tight operation; Jason takes her to talk and says he will hand her over to the Network afterwards.

## 5. Background

- The recently expelled director of the Zurich Network branch, fleeing with Elizabeth (Book 13, Ch973 L49).
- The leadership council kept the vampire programme contained at her insistence; her contacts with Elizabeth and McKean were critical (Book 13, Ch975 L24). Her backers called using Elizabeth the smart choice (Book 13, Ch975 L22).
- She worked directly with Elizabeth, "intimately" according to Remy (Book 13, Ch979 L72).
