## 1. Summary

The Network's Lyon Operations Director, who imprisons Farrah in the dimensional fortress, rises to be the second "Mr East" and later follows Gerling.

## 2. Facts

- Operations Director of the Network's Lyon branch, working out of the black site (Book 4, Ch43 L58); one of the secretive staff who knew of and worked in the dimensional fortress, and has been cultivating loyalists (Book 4, Ch51 L54).
- He leaves the dimensional space by another aperture just before it is sealed, alone, with no EOA and no prisoner (Book 4, Ch52 L94).
- In Book 5 he takes the seat of Mr East, speaking of "the previous Mr East" and of vetting his people (Book 5, Ch7 L96; Book 5, Ch8 L35). Mr North says his own minions were disappointing before Barbou came along (Book 5, Ch40 L86).
- Anna says Barbou is not hers to give; Farrah says "Barbou belongs to me" (Book 6, Ch26 L9-20).
- Gerling hits him and demands help reaching the Saint-Étienne dimensional space (Book 6, Ch30 L40-43). In the zone he is the only one not attacked by the soul attacks (Book 6, Ch42 L22) and Jason declines to take him (Book 6, Ch43 L75).
- In 2038 Farrah recalls him as the man behind her incarceration and tells Anna to retire the "bad apple" excuse (Book 13, Ch968 L34-38). Jason brought him out of the zone alive; Mr North asked for a quick death and Jason left the decision to Farrah, who describes disposing of bodies without denying what she did (Book 13, Ch968 L45-54), so Anna concludes "Barbou isn't alive" (Book 13, Ch968 L54). In Book 6 Anna did not know his fate (Book 6, Ch47 L46).

## 3. Timeline of involvement

- Book 4: dimensional fortress (Book 4, Ch43-52).
- Book 5: Mr East (Book 5, Ch7-8).
- Book 6: Gerling's prisoner and ally (Book 6, Ch26-43).
- Book 13: remembered by Farrah (Book 13, Ch968).

## 4. Roleplay notes

Calculating and self-protective; he stays at the edge of a fight and tries to look insignificant (Book 6, Ch42 L22).

## Open questions

- Farrah never says outright that she killed him; the scene implies it (Book 13, Ch968 L47-54).
