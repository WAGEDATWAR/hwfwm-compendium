## 1. Summary

Hana Shavar is the elf High Priestess of the Church of the Healer for Yaresh. She personally runs the camp for rescued refugees, oversees operations from Jason's cloud palace and goes with the gold-rank force to Cardinas. She is warm, devout and plain about danger.

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Hands-on**: she personally takes charge when people start arriving rescued from towns across the southern region (Book 9, Ch74 L43).
- **Assessing and unimpressed**: she runs "another assessing look over the infuriating man" and concludes the eyes are "the most honest thing in his appearance" (Book 9, Ch74 L71, L96).
- **Dissatisfied**: she looks Jason up and down before going back downstairs (Book 10, Ch3 L68).
- **Quiet in a tense room**: she is oddly quiet when Undeath priests are questioned (Book 10, Ch66 L84).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Gold rank: one of the remaining gold-rank participants with Amos and Carlos (Book 10, Ch59 L5).
- She senses enemy forces massing near the city walls (Book 9, Ch80 L43).

## 5. Background

- "The High Priestess of the Healer for the city-state of Yaresh" (Book 9, Ch80 L6).
- Her operations gradually changed Jason's cloud palace from a triage hospital into a larger base (Book 10, Ch3 L29).
