## 1. Summary

Knowledge is the goddess of Knowledge. Jason meets her in Book 1 on arriving at her temple, and for twelve books she charms and manipulates him. In Book 13 he learns she has prepared for half a millennium to invade Earth and has used him as a key part of the plan.

## 2. Appearance

In Book 1 a statue is replaced by a woman with colourful clothes and Mediterranean features, beautiful yet "detached and untouchable", not holding the statue's book (Book 1, Ch46 L16). She knows everything anyone in her world knows, including Jason, so she knows Mario Kart shortcuts (Book 1, Ch77 L10). A melodious voice behind Jason says "Such a shame. I was hoping to be your first" (Book 1, Ch45 L40).

## 3. Personality and voice

Warm, detached and omniscient; she answers questions honestly in a way that serves her (see the roleplay notes). In Book 1 she tells Jason "And you left. You have the same option here." (Book 1, Ch46 L15).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

She knows everything anyone in her world knows, including Jason (Book 1, Ch77 L10); she is a god, with a church that keeps libraries and checks assessments (see `../02_Cosmology_and_Powers/Gods_and_Churches.md`).

## 5. Background

Knowledge is the goddess of Knowledge (Book 1, Ch44 L68). Her temple answers questions for anyone who asks (Book 1, Ch44 L68).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: Jason meets her at her temple (Book 1, Ch46 L16).
- **Book 13**: in Jason's briefing the earliest he knows of her acting on the plan is around half a millennium ago (Book 13, Ch1017 L14).

## 7. Secrets and spoiler notes

The Book 13 briefing says she has been preparing to invade Earth for half a millennium and used Jason; that she promised the messengers' leader a chance to invade Pallimustus and claim Purity's relic; and that around a third of the priests and priestesses of Knowledge are her spies (Book 13, Ch1017 L14-20). She removes the divine essence of Gabrielle Pellin, a cube leaving Gabrielle's chest into her hand (Book 13, Ch1023 L139). Jason's last word to her: "The next time we meet, Goddess, will be the day you die" (Book 13, Ch1024 L153).
