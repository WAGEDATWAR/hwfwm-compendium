## 1. Summary

Emi Evans-Asano is Jason's niece, the daughter of Erika and Ian Evans, whom Jason calls "Moppet". She is twelve when he comes home in Book 4 (Book 4, Ch25 L60) and learns the truth about magic from him. She trains in ritual magic under Farrah, decides at thirteen that she does not want to fight as he does (Book 5, Ch53 L80), and takes her essences at about sixteen under Rufus (Book 11, Ch68 L940). By Book 13 she is an upper-silver ritualist and researcher, and Vincent Baudrillard's partner (Book 13, Ch998 L4; Book 13, Ch971 L49).

## 2. Facts

**Name and family**
- The System names her as the contact "Emi Evans-Asano" when Jason returns, and he senses her aura with Erika's and Ian's (Book 4, Ch3 L124, L132).
- Jason calls her "Moppet" (Book 5, Ch9 L23; Book 5, Ch53 L79; Book 12, Ch67 L149).
- Her father says she has always been hard to parent: "You thought you were smarter than me by age seven, and by age nine, you were right." (Book 11, Ch68 L841).
- Jason's remark on her at twelve is "twelve going on forty" (Book 4, Ch29 L103). Erika says Emi took his death hard and that Erika's own conspiracy-theory phase did not help (Book 4, Ch27 L171).
- Her cousins are Hana and Jace, Kaito and Amy's daughters (Book 4, Ch56 L46; Book 13, Ch950 L27); her great-grandmother Yumi is "Nana Yumi" to her (Book 11, Ch68 L836-837; Book 13, Ch979 L17).
- By Book 11 she is described as "the princess of the Asano clan" (Book 11, Ch68 L781).

**Book 4: Jason's return**
- At twelve she stands up to a group of boys picking on another student at Jason's old school, which Jason watches through Shade (Book 4, Ch25 L60-66).
- Jason promises Erika he will tell her everything (Book 4, Ch28 L126). Emi guesses something is wrong from how her parents are acting, throws herself into his arms when he appears, and her parents nod their agreement to let her go with him (Book 4, Ch28 L135-139, L154). He takes her for a drive, and she asks whether he is "the Starlight Angel" (Book 4, Ch29 L43). At a coastal lookout he asks "Do you believe in magic, Emi?", the car dissolves into his shadow, and she says "You're him" (Book 4, Ch29 L45-54).
- He takes her through a portal arch, her first dimensional translocation, and she endures it better than expected (Book 4, Ch29 L69-73).
- She asks Jason to help Nanna, who has Alzheimer's, and he says it looks like it worked (Book 4, Ch31 L25).
- She enjoys "having this big secret" (Book 4, Ch32 L4), is introduced to Shade as "Miss Emi" (Book 4, Ch32 L19), and has seen Jason's recordings (Book 4, Ch34 L14; Book 4, Ch57 L34-36).
- She vanishes her scooter into Shade's storage in front of two police officers (Book 4, Ch50 L53-62), and leads Farrah by the hand into the bar lounge when Farrah first arrives (Book 4, Ch57 L33-39).
- She asks to be Jason's assistant "when I get magic powers" (Book 4, Ch58 L75).
- Jason plans to take her out of school for intensive training (Book 4, Ch73 L77). At his farewell barbecue she is angry that he is leaving again, then runs back through the portal to hug him (Book 4, Ch74 L32, L145).

**Book 5: the compound and the choice**
- She sends recordings to and from Jason through Shade and is "especially excited" about her ritual magic lessons with Farrah, which take the sting out of not being old enough for essences (Book 5, Ch2 L116; Book 5, Ch3 L127).
- She shows Jason round the new family compound, pointing out the training facilities and the Network office (Book 5, Ch5 L59-68).
- She tells reporter Dennier his story was "an exercise in buffoonery" (Book 5, Ch24 L4). Erika sends her to Coffs Harbour to make sure no monster waves arrive, and Jason jokes about her "dimensional membrane protrusion-precursor analysis" (Book 5, Ch24 L12-16).
- Jason wants to take her with him on more of his work so she is prepared for the other world. Erika is unwilling to put her thirteen-year-old daughter near that (Book 5, Ch31 L134-135).
- She flies with the family to Japan and goes down the sea-cave shaft with Jason (Book 5, Ch34 L3; Book 5, Ch40 L3-18).
- The essences Jason picked for her were chosen to keep her safe, and she would not be ready for essences for about a year (Book 5, Ch43 L85-90).
- After the death of her uncle Kaito ("Uncle Kai", Book 5, Ch51 L33), she tells Jason she does not want to kill things or be an adventurer, and he says he is not disappointed (Book 5, Ch53 L80-85). She adds that he took her out of school for training (Book 5, Ch53 L86).
- She chooses a specialty, formation interactivity (the interference between nearby magical formations), though Farrah advises her to see the breadth of options first (Book 5, Ch54 L8, L13).
- In Book 6 she tells Dawn that almost everyone at the cloud palace is an Asano and Dawn should call him Jason (Book 6, Ch4 L65). Jason works at being "the cool uncle", which has not restored their old closeness, but she is less ill at ease (Book 6, Ch23 L31).

**Powers and skills**
- Ritual magic: before she had essences she had "greater command of ritual magic than most people on Earth" (Book 11, Ch68 L817). Travis names her and Hiro as the clan's people with strong magical-theory knowledge, taught by Farrah (Book 7, Ch55 L35).
- Essences: she picks Vast, Myriad and Harmonic, two legendaries and an epic, which she expects to produce the Unity confluence (Book 11, Ch68 L926-929). Rufus conducts the absorption rite (Book 11, Ch68 L940), and her confluence is Resonant (Book 11, Ch68 L1568).
- Combat rituals: she is "the one person on Earth able to use them", with stacked ritual-circle shields whose synergy magnifies each shield (Book 11, Ch68 L1695-1698). She uses Consolidate Mana to turn broken rituals into power for her next spell (Book 11, Ch68 L1702).
- Cube devices: nine cubes of shimmering dark metal from the outermost reaches of the astral space orbit her, each face divided into nine rune sections (Book 11, Ch68 L2343). They keep her and others aloft (Book 13, Ch950 L27), and she builds cube constructs with Clive and Gary (Book 13, Ch953 L84).
- Rank: she is bronze when she faces a silver-rank manticore with her team (Book 11, Ch68 L1698), silver by the time of the rooftop scene (Book 11, Ch68 L2345) and still in the upper reaches of silver in Book 13, because she favours magic research over combat (Book 13, Ch998 L4).

**Book 11: the clan and the rite**
- Once Jason and Farrah leave Earth, the family grows "strangely protective" and she goes from wizard back to teenager (Book 11, Ch68 L817). Only she receives one-on-one training from Rufus and Taika (Book 11, Ch68 L820).
- She moves to Saint-Étienne, where she befriends Lina Karadeniz, cousin of Asya, though the Karadeniz family blame Jason for Asya's death and politics strain the friendship (Book 11, Ch68 L828-832).
- Her parents refuse to let her fight a monster on her birthday. Erika wants her to use monster cores like her parents, and Ian suggests forty-eight as the age; Emi says she will not use cores (Book 11, Ch68 L909-913).
- Rufus tells her parents "Emi isn't an adventurer" and that she is too focused on fighting monsters at the cost of her studies, partly because she is running from her mother (Book 11, Ch68 L1619-1621). Her relationship with Erika is soured by her refusal to use cores (Book 11, Ch68 L1543).
- After her team's manticore fight, Rufus talks to her as a commanding officer would (Book 11, Ch68 L1714-1745).

**Books 12 and 13**
- Travis recalls that after Jason left, things were tense between Erika and Emi for a long time, and she blamed her mother and herself (Book 12, Ch58 L49).
- In the last scene of Book 12 she walks out of a bakery to find a portal arch, rams into Jason and he says "Hey, Moppet" (Book 12, Ch67 L146-149).
- She meets Zara and tells her about the recordings of Jason's first visit (Book 13, Ch956 L26, L56-58). She stands with Jason on a platform in a tree mansion in Arbour (Book 13, Ch960 L3-5).
- Vincent Baudrillard is her lover and Jason notes that she is a grown woman who does not need his protection (Book 13, Ch971 L49). Shade has looked after her since she was young (Book 13, Ch971 L30).
- She and Vincent have an apartment, and she explores the tower in Saint-Étienne with him (Book 13, Ch979 L7). She works the bar with Jason at a pop-up stand (Book 13, Ch972 L78-82), and she studies a completed ritual circle with Clive (Book 13, Ch979 L59). She waits beside Bussinger, floating just above the floor, as Bussinger wakes from the ritual (Book 13, Ch980 L73-77).
- When Nik reaches gold, she tells Jason on a rooftop that she is sorry she is not there yet (Book 13, Ch997 L11). She floats in meditation with Jason and Nik over the River Furan (Book 13, Ch998 L3-4).

## 3. Roleplay notes

- **Voice (condensed)**: sharp, literary and political at thirteen (Book 5, Ch24 L4); she can flip from a death glare to wide-eyed innocence (Book 5, Ch24 L9); she teases her uncle (Book 13, Ch972 L78).
- **Not a fighter by temperament**: she told Jason she did not want to kill things (Book 5, Ch53 L82), then ran toward monsters in Book 11 against her mother's wishes (Book 11, Ch68 L1621). Keep both pulls in the scene.
- **Her pressure points**: her mother's protectiveness, being treated as the clan's princess, and Jason's long absences (Book 12, Ch58 L49).
- **Check the era**: at twelve to thirteen she is a child with a wizard's knowledge; at fifteen to sixteen she is bronze; in Book 13 she is a silver ritualist in a relationship with Vincent.
