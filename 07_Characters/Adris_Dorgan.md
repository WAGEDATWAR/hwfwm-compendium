## 1. Summary

Adris Dorgan is an elf crime lord of Old City, the last of the Big Three, who keeps his daughter Elspeth Arella's parentage secret and seeks legitimacy. By Book 12 he is the mayor of Old City and a happy man.

## 2. Appearance

An elf with a library full of art, including a large desert painting (Book 2, Ch17 L98).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Patient and measured**: "Your mistake was your need to feel in control" (Book 2, Ch3 L4).
- **Protective father**: "A father's wish for his child to go further than he" (Book 1, Ch104 L88).
- **Quietly threatening**: "Don't forget who my daughter is" (Book 2, Ch75 L42).
- **Content with legitimacy**: "Once a powerful crime lord, he found that legitimacy sat very well with him" (Book 12, Ch29 L38).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- His aura senses are less trained than Thalia Mercer's (Book 1, Ch104 L65).
- He keeps his word, chasing down materials Clive listed (Book 2, Ch27 L4).

## 5. Background

- By Book 6 Humphrey says "Adris Dorgan's goal is legitimacy" (Book 6, Ch47 L398).
- Last of the Big Three crime lords, later mayor of Old City (Book 12, Ch27 L44).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: Jason meets him in his library (Book 1, Ch104 L65-88).
- **Book 2**: his daughter can now take tea in his courtyards openly (Book 2, Ch3 L3); he warns "Don't forget who my daughter is" in a private conversation (Book 2, Ch75 L42); he is chasing down the shipments on Clive's lists (Book 2, Ch27 L4).
- **Book 12**: Jason promises "Nothing will happen to your daughter from my people" (Book 12, Ch29 L48).

## 7. Relationships

- **Elspeth Arella**: daughter (Book 1, Ch104 L88).
- **Jason**: Jason tells him "Nothing will happen to your daughter from my people" (Book 12, Ch29 L48).
