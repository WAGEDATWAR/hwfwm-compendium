## 1. Summary

Nik is a four-foot humanoid rabbit in a top hat and tuxedo whom Jason brought into being, unplanned, when his soul realm became a transformation zone (Book 11, Ch61 L16). He curses constantly, is bleeped on the page (Book 11, Ch8 L102), and calls Jason "Dad" (Book 12, Ch39 L15). He is the control key to the zone's network (Book 11, Ch46 L32), trains at the Geller estate (Book 11, Ch68 L1034-1038), reaches gold rank (Book 13, Ch998 L4) and acts as a team's coordinator and overwatch through drones and mapping powers (Book 13, Ch966 L71).

## 2. Facts

**Origin and name**
- Nik first appears to Neil and Dustin in the soul realm as a "four-foot humanoid rabbit in a top hat and tuxedo", with swearing bleeped and a "bleeper" he does not recall being installed (Book 11, Ch8 L102-106).
- Jason names him in a forest: "Nik. NIK, no C", from "Lehenik", a word from the language of Jason's birth world meaning "first" or "firstly", and the name Jason would give a people of which Nik is the first instance (Book 11, Ch23 L42). Jason declines to name him after Kai and offers him the Asano surname if he wants it (Book 11, Ch23 L38).
- The transformation zone created him, with the Healer's gift erasing any flaws; Jason "unconsciously brought" him into being (Book 11, Ch61 L16).
- He says he is "six months old" and that most of what he knows is "random nonsense put in my head" (Book 11, Ch50 L15).
- He is "the key" to the network of control nodes Jason made while changing the zone, and the node's lock is broken (Book 11, Ch46 L30-33).

**Book 11: in the zone**
- He and one of Shade's bodies bring Jason's friends into the soul realm as "plan B" and lead them to the objective (Book 11, Ch36 L40-45; Book 11, Ch38 L3). He calls Boris "Two-Piece Feed" (Book 11, Ch36 L40-42).
- He attacks by making wind sounds with his mouth, and a small cloud appears (Book 11, Ch38 L33). He he runs the weather machine across the unified territory from a platform of control panels (Book 11, Ch50 L10-25).
- His head can pass through a portal while his body stays hundreds of kilometres away (Book 11, Ch53 L56). He learns a board game from Neil and suggests planting the tree in Jason's soul realm (Book 11, Ch57 L26-41).
- He is part of Jason's "core group of friends" in the tree city (Book 11, Ch67 L19).

**Training (Book 11 to Book 12)**
- After the zone, being normal rank among gods left him feeling "vulnerable and weak", and he trains at the Geller estate in Greenstone (Book 11, Ch68 L1034-1036).
- His combination is from Earth: the technology essence Jason brought, with Myriad and Vast, producing the Network confluence (Book 11, Ch68 L1037). Danielle Geller's introduction gets him into the family's most elite training programme, building a support set as the "central hub of a team" (Book 11, Ch68 L1038). He is bronze when he leaves Greenstone (Book 11, Ch68 L1039).
- He calls Neil "Uncle Neil" (Book 11, Ch68 L1082, L1104).

**Book 12: reunion**
- Neil and Nik are portalled in from the Mirror Kingdom with Team Shining Scabbard, led by Sigrid Freyn (Book 12, Ch15 L8).
- Jason estimates that Nik was "five, maybe" when he left (Book 12, Ch15 L45-47).
- Nik tells Jason: "Dad, you created me out of thin air like you were starting your own bible, sent me to violence boarding school and then vanished for a decade and a half" (Book 12, Ch39 L15). Jason promises to do better, warns him that Emi will hug him all week, and Nik asks him for a hug (Book 12, Ch39 L30-35).
- He travels on Jason's cloud ship (Book 12, Ch39 L3) and in Vitesse, where he points out the flowering towers (Book 12, Ch43 L46-50). At the sky port battle he offers to get the arriving groups "in line", saying he is used to jumping in with groups that do not know him (Book 12, Ch53 L92).
- His System ability: a raid group invitation notes that "the powers of [Nik Asano] interface with you" (Book 12, Ch54 L33-34).

**Book 13: Earth**
- He is nervous about meeting the Asano family, wishes Jason had come back (Book 13, Ch951 L4-11), and wants to find a team on Earth (Book 13, Ch951 L7).
- His mapping powers are more precise than Jason's tactical map, and he can relay powers and devices remotely (Book 13, Ch966 L71). He acts as overwatch through an earpiece (Book 13, Ch996 L5-9) and through a drone over briefings and the field (Book 13, Ch1007 L48; Book 13, Ch1011 L63).
- He tells Michael that Jason "created me" and complains he has not been given a girlfriend, saying he might be the last of his kind (Book 13, Ch1003 L67-72).
- He reaches gold rank, with a celebration party (Book 13, Ch997 L11, L23; Book 13, Ch998 L4). Sophie will teach him, Jason and Emi to use wingsuits (Book 13, Ch996 L181).
- After Jason destroys McKean's astral space, Nik takes over McKean's broadcast and keeps it going (Book 13, Ch1015 L48).

## 3. Roleplay notes

- **Voice (condensed)**: blunt and profane, with bleeped swearing and a love of pop-culture references such as Superman and Lois Lane (Book 11, Ch8 L102; Book 13, Ch1003 L72); fond of "Dad" and wary about being treated as a toy (Book 12, Ch39 L31).
- **What drives him**: wanting power that is his own rather than borrowed (Book 11, Ch68 L1036), and wanting Jason to stay (Book 13, Ch951 L11).
- **How he works in a group**: a support set built around being the hub of a team, with mapping and relays (Book 11, Ch68 L1038; Book 13, Ch966 L71).
- **Do not give him**: a canonical homeland, a mate or other rabbit-folk. The books leave the Lehenik as an intention, not a people (Book 11, Ch23 L42).
