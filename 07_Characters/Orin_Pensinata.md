## 1. Summary

A calm silver-rank guild adventurer, Amos Pensinata's nephew, who rises to gold and survives Team Storm Shredder's losses.

## 2. Facts

- A silver-ranker "most notable for his famous uncle, Amos", who nods to one of the princesses in the surge expedition (Book 7, Ch47 L39).
- Quiet, stable and respected in the guild team: he restrains an angry companion with "Complications. Best left alone" (Book 7, Ch47 L51-53, L72); he is the steadfast one, fresh from a rank-up (Book 7, Ch48 L7).
- His first meeting with Jason came through Vesper Rimaros's arranged meeting with Kasper Irios (Book 9, Ch25 L79).
- Orin and Kalif's team is led by Korinne Pescos; they first met Jason in a mixed-force expedition (Book 9, Ch24 L49). The team is Storm Shredder, built around buffed high-damage ranged specialists (Book 9, Ch84 L9; Book 10, Ch64 L9).
- He goes on Jason's journey because his uncle insisted, and he is slow-spoken ("My uncle is a hard man, but he likes soft beds", Book 9, Ch24 L35; Book 9, Ch28 L87-112).
- In Book 11 Storm Shredder loses two members; Orin is the only one strong enough to force Zara back from the fighting (Book 11, Ch32 L73-74). After the zone, Amos takes Orin and the scout Rosa back to Rimaros (Book 11, Ch68 L378).
- By Book 12 Orin has reached gold rank and is in a team with Kasper Irios and friends (Book 12, Ch12 L61); Hils Jaramaris's friend Orin who went on to travel with Asano is presumably him (Book 12, Ch49 L5).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Stoic and slow to speak; he keeps a team steady and says only what needs saying.
