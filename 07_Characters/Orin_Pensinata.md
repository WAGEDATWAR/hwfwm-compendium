## 1. Summary

Orin Pensinata is a calm guild adventurer, Amos Pensinata's nephew, who rises to gold and survives Team Storm Shredder's losses. Stoic and slow to speak, he keeps a team steady.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in the lines read.

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Impassive**: he stands apart while guilders tease the independents (Book 7, Ch47 L39).
- **Restraining**: "Complications. Best left alone" (Book 7, Ch47 L51-53, L72).
- **Steadfast**: the steady one, fresh from a rank-up (Book 7, Ch48 L7).
- **Intractable about coming along**: Jason had been reluctant to accept his team, but Orin had proven intractable (Book 9, Ch24 L35).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Silver-ranker most notable for his famous uncle (Book 7, Ch47 L39); gold by Book 12 (Book 12, Ch12 L61).
- Storm Shredder is built around buffed high-damage ranged specialists (Book 9, Ch84 L9; Book 10, Ch64 L9).

## 5. Background

- He goes on Jason's journey because his uncle insisted (Book 9, Ch28 L87-112).
- First met Jason through Vesper's arranged meeting with Kasper Irios (Book 9, Ch25 L79).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 7**: the surge expedition (Book 7, Ch47-48).
- **Book 9**: Storm Shredder with Korinne (Book 9, Ch24 L49).
- **Book 11**: Storm Shredder loses two members; Orin is the only one strong enough to force Zara back (Book 11, Ch32 L73-74); Amos takes Orin and Rosa back to Rimaros (Book 11, Ch68 L378).
- **Book 12**: reached gold on a team with Kasper Irios and friends (Book 12, Ch12 L61); a friend of Hils Jaramaris who went on to travel with Asano is presumably him (Book 12, Ch49 L5).

## 7. Relationships

- **Korinne**: team leader (Book 9, Ch24 L49).
- **Zara**: teammate (Book 11, Ch32 L74).
