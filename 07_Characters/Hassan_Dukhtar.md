## 1. Summary

A Pakistani doctor praised for courageous medical work in a vampire war zone, whose name Jason turns into a running joke.

## 2. Facts

- He introduces himself as Hassan Dukhtar; Jason hears "Handsome Doctor" and repeats it despite corrections (Book 13, Ch1016 L23-31).
- Jason praises his courageous medic work the week before in a vampire war zone, which saved many lives (Book 13, Ch1016 L31).
- He is a handsome Pakistani man at Rufus's tea house table with Clive and Lorelei (Book 13, Ch1016 L3).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Patient and earnest; he keeps correcting Jason's joke.
