## 1. Summary

Cole Silva is the newest of Old City's Big Three crime lords, who inherits his father's operation and, with Lucian Lamprey, orchestrates Jason's kidnapping and star seed implantation. Arrested in Book 3, his organisation falls into chaos.

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Rash**: Clarissa plans to provoke him "into the kind of rash action that his father always kept him from making" (Book 1, Ch50 L96).
- **Predatory**: his father's protection of Sophie is gone and he watches her match from a viewing box (Book 1, Ch50 L92).

## 5. Background

- His father had a small operation catering to nobles with low tastes, with the money and connections to arrange expensive cleansings (Book 3, Ch18 L128).
- The father died in his sleep "a couple of months ago"; the son stepped up (Book 1, Ch51 L37).
- Cole's father killed a man who tried to rob him (Book 2, Ch9 L60).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: watches Sophie's match (Book 1, Ch50 L92).
- **Book 2**: Jason is told he put himself "in the path of Cole Silva and Lucian Lamprey" (Book 2, Ch35 L97).
- **Book 3**: the elf Palpatine-to-Vader joke (Book 3, Ch17 L56-60); held in the prison tower with Lamprey (Book 3, Ch24 L48); his organisation fights over succession after his arrest (Book 3, Ch25 L3).
- **Book 9**: Jason's kidnapping and star seed implantation are traced to him and Lamprey (Book 9, Ch2 L84).
