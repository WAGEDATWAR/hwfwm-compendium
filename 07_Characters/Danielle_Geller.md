## 1. Summary

Danielle Geller is Humphrey's mother and the head of the Geller family's household in Greenstone. She is a silver-ranker at the start of the series who looks much younger than she is, later a gold-ranker with dimension and time powers, and Jason's political adviser on Earth.

## 2. Appearance

Silver-rank and ageless-looking: she seems about thirty and is fifty in fact (notes, Book 1); Jason sees the same agelessness in Emir and Thalia Mercer (Book 1, Ch108 L35-37). In Book 1 she "may have looked too young to be Humphrey's mother" (Book 1, Ch47 L87-89). In Book 7 she is gold-rank with dark olive skin and black hair (Book 7, Ch43 L61).

## 3. Personality and voice

She defends the family's methods (the Geller estate trains the whole family in low-magic Greenstone, notes, Book 1), gives blunt strategic advice ("Nothing speaks louder than power", notes, Book 3; "do the opposite of every instinct", notes, Book 12), and teases her son at once when she hears a name: "Gabrielle?", in a tone of having latched on to a weakness (Book 1, Ch47 L89). She is shown as a diplomacy teacher: "diplomacy is war" (Book 12, Ch23 L79).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- **Time**: a time confluence, with only forward time travel possible (Book 6, Ch14 L80-82); in Book 3 she outpaces Lucian Lamprey in a fight, teleporting behind him and cutting his neck (Book 3, Ch24 L23). She is called "Time Witch of Vitesse" (Book 12, Ch25 L7).
- **Dimension**: a dimension essence, as Emir says (Book 2, Ch5 L46), with gold rank from the notes (notes, Book 11); she teleports behind Lamprey in a fight (Book 3, Ch24 L23).
- **Estate and training**: the Geller estate's mirage chamber can run at bronze rank only "for limited periods" because Greenstone's magic is low, and at iron rank Danielle offers it to Rufus (Book 1, Ch37 L43-55); a family member from the generation that first settled the region may still be alive (Book 1, Ch37 L21). The diamond-rank ancestor in the earlier notes was not found.
- **Rank**: silver in Book 1, gold by Book 7.

## 5. Background

Mother of Humphrey and wife of Keith Geller (Book 2, Ch62 L24). The Geller family's wall is older than the Old City (Book 1, Ch37 L21).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: Gabriel Remore asks about "this outworlder of yours" in a call (Book 1, Ch49 L42); Danielle leads the search pattern on the Greenstone expedition into the desert astral space (Book 1, Ch104 L60-61), where Henry Geller dies (Book 1, Ch110 L51).
- **Book 3**: confronts Lamprey with Humphrey.
- **Book 7**: takes the 119 outworlders (Book 7, Ch36 L86) to the Geller estate in Cyrion (Book 7, Ch43 L61-62).
- **Book 11**: sits with Gwydion Remore, a priest of Hero, at her table (Book 11, Ch60 L13) and later helps Lorenn with diplomacy.
- **Book 12**: Jason asks her to be his political adviser on Earth (Book 12, Ch26 L54).

## 7. Relationships

Humphrey (son), Keith (husband), Rick and the Geller family, Jason (ally), Elspeth Arella.

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

Her gold rank and the Earth adviser role are levels 2-3.

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Blunt, quick to seize a weakness. Cues: "Gabrielle?", asked as if she has latched on to a weakness (Book 1, Ch47 L89); "I said that diplomacy is war, Jason, and wars have objectives" (Book 12, Ch23 L79). See `../10_Roleplay_Support/Tone_and_Voice.md`.

Crisp, strategic, protective of her family, fond of aphorisms about power.
