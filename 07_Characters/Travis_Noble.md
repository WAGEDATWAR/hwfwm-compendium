## 1. Summary

Travis Noble is a young Californian magitech weapons engineer who becomes Jason's outworlder weapons researcher on Pallimustus, marries the Knowledge priestess Gabrielle Pellin and, by Book 13, leads weapon development against the vampires. Earnest, happy and quick to adapt, he is the person who builds Earth's new arsenal.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in detail in the lines read; "goofy grin" when he watches his wife eat (Book 13, Ch1016 L74).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Overworked and decent**: "Travis Noble was twenty-one years old and a category two magitech weapons engineer from California. He was also having a very bad month" (Book 6, Ch28 L36).
- **Clear-sighted on Network faults**: "The Network has kidnapped Mr Asano twice, along with killing his friend, his girlfriend and his brother" (Book 6, Ch29 L52).
- **A researcher by temperament**: "an enthusiastic researcher whose expertise bled into" other fields (Book 10, Ch8 L6).
- **Devoted husband**: he is happy and worried watching his wife eat (Book 13, Ch1016 L74).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- **Magitech weapons engineer**: a specialist in large-scale weaponry (Book 10, Ch8 L6). His dimensional space is a holographic cabinet with a sliding door, from which he pulls an oversized high-tech revolver (Book 6, Ch28 L65).
- **Cross-world builder**: he works with Clive, Gary and Gabrielle on the tools and artifice side of magitech (Book 7, Ch60 L96).
- **Constructs**: he waves a rod and a mannequin-like construct walks out (Book 10, Ch30 L13).
- **Sky link**: his sky link service carries calls by Book 12 (Book 12, Ch53 L32).

## 5. Background

- Category-two Network engineer who becomes head of the Special Munition Stockpile Division when his supervisor goes AWOL, with a team of people who all hate him (Book 6, Ch28 L36-37).
- An outworlder from Earth to Pallimustus (Book 10, Ch8 L6).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 6**: Germany, Network base (Book 6, Ch28-29); he stays in France when Jason leaves (Book 6, Ch47 L155).
- **Book 7**: Greenstone and Rimaros; Jason takes him to the Church of Knowledge (Book 7, Ch56 L71); the battle seen from the cloud house (Book 7, Ch65 L44).
- **Book 9-10**: he joins Jason's circle (Book 9, Ch38 L21) and works on weapons for the siege (Book 10, Ch30).
- **Book 12-13**: he and Farrah own the island (Book 12, Ch56 L10); by Book 13 he is the lead researcher for the Joint Magitech Weapons Research Institute (Book 13, Ch995 L14).

## 7. Relationships

- **Gabrielle Pellin**: his wife, a priestess of Knowledge (Book 13, Ch1016 L74; Book 8, Ch6 L6).
- **Taika**: close friend (Book 7, Ch44 L61).
- **Clive and Gary**: collaborators (Book 7, Ch60 L96).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

His negotiations with the Church of Knowledge over what Earth magitech he may bring to Pallimustus are part of Knowledge's plan (Book 9, Ch13 L85).

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Enthusiastic, technical and polite; a worrier who gets his best work done in a crisis. Cue: his goofy grin at his wife.
