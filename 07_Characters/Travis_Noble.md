## 1. Summary

A young American magitech engineer who joins Jason and Farrah, becomes a diamond-ranker and co-builds the Sky Link Network.

## 2. Facts

- At twenty-one he is a category-two magitech weapons engineer from California who ends up in charge of the Special Munition Stockpile Division in Germany (Book 6, Ch28 L36-37).
- He works on the semi-disassembled atomic device with Jason and Farrah on a cold winter day (Book 6, Ch31 L4).
- In Book 7 he waits at a Geller country estate with Taika, complaining that most magitech is "heavy on the tech and light on the magic" (Book 7, Ch44 L61-62).
- Farrah says he is still working out of Rimaros on cloud flask research with House de Varco (Book 12, Ch12 L75) and that he introduced chosen pronouns to the gold- and diamond-rankers (Book 12, Ch12 L82).
- At the Book 12 reunion Travis, Constance and Emir share a table while Jason meets an angry Cloudweaver (Book 12, Ch14 L61-63).
- By Book 13 he is lead researcher of the Joint Magitech Weapons Research Institute (Book 13, Ch995 L14).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Wry, quick and a little overwhelmed; his engineering instincts keep up with gods and diamond-rankers.
