## 1. Summary

Remy is a portal specialist whose range is limited. In Book 4 he and Sebastian work for Adrien Barbou, hop across Australia with a rescued agent in Kakadu and kill her; in Book 5 he helps in Makassar; in Book 13 he is Marie Finnegan's portal man, caught between Jason and the vampire queen Elizabeth.

## 2. Facts

- **Kakadu**: he and Sebastian have taken Miranda (Ellis) out of Australia; her complaint that "Twelve hundred kilometres is the best you can do? And you have to wait an hour between portals?" draws "Let's see your portal power" (Book 4, Ch49 L3-4). They are in Kakadu National Park, which Remy is scornful of her not recognising (Book 4, Ch49 L7).
- **Range**: he says no essence user can portal sixteen thousand kilometres, and that the Chinese reportedly have someone near category four who can do a few thousand at a time (Book 4, Ch49 L9-10). He notes the rule that "You cannot portal where you have never been" (Book 4, Ch49 L84).
- **Barbou's men**: Miranda calls Remy a failure of "the great portal master Barbou promised" (Book 4, Ch49 L11). After a glance, Sebastian kills her with metal hummingbirds and a kick; Remy says "A personality like that is practically a suicide note" (Book 4, Ch49 L16-18). He reports to Adrien by phone (Book 4, Ch49 L19-26).
- **Book 5**: "Hi, I'm Remy. You two came in pretty hot", and he takes Jason and a companion through a portal to a command post near Paotere Harbour (Book 5, Ch26 L39-44).
- **Book 13: Finnegan's portal man**: he opens a portal "About time... If any gold-rankers turned up, I was just going to bail", is careful around "that guy", and follows Marie, Gerling and the blood orb through it (Book 13, Ch973 L73-82).
- **Hurt by Jason's field**: a teleport attempt leaves him "doubled over, coughing" in Book 13, after which the portal flickers like an old TV (Book 13, Ch975 L102). He recovers on a bus roof (Book 13, Ch975 L113; Book 13, Ch977 L48).
- **Old job**: he did a job for the Lyon branch "back when Asano had just returned to Earth the first time" and explains how a silver-rank was sent to kidnap Jason; Sebastian kicked her off a rock in Kakadu (Book 13, Ch977 L96-111).
- **Interrogated**: Jason reads a spike of panic in his aura when asked who he is protecting and tells him "Give me a name, Remy" (Book 13, Ch977 L118-125).
- **Finnegan and Elizabeth**: Jason learns from Remy that Marie has been working "intimately" with Elizabeth, the vampire queen increasingly on the outs with her subjects (Book 13, Ch979 L72).
- **Last scenes**: Remy scrambles through a portal with Elizabeth and Marie (Book 13, Ch984 L73) and wonders "What is this place?" at Elizabeth's refuge (Book 13, Ch985 L9). Elizabeth apologises for jabbing at his weakness (Book 13, Ch985 L18).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Dry, wary and mercenary: he is brave only as far as the exit and talks when cornered.
