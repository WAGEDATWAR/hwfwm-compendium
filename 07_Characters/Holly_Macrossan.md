## 1. Summary

Holly Macrossan succeeds Anna Tilden as Under-Secretary-General of the UN Office of Supernatural Affairs. After the position is lost she becomes Australia's liaison to the Multinational Supernatural Response Taskforce. Dry, careful and practical, she believes only what she can verify.

- **Sceptical**: she maintains a sceptical mindset despite two decades of magic (Book 13, Ch982 L30).
