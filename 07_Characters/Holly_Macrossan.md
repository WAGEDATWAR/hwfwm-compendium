## 1. Summary

Anna Tilden's successor at the UN Office of Supernatural Affairs, later liaison to the Multinational Supernatural Response Taskforce.

## 2. Facts

- Succeeded Annabeth Tilden as Under-Secretary-General of the United Nations Office of Supernatural Affairs (Book 13, Ch982 L30).
- Anna recommended her as successor, having kept an eye on her while Anna ran the Sydney branch (Book 13, Ch988 L52-54).
- After losing her UN position she took a post with the Australian government as "Liaison to the Multinational Supernatural Response Taskforce for the Department of Prime Minister and Cabinet", and sits in the Asano Village conference room (Book 13, Ch994 L39).
- She maintains a sceptical mindset despite two decades of magic (Book 13, Ch982 L30).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Dry, careful and practical; she believes only what she can verify.
