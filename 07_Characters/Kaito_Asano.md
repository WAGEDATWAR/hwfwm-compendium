## 1. Summary

Jason's brother, married to Amy; he is killed in Book 5.

## 2. Facts

- Lives in the house next door to his parents with his wife Amy (Book 4, Ch3 L128-133).
- Jason and Akari ride in "Kaito's helicopter" with a strike team in Book 5 (Book 5, Ch14 L37).
- Dies in Book 5. At the funeral Jason says he "died for something that truly matters. To give his children a future" (Book 5, Ch52 L20); Jason keeps the family in his spirit vault and cannot let them out for the service (Book 5, Ch53 L67).
- His daughters are Hana and Jace (Book 13, Ch950 L29). Jason's growing resemblance to his brother with each rank-up is something he stops begrudging after Kaito's death (Book 13, Ch950 L33); the mirror scene itself, where Jason sees the family resemblance after going up two ranks, is Book 4, Ch25 L44-45.

## 3. Roleplay notes

Warm, teasing and brave; a recurring ache for Jason.
