## 1. Summary

Kaito Asano is Jason's older brother, a year his senior, married to Amy, with two daughters, Hana and Jace. Easy-going and socially skilled, he is the "well-received original" Jason grew up in the shadow of. In Book 4 he reconnects with Jason, in Book 5 he flies a helicopter with Network defectors against the vampires, and he dies in Berlin.

## 2. Appearance

- Jason's face is more angular than his brother's; Kaito has a "signature easy-going smile" that Jason spent years learning to copy (Book 4, Ch25 L44).
- At a party he wears a pale suit and pastel shirt (Book 4, Ch26 L45).
- Jason's resemblance to him grows with each rank, until Jason stops begrudging it after Kaito's death (Book 13, Ch950 L33).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Socially adroit**: Jason says he never had "Kaito's skill for getting along" (Book 2, Ch11 L61).
- **The favourite**: their parents "got Kaito, so they were willing to put up with me" (Book 4, Ch7 L11).
- **Casual**: he greets the family meeting with "G'day... We're arrived for the mysterious family meeting" (Book 4, Ch27 L55).
- **Dry on pop culture**: his knowledge of classic pop culture "appeared to begin and end with Miami Vice" (Book 4, Ch26 L45).
- **Warm with his children**: he leads Hana by the hand and carries Jace (Book 4, Ch24 L101).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- After Jason's return he joins the Network-defector combat team with Asya and Greg (Book 5, Ch44 L17).
- He flies drones to sweep the ruins of a town (Book 5, Ch18 L83) and owns the helicopter Jason and Akari ride in (Book 5, Ch14 L37).

## 5. Background

- A year older than Jason; the "well-received original" at school, so Jason felt like "a disappointing rehash" (Book 2, Ch11 L65).
- He and Amy bought her parents' house next door to Jason's family (Book 4, Ch3 L133).
- He and Amy's relationship caused the fallout that left Jason dropping out of university; Jason did not want details (Book 4, Ch26 L47-50).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 4**: Jason sees his contact appear on the interface (Book 4, Ch3 L126) and sees the home he shares with Amy (Book 4, Ch3 L133); Erika and Kaito discuss Amy and Jason (Book 4, Ch24 L101-126); the family reunion (Book 4, Ch27 L55-70); the helicopter check with Erika (Book 4, Ch71 L62).
- **Book 5**: the strike team and helicopter crew (Book 5, Ch14 L37-49); dies in Berlin with Asya and Greg (Book 5, Ch51 L40); Jason at the funeral: "died for something that truly matters. To give his children a future" (Book 5, Ch52 L20).
- **Later**: Amy and the girls are Book 13 family (Book 13, Ch950 L29; Book 13, Ch1023 L33); his death is the grief behind Emi's uncle (Book 11, Ch68 L828).

## 7. Relationships

- **Jason**: brother; Jason says Kaito made his own choice to stand up for his world and that Amy knows it (Book 7, Ch1 L15).
- **Amy**: wife, with daughters Hana and Jace (Book 4, Ch24 L101; Book 13, Ch950 L29).
- **Erika**: sister who keeps the family together (Book 4, Ch71 L62).
- **Hiro**: his uncle, who spoke Japanese with his mother behind their father's back (Book 4, Ch34 L131).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

His death is why Amy's children grow up without a father "because he followed me" (Book 7, Ch1 L15).
