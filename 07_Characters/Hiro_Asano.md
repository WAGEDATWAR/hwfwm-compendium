## 1. Summary

Hiro Asano is Jason's uncle, a Sydney crime figure and the family's black sheep, who knew about hidden magic and the factions around it before Jason returned. He is bought out by the EOA at Jason's insistence, stays in the family circle through the whole series, and shares Jason's dry humour.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in detail in the lines read; he wears headphones on the cloud ship deck (Book 4, Ch30 L74).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Wary**: he compares the dangerous Vermillion with the returned Jason and "knew that fear" in how Ari reacted (Book 4, Ch8 L47).
- **Black sheep**: "Hiro's criminal connections had made him a black sheep of the family" (Book 4, Ch4 L52).
- **Long-suffering and jokey**: he teases at the family gathering about marrying your brother being "acceptable in many cultures" (Book 13, Ch1022 L19).
- **Does not like fuss**: he rubs a hand over his face unsure where to start (Book 4, Ch8 L47).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- He leads a Sydney criminal organisation and meets its leaders (Book 4, Ch9 L18). He is not an essence user in the lines read.
- Hiro's contacts in the magical world, including the EOA's pressure on Sydney, are why Jason gets him out of the way (Book 4, Ch9 L76; Book 4, Ch12 L41).

## 5. Background

- Jason's uncle; Ken Asano is his brother (Book 4, Ch4 L52; Book 13, Ch951 L66).
- He and Taika hold the Sydney operation and watch Shade warily (Book 4, Ch22 L33).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 4**: Jason arrives at his office (Book 4, Ch4 L52-54); the EOA threat and Victor Tollman's request (Book 4, Ch9 L76); the buyout and Jason's plan (Book 4, Ch12 L41; Book 4, Ch13 L3).
- **Book 11**: he takes Emi home after lunch (Book 11, Ch68 L659); Taika warns "Boss" not to talk back (Book 11, Ch68 L647).
- **Book 13**: at the family gathering (Book 13, Ch951 L66).

## 7. Relationships

- **Jason**: nephew; Hiro's "lot more than just a portal away from home" worry is family-wide (Book 13, Ch951 L66).
- **Taika**: his employee and friend (Book 11, Ch68 L647).
- **Ken**: brother, who slings a possessive arm over his son (Book 13, Ch951 L66).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

Jason arranges for the EOA to buy Hiro out "generously" as "the only safe way out" (Book 4, Ch13 L3-4).

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Laconic, dry and careful around anything with an aura; loyal to family without saying so. Cue: "There have been many cultures where it's acceptable to marry your brother, who are we to judge?"
