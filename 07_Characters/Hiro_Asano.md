## 1. Summary

Jason's estranged uncle, the family's black sheep, who runs a Sydney bar and later manages the clan's relocation programme.

## 2. Facts

- "The family's black sheep due to his hopefully minor involvement with organised crime" (Book 4, Ch1 L66).
- Jason plans to sell him gold at "well-below market rates" for an illegal sale (Book 4, Ch1 L66); Hiro's tidy office contrasts with the grimy bar downstairs, and he lifts the gold bar in Book 4 Ch4 (Book 4, Ch4 L64, L114).
- By Book 6 he manages the relocation programme for the clan (Book 6, Ch47 L53).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Wry, streetwise and loyal once convinced.
