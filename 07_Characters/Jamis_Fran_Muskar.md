## 1. Summary

An astral king whose avatar attends the Builder's observation scene and who berates defiant messengers.

## 2. Facts

- His avatar is the only one whose wings are completely absorbed into his body (Book 10, Ch15 L6).
- Says that when the task is complete "the price is paid. Zithis Carrow Vayel will have fulfilled his debt to us" (Book 10, Ch15 L9).
- Snarls at defiant messengers that they directly defied the Council of Kings (Book 12, Ch33 L51-55).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Indulgent, vain and sharp when crossed.
