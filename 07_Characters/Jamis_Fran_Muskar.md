## 1. Summary

Jamis Fran Muskar is an astral king whose avatar attends the Builder's observation scene, berates defiant messengers and, in Book 13, advises Jason on McKean with condescension. He rules from a sleek spaceship-like dimension ship.

## 2. Appearance

- Seven feet tall, "diminutive for his kind", with wings; his clothes are not the loose toga style of messengers (Book 13, Ch1000 L3).
- His avatar's wings are completely absorbed into his body (Book 10, Ch15 L6).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Vain and sharp when crossed**: he snarls at defiant messengers that they directly defied the Council of Kings (Book 12, Ch33 L51-55).
- **Dismissive of lesser threats**: Farrah says "Muskar might not think McKean is dangerous" (Book 13, Ch1003 L25).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Rules a sleek dimension ship that looks out of place on Pallimustus (Book 12, Ch36 L35).
- Says when the task is complete "the price is paid. Zithis Carrow Vayel will have fulfilled his debt to us" (Book 10, Ch15 L9).

## 5. Background

- One of the Council of Kings (Book 12, Ch33 L51-55).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 10**: observes Erigo's clothing (Book 10, Ch15 L6-9).
- **Book 12**: berates messengers; his ship arrives (Book 12, Ch33 L51; Book 12, Ch36 L35).
- **Book 13**: meets Jason (Book 13, Ch1000 L3).

## 7. Relationships

- **Zithis Carrow Vayel**: a debtor to the kings (Book 10, Ch15 L9).
- **Jason**: Jason says Muskar sees danger only for those he cares about, namely Jason himself (Book 13, Ch1003 L26).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

Nothing hidden in the lines read.
