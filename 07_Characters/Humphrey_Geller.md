## 1. Summary

Humphrey Francis Eugene Geller is the leader of Team Biscuit, a tall, broad-shouldered, handsome young man from a family that has been adventuring for sixteen generations. He begins the series a shy teenager and becomes a steady leader and one of the team's strongest fighters.

## 2. Appearance

Tall, broad-shouldered and handsome; sixteen or seventeen in Book 1 (Book 1, Ch66 L58). Rick Geller, a family member, shares his tall, broad-shouldered build (Book 1, Ch66 L58-62).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Formal in a crisis**: in Book 3, facing the Builder's forces, he announces "My name is Humphrey Francis Eugene Geller. My family have been adventurers for sixteen generations. We aren't alchemists or weaponsmiths on the side... For hundreds of years we have done one thing, and one thing only: protect our world from people like you" (Book 3, Ch68 L61).
- **Shy in personal matters**: when Jason asks in front of his mother whether he has spoken to Gabrielle, he turns white and shakes his head (Book 1, Ch47 L88-89).
- **Moral voice**: in Book 1 he confronts Jason about the cruelty of the mirage-arena ambush (Book 1, Ch74 L70). He later advises against elaborate plans: "We tried that fifteen years ago and we only got from Rimaros to Yaresh" (Book 12, Ch15 L64).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- **Essences**: the dragon confluence, reached through the might and wing essences (Book 9, Ch33 L15). Abilities include dragon wings and a flaming dragon-wing sword, a Dragon's Might aura that turns fire into dragon fire, teleporting (Book 1, Ch88 L94-106), and the ability Spartoi, which at gold rank calls up a hundred dragon-tooth bone-golem warriors in blue and gold armour (notes, Book 13).
- **Racial gift**: his human gift Special Attack Affinity evolves into Attack of the Mirage Dragon (an illusory double of his attacks, Book 2, Ch61 L147) and later into Hero's Drive, with the racial gift Hero's Sacrifice (Book 2, Ch61 L147).
- **Familiar**: Stash (Velitraxistaasch), a mirage dragon (Book 1, Ch38 L56-63).
- **Rank**: iron in Book 1; a gold-rank expedition leader by Book 13 (Book 13, Ch958 L48).

## 5. Background

Son of Danielle Geller (Book 1, Ch66 L3-6); a Geller of Greenstone, with a family tower (Book 11, Ch68 L1935) and a mirage chamber (Book 1, Ch66 L3-6). The Gellers' mothers arranged his familiar bond (Book 1, Ch38 L64).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: introduced alongside Stash; Jason nudges him toward Gabrielle Pellin; confronts Jason about the arena ambush.
- **Book 2**: breaks up with Gabrielle, who is dogmatic about Knowledge (Book 2, Ch28 L25-30); Humphrey says "I care for Gabrielle and I've enjoyed our time together. That time is just coming to an end" (Book 2, Ch28 L32).
- **Book 3**: the speech to the Builder's forces.
- **Book 9**: Hero's Drive and Hero's Sacrifice appear.
- **Book 11**: he calls Sophie "my fiancée" (Book 11, Ch68 L1177).
- **Book 13**: leads expeditions as a gold-ranker and commands the Spartoi.

## 7. Relationships

Danielle Geller (mother), Sophie Wexler (fiancée), Jason (friend), Stash, Rufus (mentor), Gabrielle Pellin (a first romance, ended in Book 2), Rick and Phoebe Geller (family).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

His race-gift evolution is level 2. His engagement is level 3.
