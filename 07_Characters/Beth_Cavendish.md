## 1. Summary

A bronze-rank Greenstone adventurer and water healer who leads a team in the Ustei campaign and the City of Fallen Echoes.

## 2. Facts

- Introduces herself to Jason at the Adventure Society as Beth Cavendish, meeting her cousin Mose (Book 1, Ch83 L23-43).
- Leads a team of Hudson Kettering (a huge human), Niko (a smoulder), Emily (a celestine archer) and her cousin Mose against the Ustei sand barge (Book 1, Ch89 L53-85); Jason joins as a roaming scout (Book 1, Ch89 L77).
- She tells Jason, "Welcome to the path to bronze rank" (Book 1, Ch90 L61).
- In the Fallen Echoes trial she is part of the front cluster (Book 2, Ch61 L51) and her team fights Rick's team (Book 2, Ch29-30), where she is struck down by the thorn fountain's retributive damage (Book 2, Ch30 L80).
- In Book 3 her team trains against Jason's in the mirage chamber (Book 3, Ch1 L86) and Jason envies her affliction-blanketing powers (Book 3, Ch8 L19).
- The Cavendish family names a Greenstone district, Cavendish (Book 1, Ch71 L3).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Competent and brisk, a clear team leader who gives orders without ceremony (Book 1, Ch89 L77).
