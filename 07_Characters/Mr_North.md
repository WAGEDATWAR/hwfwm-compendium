## 1. Summary

Mr North is the true leader of the Engineers of Ascension, first among its four Cardinals, and the Network founder's former familiar Noreth. A very old rune spider turned man, he spends centuries on Earth bargaining, scheming and experimenting, ends by disabling the Grid and by leaving Jason a vault of his research, and dies in Book 6 killing Gerling to avenge his friend.

## 2. Appearance

- A grinning man who meets Jason "grinning like a snake who found a nest full of baby mice" (Book 5, Ch40 L68). When he drops the act he is stern, adjusting his tie and cuffs, "the grinning, languid man Jason had met was nowhere to be seen" (Book 5, Ch41 L103).
- He is a gold-ranker, not an essence user, and "a bonded familiar, but your bond has been severed" (Book 5, Ch40 L74-78). Jason tells Anna that "He was a magic spider. A familiar of the man you know as the network founder" (Book 13, Ch987 L16).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Amused and mocking**: "You're a smart man, Mr Asano. More or less" (Book 5, Ch40 L72); he calls Jason "a parade of delights" (Book 5, Ch41 L33).
- **Controlled**: "He had long ago schooled himself out of dangerous curiosity and exploitable impatience" (Book 5, Ch8 L24).
- **Ruthless about outcomes**: with the Grid down for months he says "A wide-scale collapse of civic and social infrastructure does not obviate our objectives" and "So long as we rule" (Book 5, Ch8 L58-60).
- **A manipulator of principles**: "Be wary of your principles, Mr Asano. I might use them to be assured that you enter that astral space" (Book 6, Ch25 L117).
- **Paranoid and lonely**: Barbou thinks he is "older and more powerful than anyone realises", not human, and "lonely" (Book 6, Ch30 L56-58).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- **Webs**: he draws ritual circles in webbing that contain auras, sound, scent and magic (Book 6, Ch38 L71). He buries himself in a cocoon before the nuclear solar rockets, and digs out afterwards (Book 6, Ch46 L112-113).
- **Magitech without theory**: he developed magitech "with almost no knowledge of magical theory, drastically low ambient magic, and essentially zero technology", and was too paranoid to trust capable scientists; Audrey Blaine "was a buffer between North's ambitions and his paranoia" (Book 13, Ch987 L22-24).

## 5. Background

- **A rune spider**: "I was a rune spider, originally" (Book 5, Ch40 L79). The Network founder was his essence user; "My essence user was blinded by faith... giving up power and prestige to lift up a bunch of savages", and the bond broke before the founder died (Book 5, Ch40 L84).
- **Betrayal**: Noreth sold out the founder to the early magic societies (Book 8, Ch50 L52; Book 13, Ch1017 L24).
- **Centuries on Earth**: Boris supplied him with spirit coins to survive as a gold-ranker but North never trusted him; had he trusted him, "North's core research might not have been such a mess" (Book 13, Ch987 L28).
- **The EOA**: his organisation is named for his project of engineering people without essences; its leader "secretly being some kind of spider" (Book 13, Ch1001 L92).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 5**: the Cardinal's meetings and the shutdown plan (Book 5, Ch3 L5-12; Book 5, Ch8 L14-67). He takes Barbou from Mrs West's circle (Book 5, Ch32 L8; Book 5, Ch40 L86). He meets Jason in the Builder's door space, tells him to absorb the door and says "It's about time you showed up" (Book 5, Ch40 L66; Book 5, Ch41 L24-26).
- **Book 6**: he stays near Jason through the transformation zone work (Book 6, Ch35 L6; Book 6, Ch38 L3). He insists Jason will go into the astral space "whether I give you Barbou or not" (Book 6, Ch25 L107-113) and later dies killing Gerling (Book 6, Ch46 L112-127).
- **Later books**: his vault of EOA secrets goes to Jason (Book 7, Ch48 L36; Book 11, Ch36 L14) and the clan uses his research (Book 13, Ch987 L14).

## 7. Relationships

- **Jason**: sometimes ally, sometimes enemy; Jason "never trusted" him but believed his intentions were good (Book 8, Ch1 L57).
- **Barbou**: he calls him a quality subordinate and takes him after the Lyon plans fail (Book 5, Ch40 L86).
- **Audrey Blaine**: his own cardinal Mrs South, whom the cardinals murder for refusing the plan (Book 5, Ch8 L62-67; Book 13, Ch987 L12).
- **Gerling**: "You took my friend", he says, and kills him at the cost of his own life (Book 6, Ch46 L117-125).
- **Boris**: they knew each other, but "North's paranoia prevented them from working together" (Book 13, Ch987 L27-28).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

His identity as the Network founder's familiar is revealed to Jason in Book 5 (Book 5, Ch40 L78-86) and to everyone else in Book 13 (Book 13, Ch1017 L39). Noreth's failure was not seeing that Asano was an ally (Book 13, Ch954 L12).

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Dry, old and amused; he treats everyone as a long game, flatters and needles in the same breath, and drops the grin when the stakes are real. Cue: "You're a smart man, Mr Asano. More or less."
