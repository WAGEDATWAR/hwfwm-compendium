## 1. Summary

Lorenn is the last councilwoman of the brighthearts of Cardinas and, after Jason's intervention, the leader of her people. An ash-aspect brightheart who fought a war she could not win, she accepts surface allies she dislikes, hands her territories to Jason and leads her people into the new subterranean realm he builds.

## 2. Facts

- **Last of the council**: the brighthearts had a ruling council of nine, "three elected from the gold-rankers, three from the normal-rankers and three from the guilds"; only Lorenn remains, the others "having sacrificed everything to keep the rest of us alive" (Book 10, Ch72 L33). She is addressed as Councilwoman (Book 10, Ch72 L33).
- **Appearance**: an ash aspect, with "smoky greys" that she thinks far less appealing than the forge-glow of her fire-aspect friend Marla (Book 10, Ch65 L9).
- **War room**: she stands alone in the old council chamber, now a war room, before a live model of the city drawn from the natural array and mostly covered in the sickly green of the elemental messengers' tree (Book 10, Ch65 L4-8).
- **Marla**: Marla urges evacuating a number of people they can feed; Lorenn breaks down, then accepts the comfort (Book 10, Ch65 L16-24).
- **Beaufort**: she keeps the alliance with the Builder cult's commander despite loathing the cultists, "Our alliance stands" (Book 10, Ch65 L34). Later she tells the adventurers that accepting them "was not an easy choice" because "we are not warriors" (Book 10, Ch73 L35).
- **Suspicion of the surface**: she cannot bring herself to be happy at the prospect of more surface invaders (Book 10, Ch65 L38).
- **Meeting the adventurers**: she says "I am Lorenn... You, as I understand it, are Beaufort's enemies" and "So long as you can do something about the elemental messengers, I don't care what you are" (Book 10, Ch72 L44, L55).
- **Her people's story**: almost all the brighthearts died in three cycles of the messenger war, and the bodies rose; she says "We, so far as I know, are all the brighthearts that exist" (Book 10, Ch73 L45-47).
- **Accepting the loss of home**: "Losing the array is a blow our people may not recover from... if we are going to last as a people, we need to leave our home behind" (Book 10, Ch73 L57).
- **Jason's offer**: she doubts "a silver-ranker saving us all", and decides that "if we're choosing between no chance of survival and betting everything on a stranger, that's not a choice at all" (Book 10, Ch74 L25, L42). She asks what happens to the people in his soul realm if he dies (Book 10, Ch74 L57).
- **Crossing over**: she has never used a portal; she walks through with Jason as the soul realm forms chambers for her people, and fresh food appears (Book 10, Ch76 L62-75).
- **The new realm**: after her trip into it she tells Jason it is closer to the old array without its instability (Book 10, Ch77 L7).

## 3. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 10**: the war room; the alliance with Beaufort; the adventurers' arrival; the evacuation into Jason's soul realm (Book 10, Ch65-77).
- **Book 11**: she hands her territories to Jason, whose brand replaces hers; she objects to putting aside a weapon without discussion (Book 11, Ch16 L70, L77), joins the alliance council (Book 11, Ch17 L14, L39) and urges striking once the enemy messengers are destroyed (Book 11, Ch51 L61). She objects to a statue of the tree that "will loom over us, every day" (Book 11, Ch57 L17). She finds the new subterranean city "not the home she'd lost" but hopes it becomes home (Book 11, Ch62 L92-97).

## 4. Roleplay notes

Stoic, proud and exhausted; she hides grief behind formality and distrusts surface people. Her loyalty is to what is left of her people, and she bargains hard for their dignity.
