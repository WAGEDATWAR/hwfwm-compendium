## 1. Summary

A fire-aspect brightheart who commands the brightheart military defending Cardinas.

## 2. Facts

- A fire aspect with skin markings, eyes and hair the yellow-orange of steel in a forge, dark-skinned (Book 10, Ch65 L9); in Book 12 her hair and eyes glow like molten steel and she has a warrior's physique (Book 12, Ch11 L6).
- She comforts Lorenn, the city's leader, when she breaks down (Book 10, Ch65 L24).
- She briefs Jason on the citadel's defences, the children kept in the growth chambers and the messengers' failed attempts to cross the death chamber (Book 10, Ch72 L28-38; Book 10, Ch73 L7-51).
- She commands the citadel's response when Gary drinks from the Cup of Heroes (Book 10, Ch88 L116) and coordinates reinforcements (Book 10, Ch89 L89-95).
- In Book 11 she is the brightheart commander beside the Builder cult's Beaufort (Book 11, Ch4 L21; Book 11, Ch17 L80); in Book 12 she is the leader of the brightheart military (Book 12, Ch11 L6).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Commanding and practical; she answers loss with order.
