## 1. Summary

Marla is a fire-aspect brightheart who commands the brightheart military defending Cardinas. Commanding and practical, she answers loss with order.

## 2. Appearance

- A fire aspect, dark-skinned, with skin markings, eyes and hair the yellow-orange of steel in a forge (Book 10, Ch65 L9).
- In Book 12 her hair and eyes glow like molten steel and she has a warrior's physique (Book 12, Ch11 L6).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Comforting**: she comforts Lorenn, the city's leader, when she breaks down (Book 10, Ch65 L24).
- **Practical**: she briefs Jason on the citadel's defences, the children in the growth chambers and the messengers' failed attempts to cross the death chamber (Book 10, Ch72 L28-38; Book 10, Ch73 L7-51).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Commander of the brightheart military (Book 12, Ch11 L6); she commands the citadel's response when Gary drinks from the Cup of Heroes (Book 10, Ch88 L116) and coordinates reinforcements (Book 10, Ch89 L89-95).

## 5. Background

- Leader of the brightheart military in Cardinas (Book 12, Ch11 L6).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 10**: Cardinas defence (Book 10, Ch65 L9-24; Book 10, Ch72-73; Book 10, Ch88-89).
- **Book 11**: the brightheart commander beside Beaufort of the Builder cult (Book 11, Ch4 L21; Book 11, Ch17 L80).
- **Book 12**: leader of the brightheart military (Book 12, Ch11 L6).

## 7. Relationships

- **Lorenn**: the city's leader whom she comforts (Book 10, Ch65 L24).
- **Jason and Gary**: allies in the defence (Book 10, Ch72 L28; Book 10, Ch88 L116).
