## 1. Summary

A peak-bronze heir of House Irios whom Jason meets after the Zara marriage scandal.

## 2. Facts

- Jason meets him at a tailor's shop: "a peak-level bronze-ranker" (Book 7, Ch41 L3); he is shocked when Jason names himself the dead man (Book 7, Ch41 L11-12).
- Jason walks him along the street and locals glance at the House Irios crest on his arm (Book 7, Ch41 L26); a trio of young bronze-ranker friends greet him (Book 7, Ch41 L40-41).
- Zara's intended match was "a member of the Irios family" (Book 7, Ch18 L70), which is why the encounter is political.
- In Book 9 Jason's overture to the Irios family was not taken up, and Vesper had engineered the meeting for political reasons (Book 9, Ch14 L11).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Shaken and eager to please; he is caught between his family's pride and Jason's reputation.
