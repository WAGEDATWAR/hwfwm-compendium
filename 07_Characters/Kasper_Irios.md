## 1. Summary

Kasper Irios is a peak-bronze heir of House Irios whom Jason meets at a tailor's shop after the Zara marriage scandal. By Book 12 he is an adventurer on a team with Orin Pensinata.

## 2. Appearance

Not described beyond a House Irios crest on his arm (Book 7, Ch41 L26).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Shaken**: his movements are "oddly stumbling" and he freezes when he sees Jason (Book 7, Ch41 L3-10).
- **Eager to please**: shocked when Jason names himself the dead man (Book 7, Ch41 L11-12).
- **Nervous**: his friends pick up on his nervousness (Book 7, Ch41 L46).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- A peak-level bronze-ranker (Book 7, Ch41 L3), close to silver like his three young friends (Book 7, Ch41 L41).

## 5. Background

- Zara's intended match was "a member of the Irios family" (Book 7, Ch18 L70).
- Vesper engineered Jason's meeting with him for political reasons, and Jason's overture to the family was not taken up (Book 9, Ch14 L11; Book 9, Ch25 L79).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 7**: meets Jason at the tailor shop and walks the street with him (Book 7, Ch41 L3-46).
- **Book 12**: an adventurer now; Orin is the only one on his team to hit gold so far (Book 12, Ch12 L63).

## 7. Relationships

- **Jason**: unwilling companion (Book 7, Ch41 L26).
- **Orin Pensinata**: teammate (Book 12, Ch12 L63).

Nothing hidden in the lines read.
