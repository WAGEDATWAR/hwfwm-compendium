## 1. Summary

The astral king who rules the messenger forces around Yaresh and keeps her servants capped with seals.

## 2. Facts

- Jason is told she is "the astral king who rules the local messenger forces" and a known practitioner of soul engineering (Book 9, Ch78 L82).
- She watches servant race armies battle to the death, resurrecting them and bestowing abilities "to keep things interesting" (Book 9, Ch102 L65); she feels one of her seals vanish when Jason removes it from Tera (Book 9, Ch102 L66-67).
- Marek served her until recently; she was known for her fascination with soul engineering (Book 10, Ch1 L53). Jason removes the brand of "her astral king master, Vesta Carmis Zell" from Tera Jun Casta (Book 10, Ch2 L19) and says Vesta used Tera, keeping her at silver rank (Book 10, Ch2 L40).
- At the Council of Kings her prime avatar scolds Jamis (Book 10, Ch15 L8); he says she chose a separate goal from the rest of the group and is young (Book 10, Ch15 L30-43).
- Boris says she hired messengers with elemental powers and would never accept a member of the Unorthodoxy, and has no idea he is one (Book 11, Ch24 L85-86).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Cold, curious and acquisitive; she treats the lives of her servants as experiments.
