## 1. Summary

Vesta Carmis Zell is the astral king who rules the messenger forces around Yaresh and keeps her servants capped with seals. Cold, curious and acquisitive, she treats the lives of her servants as experiments.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in the lines read.

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Cold and curious**: she watches servant race armies battle to the death, resurrecting them and bestowing abilities "to keep things interesting" (Book 9, Ch102 L65).
- **Soul engineer**: known for her fascination with soul engineering (Book 10, Ch1 L53).
- **Separate goals**: she chose a separate goal from the rest of the group and is young (Book 10, Ch15 L30-43).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- She feels one of her seals vanish when Jason removes it from Tera (Book 9, Ch102 L66-67).
- Jason says Vesta used Tera, keeping her at silver rank (Book 10, Ch2 L40).

## 5. Background

- "The astral king who rules the local messenger forces" and a known practitioner of soul engineering (Book 9, Ch78 L82).
- Marek served her until recently (Book 10, Ch1 L53).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 9**: introduced (Book 9, Ch78 L82); the seal incident (Book 9, Ch102 L65-67).
- **Book 10**: Jason removes her brand from Tera (Book 10, Ch2 L19); at the Council of Kings her prime avatar scolds Jamis (Book 10, Ch15 L8).
- **Book 11**: Boris says she hired messengers with elemental powers and would never accept a member of the Unorthodoxy, and has no idea he is one (Book 11, Ch24 L85-86).

## 7. Relationships

- **Marek, Tera**: servants (Book 10, Ch1 L53; Book 10, Ch2 L19).
- **Jamis Fran Muskar**: fellow king (Book 10, Ch15 L8).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes
