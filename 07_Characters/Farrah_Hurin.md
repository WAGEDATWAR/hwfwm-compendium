## 1. Summary

Farrah Hurin (also spelt Huren) is a small, pale, strawberry-blonde ritual magician from Vitesse. She is one of the three adventurers Jason meets in the Vane cellar, teaches him aura control, perception and meditation, and treats him as an equal. She dies in Book 1, is brought back by the Reaper, and reappears on Earth in Book 4.

## 2. Appearance

Pretty, with strawberry-blonde hair, a button nose and light skin, "startlingly cute" and clearly unhappy when freed from her cage (Book 1, Ch12 L11). Physically small but "prodigiously strong": she hauls the standing stones into place in Book 7 (Book 7, Ch1 L16).

## 3. Personality and voice

A lecturer by habit: whenever she is not pointing out something specific, she is explaining (Book 1, Ch23 L15-17). She keeps her friends close: in Book 5, away from Gary, she says "I miss Gary" (Book 5, Ch30 L61).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- **Essences**: Earth, Fire and Potent, which form the Volcano confluence; she explains this herself in Book 1 (Book 1, Ch19 L30).
- **Abilities** (Book 1, Ch19 L1; Book 4, Ch41 L31): lava cannon (the Book 1 chapter title is "I Want a Lava Cannon"), a magma golem, lava streams and fire orbs, obsidian domes, shards and columns, and obsidian armour (Book 1, Ch108 L94).
- **Ritual magic**: she adjusts circles for ambient magic (Book 1, Ch23 L15-17) and builds the infrastructure of Asano Village and the Grid repairs (Book 5, Ch5 L83-89).
- **Aura teaching**: perception and memory games, meditation and aura control (Book 1, Ch40 L31-35).
- **Rank**: silver in Book 4 or 5; in Book 12 she is offered the choice of becoming a Voice of the Will or a Defier of the Will (Book 12, Ch21 L43-46); she accepts and becomes the Defier, immortal and "still a step or two from gold" (Book 12, Ch21 L43-46).

## 5. Background

Daughter of Amelia and William Hurin of Vitesse; a member of the Burning Violet guild (Book 2, Ch38 L69; Book 7, Ch7 L53). Friends with Rufus and Gary since childhood.

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: rescued with Rufus and Gary from the cult; dies covering civilians in the desert astral-space ambush ("Farrah died covering those people", Book 1, Ch113 L41).
- **Book 4**: Jason finds that she is alive in his world, held captive by the Network's Lyon branch; the Reaper let her go in a negotiation with the World-Phoenix, which was worried that Jason would let Earth die (captive of Barbou and the EOA, Book 4, Ch51 L62; the Reaper's negotiation is from the notes, Book 10). Jason rescues her.
- **Books 5-6**: builds the family compound's infrastructure and the Grid repairs, helps destroy a vampire hub, leaves Earth with Jason; of her captor Adrien Barbou she says "Barbou belongs to me" (Book 6, Ch26 L9).
- **Books 7-9**: settles in Rimaros; co-builds the Sky Link Network with Travis Noble (Book 13, Ch1016 L48: "the years we worked together, building the Sky Link Network").
- **Books 12-13**: becomes a Defier of the Will; leads Earth's grid and tracking work.

## 7. Relationships

Jason (an unusual, close bond), Rufus and Gary (friends), Travis Noble (partner), Emi (the child of the household; Farrah helped build the village for the family), Adrien Barbou (her captor).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

Her return to life is level 2 (this project's spoiler scale; the event is in Book 4). The Defier choice is level 3.

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: A lecturer who turns blunt when angry. Cues: Book 1, Ch23 L15-17; "Barbou belongs to me" (Book 6, Ch26 L9). See `../10_Roleplay_Support/Tone_and_Voice.md`.

She explains at length and corrects quickly; she is direct about danger and treats ignorance as something to be fixed.
