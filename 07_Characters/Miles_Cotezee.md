## 1. Summary

A senior Vitesse Adventure Society administrator whose silver rank comes entirely from cores and who supervises missions in the surge.

## 2. Facts

- A senior administrator, silver rank "entirely from cores", with a paper, knowledge, rune and scribe essence combination (Book 6, Ch47 L878).
- He briefs Humphrey's team in his office (Book 7, Ch12 L69) and prepares a strike force against the Purity stronghold with marshalling instructions (Book 7, Ch16 L3, L75).
- He supervises a Book 7 mission with the temporary team leader Kenneth (Book 7, Ch25 L4) and meets Jason's team at the outworlder arrival (Book 7, Ch36 L65).
- In Book 12 he hurries across the Vitesse campus of the Magic Research Association after Jason's return and shares tea with Clive's assistant (Book 12, Ch1 L53; Book 12, Ch2 L50).
