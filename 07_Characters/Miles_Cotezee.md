## 1. Summary

Miles Cotezee is a senior Vitesse Adventure Society administrator whose silver rank comes entirely from cores and who supervises missions in the Book 7 surge. A harried, courteous official, he runs paperwork and logistics.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in the lines read; he looks "ready to snap if he got bad news" (Book 6, Ch47 L880).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Harried**: he stumbles out of his chair and hurries around the table when Clive arrives (Book 6, Ch47 L880).
- **Frank about orders**: "This contract didn't come down through normal channels. It came down from on high" (Book 6, Ch47 L931).
- **Reputation-minded**: "Reputation is everything in this town" (Book 6, Ch47 L936).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Silver rank "entirely from cores", with paper, knowledge, rune and scribe essences (Book 6, Ch47 L878).

## 5. Background

- Senior administrator at the Vitesse Adventure Society (Book 6, Ch47 L878).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 7**: briefs Humphrey's team (Book 7, Ch12 L69); prepares the Purity-stronghold strike force (Book 7, Ch16 L3-75); supervises a mission with the temporary leader Kenneth (Book 7, Ch25 L4); meets Jason's team at the outworlder arrival (Book 7, Ch36 L65).
- **Book 12**: hurries across the Vitesse campus after Jason's return and shares tea with Clive's assistant (Book 12, Ch1 L53; Book 12, Ch2 L50).
