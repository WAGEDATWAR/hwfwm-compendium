## 1. Summary

Arabelle Remore is Rufus's mother, a gold-rank member of the Church of the Healer who specialises in mental health and trauma recovery. She helps Jason recover from captivity, drains life from enemies in battle, and keeps a firm hand on her son, her old teammate Callum and Jason's team.

## 2. Appearance

- Dark-skinned, "even darker skin than her husband", with long hair dyed rainbow colours "in the Vitesse style" (Book 2, Ch32 L52).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Wry and perceptive**: she tells a bureaucrat she can only assume he is eager to step through a portal, in a pointed tone that Allayeth enjoys (Book 10, Ch12 L25, L45).
- **Firm**: "The amusement passed from Arabelle's face and the flint in the tone of her next words arrested his attention" (Book 8, Ch27 L88).
- **Cares for her patients**: she helps Jason explore traumatic memories and teaches him how to recover (Book 3, Ch24 L33; Book 4, Ch55 L3).
- **Family-minded**: "I think it's time for another child, husband. A daughter, this time" (Book 3, Ch1 L73).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- **Healer and therapist**: a member of the Church of the Healer specialising in mental health and trauma recovery (Book 8, Ch2 L49).
- **Life drain**: she moves "through like a breeze, the enemies she touched with her hand collapsing to withered husks", each death leaving an urn of life force to fuel her other powers (Book 2, Ch76 L39). In Book 10 she drains the magic from the undead, leaving them inert (Book 10, Ch82 L4).
- **Medical advice**: she and Carlos conclude Jason should use his mana as much as he can tolerate (Book 8, Ch47 L37).
- **Administration**: she organises procurement lists in the cloud palace's offices (Book 9, Ch72 L25).

## 5. Background

- **Old team**: she, her husband Gabriel, Callum Morse and Emir were a team (Book 2, Ch76 L3; Book 3, Ch24 L35).
- **Never sent deep**: as a valuable healer she was never sent deeper into the city than the surface during the Book 7 crisis (Book 7, Ch66 L18).
- **Family**: Roland Remore is her father-in-law (Book 10, Ch14 L50).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 2-3**: the strike on the cult island (Book 2, Ch76 L17-39); therapy with Jason on the houseboat (Book 3, Ch24).
- **Book 8-9**: Jason's recovery (Book 8, Ch47 L37); she meets Jason's group in Rimaros and talks with him over tea (Book 7, Ch52 L89; Book 9, Ch28 L3).
- **Book 10**: she stands up for Jason in the Yaresh meeting (Book 10, Ch12 L3) and fights in Cardinas (Book 10, Ch82 L4).
- **Book 11-12**: she advises Jason about giving Rufus space (Book 11, Ch50 L6) and travels in Onslow's shell (Book 12, Ch32 L35).

## 7. Relationships

- **Gabriel**: her husband, an instructor of adventurers (Book 3, Ch1 L73; Book 2, Ch38 L23); see Gabriel_Remore.md.
- **Rufus**: her son; she shares his concerns with Jason (Book 11, Ch50 L6).
- **Sophie**: she sits with Sophie and Melody (Book 10, Ch42 L14; Book 10, Ch40 L43).
- **Carlos**: her working partner (Book 8, Ch47 L37).
- **Emir and Callum**: old teammates (Book 3, Ch24 L35).
