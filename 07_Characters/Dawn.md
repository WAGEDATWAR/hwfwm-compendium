## 1. Summary

Dawn is the World-Phoenix's First Servant, the head of its cult and its primary vessel (Book 3, Ch69 L1036-1040 describes the post; the notes identify Dawn as holder of it, notes, Book 4). She guides Jason through Books 4-8 as a mentor and agent: she paints, takes avatar bodies, reveals the invasion plan, negotiates with the Builder and brings Jason's friends to Rimaros.

## 2. Appearance

Her true body is a celestine with ruby eyes and hair (Book 7, Ch54 L87). She appears in many avatars; Jason kills and loots one (Book 8, Ch4 L116: "I killed and looted Dawn"; the replacement avatar is described in Book 5, Ch20 L22). In Book 7 her vessel is a cottage inside a floating bubble that carries her (Book 7, Ch8 L3); in Book 9 a recording crystal projects her face over a projector (Book 9, Ch6 L17).

## 3. Personality and voice

Mentoring and slightly arch. When Jason has just punched her avatar she says "You realise that you could have asked that instead of punching my nose through my brain" and teases that she is "a little more mature than someone who just turned twenty-six" (Book 5, Ch20 L21-24).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- **Role**: First Servant of the World-Phoenix, head of its cult and the primary vessel of the great astral being (Book 3, Ch69 L1036-1040). Diamond-rank vessels of great astral beings can withstand the strain and keep their selves, and former vessels end up as peak existences (Book 3, Ch69 L1035-1040).
- **One intervention**: the Hierophant is allowed to intervene in Pallimustus a single time (Soramir to Jason, Book 7, Ch60 L27); in Book 7 she says she will use one full-power intervention (Book 7, Ch60 L27-30), and she destroys a Builder rolling city with a phoenix (notes, Book 7).
- **Art**: the paintings by "Dawn" that Jason buys show two planets with four pillars and "The Invasion of Pallimustus" (Book 4, Ch23 L31-39).
- **Home**: Interstice, the city-universe at the cosmos's centre (Book 5, Ch20 L65-67).

## 5. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 4**: her paintings; Dawn reveals the Earth-Pallimustus magic link and the Builder/Purity invasion plan to Jason.
- **Book 5**: arrives at Asano Village in a new avatar; explains the danger of transformation zones as dimensional ulceration; tells him the Reaper "was the one who offered" Farrah's return (Book 5, Ch20 L40-41).
- **Book 6**: arrives in her true body to negotiate with the Builder after Shako kills Jason (Book 6, Ch18 L3-9).
- **Book 7**: her full-power intervention and the arrival of Humphrey's team in Rimaros.
- **Book 13**: Boris is certain she had "taken actions beyond the mandate of her great astral being" (Book 13, Ch954 L19).

## 6. Relationships

Jason, the World-Phoenix (Helsveth is the current prime vessel, Book 11, Ch68 L2527-2530), Shako ("I cared about you very deeply, once", notes, Book 6), Soramir Rimaros.

## 7. Secrets and spoiler notes

Dawn's identity as First Servant and her use of avatars are level 2; her exceeding her mandate is level 3.
