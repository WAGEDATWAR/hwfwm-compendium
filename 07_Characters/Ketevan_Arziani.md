## 1. Summary

Ketevan Arziani is an analytical Network officer in Sydney, Anna Tilden's assistant, who later becomes Yumi Asano's chief of staff. Brisk and sceptical, she tests leaps of logic.

## 2. Appearance

- Jason guesses she is about thirty, athletic, with short brown hair (Book 4, Ch46 L82).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Analytical**: Anna places a lot of trust in her analytical abilities (Book 4, Ch5 L10).
- **Sceptical**: in Book 11 she doubts Jason's plans while Yumi has faith (Book 11, Ch36 L54-64).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Formal title: Assistant to the Director of Operations, Sydney branch (Book 4, Ch46 L82).
- Investigates a hospital event up the coast (Book 4, Ch8 L83).
- Jason saves her life with one of Shade's bodies forming a parachute (Book 4, Ch47 L28; Book 4, Ch59 L13).

## 5. Background

- She leads the Network defectors' intake and sits on calls with the Sydney branch (Book 5, Ch9 L67; Book 5, Ch36 L33).
- After the Network's Australian branch breaks up, Yumi hires her for her administrative skill (Book 11, Ch36 L54-64).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 4**: Anna's assistant (Book 4, Ch5 L10; Book 4, Ch8 L83); parachute rescue (Book 4, Ch47 L28).
- **Book 5**: tells Jason's side "We've got a bunch of people coming in from the EOA looking to defect" (Book 5, Ch9 L67).
- **Book 11**: Yumi's hire (Book 11, Ch36 L54-64).
- **Book 12**: chief of staff to the clan matriarch (Book 12, Ch40 L52); drives Anna through Saint-Étienne (Book 12, Ch41 L3-14).

## 7. Relationships

- **Anna Tilden**: her director (Book 4, Ch5 L10).
- **Yumi Asano**: her new principal (Book 11, Ch36 L54-64).
