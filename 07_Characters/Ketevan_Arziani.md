## 1. Summary

An analytical Network officer in Sydney, Anna Tilden's assistant, who later becomes Yumi Asano's chief of staff.

## 2. Facts

- Anna places a lot of trust in her analytical abilities (Book 4, Ch5 L10); she investigates a hospital event up the coast (Book 4, Ch8 L83).
- Her formal title is Assistant to the Director of Operations, Sydney branch; Jason guesses she is about thirty, athletic, with short brown hair (Book 4, Ch46 L82).
- Jason saves her life with one of Shade's bodies forming a parachute (Book 4, Ch47 L28; Book 4, Ch59 L13).
- She leads the Network defectors' intake and sits on calls with the Sydney branch (Book 5, Ch9 L67; Book 5, Ch36 L33).
- After the Network's Australian branch breaks up, Yumi hires her for her administrative skill; in Book 11 she doubts Jason's plans while Yumi has faith (Book 11, Ch36 L54-64).
- In Book 12 she is chief of staff to the clan matriarch (Book 12, Ch40 L52) and drives Anna through Saint-Étienne (Book 12, Ch41 L3-14).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Brisk and sceptical, with an analyst's habit of testing leaps of logic.
