## 1. Summary

A celestine healer and the leader of Valdis's team, who becomes his wife.

## 2. Facts

- A celestine woman on Valdis's team who quietly apologises for him (Book 2, Ch55 L58); she keeps a dimensional space with cushions and tries to steer him toward making connections (Book 2, Ch56 L5-14).
- The system names her Sigrid Freyn when Jason's team defeats her in the mock battle (Book 3, Ch4 L43); she is "the most impactful person on the field" (Book 3, Ch4 L36).
- When the Mirror King collects Valdis, Sigrid greets Hastor, the king's portal specialist, as "Father" and confirms "He is my father" (Book 3, Ch5 L49-53, L57-58); the king himself is her father-in-law after her marriage (Book 12, Ch15 L8-10).
- In Book 12 she leads Team Shining Scabbard, a famously capable leader and healer whom Neil has been training with, and Valdis's wife (Book 12, Ch15 L8-10); she faces Neil in a healer match (Book 12, Ch20 L9).
- In Book 13 she is credited as Sigrid Volaire, Prince Valdis's team healer and wife, on the expedition (Book 13, Ch958 L39; Book 13, Ch953 L39).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Sensible and dry; she is the grown-up on a team of enthusiasts and defends her king's dignity (Book 12, Ch15 L10).

## Open questions

- Whether Hastor is still the king's portal specialist in Book 12; the text calls the specialist only "the Mirror King's own portal specialist" (Book 12, Ch15 L8).
