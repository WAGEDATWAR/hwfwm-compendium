## 1. Summary

Sigrid Freyn (Sigrid Volaire) is a celestine healer and the leader of Valdis's team, who becomes his wife. Sensible and dry, she is the grown-up on a team of enthusiasts.

## 2. Appearance

A celestine woman (Book 2, Ch55 L58).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Apologetic for Valdis**: she quietly apologises for him (Book 2, Ch55 L58).
- **Dry and practical**: she tells Valdis to be more respectful of his father and king (Book 12, Ch15 L10).
- **Networking**: she steers him toward making connections (Book 2, Ch56 L5-14).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Healer and team leader; "the most impactful person on the field" in the mock battle (Book 3, Ch4 L36); named by the system when Jason's team defeats her (Book 3, Ch4 L43).
- She keeps a dimensional space with cushions (Book 2, Ch56 L5-14).
- Leads Team Shining Scabbard, a famously capable leader and healer whom Neil has been training with (Book 12, Ch15 L8-10); faces Neil in a healer match (Book 12, Ch20 L9).

## 5. Background

- She greets Hastor, the Mirror King's portal specialist, as "Father" (Book 3, Ch5 L49-58); the king is her father-in-law (Book 12, Ch15 L8-10).
- Open question: whether Hastor is still the king's portal specialist in Book 12; the text calls the specialist only "the Mirror King's own portal specialist" (Book 12, Ch15 L8).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 13**: credited as Sigrid Volaire, Prince Valdis's team healer and wife, on the expedition (Book 13, Ch958 L39; Book 13, Ch953 L39).
