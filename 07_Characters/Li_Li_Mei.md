## 1. Summary

Li Li Mei is a Chinese silver-ranker and daughter of Li Ning who deals with Jason on Earth in Books 4-6, ends up in Pallimustus and is found hiding in Boko, and becomes a political figure in Book 13. Controlled and watchful, she adapts quickly to a new world's rules.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in the lines read.

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Diplomatic**: she hopes for a "more modest collaboration in the future" (Book 4, Ch63 L25-28).
- **Adaptive**: one of the few with political experience in both worlds (Book 13, Ch1015 L40).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Silver rank in Books 4-6 (Book 4, Ch63 L25-28); Jason uses her as a test subject for refining his control of the System (Book 12, Ch48 L26).

## 5. Background

- Daughter of Li Ning; her gold-rank uncle is Li Chen (Book 13, Ch960 L16).
- Has lived in Boko for years (Book 12, Ch30 L71-74).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 4**: meets Jason in a business resort lobby (Book 4, Ch63 L25-28).
- **Book 6**: appears beside the dome and portal with two Chinese gold-rankers (Book 6, Ch5 L59; Book 6, Ch6 L16-21).
- **Book 12**: Jason finds her in Boko more than fifteen years after they last met (Book 12, Ch30 L80; Book 12, Ch31 L3-12); she stays near him during Boko's destruction (Book 12, Ch32 L18).
- **Book 13**: meets the international committee or the Chinese delegation with Li Chen (Book 13, Ch960 L16).

## 7. Relationships

- **Li Ning and Li Chen**: father and uncle (Book 13, Ch960 L16).
- **Jason**: acquaintance and test subject (Book 12, Ch48 L26).
