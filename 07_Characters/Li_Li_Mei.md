## 1. Summary

A Chinese silver-ranker and daughter of Li Ning who deals with Jason on Earth in Books 4-6, ends up in Pallimustus, is found hiding in Boko, and becomes a political figure in Book 13.

## 2. Facts

- Silver-rank daughter of Li Ning, meeting Jason in a business resort lobby; she hopes for a "more modest collaboration in the future" (Book 4, Ch63 L25-28).
- She appears beside the dome and portal with two Chinese gold-rankers (Book 6, Ch5 L59; Book 6, Ch6 L16-21).
- In Book 12 she has lived in Boko for years (Book 12, Ch30 L71-74); Jason finds her there, more than fifteen years after they last met (Book 12, Ch30 L80; Book 12, Ch31 L3-12). She stays near him during Boko's destruction (Book 12, Ch32 L18) and its aftermath (Book 12, Ch37).
- Jason uses her as a test subject for refining his control of the System (Book 12, Ch48 L26) and counts her among the Earthlings who roamed (Book 12, Ch48 L33).
- By Book 13 she is one of the few with political experience in both worlds (Book 13, Ch1015 L40), meeting the international committee or the Chinese delegation, joined by her gold-rank uncle Li Chen (Book 13, Ch960 L16).

## 3. Timeline of involvement

- Book 4: negotiates at Castle Reach (Book 4, Ch63).
- Book 6: arrives with the Chinese gold-rankers (Book 6, Ch5-6).
- Book 12: found in Boko; helps through its destruction (Book 12, Ch30-37).
- Book 13: negotiator for the handoff (Book 13, Ch960, Ch1015).

## 4. Roleplay notes

Controlled and watchful; she hides her expressions and adapts quickly to a new world's rules.
