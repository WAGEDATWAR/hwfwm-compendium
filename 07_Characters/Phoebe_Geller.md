## 1. Summary

Phoebe Geller is a distant Geller cousin of Humphrey's, Rick Geller's sister, and a pugilist on Rick's team. Blunt and curious, she asks what everyone is thinking and sits in on Sophie's training.

## 2. Appearance

- Darker skin and much lighter hair than Humphrey, from a distant branch family; light-brown pixie-cut hair (Book 1, Ch66 L4; Book 2, Ch24 L27).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Dry**: "I suspect your mother's hand will be firmly pressed down on the scale" (Book 1, Ch66 L30).
- **Relaxed**: she settles happily in a chair sipping her second glass while watching Sophie (Book 2, Ch24 L72).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- A pugilist, "a more damage-oriented version of Sophie"; she launches off Stash's palm to strike multiple enemies (Book 10, Ch70 L42).
- Iron rank in Book 1 and with the Adventure Society for over half a year (Book 1, Ch66 L3).

## 5. Background

- Distant cousin of Humphrey; the two share a last name but barely any ethnicity (Book 1, Ch66 L4).
- Rick's sister, on his team but absent for family issues at one point (Book 9, Ch1 L83; Book 9, Ch104 L54).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: meets Humphrey on his return from a contract (Book 1, Ch66 L3); present when Rick and Humphrey arrive (Book 1, Ch110 L51).
- **Book 2**: watches Sophie's practice (Book 2, Ch24 L72).
- **Book 9**: back on Rick's team (Book 9, Ch104 L54).
- **Book 10**: fights beside Dustin with Stash's help (Book 10, Ch70 L42-44).

## 7. Relationships

- **Rick**: brother (Book 1, Ch110 L51).
- **Humphrey**: cousin (Book 1, Ch66 L3).
