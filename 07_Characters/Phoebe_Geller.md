## 1. Summary

A distant Geller cousin who is Humphrey's contemporary and later Rick's teammate.

## 2. Facts

- A distant cousin of Humphrey, iron rank and with the Adventure Society for more than half a year, with darker skin and much lighter hair than his (Book 1, Ch66 L3-4).
- Is Rick's sister and back on his team by Book 9 (Book 9, Ch104 L54).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Blunt and curious; she asks what everyone is thinking.
