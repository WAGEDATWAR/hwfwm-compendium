## 1. Summary

Ken Asano is Jason's father, a Japanese-born Australian landscape architect who embraces the Australian lifestyle with convert's zeal. He catches Jason in a huge hug when Jason returns, joins the Asano Village family, learns a skirmish confluence and by Book 13 is a regular companion on Jason's walks.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in detail in the lines read; he brushes his fingers over his son's scars (Book 4, Ch27 L109).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Convert's zeal**: "Proving there is no zealot like a convert, Ken was all about the Australian lifestyle, from pub rock to footy matches and weekend barbecues" (Book 1, Ch1 L88).
- **Warm and undemanding**: Jason finds "his father's warm and undemanding support was a balm" (Book 4, Ch72 L86).
- **Emotional**: he goes still, then "exploded forward" to hug Jason (Book 4, Ch27 L41).
- **Straightforward**: he asks whether Jason has gone "a little far" with his domain (Book 13, Ch1005 L83).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Has the skirmish confluence, which suits running battles; many powers trip up the enemy (Book 7, Ch31 L12).
- A landscape architect (Book 1, Ch17 L31).

## 5. Background

- Born in Japan, raised in Australia from a very young age (Book 1, Ch1 L88).
- The middle brother: Shiro was the favourite, Hiro the disappointment, "your father laid low in the middle" (Book 4, Ch7 L10).
