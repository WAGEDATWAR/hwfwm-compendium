## 1. Summary

Jason's father, a Japanese-born Australian landscape architect.

## 2. Facts

- Born in Japan and raised in Australia from a very young age; "all about the Australian lifestyle, from pub rock to footy matches and weekend barbecues" (Book 1, Ch1 L88).
- A landscape architect (Book 1, Ch17 L31); Jason grew up learning more about grass than he wanted to know (Book 1, Ch1 L4).
- Reunited with his family and the clan after Book 4; the family compound is described in Casselton_and_Asano_Village.md.

## 3. Roleplay notes

Cheerfully practical, fond of barbecues and sport, quick to take Jason's side.
