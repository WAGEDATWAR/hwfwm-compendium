## 1. Summary

Belinda "Lindy" Callahan is Sophie's oldest friend, a thief from Greenstone's Old City who becomes Clive's assistant and, in time, the team's planner, infiltrator and master of shapeshifting.

## 2. Appearance

The text I checked gives no physical description beyond the disguises she adopts.

## 3. Personality and voice

Playful and delighted by her own power: when she settles on the Charlatan confluence she says, with glee, "I was looking through the abilities it's known to give and they sound fantastic" (Book 2, Ch32 L72). She teases Jason about Sophie (Book 7, Ch6 L42: "Has Sophie seen this whole half-naked, dirty workman thing you've got going on?"). In Book 10 Humphrey reminds her, in a schoolteacherly tone, that she is an active silver-rank adventurer.

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- **Essences**: Magic, Trap and Adept, which she traded for in Book 2 (Book 2, Ch32 L67-72), producing the Charlatan confluence (Book 2, Ch32 L67-72).
- **Abilities**: Echo Spirit is her first charlatan ability (Book 2, Ch68 L39-45); Bait and Switch, Rune Trap, Force Tether, Lightning Tether and Pit of the Reaper (wiki); shapeshifting (notes, Book 8).
- **Disguises**: she infiltrates the Order of Redeeming Light as "Keth Gino" (Book 8, Ch40 L7-13) and, in the jungle territory of the transformation zone, passes as the silver messenger "Relia Vin Vala" (Book 11, Ch13 L4-7).
- **Aura disguise**: her power gives her an edge in learning aura disguises, which she teaches Jason (Book 8, Ch29 L49-56).
- **Storage**: a personal storage space that even the Magic Society cannot peek into "without killing her first" (Book 6, Ch47 L660).

- **Familiars**: Shimmer, a summoned astral lantern (Book 2, Ch71 L50; Book 2, Ch72 L62; see `Familiars/Shimmer.md`), and an echo spirit that looks like a blurry version of her (Book 12, Ch27 L23).

## 5. Background

Grew up in the Old City with Sophie (notes, Book 2); began working with Clive at the Magic Society after she was caught (Book 2, Ch12 L6); the notes add that in Rakesh she works as the off-campus errand-runner while the caste system dismisses her as unimportant (notes, Book 6); she tracks a signal in the desert south of Rakesh (Book 6, Ch47 L651).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Books 1-2**: a thief; joins Clive.
- **Book 6**: helps Clive escape the Magic Society in Rakesh.
- **Book 8**: infiltrates the Order of Redeeming Light.
- **Book 11**: infiltrates the messengers in the transformation zone.
- **Book 12**: teaches at Remore Academy (Book 12, Ch44 L34), with Estella (earlier notes); her ex Jory Tillman is awkward around her.
- **Book 13**: married to Estella Warnock (Book 13, Ch1011 L23); the wiki records them as dating in Book 12 (wiki).

## 7. Relationships

Sophie (friend), Clive (partner in research), Jory Tillman (ex), Estella Warnock (wife, notes Book 13), Jason (teacher).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

Her infiltration identities are levels 2-3.

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Delighted by her own cleverness, and teasing. Cues: "Charlatan," she says with glee (Book 2, Ch32 L72); she urges Jason to find an excuse to head up on deck where Sophie can see him (Book 7, Ch6 L42). See `../10_Roleplay_Support/Tone_and_Voice.md`.

Mischievous, observant, quick to play a role.
