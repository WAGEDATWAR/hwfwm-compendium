## 1. Summary

Fal Vin Garath is a silver-rank messenger master who is abusive but within the bounds his peers accept. He detests acting at the behest of Jes Fin Kaal and launches a soul attack on Jason in Book 9.

## 2. Appearance

- Fair skin and golden hair, a physical counterpart to the other silver messenger (Book 9, Ch56 L5-7).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Imperious**: he scowls at Benella and demands answers in a voice "like a command from the heavens" (Book 9, Ch52 L20-28).
- **Resentful of command**: he "detested being forced to act at the behest of the Voice of the Will" (Book 9, Ch90 L3) and sneers at his commander, astral king's Voice and Asano (Book 9, Ch91 L3).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Silver rank; he launches a soul attack on Jason through his connection to Shade and is surprised when Jason fends it off and retaliates (Book 9, Ch52 L36).
- He seizes Shade and squeezes while Shade drains his mana, until Shade's body is destroyed (Book 9, Ch52 L42-44).

## 5. Background

- Benella's master, "abusive but not outside the bounds of acceptability to his fellow messengers", free to treat the servant races as he pleased so long as it did not impinge on other messengers (Book 9, Ch56 L5).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 9**: scolds Benella (Book 9, Ch52 L20-28); soul attack and Shade fight (Book 9, Ch52 L36-44); sent on a mission by Kaal (Book 9, Ch90 L3; Book 9, Ch91 L3).

## 7. Relationships

- **Benella**: his servant (Book 9, Ch56 L5).
- **Jes Fin Kaal**: his commander (Book 9, Ch90 L3).
- **Hess Jor Nasala**: sits with him at Kaal's meeting (Book 9, Ch56 L40).
