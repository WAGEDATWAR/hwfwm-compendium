## 1. Summary

A silver-rank messenger master who is abusive but within the bounds his peers accept.

## 2. Facts

- "Fal Vin Garath was Benella's master, who was abusive but not outside the bounds of acceptability to his fellow messengers", free to treat the servant races as he pleased so long as it did not impinge on other messengers (Book 9, Ch56 L5).
- He has fair skin and golden hair, a physical counterpart to the other silver messenger (Book 9, Ch56 L5-7).
- He scowls at Benella and demands answers in a voice "like a command from the heavens" (Book 9, Ch52 L20-28).
- He launches a soul attack on Jason through his connection to Shade, and is surprised when Jason fends it off and retaliates (Book 9, Ch52 L36).
- He seizes Shade and squeezes while Shade drains his mana, until Shade's body is destroyed (Book 9, Ch52 L42-44).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Imperious and quick to anger; he assumes servants exist to answer him.
