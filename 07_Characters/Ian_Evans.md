## 1. Summary

Jason's brother-in-law, Erika's husband and Emi's father, who can enter Jason's spirit vault.

## 2. Facts

- Jason senses Erika, her husband Ian and their daughter Emi on his return (Book 4, Ch3 L132); the contact is named "Ian Evans" (Book 4, Ch3 L123).
- Jason introduces "my sister, Erika, her husband, Ian, and their daughter" to Dr Baudrillard (Book 5, Ch3 L130).
- Erika and Ian are midway through iron, having taken cores regularly (Book 5, Ch3 L126).
- Ian is one of only three people outside Jason's blood relatives who can enter his spirit vault; "I've known Jason since he was twelve years old" (Book 5, Ch44 L45).
- Vincent Baudrillard is Emi's boyfriend in Book 13 (Book 13, Ch971 L49); Ian's place in that family is as Emi's father.

## 3. Roleplay notes

Steady and humane; he trusts Jason because he has seen him at his lowest.
