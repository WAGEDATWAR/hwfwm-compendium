## 1. Summary

Ian Evans (Evans-Asano) is Jason's brother-in-law, Erika's husband and Emi's father. A doctor when he learned about magic, he becomes a healer with two decades of experience on the confluence of medicine and magic. Steady and humane, he is one of the few outside Jason's blood who can enter his spirit vault.
