## 1. Summary

A gold-rank adventurer with a soul-damaged aura who teaches Jason aura and soul techniques and is lost to an orb in Cardinas.

## 2. Facts

- Trenchant asks whether anyone has worked with Amos Pensinata (Book 7, Ch44 L30). Dawn names him as a gold-ranker whose experiences with soul trauma left him with a more capable aura than most; she has convinced him to travel with Jason to teach soul manipulation (Book 9, Ch6 L47).
- Jason knows him by reputation as the man who defeated the same forces that had forced Jason to flee the underwater complex (Book 9, Ch6 L48).
- Jason later learns from him how to combine auras into a single force (Book 9, Ch94 L30) and about controlling aspects of the soul in isolation, which helped him expand perception without a beacon-like aura (Book 10, Ch28 L7).
- Clive turns on him at the Yaresh camp: "You were brought on to help Jason, not make things worse. But your baggage..." (Book 9, Ch42 L90).
- He debriefs a Rimaros team in Cardinas (Book 10, Ch64 L9) and is among the few able to resist the zone's corruption (Book 11, Ch47 L64).
- In Book 11 he grows troubled (Book 11, Ch49 L86), does not flee with the other gold-rankers but goes into the water orb (Book 11, Ch54 L18-21), and Jason promises to try to get him back (Book 11, Ch54 L56).
- His nephew is Orin Pensinata (Book 7, Ch47 L39; see `Orin_Pensinata.md`); he joins Jason's journey partly because Orin insisted on coming (Book 9, Ch24 L35).
- Jason extracts him from his attempted "suicide-by-messenger" and the gold-rankers escape into Jason's soul realm (Book 11, Ch55 L3). After the zone he takes Orin and the scout Rosa back to Rimaros (Book 11, Ch68 L378).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Calm and wry, with a teacher's patience and a soldier's scars.
