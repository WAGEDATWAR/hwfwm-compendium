## 1. Summary

A Network committee member ("Mrs South") who survives a failed secret programme in a cloned body and later directs the Asano clan's energy-vampire research.

## 2. Facts

- In Book 5 Mrs South, named Audrey, is pressed by Mr North and Mrs West to go along with them (Book 5, Ch8 L63-65).
- She wakes in a new body that had been in stasis for more than a dozen years, the last remnant of a secret programme whose progenitors she had killed (Book 5, Ch10 L17-33).
- The new body was cloned from her own DNA with Cabal biological material (Book 5, Ch10 L24).
- In Book 13 Jason introduces Audrey Blaine to Team Biscuit as having run a sketchy joint programme between the magical factions decades ago, the source of her current state, which she has learned to manage (Book 13, Ch959 L60); she has lived in a magic city for a decade among vampires (Book 13, Ch959 L50).
- The Asano clan snatched her from the Network as a coup (Book 13, Ch987 L10) and she is the clan's energy-vampire research director (Book 13, Ch992 L116; Book 13, Ch998 L109).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Dry, practical and unsentimental; she has lived with the consequences of her own research.
