## 1. Summary

Audrey Blaine is a Network committee member ("Mrs South") who survives a failed secret programme in a cloned body and later directs the Asano clan's energy-vampire research. Dry, practical and unsentimental, she has lived with the consequences of her own research.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in the lines read; her body is a clone grown from her DNA with Cabal biological material (Book 5, Ch10 L24).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Pressed by colleagues**: Mr North and Mrs West press her to go along with them (Book 5, Ch8 L63-65).
- **Composed about her condition**: Jason says she has learned to manage the state left by her old programme (Book 13, Ch959 L60).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- She is the clan's energy-vampire research director (Book 13, Ch992 L116).
- She woke in a body that had been in stasis for more than a dozen years, the last remnant of a secret programme whose progenitors she had killed (Book 5, Ch10 L17-33).

## 5. Background

- She ran a sketchy joint programme between the magical factions decades ago (Book 13, Ch959 L60) and has lived in a magic city for a decade among vampires (Book 13, Ch959 L50).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 5**: Mrs South among the Network committee (Book 5, Ch8 L63-65); wakes in a new body (Book 5, Ch10 L17-33).
- **Book 13**: introduced to Team Biscuit (Book 13, Ch959 L60); the Asano clan snatched her from the Network as a coup (Book 13, Ch987 L10); research director (Book 13, Ch992 L116).

## 7. Relationships

- **Mr North**: former committee colleague (Book 5, Ch8 L63).
- **Jason**: brings her down to meet Team Biscuit and vouches for her (Book 13, Ch959 L57-60).
