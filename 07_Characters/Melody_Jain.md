## 1. Summary

Melody Jain is Sophie's mother, who faked her death twenty years before Book 6 and then led the Order of Redeeming Light in the Storm Kingdom, brainwashed into fanaticism by the order's god. Jason's team captures her in Book 8; she is held, treated and slowly softens, ending as a loving mother in Book 13.

## 2. Appearance

- White hair and dark skin; she wears white leather armour stained and torn from shielding the fort (Book 7, Ch32 L7-8).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Fanatical and sharp**: "sharp, fanatical and brainwashed" when Jason met her (Book 13, Ch962 L56).
- **Clean habits**: she likes showers and is "ever at the ready" in her marred armour (Book 7, Ch32 L8).
- **Commanding**: she silences her second with a gesture (Book 8, Ch18 L8).
- **Softening later**: after therapy with her daughter she is "less hostile and manipulative" (Book 10, Ch36 L13).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- An essence user responsible for a fort holding out without supplies (Book 7, Ch32 L7).
- A sword fighter fast enough to dance with Jason's blade (Book 8, Ch34 L28).
- Shackled, she still runs and hides through a cloud-stuff palace (Book 8, Ch42 L5).

## 5. Background

- Faked her death twenty years before Book 6, using a wall panel in Marta Fries's house in Kurdansk (Book 6, Ch47 L510-513). Arabelle dates the story to "around a quarter of a century ago" (Book 9, Ch27 L24).
- The Order of Redeeming Light is a creation of the god Disguise masquerading as Purity; its Flames of Redemption are a modification of lesser vampirism (Book 10, Ch36 L14).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 7**: she leads the enclave, wanting a captive to lure her daughter to Rimaros (Book 7, Ch58 L38-46).
- **Book 8**: leads the local forces (Book 8, Ch18 L3-6); fights Jason (Book 8, Ch34 L28-84); escapes briefly and is recaptured (Book 8, Ch42 L5-7).
- **Book 9-10**: locked in the cloud palace while they seek a cure (Book 9, Ch19 L3); the mind-war of her fractured brainwashing (Book 10, Ch36 L13-14); Jason pins her with his aura (Book 10, Ch40 L22).
- **Book 13**: she gives Jason a motherly smile (Book 13, Ch962 L56).

## 7. Relationships

- **Sophie**: her daughter (Book 9, Ch19 L3; Book 10, Ch36 L13).
- **Arabelle**: helps look after her (Book 10, Ch42 L3).
- **Sendira**: her second (Book 8, Ch18 L8).
- **Jason**: his team holds her captive; by Book 13 she gives him a motherly smile (Book 13, Ch962 L56).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

The more she fights the brainwashing, the more the taint fights back (Book 10, Ch36 L14).

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Controlled and sincere; early on zealot-bright, later warm. Cue: the motherly smile.
