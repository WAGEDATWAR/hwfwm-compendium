## 1. Summary

Jason Asano is the series' protagonist: an Australian from the coastal town of Casselton Beach who, at twenty-three, is pulled across the cosmos to Pallimustus by a failed summoning. He becomes an affliction specialist, a bronze-, silver- and gold-rank adventurer, a king of his own astral kingdom, and finally the administrator of the System that every essence user in the cosmos sees.

## 2. Appearance

- **On arrival**: he wakes bald and naked in a hedge maze (Book 1, Ch1 L8-10). Seeing himself later, he notes his Japanese facial features, inherited from his father, look even sharper than usual, his face is gaunt, and his bald head and absent eyebrows make him look "manic and unhinged" (Book 1, Ch8 L37). His skin is pale and taut.
- **Why**: his body ceased to exist in the deep astral; his soul, being magical, was given a new body in the way a summoned familiar is (Clive, Book 1, Ch69 L85-107).
- **Later**: his face changes with each rank. At gold his "strange, nebulous eyes" draw attention, though they return to the dark eyes he had at iron when he meets Zara (Book 12, Ch12 L20).
- **Clothing**: from Book 3 he buys a Vitesse-style wardrobe from the tailor Gilbert Bertinelli (Book 3, Ch2 L59-64).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Humour and references**: he jokes constantly and quotes films and television. He tells the Builder it and Knowledge "should get together and play Mario Kart" (Book 3, Ch57 L87); he compares a projected face to Emperor Palpatine (Book 9, Ch6 L17). He says "G'day" and "mate" (Book 1, Ch38 L55; Book 13, Ch1016 L21).
- **Self-deprecation**: "I'm only twenty-three but I've been pretty efficient about squandering my opportunities" (Book 1, Ch25 L43). He does not celebrate his birthday, which falls on 1 April: "It's not a celebratory time. I never liked my birthday anyway" (Book 5, Ch20 L25).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- **Essences**: Dark, Blood, Sin and the Doom confluence. His attributes are bound as Power (Blood), Speed (Dark), Spirit (Doom) and Recovery (the fourth essence) (Book 1, Ch99 L67-70).
- **Weapons**: a sword, Dread Salvation, an iron-rank growth item of legendary rarity made by Gary (Book 1, Ch56 L49-52), later reforged by Gary with his mentor Virid and with Jason's soul-bond guiding the work (Book 7, Ch2 L73-74), becoming the sword [Hegemon's Will] with the scabbard Hegemon's Dominion (Book 7, Ch54 L108-120); Virid's surname Martine is Book 6, Ch47 L270. Way of the Reaper martial arts (Book 1, Ch52 L7-11). The set Regalia of the Dark Hegemon includes the Amulet of the Dark Guardian (Book 6, Ch4 L29-36).
- **Abilities seen in the text**: Path of Shadows (a shadow-to-shadow teleport at iron, Book 1, Ch22 L120; at bronze a conjured shadow gate on a regional scale to a place already visited, Book 3, Ch29 L22-35; "a range of roughly two hundred kilometres" when he plans to reach Sydney from Casselton, Book 4 Ch1 L67), Midnight Eyes (awakened Book 1, Ch8 L35), Castigate (Book 1, Ch87 L85) and Verdict (Book 1, Ch99 L24), Punition (Book 1, Ch78 L54), Feast of Absolution (Book 1, Ch22 L84) and Gordon's Harbinger of Doom (Book 5, Ch31 L187).
- **Outworlder racial abilities** (Book 1, Ch1 L76-81): Interface, Quest System, Inventory, Map, Astral Affinity and Mysterious Stranger. Mysterious Stranger gives language adaptation, absorption of essences, awakening stones and skill books, and immunity to identification and tracking effects. Astral Affinity increases resistance to dimension effects and astral forces and increases the effect of dimension abilities and transcendent damage.
- **Familiars**: Shade, Colin and Gordon (see Familiars.md).
- **Titles**: Godless Prophet (Book 2, Ch65 L85), Spirit Warrior (Book 3, Ch21 L149), Indomitable (Book 4, Ch2 L22), Giant Slayer (Book 5, Ch29 L68-72), Reality Hegemon (Book 6, Ch15 L45), Keeper of the Cycle (Book 10, Ch83 L67-73).

## 5. Background

- **Home**: Casselton Beach on the Mid North Coast of New South Wales (Book 4, Ch3 L100). His father Ken is a landscape architect (Book 2, Ch11 L41); his mother Cheryl comes from a large family of mostly rural people (Book 2, Ch20 L18); his older sister Erika and her husband Ian live nearby with their daughter Emi; his brother Kaito and Kaito's wife Amy live next door to his parents' house (notes, Book 4). His uncles are Hiro, the black sheep with minor involvement in organised crime, and Shiro, the favourite (Book 4, Ch1 L66-67).
- **Before Pallimustus**: "a brief stint at university" studying political science (Book 1, Ch7 L26), then Melbourne "the last couple of years" (Book 1, Ch36 L87), seeing little of his family apart from his sister's household (Book 1, Ch52 says only that he had little contact with his family after they fell out), where he is "the cheapest childcare in town". He worked as an assistant manager at a retail bulk office supplier (Book 2, Ch14 L35).
- **Birthday**: 1 April (Book 5, Ch20 L24-26).
- **Arrival**: pulled from his bed in late November by Landemere Vane's failed summoning (Book 4, Ch3 L112).

## 6. Timeline of involvement (summary)

- **Books 1-3 (levels 0-1)**: arrives in Greenstone, joins the Adventure Society, forms the team that becomes Team Biscuit, gains Shade, Colin and Gordon, reaches bronze, and dies in the Builder's tower.
- **Books 4-6 (level 2)**: wakes in the old Casselton hospital in early June 2019, reveals magic to his family, rescues Farrah, ranks to silver after the Makassar events, claims transformation zones, is killed by Shako and leaves Earth in autumn 2021.
- **Books 7-9 (level 2)**: returns to Pallimustus as the great surge begins; the One Day War; becomes an astral king (Book 8, Ch65 L85-93); the Battle of Yaresh; takes in messengers.
- **Books 10-11 (level 3)**: the Cardinas expedition, the Death Pact, the Cup of Heroes, the transformation zone, and years inside his own soul fighting great astral beings.
- **Books 12-13 (level 3)**: a gold-rank "prime avatar of an astral nexus" (Book 12, Ch8 L100) who returns to Earth in 2038.

See the Master Timeline for dates.

## 7. Relationships

- **Mentors and early friends**: Rufus, Gary and Farrah; Rufus runs his field assessment (Book 1, Ch38 L45) and guides his first meditation (Book 1, Ch31 L120-133); Farrah trains his perception and aura projection with memory games and other exercises (Book 1, Ch40 L31, L50-52; Book 1, Ch41 L35).
- **Team Biscuit**: Humphrey, Sophie, Belinda, Clive and Neil.
- **Family**: Emi, Kaito (dies in Book 5), Amy, Erika.
- **Familiars**: Shade, Colin and Gordon.
- **Others**: Dawn, Zara, Anna Tilden, Boris, Knowledge, the Builder.

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

- **Level 2**: he has died four times by Book 6 (the Church of Death's certificate, Book 7, Ch17 L104; the card in Book 10, Ch38 L73); he is an astral king (Book 7, Ch17 L104-105).
- **Level 3**: his transformation into a prime avatar of an astral nexus (Book 12, Ch8 L100-104). Knowledge has been using him as a key part of her plan to invade Earth for half a millennium (Book 13, Ch1015 L78; the plan's origin, Ch1017 L14-15). At the end of Book 13 Umber's ritual is designed to bind his consciousness into the prime avatar, and Jason walks to the standing stones knowingly, promising Knowledge "the next time we meet, Goddess, will be the day you die" (Book 13, Ch1024 L153).

## 9. Roleplay notes
