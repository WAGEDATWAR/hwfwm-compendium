## 1. Summary

A French swordsman from Saint-Étienne whom Rufus picked out young, now an upper-silver adventurer and Emi's love interest in 2038.

## 2. Facts

- Pacing a rooftop garden in Saint-Étienne while Emi eats a sandwich and teases him; Shade speaks from Emi's shadow (Book 13, Ch971 L3-28).
- Jason has watched him grow from a boy to a man through the domain (Book 13, Ch971 L50); Rufus picked him out early for his swordsmanship and self-motivation (Book 13, Ch971 L52).
- His aura is in the upper reaches of silver, close to gold (Book 13, Ch971 L55).
- Jason's interest in him is as a match for Emi, and rank matters for the ageing question (Book 13, Ch971 L56).
- He is a member of the Baudrillard family; Chloe Baudrillard, an epidemiologist Jason befriended, is of the same family (Book 11, Ch68 L633; Book 5, Ch2 L9).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Anxious and earnest around Emi; he is flustered by Jason's attention and by Emi's teasing.
