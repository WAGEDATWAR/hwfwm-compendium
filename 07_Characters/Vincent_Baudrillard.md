## 1. Summary

Vincent Baudrillard is a French swordsman from Saint-Étienne whom Rufus picked out young, now an upper-silver adventurer and Emi's love interest in 2038. Anxious and earnest around Emi, he is flustered by Jason's attention and by her teasing.

## 2. Appearance

- Increasingly dishevelled hair that "only further set off his rakish good looks" (Book 13, Ch971 L3).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Anxious**: pacing around a rooftop garden while Emi eats a sandwich and teases him (Book 13, Ch971 L3-28).
- **Driven**: his drive is more valuable than his talent; the Remore teaching method gives the most time to those with motivation (Book 13, Ch971 L51-52).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Swordsman, close to gold: his aura is in the upper reaches of silver (Book 13, Ch971 L55).
- Rufus picked him out early for his swordsmanship and self-motivation (Book 13, Ch971 L52).

## 5. Background

- Member of the Baudrillard family; Chloe Baudrillard, an epidemiologist Jason befriended, is of the same family (Book 11, Ch68 L633).
- Jason has watched him grow from a boy to a man through the domain (Book 13, Ch971 L50).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 13**: the Saint-Étienne rooftop scene (Book 13, Ch971 L3-56).

## 7. Relationships

- **Emi**: love interest; Jason's interest is as a match for her, and rank matters for the ageing question (Book 13, Ch971 L56).
- **Rufus**: his mentor (Book 13, Ch971 L52).
