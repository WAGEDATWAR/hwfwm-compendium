## 1. Summary

Simon McKean is the former head of the Earth researchers in the Network's grid workgroup. By Book 13 he works with the vampires from a research complex beneath a French castle, preparing for a decade to fight Jason, and is Jason's final earthly adversary.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in the lines read.

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Obsessed with the fight**: Boris warns Jason that McKean "has spent the last decade preparing for this fight. To him, battling you is not a means but an end in itself" (Book 13, Ch1000 L104).
- **Cunning**: he baited the coalition into the fight, and no one knows why (Book 13, Ch1003 L9).
- **Controlled**: he watches Jason and two familiars move through his tunnels from a monitoring room (Book 13, Ch1010 L3).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Expert in the grid: "about as expert in it as anyone but you" (Book 13, Ch1003 L47) and understands it better than anyone other than Farrah (Book 13, Ch1006 L15).
- Created vampire-magitech abominations by mixing modern magitech with blood magic (Book 13, Ch1006 L60).
- Modified a vine to revive the undead and bound souls of vampires and minions to it (Book 13, Ch1008 L20-27).
- He took Gerling from the vampire queen and used him as an agent (Book 13, Ch1008 L33).

## 5. Background

- Head of the Earth researchers, ranked only behind Farrah in the workgroup (Book 13, Ch970 L38).
- He and Farrah repaired the grid together after the Engineers of Ascension sabotaged it (Book 13, Ch1003 L31).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 13**: Farrah describes him (Book 13, Ch970 L38); Boris's warning (Book 13, Ch1000 L104); Michael and Jason plan the strike (Book 13, Ch1002 L40-42; Book 13, Ch1003 L4-92); Jason will "confront McKean tomorrow" (Book 13, Ch1005 L104); the castle was a Network operations centre (Book 13, Ch1006 L15); the vine (Book 13, Ch1008 L5-33); his broadcast breaks up at the collapse (Book 13, Ch1014 L75).

## 7. Relationships

- **Farrah**: the only one in Jason's circle who knows him personally (Book 13, Ch1003 L31).
- **The vampires**: he is already working with them (Book 13, Ch1003 L86).
- **Gerling**: his agent (Book 13, Ch1008 L33).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

Jason says McKean is "just a warm-up compared to what comes next" (Book 13, Ch1005 L121).
