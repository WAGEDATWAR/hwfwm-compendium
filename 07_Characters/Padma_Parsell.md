## 1. Summary

Padma Parsell is an adventurer from Remore Academy and Farrah's half-disciple, who leads a Vitesse team in the City of Fallen Echoes. Keen, grieving and quick to speak her mind, she treats Jason as a rival after Farrah's death.

## 2. Appearance

- Onyx skin and fiery eyes typical of her people; unaffected by heat (Book 2, Ch39 L13).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Keen and grieving**: Farrah was her idol and mentor (Book 2, Ch38 L40-41); she lets Jason vent her frustration (Book 2, Ch38 L43).
- **Obedient to instructors**: Instructor Gabriel's voice triggers conditioned obedience (Book 2, Ch38 L66).
- **Ignores interruptions**: when Lance calls out under his breath, she ignores him (Book 2, Ch38 L35).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Leader of a Vitesse team of Rufus's juniors (Book 3, Ch1 L87).

## 5. Background

- Trained at Remore Academy a few years behind Rufus (Book 2, Ch38 L40).
- She had waited for Farrah's return to Vitesse and, when Farrah died, joined Emir's call for adventurers (Book 2, Ch38 L42).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 2**: cadet Padma Parsell (Book 2, Ch38 L65); Farrah's parents seek her out (Book 2, Ch38 L84); her team joins the trial late (Book 2, Ch55 L69).
- **Book 3**: wants to test itself against Jason's team; standoffish at first, warming up (Book 3, Ch1 L87-88); her team leader builds a rivalry with Valdis (Book 3, Ch1 L89).

## 7. Relationships

- **Farrah**: mentor (Book 2, Ch38 L40-41).
- **Jason**: friend and rival (Book 2, Ch39 L42).
- **Valdis**: sword rival (Book 3, Ch1 L89).
