## 1. Summary

An adventurer from Remore Academy and Farrah's half-disciple, who leads a Vitesse team in the City of Fallen Echoes.

## 2. Facts

- Cadet Padma Parsell (Book 2, Ch38 L65) trained at Remore Academy a few years behind Rufus; Farrah was her idol and mentor (Book 2, Ch38 L40-41).
- She had waited for Farrah's return to Vitesse and, when Farrah died, joined Emir's call for adventurers (Book 2, Ch38 L42).
- Jason lets her vent her frustration (Book 2, Ch38 L43); Farrah's parents seek her out (Book 2, Ch38 L84).
- She has onyx skin and fiery eyes typical of her people and is unaffected by heat (Book 2, Ch39 L13).
- Her team of Rufus's juniors joins the trial late (Book 2, Ch55 L69) and later wants to test itself against Jason (Book 3, Ch1 L87).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Keen, grieving and quick to speak her mind (Book 2, Ch38 L35).
