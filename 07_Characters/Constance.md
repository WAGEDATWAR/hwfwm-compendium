## 1. Summary

Constance is Emir Bahadir's chief of staff and later his wife. She runs his cloud palace and treasure-hunting organisation, steadies Jason's social events, and leaves to train under Callum Morse until she reaches gold rank. From Book 6 she is a gold-ranker and a fixture of Emir's team.

## 2. Appearance

- Normally shoulder-length brown hair, which she cropped short before reaching gold; pale skin and light green and brown armour (Book 6, Ch47 L542).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Steadying**: she subtly indicates which guests will not respond well to Jason's social graces (Book 2, Ch69 L54).
- **Detail-oriented**: "the detail-oriented half of the pairing, which sometimes made her closer to a babysitter than she would admit to liking" (Book 10, Ch67 L15).
- **Dry and firm with Emir**: she gives her husband "a stern look" (Book 10, Ch67 L14); Jason checks with her rather than him (Book 10, Ch10 L80-81).
- **Pragmatic**: "What we missed doesn't matter. What we do now does" (Book 10, Ch78 L28).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Silver rank in Book 2; she holds off three enemies in the island battle, one the construct that killed Farrah (Book 2, Ch76 L55).
- Gold rank by Book 9, only for a couple of years when Jason hides his aura from her (Book 9, Ch50 L92).
- She is part of the reformed gold-rank team for the Cardinas mission (Book 10, Ch59 L4).

## 5. Background

- Emir's chief of staff, "the object of his affections"; she took leave and went with Callum Morse, intending not to return until she reached gold rank (Book 6, Ch47 L494).
- Emir and Constance had moved around each other for years with a power imbalance between employer and employee, gold and silver (Book 9, Ch50 L49).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1-2**: waits at the palace exit with Emir and Jason (Book 1, Ch108 L72); among the strike force (Book 2, Ch74 L18; Book 2, Ch76 L3); watches Sophie (Book 2, Ch24 L22).
- **Book 6**: returns at gold rank (Book 6, Ch47 L542-571).
- **Book 9**: trained with Callum (Book 9, Ch50 L51); the office meeting with Jason (Book 9, Ch50 L68).
- **Book 10**: replaces Callum on the mission (Book 10, Ch59 L4); the sphere (Book 10, Ch66 L85).

## 7. Relationships

- **Emir**: employer, then husband; "Emir's wife" and chief of staff (Book 10, Ch66 L85; Book 10, Ch9 L9).
- **Callum**: her trainer, who puts a calming hand on her arm (Book 6, Ch47 L549).
- **Sophie and Humphrey**: she warns Humphrey she will tell his mother (Book 6, Ch47 L571).
