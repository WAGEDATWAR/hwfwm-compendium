## 1. Summary

Itsuki Tiwari is a young Japanese essence user from the Tiwari clan, an admirer of Jason with a doom confluence. He joins the Asano combat team in Book 5, avoids battle after the Berlin deaths, and in 2038 is a gold-ranker affliction specialist protected by Nigel's nine-person unit. He is Akari Asano's husband, though in Book 13 he moves out.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in detail in the lines read; he greets Jason with a respectful bow (Book 5, Ch38 L5).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Nervous and polite**: "I apologise for being the one to meet you" (Book 5, Ch38 L7); his aura shudders with anxiety (Book 5, Ch38 L6).
- **Admiring**: "my essences are dark, blood and omen, with a doom confluence" (Book 5, Ch38 L50) because he followed Jason's exploits.
- **Honest in pain**: he says his wife's life "doesn't have room for me anymore" (Book 13, Ch1004 L18).
- **Regretful**: he turned to monster cores out of fear and trauma, then came to regret it (Book 13, Ch1007 L43-44).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Essences dark, blood and omen forming a doom confluence (Book 5, Ch38 L50).
- Stealthy scout in Book 5, a poor fit for the combat team (Book 5, Ch46 L16).
- New to gold rank in 2038; a traditional backline affliction specialist who can "blanket massive areas in death" faster and more reliably than Jason (Book 13, Ch1007 L43-44).
- One of the first to have his monster core taint purged (Book 13, Ch1007 L43).

## 5. Background

- Son of Koya, the youngest of three Tiwari elders; Denji is patriarch and Souta the middle brother (Book 5, Ch39 L3).
- After the deaths of Asya, Greg and Kaito he avoided regular battle and turned to monster cores (Book 13, Ch1007 L43).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 5**: meets Jason at the resort and leads him to the patriarch (Book 5, Ch38 L5-92); watches Jason use his portal (Book 5, Ch39 L70); the sea cave visit (Book 5, Ch40 L3); packs his storage in a panic (Book 5, Ch42 L43); joins the combat team (Book 5, Ch46 L16).
- **Book 13**: leaves Akari (Book 13, Ch1004 L4-24); with Nigel's unit in Multan (Book 13, Ch1007 L43-46); Jason says Valdis can help Akari back to sword mastery (Book 13, Ch1022 L84).

## 7. Relationships

- **Akari**: his wife (Book 13, Ch1004 L5).
- **Koya and the Tiwari elders**: his father and uncles (Book 5, Ch39 L3).
- **Jason**: idol turned colleague (Book 13, Ch1007 L43).
- **Nigel**: commander in the vampire war (Book 13, Ch1007 L45).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

He tells Akari that "sometimes the right thing to do is let go" (Book 13, Ch1004 L20).
