## 1. Summary

Garth Larosse is the High Priest of Undeath, a nine-foot skeletal undead built from the skeletons of a dozen monsters, who leads the undead host against Jason in the Book 10 and 11 campaign.

## 2. Appearance

- Some nine feet tall, a skeleton wrapped in a dark green cloak with glowing red eyes, built from the skeletons of a dozen monsters (Book 10, Ch80 L42). The robe's drape hides what form the body takes (Book 11, Ch8 L20).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Cold and strategic**: he leaves the mindless hordes clawing at the wall until defences break (Book 10, Ch80 L45).
- **Resentful of Death's interference**: Death drives back the territory Undeath claimed (Book 10, Ch88 L58).
- **Cautious in command**: he directs his undead "with care and subtlety" so failed commands hide his purpose (Book 11, Ch30 L66).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Higher-order undead leader; priests of Undeath range from ordinary essence users to undead mockeries, including vampires commanding ghoul armies and liches (Book 11, Ch28 L4).
- He designates a priest to control the avatar while he handles Asano (Book 11, Ch29 L12).

## 5. Background

- Leader of the priests of Undeath; most who knew his nature are already slain and turned into his puppets or parts of his body (Book 11, Ch8 L20).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 10**: Death intervenes against his territory (Book 10, Ch88 L58); introduces himself as "Garth Larosse, High Priest of Undeath" (Book 10, Ch92 L65-66).
- **Book 11**: finds the avatar and regains the power to raise revenants (Book 11, Ch27 L67); tries to swarm Asano with undead (Book 11, Ch30 L56); tells a subordinate priestess he made "new allies" (Book 11, Ch24 L59).

## 7. Relationships

- **Death**: his god's nemesis (Book 10, Ch88 L58).
- **Jason**: his opponent, who answers him flippantly (Book 10, Ch92 L66).
