## 1. Summary

The High Priest of Undeath, a nine-foot skeletal undead built from the skeletons of a dozen monsters.

## 2. Facts

- A priest of Undeath, nine feet tall, a skeleton in a dark green cloak with glowing red eyes, built from the skeletons of a dozen monsters (Book 10, Ch80 L42).
- His god's nemesis, Death, intervenes and drives back the territory Undeath claimed (Book 10, Ch88 L58).
- He introduces himself as "Garth Larosse, High Priest of Undeath" to Jason, who answers flippantly (Book 10, Ch92 L65-66).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Cold, ceremonial and resentful of Death's interference.
