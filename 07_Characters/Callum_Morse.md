## 1. Summary

A gold-rank stealth assassin, former teammate of Emir and of Rufus's parents, whose long hunt for Sophie's mother Melody Jain drives his later scenes.

## 2. Facts

- Introduced by Emir as a gold-ranker who, if he does not want to be seen, cannot be seen by anyone short of diamond rank (Book 2, Ch34 L91).
- Described as "the gold-ranked assassination specialist" and the group's best scout (Book 2, Ch76 L7); his attacks on a gold-rank priest land strongly but do not kill at once (Book 2, Ch76 L41).
- He, Gabriel and Arabelle Remore have been companions for decades (Book 2, Ch76 L40). Emir, Arabelle, Gabriel and Callum were an old team (Book 3, Ch24 L35; Book 8, Ch27 L26).
- An avid monster-hunter, he is one of the few gold-rankers who obsessively raise their strength (Book 6, Ch47 L495).
- He stops Sophie and Humphrey from running off after the Order of the Reaper: "I'm stopping you until you are ready. Hate me if you like" (Book 6, Ch47 L581), and explains the order split from the cult and became self-serving assassins (Book 6, Ch47 L592).
- His fixation on Melody Jain, Sophie's mother, is because he is connected to her: she kept him as her only real connection to the order (Book 9, Ch27 L63); he lost track of her and has searched ever since (Book 9, Ch27 L91).
- Jason avoids his petitions to see Melody, and finds his aura dangerously unstable even after things are in the open (Book 8, Ch63 L140; Book 10, Ch58 L7).
- In Book 13 he conjures rats to track a creature through a pipe (Book 13, Ch958 L75).

## 3. Timeline of involvement

- Book 2: scouts for the strike on the Builder cult's island (Book 2, Ch76 L7-46).
- Book 3: reports on the cultists' escape and the Purity evidence (Book 3, Ch1 L77-82).
- Book 6: stops Sophie and Humphrey in Kurdansk (Book 6, Ch47 L521-592).
- Books 8-10: petitions Jason to see Melody (Book 8, Ch63 L140; Book 10, Ch58 L7).
- Book 13: part of the expedition (Book 13, Ch958 L75).

## 4. Roleplay notes

Quiet, patient and unfazed; he lets a hard decision stand and speaks plainly ("Sensible", Book 2, Ch36 L79). His grief and fixation on Melody are the one place his calm slips.
