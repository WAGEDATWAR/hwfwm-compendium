## 1. Summary

Jack Gerling is a US Network category-four (gold-rank) essence user with an explosion-based power set, who kills Jason's brother, lover and best friend in Book 5, fights beside and against Jason in Book 6, becomes the vampire queen Elizabeth's blood slave, and is freed and turned by McKean into a nearly immortal agent. Jason kills him in Book 13.

## 2. Appearance

- A bushy-bearded man rubbing his beard unhappily after a shower in a Network facility (Book 5, Ch47 L26).
- As Elizabeth's blood slave he looks "much as he had in life, but with blood-red sclera and swarthier skin" (Book 11, Ch68 L2267).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Gruff, bellowing and friendly to those who are kind to him**: "You're alright, Cleary... My last handler would have thrown me the chicken like I was a monster in a pit. Most people are scared of me" (Book 5, Ch47 L102).
- **Pugnacious**: "I don't mind kicking their asses back and forth a little" (Book 5, Ch47 L110); he is annoyed by being managed and "not entirely at peace" with joining Jason's push (Book 6, Ch43 L7).
- **Cynical about his handlers**: category fours "were beholden to whoever could provide the coins to keep them alive", and a handler is easier to replace (Book 5, Ch47 L106).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- **Essences**: might, potent and vast, producing the onslaught confluence; the potent essence is rare, the vast essence "so unheard of" the Network checked records hundreds of years old (Book 5, Ch47 L29-30). He could down a plane and everyone in it with a snap (Book 5, Ch47 L30).
- **Explosion flight**: periodic explosions throw him through the air faster than a helicopter (Book 5, Ch48 L103; Book 6, Ch1 L13).
- **Explosive fist**: a broad wave of force when he hits (Book 5, Ch50 L71), "devastating" with an explosive fist power (Book 5, Ch50 L35).
- **Aura and recovery**: he extends his aura to shield his men from Jason's suppression (Book 6, Ch42 L20), and his gold-rank recovery closes wounds and purges poison fast (Book 6, Ch36 L33).
- **Poor fighter**: "one of the reasons Gerling had never learned to fight very well was that his explosion powers were intuitive and effective"; he used a handful of abilities because cores meant he never had to learn more (Book 13, Ch973 L46). Farrah and Jason are "much more adept with their powers" (Book 5, Ch50 L41).
- **Immortality** (after McKean): when he dies he liquefies, and the substance burns on exposure to air; he has been killed several times (Book 13, Ch998 L110, per Farrah).

## 5. Background

- **A category four**: one of two chosen by the US branches pooling cores for years (Book 5, Ch47 L29).
- **The Network's killer**: the Network "dug up Jack Gerling and sent him to kill Asano's brother, his lover and his best friend" (Book 13, Ch967 L25).
- **Trained afterwards**: he gets a team of trainers to "refresh the skills drilled into him years ago", keeps his training quiet and his recreation loud (Book 5, Ch68 L14).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 5**: he says he does not mind "kicking their asses back and forth a little" in the war with the vampires (Book 5, Ch47 L110); his explosion flight outpaces Kaito's helicopter (Book 5, Ch48 L103); he fights Jason and Farrah (Book 5, Ch50 L35-41).
- **Book 6**: in the Slovakian dome with Chen and Guo (Book 6, Ch1 L3; Book 6, Ch6 L26); he is influenced by the vampire Tran (Book 6, Ch8 L48); with Jason's push he agrees to join the coalition (Book 6, Ch42 L66; Book 6, Ch43 L7). He grabs Barbou and flies him back (Book 6, Ch30 L29-44).
- **Book 6 end**: Noreth kills him in the crater (Book 6, Ch46 L112-127), but his immortality brings him back.
- **Book 11-13**: Elizabeth's blood slave (Book 11, Ch68 L2267); freed by McKean and acting as his agent (Book 13, Ch1008 L33); killed by Jason when the blood vine collapses (Book 13, Ch1012-1014, L72).

## 7. Relationships

- **Cleary**: his handler, whom he squeezes with his aura (Book 6, Ch19 L35) but likes (Book 5, Ch47 L102).
- **Barbou**: he beats him on a plane and demands answers (Book 6, Ch30 L29-44).
- **Elizabeth**: "his mistress, the vampire queen Elizabeth" (Book 13, Ch973 L49).
- **Jason**: enemy and uneasy ally; Jason kills him (Book 13, Ch1014 L72).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

Book 13 states that the Network "dug up Jack Gerling and sent him to kill Asano's brother, his lover and his best friend" (Book 13, Ch967 L25). His fate in Book 13 is described in Ch1011-1014.

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Cynical, practical and quick with his fists; friendly to anyone who feeds him and deeply unsentimental about everyone else. Cue: "You're alright, Cleary."
