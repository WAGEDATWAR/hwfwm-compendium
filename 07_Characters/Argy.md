## 1. Summary

A fruit grower in Palisaros who calls himself Argy and brings Jason to the mayor.

## 2. Facts

- "Emmett Dillivan, but folks just call me Argy", after the fruit he is eating, which looks like a large tangerine (Book 7, Ch13 L18-20).
- His family has grown the fruit "longer than most can remember" (Book 7, Ch13 L20).
- He tells Jason to talk to Pelli and offers to take him (Book 7, Ch13 L28).
- Jason calls the place "a genuine paradise" (Book 7, Ch13 L24).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Easygoing and proud of his town; he treats visitors as guests until they give a reason not to.
