## 1. Summary

Argy (Emmett Dillivan) is a fruit grower in Palisaros who takes Jason, Rufus and Farrah into town and to the mayor, Pelli. Easygoing and proud of his town, he treats visitors as guests.

Not described in the lines read.

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Laconic**: Jason "happily fell into Argy's laconic pace" (Book 7, Ch13 L31).
- **Hospitable**: he greets everyone he passes in the sealed streets (Book 7, Ch13 L32).
- **Friendly nickname**: "Emmett Dillivan, but folks just call me Argy" (Book 7, Ch13 L18-20).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Not an essence user in the lines read; his family has grown the fruit "longer than most can remember" (Book 7, Ch13 L20).

## 5. Background
