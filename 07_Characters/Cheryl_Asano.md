## 1. Summary

Cheryl Asano is Jason's mother, from a large Australian farming and mining family. She is the one in the family fascinated with Japan, a strong-willed realtor who shows up in Book 4 stunned at her dead son's return.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in the lines read.

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Strong-willed**: she flashes "a well-recognised look of dissatisfaction" at Jason's poke but calms herself (Book 4, Ch44 L56).
- **Stern**: Jason jokes "Mum is quite stern" (Book 4, Ch70 L86).
- **Grieving**: she looks at Jason "like she'd seen a ghost" (Book 4, Ch31 L6) and hugs him tightly once she steels her nerve (Book 4, Ch35 L84).
- **Overworked**: she hasn't been into the office in two days, unlike her (Book 4, Ch35 L52).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- She is "the pre-eminent upscale realtor in the Greater Casselton area" (Book 4, Ch24 L42).
- She is non-essence in the lines read; portal travel sickens her (Book 4, Ch44 L59).

## 5. Background

- Her family are "miners and farmers, tracing their bloodline back through bushrangers, convicts, and indigenous Australians"; she was the one fascinated with Japan (Book 1, Ch1 L88).
- Her brother is Robert (Book 4, Ch31 L3); her mother makes a miraculous recovery (Book 4, Ch35 L52).
- Jason is told his parents divorced a year ago and that his mother moved to Castle Reach (Book 4, Ch4 L105).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 4**: the hospital waiting room (Book 4, Ch31 L3-6); the hug (Book 4, Ch35 L84); the houseboat portal (Book 4, Ch44 L54-59); the family meeting (Book 4, Ch45 L73-76).
