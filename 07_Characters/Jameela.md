## 1. Summary

Jameela is a priestess of Undeath, Garth Larosse's most trusted subordinate, tall and elegant, capable and quick to learn. She helps lead the undead army in Cardinas and the desert, and dies fighting Taika when the priesthood collapses.

## 2. Facts

- **First impression**: a "tall, beautiful woman" with long legs, whose addition to Garth's group left him "almost sad to have given up his flesh"; "capable and intelligent, eager and quick to learn", the opposite of another priest he was forced to bring (Book 10, Ch88 L65).
- **Dress**: an elegant combat robe and heavy boots (Book 11, Ch24 L28).
- **Garth's feelings**: Garth feels a longing for her despite his unliving body but never acts on it, "not allowing himself such a lack of discipline" (Book 11, Ch24 L28).
- **Knowledge of Hero**: she says she has "never seen anyone who has drunk from the Cup of Heroes" and reads it as Hero's miracle at the brink of defeat (Book 10, Ch88 L70).
- **Before the god**: she and Garth drop to their knees when Undeath appears as a living man in black robes with empty sockets and a crimson spark (Book 10, Ch88 L76), and she bows to Undeath and then Garth (Book 10, Ch88 L97).
- **In battle**: she silences a skeleton's communication magic (Book 10, Ch91 L63), watches the divine giants clash (Book 10, Ch92 L46) and points out that crushing the undead at the wall will cost time to replenish (Book 10, Ch92 L51).
- **Perception**: she warns "He's not bluffing, High Priest... The world feels wrong, somehow. Like standing on a frozen lake as the ice starts to crack" (Book 10, Ch92 L84).
- **Intelligence work**: she extracts what she can about Asano from prisoners, who say his aura is like a gold-ranker's in strength and coverage (Book 11, Ch24 L43).
- **Negotiation with Boris**: in the desert she tells Boris "You are not in a position to negotiate" and "You're bluffing... You want Asano dead and he knows it" (Book 11, Ch26 L20, L26).
- **Spotting the enemy**: she is the first to point out two strange floating vehicles speeding across the desert (Book 11, Ch27 L69).
- **Death**: "One of the final priests to die was Jameela, Garth's most trusted subordinate. She refused to go down easy... her beauty destroyed by Taika's fists and her unwillingness to accept that her cause was lost" (Book 11, Ch32 L79).

## 3. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 10**: joins Garth's group; the siege of Cardinas (Book 10, Ch88-92).
- **Book 11**: the desert campaign and the fall of the priesthood (Book 11, Ch24-32).

## 4. Roleplay notes

Brisk, intelligent and unsentimental: she reads a battlefield and an opponent quickly and keeps her devotion practical.
