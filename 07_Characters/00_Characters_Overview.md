## 3. Conventions

- **Names**: the first mention gives the full name; later mentions use the usual name. Variant spellings are in the Glossary.
- **Voice notes** quote the manner of speech in paraphrase, not long passages from the books.
- **Secrets and spoilers** are gathered in one section per file.
- **Ages** follow the Master Timeline's calendar anchors.
