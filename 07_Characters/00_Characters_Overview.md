## 1. How the character files work

Each file follows the project's character template: Summary, Appearance, Personality and voice, Powers and skills as world facts, Background, Timeline of involvement, Relationships, Secrets and spoiler notes, Roleplay notes. Powers are described as facts of the fiction, never as numbers or game rules. Material after Book 3 is placed in "Later developments" sections so a scene can be set at any point.

## 3. Conventions

- **Names**: the first mention gives the full name; later mentions use the usual name. Variant spellings are in the Glossary.
- **Voice notes** quote the manner of speech in paraphrase, not long passages from the books.
- **Secrets and spoilers** are gathered in one section per file.
- **Ages** follow the Master Timeline's calendar anchors.
