## 1. Summary

Helsveth is a draconian World-Phoenix servant being groomed to become First Sister as Dawn moves to the Hierophants. Formal, deferential and earnest, she is still learning initiative; by Book 11 she is the current prime vessel.

## 2. Appearance

- A draconian with red and gold scales, in a white robe like Dawn's, humble beyond what is usual for her people (Book 3, Ch69 L920).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Reverent**: she says serving the World-Phoenix is a fulfilling life and the communion the greatest thrill and honour of her life (Book 3, Ch69 L985, L1035).
- **Earnest**: Dawn rebukes her for challenges that were all designed for her (Book 3, Ch69 L954-960).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- A servant of the World-Phoenix and its prime vessel by Book 11 (Book 11, Ch68 L2528).

## 5. Background

- Dawn says Helsveth will become First Sister while Dawn joins the Hierophants (Book 3, Ch69 L922).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 3**: introduced with Dawn (Book 3, Ch69 L920-1035).
- **Book 11**: named as the one the World-Phoenix might have sent instead of Dawn (Book 11, Ch22 L32); present at the gathering of vessels (Book 11, Ch68 L2528).
- **Book 13**: tells the team the World-Phoenix agreement ends when the bridge is complete (Book 13, Ch1024 L75-81).

## 7. Relationships

- **Dawn**: mentor and predecessor (Book 3, Ch69 L922).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

The agreement with the World-Phoenix ends when the bridge is complete (Book 13, Ch1024 L75-81).
