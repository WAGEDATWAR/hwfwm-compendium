## 1. Summary

A draconian World-Phoenix servant being groomed to become First Sister as Dawn moves to the Hierophants.

## 2. Facts

- A draconian with red and gold scales, in a white robe like Dawn's, humble beyond what is usual for her people (Book 3, Ch69 L920).
- Dawn says Helsveth will become First Sister while Dawn joins the Hierophants (Book 3, Ch69 L922) and rebukes her for challenges that were all designed for her (Book 3, Ch69 L954-960).
- She says serving the World-Phoenix is a fulfilling life and calls the communion the greatest thrill and honour of her life (Book 3, Ch69 L985, L1035).
- In Book 11 she is named as the one the World-Phoenix might have sent instead of Dawn (Book 11, Ch22 L32); she is present at the Book 11 gathering of vessels (Book 11, Ch68 L2528).
- In Book 13 she tells the team the World-Phoenix agreement ends when the bridge is complete (Book 13, Ch1024 L75-81).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Formal and deferential, earnest; she is still learning initiative.
