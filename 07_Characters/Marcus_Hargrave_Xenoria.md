## 1. Summary

Marcus Hargrave Xenoria is a massive leonid troubleshooter for the Adventure Society's continental council, sent to Yaresh to break the aristocrats' hold. Booming, cheerful and blunt, his bluster hides a politician's skill with leverage.

- A massive leonid carrying a huge axe (Book 12, Ch15 L76).
