## 1. Summary

A massive leonid troubleshooter for the Adventure Society's continental council, sent to Yaresh to break the aristocrats' hold and an ally of Jason there.

## 2. Facts

- Introduces himself as "an adventurer and the man the Adventure Society saw fit to deploy to your city" (Book 10, Ch47 L76); reads Team Biscuit's complaint and wonders if a familiar picked the name (Book 10, Ch47 L78).
- A massive leonid who carries a huge axe; "a high-level agent of the Adventure Society's continental council, Marcus was a troubleshooter and enforcer" (Book 12, Ch15 L76).
- He confronts Gormanston Bynes: "I was warned that you might give me trouble" (Book 10, Ch48 L10); his arrival and the support he wins from figures such as Gormanston leave the aristocrats scattered (Book 10, Ch58 L58-61).
- He is accommodating to Jason, which unsettles Jason's allies (Book 10, Ch50 L51; Book 10, Ch52 L4-6), and takes part in the Cardinas planning (Book 10, Ch59 L12; Book 10, Ch60 L13-46).
- In Book 12 he is dispatched to Yaresh again on hearing of Jason's return (Book 12, Ch15 L76).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Booming, cheerful and blunt; his bluster hides a politician's skill with leverage.
