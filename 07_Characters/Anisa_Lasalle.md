## 1. Summary

Anisa Lasalle is a zealous elf priestess of Purity from the Lasalle family who guides Rufus's party in Book 1, is thrown out for killing a captive, and dies serving the Builder's cult alliance in Book 3. Cold, righteous and quick to anger, she treats mercy as weakness.

## 2. Appearance

- An elf, platinum-haired and fair-skinned, in fitted white adventuring gear (Book 2, Ch10 L92; Book 3, Ch41 L53).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Zealous**: Rufus calls her "Zealous girl. Dangerously committed" (Book 1, Ch65 L132).
- **Ruthless**: Farrah says "Anisa killed him before he could get a word out" (Book 1, Ch30 L26).
- **Defiant of correction**: Gary tells her "You don't get a say in what we do anymore, Anisa... You're out" (Book 1, Ch30 L86-93).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- In the Book 3 fight she is "a near-perfect counter to Jason", protecting herself and cleansing afflictions (Book 3, Ch58 L30).

## 5. Background

- A temporary addition to Rufus's party at the start of the books (Book 1, Ch19 L74).
- After Purity is implicated in the cult she vanishes with the archbishop and much of the Lasalle family (Book 3, Ch1 L106).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: Rufus's party (Book 1, Ch19-30).
- **Book 2**: runs the temple's response to a star seed incident ("We are evacuating no one", Book 2, Ch10 L102); leads church militants to claim an abandoned site (Book 2, Ch37 L105-114).
- **Book 3**: meets the cult's Timos at the Vane estate (Book 3, Ch41 L51-76); stands near Archbishop Hendren (Book 3, Ch54 L23-24); dies in the battle (Book 3, Ch58-60 L5).

## 7. Relationships

- **Rufus and Farrah**: former party leaders (Book 1, Ch30 L26).
- **Nicolas Hendren**: her archbishop (Book 3, Ch54 L23-24).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

Jason notes most of the people he first met in this world are dead (Book 3, Ch60 L5).
