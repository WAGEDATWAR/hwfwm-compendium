## 1. Summary

A zealous elf priestess of Purity from the Lasalle family who guides Rufus's party in Book 1, is thrown out for killing a captive, and dies serving the Builder's cult alliance in Book 3.

## 2. Facts

- A priestess of Purity, an elf, platinum-haired and fair-skinned, in fitted white adventuring gear (Book 2, Ch10 L92; Book 3, Ch41 L53); she begins the books as a temporary addition to Rufus's party (Book 1, Ch19 L74).
- Rufus calls her "Zealous girl. Dangerously committed" (Book 1, Ch65 L132). She kills a captive before he can speak (Book 1, Ch30 L26), tries to snatch an item and is stopped by Gary: "You don't get a say in what we do anymore, Anisa... You're out" (Book 1, Ch30 L86-93).
- In Book 2 she runs the temple's response to a star seed incident ("We are evacuating no one", Book 2, Ch10 L102) and leads church militants to claim an abandoned site (Book 2, Ch37 L105-114).
- After Purity is implicated in the Builder cult she vanishes with the archbishop and much of the Lasalle family (Book 3, Ch1 L106). She meets the cult's Timos at the Vane estate (Book 3, Ch41 L51-76) and stands near Archbishop Hendren in the cult camp (Book 3, Ch54 L23-24).
- In the Book 3 fight she is a near-perfect counter to Jason, protecting herself and cleansing afflictions (Book 3, Ch58 L30); she dies there, and Jason notes most of the people he first met in this world are dead (Book 3, Ch60 L5).

## 3. Timeline of involvement

- Book 1: Rufus's party (Book 1, Ch9-30).
- Book 2: Greenstone temple (Book 2, Ch10, Ch37).
- Book 3: cult alliance and death (Book 3, Ch41-60).

## 4. Roleplay notes

Cold, righteous and quick to anger; she treats mercy as weakness (Book 1, Ch30 L26).
