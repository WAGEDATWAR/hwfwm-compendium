## 1. Summary

Lucian Lamprey is the corrupt director of Greenstone's Magic Society branch, an elf with a fighter's physique "banished to Greenstone for previous improprieties". He treats the Fortress as his office, orchestrates Jason's kidnapping with Cole Silva, and is cut down by Danielle Geller and imprisoned.

## 2. Appearance

- An uncommonly muscular elf who "would not look out of place in the fighting pits himself", in expensive clothes (Book 1, Ch42 L26).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Smug and indulgent**: he arrives at his viewing room "with a contented sigh" (Book 1, Ch43 L47).
- **Handsy**: he strokes his fingers over a face in the viewing window (Book 1, Ch43 L69).
- **Quick to blame**: his fury threatens to erupt when pressed (Book 2, Ch38 L19).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Danielle outpaces him in a duel and cuts him (Book 3, Ch24 L23).
- His handling of a star seed for the Church of Purity comes under scrutiny (Book 3, Ch1 L107).

## 5. Background

- "Banished to Greenstone for previous improprieties"; he is not a member of the local elf community (Book 1, Ch42 L25-26).
- His deputy Pochard maintains the city campus so he can use the Fortress as his office (Book 1, Ch42 L30).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: the fighting pits viewing rooms (Book 1, Ch42-43; Book 1, Ch50 L104).

- **Cole Silva**: co-conspirator (Book 9, Ch2 L84).
