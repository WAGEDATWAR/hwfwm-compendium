## 1. Summary

The corrupt director of Greenstone's Magic Society branch.

## 2. Facts

- An elf with an uncommonly muscular frame who was "banished to Greenstone for previous improprieties"; he uses the Fortress as his office (Book 1, Ch42 L25-30).
- His personal handing of a star seed to the Church of Purity comes under scrutiny (Book 3, Ch1 L107).
- Danielle outpaces him in a duel and cuts him (Book 3, Ch24 L23); he is held in the prison tower (Book 3, Ch24 L48).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Smug, indulgent and quick to blame.
