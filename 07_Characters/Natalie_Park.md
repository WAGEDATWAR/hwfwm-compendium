## 1. Summary

An ASIS agent assigned as Jason's liaison during the 2038 events.

## 2. Facts

- An ASIS agent and silver-ranker, she was a young girl at Jason's last visit and was trained on Farrah's and Rufus's methods (Book 13, Ch961 L8).
- The junior ASIS officer with "the unenviable position of personal liaison to Jason Asano" in the Asano Village briefing room (Book 13, Ch969 L88).
- Manages the video feeds in the briefing room (Book 13, Ch970 L52); Jason startles her by rising from her shadow (Book 13, Ch974 L31).
- Pays half attention at the symposium after realising who is sitting next to her (Book 13, Ch995 L69).
- States that it has been more than four years since a confirmed trace of McKean (Book 13, Ch998 L105).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Professional and wary; she is learning how to work beside someone the rest of the world cannot control.
