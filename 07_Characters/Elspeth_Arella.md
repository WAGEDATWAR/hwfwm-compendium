## 1. Summary

Elspeth Arella is the elf director of the Greenstone branch of the Adventure Society and the secret daughter of Old City crime lord Dorgan. A failed expedition and its investigation undo her career, trapping her in a post meant as a stepping stone. Twenty years later she is still there to receive Jason's return.

## 2. Appearance

An elf; Jason had spoken with her only once before the inquiry (Book 1, Ch83 L45).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Controlling**: Dorgan tells her "Your mistake was your need to feel in control" (Book 2, Ch3 L4).
- **Wary of Jason**: she asks "What is it you want in return for silence?" (Book 1, Ch106 L82).
- **Resentful**: "she felt trapped" while her father felt elevated (Book 12, Ch27 L44).
- **Unwilling to forget**: she recalls lifting "some jumped up iron-ranker by the throat" on the spot where he returns (Book 12, Ch27 L45).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- She can lift a person by the throat with her powers (Book 12, Ch27 L45).
- She is flanked by her deputy, Genevieve (Book 2, Ch38 L5).

## 5. Background

- Director of the Greenstone branch (Book 1, Ch65 L60). Raised in secret by her mother, with clandestine visits to her father Dorgan (Book 2, Ch3 L3).
- Her backroom dealings and her status as "daughter of an Old City crime lord" were dragged into the light after the disastrous expedition (Book 12, Ch27 L44).
- Her father rose from last of the Big Three crime lords to legitimate mayor of Old City (Book 12, Ch27 L44).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: pointed out to Jason at a social outing (Book 1, Ch65 L60); sits at the inquiry bench (Book 1, Ch83 L45); greets the party at the platform (Book 1, Ch101 L65); confronts Jason in her office (Book 1, Ch106 L80-83).
- **Book 2**: tea with her father (Book 2, Ch3 L3-6); attends the party (Book 2, Ch38 L5).
- **Book 9**: among adventurers uneasy at a strange cloak monster (Book 9, Ch89 L56).
- **Book 12**: waits in her office and is first in the meet-and-greet line (Book 12, Ch27 L43-45; Book 12, Ch29 L24-32).

## 7. Relationships

- **Jason**: antagonist turned polite acquaintance (Book 12, Ch29 L32).
