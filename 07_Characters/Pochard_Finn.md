## 1. Summary

Pochard Finn is Lucian Lamprey's long-time deputy director in Greenstone, an elf and local who does the actual work while Lamprey enjoys the title. He is practical, discreet and understands the value of discretion.

## 2. Appearance

An elf and a local (Book 1, Ch42 L30).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Discreet**: "a man who understood the value of discretion" (Book 11, Ch68 L1167).
- **Protective of his boss**: he steps up when fury crosses Lamprey's face (Book 2, Ch38 L19).
- **Judges Humphrey fairly**: he remembers the Geller son as "polite, forthright and a little lacking in confidence" (Book 11, Ch68 L1174).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- He keeps the city campus running while frequently travelling to the Fortress (Book 1, Ch42 L30).
- He arranges the lifelike water-link to Gabriel Remore (Book 1, Ch49 L27).

## 5. Background

- Long-time deputy who did the real work during Lamprey's famously corrupt tenure (Book 11, Ch68 L1167).
- His son Cassowary is Lamprey's dogsbody and go-between (Book 1, Ch43 L47; Book 1, Ch50 L104).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: explains the Fortress office arrangement (Book 1, Ch42 L30); watches Clive (Book 1, Ch42 L40).
- **Book 2**: Emir and Finn contain Lamprey's outburst (Book 2, Ch38 L19).
- **Book 11**: at his desk when Humphrey arrives (Book 11, Ch68 L1167-1175).

## 7. Relationships

- **Lucian Lamprey**: friend and employer (Book 1, Ch50 L104).
- **Cassowary Finn**: son (Book 1, Ch43 L47).
