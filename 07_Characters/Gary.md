## 1. Summary

Gareth "Gary" Xandier is an enormous leonid smith and warrior from Vitesse. He makes Jason's first sword, Dread Salvation. In Book 10 he drinks the Cup of Heroes and becomes a demigod; in Book 11 he dies; in Book 12 a new leonid, Gary Sharpton, appears who carries his reincarnated soul.

## 2. Appearance

A huge "lion man" whose voice is growly by default (Book 1, Ch9 L121); so large "he made bodybuilders look small" (Book 1, Ch14 L14). The notes call him a leonid with a lion head (Book 1, Ch11 L4-6). As a demigod he is a vision of physical might, wreathed in divine power; almost a year later he is a haggard, skeletal figure who hobbles or is carried in a floating chair (Book 11, Ch68 L703).

## 3. Personality and voice

His characteristic line when facing death: "I don't want to be a ghost, rattling around in a hole" (Book 11, Ch57 L60). Farrah describes him as "as much a craftsman as an adventurer" and "around the bottom of guild level" (Book 6, Ch47 L121-126). Dominion tells him "That is where monarchs come from... I'll be keeping an eye on you, Gareth Xandier" (Book 1, Ch95 L104).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- **Summon**: a foundry golem, a roughly three-metre black-iron figure with a molten mouth that drips molten metal (Book 1, Ch17 L58-61).
- **Equipment**: conjured full-body dark-steel plate armour (Book 1, Ch16 L28); the notes add a hammer and shield and the runes.
- **Smithing**: he forges Dread Salvation (Book 1, Ch56 L46-51); he reforges it with his mentor Virid Martine (Book 6, Ch47 L270) as Hegemon's Will (Book 7, Ch2 L71-74; Book 7, Ch54 L108-120).
- **Rank**: bronze at the start; "almost silver" by Book 3 (Book 3, Ch21 L60); silver after using a core (notes, Book 6).
- **Demigod**: Hero's power gives him divine strength; he says "I'm only a demigod" (Book 11, Ch68 L703).

## 5. Background

A friend of Rufus and Farrah, and a Vitesse adventurer (Book 1, Ch30 L47). In Book 6 he meets the gold-rank smith Virid Martine, who becomes his master (Book 6, Ch47 L270-272).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: captured with Rufus and Farrah by the Vane cult; escorts Jason to Greenstone; stands before Dominion.
- **Book 6**: meets Virid Martine and begins his blacksmith's path.
- **Book 7**: reforges Dread Salvation with Virid.
- **Book 10**: drinks the Cup of Heroes and holds the citadel wall in Cardinas.
- **Book 11**: refuses Jason's offer to bind his power to the natural array; dies; Hero decides to sanction himself (Gwydion tells Gary, Book 11, Ch68 L707-709).
- **Book 12**: Gary Sharpton appears: a young leonid who tinkers with a rune puzzle cube (Book 12, Ch62 L4-9) and travels aboard a saucer craft with Anna and Rufus (Book 12, Ch62 L4-9).
- **Book 13**: Jason tells the new Gary, "The gift was from my friend Gary. And the gift he gave us was you... He isn't you. And you aren't him." The Reaper "just passed it on" (Book 13, Ch963 L63-68).

## 7. Relationships

Jason (friend), Rufus and Farrah (friends), Virid Martine (master), Dominion and Hero, and the younger Gary (the successor).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

The Cup of Heroes, death and rebirth are level 3.

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Deep, growling and plain-spoken. Cues: Book 1, Ch9 L121; "rattling around in a hole" (Book 11, Ch57 L60). See `../10_Roleplay_Support/Tone_and_Voice.md`.

Short, growly sentences; patient with craft; the younger Gary should be written as a different person, a bright teenage engineer, not an echo.
