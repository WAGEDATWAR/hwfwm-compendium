## 1. Summary

Lorelei Grantham is a researcher who becomes Vice-Dean and later Vice Chancellor of astral magic research, recruits Clive, covers for his escape from Rakesh and becomes his partner in Book 13. Polite, administrative and quietly decisive, she defers to Clive's research and protects him from officials.

## 2. Appearance

- A fair-skinned blonde woman, bronze rank in Book 3 (Book 3, Ch69 L759).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Protective**: she shields Clive from Lady Ajus (Book 6, Ch47 L662) and stalls Lady Ajus without asking what he was doing (Book 6, Ch47 L689).
- **Deferential to research**: she later studies the notes he left (Book 6, Ch47 L741).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Vice-Dean of the astral magic research department in Rakesh (Book 6, Ch47 L662); Vice Chancellor of the Magic Research Association by Book 12 (Book 12, Ch56 L119).

## 5. Background

- She introduces herself as a researcher assigned to the Adventure Society's Continental Council when recruiting Jason's team's help against the Builder cult (Book 3, Ch69 L455).
- She recruited Clive out of Greenstone; Clive thinks she was misled too (Book 6, Ch47 L662).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 3**: "My name is Lorelei Grantham and I'm a researcher assigned to work with the Adventure Society's Continental Council" (Book 3, Ch69 L455).
- **Book 6**: Rakesh, Clive's recruitment and escape (Book 6, Ch47 L624-741).
- **Book 12-13**: brings researchers to Earth with the expedition (Book 12, Ch56 L119); works with Clive on the vampire laboratory (Book 13, Ch959 L3); leads Clive out of a lab by the arm (Book 13, Ch992 L122-124); full-time staff at Jason's school (Book 13, Ch1016 L106).

## 7. Relationships

- **Clive**: partner (Book 13, Ch992 L122-124).
