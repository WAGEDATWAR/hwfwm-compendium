## 1. Summary

A researcher and later Vice-Dean and Vice Chancellor of astral magic research who recruits Clive, covers for his escape from Rakesh and becomes his partner in Book 13.

## 2. Facts

- Introduces herself as a researcher assigned to the Adventure Society's Continental Council when she recruits Jason's team's help against the Builder cult (Book 3, Ch69 L455); a fair-skinned blonde woman, bronze rank (Book 3, Ch69 L759).
- In Rakesh she is Vice-Dean of the astral magic research department and the person who recruited Clive out of Greenstone; Clive thinks she was misled too, and she shields him from Lady Ajus (Book 6, Ch47 L662).
- She covers for Clive's escape, stalling Lady Ajus without asking what he was doing (Book 6, Ch47 L689), and later studies the notes he left (Book 6, Ch47 L741).
- By Book 12 she is Vice Chancellor of the Magic Research Association and brings researchers to Earth with the expedition (Book 12, Ch56 L119).
- In Book 13 she works with Clive on the vampire laboratory (Book 13, Ch959 L3), is full-time staff at Jason's school with Emi, Clive and Belinda (Book 13, Ch1016 L106), and leads Clive out of a lab by the arm in a scene that changes the room (Book 13, Ch992 L122-124).

## 3. Timeline of involvement

- Book 3: introduces herself as a researcher assigned to the Adventure Society's Continental Council (Book 3, Ch69 L455).
- Book 6: Rakesh, Clive's recruitment and escape (Book 6, Ch47 L624-741).
- Book 12-13: Earth expedition and Jason's school (Book 12, Ch56 L119; Book 13, Ch1016 L106).

## 4. Roleplay notes

Polite, administrative and quietly decisive; she defers to Clive's research and protects him from officials.
