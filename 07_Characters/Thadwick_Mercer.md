## 1. Summary

Thadwick Mercer is the arrogant heir of House Mercer, who torments his two "offsiders" Neil and Dustin, feuds with Jason, takes a Builder star seed and becomes a vessel for the Builder. The Builder's vessel is later "the worst vessel I ever possessed"; Thadwick himself ends as an energy vampire in Book 9 and returns in Book 13.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in detail in the lines read; his gear includes a blade with a ruby and onyx hilt and a spiked grip in Book 13 (Book 13, Ch1022 L62).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Arrogant and cruel**: "a fool whose arrogance and incompetence only escalated over time" (Book 11, Ch6 L57) and "the worst" (Book 4, Ch68 L185).
- **Fixated on Jason**: "Thadwick was always fixated on me. I think he might have had a sister complex" (Book 9, Ch26 L59), after Jason dated his sister Cassandra (Book 4, Ch68 L177).
- **Leashed**: a rival greets him, "I see your mother let you out, so long as you wore your leash" (Book 2, Ch41 L80).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Lightning, wind, potent and storm essences (Book 1, Ch110 L20; see Essence_Catalogue.md).
- He fights from behind his protectors: Dustin used his powers to protect while Neil healed him, and Thadwick "had been blasting powers" (Book 1, Ch110 L17).
- After the star seed he becomes a vessel for the Builder (Book 3, Ch46 L61; Book 11, Ch68 L160) and later "some weird vampire" (Book 9, Ch26 L57-58).

## 5. Background

- **Family**: his father Beaufort Mercer is the Duke of Greenstone's brother; his mother Thalia is an adventurer (Book 1, Ch77 L47; Book 2, Ch59 L4).
- **Companions by obligation**: "Neil and Dustin were forced to train and work with Thadwick through the obligation of their families" (Book 11, Ch6 L57, L61).
- **Sister**: Cassandra Mercer is four years older than Neil and the object of his youthful affection (Book 2, Ch27 L43).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: his entourage at the Mercer gatherings (Book 1, Ch77 L46-57); the expedition (Book 1, Ch110 L17).
- **Book 2**: he faces Timos and wipes the sneer off his face (Book 2, Ch41 L80-89); he is missing and hunted (Book 2, Ch59 L4-36).
- **Book 3**: he enters a hole to receive power (Book 3, Ch46 L61) and is in the cult's care when Jason appears as a shadow (Book 3, Ch57 L15).
- **Book 9**: an energy vampire who took the sword (Book 9, Ch26 L57-58); in Book 10 "the vampire once known as Thadwick" speaks (Book 10, Ch34 L118).
- **Book 13**: he carries the relic blade and meets Thalia in a warehouse (Book 13, Ch1022 L62-67).

## 7. Relationships

- **Neil and Dustin**: his offsiders, "both stuck under Thadwick due to their families" (Book 1, Ch77 L46).
- **Thalia and Beaufort**: parents, one adamant about training, the other guilty (Book 1, Ch53 L54; Book 11, Ch6 L59).
- **Cassandra**: his sister, who dated Jason (Book 4, Ch68 L177).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

He was possessed by the Builder at one point (Book 11, Ch68 L160-186), and is the subject of Umber's later plans (Book 13, Ch1024 L118-125).

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Petty, loud and entitled, given to threats that lean on his family name. Cue: a sneer and a leash joke from someone else.
