## 1. Summary

Clive Standish is a tall, lanky Magic Society researcher in Greenstone, raised on an eel farm, who becomes Jason's team's ritualist and astral-magic scholar. He explains the cosmos to Jason as a bowl of dumpling soup, identifies the Reaper-trial astral space as a Builder vessel, and builds the open-access Magical Research Association.

## 2. Appearance

"Rather tall, but his narrow frame and hunched posture made him seem lanky and awkward", wearing voluminous scholarly robes that "dangled off him like they'd been hung out to dry" (Book 1, Ch42 L44).

## 3. Personality and voice

- A lecturer who loses the thread and apologises ("Sorry. Where were we?", Book 11, Ch68 L748); he wants dumpling soup in the middle of his own lecture on the cosmos (Book 1, Ch69 L85-107).
- He says he venerates, not worships, the Celestial Book: "There's a difference" (Book 1, Ch85 L51); an archbishop asks "You admit to being a heretic?" and Clive answers that he "could be considered a heretic" (Book 2, Ch21 L94-98).
- He jokes about his background: "I'm the son of eel farmers" (Book 2, Ch7 L62). A stranger accuses him of sleeping with a wife he says he does not have, which becomes a running gag (Book 2, Ch32 L106).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- **Essences**: Magic, Rune, Balance and the Karmic confluence (Book 2, Ch28 L56: "Clive has the magic, rune, balance and karmic essences"). A rune essence came to him as "an epic four-star essence" (Book 2, Ch9 L21).
- **Abilities**: Wrath of the Magister, a rune circle that triggers effects when attacked, the damage-reflecting buff Mantle of Retribution and the zone ability Eye of Karma (Book 2, Ch30 L53); Mana Equilibrium (Book 1, Ch103 L44).
- **Racial gift**: a blessing from the Celestial Book changed his human Special Attack Affinity into a spell affinity (Book 1, Ch85 L22-26).
- **Familiar**: Onslow, a rune tortoise who sits at the prow of the airboat, carries Clive, and becomes a runic tattoo on his torso when called back (Book 1, Ch86 L115-116; Book 2, Ch22 L127-129).
- **Ritual and astral magic**: he leads the team's rituals and bridge research.

## 5. Background

Grew up on an eel farm in the delta near Greenstone (Book 1, Ch68 L92-94; wiki); recruited to the Magic Society by the Greenstone branch's then-director (wiki). His family sheltered in the Geller estate during the surge before last (Book 2, Ch18 L25-27).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: introduced as the Magic Society official; explains the deep astral and the dumpling-soup cosmology (chapter 69).
- **Book 3**: identifies the vessel nature of the Reaper-trial astral space (Book 3, Ch37 L13) and plans the inversion.
- **Book 6**: held at the Magic Society's Rakesh campus; escapes with Belinda's help.
- **Books 11-12**: Archchancellor of the Magical Research Association (Book 11, Ch68 L2034), a rival to the Magic Society.
- **Book 13**: by 2038 he and Lorelei Grantham are in a relationship that gives his Balance essence the equilibrium it needed (Book 13, Ch997 L32).

## 7. Relationships

Belinda (assistant and colleague), Jason (friend and pupil), Lorelei Grantham (partner, Book 13), Onslow, the Celestial Book, Humphrey and the Gellers.

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

The MRA's growth and Clive's part in the bridge are level 2-3.
