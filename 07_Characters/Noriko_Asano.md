## 1. Summary

Noriko Asano is the strongest member of the old Asano clan and its patriarch's mother, who takes control from her son and opposes Jason's family. By Book 12 she is matriarch of the old clan, hosting Jota Withers. Stern, brusque and proud, she measures people by strength.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in the lines read; she presides with eight elders at a low table (Book 5, Ch36 L3).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Contemptuous of honour**: "You sound like your new white friend, Asano" (Book 5, Ch36 L89).
- **Strength-first**: she argues there is only shame in weakness and "only the strongest shall rise" (Book 5, Ch36 L11-16).
- **Calculating**: she tells the others not to inform Mei and Akari, so they act as a mask for the clan's own plans (Book 5, Ch36 L32).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- The strongest member of the old Asano clan (Book 5, Ch36 L3).

## 5. Background

- She wrests control of the clan from her son, the former head Shiro, whose closest family settle in Asano Village (Book 5, Ch52 L68).
- She explains the government seized land from the "upstart clan" and gave it to the "true Asano clan" (Book 12, Ch63 L30).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 5**: seizes the clan and confronts Jason; Jason says "My Asano family and your Asano clan henceforth have no connections, Noriko" (Book 5, Ch36-37, Ch52; Book 5, Ch37 L92).
- **Book 12**: hosts the pirate admiral Jota Withers with daily visits and servants (Book 12, Ch63 L19).

## 7. Relationships

- **Shiro**: son she displaces (Book 5, Ch52 L68).
- **Jason**: opponent (Book 5, Ch37 L92).
- **Jota Withers**: guest (Book 12, Ch63 L19).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

Book 12 calls her matriarch of the Asano clan while Yumi is also called matriarch; the two descriptions are not reconciled (open question; Book 12, Ch63 L19).

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Stern, brusque and proud; treats compromise as weakness. Cue: "only the strongest shall rise."
