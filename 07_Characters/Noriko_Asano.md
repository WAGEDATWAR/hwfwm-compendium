## 1. Summary

The strongest member of the old Asano clan and its patriarch's mother, who takes control from her son and opposes Jason's family; she is matriarch of the old clan by Book 12.

## 2. Facts

- Introduced as "the strongest member of the Asano clan and the patriarch's mother", presiding with eight elders at a low table (Book 5, Ch36 L3).
- She argues the clan must seize the chance only it has, that there is only shame in weakness and that "only the strongest shall rise" (Book 5, Ch36 L11-16), and tells the others not to inform Mei and Akari, so they act as a mask for the clan's own plans (Book 5, Ch36 L32).
- She is contemptuous of honour ("You sound like your new white friend, Asano", Book 5, Ch36 L89) and Jason rejects any connection: "My Asano family and your Asano clan henceforth have no connections, Noriko" (Book 5, Ch37 L92).
- She wrests control of the clan from her son, the former head Shiro, whose closest family settle in Asano Village (Book 5, Ch52 L68).
- In Book 12 she is matriarch of the Asano clan hosting Jota Withers, makes daily visits and supplies servants (Book 12, Ch63 L19); she explains the government seized land from the "upstart clan" and gave it to the "true Asano clan" (Book 12, Ch63 L30).

## 3. Timeline of involvement

- Book 5: seizes the clan and confronts Jason (Book 5, Ch36-37, Ch52).
- Book 12: hosts the pirate admiral Jota Withers (Book 12, Ch63).

## 4. Roleplay notes

Stern, brusque and proud; she measures people by strength and treats compromise as weakness.
