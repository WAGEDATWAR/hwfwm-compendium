## 1. Summary

A Livaros spy and scout with fourfold senses who works for Jason's team and later marries Belinda.

## 2. Facts

- She has "made a career out of being a spy and urban scout in Livaros" and knows the island's streets, alleys and rooftops extremely well (Book 8, Ch5 L7).
- In Book 7 she waits on a roof in a small town, failing to concentrate on a book while a monster manifestation is expected (Book 7, Ch62 L79); she tells a contact she would rather keep her identity from someone who "doesn't know who I am" (Book 7, Ch62 L41).
- She is beside Jason at the gate of Yaresh (Book 9, Ch43 L33).
- Jason says her "fourfold senses make her even better than I am" at spotting messenger agents, and that she is an experienced spy (Book 10, Ch42 L41); she maps the messenger agents in the city (Book 10, Ch42 L39).
- By Book 13 she is Belinda's wife (Book 13, Ch1011 L23).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Cautious, observant and hard to surprise; she prefers not to be noticed.
