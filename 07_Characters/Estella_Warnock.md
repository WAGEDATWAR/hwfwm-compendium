## 1. Summary

Estella Warnock is a Livaros spy and urban scout with fourfold senses who works for Jason's team and later marries Belinda. Cautious and observant, she prefers not to be noticed.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in the lines read.

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Hard to surprise, prefers anonymity**: she tells a contact she would rather keep her identity from someone who "doesn't know who I am" (Book 7, Ch62 L41).
- **Nervous in waiting**: she fails to concentrate on a book on a rooftop while a monster manifestation is expected (Book 7, Ch62 L79).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Fourfold senses: Jason says they make her "even better than I am" at spotting messenger agents (Book 10, Ch42 L41); she maps messenger agents in the city (Book 10, Ch42 L39).
- She "made a career out of being a spy and urban scout in Livaros" and knows the island's streets, alleys and rooftops (Book 8, Ch5 L7).
