## 1. Summary

Jory Tillman is Greenstone's gentle alchemist and clinic-runner, Jason's first friend, and later Belinda's boyfriend. Passionate about making alchemy affordable, he becomes a Healer-backed teacher spreading his methods around the world.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in detail in the lines read; on a mission he puts away his coat and pulls out another one "covered in pockets" (Book 7, Ch4 L25).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Idealist**: "If I can make alchemical products affordable to everyone, not only can it help a huge number of people, but it will open up huge new markets" (Book 1, Ch35 L21).
- **Honest about himself**: technically an adventurer, he is "the first to admit he rarely went on adventures"; his "true calling was alchemy" (Book 1, Ch35 L10).
- **Gentle**: he tells Jason "You have a good day" to a guard and nurses patients with Healer priests (Book 1, Ch35 L57; Book 3, Ch2 L16).
- **Passionate teacher**: "He lit up when talking about his alchemy, and his passion for helping people was wildly appealing" (Book 11, Ch13 L61).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- **Alchemy**: he brews potions and elixirs from materials he gathers in towns and villages (Book 1, Ch35 L10) and runs an alchemy lab and medical clinic, the Broad Street Clinic (Book 1, Ch35 L63).
- **Miracle potion**: his facility produces the first batch of lesser miracle potions, a thank-you to Jason who funded it (Book 3, Ch16 L81).
- **In a fight**: floating clear orbs and materials from his pockets and belts in the Reaper trials (Book 2, Ch52 L72) and quick to throw enemies overboard on a skyship (Book 7, Ch4 L76).
- **Teaching**: in Book 11 he lectures a group of students on a stage in a hazy hall (Book 11, Ch68 L1403-1418).

## 5. Background

- Member of the Adventure Society and the Alchemist Association (Book 1, Ch36 L28).
- The Healer makes his clinic a sanctuary and the church backs him (Book 3, Ch16 L82); "The Church of the Healer gave him a mandate and funding to spread his methods around the world" (Book 5, Ch43 L94).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1-3**: he finds Jason, patches him up and offers a clinic (Book 1, Ch35); treats Sophie (Book 1, Ch104 L4); the Healer visit (Book 1, Ch95).
- **Book 5-6**: the Healer's mandate; Belinda contacts him through a water link (Book 6, Ch47 L843-844).
- **Book 7**: he is on the skyship to Vitesse with Gary and the team (Book 7, Ch3 L3) and is summoned away by the Church of the Healer (Book 7, Ch16 L6).
- **Book 11-13**: he teaches (Book 11, Ch68 L1411-1461) and joins the healers' discussions on Earth (Book 13, Ch953 L84).

## 7. Relationships

- **Belinda**: her boyfriend (Book 9, Ch21 L32), and the one who sees her cry in the classroom (Book 11, Ch68 L1461).
- **Jason**: first friend; "Jory is such a good friend to you" (Book 2, Ch1 L77).
- **Neil and the Healer's people**: they work together at the clinic (Book 3, Ch69 L859-860).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

The Healer's direct support is one reason the clinic is protected (Book 3, Ch16 L82).
