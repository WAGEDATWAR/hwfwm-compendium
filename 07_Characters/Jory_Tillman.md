## 1. Summary

A Greenstone alchemist who gives Jason a ride into the city and later builds a miracle-potion facility.

## 2. Facts

- The driver who finds Jason in a village swamped by people seeking healing; "technically an adventurer, although he was the first to admit he rarely went on adventures", whose true calling is alchemy (Book 1, Ch35 L10-12).
- Jason funds his alchemy facility, which produces the first lesser miracle potions (Book 3, Ch16 L81).
- In Book 11 he berates a student in a Kazlahk alchemy lab, apparently as the teacher, and shares a long look with Belinda (Book 11, Ch68 L1412-1457); the notes make him Belinda's ex and an alchemy dean (notes, Book 11).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Gentle, nerdy and brave when it counts.
