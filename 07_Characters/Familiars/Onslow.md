## 1. Summary

Clive Standish's familiar: a rune tortoise whose shell carries runes that throw elemental attacks, and whose shell the whole team rides in, shelters in and runs the battlefield from. Onslow is gentle and a little stubborn, and Clive talks to him as "little buddy".

## 2. Facts

- **First sight**: Onslow is at the front of Clive's airboat with his head jutting forward "like a dog with its head out a car window" (Book 1, Ch68 L106). Clive sits on his shell with Jason beside him (Book 1, Ch69 L56).
- **Powers**: he fires elemental attacks from the magic runes on his shell, which Clive recharges with his own mana (Book 3, Ch4 L29; Book 3, Ch38 L54). A rune dims when he takes a hit (Book 1, Ch56 L24).
- **A seat for the team**: Clive picks up Stash and climbs on Onslow's shell with Neil, and the tortoise floats off the rooftop on a cushion of air (Book 3, Ch66 L46-47). Clive drops down off him when the tower is threatened (Book 3, Ch69 L97).

## 3. Later developments, Books 7 to 9

<!-- level:2 -->
- **Precaution**: when a familiar tears open the side of a ship, Clive sends Onslow out as a precaution (Book 7, Ch6 L52).
- **Power in a fight**: he pours elemental magic into the enemy from his rune shell, throwing hailstones and lightning bolts (Book 8, Ch10 L29).
- **Open-sided craft**: he expands his shell into an open-sided flying craft, and the unshelled tortoise takes the form of a small green humanoid wearing a child's clothes from Clive, "an adorable team mascot" (Book 9, Ch81 L23).
- **Under fire**: his shell draws constant barrage from the monsters, which Clive answers with ritual-enhanced wind barriers (Book 9, Ch87 L24). Humphrey tells Belinda to keep anyone from focusing on Onslow (Book 9, Ch84 L23).
- **"Little buddy"**: Clive asks, "What do you say, little buddy? Can you get us down any faster?" (Book 9, Ch97 L49).
<!-- /level -->

## 4. Later developments, Books 10 to 13 (spoilers)

<!-- level:3 -->
- **Bunker and command post**: the shell serves as a bunker where Neil, Clive and Belinda work and where freed silver-rankers recover (Book 10, Ch61 L30), and as the safest location of the expedition because it absorbs elemental attacks (Book 10, Ch70 L20).
- **Carrier**: it holds adventurers and brighthearts in an empty tunnel (Book 10, Ch79 L3), and the team crosses overland in it with the sides open (Book 11, Ch53 L12).
- **House-sized**: in Book 13 the shell has expanded to the size of a house, serving as a combination command centre, rest area and triage tent (Book 13, Ch966 L3). Those who cannot fly ride in Onslow's expanded shell (Book 12, Ch53 L90).
- **Plodding**: when a child demands he go faster he plods over the grass on thick legs, to the child's complaint (Book 11, Ch20 L19).
- **Friendly**: he returns Neil's fist bump with a three-fingered fist (Book 13, Ch1007 L77).
<!-- /level -->

## 5. Using this in a story

Onslow is the team's place of safety. A party that has him can retreat, heal and regroup in the middle of a battle, and a story that removes him removes that base. Play him as slow, kind and immovable, a mascot who also happens to be a fortress.
