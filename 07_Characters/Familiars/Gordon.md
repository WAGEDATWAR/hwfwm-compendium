## 1. Summary

Jason's third familiar: an Avatar of Doom, a floating cloak holding a nebula in the shape of an eye, circled by blue and orange eye-orbs that fire beams. He cannot speak, but he answers questions with his orbs and has a distinct personality, and he is the key to the intrinsic-mandate magic Jason uses to work with authority.

## 2. Facts

- **Summoned**: the doom essence ability Avatar of Doom summons a familiar (Book 2, Ch64 L12). At first it is unnamed, an iron-rank incorporeal familiar whose orbs make sustained beams, one of disruptive force and one of resonating force, and whose damage leaves Vulnerable (Book 2, Ch72 L140-144).
- **Named**: asked if it wants a name, it flashes its blue orb, and Jason says, "I'm going to call you... Gordon" (Book 2, Ch72 L148-155). Orange is no and blue is yes: Gordon's orange orb flashes brightly as his signal for no (Book 3, Ch46 L96). Shade relays him: "Gordon says that I should be last" (Book 3, Ch46 L95).
- **Appearance**: a disembodied cloak with a nebula-like cloud of blue and orange in the shape of an eye, and eyeball-patterned orbs drifting around it that fire unrelenting beams (Book 3, Ch4 L28).
- **In a fight**: his beams inflict Vulnerable on a lizard (Book 3, Ch8 L76); his energy dash is fast but his reflexes are sluggish, and a blow can dissipate his vessel entirely (Book 3, Ch13 L38).

## 3. Later developments, Books 5 to 9

<!-- level:2 -->
- **Subsumed**: while he is subsumed into Jason at silver rank, Jason can call up one of each orb to use for himself (Book 5, Ch49 L43).
- **Shields**: his orbs make shields that protect Jason (Book 6, Ch38 L35; Book 7, Ch26 L29).
- **Reputation**: the Magic Society has only iron- and bronze-rank information on the "floating death orbs" and finds it terrifying (Book 8, Ch25 L48).
- **Origin**: he comes from the realm of the All-Devouring Eye (Book 8, Ch55 L92).
- **The orrery**: in Book 9 he draws a glowing orrery that looks like a solar system made of fireworks, a work he completes by floating down next to Jason (Book 9, Ch94 L92).

<!-- level:3 -->
- **The library**: he spends months with some of Jason's avatars in the soul-realm library (Book 10, Ch5 L24).
- **Intrinsic-mandate magic**: Jason can reach it through Gordon, who executes the actual magic while Jason guides the authority with will (Book 11, Ch33 L152-153).

He detonates orbs to clear space for Jason (Book 13, Ch1012 L26).
- **Nature**: Shade calls him a herald of annihilation (Book 13, Ch981 L37).

## 5. Using this in a story

Gordon is the silent member of the household, and the humour comes from how much he communicates without words. He is not shown speaking in the lines checked, so give him lights, orb movement and a taste for whatever Jason is eating. His orbs are the best shield and ranged support Jason has, and his dependence on his vessel is his weakness. Note that a different Gordon, a human, is called "Other Gordon" in Books 4 and 5 (Book 4, Ch38 L26: "Hanging Rock?" Other Gordon asked).
