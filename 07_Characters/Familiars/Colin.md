## 1. Summary

Jason's first familiar: a swarm of apocalypse leeches that lives in his blood, sprays out over enemies to bite and drain them, and can take the shape of a red-eyed blood clone of Jason. Colin begins as a hungry, wriggling swarm and grows into a being who talks back, waves from his bowl of leeches and calls Jason "smarmy".

## 2. Facts

- **Named**: Jason says, "I've decided to call him Colin. And his friends. Team Colin" (Book 1, Ch30 L85), and calls the swarm "Leechy" in the same fight (Book 1, Ch29 L23).
- **Lives in Jason's blood**: Jason pricks his finger and the leeches crawl into his hand and vanish into his blood (Book 1, Ch72 L32). His rapid regeneration only works while the familiar is in his bloodstream (Book 1, Ch72 L32).
- **Method**: Jason sprays him out over a target like a fire hose and Colin crawls over armour to find flesh (Book 3, Ch8 L76; Book 7, Ch26 L22). Colin wiggles happily when pleased (Book 3, Ch21 L132).
- **Where he comes from**: a summoned familiar is created through different means from the sanguine horror, but Clive observes it is what Jason summoned (Book 1, Ch75 L12), and if Jason reached diamond his familiar would reach the full strength of a sanguine horror (Book 1, Ch75 L14).

- **Silver vessel**: he needs a silver-rank vessel with simple requirements, such as a wheelbarrow-load of silver-rank blood quintessence (Book 5, Ch27 L57).

## 5. Using this in a story

Colin is a mascot who happens to be a nightmare. He is happiest eating, enjoys his role in a fight and has become a person with opinions, so play him as cheerfully ravenous rather than menacing. His regeneration is a reason Jason does not use him lightly in crowds of friends, and his hunger limits what he can do for long.
