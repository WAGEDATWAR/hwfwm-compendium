## 1. Summary

Belinda Callahan's astral lantern: a small living lantern that orbits her, projects shields, fires force bolts and feeds mana to her team. It is the quietest of the team's familiars, but it is on the field in almost every battle.

## 2. Facts

- **Summoned**: the final ability of Belinda's power set is a summoned familiar, an astral lantern (Book 2, Ch71 L50). Belinda names it: "How about Shimmer?" (Book 2, Ch72 L62).
- **In a fight**: it projects shields to protect the team (Book 3, Ch4 L32) and, with Gordon, beams the enemy to split their attention (Book 3, Ch11 L68). The force bolts of a lantern familiar prove more dangerous to its own side against Hendren's defences (Book 3, Ch58 L60).

## 3. Later developments, Books 7 to 9

<!-- level:2 -->
- **Mana supply**: against constructs its attacks do little, so it mostly replenishes mana (Book 7, Ch4 L78). At the battle of Yaresh it fires force bolts at worms and pumps out mana to the team (Book 9, Ch59 L67; Book 9, Ch82 L36).
- **Pairing**: Belinda's other familiar replicates Humphrey, swinging a huge sword (Book 7, Ch4 L78).
<!-- /level -->

## 4. Later developments, Books 10 to 12 (spoilers)

<!-- level:3 -->
- **Rapid-fire bolts**: its force bolts are as effective as Gordon's beams against flaming and gaseous elementals (Book 10, Ch61 L28).
- **Echo spirit**: Belinda's two summoned familiars are the astral lantern, which orbits her droopily, and an echo spirit that looks like a blurry version of her (Book 12, Ch27 L23).
<!-- /level -->

## 5. Using this in a story

Shimmer is the supply line: shields, bolts and mana. It has little personality in the text beyond its droopy orbit when Belinda is tired, so play it as a quiet companion rather than a speaker.
