## 1. Summary

Humphrey Geller's bonded familiar: a baby mirage dragon whose full name is Velitraxistaasch, who prefers "Stash". He changes shape constantly, starts as a barking lizard, a bird or a puppy and ends up large enough to crush a company of messengers, and he talks, mostly in cheerful, blunt remarks.

## 2. Facts

- **Name**: "It's short for Velitraxistaasch. He likes 'Stash,' though." (Book 1, Ch38 L58). Jason works out what he is at once and Humphrey is startled (Book 1, Ch38 L59-61).
- **Bond**: "Our mothers arranged him becoming my familiar. Or me becoming his person, depending on how you look at it" (Book 1, Ch38 L64). A bonded familiar is an actual magical creature that can die, unlike a summoned familiar (Book 2, Ch42 L70).
- **Shapes**: a canary on Humphrey's head and a puppy (Book 1, Ch38 L62), a bird that swoops from the sky and turns into a puppy in his arms (Book 2, Ch20 L29), a barking lizard (Book 1, Ch78 L84), and a bark lurker or a darting bird in a fight (Book 3, Ch4 L31).
- **Biscuits**: Jason feeds him a biscuit and he turns into a puppy to snuffle it from Jason's hand, then yaps his agreement (Book 1, Ch78 L85-88; Book 2, Ch70 L11).
- **Speech**: "Stash chase worm! Stash chase worm!" (Book 1, Ch87 L180).
- **Scouting**: he leads Humphrey's party by barking in the right direction to the margolls (Book 2, Ch20 L31).
- **Special attack**: Humphrey's racial ability evolves into Attack of the Mirage Dragon (Book 2, Ch61 L147).

## 3. Later developments, Books 6 to 9

- **Mother**: his mother is Carlivexistrix, a vast dragon with scales of changing rainbow colours who asks to be called Carli (Book 6, Ch47 L736-738, L804). Humphrey apologises for not doing the best job of helping her little boy grow up (Book 6, Ch47 L737).
- **Language**: "Porky pies!" and "Stick it up your bum, lady" at a Magic Society official, and "Telling people to bugger off is kind of my thing" (Book 6, Ch47 L761-763).
- **With Sophie**: he is sprawled in Sophie's lap in puppy form (Book 7, Ch57 L34), and she keeps biscuits for him (Book 7, Ch6 L87).
- **Forms in battle**: a marsh hydra (Book 8, Ch10 L29), a copy of Belinda and then of Jason (Book 8, Ch42 L35), and a hopping insect that pulls Neil out of a messenger's path (Book 9, Ch98 L93).
- **Given a task**: Jason tells him to stick to the messengers and keep them safe (Book 9, Ch81 L35).

## 5. Using this in a story

Stash is the team's most affectionate and least filtered voice. He is a dragon child, so give him a child's appetite for biscuits, praise and bad language, and a child's sudden courage. Keep him changing shape. His mother is a major dragon, which is a hook if the story needs dragon allies.
