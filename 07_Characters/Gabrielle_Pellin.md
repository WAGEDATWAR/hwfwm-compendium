## 1. Summary

Gabrielle Pellin is an acolyte of Knowledge who begins as Humphrey's girlfriend, becomes a stern and strikingly beautiful priestess, and ends as Travis Noble's wife. She is often at odds with Jason and uneasy about his aura, but is devoted to her goddess and her husband.

## 2. Appearance

- By seventeen she was "astoundingly beautiful"; later "basically Helen of Troy" (Book 10, Ch8 L51).
- Olive skin, chocolate hair and graceful poise, "dangerous beauty" (Book 7, Ch9 L8).
- Knowledge priests wear robes "not unlike the ones Jason preferred" in lighter colours (Book 10, Ch9 L12).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Stern and accusing**: "Where did you learn to use your aura like a messenger?" (Book 10, Ch8 L26); "You move like a messenger" (Book 10, Ch8 L74).
- **Does not rise to bait**: she glowers but doesn't answer teasing (Book 10, Ch8 L46).
- **Hostile to Jason**: their dislike was a factor in ending her relationship with Humphrey (Book 10, Ch8 L10).
- **Quiet when separated from her goddess**: she is still getting used to the absence of Knowledge (Book 12, Ch58 L41).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- A silver-rank priestess of the Church of Knowledge (Book 10, Ch8 L51).
- Jason's spirit domain cuts her off from her goddess, which makes her look like she bit a lemon (Book 10, Ch8 L83).

## 5. Background

- Introduces herself as "just an acolyte, not a full priestess" (Book 1, Ch38 L122).
- Humphrey's girlfriend, until he breaks up with her in Book 2 (Book 2, Ch28 L25-30).
- Knowledge assigns her to shadow Travis (Book 8, Ch6 L6) and later attach her to his and Farrah's project (Book 10, Ch8 L10).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1-2**: clipboards and a crystal ball with Russell at the temple (Book 1, Ch38 L132); Jason introduces her to Emir (Book 1, Ch101 L69); Humphrey's break-up (Book 2, Ch28 L25-30).
- **Book 7-9**: the grown priestess (Book 7, Ch9 L8); picked up with Arabelle and Carlos (Book 9, Ch13 L84-85).
- **Book 10**: the Knowledge party at the Emir meeting (Book 10, Ch9 L10); Travis glances at her (Book 10, Ch13 L18).
- **Book 12-13**: Travis's wife (Book 12, Ch58 L41); the kitchen scene (Book 13, Ch1016 L68-91); a cube leaves her chest into Knowledge's hand and she seizes (Book 13, Ch1023 L139).

## 7. Relationships

- **Travis Noble**: nervous around her at first (Book 8, Ch6 L6); later her husband (Book 13, Ch1016 L74).
- **Humphrey**: ex-boyfriend (Book 10, Ch8 L9).
- **Jason**: mutual friction; Jason enjoys baiting her (Book 10, Ch8 L27).
- **Knowledge**: her goddess (Book 12, Ch58 L41).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes
