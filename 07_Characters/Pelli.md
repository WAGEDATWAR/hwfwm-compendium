## 1. Summary

Pelli is a sharp-tongued core-using gold-ranker who keeps the town of Palisaros quiet and safe, is "Aunt Pelli" to the royal family, and is a contact for Jason in the Storm Kingdom. She fights a pirate captain outside Kasilaro and organises civilians at the mining facility.

## 2. Appearance

- Walks up the path from town with two celestines with emerald hair and eyes (Book 7, Ch18 L28).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Blunt**: "The name's Pelli and I'll thank you to use it" (Book 7, Ch13 L51).
- **Teasing and self-deprecating**: "I'm just an old core user"; she shakes her head at "an abundance of experience, dear gods" (Book 7, Ch15 L67-71).
- **Protective**: she fights outside the town to spare Kasilaro's residents (Book 7, Ch64 L25).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- A gold-ranker and core user; she fights a pirate captain outside a town (Book 7, Ch64 L25; Book 8, Ch41 L18).
- She had a magic barrier put on a cave behind the waterfall (Book 7, Ch13 L99).

## 5. Background

- "Aunt Pelli" to Vesper from when she was a girl (Book 7, Ch15 L67).
- Mayor-level authority in Palisaros: Argy tells Jason "You'll want to talk to Pelli" (Book 7, Ch13 L28).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 7**: meets Jason and Rufus (Book 7, Ch13 L28-99); greets Vesper (Book 7, Ch15 L65-71); tells Zara where Jason is (Book 7, Ch17 L86); the pirate fight (Book 7, Ch64 L25).
- **Book 8**: organises civilians and mining facility evacuees (Book 8, Ch41 L18).

## 7. Relationships

- **Argy**: local friend (Book 7, Ch13 L74).
- **Vesper**: calls her "Aunt Pelli" (Book 7, Ch15 L67).
- **Rufus**: she asks about his father and the sword (Book 7, Ch13 L51).
