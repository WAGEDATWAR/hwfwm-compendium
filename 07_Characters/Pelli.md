## 1. Summary

The sharp-tongued mayor of Palisaros and a contact for Jason in the Storm Kingdom.

## 2. Facts

- Argy tells Jason "You'll want to talk to Pelli" (Book 7, Ch13 L28); she insists "The name's Pelli and I'll thank you to use it" (Book 7, Ch13 L51).
- She asks Rufus how his father took him being a magic swordsman (Book 7, Ch13 L51).
- She is the mayor of Palisaros (Book 7, Ch13 L28-30).
- She asks Jason whether Soramir should have told him something (Book 7, Ch18 L43).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Blunt and watchful; she protects the town's quiet life from outsiders.
