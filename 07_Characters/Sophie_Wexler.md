## 1. Summary

Sophie Wexler is a celestine fighter known as the Nightingale in the Greenstone fighting pits. She is a thief and debt-slave when Jason meets her, has her indenture taken over by Jason in Book 2, and becomes the team's fastest fighter and, later, Humphrey's fiancée.

## 2. Appearance

Chocolate skin, shining silver hair and sharp eyes that exactly match the hair, "the matching metallics a giveaway trait of the celestine race"; normally shoulder length, tied back in a simple, practical ponytail (Book 1, Ch43 L18).

## 3. Personality and voice

Suspicious of kindness at first: "Are you trying to tell me that Jory is such a good friend to you that you'd go this far over some girl he likes?" (Book 2, Ch1 L77-80). She fights through pain without complaint; in Book 2, one-armed and struggling to her knees, she waves off the lesser miracle potion Jason offers (Book 2, Ch45 L69-72). Her laugh is "free" (Book 9, Ch48 L12).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- **Essences**: Swift, Balance and Wind, producing the Mystic confluence: Clive answers "Mystic" when Rufus asks what that combination produces (Book 2, Ch5 L82-87). Before Jason's help she had only the Swift essence and its single ability for more than half a dozen years (Book 2, Ch5 L37-41).
- **Abilities**: Eternal Moment, a massive acceleration power in which "time seemed to freeze" as she produced wind blades (Book 3, Ch49 L15); Between the Raindrops (from the Awakening Stone of the Rain; notes, Book 2); Mirage Step and Wind Blade (Book 2, Ch8 L170-173; Ch7 L129); unarmed Way of the Reaper strikes.
- **Racial gifts** (celestine): Mana Integrity evolves into Mana Wellspring (Book 2, Ch61 L146), Astral Affinity into Boundary Breaker, which removes the damage reduction from rank disparity (Book 3, Ch68 L14-15), and Celestial Swiftness into Princess of the Firmament (Book 8, Ch22 L37-38).
- **Late**: in Book 13 she has spent several years purging whole cities of disease with her wind and aura (Book 13, Ch997 L28-31).

## 5. Background

Born in Kurdansk, in the People's Holy Federation of Dreisil (Book 6, Ch47 L390, Emir). Her father's failed expedition left her with a huge debt at sixteen or seventeen, and she worked as a pit fighter; Cole Silva, whose father her father had worked for, wanted her (Book 1, Ch50 L92). Her mother is Melody Jain (Book 9, Ch19 L3-6), who faked her death some twenty years earlier (Book 6, Ch47 L510-513).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: caught as a thief, sentenced to indenture; fights in the pits as the Nightingale.
- **Book 2**: Jason takes her indenture; she joins the Adventure Society.
- **Books 3-6**: key fighter; the team's accelerator.
- **Book 7**: Jason learns that her mother leads the Order of Redeeming Light in Rimaros (Book 7, Ch58 L38-46).
- **Book 11**: Humphrey calls her his fiancée (Book 11, Ch68 L1177).
- **Book 13**: travels the world for years purging epidemics.

## 7. Relationships

Humphrey (fiancé), Belinda (oldest friend), Jason (the man who took her indenture), Melody Jain (mother), Danielle Geller.
