## 1. Summary

A silver-rank swordmaster of the Japanese Asano clan who comes to Asano Village in Book 5.

## 2. Facts

- Introduces herself as "Asano Akari" when she meets Jason (Book 5, Ch12 L18-24).
- Her essences are sword, adept and magic (the master confluence); she carries a genuine chokuto and has a blinking teleport that leaves exploding after-images (Book 5, Ch12 L27; Ch14 L61-66).
- Her father is the head of the Japanese clan (Book 5, Ch24 L140).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Lean, formal and exact; she judges before she warms.
