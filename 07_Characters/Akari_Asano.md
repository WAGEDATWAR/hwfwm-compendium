## 1. Summary

Akari Asano is a silver-rank swordswoman of the Japanese Asano clan, daughter of its patriarch, who is sent to Asano Village in Book 5. She spars with Jason, rides with Kaito's helicopter team and chases through the slums alongside him. By Book 13 she carries a gold-rank sword forged for her by Valdis Volaire, her sparring partner for a decade.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in detail in the lines read; she crouches on rooftops (Book 5, Ch12 L18) and wears a sword at her left hip (Book 13, Ch1004 L3).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Reserved and formal**: after joining the Network team, "her reserve that Jason had cracked open went back in place" (Book 5, Ch15 L47).
- **Ritualist**: she meditates on a mat with a stitched ritual circle, a monster core in her hands (Book 5, Ch15 L47).
- **Horror at Jason's methods**: she watches leeches drain a monster and melt into blood in Jason's hand (Book 5, Ch15 L3).
- **Duty-minded**: she tells her escort to inform her father of a change in plan (Book 5, Ch36 L63).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Essences sword, adept and magic, a master confluence; she carries a genuine chokuto and has a blinking teleport that leaves exploding after-images (Book 5, Ch12 L27; Book 5, Ch14 L61-66).
- Silver-rank speed and strength outstrip Dawn's, but Dawn's perfect positioning turns her hits into near misses (Book 5, Ch21 L106).
- She and Jason started sparring evenly matched; she was a specialised swordswoman caught up in human fighting styles (Book 5, Ch21 L24).
- By Book 13 she wears a rare gold-rank sword Valdis forged using other-world techniques (Book 13, Ch1004 L3).

## 5. Background

- Her father is the silver-rank patriarch of the Asano clan, named Shiro like Ken and Hiro's older brother (Book 5, Ch34 L70).
- Her grandmother claims her for a hug and wonders what Jason wants from them (Book 5, Ch34 L100).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 5**: she watches Jason pass on a rooftop (Book 5, Ch12 L18); Jason says her clan should have sent her home (Book 5, Ch12 L83); spars with him (Book 5, Ch21 L24); chases him across the slum rooftops (Book 5, Ch26 L50); the Tiwari trip (Book 5, Ch36 L63).
- **Book 13**: wears the sword Valdis gave her (Book 13, Ch1004 L3).

## 7. Relationships

- **Jason**: sparring partner and relative (Book 5, Ch21 L24).
- **Dawn**: spars with her (Book 5, Ch21 L106).
- **Valdis Volaire**: sword training partner for the past decade (Book 13, Ch1004 L3).
- **Her father and grandmother**: the clan's patriarch and his mother (Book 5, Ch34 L70-100).
