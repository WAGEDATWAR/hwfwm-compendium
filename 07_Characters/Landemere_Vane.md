## 1. Summary

The Vane heir whose failed summoning pulls Jason to Pallimustus.

## 2. Facts

- His mother wonders what Landemere was trying to summon; Dougall says it was "one of them entities from beyond the void" (Book 1, Ch9 L47-50).
- Clive explains in Book 7 that Landemere's summoning went wrong, possibly because he reached beyond his ability or a dimensional event interfered (Book 7, Ch55 L64-79).
- Jason killed him; Clive regrets losing his notes (Book 2, Ch37 L67).

- **Familiar**: Jason's first encounter with a native magical creature was Vane's familiar, which tried to take revenge for his death and fell victim to aging masonry (Book 5, Ch40 L81).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Ambitious and out of his depth.
