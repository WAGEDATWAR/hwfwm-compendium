## 1. Summary

Landemere Vane is the Vane heir whose failed summoning pulls Jason to Pallimustus. He is the first person Jason meets and the first he kills, which leaves Jason shaken for weeks.

## 2. Appearance

His mother looks to be in her early thirties "no older than her son Landemere" (Book 1, Ch11 L65); no other description appears in the lines read.

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Ambitious and out of his depth**: Dougall says Landemere summoned "one of them entities from beyond the void" (Book 1, Ch9 L47-50).
- **Traditional family**: his mother says "this is my manor, my family, and we do things the traditional way" (Book 1, Ch9 L54).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Clive explains that Landemere's summoning went wrong, possibly because he reached beyond his ability or a dimensional event interfered (Book 7, Ch55 L64-79).
- He was holding more than a thousand coins when killed (Book 1, Ch13 L13).

## 5. Background

- His mother, who looks no older than her son, is among the cultists who greet Jason (Book 1, Ch11 L65).
- The Vane family treats the summoning as a family matter: "This is my manor, my family" (Book 1, Ch9 L54).
- Familiar: Jason's first encounter with a native creature was Vane's familiar, which tried to avenge him and fell victim to aging masonry (Book 5, Ch40 L81).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: the summoning in the Vane basement (Book 1, Ch9 L47-54); Jason reflects on the "very first person Jason met" and first killed (Book 1, Ch61 L41).
- **Book 2**: Clive regrets losing his notes (Book 2, Ch37 L67).
- **Book 4-7**: the first kill shakes Jason (Book 4, Ch2 L28); Clive's later explanation (Book 7, Ch55 L64).

## 7. Relationships

- **Cressida Vane**: his mother, who calls him "poor Landemere" (Book 1, Ch9 L54).
- **Jason**: accidental killer (Book 4, Ch2 L28).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

Clive says random dimensional events connecting worlds are rare but more frequent because of the link between the worlds (Book 7, Ch55 L64).
