## 1. Summary

Cassandra Mercer is Thalia's daughter and Thadwick's older sister, an adventurer who becomes Jason's first romance on Pallimustus, ends it when her family wants a reliable match, and reunites with the changed Jason in Book 12. She leads her own team in Greenstone.

## 2. Appearance

- Smart, capable and "gorgeous", the object of many Mercer-orbit boys' crushes (Book 2, Ch27 L43).
- Her mother looks "more like her daughter's sister" thanks to silver-rank essences (Book 1, Ch65 L120).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Sweet and sharp**: she investigates Jason's background (notes, Book 1) and kisses him back forcefully (Book 1, Ch98 L35).
- **Drawn to rescue**: she thinks of a hot shower after bog monsters (Book 12, Ch46 L4).
- **Bound by family**: Neil explains that she dumped Jason "not because she wanted to, but because her family decided he wasn't going to amount to anything" (Book 11, Ch68 L1104).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Bronze rank with a hidden aura in Book 1 (notes, Book 1); by Book 12 her team fights silver-rank bog monsters (Book 12, Ch46 L3-4).
- Thalia oversaw her training personally (Book 1, Ch53 L60).

## 5. Background

- Four years older than Neil, who had a crush on her (Book 2, Ch27 L43).
- Her brother tried to kill Jason (Book 2, Ch41 L35).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: awakens to breakfast being laid (Book 1, Ch89 L3); Jason and Cassandra's first kiss (Book 1, Ch98 L35).

- **Book 12**: leaves the jobs hall with Jiralla and Henrietta Geller (Book 12, Ch46 L3); meets the changed Jason (Book 12, Ch46 L20).

## 7. Relationships

- **Neil**: admirer from youth (Book 11, Ch6 L60).
