## 1. Summary

Minor Storm Kingdom, Rimaros society and Purity-order figures with no file of their own.

## 2. Entries

### Zila Rimaros

- A diamond-rank member of the Rimaros royal house who oversees checks of the most prestigious visitors (Book 7, Ch52 L50), stands by the throne with Soramir (Book 7, Ch59 L73) and with Soramir smashes the defences of an aristocratic house that protested (Book 7, Ch66 L60). A speaker refers to her as "Aunt Zila" (Book 7, Ch17 L116).

### Baseph and the Rimaros children

- Baseph is Liara's husband, silver rank (Book 7, Ch17 L20; Book 8, Ch62 L32). He hammers a pipe to draw raiders in a trap (Book 8, Ch33 L64) and holds Liara's hand under the table (Book 8, Ch63 L3).
- Their children are Dara (eldest), Zareen (middle) and Joseph (youngest and only son), all silver (Book 8, Ch62 L32-40).

### Gibson Amouz

- Liara's husband's cousin "Gibbie" (Book 8, Ch20 L4), originally of the Amouz family, who managed an underwater mining operation (Book 8, Ch26 L29). He is held by the Purity order and freed (Book 8, Ch45 L4), and by Book 9 floats unconscious in a containment tank for recovery (Book 9, Ch28 L45; Book 12, Ch9 L14).

### Alejandro Albericci

- Proprietor of the tailor shop Sensual Attire for the Sensual Gentleman in Rimaros, with formidable society connections (Book 7, Ch42 L61; Book 9, Ch3 L75); he adjusts the formalwear for the celebration ball (Book 9, Ch13 L3). A priest named Aldrich "Al" Albericci appears in Book 7 (Book 7, Ch9 L113); the relation is not stated.

### Havi Estos

- An information broker who owns a building through layers of paperwork (Book 7, Ch43 L47). In Book 9 he promises Killian Laurent for a price (Book 9, Ch3 L86) and is carried off unconscious (Book 9, Ch4 L16).

### Autumn Leal

- An adventurer Jason met before reaching Rimaros, with a frog essence and a frog familiar ritual (Book 7, Ch20 L21; Book 9, Ch1 L49; Book 9, Ch10 L102).

### Zolit Kreen

- A valash outworlder in Yaresh with a silver-rank aura and no core use (Book 9, Ch46 L5-12), later held in the campus prison (Book 9, Ch77 L3).

### Order of Redeeming Light personnel

- Fuller entries are in `Minor_Cast_Order_of_Redeeming_Light.md`.
- Members named in Book 8: Sendira, Fila and Ramona, all women, Ramona once a celestine (Book 8, Ch9 L11); Rhett and Jaime, silver-rank members who stand apart from the compelled majority (Book 8, Ch16 L28; Book 8, Ch29 L28); Caitlyn, Heston, Marika and Elise, who challenge Melody Jain (Book 8, Ch18 L4-25); Kelleigh, Melody's cell member (Book 8, Ch18 L68); Laront, an ordinary Purity priest who never passed the flames (Book 8, Ch3 L38).
- A Ramona on Miriam Vance's team, Moon's Edge, is a different person unless the text says otherwise (Book 11, Ch46 L11). Caitlyn's group thinks pain purifies the soul (Book 8, Ch33 L45).

### Merrick Harlowe

- The commander of a fort, a minor local lord and a silver-rank core user, who watches the first shab fall to the town's defences with little joy (Book 7, Ch19 L95). Melody Jain works on his sympathies (Book 7, Ch20 L84; Book 7, Ch32 L51, L86).

### Eric Maldonado

- The organiser behind the hunt for Jason in Book 9. He has sunk favours and most of his surge earnings into it (Book 9, Ch9 L57-58) and hired a costly tracker and an Adventure Society communications official (Book 9, Ch9 L58, L62). Shade judges his team the best prepared of the hunters (Book 9, Ch9 L19).

### Rangel and Tellez

- Two team leaders hunting Jason in the jungle (Book 9, Ch7 L33, L46). Rangel admits he stole Maldonado's plan (Book 9, Ch7 L92), and Jason takes four people from his group (Book 9, Ch9 L63).

### Carilo

- A healer in Rangel's party, told to cleanse a poisoned teammate (Book 9, Ch8 L6) before Jason drags him into the scrub (Book 9, Ch8 L12).

### Emresh

- A thug and loan collector in Yaresh who kicks Jason on the floor while Bellory fetches the repayment (Book 9, Ch44 L25, L60). He is silver rank and pushes past others in a crisis (Book 9, Ch101 L54).

### Lancet de Varco

- A tournament duellist of House de Varco and sometime adventurer, well known in the mirage arenas (Book 9, Ch15 L14), who is touchy about being called a coward (Book 9, Ch15 L53, L67).
