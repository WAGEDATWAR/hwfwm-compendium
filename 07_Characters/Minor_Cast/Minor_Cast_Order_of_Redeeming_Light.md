## 1. Summary

The named members of Melody Jain's Sea of Storms contingent. Compare Melody's own file for the order's history.

## 2. Entries

### Sendira

- Melody's second in command and the person she trusts most, not the most imaginative subordinate (Book 7, Ch58 L38; Book 8, Ch9 L17; Book 8, Ch33 L15). She defends Melody against rivals (Book 8, Ch9 L50) and activates the aura beacon in the grab (Book 8, Ch13 L51; Book 8, Ch16 L86).

### Laront

- The order's church advisor, a young-looking human priest of Purity who never passed through the flames, so he can visit cities safely (Book 7, Ch58 L38; Book 8, Ch3 L38). He buys information from an Adventure Society functionary (Book 8, Ch3 L39-58) and says "I am the church's eyes" (Book 8, Ch18 L14).

### Esteban Galo

- A gold-rank adventurer who has seen more of the order's operation than Melody and Laront like (Book 8, Ch17 L5, L29).

### Fila

- A cell leader who says Melody advances her personal agenda and risks the order's assets (Book 8, Ch9 L30); she warns that most of their forces will be lost (Book 8, Ch9 L49).

### Ramona

- A human who was once a celestine, her hair and eyes now sunshine blonde and blue (Book 8, Ch9 L11); she clears tunnels with stone powers and blames Sendira for the beacon (Book 8, Ch13 L47-51; Book 8, Ch14 L71). A Ramona on Miriam Vance's team, Moon's Edge, is a gold-rank shield specialist and ritualist (Book 11, Ch46 L11); the text does not link her to this Ramona, so treat them as two people.

### Rhett and Jaime

- Silver-rank members who frogmarch a prisoner and are talkative and more human than the compelled majority (Book 8, Ch16 L28; Book 8, Ch29 L26-29). Jaime thinks torture is wrong because they "worship Purity, not Pain" (Book 8, Ch33 L45).

### Caitlyn, Heston, Marika and Elise

- Caitlyn, Heston, Marika and Elise challenge Melody's leadership (Book 8, Ch18 L4-34). Caitlyn's group thinks pain purifies the soul (Book 8, Ch33 L45). Elise and Marika are cell leaders who each try to seize control of the order after the island operation fails (Book 8, Ch44 L29-40).

### Kelleigh

- A blank-faced, dedicated member of Melody's cell who delivers a prisoner (Book 8, Ch18 L68) and quotes resonating-force siege weapons (Book 8, Ch33 L24, L47).
