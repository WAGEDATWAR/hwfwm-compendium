## 1. Summary

## 2. Entries

### Beaufort (Builder cult commander)

- A gold-rank elf who heads the Cult of the Builder's forces, gave up his essence powers and is a semi-artificial monstrosity with a fifteen-foot metal skeleton war form (Book 10, Ch65 L30; Book 10, Ch69 L3-5).
- He calls Jason "the Defier", allies with the brighthearts and Jason's expedition and leads the cultists in Book 11 (Book 10, Ch65 L39; Book 11, Ch23 L78; Book 11, Ch44 L39). He shares his name with Thadwick Mercer's father, but is not him (Book 10, Ch72 L41).

### Hils Jaramaris

- The Storm Kingdom's ambassador to Estercost, a silver-ranker who used to adventure with his friend Orin, who went on to travel with Asano (Book 12, Ch49 L3-5).

### Garret Headingway

- An upper-echelon member of House Headingway, one of Estercost's pre-eminent families (Book 12, Ch50 L3), who is held in a suppression collar in Sadi Andali (Book 12, Ch50 L120-126). A different Garret leads a superhero team in Book 5 (Book 5, Ch19 L10).

### Neiman Bassingthwaite

- The chief membership officer of a guild in Cyrion who deals with Jason and is not on the guild master's side (Book 12, Ch44 L112; Book 12, Ch45 L64).

### Kreegle

- A pirate rabble-rouser aboard Jota Withers's ship who plots against him and wants to return to Jakaar triumphant (Book 12, Ch63 L43-53; Book 12, Ch64 L12; Book 12, Ch65 L4).

### Navise Den Rigal

- A Voice of the Will serving a minor astral king who is secretly one of the Unorthodoxy's rare astral kings (Book 12, Ch26 L19-36).

### Jacinta Adeline

- Director of the Adventure Society's Cyrion branch, in one of the organisation's most political posts, who confronts Danielle after the System's withdrawal message (Book 12, Ch50 L43-52).

### Barstow

- The CIA director who briefs the international group on Pakistan and Jason (Book 13, Ch961 L59; Book 13, Ch967 L18-57).

### General Michael Mills

- A general who leads a Book 13 meeting and raises the idea that McKean siphons power from Jason's domains (Book 13, Ch1003 L3; Book 13, Ch1011 L41), and enters a later meeting in an EDJI service dress uniform (Book 13, Ch1020 L85).

### Finneus Gallow

- A monster-core user from Gormanston's aristocratic faction, confronted by Marcus over who keeps Adventure Society membership after the messenger invasion (Book 10, Ch48 L15, L31, L41).

### Geneva Andros

- Director of the Network's Geneva branch (Book 13, Ch974 L52; Book 13, Ch975 L4). She is the only silver-ranker among the gold-rankers Jason fights on the flying bus (Book 13, Ch976 L6) and accuses Bussinger of turning traitor (Book 13, Ch977 L107).

### Kol Kelis Vel

- A gold-rank messenger in the Cardinas zone alongside Galis Jay Vahal (Book 11, Ch6 L50). Belinda's hidden circle leaves her badly hurt (Book 11, Ch14 L10, L20).
