## 1. Summary

The named members of Beth Cavendish's and Padma Parsell's teams.

## 2. Entries

### Mose Cavendish

- Beth's cousin and teammate, who asks Jason questions about where he comes from (Book 1, Ch80 L5-48) and enters the Adventure Society looking flustered (Book 1, Ch83 L38). A bolt hits him in the Ustei fight (Book 1, Ch89 L72).

### Niko

- A smoulder with dark skin, glowing red eyes and jet-black hair who throws iron harpoons and has fire and iron powers with a size buff (Book 1, Ch89 L63; Book 1, Ch90 L13; Book 2, Ch30 L16).

### Emily

- A celestine archer with fair skin and golden eyes that match her pixie-cut hair, whose arrows duplicate (Book 1, Ch89 L85; Book 1, Ch90 L15). She has the legendary myriad essence (Book 2, Ch48 L58).

### Lance and Oscar

- Lance leads Padma's team, an elf whose swordsmanship relies on finesse of magic as well as hands; his essences are sword, myriad and adept, making the master confluence (Book 2, Ch39 L12; Book 3, Ch1 L90). Oscar is a teammate who makes a remark about Lance's sword essence (Book 2, Ch39 L37).
