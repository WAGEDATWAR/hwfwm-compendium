## 2. Entries

### Kylie Chen

- A bronze-rank Network operative suited to quiet scouting rather than combat (Book 4, Ch42 L27).

### Chen (category four)

- An older Network category four with "aggravating shield powers" who goes into the Slovakia dome with Gerling and two Chinese category fours (Book 6, Ch6 L31; Book 6, Ch7 L22-23); he kills anomalies fast with beak-like finger strikes (Book 6, Ch7 L72).
- Whether he is the same person as Kylie Chen is not stated (she is bronze in Book 4).

### Gordon Truffett

- The government representative at the negotiations with Jason, a man with no magic (Book 4, Ch36 L139-145). In Book 11 he snatches a phone and says he is onsite and taking command of operations (Book 11, Ch68 L476). In Book 13 he is said to need the publicity (Book 13, Ch950 L47).

### Emerson Cleary

- Deputy Director of the Network and Gerling's handler, angry that Jason escaped Berlin (Book 4, Ch53 L75; Book 5, Ch46 L86; Book 5, Ch51 L8; Book 6, Ch19 L27).
- Gerling says he is better than past handlers who treated him like a monster in a pit (Book 5, Ch47 L102).

### Gladys Erstweller

- A silver-rank (category three) Earth healer who gives her name to Jason's team (Book 4, Ch31 L48); in 2038 she treats Nanna's Alzheimer's and lectures with Neil (Book 13, Ch956 L23; Book 13, Ch995 L64).

### Hector De Lange

- A Belgian official of the International Committee who runs the Saint-Étienne operation (Book 4, Ch51 L10; Book 4, Ch52 L84). A different Hector appears in Book 9 among those intimidated by the royal family (Book 9, Ch16 L50).

### Todd "The Necromancer" Halverson

- A silver-ranker with 94 percent core taint and the territory claimant in the Saint-Étienne zone (Book 6, Ch39 L25-27; Book 6, Ch38 L76-81).

### Thanh

- A category-three member of the capture team that hunts Jason through a large city in Book 5; his light essence gives him the speed to keep up and the aura to light the slum town (Book 5, Ch1 L15-17). Jason's aura stops his dead (Book 5, Ch1 L35), and Jason ends the fight with a threat to his soul (Book 5, Ch1 L85).

### Ingrid

- The head of the security team at the base where Travis is put in charge of the Special Munition Stockpile Division (Book 6, Ch28 L37-39). Jason says she gives off an Anisa vibe (Book 6, Ch29 L13).

### Loth

- A silver-rank dino-man who chose his own name, leading almost thirty dino-monsters through the Makassar proto-space in search of the unknown being behind the rainbow smoke (Book 5, Ch27 L24-26). He loses control of them as Jason's afflictions spread (Book 5, Ch27 L36-37).

### Night Stalker

- A bronze-rank vampire who fights beside Craig Vermillion and Franklin (Book 5, Ch64 L68) and calls himself the Night Stalker; Farrah threatens to put him down for it (Book 5, Ch65 L18).

### Higgy

- A member of Nigel Thornton's team who, with Darcy and Jonno, takes and gives the big hits (Book 4, Ch39 L76). He conjures a shield and prefers Higgy to "Captain America" (Book 4, Ch40 L63).
<!-- level:3 -->
- He is still with the team in Book 12, steering a conversation back on topic (Book 12, Ch3 L38), and in Book 13 calls "CONTACT!" as the squad guards Itsuki (Book 13, Ch1007 L39).
