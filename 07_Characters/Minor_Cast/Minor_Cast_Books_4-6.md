## 1. Summary

Minor Network, government and Earth figures of the Earth books that have no file of their own.

## 2. Entries

### Kylie Chen

- A bronze-rank Network operative suited to quiet scouting rather than combat (Book 4, Ch42 L27).

### Chen (category four)

- An older Network category four with "aggravating shield powers" who goes into the Slovakia dome with Gerling and two Chinese category fours (Book 6, Ch6 L31; Book 6, Ch7 L22-23); he kills anomalies fast with beak-like finger strikes (Book 6, Ch7 L72).
- Whether he is the same person as Kylie Chen is not stated (she is bronze in Book 4).

### Gordon Truffett

- The government representative at the negotiations with Jason, a man with no magic (Book 4, Ch36 L139-145). He takes command on site as a government figure in Book 11 (Book 11, Ch68 L476) and needs publicity in Book 13 (Book 13, Ch950 L47).

### Emerson Cleary

- Deputy Director of the Network and Gerling's handler, angry that Jason escaped Berlin (Book 4, Ch53 L75; Book 5, Ch46 L86; Book 5, Ch51 L8; Book 6, Ch19 L27).
- Gerling says he is better than past handlers who treated him like a monster in a pit (Book 5, Ch47 L102).

### Gladys Erstweller

- A silver-rank (category three) Earth healer who gives her name to Jason's team (Book 4, Ch31 L48); in 2038 she treats Nanna's Alzheimer's and lectures with Neil (Book 13, Ch956 L23; Book 13, Ch995 L64).

### Hector De Lange

- A Belgian official of the International Committee who runs the Saint-Étienne operation (Book 4, Ch51 L10; Book 4, Ch52 L84). A different Hector appears in Book 9 among those intimidated by the royal family (Book 9, Ch16 L50).

### Todd "The Necromancer" Halverson

- A silver-ranker with 94 percent core taint and the territory claimant in the Saint-Étienne zone (Book 6, Ch39 L25-27; Book 6, Ch38 L76-81).
