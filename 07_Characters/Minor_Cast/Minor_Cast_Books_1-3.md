## 2. Entries

### Albert Bertinelli ("Bert")

- A clerk at the Adventure Society who introduces himself as Albert and says his brother was Bertram, "feel free to just call me Bert" (Book 1, Ch37 L98); he records Jason's contracts at the jobs hall (Book 1, Ch64 L5; Book 1, Ch107 L49).
- He mentions the lumber mill owner's fergax complaint (Book 1, Ch92 L65) and later remarks on the fate of Jason's early captors (Book 3, Ch24 L60).

### Genevieve Picot

- Deputy director of the Adventure Society branch, an elf who looks uncharacteristically aged for a silver-ranker (Book 1, Ch98 L63; Book 6, Ch47 L369).
- She meets Jason and Elspeth in a black parlour in Book 2 (Book 2, Ch59 L22).

### Darryl Caruthers

- A cultist who loudly demands worship in the Vane estate ("the magic of Darryl Caruthers", Book 1, Ch11 L73) and says "I'm the Darryl" (Book 1, Ch14 L18).
- In Book 12 a Darryl among the Earth refugees ranked up without cores and grows naturally powerful (Book 12, Ch53 L61); the text checked does not state they are the same man, and a High Priest Darryl lies dead in Book 1, Ch13 L3.

### Beaufort Mercer

- The Duke of Greenstone's brother (Book 1, Ch101 L9), Thalia's husband (Book 2, Ch59 L4) and Thadwick's father, who facilitated a Mercer scheme (Book 2, Ch15 L27) and raised Thadwick himself (Book 11, Ch6 L59).
- Jason notes in Book 10 that the Builder cult's Beaufort shares his name but not his villainy (Book 10, Ch72 L41).

### Dean Tuckell and Clementson

- Beth Cavendish's and Padma's teammates are in `Minor_Cast_Remore_Academy_and_Greenstone_Teams.md`.
- Dean Tuckell is an iron-rank adventurer who summons monsters at the Clementson mill for Thadwick Mercer (Book 1, Ch94 L25-58). Clementson is the mill owner, a normal-rank man (Book 1, Ch94 L16).
- Kyle Lindover, the leonid mill owner, is the victim of the fergax contract (Book 1, Ch93 L24-29).

### Madam Landry

- The landlady of the Greenstone inn where Jason lodges, who berates a tall man in scholar's robes in her lounge (Book 1, Ch64 L83). Jason calls her "a small business owner who works very hard to run a quality establishment" (Book 1, Ch82 L82).
<!-- level:3 -->
- By Book 12 she finds second-tier adventurers the perfect clientele for long-term tenancy (Book 12, Ch27 L5).
<!-- /level -->

### Janice

- Jory's teenage receptionist and assistant at his shop (Book 1, Ch44 L40; Book 1, Ch106 L8). Jason pleads with her for crystal wash (Book 1, Ch76 L15).
<!-- level:3 -->
- A Janice manages a patisserie in Vitesse in Book 11 (Book 11, Ch68 L2060); the text does not say she is the same person.
<!-- /level -->

### Ernest Geller

- A Geller team leader who looks like Humphrey, with the same height and broad shoulders; Jason does not assume a close relation (Book 1, Ch80 L90). He makes a "teaching moment" of his team's choices (Book 1, Ch82 L31), leads the team at the sand barge battle (Book 1, Ch90 L32) and later guards Jonah Geller (Book 2, Ch19 L73).

### Jerrick

- The burly thug adventurer, toughest of those Thadwick Mercer took under his auspices (Book 1, Ch95 L16); he fights with a spiked sphere on a chain (Book 1, Ch95 L46) and is quietly struck off the Adventure Society roll afterwards (Book 1, Ch97 L101).

### Imran Keane

- A guardian-type who conjures heavy armour and a huge shield, added to a party with Jory in the Reaper trials (Book 2, Ch46 L42) and then to Jason's party (Book 2, Ch53 L64).

### Arash

- A short, stocky runic man, blue-black skin with glowing markings, whom Jason asks about the temples of the Divine Square; he explains "Dominion issues the divine right to kings and nobles" (Book 1, Ch45 L14-18). When Hero appears he drops to his knees and drags at Jason's sleeve to make him kneel (Book 1, Ch45 L35-45).
- In Book 12 an Arash, whose nephew Jamar is a bartender, has "never been further than Hornis, down south" (Book 12, Ch38 L49, L61). The Name_Index calls the Book 1 Arash a juice vendor; the lines read do not show that.

### Dink

- An Old City thug who tries to beat Jason, certain he is weak: "that Geller lady set up a fight so you could beat all her fancy trainees... the whole thing was rigged, and really you're weak" (Book 1, Ch71 L25). One of his companions doubts him after feeling Jason's aura (Book 1, Ch71 L16, L23).

### Hiram

- A man "of late-middle years", shorter than Jason with a barrel chest and ropy muscle, who watches the aperture that is the source of the waterfall and guides Jason up the track (Book 1, Ch26 L4-5, L12). He has a might essence that "makes me strong" (Book 1, Ch27 L15).

### Ketis

- Emir Bahadir's granddaughter, the child of his dead son; she was raised by her grandmother, "not a woman of means", and never sought Emir out for money (Book 2, Ch64 L151). Emir greets her warmly (Book 2, Ch32 L41) and says she will "get the best of everything" (Book 2, Ch64 L152).
- Arabelle says she got her essences so young she has been waiting longer than most to go out into the world: "She's full of rebellious ideas" (Book 3, Ch69 L477).

### Tabitha Gert

- A stern Adventure Society official, plain in dress apart from her black Adventure Society pin, who leads the inquiry team into the Greenstone events (Book 2, Ch10 L16). She is the de facto head of the Society while the inquiry lasts and chairs the meeting on the Builder cult's next moves (Book 2, Ch36 L19-25).

### Crivens

- The Mercer family butler, in "uncharacteristic panic" when Thadwick is missed (Book 2, Ch59 L15). Thalia has him gather the team tracking Thadwick (Book 2, Ch59 L35-36).

### Adam Cosgrove

- A thirty-one-year-old Melbourne detective sergeant who "looked like a detective from a TV show", investigating the missing chunk of Jason's apartment building (Book 3, Ch69 L602-606). A woman with a swelling presence offers him the answers: "come work for us" (Book 3, Ch69 L696-700).

<!-- level:2 -->
- By Book 6 he is "a former detective and a former EOA member", an ordinary man among powerful company (Book 6, Ch24 L87; Book 6, Ch25 L18-21).
<!-- /level -->
