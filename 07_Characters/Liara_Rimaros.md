## 1. Summary

Liara Rimaros is a gold-rank Adventure Society special-operations officer and a lesser member of the Rimaros royal house. She screens adventurers for Builder cultists in Book 7, leads the Book 8 expedition into the Builder's complex, handles Jason for the royal family and stays a brisk, protective ally.

## 2. Appearance

- Tall (her eldest daughter has "Liara's height") and gold-rank fast, flitting through streets unnoticed (Book 7, Ch17 L20; Book 8, Ch62 L39).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Brisk and official**: she is working without rest as the city fills with adventurers to be filtered for cultists (Book 7, Ch17 L3).
- **Wary of politics**: she "disdained" the politics she "could never seem to escape" (Book 9, Ch11 L53).
- **Self-critical but resolute**: after Gibson Amouz's capture she "allowed shame to flash over her expression before schooling it" (Book 8, Ch20 L3); she stands up to his father, "Get out of my office, Lord Amouz" (Book 8, Ch20 L57).
- **Protective**: "Don't even think about it", she tells Zareen of Jason (Book 8, Ch62 L59).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- **Rank**: a gold-ranker, leader of the Book 8 expedition with her former teammate Jana Costi as second (Book 8, Ch7 L4).
- **Investigator**: her search for the cult's portal users is a process of elimination through portal schedules, market tracking and field reports (Book 8, Ch20 L11).
- **Not for rescues**: she understands why she was told not to take part personally in the mining-complex rescue: "Her abilities were ill-suited to the task and her emotional investment would not be an asset" (Book 8, Ch40 L29).

## 5. Background

- **Royal branch**: "from one of the more remote branches of the royal family", not close enough to be called a princess, but entitled to a home on the royal sky island and to take part in the contests for the throne in her youth (Book 7, Ch17 L14).
- **Career**: "a long and prestigious career in Adventure Society special operations" (Book 7, Ch17 L15).
- **Marriage**: in her youth she married a local lordling, Baseph, "to secure royal influence"; they live largely separate lives but remain friends (Book 7, Ch17 L19-20). Baseph is born into House Amouz (Book 9, Ch5 L3).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 7**: she has been "working without rest" as adventurers are filtered for potential cultists (Book 7, Ch17 L3); Jason is told "Pelli and Liara Rimaros will be your contact points" (Book 7, Ch18 L95). She and Keel watch Jason and Farrah through a seeing stone (Book 7, Ch11 L65), and takes Jason to a private meeting room in the administration building (Book 7, Ch38 L91).
- **Book 8**: she leads the expedition on the Builder complex (Book 8, Ch7 L4), faces Lord Amouz (Book 8, Ch20 L14-57), re-collates her files (Book 8, Ch26 L15) and, assigned to handle Jason for the royal family, meets him with an agenda rather than gratitude (Book 8, Ch62 L30).
- **Book 9**: she tells Jason's group not to roam like a pack (Book 9, Ch14 L5), sits with him at the confrontation (Book 9, Ch16 L3) and goes to the cloud house (Book 9, Ch6 L73).
