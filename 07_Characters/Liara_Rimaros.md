## 1. Summary

A gold-rank member of the Rimaros royal house who screens adventurers for Builder cultists.

## 2. Facts

- Works without rest as the city is overrun with adventurers to be filtered for potential cultists, and is a gold-ranker (Book 7, Ch17 L3).
- "Pelli and Liara Rimaros will be your contact points going forward" for Jason (Book 7, Ch18 L95).
- Her husband is Baseph and their three silver-rank children are Dara, Zareen and the youngest, Joseph, an administrative official (Book 8, Ch62 L28-32).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Brisk, official and not unkind.
