## 1. Summary

Neil Davone is an elf of Greenstone's Island families, a healer who grows from the Mercers' hanger-on into the heart of Jason's team. He is a priest of the Healer, and by 2038 he uses his growth magic to reclaim deserts.

## 2. Appearance

Muscular (Sophie and Belinda comment on his "muscular body", Book 2, Ch71 L14), though the notes describe him as "chubby for an elf" (Book 2, Ch71 L14).

## 3. Personality and voice

He grew up in the Mercer family's orbit: he started spending time at the Mercer compound as a boy because Thadwick needed friends his own age and the Davone and Kettering families had close ties to the Mercers (Book 2, Ch27 L41-43). At thirteen he had a crush on Cassandra Mercer, four years older (Book 2, Ch27 L41-48). He makes a stand in the Temple of the Healer (Book 1, Ch94 L3-7), and after being rejected by the Mercers' circle the guards treat him as "just another iron-ranker" (Book 2, Ch27 L59). He is uncertain about joining Jason's team (Book 2, Ch27 L46) and shows a nervous expression when pressed (Book 2, Ch27 L112).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- **Essences**: Shield, Growth and Renewal, producing the Prosperity confluence (Jason's notebook, Book 2, Ch28 L44): short-lived shields that intercept attacks, healing and cleansing.
- **Abilities**: size-growth power (Book 2, Ch30 L52-54) and Armour of Renewal, which reduces damage taken and gives healing over time (Book 2, Ch64 L55); the Reaper's Redoubt (wiki).
- **Summon**: a chrysalis golem "like an ogre carved out of diamond" with runes appearing on its facets as it is hit (Book 3, Ch4 L29-32).
- **Late**: his Growth, Renewal and Prosperity essences find fertile ground in desert reclamation in Australia and North Africa (Book 13, Ch997 L37-38).

## 5. Background

An elf of the Davone family, whose household is on the Island and holds tea lands in the Mistrun valley (Book 2, Ch23 L17, where a maid serves the family's blend). He was initially Thadwick Mercer's lackey and team mate (Book 1, Ch110 L40; Book 2, Ch75 L67); after the disastrous expedition Thadwick disbanded the team without notice and the two were glad to be free of being known as Thadwick's flunkeys (Book 2, Ch27 L57), and Neil then joined Jason's team (Book 11, Ch6 L63). Cassandra Mercer is not his ex: as a youth he admired Thadwick's older sister (Book 2, Ch27 L43), and he was angry that she chose Jason (Book 11, Ch6 L60). She was Jason's girlfriend and ended it (Book 2, Ch13 L19).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: stands in the Temple of the Healer; joins Rufus, Gary and Jory at the clinic reopening.
- **Books 2-3**: joins Jason's team; the chrysalis golem and shields.
- **Book 11**: a defender-healer in the transformation zone with Dustin Kettering (Book 11, Ch8 L23-24).
- **Book 12**: in the Mirror Kingdom with Nik (Book 12, Ch12 L75), portalled in with Team Shining Scabbard led by Sigrid Freyn, with whom Neil had been training (Book 12, Ch15 L8).
- **Book 13**: reclaiming deserts.

## 7. Relationships

Jory Tillman, Dustin Kettering (friend, tank), the Mercer family, Cassandra Mercer, Nik, the Church of the Healer.
