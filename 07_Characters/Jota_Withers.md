## 1. Summary

Jota Withers is a gold-rank dimension ship captain in a pirate admiral's service who is sent to Earth in Book 12. Weary, proud and cautious, he has always been one rung short of what he was promised.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in the lines read.

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Weary and proud**: he reflects that the universe-city of Interstice is "for most practical purposes, the centre of the cosmos" (Book 12, Ch57 L3).
- **Self-aware**: arrogance cost him opportunities (Book 12, Ch57 L7-12).
- **Cautious**: he watches Jason float towards him, noting the impenetrable aura and the scars probably not affectations (Book 12, Ch64 L76-79).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Gold rank; his vessel is a rarity, only gold rank, which suits certain jobs (Book 12, Ch57 L14).

## 5. Background

- Born in a feeder realm, he fell short of Interstice at gold rank and ended up serving "a self-styled cosmic admiral" who was a pirate lord (Book 12, Ch57 L7-12).

## 6. Timeline of involvement
