## 1. Summary

A gold-rank dimension ship captain in a pirate admiral's service who is sent to Earth in Book 12.

## 2. Facts

- He sits on the bridge of his dimension ship, reflecting that the universe-city of Interstice is "for most practical purposes, the centre of the cosmos" (Book 12, Ch57 L3).
- Born in a feeder realm, he fell short of Interstice at gold rank; arrogance cost him opportunities and he ended up serving "a self-styled cosmic admiral" who was a pirate lord (Book 12, Ch57 L7-12).
- His vessel is a rarity, only gold rank, which suits certain jobs (Book 12, Ch57 L14).
- On Earth he is hosted by the Asano clan matriarch Noriko, who visits daily (Book 12, Ch63 L16-19).
- He watches Jason float towards him, noting his impenetrable aura and the scars that are probably not affectations (Book 12, Ch64 L76-79).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Weary, proud and cautious; a gold-ranker who has always been one rung short of what he was promised.
