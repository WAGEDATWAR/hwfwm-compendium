## 1. Summary

A servant of the messengers who works as Zolit's aide and brings Jason to their attention.

## 2. Facts

- She corrects someone who mispronounces her name (Book 9, Ch46 L40) and offers Jason fight outfits from her duffel (Book 9, Ch46 L44).
- She is Zolit's quiet aide at the fights (Book 9, Ch47 L25, L49).
- Her master is Fal Vin Garath (Book 9, Ch56 L5); he scolds her and demands she explain the servant races' different way (Book 9, Ch52 L20-29).
- She has "one chance to prove herself still valuable to the messengers" by presenting Jason Asano as a potential threat (Book 9, Ch56 L4), and presents the issue to Voice Kaal personally (Book 9, Ch56 L20).
- Kaal probes her emotions and deduces there is more to it than she says (Book 9, Ch56 L22-26).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Careful, watchful and afraid; she hides what she thinks behind a servant's neutrality.
