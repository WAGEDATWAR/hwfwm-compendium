## 1. Summary

Benella is a servant of the messengers who works as Zolit's aide, brings Jason to the messengers' attention and is given one chance to prove herself valuable. Careful, watchful and afraid, she hides what she thinks behind a servant's neutrality.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in the lines read; she carries a duffel of fight outfits (Book 9, Ch46 L44).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Neutral by necessity**: she is relieved but not fool enough to thank the messenger, as showing that his consideration mattered would get her punished (Book 9, Ch55 L34).
- **Self-possessed**: "Benella," she corrected with an annoyed shake of her head (Book 9, Ch46 L40).
- **Uncertain under scrutiny**: she is unsure why Kaal is interested in her thought process (Book 9, Ch56 L22).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Not an essence user in the lines read; she is Zolit's aide, silent until the priest leaves (Book 9, Ch47 L49).

## 5. Background

- Her master is Fal Vin Garath (Book 9, Ch56 L5); he scolds her and demands she explain the servant races' different way (Book 9, Ch52 L20-29).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 9**: offers Jason fight outfits (Book 9, Ch46 L44-51); Zolit's quiet aide at the fights (Book 9, Ch47 L25, L49); has "one chance to prove herself still valuable" by presenting Jason as a threat (Book 9, Ch56 L4); presents to Voice Kaal (Book 9, Ch56 L20); Kaal probes her emotions and deduces more (Book 9, Ch56 L22-26).

## 7. Relationships

- **Fal Vin Garath**: her abusive master (Book 9, Ch56 L5).
- **Jes Fin Kaal**: the Voice she presents to (Book 9, Ch56 L20).
- **Zolit**: the fighter she aids (Book 9, Ch46 L51).
