## 1. Summary

Soramir Rimaros is the founder of the Rimaros royal house, a diamond-rank ruler with a palindromic name, who guides Jason through the Builder crisis. His connection to Jason is why Jason is protected from political accusations.

## 2. Appearance

- Emerald hair and eyes at a party, then natural royal blue (Book 7, Ch18 L49).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Calming and formal**: his aura spreads calm that oppresses the agitated Farrah and Liara (Book 7, Ch46 L86).
- **Plain-spoken about debts**: "It is undeniable that we have dragged Jason Asano into events he never asked for" (Book 7, Ch46 L98).
- **Deference from others**: even the Storm King defers to the founder in the private lounge (Book 9, Ch14 L4).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Diamond-rank ruler (Book 13, Ch1024 L14).
- He tells Jason that the Hierophant may intervene in the world a single time (Book 7, Ch60 L27).

## 5. Background

- Palindromic first and last names "were assigned in adulthood rather than at birth and denoted important members of a given family" (Book 7, Ch18 L41-42).
- Sits with the Storm King and core royal family members in an elevated lounge (Book 9, Ch14 L4).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 7**: meets Jason (Book 7, Ch18 L41-49); the balcony meeting with Liara and Trenchant (Book 7, Ch46 L74-98); leads Dawn away (Book 7, Ch52 L71); the Builder's fortress emerges (Book 7, Ch65 L6).
- **Book 9**: with the royal family at the confrontation (Book 9, Ch14 L4); he steps in when Jason soul-attacks a man in front of the king (Book 9, Ch23 L11).
- **Book 10**: his connection to Jason keeps the Adventure Society from accusing him (Book 10, Ch2 L58).
- **Book 13**: not among the diamond-rankers who emerge from the dimensional vessel (Book 13, Ch1024 L14).

## 7. Relationships

- **Liara**: royal officer and relative (Book 7, Ch46 L74).
- **Jason**: protector and uneasy debtor (Book 7, Ch46 L98).
- **Dawn**: he leads her away from the crowd (Book 7, Ch52 L71).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

His connection to a diamond-ranker Jason knows deferentially is not explained in the lines read (Book 7, Ch56 L6).

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Dignified, plain-spoken and used to getting his way, but aware of what it costs others. Cue: "My family is used to getting our way."
