## 1. Summary

Rufus's father, a strong adventurer who is among the first to answer when Jason's team is in trouble.

## 2. Facts

- First seen as a lifelike water-link image of "Rufus's father, Gabriel Remore" (Book 1, Ch49 L27).
- Appears in person at Farrah's wake with Rufus and Arabelle; Rufus's mother has even darker skin than her husband (Book 2, Ch32 L52).
- Helps rescue a gold-rank messenger at the death-chamber gate in Cardinas with Arabelle (Book 10, Ch78 L52-89).
- Danielle Geller tells Rufus she is investigating Jason "at your father's request" (Book 1, Ch48 L101-102).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Brisk and capable; he trusts his son's friends once they have earned it.
