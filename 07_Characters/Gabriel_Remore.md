## 1. Summary

Gabriel Remore is Rufus's father, a gold-rank fire-and-sword adventurer and academy teacher who reforms his old team with Emir and Arabelle when Jason needs gold-rankers in Book 10. Brisk and capable, he fights beside his wife and trusts his son's friends once they have earned it.

## 2. Appearance

- Looks a lot like Rufus: tall, dark and muscular, but with hair worn in colourful beads that trail down to his shoulders in chains (Book 10, Ch57 L93).
- First seen as a lifelike water-link image (Book 1, Ch49 L27).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Wry**: "I'm her husband!" (Book 10, Ch79 L44) and "Is it just me, or is this messenger a little bit odd?" (Book 10, Ch78 L97).
- **Practical about interrogation**: "it's not easy to get a gold-ranker to talk. We can all hold up to a lot of pain" (Book 10, Ch66 L87).
- **Deflates when his son is surly**: "That's fine!... Understood, son" (Book 10, Ch57 L101).
- **Wary of suspicious allies**: "If the messengers have people that can go down there... what do they need us for?" (Book 10, Ch59 L39).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Gold rank with fire powers; his ash cloud carries him and Arabelle and his sword glows yellow-white like molten steel (Book 10, Ch78 L52, L90).
- He flies a flame-trailing airship down from the sky (Book 10, Ch57 L92).
- Aura control is among the best Jason had sensed at gold rank (Book 7, Ch22 L81).

## 5. Background

- Old team: Emir, Gabriel, Arabelle and Callum; they stopped adventuring while raising their son, and Gabriel started teaching at the academy (Book 9, Ch27 L59).
- Danielle Geller investigates Jason at his request (Book 1, Ch48 L101-102).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1-2**: image on the water link (Book 1, Ch49 L27); at Farrah's wake (Book 2, Ch32 L52); jokes about Emir's palace (Book 2, Ch49 L66); the strike force (Book 2, Ch76 L8).
- **Book 10**: arrives and reunites with Rufus and Arabelle (Book 10, Ch57 L92-93); the old team reforms minus Callum (Book 10, Ch59 L4); the Cardinas expedition (Book 10, Ch66 L66; Book 10, Ch78 L52-91; Book 10, Ch79 L33); defends the wall as the god's power pushes (Book 10, Ch91 L6).

## 7. Relationships

- **Arabelle**: wife, field partner (Book 10, Ch78 L52).
- **Rufus**: son (Book 10, Ch57 L92).
- **Emir, Callum**: old teammates; Callum was swapped out (Book 10, Ch59 L4).
