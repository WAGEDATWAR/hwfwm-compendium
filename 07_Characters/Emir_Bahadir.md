## 1. Summary

Emir Bahadir is a gold-rank treasure hunter, patron of the Reaper contest and owner of a cloud ship and palace that becomes Jason's team's base. Elegant, ageless and wanderlustful, he has hunted the remnants of the Order of the Reaper for years, uses his wealth and staff for large schemes, and marries his chief of staff, Constance.

## 2. Appearance

- Dark-skinned, with dark curly hair tied back behind his head, "midnight skin" and sleek clothes (Book 2, Ch31 L90; Book 1, Ch101 L64). He wears his hair in colourful beads, close to the head (Book 10, Ch57 L93).
- He "didn't look to be more than thirty years old", with an "agelessness" to him, and dresses well, largely thanks to Constance (Book 1, Ch108 L35; Book 3, Ch2 L65).
- He carries a staff he can extend and shrink (Book 10, Ch92 L44).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Likes grand entrances**: "I'm hosting a grand event. It requires grandeur" (Book 2, Ch49 L67). He announces a competition and a grand-prize cloud palace in a speech that throws the crowd into turmoil (Book 2, Ch40 L65).
- **Warm and wry**: he tells Rufus "Comrades behind her, enemies in front of her and friends beside her" of Farrah's apparent death (Book 1, Ch110 L56) and sighs "world-weary" when Jason storms off (Book 2, Ch34 L44).
- **Cautious in accusation**: "Making that kind of accusation against a church is no small matter and even I'm not completely convinced yet" (Book 2, Ch37 L95).
- **Not used to being the responsible one**: that is Constance's job, but he is the older and higher-ranked man (Book 9, Ch51 L14).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- **Essences**: he has "staff, might, magic and mystic" (Book 2, Ch5 L88).
- **Cloud ship and palace**: a cloud ship the size of an ocean liner that morphs into a palace (Book 1, Ch101 L103; Book 2, Ch40 L54; see Cloud_Constructs_and_Palaces.md). His preferred design is a five-towered castle (Book 9, Ch50 L27).
- **Staff and speed**: he conjures copies of his staff under every construct, smashing the iron and bronze ones and tossing the silvers (Book 1, Ch111 L76) and catches Danielle before she can lunge (Book 2, Ch19 L93).
- **Aura mastery**: he can block the senses of others, normally taught only to gold-rankers (Book 9, Ch51 L11).

## 5. Background

- **The Reaper search**: he has "studied the style more than most outsiders to the Order of the Reaper" and hypothesised that it was meant to be learned through skill books (Book 6, Ch44 L12). His patron's search for Order remnants is why Emir has hunted them for years (Book 9, Ch27 L65).
- **Old team**: he, Arabelle, Gabriel and Callum Morse were a team (Book 3, Ch24 L35; Book 2, Ch76 L3).
- **Family**: a son who died, whose wife died with him, leaving his granddaughter Ketis, raised by her grandmother (Book 2, Ch64 L151).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Books 1-3**: the team moves into his cloud palace (Book 2, Ch8 L168), and his chief of staff Constance is among the silver-rankers of the strike force on the Builder cult island (Book 2, Ch76 L3).
- **Books 6-9**: he waits for Constance to reach gold and marries her (Book 6, Ch47 L494, L539-542); Jason calls on him as an old ally and tells him of his changes (Book 9, Ch49-51).
- **Books 10-12**: his palace hosts the large meetings (Book 10, Ch9 L4; Book 10, Ch16 L59); in Cardinas he deflects debris from the collapsing wall (Book 10, Ch92 L44); in Book 12 he and his wife rise to greet newcomers (Book 12, Ch51 L49).

## 7. Relationships

- **Constance**: his chief of staff and "the object of his affections" (Book 6, Ch47 L494); she returns at gold rank and they marry (Book 6, Ch47 L539-542; Book 12, Ch51 L49).
- **Jason**: he gives him a thick notebook of cloud flask knowledge (Book 7, Ch14 L21) and is startled by the change in him in Book 9 (Book 9, Ch51 L6-11).
- **Callum Morse**: old teammate (Book 6, Ch47 L494).
- **Sophie**: she and Emir used Sophie as bait for the order, which Callum resented (Book 9, Ch27 L81; Book 8, Ch27 L34).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

He knew of the Order of the Reaper's return long before the team (Book 9, Ch27 L65).

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Urbane, amused and generous, with a showman's love of timing; he turns serious quickly when someone is in danger. Cue: "I'm hosting a grand event. It requires grandeur."
