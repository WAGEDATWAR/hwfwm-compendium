## 1. Summary

A gold-rank adventurer and patron of the Reaper contest whose cloud palace becomes Jason's team's base.

## 2. Facts

- Introduces himself to Jason as "Emir Bahadir" (Book 1, Ch99 L36); arrives at the Greenstone dock in a cloud ship that opens a hole in its side (Book 1, Ch101 L64).
- Jason walks through "the halls of the cloud palace belonging to Emir Bahadir" (Book 2, Ch1 L3); the team later move in (Book 2, Ch8 L168).
- His gold-rank reflexes catch Danielle before she lunges (Book 2, Ch19 L93); he looks no more than thirty but ageless (Book 1, Ch108 L35).
- Knows the Order of the Reaper were an ancient cult of assassins, scoured by a coalition of churches (Book 2, Ch25 L73).
- Emir says he has "staff, might, magic and mystic" (Book 2, Ch5 L88).
