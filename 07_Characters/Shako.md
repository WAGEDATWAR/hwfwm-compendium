## 1. Summary

A diamond-rank servant of the Builder who kills Jason in Book 6.

## 2. Facts

- Introduces himself: "I am Shako. I am a servant of the Builder" (Book 6, Ch17 L41); he can enter through a window opened by the transformation event so long as he leaves before it closes (Book 6, Ch17 L43).
- A presence takes him over and his aura rises from diamond rank to transcendent when the Builder speaks through him (Book 6, Ch18 L79-84).
- Dawn warns him that killing Jason violated the compact between the Builder, the Reaper and the World-Phoenix (Book 6, Ch18 L79).
- The Church of Death's certification says Jason has died four times so far (Book 7, Ch17 L104); the Master Timeline counts Shako's killing as the fourth.

## 3. Roleplay notes

Impatient and proud; he believes he is the one in control.
