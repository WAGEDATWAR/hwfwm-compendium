## 1. Summary

Shako is a diamond-rank servant of the Builder who kills Jason in Book 6, apparently possessed by the Builder at the moment he does it. Later he is taken by the Sundered Throne, and the Builder replaces him with Erigo Fin Desca.

## 2. Appearance

- Pale, freckled skin, wild red hair and eyes so green they almost glow (Book 3, Ch69 L193).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Impatient and proud**: he sighs at his petitioners despite not needing breath for centuries (Book 3, Ch69 L196).
- **Contemptuous**: he sneers when Jason tries to push back (Book 6, Ch17 L68).
- **Struck still when overridden**: when the Builder speaks through him he goes stiff (Book 8, Ch54 L13).
- **Told he has no authority of his own**: "You don't have any authority, do you, Shako?" (Book 8, Ch52 L56).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Diamond-rank: his aura crushes Jason's aura and lifts him into his grip (Book 6, Ch17 L62).
- Can enter through a window opened by the transformation event as long as he leaves before it closes (Book 6, Ch17 L43).
- Moves faster than Jason can think; a tablet throws him back with a sonic boom (Book 8, Ch52 L59-60).

## 5. Background

- A servant of the Builder (Book 6, Ch17 L41); in Book 3 he is seen in an imperial throne room with an empress (Book 3, Ch69 L207-213).
- Dawn warns that killing Jason violated the compact between the Builder, the Reaper and the World-Phoenix (Book 6, Ch18 L79).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 3**: the imperial palace scene and the Reaper's district (Book 3, Ch69 L193-236).
- **Book 6**: introduces himself and kills Jason (Book 6, Ch17 L41-62); Dawn confronts him (Book 6, Ch18 L4, L27, L79-84); Jason wonders whether he was sent to "lose control" (Book 6, Ch19 L5).
- **Book 8**: tablet incident and Dawn-Builder switch (Book 8, Ch52 L56-60; Book 8, Ch54 L13); catatonic afterwards (Book 8, Ch54 L115).
- **Book 10-11**: Erigo replaces him (Book 10, Ch15 L5); Jason mentions him (Book 11, Ch64 L65).

## 7. Relationships

- **Dawn**: confronts him (Book 6, Ch18 L4).
- **Jason**: killer and later subject of questions (Book 8, Ch55 L99).
- **The Builder**: his master (Book 6, Ch17 L41).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

The Church of Death's certification counts Jason's deaths; Shako's killing is the fourth (Book 7, Ch17 L104).

## 9. Roleplay notes

**Voice (condensed)**: Impatient and proud, believing he is in control; when overridden he stiffens. Cue: "I am Shako. I am a servant of the Builder."
