## 1. Summary

Timos is a Builder cultist embedded in Greenstone for years who regrets going along with Thadwick's self-recruitment, kills Frank, and stands with Zato in the Vane estate ritual. He is careful, patient and horrified by amateurs.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in the lines read; he wears dismissive sneers until Thadwick wipes one off (Book 2, Ch41 L89).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Regretful**: he "quickly came to regret" Thadwick's aggressive self-recruitment (Book 2, Ch59 L87).
- **Cautious**: every mistake "threatened to snowball into disaster" (Book 2, Ch59 L89).
- **Compartmentalising**: he facilitated the cult's insertions and ran operatives in compartmentalised groups (Book 3, Ch34 L10).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- He kills Frank with a conjured spear (Book 2, Ch59 L106-110).
- He takes part in a ritual with Zato and Dougall (Book 3, Ch51 L85).

## 5. Background

- "Timos had been operating in Greenstone for years" (Book 2, Ch59 L87).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 2**: Thadwick presses him about flashes of memory (Book 2, Ch41 L88-89); the Frank murder (Book 2, Ch59 L87-110).
- **Book 3**: led with Thadwick across the Vane estate by Zato (Book 3, Ch46 L55); the ritual (Book 3, Ch51 L85); Zato notes the marks on his neck (Book 3, Ch41 L73).

## 7. Relationships

- **Zato**: his leader (Book 3, Ch46 L55).
- **Thadwick**: an inconvenient recruit (Book 2, Ch59 L87).
