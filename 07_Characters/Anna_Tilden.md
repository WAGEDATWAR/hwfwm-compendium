## 1. Summary

Anna Tilden is a Network director who first meets Jason on Earth in Book 4, becomes a member of its steering committee, serves as the Asano clan's UN liaison, and by Book 11 is Under-Secretary-General of the United Nations Office of Supernatural Affairs. She is Jason's most trusted political contact on Earth.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in detail in the lines read; she dresses in a sensible suit that makes her look like a government functionary (Book 4, Ch5 L111).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Measured and political**: see the roleplay notes; she is quick to move sides when the facts change.
- **Habits**: see the Background section for the contracts she sends and the meetings she arranges.

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

She is a category one, so her senses are only slightly heightened (Book 4, Ch21 L3); her strength is in organisation and contacts, not in combat.

## 5. Background

- **Roles**: Director of the Network's Sydney operation, a government-faceless "suit" who hunts magic events from a helicopter, addressed as "Director" by Ketevan Arziani (Book 4, Ch5 L111-112); on the Steering Committee of old family Network members in Book 5 (Book 5, Ch9 L52); UN Liaison to the Asano clan in Book 6 (Book 6, Ch47 L16-18); Under-Secretary-General of the Office of Supernatural Affairs by Book 11, an ex-Network official who knew Jason with "trust and scared" (notes, Book 11).
- **Family**: wife Susan; brother Terrance, the Network's media director (Book 4, Ch5 L5-7; Book 5, Ch8 L69).
- **Habits**: Nigel Thornton's team is most often contracted by her (Book 12, Ch3 L7), and Nigel says he came to Rexion on her job ("Anna Tilden. You know her, right?", Book 12, Ch10 L94). In Book 13 she sets up a meeting in Canberra for ambassadors and officials of the nations whose people were displaced (Book 13, Ch953 L9); that she works with Boris Ket Lundi is from notes, Book 13.

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 4**: meets Jason and negotiates with him; her group's agreement template for his family is "the template we intend to use for your entire family" (Book 4, Ch28 L48; Book 4, Ch36 L35; Book 4, Ch59 L35).
- **Book 6**: Anna is asked to be go-between for a meeting of Network, EOA and Cabal members held in Jason's spirit domain, and arrives with Craig Vermillion and Taika Williams (Book 6, Ch23 L15; Book 6, Ch24 L5); the meeting takes place outside the city of Nitra, where a magical dome has appeared (Book 6, Ch1 L4; Book 6, Ch24 L4), and the GDN is the Network's name after going public (Book 6, Ch1 L27); Anna is asked to be go-between for a meeting arranged by the GDN (Book 6, Ch23 L15), and arrives at the GDN camp in a party of eight that includes former EOA members who joined the GDN (Book 6, Ch24 L3, L5 to L8); that she belongs to the GDN delegation is not stated.
- **Book 12**: she is a UN official in New York with an off-the-books observation team in Europe (Book 12, Ch1 L80; Book 12, Ch3 L7; Book 12, Ch22 L73-74) and sends contractors to the Asano clan (Book 12, Ch10 L94); Asano's wish to recruit her is open knowledge among the delegation (Book 12, Ch40 L20), and she is invited to Europe to talk to Jason (Book 12, Ch26 L76); she suspects the System could be some cosmic force rather than Jason (Book 12, Ch42 L99), and Jason answers with a System Administrator window, "I'm being as open as I can, Anna" (Book 12, Ch42 L100 to L101); she then provisionally accepts his offer (Book 12, Ch42 L104 to L106). The fact that his interface was made available to everyone is stated to Jason in Book 12, Ch1 L36.
- **Book 13**: arranges diplomacy; her UN successor is Holly Macrossan (Book 13, Ch982 L30).

## 7. Relationships

Jason, Ketevan, Susan, Terrance, Boris, Taika, the Asano clan.

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

No secret is attributed to her in the lines read. Her later posts (the UN Office of Supernatural Affairs in Book 11, Book 13 diplomacy) are level 3 and are told in the timeline above.

## 9. Roleplay notes

Measured, political, quick to move sides when the facts change.
