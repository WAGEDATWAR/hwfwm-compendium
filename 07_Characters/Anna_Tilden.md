## 1. Summary

Anna Tilden is a Network director who first meets Jason on Earth in Book 4, becomes a member of its steering committee, serves as the Asano clan's UN liaison, and by Book 11 is Under-Secretary-General of the United Nations Office of Supernatural Affairs. She is Jason's most trusted political contact on Earth.

## 2. Personality and background

- **Roles**: Director of the Network's Sydney operation, a government-faceless "suit" who hunts magic events from a helicopter, addressed as "Director" by Ketevan Arziani (Book 4, Ch5 L111-112); on the Steering Committee of old family Network members in Book 5 (Book 5, Ch9 L52); UN Liaison to the Asano clan in Book 6 (Book 6, Ch47 L16-18); Under-Secretary-General of the Office of Supernatural Affairs by Book 11, an ex-Network official who knew Jason with "trust and scared" (notes, Book 11).
- **Family**: wife Susan; brother Terrance, the Network's media director (Book 4, Ch5 L5-7; Book 5, Ch8 L69).
- **Habits**: in Book 12 Nigel Thornton's team works for her and she sends him to Rexion (Book 12, Ch10 L94; the contract-operative detail is from notes, Book 12). In Book 13 she sets up a meeting in Canberra for ambassadors and officials of the nations whose people were displaced (Book 13, Ch953 L9); that she works with Boris Ket Lundi is from notes, Book 13.

## 3. Timeline

- **Book 4**: meets Jason and negotiates with him; her group's agreement template for his family is "the template we intend to use for your entire family" (Book 4, Ch28 L48; Book 4, Ch36 L35; Book 4, Ch59 L35).
- **Book 6**: Anna is asked to be go-between for a meeting of Network, EOA and Cabal members held in Jason's spirit domain, and arrives with Craig Vermillion and Taika Williams (Book 6, Ch23 L15; Book 6, Ch24 L5); the meeting takes place outside the city of Nitra, where a magical dome has appeared (Book 6, Ch1 L4; Book 6, Ch24 L4), and the GDN is the Network's name after going public (Book 6, Ch1 L27); Anna's place in the GDN delegation is from the notes (notes, Book 6).
- **Book 12**: she is a UN official in New York with an off-the-books observation team in Europe (Book 12, Ch1 L80; Book 12, Ch3 L7; Book 12, Ch22 L73-74) and sends contractors to the Asano clan (Book 12, Ch10 L94); Asano's wish to recruit her is open knowledge among the delegation (Book 12, Ch40 L20), and she is invited to Europe to talk to Jason (Book 12, Ch26 L76); that she confirms the System is Jason's interface is not located (notes, Book 12). The fact itself is stated to Jason in Book 12, Ch1 L36.
- **Book 13**: arranges diplomacy; her UN successor is Holly Macrossan (Book 13, Ch982 L30).

## 4. Relationships

Jason, Ketevan, Susan, Terrance, Boris, Taika, the Asano clan.

## 5. Roleplay notes

Measured, political, quick to move sides when the facts change.
