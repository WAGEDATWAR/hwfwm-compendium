## 1. Summary

Nigel Thornton leads a nine-person mercenary unit that began as a Network tactical team. He is the first gold-ranker outside the United States to get there without cores, and in 2038 he works as a gold-ranker for hire, infiltrating vampire strongholds and clearing blood farms alongside the Asano clan.

## 2. Appearance

- In the camp he wears black fatigues under magical tactical armour, carrying no firearms, only a thigh-mounted magic knife (Book 4, Ch39 L65).
- By Book 12 he holds a gold-rank pistol with a magical silencer, a gift from a grateful client (Book 12, Ch4 L23).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Disciplined**: "We can't match up to your gear... But we have training and discipline" (Book 4, Ch41 L96).
- **Direct and proud of results**: "We've taken out four category threes just today" (Book 4, Ch41 L92).
- **Bitter at the blood farms**: "How many farms were left running while the Asanos were hiding in their magic hole?" (Book 12, Ch10 L60).
- **Careful**: he spends more time hiding than moving in the city and freezes when a vampire sniffs the cellar (Book 12, Ch4 L23-28).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- First gold-ranker outside the US without cores; "the US had non-core training programs long before Farrah Hurin arrived" (Book 12, Ch3 L3).
- The rest of his team are silver-rank core users (Book 12, Ch3 L4).

## 5. Background

- His team is the same nine-person unit it was almost two decades earlier, when it belonged to the Network's secret paramilitary wing (Book 12, Ch3 L5).
- When the Network schismed, they left, sickened by infighting and the Australian government, and became mercenaries (Book 12, Ch3 L6).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 4**: Keith brings Nigel to the grandmother's visit (Book 4, Ch36 L74); his team sweeps a camp perimeter (Book 4, Ch40 L61); Jason shows him his party voice chat (Book 4, Ch40 L22) and tells him the Network's methods are reaching their limits (Book 4, Ch41 L89-97).
- **Book 5**: he is Gladys's bodyguard when Jason's rage flares (Book 5, Ch43 L14).
- **Book 12**: hides in a vampire city (Book 12, Ch4 L23-48); moves blood farm victims with the clan (Book 12, Ch10 L59).
- **Book 13**: infiltrates Multan, Pakistan (Book 13, Ch1007 L16); his teams bring vampires back when clearing farms (Book 13, Ch979 L22).

## 7. Relationships

- **Jason**: Jason's tutor on how teams will fail against stronger monsters (Book 4, Ch41 L97).
- **Rufus**: rides back with him from the blood farms (Book 12, Ch10 L59).
- **Gladys**: he is her bodyguard (Book 5, Ch43 L14).
