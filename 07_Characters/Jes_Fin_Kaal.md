## 1. Summary

Jes Fin Kaal is a gold-rank messenger ritualist and Voice of the Will who leads the messengers' dealings with Yaresh. Quiet, exact and unreadable, she lets others speak and listens for what they leave out.

## 2. Appearance

- Smaller than the others, barely over seven feet; Benella cannot magically detect her although she is gold rank (Book 9, Ch55 L27).
- A loose deep-red robe with white trim and pristine white wings (Book 9, Ch55 L28).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Formal**: "I am Voice Kaal, and you may address me as such" (Book 9, Ch56 L14).
- **Probing**: she reads Benella's emotions with her aura (Book 9, Ch56 L22-26).
- **Keeps things back**: Jason says she played things mostly straight but kept things from them (Book 10, Ch59 L38).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Gold rank ritualist; a Voice of the Will (Book 10, Ch9 L49).
- Boris says she believed Destruction wanted a massive explosion once the messengers claimed the soul forge (Book 10, Ch81 L36).

## 5. Background

- "New to the stronghold, referred to by the other messengers as Voice Kaal" (Book 9, Ch55 L19).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 9**: Benella's presentation (Book 9, Ch55-56).
- **Book 10**: the messengers' leadership at Yaresh (Book 10, Ch9 L49; Book 10, Ch59 L38; Book 10, Ch81 L36).

## 7. Relationships
