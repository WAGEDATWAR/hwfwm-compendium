## 1. Summary

A gold-rank messenger ritualist and Voice of the Will who leads the messengers' dealings with Yaresh.

## 2. Facts

- A messenger ritualist new to the stronghold, "referred to by the other messengers as Voice Kaal" (Book 9, Ch55 L19); she is smaller than the others, barely over seven feet, and Benella cannot magically detect her although she is gold rank (Book 9, Ch55 L27).
- She wears a loose deep-red robe with white trim and pristine white wings (Book 9, Ch55 L28) and says "I am Voice Kaal, and you may address me as such" (Book 9, Ch56 L14).
- She probes Benella's emotions with her aura to learn why she brought Jason to the messengers' attention (Book 9, Ch56 L22-26).
- In Book 10 she is the messengers' leadership at Yaresh, a Voice of the Will (Book 10, Ch9 L49); Jason says she has played things mostly straight but kept things from them (Book 10, Ch59 L38).
- Boris says she believed Destruction wanted a massive explosion once the messengers claimed the soul forge (Book 10, Ch81 L36).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Quiet, exact and unreadable; she lets others speak and listens for what they leave out.
