## 1. Summary

Charist is one of Yaresh's two resident diamond-rank elves, the more social of the pair, who dislikes the responsibilities of his power. He is Allayeth's rival and partner in Jason's Yaresh diplomacy and pushes the harder line against Jason.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in the lines read; he flies "superhero-style" while Allayeth sits on a throne of glittering lights (Book 10, Ch22 L81).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Sociable**: "Lord Charist was the more social of the two" (Book 12, Ch16 L8).
- **Courtly**: "Your father is known to me. Convey my regards" (Book 9, Ch98 L22).
- **Reluctant**: he "dislikes many of the responsibilities that come with his level of power" (Book 10, Ch14 L50).
- **Sheepish**: he looks like a child caught doing something stupid when Allayeth calls out his approach (Book 10, Ch23 L7).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Diamond-rank; no silver-ranker can open a portal that diamond-rankers can use (Book 10, Ch12 L41).
- He eavesdrops on Jason and Allayeth's talks (Book 10, Ch13 L65).

## 5. Background

- Resident of Yaresh; he and Allayeth stay in the city because its people love Hana Shavar (Book 9, Ch104 L29).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 9**: finds Schaat's corpse with Allayeth and demands to know "what's going on here" (Book 9, Ch98 L4-6).
- **Book 10**: arrives with Allayeth (Book 10, Ch22 L81); his approach discussed behind a privacy screen (Book 10, Ch23 L7).
- **Book 12**: at the reunion VIP gathering (Book 12, Ch16 L8).

## 7. Relationships

- **Allayeth**: fellow diamond (Book 10, Ch14 L50).
- **Jason**: opposed then wary; Jason tells Allayeth "you know your friend Charist is listening" (Book 10, Ch13 L65).

Nothing hidden in the lines read.
