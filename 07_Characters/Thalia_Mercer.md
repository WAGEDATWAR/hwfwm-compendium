## 1. Summary

The matriarch of the Mercer family.

## 2. Facts

- Introduced by Danielle as "Lady Thalia Mercer" at a social gathering, her bronze aura pressing down on Jason until he suppresses it (Book 1, Ch53 L45-49).
- Tried to convince her husband that Thadwick would benefit from more training (Book 1, Ch53 L54).
- Her Onslaught essence informs her powers and her inclinations (Book 13, Ch999 L3).
