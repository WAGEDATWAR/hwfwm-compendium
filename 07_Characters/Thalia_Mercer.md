## 1. Summary

Thalia Mercer is the matriarch of the Mercer family, a silver-rank explosive adventurer and friend and teammate of Danielle Geller. She wants better training for her son Thadwick, fusses over him after the Builder-cult crisis, and by Book 13 watches her estranged daughter Cassandra from the shadows.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in detail in the lines read.

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Welcoming with an edge**: "You must be the young man people are getting so curious about" (Book 1, Ch53 L47-49).
- **Wry and diplomatic**: "I daresay you're right. So, you're willing to leave Thadwick to my family?" (Book 1, Ch97 L108).
- **Unhappy about her husband's decision**: she tried to convince him Thadwick needed more training (Book 1, Ch53 L54).
- **Motherly under pressure**: she fusses over Thadwick as he is carried through a portal (Book 1, Ch111 L42).
- **Sentimental about her daughter**: she hasn't seen Cassandra "in years" and watches her from behind a tree (Book 13, Ch999 L3).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Silver rank with might, potent, swift and onslaught essences: "in terms of pure explosive power she was a match for any adventurer alive" (Book 2, Ch59 L83).
- She blasted Thadwick's door "to splinters with a special attack" (Book 2, Ch59 L84).
- Her Onslaught essence informs her inclinations as well as her power set (Book 13, Ch999 L3).

## 5. Background

- Friend and teammate of Danielle Geller (Book 2, Ch59 L83).
- Present at the estate when Danielle witnesses Jonah Geller's gruesome end (Book 2, Ch21 L19).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 1**: introduced by Danielle; boards the Geller carriage (Book 1, Ch53 L45-62); settles Thadwick's future (Book 1, Ch97 L108; Book 1, Ch111 L42).
- **Book 2**: with Danielle, Emir, Arella and the archbishop (Book 2, Ch21 L19); the black parlour and the corpse puppets (Book 2, Ch59 L22, L82-84).
- **Book 13**: watches Cassandra (Book 13, Ch999 L3); Thadwick leaves the bunker without a word of goodbye (Book 13, Ch1022 L67).

## 7. Relationships

- **Thadwick**: son (Book 1, Ch111 L42).
- **Cassandra**: daughter she hasn't seen in years (Book 13, Ch999 L3).
- **Danielle Geller**: friend and teammate (Book 2, Ch59 L83).

## 8. Secrets and spoiler notes

Her Book 13 watching of Cassandra from a tree implies the estrangement (Book 13, Ch999 L3).
