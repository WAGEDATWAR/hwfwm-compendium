## 1. Summary

Zithis Carrow Vayel is the messenger recruited as a sixteen-year-old diamond-ranker to become the new Builder, named on the Fundamental Realm Authority Token Jason loots. He is rarely seen; his prime vessel Erigo Fin Desca is the character to use in scenes.

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Boris counts him among the few who can truly shake the cosmos (Book 11, Ch27 L55).

## 5. Background

- Jason tells Allayeth: "His name is Zithis Carrow Vayel. He was a sixteen-year-old diamond-ranker when the great astral beings recruited him to replace the old Builder"; the Builder was a messenger (Book 10, Ch26 L54-55).
- Jamis says Zithis's debt to the astral kings will be paid when the task is complete (Book 10, Ch15 L9).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 8**: the token Jason loots is bound to him and to Jason; Jason wonders who Zithis is (Book 8, Ch50 L89-95).
- **Book 9**: Kaal names him with the World-Phoenix's "now-former prime vessels" (Book 9, Ch56 L97).
- **Book 10**: Jamis says "Zithis Carrow Vayel will have fulfilled his debt to us" (Book 10, Ch15 L9); Allayeth asks "The Builder was a messenger?" after Jason's account (Book 10, Ch26 L54).
- **Book 11**: Boris names him among the few who can truly shake the cosmos (Book 11, Ch27 L55).
