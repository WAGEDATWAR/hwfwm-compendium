## 1. Summary

The messenger recruited as a sixteen-year-old diamond-ranker to become the new Builder, named on the Fundamental Realm Authority Token Jason loots.

## 2. Facts

- The token Jason loots is bound to him and to Jason; Jason wonders who Zithis is and whether the name is Earth-like (Book 8, Ch50 L89-95).
- Kaal names him alongside the World-Phoenix's "now-former prime vessels" in a lineup of those involved (Book 9, Ch56 L97).
- Jamis says Zithis's debt to the astral kings will be paid when the task is complete (Book 10, Ch15 L9).
- Jason tells Allayeth: "His name is Zithis Carrow Vayel. He was a sixteen-year-old diamond-ranker when the great astral beings recruited him to replace the old Builder"; the Builder was a messenger (Book 10, Ch26 L54-55).
- Boris counts him among the few who can truly shake the cosmos (Book 11, Ch27 L55).

## 3. Roleplay notes

He is rarely seen; use his prime vessel Erigo Fin Desca for scenes.
