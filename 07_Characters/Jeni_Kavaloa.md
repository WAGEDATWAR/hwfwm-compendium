## 1. Summary

Jeni Kavaloa is the gold-rank leader of the mixed-force surge expedition in Book 7. She is irritated by the roster, suspects Vesper Rimaros of assembling it, and enlists Jason's astral magic to seal the astral aperture.

## 2. Appearance

Not described in the lines read; she checks a pocket watch (Book 7, Ch47 L33).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Impatient**: unhappy about being "saddled with the mixed group" (Book 7, Ch47 L33).
- **Suspicious of politics**: the roster is like "a mad alchemist throwing volatile ingredients in a pot" (Book 7, Ch47 L60).
- **Practical**: she plans for the worst and does not slow when she senses the clearing's oddity (Book 7, Ch49 L39).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- Gold rank (Book 7, Ch47 L33); she senses Jason's defences pop an opponent's aura like a soap bubble (Book 7, Ch47 L41-49).
- Sets up a device to seal the astral aperture, roping in Jason as astral specialist (Book 7, Ch51 L5).
