## 1. Summary

Shade's brother, an astral being of the Reaper's kind who trapped Shade in an astral space for centuries and, in Book 13, serves Knowledge's plan against Jason.

## 2. Facts

- Shade says Umber told him he had Shade trapped in an astral space for centuries "to teach you a lesson about duty" (Book 8, Ch55 L85-86).
- Shade explains that astral beings are things of spirit with defined natures: Colin an avatar of hunger, Gordon a herald of annihilation, himself "a shepherd of death" (Book 13, Ch981 L37).
- As Umber developed a personality he became fixated on the idea that changing from the original state means falling from grace; Shade and Umber have drifted furthest, and he hates Shade for embracing it (Book 13, Ch981 L62-64).
- Umber persuaded Shade's summoner to trap him (Book 13, Ch1024 L118; Book 11, Ch62 L36).
- At the marshalling yard in Book 13 Shade senses his brother, who appears as a mass of shadow with smoky orange eyes (Book 13, Ch1024 L100-104). He says he built the bridge anchor ritual to bind Jason's consciousness into the prime avatar (Book 13, Ch1024 L118-127); a word from Jason makes him reel (Book 13, Ch1024 L138).

## 3. Roleplay notes

Sensuous, opportunistic and bitter; he plays games when Shade's power is limited by a vessel (Book 8, Ch55 L86).
