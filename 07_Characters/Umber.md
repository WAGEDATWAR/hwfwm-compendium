## 1. Summary

Umber is Shade's brother, an astral being of the Reaper's kind who trapped Shade in an astral space for centuries and, in Book 13, serves Knowledge's plan against Jason. Sensuous, opportunistic and bitter, he plays games when Shade's power is limited by a vessel.

## 2. Appearance

- Appears as a mass of shadow with smoky orange eyes (Book 13, Ch1024 L100-104).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Resentful of change**: fixated on the idea that changing from the original state means falling from grace; he hates Shade for embracing it (Book 13, Ch981 L62-64).
- **Opportunistic and bitter**: he plays games when Shade's power is limited by a vessel (Book 8, Ch55 L86).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- He built the bridge anchor ritual to bind Jason's consciousness into the prime avatar (Book 13, Ch1024 L118-127); a word from Jason makes him reel (Book 13, Ch1024 L138).

## 5. Background

- Shade says Umber told him he had Shade trapped in an astral space for centuries "to teach you a lesson about duty" (Book 8, Ch55 L85-86).
- Umber persuaded Shade's summoner to trap him (Book 13, Ch1024 L118; Book 11, Ch62 L36).
- Astral beings are things of spirit with defined natures: Colin an avatar of hunger, Gordon a herald of annihilation, Shade "a shepherd of death" (Book 13, Ch981 L37).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 8**: Shade recounts his imprisonment (Book 8, Ch55 L85-86).
- **Book 13**: Shade explains the siblings (Book 13, Ch981 L37-64); marshalling yard (Book 13, Ch1024 L100-138).
