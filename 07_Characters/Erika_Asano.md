## 1. Summary

Erika Asano is Jason's older sister, a television presenter living in Melbourne, married to Ian Evans, mother of Emi. Sharp-tongued, loving and protective, she is the family member who reels in Jason's impulsiveness, mourns Kaito, and argues with the clan over her daughter's safety.

## 2. Appearance

- Not described in the lines read, apart from clothes: she and Ian wear old faded ultra-casual clothes "like it was laundry day" when Jason warns they will not want to wear them again (Book 4, Ch69 L70).

## 3. Personality and voice

- **Strict-rules household**: she and Jason "had grown up with their mother's strict rules about not bringing conflict to the dinner table" (Book 4, Ch30 L4).
- **Protective and blunt**: she storms into her daughter's bedroom when Jason, Emi and Taika are playing a rhythm game (Book 4, Ch44 L3).
- **A professional**: "being the experienced media personality, she had attached herself to the tour" of the hospital, wanting to "reel in her brother's impulsive nature" (Book 5, Ch24 L28).
- **Frank about fear**: "I'm her mother and I don't like her going out and risking her life. Is that so hard to understand?" (Book 11, Ch68 L1592).

## 4. Powers and skills as world facts

- By Book 11 she is the food logistics director for the whole clan (Book 11, Ch68 L928).
- She wants Emi to use monster cores like her parents, and Emi's refusal sours their relationship (Book 11, Ch68 L909-913, L1543).

## 5. Background

- She lives in Melbourne with Ian and Emi; her series films in Melbourne and she misses "her big parties in Melbourne" after moving north (Book 4, Ch3 L123-135; Book 4, Ch26 L29).
- She is Jason's elder sister, with Kaito among her brothers (Book 5, Ch56 L7).

## 6. Timeline of involvement

- **Book 4**: Jason finds her family's names on his contact list (Book 4, Ch3 L125), reunites with them (Book 4, Ch26 L71), and she throws a birthday party (Book 4, Ch26 L29).
- **Book 5**: she acts as media guide at the medical centre (Book 5, Ch24 L28) and, with Emi, acts as Jason's ambassador to the family while he is away (Book 5, Ch56 L47).
- **Book 11**: she meets Rufus ("I recognise you from my brother's recordings", Book 11, Ch68 L588), walks with him and Ian to the administration centre arguing over Emi's training (Book 11, Ch68 L1587-1612).
- **Book 13**: she nods Jason out onto the deck for a private talk (Book 13, Ch951 L66-71), and she and Ian tease about marriage (Book 13, Ch1022 L17).

## 7. Relationships

- **Ian Evans**: her husband, a doctor (Book 4, Ch3 L123).
- **Emi**: her daughter, whom she tries to keep from combat (Book 11, Ch68 L1592; Book 13, Ch951 L66).
- **Jason**: "Erika clinging to him like she was afraid he'd disappear again" (Book 4, Ch26 L71); she shares his look when he wants a topic changed (Book 4, Ch34 L5).
- **Kaito**: she mourns him with Emi (Book 5, Ch56 L7).
