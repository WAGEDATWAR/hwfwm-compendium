## 1. How to use the timeline files

`Master_Timeline.md` holds the calendar, the month-count conventions, the corrections to wiki and chapter-note timing and the whole-series outline. Each `Book_NN_Timeline.md` lists the events of one book in story order with a cite for every row. Use the master file to choose a period and the book file to place a scene inside it. Era guides and war files are planned in `TODO_Build_Plan.md` (Tier 3).

Spoiler levels follow the books: levels 1 for Books 1-3, 2 for Books 4-9 and 3 for Books 10-13 (see `00_Project_Structure.md`, section 6). Timing conflicts and open points are recorded in `Appendices/Open_Questions.md` and in each book file's closing notes.
