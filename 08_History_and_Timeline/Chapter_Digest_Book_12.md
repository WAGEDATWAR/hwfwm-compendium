## 1. How to use this digest

One entry per chapter, in book order: the chapter number and title, then up to two sentences from the timeline with their citations. Use it to find which chapter covers an event, then open the chapter file or the timeline for detail. It quotes no passages. Coverage in this book: 68 of 68 chapters have a summary sentence; the rest are titles only.

## 2. Chapters

- **Ch0, Don’t Tell Humphrey**: A letter opens the book: friends write that everyone is "getting super-strong" and waiting for Jason to return before gathering again (Book 12, Ch0 L19)
- **Ch1, Why He Hasn’t**: Shade gives a span of "approximately seven hours" if they leave soon (Book 12, Ch1 L10). On Pallimustus, Miles Cotezee of the Adventure Society hurries through the Vitesse campus of the Magic Research Association (Book 12, Ch1 L53)
- **Ch2, If We Can’t Change For the Better**: Jason is told he has been kept from friends and family long enough (Book 12, Ch2 L7).
- **Ch3, We’re All Stuck in a Hole**: Nigel Thornton was not the first on Earth to reach gold, or even the first to do it without cores (Book 12, Ch3 L3) Nigel's nine-person team, working mostly for Anna Tilden, is embedded in France watching the edge of Saint-Étienne for something big (Book 12, Ch3 L7, L13, L82)
- **Ch4, A Silent City**: The sky over the old clan territory turns orange and blue under a huge eye, and Nigel Thornton goes in alone while his team falls back (Book 12, Ch4 L3, L14 to L17). Jason drains the vampires' blood, and the blood rain turned to light leaves Nigel alone in a silent city (Book 12, Ch4 L40, L49 to L53)
- **Ch5, The Fundamental Things**: Finish whatever business you're on and make your way to Yaresh" (Book 12, Ch5 L89)
- **Ch6, You Don’t Want Glory**: Fiorella enjoys portal duty in a quiet room (Book 12, Ch6 L23)
- **Ch7, The Power Looming Over Us All**: Outer Rexion has been massively expanded and holds the statue of Gary (Book 12, Ch7 L8)
- **Ch8, The Topic of Pants**: The creation of the prime avatar is "a surprisingly unspectacular affair"; the reality material taken back from Death is added to the swarm of lights (Book 12, Ch8 L19)
- **Ch9, Butchery**: Saving Gibson has more in common with Earth surgery than with Pallimustus ritual healing (Book 12, Ch9 L16)
- **Ch10, Just Some Guy**: The three remaining gifts relate to different kinds of transcendent entity, System Administrator among them (Book 12, Ch10 L14). On Earth, the Asano clan's coin is spirit coins or clan contribution points (Book 12, Ch10 L74)
- **Ch11, On a Whim**: Marla, the brightheart military leader, with hair and eyes like molten steel, is in the middle of the room (Book 12, Ch11 L6)
- **Ch12, Mandatory Team Activity**: Zara Nareen, in her Outer Rexion suite, finds someone has been in her room (Book 12, Ch12 L3). She and Jason have not met in fifteen years (Book 12, Ch12 L20)
- **Ch13, Try Not to Bring Down Civilisation**: Dominion's gift: [Projection Command: Presence], a transcendent legendary item (Book 12, Ch13 L73). Jason tells those who waited on him "almost twenty years" (Book 12, Ch13 L36)
- **Ch14, We Have Forever**: Miguel Ladiv, nephew of a deputy director, had once expected a cushy Adventure Society job (Book 12, Ch14 L11)
- **Ch15, Like an Adventurer**: Rick Geller and his team arrive as a brothel fire sends women into the street and into Jason (Book 12, Ch15 L3). Humphrey says the team's elaborate plan "fifteen years ago" only got them from Rimaros to Yaresh (Book 12, Ch15 L64).
- **Ch16, People Think You’re Blowing Up Cities**: Team Biscuit is "only the beginning" of the adventurers coming to Yaresh, with Team Blood and Gold among them (Book 12, Ch16 L11).
- **Ch17, We Need to Put a Stop to It**: The Yaresh mirage chamber is larger and more involved than the one in Greenstone (Book 12, Ch17 L19)
- **Ch18, A Lunatic’s Nightmare**: In the mirage chamber, Jason fills the arena with darkness and shadow-armed figures while Prince Valdis, a gold-rank sword master, fights through them (Book 12, Ch18 L6 to L12). Jason's attacks inflict Sin, which Valdis's Sword Soul absorbs (Book 12, Ch18 L16, L37, L45).
- **Ch19, When People See My Powers**: Jason's body reforms from strands of blood, and his cloak becomes a portal that his attacks vanish into (Book 12, Ch19 L6 to L7). Emir and Constance watch from the lobby (Book 12, Ch19 L9).
- **Ch20, Listening For Whispers**: Skybranch elf craftsmen spent years helping reconstruct the city (Book 12, Ch20 L42). A System window says Jason has "half-ascended to the status of Astral Nexus" (Book 12, Ch20 L85)
- **Ch21, Defier**: Jason says he "only missed fifteen years or so" (Book 12, Ch21 L61)
- **Ch22, Vast Cosmic Power Types**: Jason asks everyone to come on this trip to expand their horizons (Book 12, Ch22 L56)
- **Ch23, Objectives**: Travis says he has done weapons for the cloud flask before, "years ago" (Book 12, Ch23 L25)
- **Ch24, The Protection of a Dictator**: General Millicent Marks, an elven spellcaster, leads the Segurado army (Book 12, Ch24 L13)
- **Ch25, You Want Your Adventurers Happy**: After the battle, General Millicent Marks on the Segurado magic tower meets Danielle Geller, who asks to be called Danielle (Book 12, Ch25 L3, L5 to L12). The Team Biscuit cook, John Miller, feeds her at a stall (Book 12, Ch25 L63, L66)
- **Ch26, Making Things Worse**: Pallimustus's Antarctica is the Dragon Lands, native land of the scaled draconians (Book 12, Ch26 L5). A messenger war of fifteen years has made the xenophobic people warier (Book 12, Ch26 L16).
- **Ch27, Sometimes We Need Scars**: Jason reaches Greenstone quietly days before the official arrival (Book 12, Ch27 L12). In a low-magic zone, a spirit coin restores recovery rates for eight hours (Book 12, Ch27 L19).
- **Ch28, That’s What Adventurers Do**: Vincent Trenslow and the Adventure Society branch prepare for the "literal shipload of gold-rankers" (Book 12, Ch28 L3). Someone who has not seen Jason in twenty years says he must have spent them doing things adventurers do not do (Book 12, Ch28 L41).
- **Ch29, A Matter of Mindset**: Boko is the origin of the anti-intellectual movement (Book 12, Ch29 L65). A man recalls finding a young man twenty years ago staring at a picture in the library of his old home (Book 12, Ch29 L40)
- **Ch30, Posturing Children**: Li Mei, after more than fifteen years, recognises the man before her (Book 12, Ch30 L80)
- **Ch31, This Is Not a Cliff**: Li Mei stands frozen as Jason, with Estella and Belinda nearby, comes to meet her (Book 12, Ch31 L3, L7, L11 to L12). Jason tells her they are there to decide what happens to her, and asks about an operation the local authorities fear is raising an undead army (Book 12, Ch31 L62, L97)
- **Ch32, Cook the Crap Out of Some Toast**: Li Mei has feared meeting Jason again for fifteen years (Book 12, Ch32 L8)
- **Ch33, The Benevolence of a Nightmare God**: Massive portals open over the ruins and disgorge an army (Book 12, Ch33 L4). The ghost fire phoenix arcs through the messenger host (Book 12, Ch33 L16).
- **Ch34, Pretext**: The assassin's blade is [Lesser Celestial Gorger (broken)], gold rank (Book 12, Ch34 L35). Jason says he lost fifteen years and will not let others dictate his actions (Book 12, Ch34 L57)
- **Ch35, Eternity Awaits Us**: Jamis Fran Muskar of the Council of Kings meets Jason to apologise for the deaths in Boko and says the two will know each other for longer than time can measure (Book 12, Ch35 L6, L8, L11). Jason refuses a proposal to blunt his fury by giving up areas, and refuses to slaughter a quarter of a million messengers to restore his ghost phoenix form (Book 12, Ch35 L66, L84, L88)
- **Ch36, A Time to Slumber**: Only Jason's astral kingdom has the space and resources to shelter a city's worth of people at once (Book 12, Ch36 L3)
- **Ch37, I Know You Can't Control Yourself Any Longer**: Jason says his cloud palace is moored offshore from Greenstone (Book 12, Ch37 L36). Krensler gives him an Adventure Society badge, gold with a diamond symbol (Book 12, Ch37 L128)
- **Ch38, Truly Outrageous Things**: Jason agrees to take Arash's nephew Jamar (Book 12, Ch38 L61)
- **Ch39, A Framework For Leadership**: Jason names the oasis city in his astral kingdom New Water (Book 12, Ch39 L61)
- **Ch40, It’s More Complicated Than That**: Anna Tilden's plane is attacked by silhouettes in the flashes of an assault (Book 12, Ch40 L5). The man opposite her, Morris Manning, is slight and lean (Book 12, Ch40 L6)
- **Ch41, A Long Few Days**: Ketevan drives Anna and Susan through the French domain, where the vampires' occupation has been cleaned up and the astral space is still partly inhabited (Book 12, Ch41 L3, L7 to L9). Nigel tells of the blood rain and of Jason's avatar finding him afterwards, and of talking about the team joining the clan (Book 12, Ch41 L42, L45).
- **Ch42, A Thing That Makes the Car Work**: In a park, Anna sees a grown Emi and the leonid Gary (Book 12, Ch42 L4 to L5, L11 to L13). After three days of briefings, Anna and Susan discuss whether Jason is straight with them (Book 12, Ch42 L21, L65, L70).
- **Ch43, A Long Time Coming**: Jason avoids the societies but joins the Burning Violet guild among his appointments (Book 12, Ch43 L62). Its guild house stands across from the Remore Academy (Book 12, Ch43 L64)
- **Ch44, A Big Juicy Hole**: The guild's intake clerk Monica interviews Jason for Burning Violet membership, noting the application goes to an executive for an in-depth interview (Book 12, Ch44 L4, L20). She mentions that the Gellers' Casselton Beach facility has produced exceptional adventurers (Book 12, Ch44 L62)
- **Ch45, A Sense of Responsibility**: Lord Neiman Bassingthwaite apologises for the reception and warns of two factions in the guild executive, saying Jason's membership could be the flashpoint (Book 12, Ch45 L3 to L4, L8, L12, L14). Arabelle and Gabriel Remore join the discussion (Book 12, Ch45 L55, L62)
- **Ch46, Trouble Always Knows Where to Find Me**: Cassandra Mercer leaves the jobs hall with two teammates, having not seen Jason in twenty years (Book 12, Ch46 L3, L15). Jason's cloud ship moves further into Estercost (Book 12, Ch46 L105)
- **Ch47, Tactical Playbook**: Team Biscuit resupplies at the base of the mountains and meets another team (Book 12, Ch47 L4)
- **Ch48, I Don’t Blow Up Cities on Purpose**: Cyrion is the capital of Estercost, ringed by concentric walls (Book 12, Ch48 L3). The Builder's world engineer golems, almost two decades after their demise, look like hills (Book 12, Ch48 L5)
- **Ch49, Following Through on Bad Ideas**: Hils Jaramaris, Storm Kingdom ambassador to Estercost, is unhappy (Book 12, Ch49 L3)
- **Ch50, Heavily Compromised Baked Goods**: Jason's letter begins that making the world better is a good way to make it worse, but to have the power and do nothing is an abdication (Book 12, Ch50 L15). The System Administrator will "shortly withdraw System access" in regions operating with slavery, indentured servitude or similar (Book 12, Ch50 L28).
- **Ch51, The Line Between Mortal and Immortal**: Vandrick arrives at Jason's cloud palace to find a mob out front, there since the System went dark (Book 12, Ch51 L3)
- **Ch52, A Petty Tyranny**: Ben Headingway leads nine adventurers from the noble houses of Cyrion with missing people to Jason's cloud palace, refusing to enter where Jason has all the power (Book 12, Ch52 L3, L6, L8). Jason says he does not have their people and points them to Sadi Andali (Book 12, Ch52 L35).
- **Ch53, Reason Eighty-Seven**: The true power of House Headingway is its adventurers (Book 12, Ch53 L5). Travis Noble is hard to reach by sky link (Book 12, Ch53 L32).
- **Ch54, Having Strangers Come Along**: Daryll, a troll with fewer abilities than an essence-user, has a few tricks (Book 12, Ch54 L29). Koa sees the sky ship dissolve into a mass (Book 12, Ch54 L16)
- **Ch55, Take Things Slow**: The final pickup is on a flatland (Book 12, Ch55 L21). A speaker tells Jason that what inspired them more than he did was the last fifteen years (Book 12, Ch55 L90)
- **Ch56, The End of What Can Be Productively Achieved Here**: Jason catches up with old friends from Rimaros (Book 12, Ch56 L117)
- **Ch57, Someone Got Sucked Off**: Jota Withers, a pirate captain raised in the feeder programme of a peripheral universe, reflects on his life and his ship's transition into the Jakaar fleet's holdout (Book 12, Ch57 L3, L7, L22). His pirate admiral Aractus hands him a file on a world sealed to anyone over silver rank, now opened to gold-rankers by the World-Phoenix (Book 12, Ch57 L36 to L38).
- **Ch58, A City With No Dawn**: Jason dines with Travis Noble and his wife Gabrielle, who is adjusting to the absence of her goddess (Book 12, Ch58 L41)
- **Ch59, A Clark Kent Problem**: Two American officials, Darius Shepherd (Department of Supernatural Affairs) and Michael Glasser (State Department), portal to the city of Nitra in Asano clan territory, where the clan met them (Book 12, Ch59 L5, L39). Boris Ketland takes them on to Jason's hidden-business city of vampires (Book 12, Ch59 L17, L24)
- **Ch60, God-King**: A cult of the World-Phoenix paid him a visit, and the World-Phoenix owed him a favour for his stopping the Cosmic Throne from turning her back into the Boundary (Book 12, Ch60 L6, L10). The favour was to open the universe to gold-rankers while the ban on diamond-rankers stays until Jason reaches diamond, which also supplies an outside enemy (Book 12, Ch60 L19 to L20)
- **Ch61, Jason’s Arrival Inevitably Caused a Massive Problem**: On the cloud ship, Danielle Geller tells Jason that mixing friends and allies needs an org chart (Book 12, Ch61 L3 to L13). Jason and Zara talk about getting politics right on the ship's edge (Book 12, Ch61 L55 to L78)
- **Ch62, It Sets a Tone**: Anna, Rufus, Taika and Gary travel in a flying saucer carried by Taika in bird form (Book 12, Ch62 L4, L51). Lenora meets them at the doors, past the site's gold-rank security under Commander Higgins and Lord Willoughby, and waives the screening (Book 12, Ch62 L98, L101, L117)
- **Ch63, We Already Know Who You Are**: In Lenora's office, Anna and Rufus warn that her city will become a gold-rank battle zone; Jason will try to draw the fight into the desert (Book 12, Ch63 L3 to L5). Rufus gives Jason's arrival as "roughly three days" away (Book 12, Ch63 L15)
- **Ch64, Good Luck in Your Battle**: The artefact city has been mostly evacuated of its nine thousand or so workers, and a security force stands about half a kilometre back from the site, with Rufus and Taika free to roam (Book 12, Ch64 L21, L48). Jota says Admiral Jakaar likes results (Book 12, Ch64 L96)
- **Ch65, Second Chance to Make a First Impression**: A harpoon with the Inescapable condition catches him; Belinda's tether then binds the pirates and Jason leaves an empty cloak (Book 12, Ch65 L53, L55)
- **Ch66, The Right Move**: Team Biscuit and Farrah arrive in stage two of Jason's plan, using Sophie's Eternal Moment, Belinda's Force Tether and the attacks of Clive and Farrah (Book 12, Ch66 L5 to L11). Neil heals the hole in Jason's torso (Book 12, Ch66 L13 to L17)
- **Ch67, A Lot of Things to Do**: The pirates are rounded up and Jason says they will go to his prison planet, which holds messenger armies that surrendered to him (Book 12, Ch67 L5, L19, L22) Higgins confronts Jason's companions; Jason steps through Shade into the bunker and meets Lenora and Anna (Book 12, Ch67 L30, L51, L55 to L58).
