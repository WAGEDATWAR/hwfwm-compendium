Book 12 opens after Jason sits on the throne at the end of Book 11. It follows him, as a gold-rank prime avatar, through the Pallimustus he left, then to Earth.

## 1. Time anchors used in this book

The book keeps two counts that must not be confused.

- **Since Jason left (about fifteen years)**: Jason says "I only missed fifteen years or so" (Book 12, Ch21 L61); someone says he left the world "fifteen years ago now" (Book 12, Ch28 L76); he "lost fifteen years" (Book 12, Ch34 L57); Zara and Jason had not met "in the fifteen years since" (Book 12, Ch12 L20); Humphrey says the team tried a plan "fifteen years ago" that only reached Yaresh from Rimaros (Book 12, Ch15 L64); Li Mei was afraid of meeting him for "fifteen years" (Book 12, Ch32 L8).
- **Since the arrival (about twenty years)**: Jason freed the stasis-trapped churchmen "around twenty years ago" (Book 12, Ch7 L78) and says "almost twenty years" at Ch13 (Book 12, Ch13 L36). Cassandra has not seen Jason "in twenty years" (Book 12, Ch46 L15), and Elspeth recalls lifting an iron-ranker by the throat "twenty years ago" (Book 12, Ch27 L45).
- **Since the Earthlings vanished (about seventeen years)**: Lenora speaks of the people who vanished "seventeen years ago" (Book 12, Ch67 L65), and Travis asks whether Earth has done weapons research "in the last seventeen years" (Book 12, Ch67 L42).
- **Jason's age**: Jota says Jason was born on Earth "forty-four years ago" (Book 12, Ch67 L113). With the birthday on 1 April 1994 (see Master_Timeline, section 1) that puts the Earth arrival after 1 April 2038.
- **On Earth**: "Less than fifteen years ago, no one knew magic existed" (Book 12, Ch2 L107); France "hasn't existed for almost two decades" (Book 12, Ch26 L68); Nigel's team is the same nine-person unit as "almost two decades earlier" (Book 12, Ch3 L5); Jason says "Twenty years ago, it was still a secret" (Book 12, Ch22 L27).
- **Nik** was "five, maybe" when Jason left (Book 12, Ch15 L47).
- **The present**: the Pallimustus chapters (about fifteen years since Jason left, almost twenty since the arrival) fit late 2037 to early 2038. The Earth arrival (Ch64 to Ch67) is best placed in 2038, because Lenora counts seventeen years from the vanishing (Book 12, Ch67 L65) and Jota gives Jason's age as forty-four (Book 12, Ch67 L113). Book 13 is dated 2038 (Book 13, Ch951 L50). The time between the Pallimustus tour and the Earth arrival is not stated. See Open_Questions 18.
- **The tail of the book** takes in the arrival at Earth; Rufus gives the arrival as "roughly three days" away at the briefing in Ch63 (Book 12, Ch63 L15). Book 13 follows, also in 2038 (see `Book_13_Timeline.md`).

## 8. Notes on the interval

- The text gives two stable spans: about fifteen years for Jason's absence and about twenty for the time since the arrival. The older wiki "M-counts" for this period are not used.
- Gaps between scenes inside the book are not stated. Rows are in chapter order, and the "When" column says only what the text supports.
- Chapter numbers in the old chapter notes for Book 12 are off for the later chapters. For example, the notes place the Earthling pickups at Ch53 to Ch56 and the Sky Link island at Ch56, while the lines above show the Earthling pickup at Ch53 to Ch55 and the island dinner at Ch58. Only the cited lines are used.
