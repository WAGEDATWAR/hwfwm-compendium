Chapter-by-chapter order of Book 7, with the in-world time wherever the text gives it. Book 7 opens as Jason and Farrah leave Earth, the same day the surge begins to be felt on Pallimustus.

## 1. Time anchors used in this book

- **Surge**: monster surges come "every ten years or so", and the current one is "some five years overdue" (Book 7, Ch1 L56).
- **Forging**: Gary and Virid are sealed in the smithy "for nineteen days" (Book 7, Ch2 L76).
- **The crossing**: Rufus tells the team the rising magical saturation will trigger manifestations "in earnest" within a few days (Book 7, Ch14 L9), and Soramir says he sensed Jason's arrival "several days ago" (Book 7, Ch18 L48).
- **On the road**: Jason's team returns "after two weeks on the road" (Book 7, Ch57 L3).
- **Intervals**: Estella says Havi Estos hired her "a few weeks ago" (Book 7, Ch62 L43), and the aftermath begins "a month" after the War of Four Cities, also called the One Day War (Book 7, Ch66 L27).

## 9. Notes on the interval

- The "When" labels are placements by chapter order, anchored only where the text states an interval: nineteen days of forging (Book 7, Ch2 L76), several days after arrival (Book 7, Ch18 L48), two weeks on the road (Book 7, Ch57 L3), "a few weeks ago" (Book 7, Ch62 L43) and a month after the war (Book 7, Ch66 L27). The book gives no calendar dates.
- Counting from Jason's departure in autumn 2021 (Book 6, Ch47 L155), Book 7 ends about a month after the One Day War, so the surge is still running when Book 7 closes. The old Master Timeline placed the arrival at "M50" and the end of the surge at "M55-M56"; neither figure is supported by the text.
