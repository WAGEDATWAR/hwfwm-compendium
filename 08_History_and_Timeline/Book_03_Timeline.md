Chapter-by-chapter order of Book 3. The book's epilogue sections are part of Ch69 in the chapter files, so their lines are cited as Ch69 with high line numbers.

## 1. Time anchors used in this book

- **Season**: Book 3 opens in a pleasant Greenstone winter (Book 3, Ch1 L14), and "the mild desert winter moved almost imperceptibly into spring" before the veldt contract (Book 3, Ch27 L62). Greenstone is in the southern hemisphere, so winter is June to August.
- **Six months since Jason arrived**: Neil says "Three days, six months ago" about Jason's first stay at the quarry village (Book 3, Ch12 L95), and Jason reflects that "In the last six months he had undergone enough suffering" (Book 3, Ch19 L3).
- **After the kidnapping**: he does not set foot on the Adventure Society campus for "almost two months" (Book 3, Ch24 L48).
- **In the astral space**: Humphrey says "We've been tramping over this place for the last five months" (Book 3, Ch55 L95), and Jason recalls his capture at the Vane Estate as "a few months more than a year ago" (Book 3, Ch62 L96).
- **After his death**: the farewell gathering in the cloud palace is "more than a month" after the memorial (Book 3, Ch69 L894).
- **Return to Earth**: Book 4 puts his return in "early June, a year and a half later" than his departure (Book 4, Ch3 L112).

## 7. Notes on the interval

- The book gives its own anchors for a long Book 3: a winter start, a spring contract, a five-month stay in the astral space, and a death "a few months more than a year" after arrival. Together they put the astral space entry in spring (about September to October 2018) and the death in the following autumn or late summer (about February to April 2019, M15 to M17).
- The old Master Timeline placed Jason's death at "M18" (the earlier wiki-based count). The text instead fixes his return to Earth at M18 (early June 2019, Book 4, Ch3 L112), and the death some weeks to months earlier.
- The memorial and farewell gathering follow Jason's death by at least a month (Book 3, Ch69 L894), which fits a death in the M15 to M16 range and the Earth awakening at M18.
