Book 11 has two halves. Part I (Ch1 to Ch67) is the unified transformation zone around the Cardinas natural array. Part II (the file for Ch68 holds all of it) is the long epilogue that follows Jason into the void and the people left on Earth for years.

## 1. Time anchors used in this book

- **Zone age**: the territory "had existed for less than two weeks" at Ch18 (Book 11, Ch18 L4). Nik is "six months old" at Ch50 (Book 11, Ch50 L15), so the zone itself runs for months.
- **Gary**: after the fight he is told he has "another four to eight months" of divine power in his body (Book 11, Ch34 L33). Jason offers that if he stayed "the next few months, maybe as much as a year" in the soul realm he could keep the power (Book 11, Ch48 L22). In Part II, "almost a year later" the divine power is wrapped around a haggard figure (Book 11, Ch68 L703).
- **Recovery**: Jason is told he has been recuperating "almost two weeks" (Book 11, Ch41 L59).
- **Linking the nodes**: "a few weeks" (Book 11, Ch46 L20), and the adventurers are still taking part in linking the nodes at Ch49 (Book 11, Ch49 L69).
- **Part II**: Jason's thanks to Gary say "six years since you first gave it to me" (Book 11, Ch68 L115). Emi is "fifteen years old" when Rufus and Taika arrive (Book 11, Ch68 L782). Lenora has spent "a year" in the monitoring-station room (Book 11, Ch68 L412). The Clive-led group works for "two years" before a break (Book 11, Ch68 L1034), Belinda says "It's been two years" (Book 11, Ch68 L1341), Emi is later nineteen (Book 11, Ch68 L918), and the World-Phoenix and the Reaper have been walking an empty road for "two years" (Book 11, Ch68 L2244).
- **Open tension**: see section 7.

## 8. Notes on the interval

- The zone is a matter of months: the "less than two weeks" mark at Ch18 (Book 11, Ch18 L4) and Nik "six months old" at Ch50 (Book 11, Ch50 L15) bracket it, and Gary's divine power was given "four to eight months" at Ch34 (Book 11, Ch34 L33). Book 10 puts its end in mid 2023; Part I of Book 11 then runs for some months after that.
- Part II covers years. The stated intervals are chained inside the file: a year in the room for Lenora (L412), almost a year for Gary (L703), two years of team work (L1034), two years more on Earth (L1341), and two years on the empty road (L2244). They are not on one clock, so no total is asserted here.
- **Open tension (Emi's age against the sword line)**: Emi is fifteen when Rufus arrives (L782) and was thirteen in spring 2020, so fifteen fits late 2022 to early 2023. Yet "six years since you first gave it to me" (L115) and Jason's age of 29 (Book 10, Ch33 L76) place the departure in late 2023 or 2024. Both statements are in the text; this file does not resolve them (see `Appendices/Open_Questions.md`).
- Book 12 opens roughly twenty years after the arrival, in the world's own count (see `Book_12_Timeline.md`).
