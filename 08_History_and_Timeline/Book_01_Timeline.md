Chapter-by-chapter order of Book 1, with the in-world time wherever the text gives it. "M0" is the day Jason arrives (late November 2017 by the calendar anchors in `Master_Timeline.md`). The order of rows follows the chapter order, which is the story order; time labels are given only where a line supports them, and a label with "about" is read from a stated interval.

## 1. Time anchors used in this book

- **One week after arrival**: when Jason leaves the delta, Farrah says "This time last week he didn't know magic existed" (Book 1, Ch33 L74), and at the end of the field assessment he feels like "a different man than the one who climbed on a week earlier" (Book 1, Ch60 L34).
- **About one month**: Jason says "A month ago I still thought auras were made up" (Book 1, Ch46 L33).
- **Less than two months**: "Less than two months ago, I walked into Greenstone with no name and no background" (Book 1, Ch58 L85), and in his recording "It's been about two months since I arrived here" (Book 1, Ch62 L5).
- **Season**: the days are "growing shorter as summer moved into autumn" at Leust (Book 1, Ch93 L5).
- **Local calendar**: twelve months of thirty days, with early, mid and late divisions (Book 1, Ch22 L19). Field assessments happen at the start of every month (Book 1, Ch38 L41).
- **Surge cycle**: a monster surge comes every ten years, and the last was eleven years earlier (Book 1, Ch31 L71-73); it has been as little as eight or as many as thirteen years (Book 1, Ch31 L74).

## 11. Notes on the interval

- The text gives an explicit "about two months" at Ch62 L5, so the field assessment ends in late January 2018 by the calendar anchors. "Summer moved into autumn" at Ch93 L5 puts the Leust contract in the southern autumn, about March 2018, so Book 1 runs for about four months, M0 to M4.
- Between Ch62 and Ch93 the book gives only short intervals: eighteen days of contracts (Book 1, Ch65 L50), a week in the arena (Book 1, Ch66 L7), "more than a week" on the thefts (Book 1, Ch100 L79) and three weeks since the memorial service (Book 1, Ch101 L4). The "M2 to M3" labels in the tables are placements from those intervals, not stated dates.
