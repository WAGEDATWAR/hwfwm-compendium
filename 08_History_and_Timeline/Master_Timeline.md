## 1. How to read this timeline

- **Month counts** are months since Jason's arrival on Pallimustus ("M0"). Where the book text gives a cue it overrides the old wiki figures (see section 4); any count without a chapter-line citation is approximate. Discrepancies are listed in section 7.
- **Citation format**: every fact taken from the books carries "(Book N, ChX LY)" against `Source_Text/`. This replaces the old "[book]" marker; the register rows are in `Appendices/Source_Register.md` and the anchors in `Appendices/Source_Map.md` section 6.
- **Earth and Pallimustus run at the same rate.** The two universes are linked and their time-space is synchronised (Book 11, Ch63 L80).
- **Calendar anchors.** Taken together, the following give a calendar for the whole series:
  - Jason left Earth in **late November** and returned in **early June, a year and a half later** (Book 4, Ch3 L112).
  - His birthday is **1 April** ("April Fool's Day"); he has just turned **26** in Book 5 (Book 5, Ch20 L24-26).
  - He was **23** at arrival (Book 1, Ch25 L43; Book 13, Ch950 L29), **29** in Book 10 (Book 10, Ch33 L76), and was **born forty-four years before** the Book 12 Earth scenes (Book 12, Ch67 L113).
  - Book 13 is set in **2038** (Book 13, Ch951 L50), and mentions transformation-zone events "like those in 2020" (Book 13, Ch964 L41).
  - These fit one calendar: born 1 April 1994; arrival late November 2017 (age 23); return June 2019 (age 25); the age-26 birthday in April 2020, which is the year of the first transformation zones. The year numbers are inferred from the book statements, not stated in Book 1.
  - Further consequences: the departure from Earth (end of Book 6) falls in the autumn of 2021, the Earthlings vanish from Earth then, and Book 13 in 2038 is about sixteen and a half years later, which the characters round to "sixteen" or "seventeen". Jason's twenty-ninth birthday (1 April 2023, about M65) means Book 10, in which he says he is twenty-nine, begins at about M65 or later.
  - A Book 12 speaker says the priests Jason freed were released "around twenty years ago now" (Book 12, Ch7 L78) and another says "it's been almost twenty years" (Book 12, Ch13 L36), which fits Book 2 at about M5 (April 2018) and the Pallimustus part of Book 12 in late 2037 or early 2038.
- **Jason's age** summary: 23 at arrival; 25 on returning to Earth (derived); turns 26 on 1 April in Book 5; 29 in Book 10; 44 in Books 12-13.
- **Ranks**: iron within the first days (notes), bronze around M15 (wiki; Book 5 says he had been bronze "for a year" when he had been back nine months, Book 5, Ch3 L170, and that iron to bronze took "about a year and a quarter", Book 5, Ch3 L212), silver in Book 5 (about M28-29), gold by Book 12 ("how you got to gold rank", notes, Book 12).
- Events are grouped by book. Items marked **(Level N)** are spoiler-sensitive.

## 2. Before the series (background)

1. **Distant past**: the original Builder crafts the twin universes of Earth and Pallimustus; Pallimustus is magic-rich, Earth magic-poor. The great astral beings sanction him and a mortal messenger (Zithis Carrow Vayel, a sixteen-year-old diamond-ranker when recruited, Book 10, Ch26 L55) takes his place. **(Level 3)** The wiki's "billions of years" is unchecked.
2. **Long ago**: the great astral beings sunder the Cosmic Throne to escape regulation, replacing it with pacts. **(Level 3)**
3. **Roughly 500 years ago**: "some five centuries ago, a new kind of magic appeared" on Earth (Book 4, Ch11 L4); it is collected into secret societies, and the Network's founder, an Earth-born outworlder, sets in motion schemes that continue into the present; his familiar, Mr North, a rune spider, is betrayed with Boris's help to the early societies that become the Network (Book 13, Ch1017 L14-26). In Pallimustus, Purity is likely about to sanction himself; Knowledge interferes, Disguise takes Purity's place, and the Order of the Reaper is destroyed by the churches (Book 13, Ch1017 L16-24). **(Level 3)** The notes for Book 13 add that the founder returned to Earth with magic bargained from messengers, built the secret societies and the Grid as a pressure-release valve, and was betrayed by his familiar to US secret societies at about the time of the American Revolution (notes, Book 13; the first part is confirmed at Book 13, Ch1017 L19-28).
4. **Five years before Book 7**: the monster surge, normally due about every ten years (Book 1, Ch31 L71), is "some five years overdue" when Book 7 begins (Book 7, Ch1 L56). (An earlier version of this document said fifteen years; that figure applies instead to the Church of Knowledge building up fighting forces "over the last fifteen years or so", Book 7, Ch56 L62.) **(Level 2)**
5. **Earlier on Earth**: in 1948 local Network operatives discovered the anomalous incursion space near Saint-Étienne, and that sector of the grid has been offline for about seventy years (Book 4, Ch51 L30, L37). The Engineers of Ascension take Perth about two years before Book 4's scene, Melbourne a year later, and are pushing into Sydney (Book 4, Ch9 L76; Book 4, Ch5 L113). Network partnerships with governments and militaries have been in place "over the last few years" before the monster waves of Book 5 (Book 5, Ch13 L37). The claim that the Network reveals itself to governments "about three years before Book 4" is unchecked. **(Level 2)**

## 5. The Pallimustus calendar

## 7. Known conflicts and open points

- **Resolved - Earthlings' arrival**: "fifteen years ago" (Book 12 characters on Pallimustus) and "seventeen years ago" (Lenora Coleman on Earth, 2038) are rounded statements about the same autumn-2021 event.
- **Resolved - Book 3's winter**: Greenstone is in the southern hemisphere, so a Book 3 opening in winter does not conflict with the Book 2 contest at about M5.
- **Resolved - surge delay**: "some five years overdue" (Book 7, Ch1 L56), not fifteen.
- **Resolved - time away**: "fifteen" in Book 12 and "sixteen" in Book 13 are roundings of the roughly sixteen and a half years between autumn 2021 and 2038.
- **Resolved - Book 5 opening**: nine months after the June 2019 return (Book 5, Ch3 L170), about March 2020.
- **Resolved - Book 3 death and Book 5 Kaito**: see section 4.
- **Open - Emi's age against the sword line**: Emi is "fifteen years old" when Rufus and Taika arrive (Book 11, Ch68 L782); she was thirteen in spring 2020, so fifteen fits late 2022 to early 2023. But "six years since you first gave it to me" (Book 11, Ch68 L115) and Jason's age of 29 (Book 10, Ch33 L76) place the departure in late 2023 or later, and Emi is "nineteen" later (Book 11, Ch68 L918). Not resolved here.
- **Narrowed - Book 12 present**: the Pallimustus chapters fit late 2037 to early 2038 by their roundings, and the Earth arrival is best placed in 2038 because Lenora counts seventeen years from the Earthlings' vanishing (Book 12, Ch67 L65) and Jota gives Jason's age as forty-four (Book 12, Ch67 L113). Book 13 is dated 2038 (Book 13, Ch951 L50). The book never states a year or the gap between the tour and the arrival.
- **Narrowed - vampire war length**: Book 13 gives "decades" (Book 13, Ch1002 L69) and "a decade" for the initiative and the team's monster fighting (Book 13, Ch1001 L6; Ch1003 L91). The renewed war is placed "two years" in at Book 13, Ch995 L69 (see `Book_13_Timeline.md`).
- **Resolved - clan hiding period**: the figures are snapshots at different dates, not a conflict. The clan "had not been heard from in two years" at the end of Book 11 (Book 11, Ch68 L2260), "half a decade" at the opening of Book 12 (Book 12, Ch1 L93), and the city of Nitra shows "more than a decade of abandonment" by the Book 12 arrival (Book 12, Ch59 L5).
- **Open - public reveal of magic**: "Less than fifteen years ago, no one knew magic existed" (Book 12, Ch2 L107) against "twenty years ago" (Book 12, Ch22 L27); likely different counting points.
- **Open - Part II of Book 11**: the intervals (a year for Lenora, almost a year for Gary, two years of team work, two years of empty road) are told on separate clocks, so no total is asserted.
- **Jason's death count** is settled by Books 5-10: apartment (first), the Builder's tower (second, Book 3), the Dinosaur King at Makassar (third, Book 5), Shako (fourth, Book 6), shown by the Church of Death's certificate in Book 7 (Book 7, Ch17 L104).

## 10. The Book 11 Part II clock

Part II mixes several threads, each told on its own clock (see Book_11_Timeline.md section 7): a year for Lenora in the monitoring room (Book 11, Ch68 L412), almost a year for Gary (L703), two years of team work (L1034), two years more on Earth (L1341) and two years on the empty road (L2244). The text does not add these into one total.

**Working convention for the compendium** (an editorial choice, not a book statement):

1. Treat Jason's void departure as late 2023, following Jason's age of twenty-nine (Book 10, Ch33 L76) and the "six years since you first gave it to me" line (Book 11, Ch68 L115).
2. Start the Earth-side threads from Rufus and Taika's arrival, shortly after that date, and run them forward: Lenora's year and Gary's almost-year overlap at the start, the team's two years and the further two years follow, so the Earth-side threads end several years after the departure and well before 2038.
3. Treat Emi's "fifteen years old" at Rufus's arrival (Book 11, Ch68 L782) as the single outlier. It fits late 2022 to early 2023 and conflicts with convention point 1 (see Open_Questions).
4. Treat the void threads (the empty road, the nested soul) as running on Jason's own clock, which the text does not tie to Earth's, and add nothing to the Earth total.

This convention produces no contradiction with Book 12's "almost twenty years" since arrival (Book 12, Ch13 L36), which counts from late 2017 to late 2037 or early 2038.
