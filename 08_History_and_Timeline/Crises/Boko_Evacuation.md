## 1. Summary

Boko is destroyed on the day an assassin spears Jason's avatar there. Jason uses his aura to lift the city's people out and drop them in the desert, holds his draining power while the containment ritual is built, and dies in the blast. A messenger army then descends on the survivors until Jason's ghost-fire phoenix form rises from the crater. The survivors are sheltered in his astral kingdom.

## 2. Before

- **Li Li Mei** has lived in Boko for years; someone zeroing in on her sends her fleeing to a storehouse, where she meets Jason (Book 12, Ch30 L71-81). Boko is the origin of the anti-intellectual movement (Book 12, Ch29 L65).
- **Jason's arrival**: Jason reached Greenstone quietly days before the official arrival (Book 12, Ch27 L12). In Boko he meets Li Mei with Estella and Belinda (Book 12, Ch31 L3-12).

## 3. The attack

- **The assassin**: a man in black and red armour appears behind Jason; a slab of black-red metal is run through his body (Book 12, Ch32 L13). The blade is [Lesser Celestial Gorger (broken)], gold rank (Book 12, Ch34 L35).
- **The alert**: a System Alert announces a magical incident; occupants will be evacuated immediately; clergy are told to evacuate holy ground (Book 12, Ch32 L15-17). A regional alert tells silver-rank-and-below adventurers not to approach, and gold-rankers not to approach until after the blast (Book 12, Ch32 L24-26).
- **Jason's message**: "Got attacked. Going to blow up and take some or all of Boko with me. Getting people out, need wide-area containment ritual" (Book 12, Ch32 L29).
- **Shade's report**: the weapon drains Jason's power, which is infinite, until either the weapon or the avatar cannot contain it (Book 12, Ch32 L42-43).

## 4. The evacuation

- **Jason's aura**: aura floods out; the whole population floats up and is moved away from him and his attacker (Book 12, Ch32 L19-21). He drops the residents in the desert, as far from the city walls as he could (Book 12, Ch32 L66).
- **The ritual**: Clive and Farrah lead an improvised wide-area containment ritual with Belinda, Farrah and Humphrey's readings, built in the sky from Onslow's shell (Book 12, Ch32 L37-54). Danielle, Gabriel and Arabelle organise order and healing (Book 12, Ch32 L46-47).
- **The toll**: the aura becomes a spiritual wildfire; many normal-rank evacuees, mostly the very old and very young, die of haemorrhages and heart attacks (Book 12, Ch33 L5-6). Most of Boko's ninety thousand people are evacuated (Book 12, Ch33 L4).

## 5. The blast and the invasion

- **The blast**: when the containment gives out the outer regions are rubble and the space within is a hole (Book 12, Ch33 L3). A System alert reads "[System Administrator] assassinated. The Hegemon has arisen. Beware his wrath" (Book 12, Ch33 L9-10).
- **The messenger army**: four Voices of the Will watch an assassin kill Jason and say "It is time for an example to be made" (Book 12, Ch32 L49-53). Massive portals open and disgorge messengers by the thousand (Book 12, Ch33 L4, L7).
- **The phoenix**: a vast dark bird, speckled with lights like a starry night and limned in silver flames, rises from the crater and batters the messengers into disorder (Book 12, Ch33 L11-12). The ghost fire phoenix arcs through the host (Book 12, Ch33 L16).

## 6. Aftermath

- **Shelter**: only Jason's astral kingdom has the space and resources to shelter a city's worth of people (Book 12, Ch36 L3). He builds an oasis city for them, later named New Water (Book 12, Ch36 L4; Book 12, Ch39 L61).
- **The crater**: the coastal side collapses and becomes a harbour with floating temples as islands (Book 12, Ch39 L37).
- **Diplomacy**: Jamis Fran Muskar apologises for the deaths in Boko; Jason refuses a proposal to blunt his fury by giving up areas, and refuses to slaughter a quarter of a million messengers to restore his ghost phoenix form (Book 12, Ch35 L6-8, L66, L84, L88).
- **Later**: portals again open over the crater and messengers pour out, never close to the survivors (Book 12, Ch36 L6).
