## 1. Summary

During the Rimaros monster surge, the Builder cult sends mobile cities against the Storm Kingdom: a ground city that walks south, an underwater city and a flying fortress-city that appears over Livaros. The adventurers answer with six diamond-rankers, gods' miracles and a single true bomb carried into the flying city. The war is later called the War of Four Cities or the One Day War (Book 7, Ch66 L27).

## 2. Warning signs

- **Rumours and reports**: Rick Geller reports a "ground city" that moves on thousands of thick legs, which started moving south several weeks earlier (Book 7, Ch59 L124). It passed through the arid lands and the Arkivahl Desert; two cities have already fallen, with news several days old (Book 7, Ch59 L127-129).
- **The assessment**: at least four diamond-rankers are needed to attack it, five or more would be better (Book 7, Ch59 L130).
- **A second city**: an underwater city is already in the kingdom and on the move; the two converge on the northern reaches (Book 7, Ch59 L131).
- **The Builder's invasion platforms** appear worldwide (Book 7, Ch8 L36). Soramir and Jason look to Dawn, who answers "No" (Book 7, Ch59 L133-134).

## 3. Preparation

- **Rallying**: the royal family, the Irios house and the royal guard are reassured by the gods' help (Book 7, Ch65 L18); Archbishop Rimmon says each of the gods will do what they can, with the Archbishops of War and Ocean empowering defences and the sea (Book 7, Ch65 L17-19).
- **Soramir's warning**: "many of us will fall today" (Book 7, Ch65 L20).
- **The weapon**: many teams, Jason's team among them, are handed devices; only one is the true bomb, so the cult will have to guess (Book 7, Ch65 L21). Jason's team is given a dummy; Keel's crystal turns red to confirm it (Book 7, Ch65 L70-75). Liara holds the true device (Book 7, Ch65 L77).
- **The magic defences**: Farrah is a formation magic expert, wanted to breach the Builder city's defences (Book 7, Ch65 L27).
- **Travis's weapon**: Jason brought Travis into the fold to produce the weapon that destroyed the flying city (Book 7, Ch66 L44).

## 4. The battle

- **The kraken city**: the floating city is "actually a city-sized kraken construct", with ten panels opening into tentacles each a diamond-rank construct, swatting airships from the sky (Book 7, Ch64 L10-12).
- **The diamond-rankers**: the adventurers have six, with Soramir conspicuously absent (Book 7, Ch64 L14). Each faces a Builder cult diamond-ranker, and the clashes spell doom for lower-rankers nearby.
- **The lower ranks**: their objective is to invade the city, fight to its core mechanisms and destroy them, while the cult aims to bleed them (Book 7, Ch64 L15-16). The adventurers' quality tells over time (Book 7, Ch64 L17).
- **Barrier**: a diamond-ranker slays the lamprey and turns to breach the defence screen, with gold-rankers pounding it (Book 7, Ch64 L18).
- **The flying city**: a rainbow light above Rimaros disgorges a city in the sky, a stone disc with a ritual circle on its underside that fires a beam at Livaros; the gods' intervention shields Livaros (Book 7, Ch65 L28-30; Book 7, Ch66 L34). Jason notes "That is a Builder fortress-city, appearing over Livaros" (Book 7, Ch64 L72).
- **The raid on Arnote**: while forces are mobilised, pirates raid Arnote, and Pelli fights the pirate captain outside the town of Kasilaro (Book 7, Ch64 L20-25). The raiders who enter the town are struck down by black, withering death (Book 7, Ch64 L28-29).
- **The bomb**: Liara, with the siblings Ledev and Jana, carry the true device through the underground industrial depths; they join a mixed group including Vesper and Jeni Kavaloa (Book 7, Ch65 L77-80). Vesper tells her "We're close now," and the group draws attention so Liara can sneak the rest of the way (Book 7, Ch65 L83-87). Liara feels Vesper's aura wink out (Book 7, Ch65 L88). Ledev stays behind (Book 7, Ch65 L90).

## 5. Aftermath

- **A month later** the Storm Kingdom and the Adventure Society are still adjusting (Book 7, Ch66 L27).
- **Dead**: Ledev and Vesper both died saving the city, and Jeni Kavaloa died fighting alongside Vesper (Book 7, Ch66 L36).
- **Effect on adventuring**: quotas are raised and safety standards lowered; bronze-rankers were kept out of the war and are combined into larger groups sent on silver-rank missions (Book 7, Ch66 L29).
- **The other cities**: the kraken city sank to the sea floor; the flying city fell and Livaros was shielded; only minor coastal flooding hit Provo and Arnote (Book 7, Ch66 L34).
- **Jason's part**: he is at the periphery of the events; Arabelle reminds him that his intervention persuaded Dawn to move and that Travis's weapon destroyed the flying city (Book 7, Ch66 L37-44).
