## 1. Summary

The messenger war is the long conflict between the winged servants of the astral kings and the people of Pallimustus, with Jason caught between them as a would-be astral king. It begins with the invasion of the south in Book 9, includes the Battle of Yaresh and the Cardinas expedition, and ends with the reprisal at Boko and an uneasy peace in Books 12-13. This file sets out the sequence; the Battle of Yaresh and the Cardinas expedition have their own era guides.

## 2. Before the war

- **How messengers move**: Clive explains that messengers create dimensional channels (Book 7, Ch36 L47). Messenger architecture is obsessed with circles (Book 9, Ch55 L3).
- **Preparation**: the noble houses expect the conflict with the messengers "to start" and grab what they can while they can (Book 9, Ch13 L52). The vast majority of astral kings are messengers, and a war against them is in the offing (Book 9, Ch23 L44).

## 3. The campaign

- **Yaresh (Book 9)**: Marek Nior Vargas leads the breach force over the entertainment district (Book 9, Ch86 L16); Jes Fin Kaal, the Voice of the Will, directs from a circular tower (Book 9, Ch66 L3). The battle is covered in ../Era_Guides/Battle_of_Yaresh.md.
- **Why the southern front matters**: the messengers are fighting on multiple fronts (Book 9, Ch52 L74) and use apocalypse beasts as weapons (Book 9, Ch62 L26).
- **After the battle (Books 9-10)**: Marek, Tera and others become Jason's captives; Jason tells Marek his story about the war guilt clause in the peace treaty after the Great War (Book 10, Ch4 L31). Marek works on plans for an uprising among the captives (Book 10, Ch42 L4).
- **Cardinas (Books 10-11)**: the messengers' device and soul forge attempt, covered in ../Era_Guides/Cardinas_Expedition_and_Zone.md; Boris says Vesta Carmis Zell would never accept an Unorthodoxy member (Book 11, Ch24 L85).
- **A withdrawal**: by Book 11 the messengers are done in the region, their numbers and resources too low to defend (Book 11, Ch68 L361).

## 4. The reprisal (Book 12)

- **Boko**: four Voices of the Will watch the assassination and say "It is time ... for an example to be made" (Book 12, Ch32 L49-53). Massive portals open over the ruins and disgorge an army (Book 12, Ch33 L4). See Boko_Evacuation.md.
- **The Council of Kings**: Jamis Fran Muskar is told "We are all astral kings" and that the Council may guide but does not rule (Book 12, Ch33 L55). He apologises for the deaths in Boko; Jason refuses to blunt his fury by giving up areas or to slaughter a quarter of a million messengers (Book 12, Ch35 L6, L66, L88).
- **Later portals**: more messengers pour out over the crater but never reach the survivors (Book 12, Ch36 L6).

## 5. Aftermath (Book 13)

- **Rehabilitation**: someone is wanted to rehabilitate the messengers whom Jamis Fran Muskar surrendered to Jason (Book 13, Ch973 L24). Jali Corrik Fen is the caretaker of the messengers living with the Asano clan (Book 13, Ch973 L85).
- **Jamis on McKean**: Farrah says Muskar might not think McKean is dangerous (Book 13, Ch1003 L25).

## 6. Who is where

- **Messengers**: Kaal, Marek, Hess Jor Nasala, Fal Vin Garath, Tera Jun Casta, Jali Corrik Fen; the astral kings Vesta Carmis Zell and Jamis Fran Muskar.
- **Jason's side**: Jason, his team, Yaresh's gold-rankers, the Church of the Healer and the Adventure Society.
