## 1. Summary

The Earth vampire war runs in two phases: a first war that "ended without conclusion" when both sides were exhausted and the vampires got nuclear weapons, then a renewed war that was in full swing again by the Book 13 symposium and ends at McKean's castle. This file collects the duration statements. For the events, see ../Era_Guides/Earth_Vampire_War.md.

## 2. Duration statements

- **The first war**: it "ended without conclusion" when both sides were exhausted and the vampires got nuclear weapons (Book 13, Ch987 L63).
- **The renewed war**: it was "back in full swing" (Book 13, Ch988 L26), and Anna says military forces operate out of Asano territory again (Book 13, Ch993 L81).
- **The symposium**: a character places it "two years into the renewed vampire war" (Book 13, Ch995 L69).
- **Decades**: Simon McKean says the conflict has been fought "off and on for decades" (Book 13, Ch1002 L69).
- **The end**: Jason says the operation will bring it to a final conclusion (Book 13, Ch1002 L64), and it ended in the months before the farewell (Book 13, Ch1023 L12).
- **Not known**: the first war's length (Open_Questions item 19).

## 3. The start (Books 5-6)

- Vampires rise when transformation events release reality cores (Book 5, Ch55 L92; Book 5, Ch51 L127). Russia and Europe suffer massive vampiric occupation (Book 5, Ch66 L8).
- In Book 6 the vampires of the Saint-Étienne zone are partly allied with Jason; they later abandon France (Book 6, Ch47 L5).

## 4. The détente and the purge

- Under Elizabeth the vampires establish a détente: every major city hides vampires, and bombings in Europe are answered by assassinations in four world capitals (Book 11, Ch68 L1489).
- Humanity tries to kill Elizabeth with a modified nuclear missile that floods a region with magical sunlight (Book 11, Ch68 L1490).
- Jason's purge of reality-core remnants in Book 12 (Book 12, Ch5 L70).

## 5. The final offensive

- Belgrade and Multan (Book 13, Ch1007 L3-16); McKean's castle and the blood vine (Book 13, Ch1008 L20-27; Book 13, Ch1011 L18-44); the last vampire lord (Book 13, Ch1013 L34).
