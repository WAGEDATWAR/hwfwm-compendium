Book 13 is set almost entirely on Earth, and its chapters carry on the series-wide numbering from Book 12's Ch67.

## 1. Time anchors used in this book

- **The year**: a character asks how not knowing a kids' show "in 2038" could be un-Australian (Book 13, Ch951 L50).
- **Jason's absence**: "In the sixteen years I've been gone" (Book 13, Ch972 L51). Elsewhere the text gives "twenty years" for the time since Earth learned of magic and since earlier meetings: Jason and Zara first met "twenty years ago" (Book 13, Ch956 L41); a Chinese contact says "twenty years ago" of a first meeting (Book 13, Ch960 L23); governments took "twenty years" to listen (Book 13, Ch974 L78); Farrah is found by someone she had not seen "in twenty years" (Book 13, Ch998 L99).
- **The extra time**: the Cosmic Throne detour gave Earth "a precious fifteen extra years to prepare" (Book 13, Ch954 L17).
- **The Network**: it fragmented into four "well over a decade ago" (Book 13, Ch964 L9).
- **The initiative**: Jason has been "pushing the Earth Defence Joint Initiative for a decade" (Book 13, Ch1001 L6), and Neil says the team has "been running around this planet for a decade" (Book 13, Ch1003 L91).
- **Clan research**: the clan has not been experimenting on humans for "a decade and a half" (Book 13, Ch987 L37).
- **The renewed vampire war**: a character places the symposium "two years into the renewed vampire war" (Book 13, Ch995 L69). The first vampire war ended without conclusion (Book 13, Ch987 L63), and the renewed phase ended in the months before the farewell (Book 13, Ch1023 L12).

## 8. Notes on the interval

- Book 13's present is 2038: a character asks how not knowing a kids' show in 2038 can be un-Australian (Book 13, Ch951 L50). If Jason left Earth in autumn 2021 (Book 6, Ch47 L155) and has been gone sixteen years (Book 13, Ch972 L51), his return falls around 2037 to 2038, which agrees with the dated line. The "twenty years" lines count from the arrival or from the first events on Earth, not from his departure.
- The order inside the book is chapter order. Gaps between scenes are not stated except for "a decade" and "fifteen years" of background.
- The old notes for this book mix up several chapter numbers (for example Nik's gold rank); the rows above cite only lines that were read.
