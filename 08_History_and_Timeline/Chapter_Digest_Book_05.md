## 1. How to use this digest

One entry per chapter, in book order: the chapter number and title, then up to two sentences from the timeline with their citations. Use it to find which chapter covers an event, then open the chapter file or the timeline for detail. It quotes no passages. Coverage in this book: 67 of 67 chapters have a summary sentence; the rest are titles only.

- **Ch1, Too Valuable to Lose**: Bronze-rankers cannot fight through lethal blows the way a silver-ranker can (Book 5, Ch1 L38)
- **Ch2, All I Can Do is My Best**: Jason continues to heal in the West African Ebola outbreak, saying he does not want to leave while it is ongoing (Book 5, Ch2 L126). He is introduced to Dr Chloe Baudrillard (Book 5, Ch2 L9).

- **Ch8, What’s Left of Your Principles**: Mr North says "One week" (Book 5, Ch8 L54); Barbou recommends waiting two weeks (Book 5, Ch8 L35) because the grid takes one to two weeks to reactivate (Book 5, Ch8 L44)
- **Ch9, Contingencies**: The beach birthday party for Emi is going well apart from her uncle's absence (Book 5, Ch9 L5). Jason arrives to say they are all moving to the compound that day (Book 5, Ch9 L23).

- **Ch23, Sword-Fighting With No Shirt On**: Audrey says a clockwork king was dug up in the eighties with Assyrian relics more than two millennia old (Book 5, Ch23 L94). Terrance names Jeremy Westin as the only member of the press who has been inside the village (Book 5, Ch23 L37)
