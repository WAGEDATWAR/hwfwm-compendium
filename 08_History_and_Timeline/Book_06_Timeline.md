Chapter-by-chapter order of Book 6. The Earth chapters (Ch1 to Ch46) cover Jason and Farrah; the later sections of Ch47 follow Gary, Sophie, Humphrey, Clive and the others on Pallimustus in the same period, and are cited with high line numbers.

## 1. Time anchors used in this book

- **Sword age**: Dread Salvation was made "roughly three years" before (Book 6, Ch1 L85).
- **Walkabout**: Jason spent "six months in which he wandered across Asia, Europe and Africa" (Book 6, Ch5 L72).
- **Jason's gap**: the vampire watch notes the activity in "the two weeks you've been gone" (Book 6, Ch21 L31), and a month after the Slovak dome came down gold-rankers are still exploring his domain (Book 6, Ch23 L3).
- **Winter**: the Saint-Étienne planning happens on "a cold winter day" (Book 6, Ch31 L4), in the northern hemisphere, so December to February.
- **Spirit domain age**: six months after the Saint-Étienne spirit domain is established, silver-rank monsters have manifested there with semi-regularity (Book 6, Ch47 L4), and the last transformation zone was forty-two days earlier (Book 6, Ch47 L14).
- **Departure**: "Jason made his farewells in France, on a warm autumn day" (Book 6, Ch47 L155).
- **Pallimustus epilogues**: Sophie's group last saw Gary "in two years, since Jason's memorial" (Book 6, Ch47 L941).

## 7. Notes on the interval

- The text gives a relative frame (two weeks, a month, a cold winter day, six months, a warm autumn day), not dates. Counting from the Nitra dome in about October 2020 (Book 5, Ch68 L68), the Saint-Étienne zone in winter (about December 2020 to February 2021) and six months of the French domain together put Jason's farewell in about September or October 2021, matching the autumn departure in `Master_Timeline.md`.
- The "two years since Jason's memorial" for the Pallimustus group (Book 6, Ch47 L941) matches a memorial in about spring 2019 and the same autumn 2021 end point.
