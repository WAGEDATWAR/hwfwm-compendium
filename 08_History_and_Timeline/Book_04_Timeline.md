Chapter-by-chapter order of Book 4, with the day or week wherever the text gives one. "Week 1" starts on the night Jason wakes in the hospital.

## 1. Time anchors used in this book

- **Return date**: "It had been late November when Jason left and now it was early June, a year and a half later" (Book 4, Ch3 L112). Jason works out for himself that it should be "somewhere near the start of winter" (Book 4, Ch1 L63).
- **Days of the week**: Erika's birthday is "next week" on the first night (Book 4, Ch3 L108) and "next Friday" on the day he meets Hiro (Book 4, Ch6 L105); the party falls on a Friday (Book 4, Ch24 L66). He meets Tollman at noon on a Tuesday (Book 4, Ch9 L65).
- **After the party**: the family conversation happens on a winter Saturday (Book 4, Ch27 L3; Book 4, Ch30 L38), the negotiation with the Network is set for "Tuesday", "the day after tomorrow" (Book 4, Ch33 L76-77).
- **Mid-book**: Jason tells Erika "last week, I got mildly kidnapped" (Book 4, Ch50 L84), and the high school students filming his training "started filming us last week" (Book 4, Ch64 L82).
- **End**: Jason's month of seclusion and aura retraining ends with the farewell barbecue and his departure on a walkabout (Book 4, Ch74 L32, L65). Book 5 opens when he has been back "nine months" (Book 5, Ch3 L170) after a walkabout of about six months (Book 5, Ch8 L5), so this book covers about three months, June to about September 2019.

## 10. Notes on the interval

- The text fixes the return (early June 2019), the first weeks by weekday, and the end by the month of seclusion; the "Week N" labels from week 5 onward are counted from those anchors and from the "last week" remarks (Book 4, Ch50 L84; Book 4, Ch64 L82), not from stated dates.
- Earth time and Pallimustus time run together, so Book 4 overlaps no Pallimustus event: the team is out of the story.
