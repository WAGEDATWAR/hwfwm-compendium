Chapter-by-chapter order of Book 10, with the in-world time wherever the text gives it. Book 10 opens in the weeks after the Battle of Yaresh (the end of Book 9).

## 1. Time anchors used in this book

- **Aftermath**: Jason tells Kaal that "a few weeks ago" her people came into the city (Book 10, Ch17 L52), so the early chapters are some weeks after the battle.
- **Adventuring**: the team's "non-stop adventuring" is in its third week at Ch31 (Book 10, Ch31 L9).
- **Jason's age**: at Ch33 he says "I'm twenty-nine years old" (Book 10, Ch33 L76). He was born on 1 April 1994 (see `Master_Timeline.md`), so Ch33 is after 1 April 2023.
- **Surge**: the highway to the expedition's start is "a little worse for wear after more than six months of neglect, starting back with the monster surge" (Book 10, Ch43 L13).
- **On the expedition**: Boris's block on his brand holds "another twelve days or so" (Book 10, Ch79 L86); Emir tells Jason that he is not "that lost kid I met six years ago" (Book 10, Ch84 L85).
- **End**: the book ends as Clive's ritual starts and the transformation zone begins around the Cardinas natural array (Book 10, Ch92 L14).

## 8. Notes on the interval

- The early chapters are "a few weeks" after the Yaresh raid (Book 10, Ch17 L52), and Jason is twenty-nine by Ch33 (Book 10, Ch33 L76), after 1 April 2023. Together these put the Battle of Yaresh no later than about February 2023 and revise the Book 9 estimate: the road south, the Yaresh stay and the battle together take longer than the autumn 2022 placement in the old notes. Book 10's expedition falls in about April to mid 2023, ending as the transformation zone begins around the natural array.
- The old Master Timeline placed the expedition at "M65" and the Death Pact in the same period; the text supports an end of Book 10 in mid 2023, and Book 11 then runs for about nine months of the zone (see `Book_11_Timeline.md`).
- The labels after "Third week" are chapter-order placements after the one stated anchor, the third week of adventuring (Book 10, Ch31 L9); they say only what the chapters order.
