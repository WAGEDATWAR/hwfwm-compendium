Chapter-by-chapter order of Book 9, with the in-world time wherever the text gives it. Book 9 opens after the Builder's withdrawal and the monster surge's end, with the festival in Rimaros.

## 1. Time anchors used in this book

- **Years since arrival**: "the five years since his first arrival on Pallimustus" (Book 9, Ch21 L4), a rounded figure, with "three years of spirit coins" for Jason's absence from his team (Book 9, Ch9 L42).
- **Departure**: Jason and Estella watch the island shrink and recall arriving in Rimaros "half a year ago"; they are now "at the start of the wet season in the tropics" (Book 9, Ch24 L44).
- **Monsoon**: the road to the southern cities will take "About four months" of monsoon weather (Book 9, Ch26 L26); Rosa says the Storm Shredder team has had "almost half a year of responsibility" (Book 9, Ch28 L108).
- **Surge**: "The monster surge was five years late. Five years of the economy being strained" (Book 9, Ch31 L40).
- **Yaresh**: Emir plans to arrive at Isart "in about two weeks" (Book 9, Ch39 L26); Humphrey estimates Jason and Estella will remain in the city for two weeks (Book 9, Ch42 L31); Jason says "See you in two weeks" (Book 9, Ch43 L72); Emir meets Jason at the end of "next week" (Book 9, Ch49 L80).
- **The battle**: the messenger raid on Yaresh (Ch79 to Ch103) is followed by "the weeks following the battle of Yaresh", when rubble is repurposed into construction projects and tent cities remain (Book 9, Ch103 L85).

## 8. Notes on the interval

- The departure from Rimaros is "half a year" after the arrival (Book 9, Ch24 L44), so about April 2022 by the calendar anchors. The road south then takes "about four months" of monsoon weather (Book 9, Ch26 L26), and the team stops at Rajoras, Cartise and elsewhere on the way. Book 10 fixes the outer limit: Jason is twenty-nine in Book 10, Ch33 (Book 10, Ch33 L76), which is after 1 April 2023, and the chapters before it are "a few weeks" after the battle (Book 10, Ch17 L52). The Battle of Yaresh therefore falls no later than about February 2023, and the arrival at Yaresh somewhat earlier. The old Master Timeline gave "wiki M58" for the battle; counted from November 2017 the text supports about M60 to M62.
- The "Early in the stay", "During the stay" and "Later in the stay" labels are placements by chapter order within the stay in Yaresh, anchored by the two-week schedules in Ch42, Ch43 and Ch49. Humphrey says Jason and Estella will remain in the city for two weeks (Book 9, Ch42 L31); Jason says "See you in two weeks" (Book 9, Ch43 L72); a speaker says he will not be available until the end of next week (Book 9, Ch49 L80). The book gives no calendar date for the battle.
- Ch21 and Ch100 round Jason's time on Pallimustus up to "five years" and "six years" (Book 9, Ch21 L4; Book 9, Ch100 L51); the calendar anchors give about four and a half years at Ch21 and about five at Ch100.
