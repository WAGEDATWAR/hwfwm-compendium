## 2. Chapters

- **Ch1, Looting a House as it Burns Down Around You**: Jack Gerling is a gold-rank essence user, a category-four in Network terms, from the US branches (Book 6, Ch1 L3). He has been sent to Slovakia, where a massive dome has appeared on the plains outside Nitra (Book 6, Ch1 L4).
- **Ch2, Domain**: The living anomaly is weak for a silver-rank monster (Book 6, Ch2 L34), and Dread Salvation undergoes random changes (Book 6, Ch2 L12).
- **Ch3, Needs of the Moment**: With three soul-bonded items, Jason qualifies for the Soul-Imprinting Triune (Book 6, Ch3 L67). The transformation zone has a ticking clock on its stabilisation (Book 6, Ch3 L39)
- **Ch4, A Beautiful Woman and a Sack of Cash**: The Amulet of the Dark Guardian is added to the set Regalia of the Dark Hegemon (Book 6, Ch4 L30). The cloud flask is the work of an unknown diamond-rank craftsman, and Jason has used it for a couple of years (Book 6, Ch4 L40).
- **Ch5, You Have to Be True to Yourself**: The territory is renamed Arrival Pagoda (Book 6, Ch5 L41). Jason recalls spending much of his six months of wandering in China (Book 6, Ch5 L72)
- **Ch6, One More Secret**: The expanded domain has retro-future cars parked like set dressing (Book 6, Ch6 L7).
- **Ch7, Instinct is All We Have**: Chen arrives through the portal, followed by Gerling and the other two Chinese category-fours (Book 6, Ch7 L22)
- **Ch8, Step Back**: Tran stands holding the spheres ripped out of the anomalies (Book 6, Ch8 L3).
- **Ch9, Guns & Money**: The dead anomalies lie thickest around the pagoda (Book 6, Ch9 L7). The Instability Regulator is a silver-rank epic item (Book 6, Ch9 L43)
- **Ch10, Old Habit**: Jason expands his spirit domain from a rooftop into a city-like territory (Book 6, Ch10 L3-5). He holes up in a store to treat his wounds as anomalies converge (Book 6, Ch10 L19-25).
- **Ch11, It’s Still Not About Killing Monsters**: Reclamation cores could potentially drain the power from gold-rankers and ancient vampires (Book 6, Ch11 L14)
- **Ch12, Open to the Unanticipated**: A Dark Orb unseals a random Dark Essence ability (Book 6, Ch12 L7). The third territory is renamed Tranquil Shadow Woods (Book 6, Ch12 L59).
- **Ch13, I’m Going to Bet on Myself**: The rescued celestine family asks where they are (Book 6, Ch13 L3). His Spirit Vault evolves to a secondary gift (Book 6, Ch13 L77)
- **Ch14, No Perfect Options**: His eyes are "A shifting mix of gold, silver and blue" (Book 6, Ch14 L31). Shade says the industrial city is from one of the first worlds the new Builder plundered (Book 6, Ch14 L74).
- **Ch15, Stillness**: Jason fights zombie cyborgs across industrial ruins (Book 6, Ch15 L3-7). He meditates to extend his aura into the territory (Book 6, Ch15 L40).
- **Ch16, Whatever We Face**: The territory is renamed Steamforge Circuit (Book 6, Ch16 L60). Guo is warped by the unstable cores into a tentacle monster (Book 6, Ch16 L83)
- **Ch17, You Really Aren’t Local**: 1327 stable genesis cores are consumed in the apotheosis (Book 6, Ch17 L13). A red-haired, freckled man with inhumanly bright green eyes stands in front of the portal (Book 6, Ch17 L37).
- **Ch18, A Sliver of Hope**: Dawn tells Shako he has come to this world and killed Jason Asano in violation of the compact (Book 6, Ch18 L6). The Fundament Gate would let Jason securely anchor the bridge (Book 6, Ch18 L39).
- **Ch19, End-User Licence Agreement**: The pagoda is redesigned floor by floor (Book 6, Ch19 L21). Anything hostile to Jason's domain acquires Blood From a Stone, Mortality and Weakness of the Flesh (Book 6, Ch19 L65)
- **Ch20, Negotiations**: Two gold-rank essence users and three vampires approach the pagoda through the air (Book 6, Ch20 L38). Jason says the Slovak government itself caused the violence when the Network split (Book 6, Ch20 L81)
- **Ch21, Another Day for Vampires**: The Blade of the Blood Queen is an iron-rank legendary growth item (Book 6, Ch21 L4). Farrah's report: in the two weeks Jason has been gone, activity around the blood treatment facility has jumped (Book 6, Ch21 L31).
- **Ch22, Less Freud and More God of Healing**: Jason expects to find nodes directly through node space rather than proto-spaces (Book 6, Ch22 L5). Erika leaves Emi playing a board game from the collection Greg bequeathed (Book 6, Ch22 L52)
- **Ch23, Little Cost in Exploring**: In the month since the dome came down, gold-rankers have been exploring his domain (Book 6, Ch23 L3)
- **Ch24, Intentions**: A delegation includes an EOA representative named Alexander Clerck (Book 6, Ch24 L6)
- **Ch25, I Need That Song to Play Out**: Vermillion explains ghouls (Book 6, Ch25 L79). The target is a sealed astral space such as the one at Saint-Étienne (Book 6, Ch25 L102).
- **Ch26, Wash Them First**: Anna reports that of Gerling "He's gone rogue" (Book 6, Ch26 L63), and Akari and Jason discuss the combat trance (Book 6, Ch26 L71)
- **Ch27, The Language of Passion**: The plan is to steal a magically enhanced SADM, a nuclear bomb in a backpack (Book 6, Ch27 L51)
- **Ch28, Forthright Honesty**: Travis Noble is twenty-one, a category-two magitech engineer, having a very bad month (Book 6, Ch28 L36).
- **Ch29, Pertinent Factor**: His family has been in the Network since before the Revolutionary War (Book 6, Ch29 L84)
- **Ch30, More of a Focus on Nipples**: Gerling grabs Adrien Barbou on a whirlwind trip back to the US (Book 6, Ch30 L29)
- **Ch31, The Job That’s in Front of You**: Jason, Farrah and Travis sit on a covered deck on a cold winter day (Book 6, Ch31 L4). The Saint-Étienne astral space has suffered the struggle for control (Book 6, Ch31 L62)
- **Ch32, Going Suspiciously Well**: Jason leaves the nuke in an old wine cellar (Book 6, Ch32 L51). The van holds Chen, Shako and a silver-rank driver carrying the dimension-collapsing device (Book 6, Ch32 L42).
- **Ch33, One of Asano’s Secrets**: Ghost Fruit is a gold-rank common item (Book 6, Ch33 L6).
- **Ch34, Swarm Against Swarm**: A group of newly transformed celestines approach Jason's palace (Book 6, Ch34 L51). Jason recalls his familiars other than Shade (Book 6, Ch34 L6)
- **Ch35, Necessary Evil**: Mr North says the grid shutdown was "a necessary evil" (Book 6, Ch35 L15). Carmen "just turns up" and is aboard the Last Ferry (Book 6, Ch35 L112).
- **Ch36, Balls**: Jason looks into a cloud mirror at his eyes, now eye-shaped nebulas (Book 6, Ch36 L73)
- **Ch37, Which One of Us is the Villain**: Henrietta Geller's summoned angelic being has potent healing powers (Book 6, Ch37 L93).
- **Ch38, That Passion Didn’t Come From Nowhere**: A nascent spirit domain has expanded into adjacent territory (Book 6, Ch38 L21)
- **Ch39, The Upstart Magician**: Jason found vampires in elaborate coffins in a crypt (Book 6, Ch39 L12).
- **Ch40, Too Much Over Pride**: Elizabeth, Klaus and Georges choose to join and each get a carriage (Book 6, Ch40 L18).
- **Ch41, Trust All the Way**: Remains drained of all magical energy cannot be looted (Book 6, Ch41 L8). Progression to gold is at 30% (Book 6, Ch41 L39).
- **Ch42, Time to Choose**: A System message reports "You no longer have access to [Party Interface]" (Book 6, Ch42 L101).
- **Ch43, Four-Score Men**: Mr North explains that his formerly bonded essence user is known to the Network (Book 6, Ch43 L48). Jason says the severity reduction does not perfectly follow expectations (Book 6, Ch43 L18)
- **Ch44, The Very Opposite of Fantastical**: The anomalies are a bland version of human: identical men in identical black suits (Book 6, Ch44 L4).
- **Ch45, Small Mercies**: The vorger pour in from a single rift in the sky (Book 6, Ch45 L34)
- **Ch46, Salus Mundi Suprema Lex Esto**: The illusion Jason was trapped in vanishes as his mind clears (Book 6, Ch46 L31). Travis' Big Rocket is a silver-rank rare item (Book 6, Ch46 L53).
- **Ch47, Something Other Than Human**: The Saint-Étienne spirit domain encompasses most of the city (Book 6, Ch47 L3). Silver-rank monsters have manifested with semi-regularity in the six months since the domain was established (Book 6, Ch47 L4).
