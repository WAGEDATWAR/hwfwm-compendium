Chapter-by-chapter order of Book 5, with the in-world time wherever the text gives it. Book 5 opens at the end of the walkabout that closed Book 4.

## 1. Time anchors used in this book

- **Opening**: Jason "had been back in his own world for nine months and bronze rank for a year" (Book 5, Ch3 L170). Counting nine months from early June 2019 puts the opening in about March 2020.
- **Walkabout**: he describes himself as "a guy who ran away from his family for six months" (Book 5, Ch8 L5), and the family compound was built in "six months" (Book 5, Ch5 L104).
- **Grid shutdown**: a blackout lasts three or four days because of the grid's self-repair function (Book 5, Ch5 L7); after a total shutdown the grid is expected to need one to two weeks to reactivate (Book 5, Ch8 L44), and the EOA waits two weeks before the strike (Book 5, Ch8 L35).
- **Season**: Ch16 is a motorcycle ride "in the warm sun of late summer" (Book 5, Ch16 L78). Jason's birthday is 1 April, April Fool's Day (Book 5, Ch20 L26), and he has "just turned twenty-six" at Ch20 (Book 5, Ch20 L24), so Ch20 is in early April 2020.
- **Intervals**: "the almost two months since Shade had reached silver-rank" at Ch18 (Book 5, Ch18 L22); Jason and Akari spar for two months (Book 5, Ch21 L32); the Makassar response lasts eight days (Book 5, Ch30 L57); the funeral for Kaito is "conducted under a bright, clear sky, despite the winter" (Book 5, Ch52 L3), which in Australia is June to August 2020; Jason's family spends "weeks in isolation" in his spirit vault (Book 5, Ch53 L67); the team works "two weeks" to identify its Australian node (Book 5, Ch66 L5).
- **Jeremy's cue**: Jason returned "ten months ago" when interviewed (Book 5, Ch24 L61), which fits April 2020.

## 9. Notes on the interval

- The text fixes the opening at about nine months after the return (March 2020), Jason's birthday on 1 April at Ch20, and the funeral in an Australian winter at Ch52. The labels from "May" onward are placements based on those anchors and on the stated intervals ("two months", "eight days", "weeks in isolation", "two weeks"); no chapter gives a calendar date after Ch20.
- The old Master Timeline gave "M36" for Kaito's death. With the funeral in an Australian winter, his death falls in about May to June 2020, which is M30 to M31 counting from November 2017.
