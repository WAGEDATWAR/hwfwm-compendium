Chapter-by-chapter order of Book 2, with the in-world time wherever the text gives it. Book 2 starts the day after Book 1 ends, in the cloud palace.

## 1. Time anchors used in this book

- **About four months since arrival**: Jason tells Sophie "it's been about four months. I think I missed a birthday" (Book 2, Ch9 L67).
- **Six months, rounded**: in Ch14 he says "Six months ago, I was assistant manager at a retail bulk office supplier" (Book 2, Ch14 L35) and, during the trials, "Six months ago, I didn't know anything that happened in the last five months was possible" (Book 2, Ch37 L62).
- **The inquiry**: the team from the Continental Council has little effect on Jason "over the first few days" (Book 2, Ch10 L51), and he spends four days in the delta before hearing that people think he has gone missing (Book 2, Ch12 L88), with "almost two weeks" away in all (Book 2, Ch13 L8).
- **The trials**: they close after eighteen days (Book 2, Ch44 L87), and Keane counts six days left partway through the city (Book 2, Ch54 L6).
- **The surge**: Rufus says in Ch10 that there "should be a monster surge by now" (Book 2, Ch10 L62), and Cassandra and Thalia Mercer have been back in the city for six months in preparation for it (Book 2, Ch27 L44).

## 6. Notes on the interval

- The text states about four months since arrival at Ch9 (Book 2, Ch9 L67), roundings of "six months" at Ch14 and Ch37, and an eighteen-day trial window (Book 2, Ch44 L87), so Book 2 runs from about M4 (late March 2018) to about M6 (May-June 2018). The southern winter that opens Book 3 (Book 3, Ch1 L14) follows this.
- The "Day N" labels in section 4 follow the text only at day 1 (arrival), day 12 (six days left, Book 2, Ch54 L6) and the eighteen-day limit (Book 2, Ch44 L87). Other rows are placed by chapter order. The Greenstone scenes of Ch59 happen in the city while the iron-rankers are away.
