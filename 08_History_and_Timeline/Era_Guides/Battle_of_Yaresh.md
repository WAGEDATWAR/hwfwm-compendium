## 1. Summary

After a four-month journey south from Rimaros, Jason's convoy reaches the rainforest elf city of Yaresh as messengers besiege it. The Church of Knowledge gathers an army in the background, the messengers use apocalypse beasts and world-taker worms as weapons, and the battle ends with a breach the messengers' commander Marek has to concede. Jason's identity as an astral king comes out, and Marek and Tera become his captives.

## 2. The world at the start

- **The journey**: the trip will take "about four months" (Book 9, Ch26 L26); the convoy stays at Rajoras for a few days (Book 9, Ch29 L13) and clears Cartise's ruins on the way (Book 9, Ch31 L11).
- **Orders from Knowledge**: a priest of the Church of Knowledge tells Humphrey's team to follow the Adventure Society's orders for a couple of weeks, after which the church will requisition them for the main conflict (Book 9, Ch42 L57). Clive says Knowledge's forces are mobilising (Book 9, Ch42 L80).
- **Yaresh**: the walls are a line of massive trees with glossy black stone between (Book 9, Ch43 L33). Jason says "See you in two weeks" (Book 9, Ch43 L72) and returns to the city's defence. Hana Shavar runs the refugee camp (Book 9, Ch74 L43).
- **The messengers**: the messenger architecture is obsessed with circles (Book 9, Ch55 L3); Jes Fin Kaal, the Voice of the Will, leads them (Book 9, Ch66 L3).

## 3. Key events

- **Messenger agents in the city**: Benella presents Jason to Kaal as a threat (Book 9, Ch56 L4-20); Fal Vin Garath calls Shade "Reaper spawn" (Book 9, Ch52 L32).
- **Apocalypse beasts and worms**: Carlos suspects a heat-consuming parasite with a swarm hive mind (Book 9, Ch57 L16); "[Inexorable Doom] on [World-Taker Worm]" (Book 9, Ch58 L45); the messengers know the apocalypse beasts and use them as weapons (Book 9, Ch62).
- **Defence plans**: the two cloud palaces are reconfigured into defence bunkers (Book 9, Ch76 L88); Hana directs the clergy away from Jason's bunker (Book 9, Ch80 L7).
- **The trigger**: an Adventure Society functionary hides his anger behind an aura mask (Book 9, Ch77 L13); a naga egg is triggered in the city (see ../../06_Places/Yaresh.md).
- **The breach**: Marek Nior Vargas leads the breach force over the entertainment district (Book 9, Ch86 L16); the breach is a trap (Book 9, Ch96 L14). Eilaf Hayel commands the defence of the entertainment district (Book 9, Ch94 L3).
- **Intervention**: Raythe tells Schaat "Leave Asano be. Turn around and leave this city" (Book 9, Ch97 L74); Schaat is found dead (Book 9, Ch98 L4-5).
- **The duel**: Tera Jun Casta challenges Jason; Jason's last word stops her dead (Book 9, Ch101 L18).
- **The end**: the command council advises withdrawal (Book 9, Ch102 L9); Marek orders his people in (Book 9, Ch103 L64) and later asks Jason to wait (Book 9, Ch104 L5). In the weeks following the battle, countless tons of rubble are repurposed to rebuild the city (Book 9, Ch103 L85).

## 4. Who is where

- **Jason's team**: the convoy, Taika and Zara among them (Book 9, Ch41 L119); Rufus, Sophie and Jason as mobile responders (Book 9, Ch79 L21).
- **Allies**: Charist and Allayeth, Hana Shavar, Marcus Xenoria later, the gold-rankers of Yaresh.
- **Messengers**: Kaal, Marek, Hess Jor Nasala, Fal Vin Garath, Tera; their astral king is Vesta Carmis Zell (Book 9, Ch78 L82).
- **The wider world**: Knowledge's army gathering (Book 9, Ch42 L80); Dawn's recording supplies the real list of auxiliaries (Book 9, Ch6 L41).

## 5. Known and unknown

- **Known by the end**: a healer realises Jason is a silver-rank astral king (Book 9, Ch62 L30-34).
- **Unknown**: the messengers' real aim, which is the soul forge beneath Yaresh (revealed in Book 10; spoiler level 3).

## 6. Dangers and hooks

Besieging messengers, apocalypse beasts, world-taker worms, infiltrators in the Adventure Society. Hooks belong in ../../10_Roleplay_Support/Plot_Hooks.md.
