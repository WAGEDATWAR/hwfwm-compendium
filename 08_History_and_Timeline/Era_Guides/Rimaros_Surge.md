## 1. Summary

Jason returns to Pallimustus at the standing stones in a cyclone, just as a long-delayed monster surge begins. In the Storm Kingdom he meets its royal family, settles at Palisaros, helps defeat the Builder's invasion in the One Day War, and then faces the Order of Redeeming Light, a mining complex rescue and the birth of his astral kingdom. The era ends with the Builder withdrawing and Jason technically an astral king.

## 2. The world at the start (Book 7)

- **How he arrives**: standing stones in concentric circles on a dirt oval on Earth (Book 7, Ch1 L5); on Pallimustus the surge is "some five years overdue" and his companions gather in a recently fortified town (Book 7, Ch1 L30, L56).
- **Where the team is**: Gary and Virid are sealed in a smithy for nineteen days (Book 7, Ch2 L76); the team arrives in Rimaros by skyship, with Farrah pointing out "the city of islands" (Book 7, Ch5 L56).
- **The Storm Kingdom**: obsessed with extreme specialisation (Book 7, Ch5 L13); Emir is not allowed in it, having stolen from the royal family (Book 7, Ch3 L51). Dawn warns that invasion platforms are appearing worldwide (Book 7, Ch8 L36).

## 3. Key events

### Book 7: the surge and the One Day War

- **Registration and search**: Rimaros requires outside adventurers to register, with tests for Builder infiltrators (Book 7, Ch7 L58, L64); the Builder cult response team takes Jason in (Book 7, Ch11 L5).
- **The royals**: Zara's mourning period is nearly over (Book 7, Ch17 L111); Liara Rimaros and Vesper Rimaros handle him; Soramir tells him he sensed his arrival (Book 7, Ch18 L42).
- **Settling**: Jason's team reaches Palisaros and the cloud house (Book 7, Ch13 L75; Book 7, Ch57 L3). Humphrey names the team Team Biscuit (Book 7, Ch56 L74).
- **Purity stronghold**: the Ecumenical Council declares Purity fallen and a strike force is prepared (Book 7, Ch16 L51).
- **The expedition**: bone feasters attack the fortress town of Arcazitlan (Book 7, Ch25 L78); Jeni Kavaloa leads a mixed expedition into an astral aperture and seals it (Book 7, Ch47 L33; Book 7, Ch51 L5).
- **The Order**: Melody Jain leads the enclave with Sendira (Book 7, Ch58 L38).
- **The war**: Rick Geller reports a ground city of thick legs (Book 7, Ch59 L65-72); Jason's team is given a device to carry into the city, only one of which is the true bomb (Book 7, Ch65 L21). A Builder fortress-city appears over Livaros (Book 7, Ch64). It had been a month since the War of Four Cities, or the One Day War (Book 7, Ch66 L27).
- **The cost**: Ledev and Vesper both died saving the city (Book 7, Ch66 L36).

### Book 8: the Order and the throne

- **The Order**: Sendira, Fila and Ramona raid; the Order travels by underwater vehicle (Book 8, Ch9 L3, L11). Liara leads an expedition into the Builder complex (Book 8, Ch7 L4).
- **Casallini**: a gang is hired against Sophie (Book 8, Ch24-26; see ../../06_Places/Minor_Places/Casallini.md).
- **The mining complex**: below the sea floor; Korinne Pescos leads the rescue (Book 8, Ch30 L3; Book 8, Ch31 L33).
- **The throne and gate**: the Fundament Gate is broken down (Book 8, Ch49 L87), an incomplete spirit realm consumes the Firmament Core, and the astral throne lets avatars exist in his domains (Book 8, Ch49 L105; Book 8, Ch50 L65).
- **The Builder's deal**: Jason tells the Builder "I want them gone. Today" (Book 8, Ch54 L63); the Builder withdraws his forces (Book 8, Ch56 L35). Weeks later the Builder's forces abandon the Storm Kingdom as a battlefront (Book 8, Ch59 L6).
- **Aftermath**: for two months Jason sits in his house "telling the most powerful people in the city to bog off" (Book 8, Ch62). Dawn says he is an astral king because he has an astral throne and gate (Book 8, Ch65 L88).

## 4. Who is where

- **Jason**: Arnote and Palisaros; later astral king (Book 8, Ch65).
- **Team Biscuit**: Humphrey, Sophie, Neil, Belinda, Clive and Gary, with Rufus and Farrah (Book 7, Ch56 L74).
- **Royals**: Soramir, Liara, Vesper and Zara (Book 7, Ch17 L111; Book 7, Ch18 L42); the Storm King rises from the throne to look down at Jason (Book 7, Ch59 L86).
- **Enemies**: the Builder and his forces, the Order of Redeeming Light (Melody, Sendira, Laront), Purity loyalists.
- **Elsewhere**: Dawn advising; Travis at the largest Artifice Association workshop in Rimaros (Book 7, Ch60 L94).

## 5. Known and unknown

- **Known**: the Builder's invasion platforms (Book 7, Ch8 L36); the Church of Death's certificate says Jason has died four times (Book 7, Ch17 L104).
- **Unknown at this point**: Knowledge's plan and the Network founder (spoiler level 3).
