## 1. Summary

When transformation events release reality cores, ancient vampires wake and feed on blood infused with core energy. They take most of Europe and Russia under a queen, Elizabeth, then fight the Network and the Earth powers for two decades, with Jason cleaning their domain in Book 12 and the Earth forces storming Belgrade and Simon McKean's castle in Book 13. This guide follows the war across the series.

## 2. The war at its start (Books 5-6)

- **Reality cores as fuel**: some Cabal upper echelons work on imbuing blood with the power of reality cores (Book 5, Ch51 L127); Craig says the cores will only last so long, and "once they can't make any more of this, the old ones will go back to sleep" (Book 5, Ch55 L92).
- **Occupation**: Russia and Europe suffer massive vampiric occupation (Book 5, Ch66 L8).
- **Jason's approach**: Farrah says cutting off the supply of cores will save more lives than killing vampires now (Book 5, Ch67 L39-45).
- **Saint-Étienne**: Elizabeth, Klaus and Georges choose to join Jason's side in the second zone (Book 6, Ch40 L18); Elizabeth pairs her ritual magic with Mr North's against the vorger (Book 6, Ch45 L39-40). The vampires later abandon France (Book 6, Ch47 L5).

## 3. The long war (Book 11, Books 12-13)

- **Skirmishes**: a war of skirmishes between elite forces began in earnest shortly after Jason left (Book 11, Ch42 L38).
- **Elizabeth and the détente**: she leads the vampires and establishes a détente with the world; every major city hides vampires; bombings in Europe are answered by assassinations in Beijing, Washington, Moscow and London (Book 11, Ch68 L1488-1489). Humanity tries to kill her with a sunlight device based on Travis's design (Book 11, Ch68 L1490).
- **Jason's purge (Book 12)**: the most powerful vampires got that way by consuming blood infused with stolen reality-core power (Book 12, Ch2 L116). Jason removes the remnants and liberates a blood farm inside his domain, though one outside it is beyond his reach (Book 12, Ch5 L70-76). Nigel's team is embedded in France watching the edge of Saint-Étienne (Book 12, Ch3 L7, L13). Ketevan shows Anna the cleaned-up French domain (Book 12, Ch41 L3-9).
- **The war resumes (Book 13)**: Anna says the vampire war has resumed and military forces operate out of Asano territory again (Book 13, Ch993 L81). A symposium debates whether untested procedures on vampires violate the Saint-Étienne Summit Accords (Book 13, Ch995 L57).
- **Marie Finnegan and McKean**: investigators find Finnegan ran a tight operation with Elizabeth and McKean (Book 13, Ch979 L72); Gerling opens a box on Elizabeth's order and is speared by a red tentacle (Book 13, Ch983 L3, L15). McKean takes Gerling from the queen (Book 13, Ch1008 L33).
- **The offensives**: the offensive against Belgrade, the largest European city under full vampire control, begins with bombardment and a drop of enhanced soldiers; Prince Valdis lands with the paratroopers (Book 13, Ch1007 L3-5). Multan is infiltrated by Nigel's team (Book 13, Ch1007 L16).
- **McKean's castle**: the facility grafts vampire flesh onto blood oaks (Book 13, Ch1011 L18-44); the blood vine revives the undead (Book 13, Ch1008 L20-27). The last vampire lord is killed (Book 13, Ch1013 L34).

## 4. Who is where

- **Vampires**: Elizabeth, Klaus, Georges; Gerling as her blood slave (Book 11, Ch68 L2267); McKean's vampire-magitech abominations (Book 13, Ch1006 L60).
- **Earth powers**: the Network, the Cabal (Craig Vermillion, Boris Ketland), the UN's Office of Supernatural Affairs (Anna, then Holly Macrossan), the Australian forces, Nigel Thornton's unit.
- **Jason's side**: Jason with Team Biscuit and Farrah; the clan holds Saint-Étienne and Nitra.

## 5. Known and unknown

- **Known**: the vampires' power comes from reality-core blood and ends with a supply cut (Book 5, Ch55 L92; Book 5, Ch65 L41).
- **Open**: the war's exact casualty counts and the vampire population (Open_Questions item 19).

## 6. Dangers and hooks

Blood farms outside Jason's domain, a queen who treats humans strategically (Book 11, Ch68 L2261), vampire-flesh experiments, and a diplomatic web that wants the war to end. Hooks belong in ../../10_Roleplay_Support/Plot_Hooks.md.
