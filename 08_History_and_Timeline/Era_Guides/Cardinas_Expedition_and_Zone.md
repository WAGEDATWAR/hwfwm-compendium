## 1. Summary

A descending expedition goes beneath Yaresh to stop the messengers' device from turning the city's natural array into a soul forge. In Book 10 the allies reach the brightheart city of Cardinas, face the Church of Undeath and Hero's divine power; in Book 11 the failed forge becomes a transformation zone that Jason must claim territory by territory. It ends with Jason holding the astral throne, gate and soul forge, and with Rufus and Taika leaving for Earth.

## 2. The world at the start

- **After the battle**: Yaresh is rebuilding, the messengers are Jason's captives, and the Adventure Society is deciding whether to revoke his membership (Book 10, Ch2 L65; Book 10, Ch30 L63).
- **The device**: the messengers give Jason a device that is meant to turn the natural array into a soul forge (Book 10, Ch57 L3, L53); Clive says the messengers want this and cannot do it alone because the place corrupts them (Book 10, Ch72 L65).
- **Allies**: Musin Heath presides at the meetings (Book 10, Ch9 L38); Marcus Xenoria names Jason operations commander (Book 10, Ch60 L14); the gold-rank participants include Amos, Carlos and Hana Shavar (Book 10, Ch59 L5).

## 3. Key events

### Book 10: the descent

- **The descent**: Jason and Kaal go over the operation to descend the access shaft (Book 10, Ch17 L39). Brightheart stone-shapers guide thousands into switchback queues for the portal (Book 10, Ch76 L45).
- **Cardinas**: ruled from the Citadel of Pillars (Book 10, Ch65 L3); Beaufort heads the Builder cult's forces as an uneasy ally (Book 10, Ch65 L30). Lorenn says the citadel chamber is the site of the echo array (Book 10, Ch72 L58).
- **Undeath**: Garth Larosse, a priest of Undeath about nine feet tall (Book 10, Ch80 L42). Death appears as a stocky, middle-aged woman in a colourful dress (Book 10, Ch82 L62) and names the pact "Death Pact: The Sanctity of Death" (Book 10, Ch83 L61).
- **Hero**: Gary greets "Lord Hero" (Book 10, Ch86 L19); a divine forge golem stands over twice Gary's height (Book 10, Ch87 L50). Gary and the avatar stare at each other like kaiju (Book 10, Ch91 L46).
- **The tree**: the birthing tree is the result of a failed attempt to create a soul forge (Book 10, Ch80 L29). Clive and Farrah overlook the ring of ritual circles round the tree (Book 10, Ch92 L14).

### Book 11: the zone

- **A third zone**: Jason's third transformation zone, unified by claiming all non-central territories (Book 11, Ch1 L15, L39). A replica of Casselton Beach occupies one (Book 11, Ch5 L8).
- **Battles**: the Undeath priests fall quickly (Book 11, Ch4 L21); Garth throws off his robes (Book 11, Ch24-26); Jason fights him in a warped castle (Book 11, Ch30-31).
- **Factions**: each faction has Builder cultists, Undeath priests or brighthearts leading messengers (Book 11, Ch9 L108). Lorus Durrum tries to reconcile the Builder cult and the brighthearts (Book 11, Ch10 L12). Territory holding follows Lorenn's rules (Book 11, Ch17 L27).
- **Nik**: a four-foot humanoid rabbit Jason brings into being when his soul realm becomes a zone (Book 11, Ch61 L16); he is "six months old" in the zone (Book 11, Ch50 L15) and calls the structure a weather machine, not a satellite weapon (Book 11, Ch50 L11).
- **Boris**: reveals himself as Unorthodoxy and tells the astral-king story (Book 11, Ch24 L85; Book 11, Ch25 L51); he says the process to become an astral king takes years (Book 11, Ch43 L131).
- **Gary**: drank from the Cup of Heroes; Jason offers to replace the divine power (Book 11, Ch48 L22; Book 11, Ch48 L30).
- **Reintegration**: Jason separates the natural array, the soul forge and the tree and claims the forge (Book 11, Ch60 L43-46; Book 11, Ch61 L19-21). The forest city has a soul of its own, like Nik (Book 11, Ch61 L16). Lorenn gathers her people in the new brightheart home (Book 11, Ch62 L89).
- **The Cosmic Throne**: Velius says the throne must be left empty as a neutral arbiter (Book 11, Ch65 L8).
- **Departure**: Boris can take two people to Earth, Taika among them (Book 11, Ch66 L89).

## 4. Who is where

- **Jason** in the zone, leaving his body in a cloud chair during soul battles (Book 11, Ch35 L39).
- **The team**: Humphrey, Sophie, Clive, Belinda, Neil, Gary, Rufus, Taika and Farrah; Zara, Dustin, Korinne and Moon's Edge (Book 11, Ch21 L9; Book 11, Ch32 L75).
- **Local forces**: Lorenn and the brighthearts; Miriam Vance as co-commander (Book 11, Ch19 L56).
- **Enemies**: the Church of Undeath, Builder cultists, messengers.
- **Earth**: the clan holds two domains, Saint-Étienne and near Nitra (Book 11, Ch36 L3).

## 5. Known and unknown

- **Known**: the soul forge is the third part of an astral kingdom (Book 8, Ch65 L94); Boris is Unorthodoxy (Book 11, Ch24 L85).
- **Unknown**: how long the zone lasted (see Open_Questions on the timing, Book 11 Part II), and what Gary's ultimate bargain with Hero is.
