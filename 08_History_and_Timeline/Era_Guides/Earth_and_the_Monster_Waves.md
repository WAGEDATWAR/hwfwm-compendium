## 1. Summary

Jason wakes on Earth in early June, a year and a half after he left (Book 4, Ch3 L112). Earth's magic is rising; the Network, the Cabal and the Engineers of Ascension fight for influence, monsters arrive in waves, vampires rise, and Jason builds a family compound, a clan and his first Earth domains. The era ends with Jason leaving Earth, in a warm French autumn (Book 6, Ch47 L155).

## 2. The world at the start (Book 4)

- **Where Jason is**: a maternity theatre in an abandoned hospital at Casselton West, with Shade's bodies merged into a car (Book 4, Ch1 L38; Book 4, Ch3 L80). He is bronze.
- **What has changed**: a "gas explosion" in his Melbourne building and the army running "terrorist response exercises" cover the magic (Book 4, Ch4 L87, L94). Earth's magic desolation slows his recovery (Book 4, Ch2 L8).
- **The factions**: the Network, a Cabal front man in Sydney, and the Engineers of Ascension, whom Vermillion calls those who came to magic from outside the normal channels (Book 4, Ch11 L72). The EOA has seized Perth and Melbourne (Book 4, Ch9 L76).

## 3. Key events

### Book 4: return and the family

- **Contact**: Hiro at the Stone Wall bar in Kings Cross (Book 4, Ch4 L8); Anna Tilden and Ketevan Arziani of the Network (Book 4, Ch5; Book 4, Ch21). Jason's coins left on Anna's counter are authentic category-one coins marked PRODUCT OF JASON (Book 4, Ch22 L83-85).
- **The Starlight Rider**: the media name for his cloaked figure (Book 4, Ch15 L12).
- **Family**: he says "Hello, Eri" (Book 4, Ch26 L59), reveals magic to Emi (Book 4, Ch28 L139), and tells Kaito he has waited six years to say what he says (Book 4, Ch27 L97).
- **The Saint-Étienne rescue**: a French branch hides the Dimensional Fortress, an incursion space found in 1948 (Book 4, Ch51 L30); Jason frees Farrah from it (Book 4, Ch53; Book 4, Ch55 L8).
- **Dawn's explanation**: the link between worlds has been siphoning magic from Pallimustus for centuries, and Purity has struck a bargain with the Builder (Book 4, Ch68 L59, L139).

### Book 5: the waves and the compound

- **Setting**: Jason has been home nine months and bronze for a year (Book 5, Ch3 L170). The family compound went up in six months (Book 5, Ch5 L59). The village committee becomes a formal body under Erika's direction (Book 5, Ch13).
- **The waves**: the first recorded monster manifestation is in Angola (Book 5, Ch13 L35). Broken Hill is hit; the safe-zone claim is blamed (Book 5, Ch17 L11, L28). Jason's broadcast: "If you want to find heroes, go digging through the rubble" (Book 5, Ch19 L26).
- **Japan**: the old Asano clan's compound in Ashiya, near Kobe (Book 5, Ch34 L66), and Noriko's challenge (Book 5, Ch36-37).
- **Transformation events**: Kostroma first (Book 5, Ch44 L48); reality cores become the prize (Book 5, Ch45 L11; see ../../09_Items_and_Artefacts/Reality_and_Genesis_Cores.md).
- **Vampires**: ancient vampires rise using blood infused with reality-core energy (Book 5, Ch55 L92; Book 5, Ch51 L127). The Berlin attack kills Kaito, Asya and Greg (Book 5, Ch51 L40).
- **Silver**: Jason ranks to silver (Book 5, Ch31 L6).

### Book 6: the zones

- **Nitra**: a dome appears; Jason opens a door and claims territories, renaming the first Arrival Pagoda (Book 6, Ch1 L4; Book 6, Ch5 L41). 1,327 stable genesis cores are consumed in his apotheosis (Book 6, Ch17 L13).
- **Shako**: a red-haired, freckled man with bright green eyes kills Jason (Book 6, Ch17 L37); Dawn tells him he has broken the compact (Book 6, Ch18 L6).
- **Network schism**: Jason says the Slovak government caused the violence when the Network split (Book 6, Ch20 L81). Gerling goes rogue (Book 6, Ch26 L63).
- **Travis Noble** joins; the plan for a stolen backpack nuke (Book 6, Ch27 L51; Book 6, Ch28 L36).
- **Saint-Étienne**: a second zone, claimed with vampires fighting alongside (Book 6, Ch44-46); it becomes a spirit domain encompassing most of the city (Book 6, Ch47 L3).
- **Leaving**: he says his farewells in France on a warm autumn day (Book 6, Ch47 L155).

## 4. Who is where

- **Jason** on Earth, bronze then silver; **Farrah** freed and beside him; **Dawn** advising; **Shade** as car and body.
- **Family**: Ken, Cheryl, Erika, Ian, Emi, Kaito and Amy; Yumi and Hiro; later the whole clan at Asano Village (Book 5, Ch52 L68).
- **The Network** (Anna Tilden, Keith, Mr North, Gerling, Nigel Thornton's teams), **the Cabal** (Craig Vermillion, Boris), **the EOA** (the Four Cardinals; Book 5, Ch3 L5).
- **Pallimustus friends**: Rufus, Gary, Humphrey, Sophie, Neil, Belinda and Clive are not on Earth in this era; Gary says he had not seen Jason in two years (Book 6, Ch47 L941).

## 5. Known and unknown

- **Known**: magic is on Earth and rising; the link between worlds was tampered with by an outworlder who brought a door (Book 5, Ch22 L48); Jason can enter transformation events.
- **Unknown at this point**: the Network founder's identity and Knowledge's role (spoiler level 3).

## 6. Dangers and hooks

Proto-spaces opening in towns, vampire occupation of Europe and Russia (Book 5, Ch66 L8), political factions that fight over cores, and the family's safety. Hooks belong in ../../10_Roleplay_Support/Plot_Hooks.md.
