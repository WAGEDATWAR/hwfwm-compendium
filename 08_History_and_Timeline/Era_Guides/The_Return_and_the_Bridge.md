## 1. Summary

After roughly fifteen years away, Jason returns to Pallimustus' cities, destroys Boko by accident of assassination, meets the Council of Kings, triggers the System's withdrawal from regions that keep indenture, and then sails with his friends to Earth to build the bridge. In 2038 he tells the world about the great astral beings and Knowledge's plan, ends the vampire war with his allies, and stands at the bridge anchor site.

## 2. The world at the start (Book 12)

- **How long**: Jason tells those who waited "almost twenty years" (Book 12, Ch13 L36) and says he "only missed fifteen years or so" (Book 12, Ch21 L61). A letter opens the book: friends write that everyone is getting super-strong and waiting for him (Book 12, Ch0 L19).
- **What changed**: the System appeared (Book 12, Ch1 L44-54); Earth's magic is known to the public, because "less than fifteen years ago, no one knew magic existed" (Book 12, Ch2 L107).
- **The call**: Clive tells friends "The portal to Jason's soul realm has opened up again. Finish whatever business you're on and make your way to Yaresh" (Book 12, Ch5 L89).

## 3. Key events

### Book 12: Pallimustus

- **Reunions**: Rexion and Gary's statue (Book 12, Ch7 L8); Marla, with hair and eyes like molten steel (Book 12, Ch11 L6); Rick's team arrives in a brothel fire (Book 12, Ch15 L3); the Valdis duel in Yaresh's mirage chamber (Book 12, Ch17-19).
- **Greenstone and Boko**: Jason reaches Greenstone quietly days before the official arrival (Book 12, Ch27 L12). Boko is destroyed after his assassination; massive portals disgorge an army, and the ghost fire phoenix arcs through the messenger host (Book 12, Ch33 L4, L16). Jamis Fran Muskar apologises (Book 12, Ch35 L6-11).
- **New homes**: Jason names the oasis city New Water (Book 12, Ch39 L61).
- **The System**: the mob outside his Cyrion palace has been there since the System went dark (Book 12, Ch51 L3); see ../../02_Cosmology_and_Powers/The_System.md.
- **The plan to Earth**: a secret council of Earth's powers makes a deal to bring outsiders to remove Jason (Book 12, Ch59 L60-72); Jason explains his plan, which depends on a favour owed by the World-Phoenix (Book 12, Ch60 L6-10).
- **The pirates**: a pirate captain, Jota Withers, arrives in a dimension ship (Book 12, Ch57 L3; Book 12, Ch63 L16-19); Team Biscuit and Farrah fight in stage two of Jason's plan (Book 12, Ch66 L5-11).

### Book 13: Earth, 2038

- **The visit**: the family reunion and Emi's cousins (Book 13, Ch950 L27-41); a formal expedition tours Jason's astral kingdom (Book 13, Ch962 L5).
- **Diplomacy**: Li Mei and the international committee plan the handoff (Book 13, Ch960 L16); Boris Ketland openly leads the Cabal (Book 13, Ch1015 L49).
- **The vampire war and McKean**: see Earth_Vampire_War.md.
- **The lecture**: Jason tells the gathering about the great astral beings, the Cosmic Throne, outworlders and Knowledge's plan (Book 13, Ch1017 L4-47). He describes the long process by which the original Builder was replaced (Book 13, Ch1021 L4).
- **The bridge anchor**: a crowd gathers at the bridge anchor site in Australia, where no trace remains of the small town that stood there (Book 13, Ch1023 L3).

## 4. Who is where

- **Team Biscuit and friends**: Humphrey, Sophie, Neil, Belinda, Clive, Gary, Rufus, Farrah, Taika, Zara, Travis and Gabrielle, with Nik.

## 5. Known and unknown

- **Known**: the System is Jason's, and Knowledge's plan is laid out in Book 13, Ch1017.
- **Unknown at the start**: who is behind the secret council of Earth's powers; how the bridge will actually work once built.
