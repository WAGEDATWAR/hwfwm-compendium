## 1. Summary

A soul forge is the third part, with the astral throne and astral gate, of a full astral kingdom; it is used for soul engineering. Every astral king has one except Jason until Book 11. The messengers' attempt to turn the Cardinas natural array into one corrupts the array into a birthing tree; Jason separates the three and claims the forge in his own soul realm.

## 2. What it is

- **The third element**: Dawn tells Jason that a space like his needs an astral throne and an astral gate and a third thing, the soul forge, "for soul engineering" (Book 8, Ch65 L88-94).
- **Soul engineering**: an ethically fraught crafting profession (Book 10, Ch57 L46). Jason is put off by "souls being used as crafting mats" (Book 8, Ch65 L95).
- **Every astral king has one**: "every astral king but one" lacks it (Book 10, Ch57 L42).
- **Form unknown**: Jason does not know if it is a physical thing, a loose matrix or a purely spiritual construct; it might be a tree (Book 10, Ch76 L40-41).
- **What an astral kingdom without one lacks**: Marek thinks Jason's astral kingdom is unstable because it lacks a forge (Book 10, Ch1 L52-55).

## 3. The messengers' attempt

- **The goal**: the messengers want to turn the natural array beneath Yaresh into a soul forge (Book 10, Ch72 L65-66).
- **Why Kaal wanted it**: a soul forge would explain the resources she spent (Book 10, Ch1 L53); Boris says Kaal believed Destruction wanted a massive explosion once the messengers claimed it (Book 10, Ch81 L36).
- **The result**: the failed attempt produced a corrupted birthing tree: "the result of a failed attempt to create a soul forge" (Book 10, Ch80 L29). The messengers tried to force the whole array to stay together instead of separating a part (Book 11, Ch56 L20).

## 4. Jason's use

- **The goal**: he wants the forge for himself, partly to use a gift from the Healer that only works once he has one (Book 10, Ch57 L48).
- **Why messengers failed**: they treated it as theirs and applied messenger sensibilities to it (Book 11, Ch43 L97).
- **The extraction**: he separates the natural array, the forge and the tree, and puts each in its proper place (Book 11, Ch60 L45). The array is pushed out of the soul realm; the forge stays within it (Book 11, Ch60 L43).
- **Result**: the astral throne, astral gate and soul forge set him on the path to becoming an astral king and his soul realm begins to break down (Book 11, Ch61 L21).
