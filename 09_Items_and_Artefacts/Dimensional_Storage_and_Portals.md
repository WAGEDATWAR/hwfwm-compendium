## 1. Summary

How things and people are carried across a world without carts or roads: storage spaces that hold a person's goods, dimensional bags that expand them, and portal abilities that move people between places they have seen. Jason's own inventory and shadow gates are the best-documented examples, and the rest of the world treats both as everyday professional tools with hard limits.

## 2. Facts

- **Jason's inventory** is "a video-game inventory only Jason could see", unlike Clive's storage, which is opened by making a circle of floating runes with a portal inside (Book 2, Ch7 L47).
- **Dimensional bags** are items with a rank. They add slots to an inventory in proportion to their rank, and at iron rank one bag slot is available and holds only iron-rank bags (Book 1, Ch76 L43, L49). Bags come in novelty shapes, such as a pair of boots (Book 1, Ch76 L58).
- **Storage as a profession**: adventurers carry their possessions in dimensional bags or dimensional space abilities and travel light (Book 2, Ch32 L38). Some portal workers "aren't even adventurers and do storage space transport for a living" (Book 7, Ch20 L23).
- **Loot goes straight in**: looted coins appear in the storage abilities of Clive, Humphrey and Jason (Book 2, Ch42 L79), and Jason keeps a ledger of how much belongs to whom (Book 2, Ch19 L60).
- **Storage can be looted**: Jason could have taken the items from Farrah's storage space by looting her body like a monster, and neither man suggested such a defilement (Book 1, Ch112 L17).
- **Limits on contents**: it is "almost impossible to place living material inside dimensional storage", with only a few carefully chosen plants viable (Book 2, Ch24 L13).
- **Portal-type abilities**: they open only to places the user has visited before, along with other limits (Book 1, Ch113 L58). A portal fails if the destination has changed enough that the visualisation is wrong; redecorating is fine, demolishing the building is not (Book 3, Ch39 L37).
- **Jason's shadow teleport**: the iron-rank effect of Path of Shadows is "Teleport using shadows as a portal. You must be able to see the destination shadow" (Book 1, Ch22 L120).
- **Archways**: the portal arches of the Order of the Reaper's astral space are built on the same ability (Book 3, Ch7 L85), and the shadow gates there look like Jason's own (Book 3, Ch29 L97). Travelling through a dark aperture feels exactly like his shadow teleport (Book 2, Ch43 L24).
- **Stability**: a portal may not stay stable long, so travellers follow quickly (Book 3, Ch33 L62).

## 3. Later developments (spoilers)

<!-- level:2 -->
- **Capacity and cooldown**: portalling a silver-ranker uses all of Jason's portal's capacity, so the others wait out a ten-minute cooldown (Book 5, Ch24 L135).
- **Magically sound destinations**: the destination must be sound, while the start point can be shakier because the user is present and the ability adapts through the user's senses (Book 8, Ch39 L16).
- **Beacons and range**: some portal abilities can target places never visited if an aura-based marker is there to home in on (Book 8, Ch16 L90). Jason's ability to skip the visit works at less than a tenth of his normal portal range (Book 8, Ch26 L5).
- **The Builder cult's network**: the cult and the Church of Purity moved about through a transportation network of portal arches (Book 6, Ch47 L640), which the Magic Society cracked and could track (Book 7, Ch16 L73).
- **Contract use**: a contract in the Storm Kingdom would deploy Jason's team widely to give them many portal destinations for supply runs and rapid response (Book 7, Ch58 L10). Portal specialists of gold rank are in demand (Book 7, Ch47 L62).

- **Gold-rank travel**: with Sophie's time acceleration the team arrives at teleport-like speed without the disorientation of a real teleport, and they are "long past throwing up like first time portal travellers" (Book 12, Ch66 L6).

## 4. Using this in a story

Treat storage as a luggage problem solved at a cost: anything alive is awkward, and a storage space holds what is lost with its owner if the owner can be looted. Treat portals as a map problem: the party can only be where someone has been, and a changed destination fails the portal. A portal specialist is a job, not just a power, and the scarcity of gold-rank specialists is a reason for delays in any story that wants them.
