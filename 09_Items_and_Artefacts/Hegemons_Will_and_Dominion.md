## 1. Summary

The reforged form of Jason's sword Dread Salvation and the scabbard that was made with it. Hegemon's Will is the weapon and Hegemon's Dominion is the scabbard; the game treats them as one item. The scabbard stores afflictions that land on Jason and turns them against the next enemy he cuts. Soul_Bound_Items_and_Regalia.md covers where the sword came from; this file covers what the two pieces do.

## 2. Facts

- **Hegemon's Dominion**: "silver rank [growth], legendary", "the scabbard to Hegemon's Will" and "the embodiment of hegemonic control, representing the hegemon's mastery of his domain" (Book 7, Ch54 L118-119).
- **Bound to Jason**: it cannot be used by anyone else, shares his ability to ignore rank disparity, and is "treated as the same item" as the sword for all magical effects (Book 7, Ch54 L120).
- **What the scabbard does**: afflictions affecting the wielder are periodically moved to it, which removes their effects from him but is not a cleansing effect. The rate depends on the effects' rank relative to the wielder; suppressing the originator's aura speeds the transfer and being aura suppressed slows it. If the sword is sheathed, all afflictions pass to the weapon and affect the next enemy struck. Anything the target resists or is immune to is negated and cannot be passed to another target (Book 7, Ch54 L121).
- **Growth**: its growth is no longer capped by rank, but the upgrade materials are ones Jason has never heard of and are presumably gold rank (Book 7, Ch54 L123).
- **Gary's account**: the crafting was strange and "it's almost like the sword knew how it wanted to be reforged" (Book 7, Ch54 L124). Jason says that after a recent lost fight the scabbard's ability is exactly what he needed (Book 7, Ch54 L125).
- **Hegemon's Will**: "silver rank [growth], legendary", "reforged with a renewed sense of purpose" with the help of a grandmaster craftsman, bound to Jason, and sharing his ability to ignore rank disparity (Book 7, Ch54 L128-130).
- **Invoking conjured weapons**: the wielder may invoke all effects of one conjured weapon into the blade at the weapon's normal conjuring cost, one at a time, and add extra damage for an ongoing mana cost scaled by aura strength (Book 7, Ch54 L131-132). Available invocations are Ruin, the Blade of Tribulation (low cost; the item screen lists corrosive damage and Corrosion, Book 7, Ch54 L133-135) and Penitent, the Blade of Sacrifice (moderate cost; disruptive-force damage, Book 7, Ch54 L136-138). The invocation message for Ruin says necrotic damage will be inflicted, not corrosive (Book 7, Ch54 L146).
- **Mercy and Benevolence**: the wielder may cleanse the holy afflictions his own abilities and soul-bound items inflicted on a touched enemy. Each cleansed affliction gives the enemy an instance of Hegemon's Mercy and the wielder an instance of Benevolent Hegemon (Book 7, Ch54 L139). Hegemon's Mercy is a holy affliction that suppresses all magical abilities and drops off rapidly outside the wielder's aura; Benevolent Hegemon strengthens overlapping allied auras (Book 7, Ch54 L142-143).
- **Set effect**: as part of the Regalia, enemies struck are drained of mana and inflicted with Hegemon's Tribute, and the wielder gains Benevolent Hegemon (Book 7, Ch54 L140, L144).
- **Visible effect**: the white sigils on the blade glow life-force red for Ruin and blue for Penitent (Book 7, Ch54 L145-149).
- **In use, Book 8**: Hegemon's Will prevents a conjuration and gains all the properties of the conjured weapon (Book 8, Ch31 L6); an affliction that cannot be resisted is moved into the scabbard, and returning the sword to it makes the next attack inflict that affliction (Book 8, Ch34 L63).

## 3. Later developments (spoilers)

- **Reforged as relics**: Gary shoves the sword and scabbard into a golden blaze and both items change from legendary to relic. Relics need transcendent power and most of their abilities stay sealed until Jason gains it (Book 11, Ch68 L103-106).
- **Mercy at work on Earth**: touching a captured pirate with the sword absorbs her holy afflictions and inflicts an instance of Hegemon's Mercy for each, with a matching Benevolent Hegemon for Jason (Book 12, Ch66 L63-66). Jason calls the sword the first and last gift he got from Gary (Book 12, Ch66 L67).

## 4. Using this in a story

Treat the two pieces as a pair: the sword delivers, the scabbard stores. A sheathed blade is a loaded one. The scabbard converts Jason's pain into the next victim's problem, so a fight against afflictions suits him better than a fight against raw damage. Resisted or immune effects do not pass on, which is the limit a clever opponent can use. When the sword's damage type matters, quote the screen or the message rather than choosing between them.
