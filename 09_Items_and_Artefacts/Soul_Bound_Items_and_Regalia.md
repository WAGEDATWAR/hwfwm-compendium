## 1. Summary

Jason's three soul-bound growth items, joined in Book 6 into the Regalia of the Dark Hegemon by the Soul-Imprinting Triune, plus the Robes of the Astral Verdict.

## 2. Facts

- **Dread Salvation**: a sword "crafted with gratitude, in hope it would be of the greatest use in the moment of greatest need"; "iron rank [growth], legendary"; when an affliction special attack meets a physical immunity it gains Stone Cutter, and for a magical immunity Spell Breaker (Book 1, Ch56 L49-52). Gary made it, as a gift (Book 1, Ch56 L47).
- **Dread Salvation in Book 6**: bronze rank, bound to Jason and sharing his ability to ignore rank disparity; it can be imbued with aura for damage at an ongoing mana cost; "The original creator must demonstrate his growth and reforge the weapon" (Book 6, Ch4 L72-77). The window says it "must be reforged by the original craftsperson in order to advance further" (Book 6, Ch4 L82). The reforged sword is named Hegemon's Will on its item screen, with its scabbard Hegemon's Dominion treated as the same item; Gary gives it to Jason in Book 7 (Book 7, Ch54 L108-120). The reforging by Gary and Virid is Book 7, Ch2 L73-74.
- **Cloud Flask**: iron rank growth item that Jason binds in Book 2; it creates cloud buildings and vehicles, with forms limited by rank (Book 2, Ch74 L34-46). By Book 6 it is silver rank, with the cloud house at iron, cloud vehicle at bronze and cloud palace at silver (Book 6, Ch4 L42-50). Its maker is "some unknown diamond-rank craftsman" (Book 6, Ch4 L40).
- **Amulet of the Dark Guardian**: silver rank growth item, the last item produced by his old quest system, a reproduction of his personal crest on an obsidian chain (Book 6, Ch4 L29-39). Its boon Guardian's Blessing absorbs damage per affliction applied and can be given to another person by touch (Book 6, Ch4 L34-37).
- **Soul-Imprinting Triune**: Jason receives it with a hoard of spirit coins in Book 5 (Book 5, Ch29 L77-80); it turns three soul-bound growth items into a set when all three are on his person (Book 6, Ch4 L6-8).
- **Regalia of the Dark Hegemon**: the set bonuses are Hegemon's Authority from the amulet (allies in his aura resist aura suppression, and he can spend instances to strengthen his own suppression), a mist shroud from the cloud flask, and mana drain with Hegemon's Tribute from the sword (Book 6, Ch4 L35-38, L47, L79-80).
- **Robes of the Astral Verdict**: bronze rank, rare cloth armour for summoners that boosts dimension spells, summon damage reduction and disruptive-force resistance (Book 1, Ch7 L17-21).

## 3. Using this in a story

Keep the three Regalia items together on his person; losing any one breaks the set. The Regalia window states that Dread Salvation must be reforged to advance (Book 6, Ch4 L82); the reforging happens in Book 7 (Book 7, Ch2 L73-74; Book 7, Ch54 L108-120).
