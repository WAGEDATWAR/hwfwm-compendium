## 1. Summary

Transcendent-rank items that mark the favour or authority of great astral beings and gods: astral blessing tokens, divine awakening stones, the Fundamental Realm Authority Token and the Cup of Heroes.

## 2. Facts

- **Astral blessing tokens**: Clive says they are "something great astral beings give out to bestow blessings, as signs of approval"; they trigger racial gift evolutions, and are harmless, unlike star seeds (Book 2, Ch63 L45-47).
- **World-Phoenix Token**: Jason finds it in his starting inventory, "transcendent rank, legendary", "Uses remaining: 1/1" with unreadable effects (Book 1, Ch1 L103-107). It is consumed in Book 3 (Book 3, Ch69 L108).
- **Reaper Token**: an obsidian square engraved with a silver scythe, "transcendent rank, legendary" (Book 2, Ch63 L37-38). Jason takes the Scythe of the Reaper by placing his inventory window behind it, and the token is added (Book 2, Ch60 L173-179). It is melded into his body with the awakening stone of the Reaper in Clive's ritual and consumed (Book 2, Ch63 L65-82).
- **Knowledge's token**: a small stone square like the World-Phoenix Token but washed-out blue, offered with two awakening stones (Book 2, Ch13 L57); Jason is told it would take him home and wonders whether to trust it (Book 2, Ch73 L58).
- **Divine Awakening Stone of Inevitability**: "transcendent rank, epic", crafted by a god to bestow a specific aura power (Book 2, Ch13 L60). The stone's effect line names the ability [Inescapable Doom] (Book 2, Ch13 L62) but its ability description names [Inevitable Demise] (Book 2, Ch13 L66); the compendium uses Inevitable Demise and treats the other as a variant, since no later list shows either. The Soul-Purgation Tablet (aura) removes an existing aura essence ability and cannot be forcibly used on another (Book 2, Ch13 L76-78).
- **Fundamental Realm Authority Token**: "transcendent rank, unavailable", bound to Jason and usable only by him and Zithis Carrow Vayel; it lets the wielder open gates to the fundamental realm of any physical reality (Book 8, Ch50 L87-91). Jason looted it from the destroyed artefacts (Book 8, Ch50 L94-95). Dawn tells him "You can't have this" and that he will need to give it back (Book 8, Ch51 L68-70).
- **Firmamental Bridge Anchor**: the other looted item, "transcendent rank, legendary", with unreadable effects and one use (Book 8, Ch50 L97-100).
- **Cup of Heroes**: the god of heroes' offer of divine power; Gary "has drunk from the Cup of Heroes" and the window warns the divine power will work to leave his soul and rejoin the god (Book 10, Ch86 L59-61).

## 3. Using this in a story

These items are leverage between beings, not loot; their descriptions should stay incomplete until the owner is strong enough to read them.
