## 1. Summary

The Builder's astral door, the Fundament Gate that Jason absorbs, and how they let him work in the fundamental realm.

## 2. Facts

- **The door**: Mr North tells Jason "It's not a literal door... It's an astral construct with the power to manifest in physical reality in the form of a portal", and that Jason must absorb it so it cannot be taken from him (Book 5, Ch41 L24-26). Jason notes "This door is an object of the Builder" (Book 5, Ch41 L28).
- **The Fundament Gate**: a ring of rough marble standing stones in a dell, "transcendent rank, legendary", whose window lets Jason incorporate it into his Spirit Vault, purging the Builder's influence (Book 5, Ch41 L52-62). It improves his sense of Builder items and adds to Path of Shadows the ability to open a portal to the Fundamental Realm (Book 5, Ch41 L62-64).
- **Consequences**: Jason used the Builder's door when he flooded himself with magic and later "accidentally trashed it for parts" (Book 8, Ch57 L28, L64-67); "the magic of the Builder's door that Jason had absorbed" is distinct from the soul-attack trick he learned from the star seed (Book 7, Ch50 L8).
- **Builder's view**: in Book 6 Shako says that without the Fundament Gate the Builder forged, Jason could not have remade the zone (Book 6, Ch18 L11, L30-36), and Dawn asks for the designs so the World-Phoenix can create a complementary item to anchor the bridge (Book 6, Ch18 L39, L124).
- **Later items**: the Fundamental Realm Authority Token and the Firmamental Bridge Anchor are looted from the destroyed artefacts (Book 8, Ch50 L94-100); see `Tokens_Stones_and_Relics.md`.

## 3. Using this in a story

Treat the door as borrowed authority: powerful, traceable to the Builder and always one dispute away from being taken back.
