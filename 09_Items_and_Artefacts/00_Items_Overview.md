## 1. Summary

How the story's items are described by the System: rank, rarity, kind, bound status and effect lines, and the main named items in Jason's possession.

## 2. Facts

- **Item windows**: the System shows each item with a rank and rarity in the form "Item: [Dread Salvation] (iron rank [growth], legendary)", then a description, a kind in brackets and effect lines (Book 1, Ch56 L49-52). Unidentifiable items show question marks, such as the "transcendent rank, legendary" World-Phoenix Token with "???" effects (Book 1, Ch1 L103-107).
- **Growth items** have a rank that can rise; soul-bound growth items are rare, Clive says, and the advancement requirements are "usually quite prohibitive" (Book 2, Ch74 L45-46).
- **Bound items** "cannot be used by anyone else" (Book 2, Ch74 L36; Book 6, Ch4 L33).
- **Currency items** are covered in `../04_Peoples_and_Cultures/Economy_and_Money.md`; essences and awakening stones in `../03_Magic_and_Adventuring/Essences.md` and `../03_Magic_and_Adventuring/Awakening_Stones.md`.
- **Rarities seen**: common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary, and "unavailable" for the Fundamental Realm Authority Token (Book 8, Ch50 L87).
- **Tiers of power**: ordinary items are ranked iron to gold; astral blessing tokens and divine awakening stones are "transcendent rank" (Book 2, Ch63 L38; Book 2, Ch13 L60).

## 3. Using this in a story

Describe items the way the System does, in rank, rarity and bound status, and keep the question-marked transcendent items vague until a character earns the power to read them.
