## 1. Summary

Emir's cloud ship and palace, and Jason's own cloud houses, yachts and palaces made from his Cloud Flask.

## 2. Facts

- **Emir's cloud ship**: it arrives at the Greenstone dock and a walkway of cloud connects to the shore as a hole opens in its side (Book 1, Ch101 L64). Moored, it morphs over about ten minutes into a palace of clouds floating on the water (Book 1, Ch101 L103).
- **Emir's cloud palace**: floating just offshore on the north side of the Island, "fully exposed to the waves and currents, yet remained as immovable as solid ground", with wings and towers in blue, purple, orange and gold (Book 1, Ch108 L3). Walls, floors and ceilings glow with absorbed sunlight the palace stores and distributes, and the floors have a bouncy-castle spring (Book 2, Ch1 L3).
- **Low-magic limits**: out of Greenstone the ship's full functionality is restored when Emir's cloud ship floats above Kurdansk (Book 6, Ch47 L459).
- **Team base**: Jason's team move into Emir's palace after the contest (Book 2, Ch8 L168).
- **Jason's Cloud Flask**: it creates buildings and vehicles of cloud, with forms limited by rank, iron cloud houses, bronze cloud vehicles and silver cloud palaces (Book 6, Ch4 L42-50); items inside a returned construct are stored in a dimensional space until another is formed (Book 2, Ch74 L43).
- **Disguised constructs**: in Venice his family and Dawn stay in a cloud palace disguised as a yacht (Book 6, Ch4 L53; Book 6, Ch10 L34).
- **Asano Village**: Farrah prepares a place for Jason's cloud house at the end of a glass tunnel along the seafloor, two kilometres out, reached by a tramway from the compound and sealed by a wall of water, so the house can run underwater (Book 5, Ch5 L106-118); the text checked does not show the house later sitting there.
- **Uses later**: Hana Shavar runs relief operations from Jason's cloud palace after the messenger attack (Book 10, Ch3 L29); in Cyrion he sits meditating in his cloud palace on the lake (Book 12, Ch48 L26).

## 3. Using this in a story

A cloud palace is both home and statement; Emir's flair is the model for how much a palace can show off.
