## 1. Summary

Jason's combat robes are the flowing, tailored armour that gives him his dark, shadowy look. The first is the Trap Weaver Battle Robe, bought from Gilbert Bertinelli in Book 1; it is replaced at each rank by a new robe made by Gilbert from monster materials Jason supplies. The robes are armour in a robe's cut, stored in his inventory and swapped on by the outfits tab.

## 2. The Trap Weaver Battle Robe (iron rank)

- **Where it comes from**: Gilbert sells it, with Gary haggling the price, in bronze coins, which is unusual for iron-rank equipment (Book 1, Ch61 L92-114). It is made from trap weaver silk, alchemically treated, with leather panels, "the magic integrated right down to the weaving pattern of the cloth" (Book 1, Ch61 L92).
- **Look**: "almost a robe", in shifting shades of dark grey, with black leather panels over the torso, arms and legs and flowing cloth draped over it; "a strange combination of tactical armour from Jason's world and some kind of wizard robe" (Book 1, Ch61 L93). The drape breaks up the body's lines in the dark (Book 1, Ch61 L99).
- **Item entry**: "[Trap Weaver Battle Robe] (iron rank, epic)", full body armour of silk and leather (Book 1, Ch61 L102-103).
- **Effects**: increased resistance to damage, strong against cutting and piercing but weaker against blunt; repairs damage over time, though extensive damage may need external repair; absorbs blood so it leaves no trail; resists bleed and poison; resists adhesives; adapts its fit to the wearer (Book 1, Ch61 L104-109).
- **Source monster**: trap weavers are iron-rank monsters "responsible for more iron-rank adventurer deaths than any other monster in the Greenstone region" (Book 1, Ch58 L40). Jason later offers Sophie the same silk through his tailor (Book 2, Ch21 L12).

## 3. Bronze: the Dark Hydra Robe

Jason asks about the next robe once he nears bronze (Book 3, Ch2 L75). Gilbert makes it from umbral mountain snake leather Jason supplied, marsh hydra leather and a lining of deep wyrm silk (Book 3, Ch32 L45-46). The entry reads "[Dark Hydra Robe] (bronze rank, epic)" (Book 3, Ch32 L48-49). It is darker than the old robe: mostly scaled matte black with grey trim, keeping the sleek draping lines (Book 3, Ch32 L45). Jason wears it as his bronze-rank battle robe (Book 3, Ch48 L10).

## 4. Later robes

- Jason's silver-era robe is again custom-made by Gilbert; in Book 5 its magic has died after Jason pushed it close to destruction many times (Book 5, Ch30 L58).
- The robes change with Jason's ranks; the old iron-rank robe had much more grey than the black bronze one (Book 4, Ch25 L80).

## 5. How they work in play

- **Outfits tab**: Jason slots armour into an outfit in his inventory and taps equip; smoke surrounds him and the clothes swap (Book 1, Ch61 L115; Book 2, Ch14 L58).
- **Self-repair**: a robe recovers to pristine condition in about four days, and it is cleaned with the rest of the wearer (Book 3, Ch14 L27-29).
- **Recognised as adventurer dress**: workers see the combat robes and weapons and know better than to interfere (Book 1, Ch93 L85).
- **Fit for combat**: loose but designed so it does not tangle in wheels (Book 4, Ch14 L28).
- **Trade-off**: light robes give up protectiveness for flexibility, a weakness Jason uses against Hendren's identical style (Book 3, Ch59 L87).
