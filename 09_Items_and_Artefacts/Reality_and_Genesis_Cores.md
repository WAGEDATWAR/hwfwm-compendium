## 1. Summary

Reality cores are egg-sized crystals of concentrated power found at transformation events on Earth. Factions fight over them, vampires use them to power their blood, and Jason's work shuts the supply off. Genesis cores are different: Jason loots them from anomalies in the zones to expand his spirit domain.

## 2. Reality cores

- **What they are**: "roughly the size and shape of an ostrich egg", dark crystal glowing with internal transcendent light (Book 5, Ch45 L11); "batteries for the universe", fuelling the nodes of reality (Book 5, Ch42 L54).
- **Where they appear**: each transformation event reveals a single reality core (Book 5, Ch48 L71). Jason's door gives him ready access to a treasure trove of them under the landscape, which he leaves where they are (Book 5, Ch48 L71).
- **Use**: they offer a path forward to those bottlenecked at the top of power (Book 5, Ch43 L66). The power can be consumed directly with a simple ritual (Book 5, Ch47 L111) and is being tested for category-four suppression collars (Book 5, Ch49 L22). They have already pushed two near-category-four users over the threshold (Book 5, Ch68 L8).
- **Factions**: the Cabal claims the cores in Kostroma and Africa, the Network the one in the USA (Book 5, Ch45 L11). The fight over cores strains cooperation (Book 5, Ch45 L23). The Network keeps a secure store (Book 5, Ch53 L31).
- **Vampires**: blood infused with reality-core energy makes the oldest vampires powerful (Book 5, Ch55 L92; Book 5, Ch60 L64; Book 12, Ch2 L116). Dawn says the cores have the same effect on advancement to a lesser degree (Book 5, Ch61 L72).
- **Stopping the supply**: Farrah says the only way to stop people from taking cores is to cut off the supply (Book 5, Ch65 L41). Jason's work stops the transformation zones forming (Book 12, Ch60 L28).
- **Extraction elsewhere**: a pirate admiral calls them impossible to extract normally and notes that Pallimustus is cracked like an egg, making them accessible (Book 12, Ch57 L48-51).
- **Jason's own use**: for ten years he sucks reality-core energy from vampire lords and builds a backup avatar (Book 13, Ch1003 L85).

## 3. Genesis cores

- **Stable Genesis Cores**: unranked, common items looted from anomalies in a zone (Book 6, Ch2 L21, L36). Jason uses them to expand his spirit domain; the cost rises as the domain grows (Book 6, Ch2 L39; Book 6, Ch5 L9; Book 6, Ch6 L35; Book 6, Ch7 L76).
- **Unstable Genesis Cores**: after corpses changed the cores they carried, refining them to a usable state, Jason reasons that since the ones he looted were stable, these must be the unstable variety (Book 6, Ch8 L3).
- **Genesis Reclamation Core**: see ../06_Places/Astral_Spaces/Transformation_Zones.md for the zone-level mechanics.
