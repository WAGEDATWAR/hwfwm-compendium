## 1. Summary

The Lehenik are a people that exist only as a name and a promise: Jason's word for the people he intends to make from his own soul realm, of whom Nik is the first. Nik is the only one in the text.

## 2. Facts

- **The name**: Jason names Nik from the word "Lehenik", "in a language from the world where I was born, it roughly translates first or firstly. The first instance", and says that is what he wants to call Nik's people "once you're a species and not a unique being: Lehenik. The first people to belong in this place" (Book 11, Ch23 L42).
- **Belonging**: he explains that everything else in his soul realm "either doesn't belong or is an extension of me. Except for you. You came from me, but you belong to no one but yourself" (Book 11, Ch23 L44).
- **A promise**: Nik asks "You're going to make more like me?" and Jason answers "Yes. I believe I know how" (Book 11, Ch23 L45-46).
- **Not yet a species**: in Book 12 Jason says he cannot "make more of your kind, turn you into a species proper, until I reach the fullness of my power" (Book 12, Ch39 L17). He jokes about making a female lehenik and Nik rejects the idea (Book 12, Ch39 L19-20).
- **Others like them**: Raythe tells Jason that astral kings cannot ordinarily use planets in their astral realms to birth messengers, "but you are not the first original to do so. Or to birth more than messengers. There are others like your lehenik to be found amongst the countless worlds" (Book 13, Ch991 L5).

## 3. Using this in a story

The lehenik are a future people, not a present one. A character who meets Nik meets the only one; any other lehenik is an invention.

## 4. Not in the text

No other lehenik, their abilities, society, appearance beyond Nik's or numbers are given in the lines read. Nik's own story is in Nik_Asano.md.
