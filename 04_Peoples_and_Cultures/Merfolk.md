## 1. Summary

Merfolk are named once in the books as people, not fish; no detail beyond that is established here.

## 2. Facts

- **Only reference**: Emir answers a joke about someone's partner ("Man, woman or fish?") with "Merfolk are not fish. They happen to be very sensual people" (Book 3, Ch69 L509).
- **Open gap**: no chapter text found so far gives merfolk appearance, homeland or gifts.

<!-- level:3 -->
- **A joke, not a sighting**: in Book 12 Travis asks "Is that some kind of merman?" of a passer-by with webbed hands and feet, and Jason answers that he is the actor Patrick Duffy (Book 12, Ch58 L42-43). The line is an Earth joke, not a merfolk sighting.
- **Searched**: a scan of the chapter files for merfolk, mermaid, merman and merrow finds no other mention (a "siren" simile at Book 13, Ch983 L113 is a comparison, not a species).
<!-- /level -->

## 3. Using this in a story

Use only the line above; any other merfolk detail is invention.
