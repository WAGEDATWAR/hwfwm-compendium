## 1. Summary

The Pallimustus calendar is close to Earth's but not the same: twelve months of thirty days, five extra days and a six-day week with simple day names. Naming customs and forms of address mark rank, family and role, and Rimaros has a distinctive palindrome custom for prominent adults.

## 2. Calendar

- **Months and seasons**: "twelve months of thirty days, divided up into early, mid and late stages of each season" (Book 1, Ch22 L19). Seasons appear in the text as early autumn (Book 2, Ch20 L3).
- **Extra days**: "five additional days that didn't count as days of the month for the solstices, the equinoxes and the new year, which was at the beginning of spring" (Book 1, Ch22 L19).
<!-- level:2 -->
- **The six-day week**: Jason acknowledges "you have a six-day week" (Book 6, Ch10 L41).
- **Day names**: the names are "super-simple": Jason muddles "Fifthday" and "Fourthday" (Book 8, Ch58 L19).
<!-- /level -->

## 3. Holidays and festivals

The festivals the text names (Greenstone's flower wine festival, post-surge festivals in Rimaros) are collected in Clothing_Food_Festivals_and_Transport_by_Region.md. The text names no religious holiday on Pallimustus in the lines read; the holiday Jason refers to in Book 2 is an Earth one ("a religious holiday that we appropriated to stimulate the economy once a year", Book 2, Ch52 L32).

## 4. Naming customs

- **Palindromic names**: "in the local culture, palindromic first and last names were assigned in adulthood rather than at birth and denoted important members of a given family", most common in small families and for a prominent adventurer or high civic official; the more prominent the family, the less common the practice (Book 7, Ch18 L42).
- **Mononyms**: Zato says his name is "my only name" (Book 3, Ch57 L121-122).
- **Family names**: "family names appeared on plaques near the entry gates" of Greenstone's houses (Book 2, Ch23 L3). The Gellers send scions from branches around the world to train in Greenstone even where they share a last name only (Book 1, Ch66 L4).

<!-- level:2 -->
- **Dropped names**: Elizabeth the vampire says she "discarded" her last name "long before you were born" (Book 6, Ch41 L116-117).
- **First-name usage**: using a first name is a step in a relationship (Book 5, Ch35 L71); Shade uses first names only for people he likes (Book 9, Ch93 L91-92). Jason gives his surname and personal name in that order, "My surname is Asano and my personal name is Jason" (Book 9, Ch100 L23).
<!-- /level -->

<!-- level:3 -->
- **Given or family**: people ask whether a name is "given" or "family" (Book 10, Ch7 L42-43).
- **Naming a being**: Jason says "names are important" and that "the right name empowers us, while the wrong one has power over us" (Book 11, Ch23 L33); see Lehenik.md.
<!-- /level -->

## 5. Forms of address

- **Servants to nobles**: "milady" and "milord" (Book 1, Ch9 L28, L53). A cult high priestess styles herself "Lady Cressida Vane" (Book 1, Ch11 L80); "Lady Geller" is used for Danielle (Book 1, Ch37 L8).
- **Officials and clergy**: "Madam Director" for a director present at the star-seed ritual (Book 2, Ch19 L75); "Instructor" for assessors (Book 1, Ch54 L36); "Priestess Pellin" (Book 7, Ch9 L18). See Tone_and_Voice.md.
- **Royalty**: the Mirror King is "Your Majesty" (Book 3, Ch5 L35), and kneeling is expected before him (Book 3, Ch5 L23).

<!-- level:2 -->
- **Messengers**: "lord" whatever their sex, and "Voice Kaal" for a Voice of the Will (Book 9, Ch56 L13-14).
<!-- /level -->

<!-- level:3 -->
- **Houses and titles**: "Lord Headingway" is used for Ben Headingway (Book 12, Ch52 L7), and "Archchancellor" for Clive (Book 12, Ch2 L52).
<!-- /level -->

## 6. Not in the text

The names of the months and seasons, a complete list of the day names, religious holidays and the full rules for palindromic names are not given in the lines read.
