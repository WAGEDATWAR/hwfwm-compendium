## 1. Summary

The Dragon Lands are the volcanic island continent that is the native land of the draconians, a largely isolationist people who export agricultural produce through proxies. The text shows them only from the air in Book 12.

## 2. Facts

- **The people**: large, scaled humanoids who "took pride in their claimed dragon ancestry" and have issues with other races wielding the dragon essence (Book 2, Ch39 L34; see Draconians.md). They are rare outside their land (Book 2, Ch39 L15).
- **Closed society**: they "aren't very welcoming to strangers" and use proxies outside their lands (Book 12, Ch26 L11).
- **Farming and trade**: the land is famously rich farmland and the best place for gold-rank cooking ingredients, because the leaking magic has earth as well as fire affinity (Book 12, Ch26 L9). Agricultural products from the Dragon Lands are the second-largest trade through Greenstone's port, after low-rank spirit coins (Book 12, Ch26 L11). Most nations tariff the goods heavily over political issues, making them a luxury food (Book 12, Ch26 L12).
- **The land**: dark stone mountains rising from green lowlands of forest and farmland laid out like a map, with a climate like Scotland or Ireland (Book 12, Ch26 L7), volcanoes, hot springs and flame geysers called dragon mouths (Book 12, Ch26 L5-6).
