## 1. Summary

Dark-skinned people with glowing red eyes and affinities for earth and fire; the brighthearts are the smoulder of the deep underground realm of Cardinas.

## 2. Facts

- **Smoulder**: "They have natural affinities for earth and fire"; a smoulder friend of Humphrey's parents has the wind essence and his earth affinity evolved into an air affinity (Book 1, Ch85 L45). Niko, a member of Beth's team, has dark skin, glowing red eyes and jet-black hair (Book 1, Ch89 L63).
- **Brighthearts**: in Book 10 fewer than ten thousand of the brightheart smoulder survive, crammed in the citadel cavern (Book 10, Ch65 L6). Marla is a fire aspect with skin markings, eyes and hair the yellow-orange of steel in a forge; Lorenn, an ash aspect, has smoky greys (Book 10, Ch65 L9).
- **War form**: Beaufort prefers his fifteen-foot metal skeleton "war form" but must meet the brightheart smoulders in a form they will accept (Book 10, Ch69 L4).
- **Home**: the largest chamber of the underground realm was home to most brightheart smoulders (Book 10, Ch71 L52).

## 3. Using this in a story

Smoulder adventurers are ordinary Pallimustus people; brighthearts are a besieged, deep-living society that welcomes outsiders only after great loss.
