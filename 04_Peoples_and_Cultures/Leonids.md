## 1. Summary

Lion-headed, furred, strong and fast people; Gary is the best-known leonid.

## 2. Facts

- **Strength and stamina**: "Members of his leonid race are stronger and faster than us, and that's only the beginning of what leonids can do" (Book 1, Ch15 L50); a leonid needs far more exertion than others to tire, another racial gift (Book 1, Ch15 L84).
- **Appearance**: a huge "lion man" whose voice is growly by default (Book 1, Ch9 L131); Gary is "so huge he made bodybuilders look like they w[ere small]" (Book 1, Ch14 L14). He is covered in fur (Book 1, Ch15 L84).
- **Size**: draconians are larger even than male leonids (Book 2, Ch38 L29).
- **Mixed company**: a leonid, an elf and a celestine are among the eleven people who emerge from Dawn's cottage in Book 7 (Book 7, Ch52 L66).
- **Notable leonids**: Gary Xandier, a Vitesse adventurer (Book 1, Ch30 L47), and Gary Sharpton in Book 12, who designs the flying vehicle the group travels in and has spent most of his life in an astral space (Book 12, Ch62 L4-9).

## 3. Using this in a story

Leonids are natural front-liners: expect size, endurance and a deep voice.
