## 1. Summary

A regional table of how people dress, eat, celebrate and travel. `Daily_Life.md` covers Greenstone and the delta in detail; this file adds Rimaros and the Storm Kingdom, Yaresh, Kurdansk, Pranay, Vitesse and smaller places, and gathers the festivals the text names.

## 2. Festivals

- **Greenstone's flower wine festival**: a festival in the Greenstone year that Gary and his friends attend (Book 1, Ch49 L79, L113); afterwards Jason asks Bahadir "How was the wine festival?" (Book 1, Ch99 L39).
- **Memorials as celebrations**: for a dead adventurer the mood "shifted from mourning to a celebration of life" at a wake (Book 1, Ch91 L64).
- **A town festival for weeks**: a small town on a lake had been "going through what was essentially a festival for the better part of three weeks" as adventurers poured through (Book 2, Ch62 L3).
- **Wall-completion feast**: a small town holds a feast with tables and spit roasts in the central square to celebrate completing its new walls (Book 6, Ch47 L350).
- **Kurdansk parade**: Neil and the others merge into "a raucous parade" in a Kurdansk street (Book 6, Ch47 L424).
- **Rimaros after the surge**: "The post-surge festivals here are world-famous" (Book 8, Ch37 L63). At the end of the surge the Magic Society announces the end and Rimaros holds a lengthy festival, "a week for some much-needed celebratory catharsis" (Book 9, Ch2 L16-19). The street festival takes over the market district, boutique ward and Adventure Society campus, with food stalls everywhere and thousands of colour-changing paper lanterns released by the Magic Society (Book 9, Ch3 L3). The aristocratic celebration ball is a political "show"; "the festivals on the streets are the real celebration" (Book 9, Ch2 L20). Formalwear for the ball is tailored for the team (Book 9, Ch13 L3-25).

## 3. By region

### Greenstone and the delta

See Daily_Life.md: loose breathable clothes for the heat (Book 1, Ch59 L30), familiar dishes with different ingredients (Book 1, Ch45 L8), spirit-coin-run household devices (Book 1, Ch80 L40), heidels and the loop (Book 1, Ch17 L106; Book 1, Ch43 L5).

### Rimaros and the Storm Kingdom

- **Food**: the night markets of Livaros supply breakfast ingredients (Book 7, Ch12 L66); the stalls serve deep-fried mash sausage on a stick (made of vegetables) and sweet-and-savoury dumplings (Book 7, Ch12 L24-34); a woman eats a fruit pastry as Jason questions her (Book 7, Ch11 L62). Jason's clifftop barbecue draws crowds (Book 7, Ch18 L3).
- **Dress**: formal pantsuits with defensive magical augmentation for the team at the ball, tailored by Alejandro Albericci, who notes leonid fashion has "given me pause" (Book 9, Ch13 L19-25).
- **Transport**: flying carriages reach the royal sky island through a defended ring (Book 7, Ch17 L3, L16). See Rimaros.md for the city's layout.

### Yaresh

- **Dress**: elves wear "neat casual suits", like business suits "worn loose, with long, tapered sleeves and coattails", in light colours by night and darker shades for thugs (Book 9, Ch44 L16). Elf fashion has flowing, tapered lines (Book 9, Ch46 L5).
- **People and hair**: skin tones from almond to milk chocolate, and hair from honey to rich brown, in waves, ringlets or frizz (Book 9, Ch43 L64).
- **Food and drink**: bars, cafes and places offering delights "from the chaste to the downright saucy" in the entertainment district, and small pubs with chalkboard menus (Book 9, Ch43 L85).
- **Transport**: land skimmers, carriages and personal floatation discs are present but minimal and "never driven by the elves that made up the bulk of the population" (Book 9, Ch43 L64).

### Kurdansk (Dreisil)

- **Transport**: see Kurdansk_Culture.md for the skyport.
- **Festivals**: a street parade (above).

### Pranay and Jayapura

- **Dress and food**: the team's visit brings "new customs, new food. New sights and sounds, tastes and smells" (Book 2, Ch74 L14); Jason recalls kulfi-like ice cream (Book 7, Ch14 L52).
- **Transport**: platter-sized floating discs for those who can afford them (Book 2, Ch70 L3).
