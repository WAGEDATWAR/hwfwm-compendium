## 1. Summary

The civilisation-building peoples of Pallimustus, what a racial gift is, and the peoples that are not native to the world. Each people has its own file.

## 2. Facts

- **Civilisation-building peoples**: Jason's team finds bodies of "every civilisation-building species: humans, elves and leonids, celestines, runic, smoulder and draconians" in the Book 1 desert astral space (Book 1, Ch86 L40).
- **Where they live**: the Greenstone region is human-dominated, while a port city shows "all kinds of people" (Book 1, Ch15 L56).
- **Racial gifts**: "Every race has six racial gifts"; for humans they are an affinity for special attacks, faster essence advancement and latent powers that adapt to the person's essences (Book 1, Ch85 L35). A racial gift can change in "racial power evolution"; the latent human gifts are blank slates guaranteed to do so (Book 1, Ch85 L39). Triggers are often a traumatic event, a near-fatal fight or long habit (Book 1, Ch85 L41); a god's blessing, or that of a great astral being, can trigger it (Book 1, Ch85 L47; Book 1, Ch85 L22-26).
- **Sharing gifts**: the Magic Society prizes the details of racial gifts, and Humphrey advises Jason to keep his to himself and trade them for favours (Book 1, Ch38 L72).
- **Other peoples named**: merfolk ("Merfolk are not fish... They happen to be very sensual people", Book 3, Ch69 L509), goblins, fairies and valash, the last three appearing as forms taken by people changed in Earth's transformation zones (Book 5, Ch54 L52; Book 6, Ch23 L91; Book 9, Ch45 L57).
- **Not native**: outworlders and valash are not native to Pallimustus (see Outworlders.md and Valash.md); messengers are covered in the Cosmology files.
- **From race to nature**: in Book 12 Jason's character screen says "nature" instead of "race" and calls him a "prime avatar of an astral nexus"; Shade suggests that "race" is a term Jason took from games and cannot cover the breadth of people the System now serves (Book 12, Ch8 L100-102). Jason keeps the six powers that used to be racial gifts (Book 12, Ch8 L103).

- **Life and society files**: `Daily_Life.md` (calendar, clothing, food, transport), `Economy_and_Money.md` (spirit coins, farms, trade), `Law_and_Indenture.md` and `Religion_in_Practice.md`.

## 3. Using this in a story

Treat race as a matter of body, gifts and culture, not value: gifts are real advantages, but anyone's gifts can evolve. Greenstone scenes are human-heavy; ports, Vitesse and Yaresh are mixed.
