## 1. Summary

People whose souls re-entered a physical reality, manifesting a new body; they carry unusual gifts and cannot be easily tracked.

## 2. Facts

- **Definition**: Clive: "An outworlder is someone whose soul has re-entered a physical reality, reflexively manifesting a body for itself" (Book 2, Ch25 L57); the body is "akin to that of a monster, or a summoned familiar", physical substance forged from raw magic, between the astral and the physical (Book 2, Ch25 L59).
- **How it happens**: natural magical phenomena connecting two realities drag someone through; their body is annihilated in the astral and their soul reconstitutes one (Book 2, Ch25 L61).
- **Advantages**: Farrah says outworlders "might have their advantages", and Jason's abilities (map, interface, astral affinity) are outworlder abilities (Book 1, Ch15 L50, L74-76).
- **Children**: an outworlder with another species produces offspring of that species; two outworlders produce a celestine (Book 5, Ch44 L72).
- **Arrivals**: a hundred and nineteen outworlders arrive at once in Book 7 (Book 7, Ch36 L86); two outworlders among the eleven people in Dawn's cottage are bronze rank (Book 7, Ch52 L66).
- **Jason**: his character screen lists him as a formerly human outworlder until Book 12 (Book 12, Ch8 L100).

## 3. Using this in a story

Outworlders are fish out of water: a body that is partly astral, gifts nobody can explain, and an arrival story that usually involves loss.
