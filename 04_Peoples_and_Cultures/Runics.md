## 1. Summary

Short, stocky, hairless people with deep-blue skin and naturally glowing runes.

## 2. Facts

- **Appearance**: "short and stocky, with skin of such a deep blue it was almost black", no hair and covered in faintly glowing, tattoo-like markings that are natural to the race (Book 1, Ch45 L14).
- **Rarity**: they are "a rarity in Greenstone" (Book 1, Ch45 L14); a runic barman in Old City is stocky and hairless with blue-black skin and glowing runes (Book 1, Ch71 L40).
- **Bones**: the runes show faintly on their bones; a short, broad skeleton in the desert astral space is identified as a runic by them (Book 1, Ch86 L31).
- **Notable**: Arash, a runic Jason meets in the temple district, explains Dominion's role: "Dominion issues the divine right to kings and nobles" (Book 1, Ch45 L14-18).

- **Part of the civilisation-building species**: the list of species that build civilisations is "humans, elves and leonids, celestines, runic, smoulder and draconians" (Book 1, Ch86 L40).
- **Common in Old City**: a runic works as a barman (Book 1, Ch71 L40), and a runic man appears at Jory's clinic (Book 1, Ch102 L72).

<!-- level:2 -->
- **Inherent markings**: the glowing runes "looked like magic tattoos but were an inherent trait" (Book 7, Ch38 L10).
- **Spellcasters**: "Elves and runics were the most common affliction wielders, as their predilection for spells made a ranged power set much more likely" (Book 7, Ch15 L5).
- **Earth zones**: when zones turn Earth people into other species, Jason, Farrah and Dawn spot elves and "the dark-skinned runic people" (Book 5, Ch44 L56). A runic woman appears with Rick Mercer's team (Book 7, Ch59 L65).
- **Kinship with smoulders**: "Smoulders and the runic were quite similar, with their dark skin and glowing features" (Book 10, Ch71 L63; see Smoulder_and_Brighthearts.md).
