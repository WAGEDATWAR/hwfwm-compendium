## 1. Summary

Dreisil is the nation of Kurdansk, Sophie's birthplace. The text names it once and gives its full title. Kurdansk_Culture.md covers the life of the city and is the main file for what is known of the nation's people.

## 2. Facts

- **Name**: Kurdansk lies "in the People's Holy Federation of Dreisil" (Book 6, Ch47 L390).
- **Humphrey's view**: "The more they try to make a nation sound free and righteous, the more tyrannical and corrupt it is" (Book 6, Ch47 L392).
- **Prisons**: Kurdansk uses prisons rather than indenture as punishment (see Kurdansk_Culture.md).

## 3. Not in the text

The Federation's structure, ruler, religion and borders are not described in the lines read. Dreisil is named only in Book 6.
