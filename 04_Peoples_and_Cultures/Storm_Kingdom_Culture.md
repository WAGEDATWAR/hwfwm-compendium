## 1. Summary

A sea kingdom ruled from Rimaros, where a gold-rank monarch rules, heirs prove themselves as adventurers, and sky islands house the cream of society.

## 2. Facts

- **Extent**: "It rules the Sea of Storms, all the islands on it and all the coastal regions that surround it" (Book 7, Ch3 L47); the capital is Rimaros (Book 7, Ch3 L82).
- **Sky islands**: reserved for "the cream of society": big-name adventurers, long-standing adventuring families and aristocrats; a family known for producing good adventurers is mostly inducted into the nobility, and a house without good adventurers sinks into obscurity (Book 7, Ch5 L65).
- **Monarchy**: the Storm King is a gold-ranker, which one must be to take the throne; royals are not raised on cores as in most nations, since that disqualifies them here, and potential heirs from every branch are adventurers proving themselves in monster fighting and statecraft in a decades-long contest until the monarch chooses an heir (Book 7, Ch5 L67).
- **Titles**: the firstborn daughter of the Storm King, if competing, is the Hurricane Princess and the firstborn son the Storm Prince; many royals bow out, often adopting into their non-royal parent's family (Book 7, Ch5 L69).
- **Palindromic names**: first and last names that are palindromes are assigned in adulthood and mark important family members; the more prominent the family, the rarer the practice, and the royal family shares a name with the capital (Book 7, Ch18 L42).
- **Royal looks**: the royal family's iconic feature is sapphire hair and eyes (Book 8, Ch62 L36; Book 12, Ch12 L17).
- **Royal guard**: the Sapphire Crown is one of the most powerful guilds, backed by the royal family, and its non-royal members must periodically serve in the royal guard (Book 7, Ch21 L11).
- **Public islands**: Livaros, Arnote and Provo lie in an east-west line (Book 7, Ch7 L70). Arnote is mostly residential, home to successful merchants and retired adventurers, with a relaxed lifestyle that is part of Rimaros's cultural identity, and a minor royal branch lives there (Book 7, Ch7 L70, L72). Livaros is the "adventurer island", with the Adventure Society campus and most adventurer services (Book 7, Ch7 L78).
- **Rivalry with Estercost**: friendly rivals whose distance gives little cause for conflict (Book 12, Ch49 L3).
- **Politics later**: by Book 12 Zara's brother is the new Storm King, and she remains adopted into House Nareen rather than on the outs with the royals (Book 12, Ch12 L19).

## 3. Using this in a story

Rimaros culture is about relaxed island life backed by sharp royal competition; do not assume a royal is soft because they smile.
