## 1. Summary

A large, densely populated kingdom of old noble houses, garden towers and high-magic farmland, and the homeland from which Greenstone was colonised.

## 2. Facts

- **Standing**: "One of the most prominent kingdoms in the world" with a royal family (Book 11, Ch68 L1862); the Queen of Estercost and other members of its elite ask Jason to arbitrate over the System's withdrawal (Book 12, Ch51 L13).
- **Population**: a "large and densely populated kingdom" and the land from which Greenstone was colonised centuries earlier (Book 11, Ch68 L1343); the region is "filled with towns, villages and smaller cities, due to the idyllic climate and rich natural resources" (Book 12, Ch43 L47).
- **Capital and councils**: Cyrion is the capital, yet the Adventure Society's continental council sits in Vitesse, where the royal family keeps a tower as a palace (Book 6, Ch47 L604); in Book 7 the Cyrion branch takes over the site of the operation from Vitesse (Book 7, Ch43 L58).
- **Vitesse**: the city of flowers, on what Jason would know as the French Riviera, with garden towers that house the wealthy elite and key organisations (Book 6, Ch47 L603). Adventurers think of it as the continent's adventuring capital, because its high magic spawns powerful, frequent monster manifestations (Book 12, Ch43 L47).
- **Aristocracy**: the Remore family has no title, while the Gellers hold only the title from their small provincial city of origin and refuse all others; neither is begrudged a premier tower (Book 6, Ch47 L605). Cyrion's laws "were very much built to favour the aristocratic families" (Book 12, Ch48 L53). House Headingway is "one of Estercost's pre-eminent families" (Book 12, Ch50 L3).
- **Cyrion's land**: concentric walls ring the city with vast tracts of agricultural land between them, "some of the most magical and valuable growing land on the planet" (Book 12, Ch48 L3); it is the largest city in the world, with lakes, forests and heavy air traffic (Book 12, Ch48 L22).
- **Clothes and ships**: the fitted "Estercost style" is worn in red and brown by one visitor (Book 12, Ch34 L64); specialty airships that run only in high-magic zones are a signature of Estercost (Book 12, Ch53 L60).
- **Kazlahk**: a sandstone coastal city on Estercost's eastern border, surrounded by desert and used for spirit coin farms, with denominations more valuable than Greenstone's because the magic is higher (Book 11, Ch68 L1380).
- **Rivals**: the Storm Kingdom treats Estercost as a friendly rival, its distance leaving few reasons for conflict (Book 12, Ch49 L3).

## 3. Using this in a story

Estercost scenes are about old money, tall flowering towers and polished manners; its aristocrats are used to the law being on their side.
