## 1. Summary

Jayapura is a northern-coast sea-trade port of Pranay and the birthplace of Hester. Pranay_Culture.md covers the land; this file gives Jayapura's own life: the mirage arenas as entertainment, floating discs, markets and the Mystic Quarter.

## 2. Facts

- **Trade port**: one of the important sea-trade ports on Pranay's northern coast (Book 2, Ch69 L65-66).
- **Arena culture**: its mirage chamber complex is the city's premier entertainment venue, with essence users facing each other or illusion challenges before a paying audience (Book 2, Ch73 L4). "There are whole essence user teams who never become adventurers"; they earn money in the arena and use monster cores to rank (Book 2, Ch73 L7-8). Two leagues a year in silver, bronze and iron divisions lead to grand championships (Book 2, Ch73 L10). See Mirage_Chamber_Association.md.
- **Retirement**: arena professionals stop using cores before gold, retire when silver longevity runs out and become gold-rank managers of the arena (Book 2, Ch73 L12-14).
- **Layout**: a flat circular arena building at the edge of the Mystic Quarter, spilling into the theatre district (Book 2, Ch73 L6).
- **Transport**: those who can afford them ride platter-sized floating discs (Book 2, Ch70 L3); floating discs are sold in the Mystic Quarter (Book 3, Ch5 L83).
- **Markets and food**: the team visits its markets and finds new customs and food (Book 3, Ch32 L65; Book 2, Ch74 L14). A nondescript tattoo parlour run by Tilly is where Jason gets his crest (Book 2, Ch73 L62).
