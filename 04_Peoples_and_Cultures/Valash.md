## 1. Summary

A skinny, chihuahua-headed species that is not native to Pallimustus; humans are turned into valash by Earth's transformation zones.

## 2. Facts

- **Origin**: Jason had only seen valash when Earth's transformation zones turned humans into them (Book 9, Ch45 L57); they are "a skinny sapient species not native to Pallimustus" (Book 9, Ch46 L5).
- **Appearance**: "skinny bodies and Chihuahua-like heads", shorter than Jason (Book 9, Ch45 L58; Book 9, Ch46 L5).
- **Outworlders**: the valash Jason meets in Yaresh is an outworlder with a silver-rank aura and no sign of core use (Book 9, Ch45 L58; Book 9, Ch46 L6).
- **Fashion**: his fitted white suit is not in the local style and "would have been more at home in a Miami nightclub in the eighties" (Book 9, Ch46 L5).

## 3. Using this in a story

Valash on Pallimustus are almost always outworlders from Earth's zones; they read as foreign and out of place.
