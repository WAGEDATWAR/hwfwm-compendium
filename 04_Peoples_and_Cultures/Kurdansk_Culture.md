## 1. Summary

A river city in the People's Holy Federation of Dreisil, where celestines with dark skin and silver hair are common, entertainment and crime share the old docks, and prisons rather than indenture punish crime.

## 2. Facts

- **Nation**: Kurdansk lies "in the People's Holy Federation of Dreisil" (Book 6, Ch47 L390).
- **Celestines**: "Like many of Kurdansk's celestines, she had dark skin and silver hair", and the rowhouses are narrow and wedged together (Book 6, Ch47 L508).
- **Entertainment quarter**: a former warehouse district near the Kurdan River docks, now the most viable site for large theatre halls, "a heady mix of pleasure, criminality and money" (Book 6, Ch47 L436).
- **Skyport**: it has a busy skyport with towers where airships dock (Book 6, Ch47 L459).
- **Law**: Kurdansk locks criminals up for years in prisons, which a Book 12 speaker calls costly to run (Book 12, Ch49 L77).
- **The Reaper link**: Melody Jain's story begins in Kurdansk around twenty-five years before Book 9 (Book 9, Ch27 L24).

## 3. Using this in a story

Kurdansk is a crowded, mixed city of theatre halls, docks and celestine neighbourhoods; its prisons contrast with Estercost's indenture.
