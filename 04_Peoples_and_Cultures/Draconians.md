## 1. Summary

Large, scaled humanoids who claim descent from dragons and whose homeland is the Dragon Lands.

## 2. Facts

- **Appearance**: "a member of the rare draconian race, larger even than male leonids and covered in glossy scales", the colour of dark leaves, green moving into purple (Book 2, Ch38 L29); Maximilian is the tallest person in Greenstone, with a long hairless head and glossy scales (Book 2, Ch39 L15). A draconian skull is distinctive enough to identify skeletons (Book 1, Ch86 L31).
- **Pride in descent**: draconians "took pride in their claimed dragon ancestry and often had issues with other races wielding the dragon essence" (Book 2, Ch39 L34).
- **Homeland**: the Dragon Lands, Pallimustus's version of Antarctica, "the native land of the large, scaled humanoids known as draconians", with active volcanoes, hot springs and "dragon mouth" geysers from subterranean apertures to a realm of fire (Book 12, Ch26 L5-6).
- **Rarity**: draconians are rare in Greenstone, where Maximilian is the only one present at a gathering (Book 2, Ch39 L15).

## 3. Using this in a story

A draconian is a rare, imposing figure; mention of a dragon essence in their company is a social risk.
