## 1. Summary

How people are taught: family academies for adventurers (Remore, Geller), Magic Society research wings, universities (Kazlahk's medical school) and, on Earth, the Asano Academy. The text shows education mostly through what it produces: skilled adventurers, researchers and healers.

## 2. Adventurer academies

- **Remore Academy**: "one of the most exclusive prep academies for adventurers in the world. Kings go to that school" (Book 1, Ch20 L136). The family runs it, and it is "a big deal everywhere, not just Vitesse" (Book 1, Ch30 L58). Rufus, a graduate, treats it as a source of method (Book 1, Ch37 L40; Book 2, Ch5 L38).
- **What it teaches**: it studies adventurer methodology from across the globe so as to educate students from around the world; its students are "scions of international mercantile guilds, famous adventuring families, aristocrats and even royalty", prepared for diplomatic trouble, assassination or pitched battle (Book 9, Ch20 L26-27). A scouting programme gives scholarships and dormitories to people with potential (Book 6, Ch47 L129).
- **Greenstone annex**: a Remore Academy training annex with the Gellers (Book 1, Ch88 L3; Book 3, Ch26 L9). It is open to all essence users, with tuition deferred until they have Adventure Society membership (Book 7, Ch40 L26).
- **Faculty and style**: the academy is "famous for recruiting adventurers, especially those close to or just having reached gold rank" (Book 11, Ch68 L1835); its method is "individualised outcomes", with most effort to those with drive (Book 13, Ch971 L51).
- **Geller training**: the family trains its young in Greenstone with a mirage chamber and arena (Book 1, Ch66 L18, L27); its most elite programme is entered on Danielle's introduction (Book 11, Ch68 L1038). People "scramble to be on a team with a Geller who went through their Greenstone training" (Book 9, Ch21 L20).

## 3. Research and learning

- **Magic Society**: its branches keep libraries and research wings. In Rakesh an astral magic research wing is staffed by officials such as Lorelei Grantham (Book 6, Ch47 L624); astral researchers question outworlders in a room for the purpose (Book 7, Ch45 L79).
- **Temples of Knowledge**: the Rimaros temple is an important resource for researchers, with a symbiotic link to the Magic Society and a portal chamber (Book 7, Ch9 L4). Greenstone's temple library is used by researchers (Book 2, Ch3 L22).
- **Universities**: Boko's universities draw scholars (Book 12, Ch31 L34, L79).
- **Kazlahk University of Medicine**: most healing schools focus on essence and ritual magic because the Magic Society and Adventure Society fund them; KazMed is "a prominent exception", focused on alchemical medicine, funded largely by trade associations, with some money from the Healer's church (Book 11, Ch68 L1382-1384). It runs a School of Alchemical Efficiency (Book 11, Ch68 L1397).
- **Magical Research Association**: open-access research libraries for young researchers (Book 12, Ch1 L57); see Magical_Research_Association.md.
- **Skill books**: they are looked down on by some trained adventurers, who say "that's the first thing we have to beat out of skill book users at my family's academy" (Book 1, Ch39 L117); see `../03_Magic_and_Adventuring/Skill_Books_and_Learning.md`.

## 4. The Asano clan and Earth

- **The clan's schools**: the clan has a school for ritual magic and a training centre where all essence users go through a basic programme, with children earning contribution points for their families by attending school (Book 12, Ch10 L80).
- **Asano Academy**: "the premier educational institution for magic on planet Earth"; international students are "one of the strongest cards the clan had to play"; full-time staff include Emi, Clive, Belinda and Lorelei, with guest lectures by Jason and a Rufus workshop on the integration of physicality and magic (Book 13, Ch1016 L106).
- **New Water**: Knowledge's clergy set up schools and training programmes in the new city, with language training for adults and children from "both Earth and Pallimustus" (Book 13, Ch951 L38-40).
- **Earth's schools**: Jason's own school was the private Casselton Educational Institute, with "excellent teachers, quality education. Exorbitant cost" (Book 2, Ch11 L37).

## 5. Not in the text

Ordinary schools in Greenstone and Pallimustus's cities, fees for the family academies and the ages at which children begin training are not given in the lines read.
