## 1. Summary

Goblins and fairies appear as monsters and as people of Earth transformed by the zones; fairies are also a small hovering manifestation.

## 2. Facts

- **Goblins as monsters**: Jason jokes about killing ten goblins for an uncommon spear in the cannibals' cave (Book 1, Ch4 L26).
- **Transformed humans**: in West Canfield, Detroit, people were "turned into goblins and their homes into underground warrens", which became a lawless combat zone (Book 5, Ch54 L52).
- **Cabal beings**: the Cabal's mythological beings include centaurs, ogres, fairies and more (Book 5, Ch54 L55).
- **Elves, goblins and fairies**: people in a magic city were "turned into elves and goblins and fairies" (Book 6, Ch23 L91).
- **Fairy manifestation**: a small hovering fairy-like figure with an androgynous body bites Clive in Book 2 (Book 2, Ch26 L99).

## 3. Using this in a story

Goblins and fairies are Earth-side cases; no native Pallimustus culture of either is established here.
