## 1. Summary

Elves are powerful spellcasters whose healing magic is unparalleled.

## 2. Facts

- **Reputation**: "Elves are powerful spell casters and their skill with healing magic is unparalleled" (Book 1, Ch15 L50).
- **Aptitude**: the elven aptitude is for spells rather than the special attacks humans favour (Book 3, Ch1 L90).
- **Racial gift**: Neil's elf racial ability, Life Affinity, evolves into Life Guard (Book 2, Ch61 L145).
- **Local families**: Greenstone has local elf families; Lucian Lamprey is an elf banished there who is not one of them (Book 1, Ch42 L25-30), and Pochard Finn is an elf and a local (Book 1, Ch42 L30).
- **Elf fashion**: "flowing, tapered lines" (Book 9, Ch46 L5).
- **Notable elves**: Allayeth and Charist, Yaresh's two diamond-rank elves (Book 9, Ch98 L4); Hana Shavar, High Priestess of the Healer (Book 10, Ch3 L29); Elspeth Arella, director of the Greenstone Adventure Society (Book 1, Ch83 L45).
- **Predation**: cannibals in Book 1 discuss elves as food (Book 1, Ch3 L43; Book 1, Ch15 L51).

## 3. Using this in a story

Good for healers, spellcasters and long-lived local power brokers; elf aristocrats tend to hold municipal office.
