## 1. Summary

Zartos is a subterranean city built around an underground river, largely populated by celestines and home to the diamond-ranker Virid, Gary's mentor. The text shows it only as a place to work and rest.

## 2. Facts

- **Setting**: "a subterranean city built around an underground river, largely populated by celestines" (Book 7, Ch2 L75).
- **Celestine community**: Gary says "Zartos has a large celestine community" (Book 1, Ch16 L57); Rufus has not been, though his brother recommended it (Book 1, Ch16 L53-58). See Celestines.md for the people.
- **Craft**: Virid keeps a personal smithy there, "the best place to forge a great work" (Book 7, Ch2 L71-72). Gary and Virid forge while the team rests (Book 7, Ch2 L75).

## 3. Not in the text

Zartos's rulers, food, dress, festivals and religion are not described in the lines read.
