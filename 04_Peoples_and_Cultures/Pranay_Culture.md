## 1. Summary

The large southern land corresponding to Sri Lanka, where portal stopovers and isolated city-states form a trading hub, and where Jayapura is the port Hester calls home.

## 2. Facts

- **Geography**: Pranay is this world's equivalent of Sri Lanka, "larger and further south", a huge landmass in the Indian Ocean (Book 2, Ch34 L58). Its northern coast has several important sea-trade connections in a world without the Arabian Peninsula (Book 2, Ch69 L65).
- **Jayapura**: Hester's birth city; personal transport for those who can afford it is a platter-sized floating disc ridden by shifting weight (Book 2, Ch70 L3).
- **Mirage chambers**: Jayapura has a vast mirage chamber complex, more sophisticated than the Gellers' private chamber in Greenstone because the higher magic allows potent dimensional magic and concurrent chambers (Book 2, Ch73 L3).
- **Massive city**: Jason's team visited Pranay's urban centre after their first Reaper astral space and saw only familiars as magical beasts (Book 9, Ch33 L19).
- **Portal stopover**: in Book 11 it is a portal stopover on the way to Estercost, "the last of the isolated city-states they would visit for a while" (Book 11, Ch68 L1343).

## 3. Using this in a story

Pranay is the first place that shows Jason how rich a high-magic port can be; discs, mirage chambers and teleport stops are the texture.
