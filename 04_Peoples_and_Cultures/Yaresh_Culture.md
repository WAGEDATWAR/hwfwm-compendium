## 1. Summary

A small-population city that grows around its trees, whose civic government is weaker than its churches and societies, and whose two elf diamond-rankers dominate its adventuring scene.

## 2. Facts

- **Size and design**: only a few tens of thousands live there, but the design accommodates the environment so that the city has the footprint of a much larger centre (Book 9, Ch42 L26).
- **Walls and gates**: its walls are a line of massive trees with glossy black stone filling the gaps, tunnels pass through the trunks, and vehicles queue at a gate for inspection (Book 9, Ch43 L33).
- **Streets**: alleys are nicer than the norm and resemble gardens, with gravel paths through long grass (Book 9, Ch44 L3).
- **Government**: "In Yaresh, the civic administration is considerably weaker than the churches and the major societies", because one group undermines the Duke for its own ends (Book 10, Ch14 L28-31).
- **The Aristocratic Faction**: wealthy aristocrats and a few merchant barons who own most of the land and provide most of the jobs, and who use that power to shape their tenants' ideology (Book 10, Ch14 L33). They have no adventurers or researchers behind them, just money and political influence (Book 10, Ch14 L35).
- **Diamond-rankers**: Allayeth and Charist, both elves (Book 9, Ch98 L4); Yaresh has "not just one but two resident diamond-rankers", with Charist the more social and Allayeth the more reclusive, who raised the all-woman team Moon's Edge, led by Miriam Vance (Book 12, Ch16 L8; Book 10, Ch59 L17).
- **Reunion**: in Book 12 much of the reunion group has already left Yaresh to make preparations for the expedition to Earth (Book 12, Ch21 L79).

## 3. Using this in a story

Yaresh feels calm, green and old, but it is run by churches and societies, with landlords pulling strings.
