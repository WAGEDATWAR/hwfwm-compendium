## 1. Summary

Girlano is an inland nation south of the Storm Kingdom, ostensibly a kingdom but controlled by cartels that produce banned or heavily controlled substances. It has a reputation, and the text shows it only through reputation and a border city.

## 2. Facts

- **Rule**: "ostensibly a kingdom", it is "famously controlled by cartels known for producing substances that ranged from heavily controlled to outright banned"; the cartels keep power through under-the-table deals with powerful groups in neighbouring countries, which prop up a puppet government while their own governments pay lip service to suppression (Book 8, Ch24 L22-23).
- **Economy**: the cartels exploit the region's rare plants and minerals, and the nation is surrounded by strong public sanctions and border controls (Book 8, Ch24 L22).
- **Smuggling culture**: the border with the Storm Kingdom is jungle-covered hills with many semi-hidden smuggling paths to the shore (Book 8, Ch24 L26). The border city Casallini is a small city where the Adventure Society keeps a large force because it is a border city (Book 8, Ch25 L136; Book 8, Ch26 L39).
- **Neutral Society**: the Adventure Society ignores borders and politics and keeps monsters out of population centres, so long as its work is not interfered with (Book 8, Ch24 L24-25).
- **Reputation**: Liara calls it "a stain full of drug dealers and smugglers" (Book 8, Ch25 L120). Belinda finds it "famously sketchy" with "all the opportunities it offered to an enterprising and open-minded young lady" (Book 9, Ch26 L3).
- **Hired muscle**: the men who attack Sophie's delivery are local hires from Casallini (Book 8, Ch25 L119).
