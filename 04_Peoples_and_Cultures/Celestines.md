## 1. Summary

Metallic-haired, metallic-eyed people with an astral affinity, produced when two outworlders have a child.

## 2. Facts

- **Appearance**: Sophie has chocolate skin and shining silver hair, her eyes matching it exactly, "the matching metallics a giveaway trait of the celestine race" (Book 1, Ch43 L18). In Kurdansk many celestines have dark skin and silver hair (Book 6, Ch47 L508).
- **Astral affinity**: astral affinity is a celestine racial gift (Book 1, Ch15 L76-80); celestines are similar to elves and humans (Book 1, Ch15 L78). The rare Child of the Celestial Wind appears only in races with astral affinity, on Pallimustus meaning celestines, or potentially outworlders (Book 8, Ch21 L40).
- **Origin**: Dawn says two outworlders who have a child produce a celestine, and celestines propagate with their own kind; she is herself a child of two outworlders (Book 5, Ch44 L72).
- **Gift evolutions**: Sophie's Mana Integrity becomes Mana Wellspring (Book 2, Ch61 L146), her Astral Affinity becomes Boundary Breaker (Book 3, Ch68 L14) and her Celestial Swiftness becomes Princess of the Firmament (Book 8, Ch22 L37).
- **Communities**: Zartos is a subterranean city largely populated by celestines (Book 7, Ch2 L75).
- **Messengers**: messengers are about half again as tall as a celestine, and their servants take care never to imply the resemblance (Book 9, Ch55 L9).
- **Dawn's true form**: a celestine with ruby eyes and hair (Book 7, Ch54 L87).

## 3. Using this in a story

Celestines are visibly striking and often the children of the outworlder arrivals; their culture is as varied as any people's.
