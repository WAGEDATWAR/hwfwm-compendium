## 1. Summary

The Mirror Kingdom, also called the Kingdom of Mirrors, is a small, affluent kingdom founded and ruled for more than eight centuries by a diamond-ranker, the Mirror King. Its princes and princesses are many, and its prince Valdis leads an adventuring team in Greenstone.

## 2. Facts

- **Nature**: "the diminutive but influential Kingdom of Mirrors. Small, affluent and geographically blessed, it had neither expanded its borders nor been had its borders encroached upon in more than eight centuries" (Book 2, Ch51 L8).
- **The king**: the diamond-ranker known as the Mirror King founded the kingdom and rules it still; he has had "a series of queens, reportedly doting on each, even as they grew old and died beside him", and Valdis is one of the current queen consort's sons (Book 2, Ch51 L8).
- **Court manners**: kneeling is expected before the king; his protocol officer Hastor tells Jason "You should kneel", and the king says Hastor "starts getting snippy" without a seating chart by rank (Book 3, Ch5 L23, L34-36). Valdis's team drops to one knee on the king's arrival (Book 3, Ch5 L20). The king is amused, warm and teasing with his son (Book 3, Ch5 L40-48).
- **Teams built around princes**: Valdis's team was built around him, "the shaft to his spearhead" (Book 3, Ch3 L73-80). The kingdom has its own battles with the Builder cult (Book 3, Ch69 L895).
<!-- level:2 -->
- **The king's aura**: Jason, an iron-ranker, finds the king's aura felt like part of the world, "in perfect symbiosis with the world", and it later inspires his own aura control (Book 4, Ch73 L98; Book 4, Ch74 L12).
<!-- /level -->
- **Portals**: the king has his own portal specialist, Hastor, who calls up a glass-like portal (Book 3, Ch5 L50).

<!-- level:3 -->
- **A surplus of royals**: Valdis says his father is "so horny and immortal" that "the kingdom's thick with princes and princesses" and he will take a good adventurer over any of his kids (Book 12, Ch15 L9). Valdis's administrative prowess assembles an arena event in under two days (Book 12, Ch16 L11).

## 3. A noted inconsistency

A Book 13 line places a relic "in the Mirror Kingdom. A wild place, so far north that the ice never thaws" (Book 13, Ch1022 L51), which does not match the "small, affluent and geographically blessed" kingdom of Book 2, Ch51 L8. Both are recorded.
