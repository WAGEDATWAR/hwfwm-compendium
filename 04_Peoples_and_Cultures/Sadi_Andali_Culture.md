## 1. Summary

Sadi Andali is "a little dirtball country" in or near an eastern desert, famously almost lawless, with slave markets and no Adventure Society branch. It appears in Book 12 when noble heirs are taken there.

## 2. Facts

- **Setting**: a captive's guess of "Eastern desert?" is answered "Obviously, ever hear of a little dirtball country called Sadi Andali?" (Book 12, Ch50 L125-126). Its cells are mud brick (Book 12, Ch50 L124).
- **Law**: "famous for being almost lawless"; the Adventure Society has no branch there and only sends people in to sweep for illegal research and restricted essences, finding plenty of both (Book 12, Ch50 L128).
- **Slave markets**: unusual products move through them (Book 12, Ch52 L35-37); Ben asks whether destroying its slave trade is Jason's price (Book 12, Ch52 L41).
- **Reach**: events "all across Sadi Andali" are set against the great houses of Cyrion (Book 12, Ch52 L74).
- **Effect on visitors**: Percival Headingway thinks Garret's time there may have "knocked some sense" into him (Book 12, Ch53 L7).
