## 1. Summary

The Ustei are nomads from a region to the north of Greenstone, living on oases connected to an astral space and moving with their war barge. Ustei.md covers who they are; this file collects what the text says of their homeland and customs.

## 2. Facts

- **The homeland**: a region to the north of the Greenstone desert (Book 1, Ch89 L50), whose oases are connected to the astral space that supplies the desert (Book 1, Ch97 L21). When the astral space's water supply failed, the oases "were no longer able to support all the nomad tribes" (Book 1, Ch97 L21).
- **Travel**: the entire tribe lives on a war barge, "easily the equal of a passenger liner", built on giant bones with multiple decks (Book 1, Ch90 L14). See Desert_Transport_Skimmers_and_Barges.md.
- **Warriors and ranks**: only a fraction of the warriors are iron-rankers, most have one or two essences; bronze-rank captains lead them, and a silver-rank tribal chief commands the tribe (Book 1, Ch90 L8-9, L42).
- **Dress**: Humphrey identifies them as northerners "from the hair and clothes" (Book 1, Ch81 L43).
- **Women, children and slaves**: the tribe's women and children were locked away in conditions like slaves, and actual slaves were found in the same conditions (Book 1, Ch90 L44). Someone floated the idea of taking the Ustei men as slaves, "which would at least be something they understood" (Book 1, Ch92 L30).
- **Law of surrender**: "Their culture doesn't allow warriors to be made slaves. For them, capture means release or death" (Book 1, Ch92 L36). After defeat, they claim the barge and everything in it, including the women, children and slaves, belongs to the victor; "the idea is that now they have to go raiding for more women" (Book 1, Ch92 L12-16).
- **After the surrender**: the Adventure Society plans to relocate them north and investigate the astral space (Book 1, Ch97 L23).
