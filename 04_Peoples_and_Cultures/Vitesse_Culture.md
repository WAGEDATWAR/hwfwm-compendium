## 1. Summary

Vitesse is the garden-tower city of Estercost: wealthy, prosperous and full of powerful adventurers, with the Remore Academy and the continent's adventuring elite. This file covers how its people live; the geography is in `../06_Places/Vitesse.md`.

## 2. Facts

- **The city of flowers**: it is "known as the city of flowers", in what Jason would know as the French Riviera, with garden towers that have every third or fourth floor given to gardens "using water, light and plant magic to create lush refuges of peace" (Book 6, Ch47 L603). The towers house the wealthy elite, aristocrats and adventurers, and the headquarters of key organisations (Book 6, Ch47 L603).
- **Standing of the great families**: the Remore family has no aristocratic title and the Gellers only a small provincial one, and neither is begrudged residences in premier towers (Book 6, Ch47 L605).
- **Adventuring**: iron-rankers there may not hunt alone, because of the high magic (Book 2, Ch53 L27).

<!-- level:3 -->
- **Adventuring capital**: many consider it the adventuring capital of the continent; high magic makes monster manifestations powerful and frequent across the region, which matters because Estercost is full of towns, villages and smaller cities (Book 12, Ch43 L47).
- **Detection**: the local Adventure Society intercepts monster appearances with a network of towers, something like the Earth grid, instead of leaving notes for wandering adventurers (Book 12, Ch43 L48).
- **A prosperous city**: Jason finds "trees lined every street, heavy with blossoms" and "social programs and labour laws meant that even the poorest sections of the city were safe and relatively prosperous", from an economy thriving in multiple sectors (Book 12, Ch43 L52).
- **Lucrative adventuring**: a steady stream of silver- and gold-rank monsters with efficient looting protocols; Vitesse adventurers are in demand around the world, and the Magic Society offers portal travel and airship construction (Book 12, Ch43 L53).
- **Safe and gossipy**: with so many powerful adventurers, "if some crazy monster or lunatic cult showed up, he could relax and hear about it all later, just like everyone else" (Book 12, Ch43 L54).
- **The Academy and the guild**: Remore Academy and Burning Violet face each other, and the Remore family's control of the guild is contested (Book 12, Ch43 L64; Book 12, Ch45 L12-14).

## 3. Using this in a story

Vitesse is a city where everyone is someone: expect good manners, good gardens and politics conducted through guild and academy membership.
