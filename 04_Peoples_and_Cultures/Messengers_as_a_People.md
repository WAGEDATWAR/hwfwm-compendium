## 1. Summary

Messengers are the gestalt people who serve astral kings: long-lived, patient and hierarchical, with a master-and-servant social order. This file collects what they are as a society; their powers and their place in the cosmos are in `Astral_Kings_and_Messengers.md`.

## 2. Facts

- **Origin**: messengers are "budded, not born", and Jason says they are "actually plants, which is kind of crazy" (Book 12, Ch67 L23). They are gestalt beings whose body and soul are one (Book 9, Ch52 L60; see the 02 file).
- **Appearance**: a messenger in Book 9 "looked like a celestine with alabaster skin and long hair of spun gold", with solid gold eyes, standing some eight feet high with a pair of wings (Book 9, Ch52 L5). A messenger's alabaster skin dulls when drained of mana (Book 9, Ch52 L42).
- **Hierarchy**: masters hold servant races, and a master "was free to treat the servant races however he pleased, so long as it did not impinge upon the interests of other messengers" (Book 9, Ch56 L5). Fal Vin Garath, a silver-rank messenger, is Benella's abusive master (Book 9, Ch56 L5).
- **Names**: the messenger names in the text are three-part names with a middle particle: Fal Vin Garath, Jes Fin Kaal, Vesta Carmis Zell, Jamis Fran Muskar, Erigo Fin Desca, Cas Vin Baral and Relia Vin Vala. Boris Ket Lundi is also named in this pattern (first appearances: Book 9, Ch55 L12 and L19; Book 9, Ch78 L82; Book 10, Ch15 L5-6; Book 10, Ch79 L54; Book 11, Ch6 L55). The text gives no meaning for the middle particles; this note records the pattern only.
- **Belief**: Dawn tells Jason that astral kings rule through messengers, whose message is "Kneel" (see Astral_Kings_and_Messengers.md (Book 8, Ch64 L72-80). Hess confesses to feeling the instinct to kneel (Book 10, Ch16 L48).
- **Rank limits**: rank limits are a function of the brand, which did not exist when messengers first came into being (Boris, Book 11, Ch25 L51).
- **Resurrection**: does not work on messengers (Book 9, Ch65 L23).

- **Among humans**: Boris and his messengers constantly deal with people who believe them to be angels of the Lord despite his denials (Book 11, Ch68 L1521). Fifteen years after the revelation, many Cabal members are relaxed about masking their nature (Book 13, Ch954 L5; see The_Cabal.md).
