## 1. Summary

A nomad tribe from a region to the north that raids spirit coin convoys in the Greenstone desert and will not accept captured warriors as slaves.

## 2. Facts

- **Who they are**: the "sand pirates" are actually the Ustei Tribe, nomads from a region to the north (Book 1, Ch89 L50); Humphrey identifies them as northerners from their hair and clothes (Book 1, Ch81 L43).
- **The raids**: an expedition is formed to find out why they are so far south and stop them raiding spirit coin shipments (Book 1, Ch83 L4).
- **The response**: the best outcome is a peaceful return north or an agreement not to attack convoys; failing that, they are to be captured and interrogated (Book 1, Ch89 L50-51). Vincent expects them to talk if shown strength (Book 1, Ch89 L68).
- **Captives**: "Their culture doesn't allow warriors to be made slaves. For them, capture means release or death" (Book 1, Ch92 L36).
- **Later**: by Book 12 the Ustei who came south are said to predate the Earth arrivals by about three years (Book 12, Ch30 L9).

## 3. Using this in a story

The Ustei are proud, mobile raiders; treat them as a cultural unit rather than as bandits.
