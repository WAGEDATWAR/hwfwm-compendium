## 1. Summary

The most common people of Pallimustus and Earth's native people, with gifts that favour special attacks and fast essence advancement.

## 2. Facts

- **Racial gifts**: an affinity for special attacks, essences that advance more rapidly than others', and latent powers that adapt to the essences (Book 1, Ch85 L35). The latent gifts are dormant until awakened and then take a form based on the person's essences (Book 1, Ch38 L73).
- **Evolution**: Clive's blessing from the Celestial Book changes his human special attack affinity into a spell affinity (Book 1, Ch85 L22-26); his Human Ambition becomes Thirst for Knowledge and Humphrey's Special Attack Affinity becomes Attack of the Mirage Dragon (Book 2, Ch61 L144, L147). Taika's human abilities have been replaced with outworlder ones (Book 9, Ch33 L15).
- **Distribution**: the Greenstone region is human-dominated (Book 1, Ch15 L56).
- **Humans and outworlders**: an outworlder who has a child with another species produces offspring of that species, so a human and an outworlder have a human child (Book 5, Ch44 L72).
- **Lifespan**: essence users age slower; bronze-rankers can live well past a hundred, silvers double that and gold-rankers live for centuries (Book 1, Ch22 L58).

## 3. Using this in a story

Humans are the default people of the delta and of every Earth scene; their gifts reward specialised, skilled builds.
