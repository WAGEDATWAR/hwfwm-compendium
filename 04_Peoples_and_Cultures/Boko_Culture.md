## 1. Summary

Boko is an old desert city north of Greenstone with a native population, known for its scholars and artists. The Adventure Society has a branch there, but it is not a strong adventuring city. Its fate in Book 12 is in Boko.md.

## 2. Facts

- **Older than Greenstone**: "A lot older than Greenstone, with a population native to the area" (Book 12, Ch29 L60).
- **The arts**: "Scholars of the arts. Painting, poetry, sculpture, dance", with people travelling from across the continent to see its theatres (Book 12, Ch29 L61). It has beautiful architecture and gardens (Book 12, Ch30 L71) and universities (Book 12, Ch31 L34).
- **Look and dress**: desert stone like Old City with drier air (Book 1, Ch109 L21); the people are almost entirely human and very dark-skinned, with loose, breathable fashion calmer than the Ustei's (Book 1, Ch113 L21).
- **Not a port**: a coastal city that was never a port like Greenstone, with no natural harbour (Book 1, Ch113 L21).
- **Adventuring**: the Adventure Society has a branch (Book 1, Ch113 L28) but Boko "was not a strong adventuring city" (Book 12, Ch33 L7). Boko has a mirage chamber that Clive helped rebuild (Book 2, Ch17 L60).
- **Return of the Ustei**: the Society returns Ustei captives to Boko (Book 1, Ch109 L7-21).

<!-- level:3 -->
- **Politics**: its Duke is a silver-ranker through cores, as is normal for political leaders, and noble houses exist (Book 12, Ch39 L80, L110). A movement against intellectuals among low-rankers began in Boko (Book 12, Ch29 L65).
- **Temples**: floating temples shielded by their gods survive the city's destruction (Book 12, Ch33 L3; Book 12, Ch39 L37).
